Reading

The geometry every window shares, and the one lemma that prices what each can read.

A window is a way of reading a number through a nested grid of cells refining toward a point: the depth-t cell of a number is the set of numbers agreeing with it on its first t digits, whatever the digits are. A reader of a map f at lookahead c sends each input cell at depth t + c into an output cell at depth t containing its image, the chosen cells nesting as the input refines, so the output is one committed digit stream; cmin(t) is the least lookahead that works at depth t, and a map is gated when no bounded lookahead works at all. The positional pair holds the two ends of the story. The tower reads the trailing digits of a base — every finite place kept, the archimedean place deleted, the first deletion (Walls) — while floating point reads the leading digits, the mirror deletion that keeps only the archimedean place; their shared criterion is on The dual pole. Beside the pair sit the continued-fraction window, whose digits are partial quotients and which is not positional at all, and the Zeckendorf window, which counts by Fibonacci numbers instead of powers (Completions). A digit set larger than the base — digits {−a, …, a} with 2a + 1 > b — is redundant: its cells overlap instead of partitioning. Throughout, rad m is the product of m's distinct primes.

The reading lemma

Formally a window is a metric measure geometry (X, d, μ, {Ct}): a metric, a measure, and a nested cover sequence whose mesh δt — the largest cell diameter at depth t — tends to 0, with Lebesgue numbers ℓt, the largest radius such that every ball of that radius sits inside some depth-t cell. A reader at lookahead c exists exactly when every deep input cell's image fits one output cell.

The reading lemma rule

If f is Lipschitz with constant L, it fits cell-to-cell at lookahead c once L·δt+c < ℓt, and where cells nest this per-level fitting is readability outright. Necessity is coarse: a readable map need only be Lipschitz at cell scale, and pointwise Lipschitz follows only where the cells are metric balls — the leading window has a map readable at lookahead 0 with unbounded pointwise ratio (permuting the third digit; the ratios run 19, 181, 1801, …). Three cover shapes give three regimes: balls of an ultrametric (ℓt = δt, fitting automatic, margin 0); a partition of a connected space (ℓt = 0, the lemma silent, readability a per-map alignment question); an overlapping cover with ℓt = ρδt, where fitting costs the margin logb(1/ρ).

Scope. Rule at scanned scopes; the two-pole reading criterion on The dual pole is its positional instance.

The stream correction rule

At an overlapping cover a redundant cell's children keep the overlap only in their interior, so the committed stream departs from the lemma's per-level bound by at most one digit in either direction. The exact law is the residual safety game the reader plays against the next digit; the bound is its worst-alignment reading, exact where cells nest.

Scope. Rule at the scanned redundant scopes — twelve slopes over two digit sets, every deviation exactly one digit; the mechanism argued, the general statement open.

Readability is metric, not topological property

Reading every other digit of a trailing base-2 string is uniformly continuous — readable at a modulus that grows with depth — and readable at no bounded lookahead, its Lipschitz exponent growing with the depth of the pair. Continuity is not the gate; the class this opens, and the exact price of its member, are on Completions.

The wall criterion rule

At a partition cover the lemma is silent, and for an interval partition the permanent-wall half of the alignment question is answered by the cover's vertex set ∂, the cell-boundary points, which stay endpoints at every deeper level. For f continuous and strictly monotone at an irrational input y, the number of digits emitted grows without bound iff f(y) ∉ ∂: an image on a vertex is interior to every image interval, the vertex's two sides agree only to its own depth, and emission freezes forever at a computable constant, where a rate stall only slows it. A map's permanent walls are exactly f−1(∂) ∖ ∂. A redundant cover dissolves every wall — a positive Lebesgue number plus the stream correction leave no uniformly continuous map a wall — so wall exposure is the size of the vertex set, and the continued-fraction window's, all of Q, is the largest a rational-vertex grid can have (base b: Z[1/b] ⊂ Q). The cross-window witness: x² walls permanently at √(1/3) in the continued-fraction grid and reads on in base 10.

Scope. Rule at the scanned witnesses, the argument elementary for continuous strictly monotone maps: x² walls at every scanned √r (√2, √3, √6 freeze at once, √(3/2) and √(7/3) after one digit, √(17/12) after three), while √x is wall-free, a rational square root forcing a rational input.

The third deletion: the continued-fraction window

Depth t is the first t partial quotients a1, …, at of the continued fraction [a0; a1, a2, …]; the cell of a prefix is a Stern–Brocot interval, running between the convergent pt/qt and its mediant neighbour, of length 1/(qt(qt + qt−1)); cells partition the irrationals at each depth and nest across depths. This is the first window here that is not positional, and the first whose cells' size is not a function of their depth: what it deletes is the constant ruler, the per-digit scale both positional windows hold fixed.

The metric pair and the two delays rule

The window carries two metrics: the ultrametric on quotient strings, where reading at lookahead c is verbatim Lipschitz with constant 2c, and the value metric, where an integer Möbius map x ↦ (ax + b)/(cx + d) has an honest Lipschitz constant. Their exchange rate is the Gauss-map entropy π²/(6 ln 2) almost everywhere (Shannon–McMillan–Breiman), with real fluctuations — the all-1s chain, the golden ratio φ, runs at the floor 2 ln φ, the fattest cells — and a positional window is exactly the zero-variance case, where the two metrics are one ruler. So bounded lookahead forks into digit delay (quotients consumed minus quotients emitted) and scale delay (log of precision consumed over precision emitted), and the two disagree for real: at (2x, φ) the scale ratio holds near 1 while the digit ratio sits at 1/3. The window's own measure is the Gauss measure dx/((1 + x) ln 2), and the Gauss–Kuzmin digit law is this window's Benford — the invariant measure read through the digit grid; cells are not measure-uniform, and the metric and the measure meet at the entropy.

Scope. Rule at scanned scopes; the ergodic facts are classical and enter as controls.

The unit gate rule

An integer Möbius map with det = ±1 — unimodular, a member of GL(2, Z) — reads continued-fraction streams at bounded digit delay: every nonnegative-entry unimodular map is a positive word in the three maps x + 1, x/(x + 1) and 1/x, the Stern–Brocot factorization, each an exact transducer whose delays add over a fixed word. A map with det ≠ ±1 owns a mismatched witness: 2φ = [3; 4, 4, 4, …] with φ³ = 2 + √5 exactly, so emitting t quotients of 2φ consumes 3t quotients of φ, the delay linear. Necessity is proved at quadratic streams: readability transports through unimodular factors (Smith normal form), reducing the gate to the scalings x ↦ nx, where φ is a universal witness — a matched rate would force the tail of nφ to carry φ's own unit, which floor rigidity (the period trace is strictly increasing in every digit) reserves for the all-1s word alone, so nφ's stream would be φ's own — while the multiplier ring sits inside the lattice and φ ∉ Z + Znφ. Serret's theorem — equal tails ⇔ GL(2, Z)-equivalence — is the classical shadow: readable multiplication is, again, the window's native units.

Scope. Sufficiency a rule at nonnegative-entry unimodular maps; sign-crossing words at general streams unscanned, and at quadratic streams not needed, the flow layer below giving boundedness outright. Necessity a rule at proved scope, the algebra covering every primitive integer Möbius map with det ≠ ±1, resting on rate forcing and floor rigidity (exhaustive at 5,460 words).

The rate law: the gate is orbit-local rule

Write r(y) for a stream's rate, its scale per quotient. The local emission slope σ of a reader satisfies σ·rout/rin = 1 within 3%, so the digit delay is the signed accumulated rate mismatch: witnesses with rout < rin run ahead linearly. The sharp print is (2x, √2): 2√2 = [2; 1, 4, 1, 4, …] keeps the input's rate, and the pair reads at delay 0 despite det 2. Non-unimodularity does not stall every orbit; it guarantees a mismatched one exists.

Scope. Rule at nine scanned pairs, rate ratios down to 5/13.

The conductor law rule

At a quadratic stream the rate is arithmetic. The tail's period-matrix eigenvalue η is the fundamental automorph of the lattice Z + Zy, which is εi(y) with ε the field's fundamental unit and i(y) the unit index [OK× : O′×] of the lattice's multiplier order O′; so the rate is r(y) = 2 i(y) RK / ℓ(y), RK = ln ε the regulator and ℓ the period length, and a measured stall ratio is (iout/iin)·(ℓin/ℓout). The delay is an instrument: the stall slope of x ↦ nx measures the unit index of a conductor order — the suborder Z + fOK of conductor f — and where the classical index ceiling f·∏p|f(1 − (D/p)/p) is not attained the law follows the realized index, the deficit being the order's ring class number against the field's — (5x, √2) at index 3 against ceiling 6, (8x, φ) at 6 against 12. Ratio 1 arrives three ways — index and period cancelling, landing on the maximal order, the input conductor compensating — so matchedness is orbit-local, never the map's. And the law is map-blind: any monotone C¹ map with nonzero derivative is conductor-priced at quadratic orbits, entering only through which output orbit it selects (x² keeps φ's tail, since φ² = φ + 1).

Scope. Rule at 17 measured pairs, four predicted before measurement; the identity η = εi exact at all 53 scanned pairs, the eigenvalue identity itself classical; map-blindness proved at the flow layer's scope and measured within 1% at six nonlinear pairs.

The boundary collapse and the hug rule

Cells partition, and at the positional leading window that made permanent alignment walls; for Möbius maps it does not. Output boundaries are rationals, a rational's preimage under an integer Möbius map is rational, so every hug — a stretch where the image rides a cell boundary — resolves at the preimage's continued-fraction length, itself the rate conversion of the boundary's scale: partition wall and rate stall are one mechanism for Möbius maps, the wall criterion's empty-rationalizing-set case. Near a genuine wall, hugs decouple from the boundary: an x² hug at the fixed vertex 2 lasts m digits for every m at bounded input digits, commitment freezes in both rulers, and the hugged scale is repaid through one giant output quotient of size ln N ≈ 2m·ln(1 + √2), committed near input depth 2.83m — a cell at quotient N having size about 1/N², the hugged scale is paid twice.

Scope. Rule at the scanned families: the halved Fibonacci boundaries for the collapse, the vertex 2 under x² for the hug.

The redundant cover

The window's native overlapping cover adds to the Stern–Brocot cells the straddle chains: at a vertex v with Farey parents (l, r), the chain cell Sk(v) runs between the k-th mediants of (l, v) and (r, v) and contains v in its interior at every k. The root straddle is the image (1/2, 2) of the modified Stern–Brocot digit M — Niqui's {L, R, M} digit set — and productivity for Möbius maps on such alphabets is classical (Gosper, Vuillemin, Edalat–Potts); what the overlap does and cannot do is the content.

The ladder law rule

The cover cells containing a point z are z's own cell ladder plus a chain segment of exactly K(v, z) = ⌊1/(qv²|z − v|) − qfar/qv⌋ rungs at each approached vertex v, qfar the denominator of the parent on z's side; at a convergent the ratio terms cancel and K is the next partial quotient verbatim — the complete-quotient identity rearranged — and at a rational the chain is infinite. The cover is graded: the rank of Sk(v) is depth(v) + k, stepping by +1 along every child relation, so overlap adds routes and never length, and the honest digit clock is the rank.

Scope. The closed form exact at every scanned node, 510 nodes at 12 chain indices; the grading a property of every child relation.

The wall gate falls, the rate gate stands rule

Through the chain, x² reads √2, √3 and √(3/2) with never-freezing emission, the slope of ln k equal to the input's scale rate to four decimals — the emitted stream is an infinite redundant representation of the rational output, the continued-fraction mirror of 0.1999…; walls are ladder termination, and redundancy extends exactly the terminated ladders. The rate gate survives: at (x/2, 2φ) the maximal rank of a cover cell containing the image equals the plain tree position at every sampled depth, so ladder spacing is orbit data no overlap changes and rate forcing survives redundancy verbatim. And a delay's sign belongs to the clock: counted in quotients, (2x, φ) stalls at 1/3; counted in Stern–Brocot tree steps — the tree clock — it runs ahead at 4/3, and the inverse reading (x/2, 2φ) does the reverse. The flow layer

Every scanned window's digit process is the shift of an expanding map T with an absolutely continuous invariant measure, and its per-digit scale is the roof ln |T′|: constant ln b at base b, 2 ln(1/x) at the Gauss map. Rokhlin's formula with Abramov's makes the suspension flow — the flow that spends roof-time at each digit — have entropy exactly 1: entropy per digit equals scale per digit, and the metric pair's exchange rate is this identity read per stream. A positional window is the constant-roof case, its suspension circle the scale circle whose Haar measure is Benford (The dual pole). The continued fraction's suspension is the modular geodesic flow (Artin, Series, Adler–Flatto), where a quadratic tail rides a closed geodesic of flow period 2 ln η = 2 i RK per tail period, so the conductor rate is flow period per return; over one tail period the product of the complete quotients equals η, exact at 25 tails. The suspension, the roof and Abramov's formula are classical imports; the reading layer on top is the content.

The commitment bound rule

The commitment deficit after n input digits is D(n) = scale(image interval) − scale(committed cell), scale = ln(1/length). For f monotone and C¹ with f′(y) ≠ 0 and z = f(y) irrational, badly approximable (partial quotients ≤ A) and not a vertex, 0 ≤ D(n) ≤ 3 ln(A + 2) + O(1): the reader is scale-synced at every such output whatever the map, since |z − p/q| ≥ 1/((A + 2)q²) keeps z proportionally interior to its cells and one refinement step costs at most the cell ratio. A wall is the bound's infinite endpoint — the output on a vertex, the deficit growing linearly at slope 1.7627 = 2 ln(1 + √2) at (x², √2) — where the redundant chain reader re-syncs, committing at that slope what the partition reader forfeits. In the flow's own dictionary a hug is a finite cusp excursion of the output ray and a permanent wall the excursion that never returns.

Scope. Rule at proved scope — scale accounting plus bad approximability.

Rate forcing rule

If moreover y's tail is eventually periodic and z is quadratic, the emission after n digits is (rin/rout)·n + O(1), the ratio the rational (iin·ℓout)/(iout·ℓin): digit delay is bounded iff the rates match, else linear with the mismatch's sign. Two corollaries: the conductor law's map-blindness, f entering only through |f′|, an O(1), and through which output orbit it selects; and the unit gate's necessity resting on proved pieces alone. The wall stays a property of the pair (map, point): the flow layer prices rates and delays from orbit invariants, and which ray the output is remains Diophantine data — under x², φ's output is a closed geodesic and √2's a cusp ray.

The two gates

The Lipschitz gate theorem

A window's completion is the limit object its nested cells define — the infinite digit strings, with the finite ones dense inside, under the agreement ultrametric d(x, y) = 2−(depth of agreement). A map is readable at bounded delay iff it extends to a Lipschitz self-map of the completion, the minimal delay being log2 of the constant: agreement to depth t + c forcing image agreement to depth t is exactly d(fx, fy) ≤ 2c·d(x, y), and a Lipschitz map on a dense subset of a complete space extends uniquely with the same constant. Read as continuity the gate is false: a completion is compact, so continuity buys a modulus s(t), and bounded delay needs s(t) ≤ t + c, which nothing forces. Three classes, then: Lipschitz (bounded delay), middle (continuous, readable only at a lookahead growing with depth), discontinuous (unreadable at any modulus). The middle is inhabited — the every-other-digit map above is its member, its least lookahead at depth t exactly t − 1 — and the arithmetic maps keep out of it wherever that has been proved (Completions).

Scope. Theorem; the middle member's column confirmed to the full row at range.

Redundancy splits the gates by shape rule

The redundant leading window dissolves its gate wholesale — every ×m local at bounded lookahead, priced by size — because positional roofs are constant: scale rates always match, so the only gate was alignment, which any positive Lebesgue number kills. The redundant continued-fraction cover dissolves only the wall gate: its roofs are orbit-pinned, and the ladder law shows overlap extending terminated ladders while never raising the reachable rank at an irrational. One purchase, a positive Lebesgue number, two fates, decided by whether the window's roof is constant or carries orbit data.

Scope. Rule at the two covers scanned; the leading window's price is on The dual pole.

Deletion is the price of locality: a window reads exactly what is continuous in the geometry its deletion leaves it, and every wall, gate and cure here — at the two ends of a numeration and along the continued fraction's grid — is that statement's positive or negative space.

What the windows complete to

The gate's native form is an action on the completion, and that object is not the same twice. Base b completes to a ring, so every integer multiplication is continuous there and only division is gated. The Zeckendorf window completes to an odometer, a space cut by a circle rotation's coding, where ×2 has no continuous extension — a theorem with no range cap — and the golden positional base completes to nothing new at all. Above those three stands a tower of torus shapes, one per recurrence degree, and past it a fifth where the image limit points over one input limit stop being finitely many; wherever it has been read, the arithmetic keeps clear of the class between Lipschitz and discontinuous — Completions.

The family above Zeckendorf

Zeckendorf is one window of a family with a member for every irrational α, and across the quadratic members the gate tracks the unit group of α's order. The tear is a theorem at every irrational window — multiplication and floor division by any m ≥ 2 have no continuous extension — and what each window's own engine adds is the address of the flip: by a proved comb at every constant-a, [0; 1, a] and [0; 1, 1, 2] window, by a certified boundary family at the arbitrary-period windows, and at every irrational window for every shift-free relation by one containment principle, no comb, no certificate, no period — Quadratic windows.

The second deletion

The positional pair carries one criterion with the poles swapped: a window reads a map at bounded lookahead iff the map is Lipschitz at cell scale in the window's own metric and each deep cell's image fits one output cell, the fitting clause priced by the cell family's Lebesgue number. The two poles exchange crown and blind spot — the tower's exact zero-test against floating point's native size, sign and order — and base b's trailing digits read exactly the primes p | b that its leading digits perfectly hide — The dual pole.
