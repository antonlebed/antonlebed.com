The sighted probe

  • The exponent identity theorem — For an odd prime l whose door is open the slack δl equals vl(V) exactly, so the slack vector is the counter vector of the growth machine: a Minsky machine's counters and an observer's eye read the same numbers. — explore_headroom.py
  • The headroom ledger theorem — Every move splits the headroom as V(Nm) = V(N)/gcd(m, V(N))·G′ with the premium G′ a positive integer: a move never destroys more headroom than it spends, and V rises exactly when G′ exceeds the burn. — explore_headroom.py, explore_premium.py
  • The premium's closed form theorem — The premium is a closed form in λ and one coordinate vl(V) per prime l of the move and nothing else; the primes of a move interact, 35 opening the door 13 at λ = 1 where neither 5 nor 7 does. — explore_composite_move.py, explore_premium.py
  • Only the quotient is path-free theorem — Along a split m = ab the wall gain telescopes and the unpaid part does not, so the premium is submultiplicative, G′(N, ab) ≤ G′(N, a)·G′(Na, b), only the quotient of the two flows path-free. — explore_composite_move.py
  • The wall map is a closure operator theorem — N ↦ W(λ(N)) is monotone, inflationary and idempotent, so a transparency episode is a walk in the divisor lattice of V whose fixed points are the closed states, every one above 2 divisible by 24, and mortality is its absorbing branch. — explore_headroom.py, explore_standing_move.py
