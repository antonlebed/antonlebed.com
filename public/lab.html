ESP32 Mesh

Three ESP32 boards form an ESP-NOW mesh. Each CRT-encodes data into Z/510,510 residues (7 bytes), broadcasts over radio, and the receiver reconstructs and checks ECC. Three test phases, 20 rounds each.
