ESP32 Mesh

Three ESP32 boards form an ESP-NOW mesh. CRT-encoded packets fly over radio. ECC detects and corrects errors in transit. Clock synchronization via triangle closure — no master, no ground truth.

All measurements below used Z/214,414,200 — the same 7 primes with raised exponents (moduli 8, 9, 25, 49, 11, 13, 17) for higher resolution per channel. The foundation ring Z/510,510 uses each prime at exponent 1.
