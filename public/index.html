Z/214,414,200

23 × 32 × 52 × 72 × 11 × 13 × 17

The ring

Seven primes, each raised to a chosen exponent. By the Chinese Remainder Theorem, the integer ring Z/214,414,200 splits into seven completely independent channels — what happens in mod 8 cannot affect mod 9, or mod 25, or any of the others. Every number in the ring has a unique 7-tuple of residues, and every 7-tuple maps back to exactly one number. The decomposition is a bijection.

Built-in error correction

Four of these channels (mod 8, 9, 25, 49) carry data. The other three (mod 11, 13, 17) are algebraically determined by the data — they are parity. This gives a rate-4/7 error-correcting code that lives inside the ring's own arithmetic: corrupt any single channel, and the other six reconstruct the original. No separate encoding layer. The ring is the code.

Why these primes

The seven primes are the first seven consecutive primes, derived from seeds {1, 2} by a single map (c(n) = 2n+1) plus two algebraic extensions. The exponents are Pareto-optimal: the largest compatible with the ring's internal constraints. Nothing is arbitrary.
