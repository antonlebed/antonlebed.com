The ring

The product of the first seven primes. By the Chinese Remainder Theorem, every element maps to a unique 7-tuple of residues — one per prime field. What happens in mod 2 cannot affect mod 3, or mod 5, or any of the others. Every 7-tuple maps back to exactly one number. The decomposition is a bijection.

Built-in error correction

Four channels (mod 2, 3, 5, 7) carry data. Three (mod 11, 13, 17) are parity. This gives a rate-4/7 error-correcting code that is maximum distance separable: any 4 of 7 channels reconstruct the original. No [7,4] code can do better. No separate encoding layer. The ring is the code.

Why these primes

The first seven consecutive primes. Not arbitrary — two Cunningham chains (each step: double and add 1) starting from seeds 1 and 2 produce 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, then 13 and 17 close the set algebraically. Every channel is a prime field — no zero divisors, every nonzero element invertible.

What you can do here

Everything on this site is computed live in your browser. Type any number and watch it decompose into 7 channels. Corrupt a channel and watch the ring heal it. Trace power-map orbits across the ring's dynamics. The engineering section documents a CRT-decomposed neural architecture built on this structure — what worked, what didn't. The hardware lab shows real measurements: an FPGA running CRT arithmetic at 267 MHz, three ESP32 boards correcting errors over radio.
