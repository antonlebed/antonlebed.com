Menu collisions

The one door to robust forgetting is prime recycling. How wide that door is turns out to be a question about unique factorization — when two different pairs of menus weigh routes identically at every temperature — and the answer runs from a collision so generic it is almost never a failure of factorization at all, down to a bound on how far a genuine failure can escape a single variable.

The systems are the small grown worlds of the amnesia certificate. A state is a positive integer, a move multiplies it by a member of that state's menu — the moves the state admits — and a route is the sequence of moves from the start state 1, its product being the state it ends at. A world is read at a temperature β: a move m is taken with probability proportional to m−β, normalized by the state's normalizer, the sum of m−β over its menu. A route's probability is the product of its moves' weights divided by the product of the normalizers it passed through — and since the moves' product is the endpoint, two routes reaching one endpoint differ only in those interior normalizers. A world free to reuse a prime it already holds recycles; one every move of which is coprime to the state it acts on cannot, and a coprime world can never hold a multi-route fiber flat at every temperature: certified forgetting costs prime recycling. What is left over is how much room recycling gives, and it is a question about the normalizers alone. Two routes are flat at every β exactly when their interior-normalizer PRODUCTS agree as functions of β; a structural witness for it would be the two MULTISETS agreeing. The door's width is the gap between those two conditions.

A menu's normalizer, read as a function of β, is a Dirichlet polynomial, the sum of m−β over the menu's members; writing one variable xp for p−β at each prime p makes it a 0/1 polynomial in the primes' variables, one term per member, the term's exponent vector being the member's factorization. Two pairs of menus collide when their products agree, ZA·ZB = ZC·ZD with {A, B} ≠ {C, D}. A menu's core is its polynomial with its largest single-term factor divided out; a seed is a menu whose core has a factor, irreducible over the integers, carrying a negative coefficient; a menu's size is its number of members, and a product of sizes s₁ and s₂ has s₁s₂ terms counted with multiplicity — it is in frame when every coefficient is 0 or 1, so the terms are distinct. The exponent vectors of a product's terms span a hull, its Newton polytope, whose dimension is the product dimension, a core's own polytope giving its core dimension; a direction picks out a face of it, the terms extreme that way, and keeps from each factor its initial form, that factor's own extreme terms. A box is a bound on menu elements, which nothing in the mathematics names, so “alone in the box” never means alone.

The semiring

The normalizer semiring is not factorial observation

Collisions are generic rather than exotic: 4,849 colliding pairs among menus drawn from {2..16} ∪ {32} of size at most 3, falling into 4,487 classes by the product they share. Almost none of it is non-unique factorization: 4,839 of the 4,849 are REGROUPINGS of one multiset of factors into two blocks — 2·6 = 3·4 in the integers, the scaling family ZA·ZcB = ZcA·ZB with cA the menu A multiplied through by c — and the other 10 sit in the 6 products that factor non-uniquely, every one in exactly two ways, all generated by three seeds, {2, 16}, {4, 32} and {2, 8, 32}. Whether a collision REALIZES — whether menus carrying those polynomials assemble into a world with a fiber flat at every temperature — has teeth at exactly one degeneracy: the unrealized collisions coincide with the all-singleton ones, 44 of 44, where four singleton menus force equal move products, hence equal leading moves, hence a conflict at the first state; every collision with a non-singleton menu realizes, 4,805 of 4,849.

Scope. Exhaustive at the census's own box, the realization search's opening moves scanned to scale 8; the singleton direction proved. The same scan cross-checks the coprime half of the coprimality dichotomy from outside its proof: zero coprime realizations across all 4,849 collisions. Toy scale.

Non-uniqueness is a property of one menu property

Polynomials over the integers factor uniquely and menu polynomials have no common factor across their coefficients, so every factorization of a product into nonnegative factors is a partition of its integer-irreducible factors into blocks with nonnegative products. If every such factor is nonnegative the factorization into nonnegative irreducibles is UNIQUE; non-uniqueness needs a factor with a negative coefficient, which stands for no menu and so must be paired around. The factors of ZA·ZB are those of ZA with those of ZB, so carrying one is a property of a SINGLE menu — a seed — and never of the pairing; a census of colliding PAIRS carries the property quadratically where it lives linearly. The rogue world's product is one of the six, with factorizations {x, x, 1 + x + x², 1 + x³} and {x, x, 1 + x, 1 + x² + x⁴}; that the last two are irreducible HERE is nonnegativity biting — a factor pair must carry constant terms 1, forcing the linear coefficients to vanish, and nothing over the nonnegative integers then reaches the top term.

Scope. Follows from unique factorization over the integers.

Menu size is the grading rule

Factorization of 0/1 polynomials into nonnegative factors is classified by NUMBER OF TERMS (van de Woestijne 2011) — under a nonnegative factorization term counts multiply and each factor is itself 0/1, the term-count law, and the count is unique at 4, 8 and 9 terms, in several variables too, by the same paper's own reduction — so a product of sizes s₁ and s₂, with s₁s₂ terms, is classified by SIZE PAIR, and the small pairs settle at ANY bound on the elements: (2, 2), (2, 4) and (3, 3) factor uniquely. At (2, 3), six terms, non-uniqueness exists but is forced onto a line — the linear system the two factorizations must satisfy is translation-invariant, so its solution space is spanned by the all-ones vector and one progression vector, and the exponent vectors sit in an arithmetic progression along a single direction, collinear. The Newton polytope is a SEGMENT and the mechanism is an image of the one-variable one: a monomial substitution where the direction is nonnegative, a homogenization where it is mixed — {8, 27}·{4, 6, 9} = {2, 3}·{16, 36, 81}, the shared negative factor x² − xy + y². Every collision at ten terms or fewer is one-variable in this sense; at ten the three sporadic identities are regroupings of the cyclotomic factors of 1 + x + ⋯ + x⁹ — the factors vanishing at roots of unity, the cyclotomic ones — one bare and two beside a factor common to both sides.

Scope. The classification by term count is the cited prior work, holding at any element bound and in several variables; the collinearity at six terms is proved here from it. Confirmed on this corpus's own menus: 0 non-unique products among 1,854 pairs at 4 terms, 43,257 at 8 and 4,495 at 9, and all 39 found at 6 terms collinear.

The mechanism escapes one variable at size pair (2, 5) property

{2, 54}·{2, 6, 10, 30, 90} and {2, 6}·{2, 10, 54, 90, 810} share the ten-element product {4, 12, 20, 60, 108, 180, 324, 540, 1620, 4860}, which factors two ways with a TWO-dimensional Newton polygon — the image of no one-variable polynomial under either operation. What hid it is menu SIZE, not the element bound: the same family at its cheapest primes is {2, 16}·{2, 4, 6, 12, 24} = {2, 4}·{2, 6, 16, 24, 96}, largest element 96, barely past the size-3 sweeps' bound of 81. The negative factor is still x² − x + 1, of Newton dimension 1: the second variable rides the CO-FACTORS, so neither the product's variable count — a single-term factor raises it for free — nor the negative factor's Newton dimension grades a mechanism, both reading one factor at a time.

Scope. A hand construction, verified exactly on the menus given; nothing is asserted about the size pair (2, 5) at large.

The descent dimension

The descent dimension property

A collision is an identity of convolutions of 0/1 indicator functions on the exponent lattice. Its support-only content is classical and strictly weaker: a product's Newton polytope is the Minkowski sum of its factors' (Ostrowski), and enumerating those decompositions is a published factorization instrument (Gao and Lauder 2001) — while {0, 1} + {0, 1, 3} against {0, 1, 2} + {0, 2} share a sumset and a mass with different convolutions. The productive half is the other one. Initial forms multiply as the factors they come from do, so a collision restricts to EVERY face of the product's polytope, and the descent dimension δ of two factorizations is the smallest dimension of a face whose induced factor multisets — each initial form divided by its own single-term content, a division that is load-bearing — already differ. What δ buys is the LOCATION of the mechanism: small δ says it is inherited from a low face. In frame, δ ≥ 1 always: at a vertex every initial form is a single term of coefficient 1, the division empties both multisets, and no vertex grades a collision. Off the frame it is not so: a vertex coefficient above 1 survives the division, and x² − x + 2 hidden three ways among 1 + x, 1 + x + x² and 2 + x gives a vertex reading (2, 2) against (4), δ = 0 — the integers' grouping graded and the hiding not.

Scope. The face restriction and the polytope identity are the cited prior work; δ is a definition, δ ≥ 1 in frame is proved from it, and the δ = 0 object off the frame is exact. A sampled face reading is an UPPER bound on δ — a missed direction can only exhibit a differing face — so a report of δ = 2 is read only where the polygon's faces are enumerated exactly.

Up to ten terms, every collision descends to an edge rule

The size-(2, 5) escape reads δ = 1, its two factorizations inducing (1 + v³)(1 + v + v²) against (1 + v)(1 + v² + v⁴) on one edge — the six-term identity itself, the case the escape was supposed to have left behind. Up to ten terms that is the rule, δ ≤ 1 throughout: the six-term form and the three sporadic ten-term identities spend one of their two free exponents on a translation, so every lift is collinear, and the two ten-term two-parameter families descend to an edge carrying the six-term identity. COROLLARY: δ ≥ 2 needs twelve terms — 11 is prime and unique — and twelve admits exactly the MENU size pairs (2, 6) and (3, 4), the qualifier load-bearing: a factor with a coefficient above 1 is outside the frame, and the literature's own twelve-term example is one. The twelve-term instance the corollary names first, {2, 8, 32} against {2, 4, 16, 32}, reads δ = 1 like everything below it.

Scope. Proved from the term-count classification cited above, read in full, together with the grading of each family it leaves standing; the instance is one graded object, not a search.

The (3, 4) half: every in-frame collision descends to an edge observation

The walk of 85,253 pairs — the one size-3 seed {2, 8, 32} of {2..32} against every size-4 menu of {2..24}, and the 17 size-4 seeds there against every size-3 menu of {2..32} — finds 336 collisions, of which 71 are in frame; 263 have ten terms and 2 have eight, a coefficient above 1. All 71 sit at product dimension 2, so δ was free to read 2 at every one, and read 1 at every one. The two dimension-1 collisions, where δ ≤ dim forces the answer, are both outside the frame. The graded instance itself, dimension 1 and δ = 1, sits outside the box and is the only place either box has where a COLLINEAR seed meets a collinear seed, each half the unique collinear seed of its size in its box.

Scope. Exhaustive inside the box named, silent outside it, each δ read off a sampled set of directions and so an upper bound — the safe side for a search whose target is δ ≥ 2. Toy scale.

Non-uniqueness is confined to product dimension 2 observation

A pair's reachable dimension is the rank of its joint direction space and needs no factorization: the 85,253 pairs distribute 2 / 3,002 / 30,127 / 52,122 over dimensions 1 to 4, and the collisions 2 / 334 / 0 / 0 — 11.13% of the dimension-2 pairs, and not one of the 82,249 at dimension 3 or 4. The cheap deflation dies on the walk's own histogram: 95.6% and 96.6% of the two seed populations reach dimension 3 or more, the PARTNER being unbounded in rank, so what collapses the dimension is the collision and not the seed. The mechanism sits at the integer factors: negativity cannot cancel across disjoint variables, so a second factorization must place the seed's negative factor beside a factor sharing its variables. Where the seed is collinear, 64 of the 71, the partner's core supplies one on the seed's own line at every one; where that partner is not itself a seed, 48 of the 64, the term-count law prices its core as two 0/1 binomials, one spent on the absorption and one direction left — dimension 2 by the law. The 16 whose partner is itself a seed are the mixing family; 7 are measured and unexplained.

Scope. The same walk as the block above, read over its in-frame products; the term-count argument is proved and covers the 48 it names. Toy scale.

Whatever room the one surviving corridor has is in the (2, 6) half: a seed of dimension 3 needs six members at least, by the half-size ceiling, and most six-member seeds have it.

Full dimension occurs observation
(x+y)(x3y+x3+x2+xy2+y2+y)  =  (x+1)(x3y+x3+x2y2+xy+y3+y2)(x+y)(x^3y+x^3+x^2+xy^2+y^2+y) \;=\; (x+1)(x^3y+x^3+x^2y^2+xy+y^3+y^2)

Twelve terms, every coefficient 0 or 1, four 0/1 factors — {2, 3}·{3, 4, 8, 9, 18, 24} in menu clothes — and every proper face of its Newton polygon, vertex and edge alike, induces the same factor multiset on both sides. Only the whole polygon separates them, so δ = 2 and the mechanism is inherited from no face. So the reading that survived the classification and the (3, 4) half — every collision a lower-variable identity with co-factors riding it — is FALSE at the first term count where it could fail, and the classification is structurally incomplete rather than unfinished. Counted at the core, a menu and its multiple by a fixed integer being one object, the box {2..24} at size pair (2, 6) holds 8 objects, 7 at δ = 1 and this one at δ = 2, wearing the eight suits {2c, 3c}, c = 1..8.

Scope. The witness is exact and settles existence at any bound: the identity is checked as written and the proper faces of its polygon enumerated outright, which a δ of 2 needs and a δ of 1 does not. The object count is exhaustive over its box. Toy scale.

And it is alone there observation

The box {2..32} holds 736,281 six-member menus, 7.3 times the population that found the witness, and 203 seeds among them. Its in-frame collisions are 22 objects, 21 at δ = 1 and exactly ONE at δ = 2 — the same identity, wearing ten suits — with 4 more objects out of frame; the frame trap is a fact about a population, 0 of 8 objects at {2..24} and 4 of 26 here. So the incompleteness the witness opens is real and narrow. And the confinement survives where the seed side was free to break it: 177 of the 203 seeds have core dimension 3, so the 177 × 465 = 82,305 pairs they seed against a two-member menu reach dimension 3 or more with no factorization consulted, and not one collides. The witness's own seed has core dimension 2, so full dimension is not bought by the dimension a six-member seed can add — whatever selects it, it is not that.

Scope. Exhaustive over the one box, and a statement about that sample of an unbounded population; the confinement is read over the in-frame objects only, the 4 out of frame having no dimension reading here. Toy scale.

The mechanisms

What the second factorization IS is settled three ways. Where the two factorizations share a factor, δ is derived rather than read: a face separates them exactly when the negative factor's initial form has more than one term and the two factors beside it do not read the same content-free form there — at product dimension 2, an edge of the negative factor's polygon parallel to an edge of one of theirs — a criterion proved both ways, and the full-dimension witness is where that parallelism fails at every edge. Where they share no factor, a pair with a three-term factor on either side is one explicit family or one-variable, a theorem at twelve terms, and every such pair either box holds is that family. And at a seed whose core is two irreducible factors, whether a pair collides at all is one sign scan on the product of two of them: Collision mechanisms.

The seeds

Which menus are seeds has a closed form at two and three members — a pair whose members, divided by their gcd, are d-th powers for a d with an odd prime factor, and a collinear trinomial whose exponents {0, a, b} meet all three residues modulo 3 with {a, b} not {1, 2} — and a seed's core has dimension at most HALF the menu's size, a theorem at every size that caps the seed side of every pairing above and puts the first dimension-3 seed at six members: Menu seeds.
