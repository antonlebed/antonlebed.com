Completions

What a window's cells complete to, and which arithmetic survives there.

A window is a nested grid of cells refining toward a point, and it reads a map at lookahead c when the output cell at depth t is a function of the input cell at depth t + c; a map is gated when no bounded lookahead works (Reading). A trailing window cuts the nonnegative integers by their low-order digits: the depth-t cell of n is the integers agreeing with n on their t lowest digits, and base b's is the residue class mod bt. Such cells nest and partition at every depth, which makes the metric an ultrametric — two integers are close exactly when they agree far — and a Cauchy sequence of integers under it has a limit the integers need not contain. The completion is the space of those limits, the infinite digit strings with the finite ones dense inside. A map is readable at bounded lookahead iff it extends to a Lipschitz self-map of that space, continuity alone being strictly weaker (the Lipschitz gate), so which maps a trailing window reads is a question about the completion — and the answer changes from window to window.

A window's roof is its per-digit scale, how much a cell shrinks from one depth to the next; a positional base is the case where that is constant. Rate forcing is the continued-fraction mechanism tying a bounded delay to matching input and output rates (Reading). Throughout, rad m is the product of m's distinct primes.

The Zeckendorf window

Every nonnegative integer is uniquely a sum of non-consecutive Fibonacci numbers — its Zeckendorf digits, n = Σ dk Fk over k ≥ 2 (F2 = 1, F3 = 2, F4 = 3, …), digits {0, 1}, no two adjacent 1s. The trailing window's depth-t cells partition the nonnegative integers and nest across depths, an ultrametric window like base b's trailing end, but non-positional: a cell is not a congruence class of any modulus, the low digits of n being set by where n/φ falls mod 1 in a three-distance partition of the circle. The base ratio φ is a unit of Z[φ]; an integer m ≥ 2 is an element of Z[φ] and not a unit.

The Zeckendorf gate rule

The units read and every tested non-unit fails. Multiplication and division by φ are the digit shifts, carry-free on strings, at lookahead 0 and 1; the successor n + 1 reads at exactly 1. Against them 3n, 4n and ⌊n/2⌋ are unreadable at every lookahead ≤ 12 at every depth ≤ 11, and ×2 at every depth and every lookahead, the theorem below. The roof is near-constant — every depth-t cell holds one of two counts of integers below the bound, two consecutive Fibonacci numbers, by its last-digit class — so rate forcing is unavailable, and what fails instead is carry alignment: Zeckendorf doubling has a down carry, 2Fk = Fk+1 + Fk−2, that walks a receptive pattern of low digits to the lowest digit, where base-b carries go only up. ×3's failure is not a comb's: 3Fk = Fk+2 + Fk−2 spreads instead of descending.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope, exhaustive below F26 = 121393: the unit delays uniform over depths 1–14, the non-unit failures at every lookahead ≤ 12 and depth ≤ 11, the two-valued roof at depths 6, 8, 10 — a two-valuedness that is Ostrowski legality's own at every window whose values are Fibonacci (Quadratic windows).

The Zeckendorf completion is not a ring theorem

Along the step-3 comb CK = F4 + F7 + ⋯ + FK the double is 2CK = FK+2 − 2, whose lowest digit reads the parity of K: the pairs (CK, CK+3) agree to depth K + 1 while their doubles differ at the lowest digit, and along the infinite comb the images converge, by the parity of K, to two distinct points of the completion. So ×2 has no continuous extension, and no continuous addition on the completion extends the integers' — x ↦ x + x is one. The measured extremal pair (C22, C25) is a member of this family.

Scope. Theorem, no range cap.

The empty middle

The window forces the gate's native form. At trailing base b every integer multiplication reads at lookahead 0 and unit-ness gates only division; at Zeckendorf the gate binds multiplication itself, though every integer lies in Z[φ]. The uniform statement is the action on the completion, not membership in a structure — and between Lipschitz and discontinuous sits the middle class, maps continuous but readable only at a lookahead growing with depth.

The arithmetic middle is empty theorem

On a trailing positional window and on the trailing digit window of any irrational α — the Ostrowski windows, Zeckendorf the golden one — ×m and ⌊n/m⌋ are continuous iff Lipschitz. On the ring completion of base b an integer-linear map is 1-Lipschitz outright, and floor division is the residue's visibility: rad v | rad b makes n mod v locally constant, otherwise some prime of v has a residue no trailing cell settles and the division tears at every point with bounded spread. On the odometer completion of an irrational's window the cells are the intervals of a circle rotation's coding, with endpoints on the backward orbit {−jα}; a continuous ×m would descend to z ↦ mz on the circle, which tears exactly where a point that is no cell endpoint meets one — at the address (1 − α)/m, while the mirror (1 + α)/m, whose image is no endpoint, prints continuity — and ⌊n/m⌋ reads two invisibles, the residue and the branch of nα mod m, every class dense in every cell, torn everywhere. The middle is inhabited even so, by design: reading every other digit of a base-2 string (Reading) has least lookahead exactly t − 1 at depth t; carried to Zeckendorf it dies, extract-and-pack-down falling to the same down-carry that kills doubling, while a stretcher — constant on each “lowest nonzero digit at k” cell and sent to lowest nonzero digit ⌈k/2⌉ — is continuous and Lipschitz at no constant at every window. Compression enters the middle only where carries go up, the stretcher everywhere, arithmetic nowhere. Three shapes so far, then: ring (base b: all multiplication continuous, division gated), odometer (units and the successor only), and discrete (the next block, vacuous) — and the atlas below shows the shapes do not stop at three.

Scope. Theorem at the ring and odometer shapes, every base and every irrational α; the discrete shape holds it trivially. The scans are its independent instruments — the cap gap, deepest realized agreement minus the least lookahead at fixed depth, bounded under discontinuity and growing under continuity, read against ranges indexed by depth — pinning ×2, ×3 and ⌊n/2⌋ at four continued-fraction windows and every tested integer map beyond the successor at four quadratic windows, the base-10 divisions splitting at rad v | rad b with no third behaviour; the every-other-digit column exact at every scanned range; its Zeckendorf fate an observation at scanned frames; the stretcher a property. At the torus shape the verdict is a rule at scanned scope, degree 3 only.

The positional control property

The golden positional window isolates the axis. Base-φ digits — n a finite sum of powers φk over k ∈ Z, digits {0, 1}, no two adjacent 1s — have no bottom position, so a trailing cell is agreement at every position ≤ a cutoff c. That window is rigid: the conjugation φ ↦ −1/φ fixes every integer and bounds a cell's diameter by φ1−c, so cells are singletons from cutoff 1 up, consecutive pairs only at cutoff 0, every cell finite. The completion adds no new points, and no gate question exists. Infinite trailing cells are bought by a non-unit base's index or by non-positional cells; the golden positional base has neither.

Scope. The rigidity a property, by the conjugation bound; the cutoff-0 collision census — consecutive pairs only — a rule below 5000.

The tower above the odometer

The Pisot tower theorem

The trailing d-bonacci window — qk = qk−1 + ⋯ + qk−d, greedy digits {0, 1}, no d consecutive 1s — refuses the ring at every degree, by one proof uniform in d. The down-carry 2qk = qk+1 + qk−d doubles the step-(d + 1) comb TK = qd + q2d+1 + ⋯ + qK to a step-d comb whose bottom cycles through d phases with period d(d + 1) in the top tooth, so d image limit points, pairwise distinct at a digit ≤ d − 2, sit over one input limit (the inputs agreeing to depth K + d + 1), and ×2 has no continuous extension. Zeckendorf's two phases and Tribonacci's three are the d = 2, 3 rows. Each d-bonacci window completes almost one-to-one over a translation of the (d − 1)-torus, so the odometer is the degree-2 member of a tower of torus translations: every floor above it is neither a ring, nor cut by a circle rotation's coding, nor discrete. At degree 3 the gate scan prints the two-class verdict, the successor Lipschitz and ×2, ×3, ⌊n/2⌋, ⌊n/3⌋ torn with no middle signature; past ×2 at d ≥ 4 nothing is scanned, since 3qk keeps a copy at k and the comb move does not transplant.

Scope. Theorem at every degree d ≥ 2, no range cap; the four-phase instance verified at degree 4 and the generic table at degrees 5 and 6. The torus factors are classical (Rauzy at degree 3; the d-bonacci substitution is Arnoux–Rauzy and has pure discrete spectrum, Berthé–Steiner–Thuswaldner). The no-middle verdict beyond ×2 is a rule at scanned scope at degree 3 (agreement depth 17 below 200000), unscanned above; the arithmetic middle over the torus is measured, not proved, its cut locus being the Rauzy partition's fractal boundary and not a countable orbit.

Off the spine

A window is a Parry word, the expansion of 1 in its base β; its places are seeded by the admissible language — qk = t1qk−1 + ⋯ + tkq0 + 1 below the word's length, the plain recurrence above — which is what makes qk one more than the largest admissible sum below k. A Pisot base is an algebraic integer above 1 whose other conjugates all lie inside the unit circle. The d-bonacci tower is the all-ones line, the spine, through the legal words; off it the shape of the witness changes before the shape of the completion does.

The second tooth theorem

At the Narayana window (qk = qk−1 + qk−3, the language 1-indices at least 3 apart) the down-carry has a second tooth, 2qk = qk+1 + qk−2 + qk−7, so every comb step s = 3..30 converges to one input limit while the image limit-point count runs 1, 2 or 3 by step: the phase count is the witness's, not the window's. The spine's implicit selector — the image a single-step comb above its bottom — picks s = 7 alone, where the image is a step-3 comb with three phases of period 21 in K, by a cascade: the carry's low tooth lands adjacent to the image's top tooth, and qj + qj+1 = qj+2 + qj−3 hands the adjacency down the comb. ×2 has no continuous extension there; the count is the bottom tooth's orbit length, exhibited and not derived from the degree.

Scope. The cascade at s = 7 a theorem; the second-tooth carry a rule, proved; the language exhaustive to index 18; the per-step counts a rule at scanned scope.

The cyclotomic ghost rule

At the word 1001 (x⁴ = x³ + 1, irreducible, Pisot) the selector picks s = 15, the image is a step-9 comb and the count is 3 — order 4, image step 9, count 3, three numbers that had never disagreed. At seven of the eight selecting binary windows the count is img/gcd(img, s*); the exception is 10001, the plastic number, measuring 15 where that predicts 5 with least period 210. x⁵ − x⁴ − 1 = (x² − x + 1)(x³ − x − 1), so the seeded places are a Pisot part plus a periodic part of period 6 and amplitude 3/7, 7 the two factors' resultant, which β and the completion's torus cannot see; it reaches the arithmetic as an integer, 2qk − qk+2 − qk−5 running 0, 1, 1, 0, −1, −1 by k mod 6, and 15 = lcm(5, 3) and 210 = 7·lcm(5, 6) are its numbers. The ghost criterion: a Parry cofactor is never contracting — Kronecker leaves it cyclotomic or with a second root outside the circle — and the ghost on a simple cyclotomic factor Φm vanishes iff m divides the word's length.

Scope. The ghost criterion a property, proved, 505 of 505 words with a cyclotomic cofactor to length 9; the seeded decomposition a rule exact to k = 399, the integer ghost to k = 299; that a comb's count reads its ghost is 10001's alone, an observation — at 205 other ghost-carrying selector-read words the count is 1, and at 111100111 and 111101011 the ghost's period is absent from the orbit outright.

The fifth shape observation

The selector is silent at 13 of the 21 legal binary words to length 6 — every all-ones word speaks, every mixed word is silent — and the silence is the instrument's: the refusal needs only a family converging to one input limit whose images take two values at a bounded digit, and with the single-step-image demand dropped every one of the 13 carries admissible progression combs with some step separating at depth 4. So ×2 has no continuous extension at any legal binary window to length 6. Ten of the 13 have a measured least period in K, from 6 to 51; at 10101 the carry has three low teeth, 2qk = qk+1 + qk−2 + qk−9 + qk−11, which is why no single cascade closes there. And the count parts from the separation: 100001, 101001 and 110101 print every depth-80 prefix distinct at tails 120, 240 and 480 while the controls saturate (Tribonacci 3, 3, 3; 10101 5, 5, 5), so those three carry unboundedly many image limit points over one input limit — the infinite-fibre row of the atlas, of which the torus row is the finite case, hidden until now because a comb image has finitely many phases by construction. Their low digits settle even so: the separation is decided where the count is not.

Scope. Observation at scanned scope, the scope the binary alphabet: legal words to length 6, comb steps 3..40, separations over 120 samples per word, periods searched to 100. Off that alphabet the count is 1 at 169 of 172 words carrying a digit ≥ 2 (the exceptions 2002, 20002, 200002 count 2), so the instrument reports nothing there. The unboundedness is a distinguishing sweep and not a demonstration: no cascade theorem is derived for any silent window. The 10101 carry is a rule, derived and canonical at every k from 11 to 60.

The hypothesis law of the fifth shape rule

A window's Parry polynomial is the characteristic polynomial of the recurrence its word seeds, with the base β its largest root. Read against the count above — a window settles when its depth-80 prefix count saturates across the tails 120, 240 and 480, and tracks its tail when every sample is distinct at every tail — the 125 legal binary words to length 9 sort in two moves. Where the Parry polynomial is exactly the minimal polynomial of a Pisot number, the count settles, 39 of 39: that is the hypothesis of Frougny's theorem read to the letter — normalization is then a finite automaton — and the doubled comb's digits are that rewriting of the digit 2 placed on the comb's positions; that the automaton makes the low digits eventually periodic in K is argued through the automaton's left- and right-sequential decomposition and not proved here. The fifth shape therefore needs the hypothesis to fail — a root other than β outside the unit circle, or a root-of-unity factor beside a Pisot minimal polynomial — and every tail-tracking window is one of those, 125 of 125. Where the hypothesis fails nothing read decides: of the 62 non-Pisot windows 38 track and 23 settle, of the 24 ghost windows 2 track (1001001 and 10100101, to tail 1920) and 22 settle, and neither the wild root's size (all settle below 1.020, all track above 1.048, mixed between) nor whether 2 has a finite expansion in the base sorts them.

Scope. The settled count at the hypothesis a rule at scanned scope, 39 of 39, its periodicity argued and not proved; the fifth shape occurring only outside the hypothesis a rule in range, 125 of 125 words to length 9; everything off the hypothesis observation at that scope, and open — what the doubled comb's rewriting needs beyond a Pisot base is derived for no window. Frougny's theorem and its converse are classical.
