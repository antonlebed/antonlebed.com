Forgetting

When “this state provably no longer contains X” is a designable property of a system that still works: one object grading how thoroughly a state forgets, what keeping the system working costs that grade, and where robust forgetting stops being purchasable at all.

The systems here are small grown worlds. A state is a positive integer, a move multiplies it by a member of that state's menu — the moves the state admits — and a route is the sequence of moves from the start state 1, its product being the state it ends at. A world is read at a temperature β: a move m is taken with probability proportional to m−β, normalized by the state's normalizer, the sum of m−β over its menu. A route's probability is the product of its moves' weights divided by the product of the normalizers it passed through — and since the moves' product is the endpoint, two routes reaching one endpoint differ only in those interior normalizers. Everything about which route was taken lives in what the intermediate states could have done otherwise.

Three worlds carry the census, an exhaustive fiber-by-fiber read, exact in rationals throughout. The depth column takes the constant menu {2, 3} at every state; plain breadth takes the squarefree moves 2..30 coprime to the state; and the tuned amnesiac is a hand-built world — menu 1 → {2, 3}, 2 → {3, 5, 15}, 3 → {2, 10} — whose menus were solved to make one endpoint's routes equiprobable at a named temperature (the one-way design). They are read to ages 5, 3 and 2, at β ∈ {1, 2, 3}, with any search over partitions capped at 8 routes per fiber; that is the census scope every scope line below names. A world free to reuse a prime it already holds recycles; one every move of which is coprime to the state it acts on cannot.

The certificate's data is three things and a family. A forgotten datum X, any function of history — the route itself, the first move, the multiset of moves. A state map Φ, carrying a history to the still-working present. The weight family, here the temperatures. The fiber of a state is the set of histories Φ sends to it, and the posterior of X there is its conditional distribution on that fiber. What a state keeps is what Φ keeps — the dated endpoint, the value reached together with the number of moves, or the age alone.

The certificate

The four grades property

What a state map forgets is graded at one of four grades, increasing in strength; a certificate for X at Φ is a witness at one of the three above the lowest. R, readable: X is a function of Φ, and there is no certificate. P, possibilistic: every fiber meets at least two X-classes, so X is not recoverable as a value; the strongest witness for it is a state-side factoring, a presented split of the history space into X and a remainder with Φ constant in the X slot, which needs no weight at all. T, tuned-flat: at one named temperature the posterior of X is uniform on every fiber's consistent values. S, robust-flat: uniform at every weight in the family. Flatness is fiber-uniformity — maximum entropy behind what the living state logically pins, never posterior = prior, since a grown world's state is its present and always informs its past possibilistically. It upgrades to posterior = prior for every prior exactly when the state-side factoring is total, and that is an annotation on grade S, never its base clause.

Scope. Definitions, with the upgrade clause proved from them.

The two structural witnesses theorem

Grade S is bought two ways, and the two forgetting specimens here sit there by different witnesses, one lemma apart. Inheritance: where the history space splits as X against a remainder, the weight a product of a weight-free prior on X and a weight on the remainder, and Φ reads only the remainder, the posterior of X equals its prior at every state and every weight, with no tuning. Destination universality is grade S by that witness: the adapted reader's state is a function of its metabolism — the resources it is given to spend — and never of the data it is shown, which is the factoring. Weight-side symmetry: where the multiset of interior normalizers agrees along every route of a fiber, the route posterior is uniform at every β — route weight is proportional to the product of the reciprocals of those normalizers, the moves' weights cancelling against the endpoint, and equal multisets have equal products. The depth column is grade S by that witness, every normalizer in it being the same polynomial, the witness present at every one of its mixed fibers.

Scope. Both lemmas proved, scope-free. The destination-universality row enters by inheritance together with the factoring measured on the reader side, which holds under every loss that reads only what the reader has committed to and fails outside that family.

Witness collapse theorem

The obvious alternative witness collapses onto the possibilistic grade: a quotient through which Φ factors, each of whose classes meets every X-class, exists if and only if every Φ-fiber meets every X-class. So “factors through a quotient killing X” is extensionally the FULL-SPREAD case of grade P — every fiber meeting every class rather than merely two — and a split witness adds provenance, its weight-freeness, and never extension.

Scope. Proved, scope-free.

The two clauses are independent observation

A certificate carries a structural clause — is there a witness? — and a measure clause — is the posterior flat? Neither implies the other, and both crossings print. Structural kill without flatness: at all 363 of breadth's multi-route fibers the state factors through the quotient that remembers which moves were made and not the order they came in, so the ORDER is possibilistically dead at every one of them — and the posterior still reads that order through the normalizers at β = 1, with zero of the 363 flat there. Flatness without any structural witness: the tuned world's endpoint 6 at age 2 is flat at β = 1 with the witness absent, the signature of tuning — it buys one temperature, and at the tuned world's age-2 fibers provably no other. The grade tables land whole, with zero fibers the four grades cannot express: R 3 and T 2 in the tuned world, R 10 and S 10 in the depth column, R 18 and P 363 in breadth, 406 fibers in all. And what a world forgets is fiber-local: the tuned world hides the ORDER of a determined move set at endpoint 6 and hides the move SET at endpoint 30 ({2, 15} against {3, 10}, each admitting exactly one order).

Scope. Exhaustive at the census scope, the datum being the route. The crossing in the remaining direction — robust flatness with no witness — occurs at no fiber here; whether it can occur at all is the rogue world below. Toy scale.

The count leak rule

A certificate for X does not descend to functions of X. Take the depth column, route-uniform at every temperature with the weight-side witness present — perfect symmetry, the strongest grade there is. The posterior of the FIRST MOVE at the endpoint 2a3b is nonetheless exactly (a/(a+b), b/(a+b)) — independent of β, and a leak. Route-uniformity does not coarsen to feature-uniformity, because a uniform posterior counted over unequal coarse classes is not uniform. Two leak channels stand named: the WEIGHT leak, where the measure itself is asymmetric — breadth's footprint — and the COUNT leak, where only the geometry of the fiber is. Every coarsening needs its own flatness clause.

Scope. The a/(a+b) law proved for the two-move constant menu — equal route weights with binomial fiber counting — and verified at every mixed fiber to age 5; other menus were not scanned. The two-channel reading is an observation.

The grade read at a second consumer observation

The same grades read a system that is not a grown world: a family of readers adapted to a data stream under a loss, where the datum X is the row — which map and which stream the reader was shown, nine rows in all — and the state map is the adapted state. The data door is where a decision-valued prediction loss breaks the inheritance witness at the first scored bit, and what the break leaves is graded per state map, because the door holds two presents over one history. The individual — one adapted state — loses the witness and with it the posterior = prior annotation; at the full loss its fibers are almost never row-exclusive, 31 of its 38 spread over several rows, so value recovery is mostly blocked. The ensemble — the optimum SET, a present no single state holds — never inherited the annotation: its partition of the rows under the baseline loss has three blocks, one holding a single row, and that block is READABLE at the datum and at its coarsening, the stream, both. Three further facts are the laws above firing here. Where Φ is a function of X, flatness is forced and only spread is open: the posterior on such a fiber is the weight restricted, so at the uniform weight every row-spread fiber is flat — 2 of 2, 3 of 3 and 31 of 31 — and nothing is measured. What grades is the COARSENING, and the count leak fires there: read on the stream inside the row the same fibers give 1 of 2, 2 of 3 and 15 of 28, the ensemble's five-row block flat over rows at 1/5 five times and tilted over its streams at 2/5 against 1/5, one stream arriving under two maps and its neighbours under one. And where Φ is a relation, nothing is determined: a row carries 2 to 32 co-optima, so “the adapted state” needs a selection, and the same 28 fibers read 0 flat under a uniform choice among co-optima, 15 with no selection factor, 2 of 3 under a fixed tie-break. A grade over equals is a statement about the selection rule.

Scope. Exact and exhaustive on the frozen arena — 9 rows, 100 policies, 38 behaviour classes, horizon 120 — read at the uniform weight. Grade S is refused at this door: it fixes its loss to ask its question, and the losses are what index the weight family elsewhere, so every reading is T or not at one named weight. Toy scale.

Forgetting while the system still works

A still-working system must RETAIN a required function g of its history while certifying that it no longer holds X — the working clause. Φ computes g exactly when g factors through Φ — when Φ's partition of the history space refines g's — so the admissible state maps are exactly the interval from g to the partition into single histories, and the question is whether that interval holds a partition that is spread (every block meets at least two X-classes) and flat — a cure. It decomposes fiber by fiber, and the price of a cure is the number of blocks it takes, summed over fibers. Where X is a function of g there is nothing to design: the state is readable.

Two-class conservation rule

On a g-fiber carrying exactly two X-classes, of masses m₁ and m₂, every spread block holds both classes and flatness forces their two masses equal inside each block; summing over blocks gives m₁ = m₂. So a two-class fiber is curable only where Φ = g is already flat: refinement never cures a two-class leak. The count leak is therefore UNREMOVABLE while the dated endpoint is retained — deleting a record's attribute while keeping the record runs into a parity-style obstruction no state design crosses.

Scope. Proved. Its instances at the census scope, in the depth column with the dated endpoint retained and X the first move: every fiber whose two exponents differ leaks, 8 of 20, the equal-exponent ones cure at price 1 and the pure powers are readable.

The no-majority criterion criterion

On a uniform-weight g-fiber whose X-class counts are c₁ ≥ ⋯ ≥ cr totalling N, a spread flat partition exists if and only if c₁ ≤ N − c₁ — if and only if no class holds a strict majority. Necessity survives the weighted case as a MASS-majority test, which is partition-free and so runs at any fiber size, however far past exhaustive search. Sufficiency is exact subset-mass matching and is NOT claimed weighted, and the distance between the two, the matching gap, is where the real price sits.

Scope. Proved at uniform weight, and cross-validated against exhaustive partition search on all 49 uniform fibers inside the census cap, with zero mismatches.

Retaining less exposes more rule

Retention is not monotone the way admissibility is. Conditioning on a dated endpoint cancels the move weights in the numerator, the moves' product being the endpoint itself; conditioning on the AGE alone surfaces them. In the depth column the posterior of first move 2 at an age fiber is exactly 3β/(2β + 3β) — 3/5 at β = 1, 27/35 at β = 3 — a strict majority at every temperature, and a mass majority is partition-free, so every age fiber leaks whatever state map is built over it; the tuned world's age-2 posterior reads 3/5 against 2/5 the same way. A coarser retained function admits MORE state maps and certifies FEWER.

Scope. The mechanism proved; the instances exhaustive at the census scope. Toy scale.

The laws read masses and counts only property

Neither of the two proofs above reads a menu, a prime or a temperature: the data is a finite set with a positive weight, a colouring by X, and a partition drawn from the interval above g, and every step reads class MASSES and COUNTS and never a fiber's elements. The working clause is a fact about a finite weighted partition lattice, and grown worlds enter only by SUPPLYING the weights. That supply is the cost of any port. Descent in reader space hands over the partition but reads only the ORDER of its losses, invariant under any monotone reparameterization, so a Boltzmann family built over them fixes masses the learner never sees. And under a prior from outside — the inheritance witness's split — two-class conservation applies verbatim: a retained function's fiber carrying two data-classes of unequal prior mass admits no refinement both spread and flat, an obstruction from prior imbalance alone.

Scope. Read off the two proofs; nothing here is measured.

The strict working amnesiac observation

What the laws above price is curability; what they leave open is whether refinement ever STRICTLY beats the coarsest working state. It does, rarely and cheaply. In the depth column with the dated endpoint retained and X the first TWO moves, five fibers cure strictly: the coarsest working state Φ = g leaks, and a refinement of it is spread and flat at every temperature. The specimen is exponents (3, 1), class counts (2, 1, 1) with baseline posterior (½, ¼, ¼), cured at price 2 by pairing the two singletons; the verdict table reads 8 readable, 8 cured (5 of them strictly), 2 leaking. Partition design alone buys a robust certificate the coarse state lacks. The tuned grade, by contrast, is refinement-stable: the tuned world's two-history fibers have trivial partition intervals, so what tuning bought refinement cannot extend and what tuning missed it cannot add.

Scope. Exhaustive at the census scope. Toy scale.

A witness-free tie closes a strict amnesiac property

The ties a strict cure rides need not be symmetric. Write x for 2−β. A designed world whose every move is a power of 2 — menus 1: (2, 4, 8, 16), 2: (4, 8), 4: (4, 8), 8: (8), 16: (8, 16), 64: (2, 4), 128: (2) — has every normalizer a 0/1 polynomial in x, and closes its dated fiber at endpoint 256, age 3, at exactly four routes. Two of them tie by EQUAL normalizer multisets, the weight-side witness's shape; the other two tie by the identity x³·(x + x²) = (x³ + x⁴)·x, with multisets {x³, x + x²} and {x³ + x⁴, x} that DIFFER — a witness-free tie, the regrouping of one factor multiset that Menu collisions counts as the generic kind. The two tie classes' whole-fiber products, x⁵(1 + x)² against x⁴(1 + x), differ by x(1 + x) and never by 1, so Φ = g leaks at every β and the pairing refinement is spread and flat at every β: a strict working amnesiac at grade S in a multi-route, order-carrying world.

Scope. The tie proved as an identity; the fiber census of the one world exact.

Breadth is a tie desert observation

Across every multi-route fiber of plain breadth the interior-normalizer product is INJECTIVE at β = 1 — not one pair of routes carries equal products in the 363 fibers at ages up to 3, nor in the 441 at age 4, the largest holding 168 routes — hence no pair ties across all temperatures at once, and no working state hides the route anywhere at scope. For X the first move, of the 291 fibers inside the search cap 173 die by mass majority and 118 pass necessity yet admit no exact-tie partition, the matching gap; none cures. Partition design manufactured no tie anywhere it searched, and no design puts an all-temperature tie into breadth at any age, by the quarantine theorem below.

Scope. Exhaustive at the census scope, the tie scan run one age deeper in breadth. The 72 breadth fibers above the cap carry no all-temperature mass majority — the partition-free test runs at any size — so they stay open rather than decidably leaking. At every temperature at once the desert is a theorem; at a single temperature it is a measurement, injective through age 4 and unsearched beyond. Toy scale.

The ambient trichotomy pattern

The matching gap is not local to these worlds. Three places state ONE primitive — given k weights, which elements of an ambient group are subset sums, and does a named target land in that image — and differ only in the AMBIENT, which decides the answer's shape. The matching gap has positive real masses in a continuum, and its deficit is total: at real weights the subset sums are injective, 2N distinct of 2N at N = 10, 16 and 20, so an exact hit is a coincidence. The naming criterion has residues in a cyclic group of s slots with no ends, and its deficit is positional noise that vanishes past a logarithmic threshold. The subset spectrum has the primes themselves in a bounded interval of T + 1 slots with thin ends, and its deficit is six values at the ends, permanent. One discriminator runs across the three, 2k against the number of SLOTS the sums can land in, and what varies the slot count is the ORDER of the ambient. The two bounded faces agree on a coverage fraction at k = 7, 53 of 59 against 37 of 41, from mechanisms with nothing in common — exactly the misreading the identification rules out. The transfer that holds is the other one: quantize the masses to W levels and the gap becomes a threshold on the naming face's own logarithmic line — the smallest N at which half the trials hit an exact target tracks log₂(N·W) to within 1.4 across the sampled range, 5 against 6.32 at W = 16, 15 against 15.91 at W = 4096. The desert is what a fine enough supply produces on its own, and the tying worlds the transfer predicts exist exactly where it says, in a coarse, recycled-prime supply — the witness-free tie above.

Scope. A pattern over N = 2..24 and W = 2..4096, 400 trials per cell. The transfer reads the working clause's FEASIBILITY primitive — whether a subset hits an exact target mass — and not the full spread-and-flat search, so it prices the gap without closing it; the single-temperature desert past age 4 stays a measurement.

What robust forgetting costs

That leaves the obstruction the certificate opened: does robust flatness FORCE a structural witness? Two routes flat at every temperature means their interior-normalizer PRODUCTS agree as functions of β; a weight-side witness means the two MULTISETS agree. So the question is whether equal products force equal factors — and it settles both ways on one axis, coprimality. In a coprime world no dated multi-route fiber is flat at every temperature at any age: the quarantine theorem, so there the answer is yes, vacuously. A world free to recycle its own primes is the other half.

The rogue world property

Write x for 2−β. The designed world with menus 1: {2, 32}, 2: {2, 4, 8}, 16: {2, 16}, 32: {2, 4} and 128: {2, 8, 32} closes its dated fiber at endpoint 256, age 3, at EXACTLY two routes, (2, 8, 16) and (32, 4, 2), with posterior (½, ½) at every β and DISTINCT interior-normalizer multisets: grade S with the witness absent is designable. Flatness holds by the polynomial identity (x + x² + x³)(x + x⁴) = (x + x²)(x + x³ + x⁵), the cyclotomic regrouping of (1 + x)(1 + x + x²)(1 − x + x²): the factor 1 − x + x² cannot stand alone among polynomials with nonnegative coefficients and so admits two factorizations around it, the classical source of non-unique factorization in such polynomials wearing menu clothes. A smaller sibling at endpoint 128 holds the two orderings (32, 2, 2) and (2, 2, 32) of ONE move multiset — the permuted shape coprimality kills, alive here because the moves recycle the prime 2. Age 2 stays barred, uniqueness of Dirichlet polynomials forcing the two menus to coincide; the collision needs products of pairs, hence age 3.

Scope. An exact construction, its fiber closed at two routes and the identity checked as written. The quarantine theorem's proof-independent cross-check: 0 coprime realizations of any flat two-route fiber across all 4,849 colliding menu pairs of the census on Menu collisions, against 4,805 realizations for worlds free to recycle.

The three doors to exact amnesia rule

Exact amnesia — a route posterior uniform on its fiber — is bought exactly three ways. SYMMETRY: constant menus, the depth column, β-free. TUNING: one temperature, the tuned amnesiac. RECYCLED-PRIME MENU DESIGN: every β, the rogue world. The two all-temperature doors both need recycled primes — coprimality forbids a constant menu, and the quarantine theorem bars coprime all-β flatness outright — and tuning is the coprime-compatible door, buying one temperature only. So many-window growth, where every move is coprime to its state, admits ONLY the tuned door: no all-temperature amnesia there, at any age. Menu design manufactures the exact ties partition design never found, but robustly, at every β, only with recycled primes.

Scope. The chart is complete by the quarantine theorem and the three constructions; each door's instance is exact.

verifiers: explore_rogue_world.py, explore_forgetting_certificate.py

Retention is what prices the certificate. Two-class conservation freezes the count leak, the no-majority criterion prices what is left, and the numerator effect shows that a certificate is not monotone in what is kept — a system holding less of its own history does not thereby forget more of it. And the boundary of robust forgetting is neither age nor the order of the moves but prime recycling: a world whose menus never reuse its own primes cannot help writing its history into its weights. Read as a deletion guarantee — a record removed under a certificate rather than under a retraining story — the price line is that certified forgetting costs prime recycling, and that certifying the erasure of a record never certifies the erasure of its attributes.

How much room the recycling door leaves is the one question the price line does not answer, and it is a question about unique factorization: equal PRODUCTS of interior normalizers against equal MULTISETS, the door's width being the gap between the two. Menu collisions carries it, from collisions so generic that almost none of them is a failure of factorization at all down to a bound on how far a genuine failure can escape one variable.

The worlds turned outward

Because every posterior above is an exact rational, these worlds also serve as a measuring instrument for tools that estimate such a posterior from samples: what a deletion audit targets — the optimal success of an attribute or membership adversary holding the released state — is a functional of exactly that posterior, and the count leak is the dial, the exponents a and b placing the first-move posterior at any chosen rational. Two published deletion audits were scored that way, and both state a certainty that outruns the accuracy behind it — one a band that narrows with data while its coverage stays where it was, the other a probability more extreme than its exact posterior over a majority of the audited mass, a miss more data concentrates rather than cures. Deletion audits carries all of it.
