Walls

What deleting the archimedean place costs, measured level by level.

  • The size wall — no finite window reads size: the locality criterion, the hiding laws, the exact comparator and its cost law
  • Number systems — index transforms, the Zech wall, and designed towers
  • Meaning — the ring as a live medium: exact binding and its tolls
  • The dual pole — the mirror deletion: floating point as the dual rung
  • The order wall — sign's minimal machine counted exactly for every finite digit set, and the algebra above it
  • The division algebras — Cayley–Dickson climbed over one channel, and where it floors

One question runs through the whole section: through which residue window can an operation be read? A rung of the primorial tower is Z/N with N the product of the first k primes (The Object); the residue mod p is the rung's channel at p, a reduction at one finite place of Q, and a window is a quotient Z/M for a divisor M of N, the part of the ring that reduction mod M reads. Every finite place is kept and the archimedean one, where size lives, is deleted. The walls measure that deletion and its echoes one recoding down, and every known escape is priced. Three walls fail the question in three different modes. Size is the deleted window itself: no finite window reads it, an information obstruction. The Zech wall meets the recoding of a channel by a discrete logarithm, which turns × into + and leaves the addition table incompatible with every nontrivial proper quotient of the ring the indices live in, a structure obstruction. The super-logarithm, the recoding that would do to powering what the index did to multiplication, does not exist at all, an existence obstruction. The first is measured on The size wall, the second and third on Number systems. This page carries what is left once size is gone: which relation the finite windows organize in order's place, the two orders a rung carries, the ladder pinning every wall to the least structure that carries it, and whether the deletion was forced at all.

What replaces order

Over Z the order relation organizes induction, descent, Euclid and comparison, and it is the deleted place. The comparator buys it back by bolting on a channel rather than by reading the k already there. Commit instead to the residue tuples as they stand, never reconstructing the value, and ask which relation the existing windows organize. The locality criterion has a relation-shaped form: call R channel-conjunctive when it equals the conjunction of its own channel projections, the unique minimal conjunctive candidate.

The conjunctive split rule

Equality and divisibility pass, divisibility's conjunct at p being “yp = 0 or xp ≠ 0”. Order, cyclic betweenness and the grading ord(x) | ord(y) on multiplicative orders fail, in two ways. Order and betweenness fail maximally: every channel projection saturates, so the channels retain nothing. The grading fails properly: the residue pair (3, 1) at channel 7 occurs in no true pair, because covering an order 6 depends on the orders the other channels have available; its onset is rung 3, a shared-torsion fact about products of two abelian groups. Cyclifying buys nothing: btw(0, a, b) holds exactly when a < b, so the ternary cyclic relation carries the whole size wall. A second axis, the consumption partition, per channel the coarsest partition through which R factors, sharpens the split: divisibility reads one bit per channel, the valuation; the grading reads the order class and conflates 0 with 1; order and betweenness need the discrete partition at every channel, no per-channel compression at all.

Scope. Exhaustive at Z/210, the ternary relations and the consumption partitions at Z/30.

Location refined by torsion rule

The survivors collapse to one lattice. Divisibility is support reverse-inclusion, x | y iff supp y ⊆ supp x, the support being the set of channels where an element is nonzero, and Green's J-preorder on the multiplicative monoid is support inclusion: the lattice of idempotents, one eS per set S of channels, 1 on S and 0 off it, read in opposite directions. The grading is the powering period, the lcm of per-channel orders, a coarse invariant strictly between the conjunctive class and the wall; in the limit it completes to Steinitz's supernatural divisibility lattice as the idempotents complete to the power set of the primes. But it separates nothing that matters: almost every element of ∏p Fp sits at the top grade and so does every fixed integer. Only support separates Z from the bulk, and every support stratum is Haar-null. What replaces order is location, the support, refined by torsion, the grade, with the refinement degenerate on it.

Scope. The powering period exhaustive at Z/210; the two collapses algebraic; the top-grade density computed to 300 channels, its limit resting on Dirichlet plus Borel–Cantelli, the stratum measures on Mertens.

Cyclic orientation is totally hidden rule

Order and betweenness need every channel at full resolution, and this is how far that goes. A known proper subset of channels, of modulus M, pins each point of a triple to its own uniform ladder of N/M positions, and among the triples completing those three ladders both orientations occur: the fraction of cyclic orientation the known channels determine is exactly 0. Orientation is translation-invariant, so move the triple's base point to 0, where orientation is integer comparison on (0, N); each remaining ladder holds an element ≤ M and an element ≥ (c − 1)M for c = N/M ≥ 2, and pairing the extremes one way and then the other realizes both orientations. The one configuration that escapes, all three residues equal at c = 2, asks for three distinct points on a two-point ladder and holds no triple at all. Primality, squarefree- ness and the tower's own sequence of rungs are unused: one bit of cyclic orientation costs the entire complement, at every rung and at every designed tower, a ring built on chosen primes, alike.

Scope. Proved for every Z/N and every proper divisor M; ground truth over all N ≤ 72 against every proper divisor, 252 pairs, non-squarefree N included. The claim is determinacy, not independence: both orientations occurring in every class says nothing about their frequencies, and what the known channels leak statistically is the half-coverage law.

Nothing plays unbounded induction rule

At a rung, descent is rung-bounded: strict support chains have length exactly k + 1, the idempotents realizing every set of channels, and the chain of unit-group exponents λ = lcm(p − 1), λ(λ), … has height 4 at Z/510510, so recursion depth is a constant of the ring, not a function of the element, and support-descent recursions are bounded loops. Euclid splits along the same seam. The gcd ideal is a one-step support read, (x, y) generated by the idempotent of supp x ∪ supp y, while Euclid's loop has no analogue in the ring: the sizeless remainder x(1 − esupp y) enters a 2-cycle and never terminates. Division with remainder died with the deleted place and only its purpose survives. In the limit both surviving lattices, support and grade, have infinite descending chains, so well-founded descent does not survive either. The trade against Z is exact: Z is well-founded with unbounded recursion depth, the tower bounded-depth at every rung with no well-foundedness in the limit.

Scope. Chain lengths attained k ≤ 12; the gcd ideal exhaustive over all 900 pairs of Z/30; the non-termination is a witness. The bounded-loop shape is Meyer–Ritchie's.

Two orders

Two orders do live on a rung, and neither is the integer's. The canonical cycle is the map n ↦ n + 1, the ring walked as the diagonal flow on its torus (Classical structure); a cycle t is the map n ↦ n + t for a unit t, and the ring carries exactly φ(N) translation-invariant cyclic orders, one per unit. The coupling order is an order on the channels, 2 first, then 3, 5, …, the order in which their moduli enter the tower. What each is made of is the last thing the deletion prices.

The coupling order picks the canonical cycle criterion

The canonical cycle embeds Z/N in the circle at angle n/N, and that angle is a sum of channel-local phases, n/N ≡ Σp (np wp mod p)/p (mod 1): the partial-fraction identity read order-side, and the φ(N) cyclic orders are exactly its φ(N) choices of a unit weight per channel, combined by mod-1 addition, the one cross-channel shape the conjunctive test cannot express. Cycle t rotates channel p at speed (t mod p)/p; call t speed-monotone when those speeds fall along the coupling order. Then t = 1 is the unique speed-monotone cycle iff 2 is among the primes and no gap doubles, pi+1 < 2pi throughout: the channel at 2 anchors the speed at 1/2 and the chain inequality forces t ≡ 1 down the chain; without the anchor the cycle with residues (2, 1, …, 1) is also speed-monotone, and at a doubled gap so is the one with residue 2 at the gap. Primorial rungs pass both legs, the anchor by construction and the gaps by Bertrand's postulate, so at every rung the coupling order singles out the sieve's own cycle; the designed set {3, 7} has six speed-monotone cycles.

Scope. Proved; the angle identity exhaustive at Z/210, the bijection at Z/30; the criterion swept over all 439 prime subsets of the first ten primes with N ≤ 40000, witnesses re-verified on every failing set. The passing sets include non-prefixes such as {2, 3, 5, 7, 13}: the criterion is gap-shaped, not primorial.

Time is the deleted place, coupling distance the profinite metric rule

Channel p crosses zero at the multiples of p in every cycle, so the crossing pattern of the flow is the sieve of Eratosthenes, shared by all φ(N) cyclic orders; only the braid of phases between crossings separates them, and a two-channel crossing word is a Christoffel word of slope p/q, the full word the cutting sequence of the torus diagonal. Symmetrized elapsed time along the canonical cycle is circular size |x − y|, the archimedean metric in dynamical dress, and the thresholded relation |x − y| ≤ d fails the conjunctive test at every 1 ≤ d < N/2, properly while 2d + 1 is below the largest prime and maximally from there on. Agreement depth along the coupling order, the number of leading channels two elements share, is instead an ultrametric whose balls are exactly the cosets of the tower ideals (pm#): the finite-rung profinite metric, whose limit is the native topology of the limit object. The two orders are the two sides of Ostrowski, one pricing in the archimedean triangle inequality and one in the ultrametric.

Scope. The skeleton and the braid exhaustive at Z/30; the threshold modes exhaustive in d at Z/30, the boundary confirmed at Z/210; the ultrametric and its balls exhaustive at Z/210.

The provenance ladder

Which of these obstructions are the tower's own? Almost none: each wall, pinned to the least structure that carries it, sits at or below the squarefree floor.

The provenance ladder rule
  • A cyclic group with a proper quotient, no product, no field. Size and sign hiding, orientation hiding, and the channel quantifier [a = 0], which no polynomial computes on any composite N while at one field it is Fermat's 1 − ap−1, the wall vanishing.
  • A single field. The Zech wall; the type obstruction, xy ill-defined on residue pairs with p − 2 witnesses; the floors of the division ladder; the super-logarithm's existence grades (Number systems).
  • A product of two or more coprime windows, fields unneeded. Order's and betweenness's maximal conjunctive failure: Z/6 and the non-field Z/36 fail identically.
  • A product of two abelian groups, no ring at all. The grading's proper failure, whose least carrier is C2 × C4 = U(15), so on the tower the grading wall first appears at rung 3.
  • A product of fields, squarefree. The locality criterion itself, Z/4 the boundary; the identity xλ+1 = x, which fails at 2 in Z/4; the meadow inverse and everything riding it, division total by a pseudo-inverse x′ with x·x′·x = x and 0′ = 0 at every element.
  • The primorial trajectory. One result, a selection rather than an obstruction: the coupling order's choice of the canonical cycle.

No wall needs the trajectory: the hiding is generic to any coarsening of any cyclic group, and the deletion names which window is missing. The mirror chart, capabilities pinned up to their birth rungs and permanent because channels only accumulate, is the genesis ladder.

Scope. Every row is a rule pinned to the least carrier it names, by the cited proof's own hypotheses or by exhibiting the wall on the smaller structure; the grading's pairs exhaustive for all group orders n ≤ 10 plus the order-8 cases.

What a wall-free design would need

A wall in the design at hand is no evidence that the wall is necessary, so the archimedean one was put to the other question: is there a substrate holding both of the capabilities the deletion trades between? Flat-role independence is what the CRT windows give, channels carrying roles with no channel's read depending on where any other sits, order-invariant by construction. A native positional read is the other: a magnitude built of digits, a digit's place being its weight, the structure unbounded nesting runs on. The tower has the first outright and buys the second with a borrow, a re-import of the deleted place priced on The recovery chart. Three designs asked one substrate for both at once, each made to host matched brackets nesting freely and read back.

The exclusive triple observation

At none of the three do the two capabilities co-habit, and what blocks them is resource rather than the particular ring. Keep one prime's powers as a depth register, Z/(pt · m) with m squarefree and coprime to p: flat roles survive on the squarefree part and the register natively reads a valuation and the bottom digit, but peeling a higher digit is division by p, a zero-divisor, so the digit read is never a ring operation, and a base coprime to the modulus is invertible but generates no filtration to read; the only escape is an infinite modulus, the whole deleted place. Move to the mirror pole, the floating-point number that keeps only the archimedean window (The dual pole): division by the base is native and the digits of a tree code are readable to the precision, collisions on the census trees falling to zero as the precision climbs, but the pole's only native binding is carry-coupled magnitude, which fails to decompose per role on 24 of 54 reads where the CRT binding fails on none. The two capabilities exchange across the poles. Give one algebra a second, non-commutative operation and the axis becomes branching: a factor-permuting operation separates left from right but wraps at the group order, a coordinate-mixing one separates every path but its entries grow as Fibonacci numbers and couple the roles, and the same operation over a fixed ring saturates at 6 images. Unbounded branching, fixed resource, independence kept: no cast holds all three, because a fixed resource has a finite image and cannot inject an unbounded family of paths, and an infinite image is the deleted magnitude re-imported. One priced escape is recorded and refused as a destination: a rung times a floating-point register puts flat roles in the windows and magnitude in the register, and binding one to the other crosses the CRT boundary, which is the borrow. Two substrates stapled, and the staple is the borrow.

Scope. Observation at toy scope: three casts, codes and readers hand-designed, bracket trees to length 4 at N = 214 · 105 and 22 census trees at precisions 2 to 7; the non-commutative cast swept three corners of its own design space on S3 and SL2(F2), not all finite algebras, so a genuinely different integrated algebra is open rather than impossible. One partial did land: the non-commutative operations are invertible, so undoing a nesting step, which a valuation ratchet cannot express, is recovered natively. A further design variable is spent at the other pole: redundant digits make rational scalings locally readable there, but the tower's windows have cosets for fibers, equal or disjoint, so no digit set buys them that overlap.

The deepest reading of the deletion is not a cost at all: read as an operation, the missing window is the borrow whose absence makes the tower's depth face, the moduli and their monotone growth, decidable, the knife edge on Computation.

The size wall

On a squarefree ring the channel-parallel functions are exactly the polynomial ones, and sign, comparison and overflow are none of them at any rung from the second, under every change of coordinates inside the ring. What the unknown channels hide they hide almost completely, the sign bias over a fiber at most half an element and exactly zero when an even modulus is among them, and at or below half coverage no archimedean fact about a range is certain. Every exact escape pays one of three prices; the diagonal function with one residue as tie-break is an exact comparator on any squarefree modulus set, narrower than reconstruction exactly when Σ 1/p < 1, which no primorial rung achieves, and that law grades a published comparison method and locates a masking dispute below the algebra. The size wall.

Number systems

Ask the same question of coordinates rather than of operations and the walls change kind: recoding each channel by a discrete logarithm turns × into + and leaves addition walled by a structure obstruction where size is an information one, a positional base is exactly where two readings of one digit string coincide, and the designed tower filling a 16-bit word is the ring every internet checksum runs in, with the characteristic-2 mirror beside it, where the wall keeps its information core and loses everything archimedean. Number systems.

Meaning

Run the same ring as a live medium and one minimum distance decides both halves of a symbol system built on it: any single corrupted window snaps back to its whole, and the classical superposition bundle provably never retrieves. Set it in motion and no rule made of ring operations couples two channels at all, so every coupling is bought at a price in bits. Meaning.

The second deletion

Every wall above measures one deletion, every finite place kept and the archimedean window removed. The mirror deletion keeps only the archimedean window: a floating-point number is the dual rung, its fibers hiding residue exactly where a finite window's fibers hide size. Its walls and the reading criterion that covers both poles are on The dual pole, and the one wall with a shape, sign finite-state and not finite-window, is counted on The order wall; the windows beyond the positional pair have a section of their own, Reading.

The division algebras

Climb Cayley–Dickson doubling over one channel rather than over R and division is gone by the second doubling everywhere and at four of the seven channels by the first, the classical finite-field walls total from the quaternion floor up. What the split floors keep is a multiplicative size that is channel-local, the composition norm, and at the quaternion floor the channels where the algebra stays a division ring are a design knob whose tower-visible half can have odd size, because the partner sits at the deleted place. That knob is one face of a budget priced exactly: what deleting a place buys at a global constraint is the deleted term's own local group, its torsion and its rank. The division algebras.
