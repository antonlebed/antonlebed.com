The delay line

A growing pool of windows is not memory: its whole content is the operation stream played back mod the supply. What that normal form decides, how far the residual it leaves can be certified, and the one channel neither closes.

The machine is the element face of the growth apparatus with no imported primitive: r registers, each a column over a pool of windows Z/m, with componentwise add, subtract, multiply and write-constant; one global zero-test, the AND of a register's per-window zero bits; grow, which appends a fresh window born at zero; and the frontier singleton, the register reading 1 at the freshest window and 0 at every older one, the only handle a program has on a window. The supply is the sequence mg, the modulus of the g-th window grown; its rate is o(g) below linear and Ω(g) at or above, and the canonical sublinear supply mg = max(2, ⌈√g⌉) is the sqrt pole. A bounded supply leaves the machine finite-state and a linear one makes it universal (the supply law); everything here is the band between. A rider is a counter held as a residue in one pointed window at the frontier, the window a pointer register singles out; its zero-test fires when the value wraps to 0, a fire is pending while its next true lies in the future, and the fire's time is a landing. A register is faithful when its value tracks its increment count. The inverse supply is M(d) = max{g : m(g) ≤ d}; a landing with gap d sits in the window (M(d − 1), M(d)] and its offset is the distance from the window top down to the landing. A supply is tame when the questions a decider extracts about it — is some landing ≡ a mod L — are decidable. Universality is Minsky's, cited.

The rate, and why everything caps

The phase boundary is the linear rate, and the crux is re-basing, not carry rule

A carry across two addressed windows is in the class: save the singleton as a persistent idempotent, one addressed window per register, and carry when the low digit wraps by increment-then-zero-test on the sparse low register. But a positional value Σ digiti·Wi has the moduli as its bases, and migrating a digit-window to a fresher modulus rescales the higher weights by the unknown ratio m′/m, a multiplication by an unknown the class cannot perform; only the top digit migrates cleanly. The cap lemma: a faithful value never exceeds Wd·mfrontier, with Wd the frozen product of the lower bases, a constant the program chooses. So a counter survives exactly when Wd·mg ≥ g for some fixed Wd, which is mg = Ω(g); on the sqrt pole the cap is exactly Wd², and a value-dependent grow schedule is the regress — value 100 at base 3 needs 1,089 grows before counting. Every scheme built caps on any o(g) supply by the same principle, born at zero: a fresh window carries no value information and the only native load into it is the unary transfer, bounded by one modulus. The single rider wraps at count 3; the positional counter caps by re-basing; the CRT counter, value as residues and exact while it is below the lcm, caps by lcm freeze, a grown window being born 0 and not the value's residue; and the unary support counter has no decrement, being the growth machine.

Scope. Rule on the universal side, verified at mg = ⌈g/3⌉ with the carry exact at bases 3 and 4 over counts 0 to 11; the sqrt-pole caps 4, 9, 16, 25 measured at Wd = 2 to 5. That no construction simulates two counters on an o(g) supply is conjectured: three exact schemes are ruled out, and the normal form below makes born-at-zero a theorem rather than an argument.

The decidable side is a third class, and its mechanism is bandwidth rule

The o(g) machine has unboundedly many distinct moduli — 20 after 2,000 steps and rising — so no finite column alphabet, and its registers reset, so no monotonicity; one program exhibits both and is still decided, so the class is neither finite-state nor well-structured by monotonicity. The bandwidth principle: the componentwise operations never move value between windows, so value crosses windows only through the global zero-test, one bit out, and the frontier singleton, one unit in, O(1) per operation. Two consequences, both measured: an addressed faithful value is bounded by a program constant — the top-migratable counter's faithful top digit is 3 at horizons 200 and 800 on the sqrt pole and grows 50 to 200 on the linear successor supply — and a faithful register's firing period is a program constant: the CRT register's zero-test is a pulse that fires only once growth stops, at the lcm of the frozen set (60, 420, 4,620 for 0, 1, 2 grown windows), so an unbounded firing period needs freezing at a data-dependent window count, the regress again. The counterexample face is closed: a cross-window product materializes at no window, blind pre-growth of G0 windows buys capacity ⌊√G0⌋ (11, 21, 31 at 100, 400, 900), and an unbounded period detector is the regress.

Scope. Rule for the structure and the three named schemes; that no fourth scheme exists is the conjecture above.

The normal form, and the reduction it buys

Born at zero is a theorem: the suffix-evaluation normal form theorem

Write the op word for the sequence of operations a run has executed. Along any run, register i at a window born at time τ with modulus m holds Ei(the op word since τ) mod m, the free evaluation over Z from the all-zero state. The induction on the op word is short: reduction mod m is a ring homomorphism, so the componentwise operations commute with it; write-constant writes one integer; a grow never touches an old window; and the frontier singleton is 1 − ONES, where ONES is a register set to 1 and re-set at every grow, both moves native. So the registers carry no information beyond the op word; two windows of equal age and modulus are identical forever; and the configuration abstracts to (control, the set of pairs (column mod m, m)) with every operation broadcast and grow inserting the zero pair — a reset-broadcast structure, for every program on every supply, with no rate hypothesis. The window pool is a delay line of the op stream sampled mod the supply, never writable memory. One sharpening: a window under the shared op word realizes any fixed integer polynomial recurrence in its age, not only a constant or the age mod its own modulus. Fibonacci runs as a growing-window clock, its zero-set at modulus m the multiples of the rank of apparition, the least index at which m divides a Fibonacci number, and the global zero-test fires as a period-60 pulse at frozen moduli 2, 3, 4, 5 — the lcm of the ranks 3, 4, 6, 5, a different computation from the CRT pulse's lcm 60 at moduli 3, 4, 5.

Scope. Proved by induction on the op word; 5,571 window-checkpoints over 60 random programs on three supplies are the cross-check, 0 mismatches, never the proof. The Fibonacci witness is observation at moduli 2, 3, 4, 5. The capacity arguments above are untouched, having only used that a fixed window evolves inside (Z/m)r under the shared word, which is the normal form itself.

Halting reduces to semi-deciding that the run grows forever rule

After a run's last grow the whole configuration lives in Q × ∏(Z/mk)r and the machine reads no input between grows, so the plain decider — simulate, HALT on halt, LOOP at the first repeat of the full configuration with no grow between — is complete for every run with finitely many grows, by pigeonhole; the exact repeat is sound and exponentially patient, where a compressed descriptor's first repeat is fast and wrong (below). If every forever-growing program admits a finite certificate, halting is decidable, the three certificates searched in parallel; any undecidable supply must carry forever-growing programs no scheme covers. "Grows forever" is co-semi-decidable for free, its complement being halt-or-loop.

Scope. The reduction is derived from the normal form; the plain decider is a rule on its battery — HALT, a certified LOOP far inside its pigeonhole bound, and NO-CERTIFICATE at the step cap on a forever-growing program, both positive controls passed first. Whether decidability conversely forces certifiability is not claimed.

The growth certificate

A sound certificate exists: a segment whose tests are forced rule

A certificate is a segment of the run between two equal program counters holding at least one grow; it repeats forever exactly when every test inside it reads the same bit at every repetition, and the scheme's job is to force those bits, sufficient and never necessary. Every window at a test is old (in the pool when the segment started), young-same (born at a grow in the same repetition) or young-earlier (born in a previous one), and the normal form gives each its content. The forcings. LOCK-ONE: the last write to the tested register inside the segment is the constant 0, so every window reads 0 at any age. LOCK-ZERO-C: the last write is c ≠ 0 and one pool window has m ∤ c. LOCK-ZERO-F: the window born at the segment's last grow before the test holds a fixed integer E with m > |E| — the one lock that spends the supply, which a bounded supply cannot carry, witnessed by a program that certifies on the sqrt pole and prints no certificate on a bounded one. LOCK-STATIC: the register is written nowhere in the segment, so the bit is constant and equal to the one read, the scan over the whole segment, since a backward scan from the test mislabels 13 of 46. And for old windows the segment's orbit on (Z/m)r is eventually periodic, so "reads 0 at this offset forever" is decided per window and the same orbit pays a ZERO lock. Young-same is one fixed integer per grow offset, derived-necessary and shadowed by LOCK-ZERO-F on the census; young-earlier is the free integer action of the segment word iterated over Z, decided whenever it reaches a fixed point or a cycle. Every lock is reachable and fires alone on a program built for it.

Scope. Soundness and supply-specificity are derived from the normal form, with 102 certified programs replayed to 600 grows apiece, 0 unsound, every test's bit checked at every offset of a cyclic walk — positions and not instructions, one test executing twice in a segment at two positions with two different last writes. Coverage is the next block's, an observation.

The forced-one wall fell, and the residue on one draw is one program observation

Forcing a test to read false needs one persisting witness; forcing it to read true needs every window the machine owns, unborn ones included, and that looked like a wall. On a draw of 600 random programs, 109 grow-heavy — the run reaching 120 grows inside the step cap, an observation and not a decision that it grows forever — the three original locks certified 52, 49 of them holding no test at all (a branch-free cycle repeats because it has no branch), so lock-carried coverage was 3 and never a rate; across seeds it runs 0 to 3 per cell. With the static lock and the three-family reading attached the same draw certifies 102, lock-carried coverage is 53, the branch-free share falls from 94% to 48% with the 49 unchanged, and the free case carries the plurality and not the bulk (32 by LOCK-STATIC against 20 by the dynamic arms, the order reversing at program length 20). Of the 7 left, 6 hold a test whose bit takes both values on replay, so that segment cannot repeat with the same answers, and the certifier's cut — the first repeated program counter holding a grow — is its own choice: retried at repeats 2 to 7, 6 of the 7 certify. The survivor is the route limit: its free integer action neither stabilises nor cycles. Over the 34 integer actions the census reached, 32 hit a fixed point, 2 neither, and a proper cycle never appears; the survivor's read value settles while its state does not. None of this touches the capacity conjecture: what is bounded is a scheme, and a program can be built to defeat any arm.

Scope. One census cell of 600 random programs at one seed, the segment choice swept 11 repeats deep, seed and program length swept one dial at a time; the stable form across the sweep is 48 to 62% of grow-heavy programs carried by the free case and the three-family reading together.

The decision procedure

Pending fires land on the sublinear side, and the first-repeat rule is phase-blind rule

A riding counter's pending fire lands at a computable time on every o(g) supply with any fixed grows-per-pass: the value advances by one while the modulus is non-decreasing, so the first pass with v + 1 ≥ m has equality and the wrap cannot be skipped. On the linear side the same fire hangs pending forever, a faithful counter never re-zeroing. So the o(g)/Ω(g) line is drawn a third time: capacity cap, class placement, fire landing. The first-repeat heuristic — LOOP on a repeated (control, zero-vector) signature — is doubly unsound: a frozen lcm pulse of period 60 draws a false LOOP at step 15 against a true halt at 250, and a riding counter's growing gaps (fires at 9, 24, 45, 72, 105, 141) draw one unboundedly. The phase ladder: bounded hidden phase (frozen, recovered by tracking the frozen residues), growing but computable phase (riding, recovered by the landing), phase pushed into the supply (not recoverable machine-side).

Scope. The landing is proved by the crossing argument and mechanized: landings on the ⌈√g⌉ and ⌈log2 g⌉ supplies within the derived bound, the linear hang exhibited over 4,000 passes.

The three-verdict decider is sound on its fragment, and decidability is rate plus supply tameness rule

Verdicts HALT(t), LOOP(certificate), SUPPLY-ARITHMETIC(extracted question). Signals are classified riding, frozen, growing-uniform or constant, predictions are bisimulation-checked against the concrete machine tick for tick, and an exact descriptor walk decides halts, with a fire branch that consults another signal's phase having its question extracted rather than guessed. Zero false verdicts on the battery, both naive killers included; outside the fragment the answer is out-of-fragment. The supply oracle closes the other face: the machine reads one bit per landing about the supply's fine structure — on an all-0-mod-6 track every wrap lands at clock phase 0; one tail switch to 1 mod 6 and the first post-switch wrap trips the detector — so for computable o(g) supplies encoding "machine e halts" in a tail switch, halting is undecidable with every capacity cap intact and universality not reopened. The rate caps the machine's own arithmetic and forces every fire to land; the supply's arithmetic is the one remaining input channel.

Scope. Rule for the fragment, battery-verified per call; the supply oracle is a refutation by construction. Taxonomy completeness — that every o(g) program's signals reduce to the four kinds — is conjectured on the bandwidth principle, the normal form's widened repertoire being a claim about what a window may hold and leaving the four-way split of signals standing.

Which supplies are tame

The sqrt pole is solved: the wrap word has a closed form, and the residue questions are decided by a finite check rule

With s(v) the largest 3·2n ≤ v, every landing is v² − 2s(v) − 1 or v² − v − 2s(v − 1) − 1, the two coinciding at v = 3·2n. The proof is the offset recurrence: the distance from a gap value's window top to its last landing counts down by one per value and resets to v at zero, the window having length 2v − 2 and being walked in steps of v; the gap values used once rather than twice are exactly the powers-of-two multiples of 3, the sequence's whole non-polynomial content, and multiplicity is at most 2. The recurrence never reads the frontier offset, so the doubling is the supply's while the seed is the start's (3 at offset 0, 2 at offset 1, 5 at offset 5). At the rig's own offset the landings mod 6 are v² − 1 and v² − v − 1, which never take the value 4, so a program halting on a landing ≡ 4 mod 6 loops forever, decided — but the exclusion belongs to the offset and not to the supply, every other offset tested hitting all six classes. The extracted class is decided for every L by a split check: below the point where a block [3·2n, 3·2n+1) first spans L values the landings are enumerated, above it the reachable residues follow from v² and v² − v over v mod L against the tail set of 6·2n mod L.

Scope. Proved by the offset recurrence and checked on 199,999 landings to about 1010; the split check equals enumeration for every L ≤ 60 and is load-bearing at every L divisible by 4, 22 of 60 classes excluded at L = 60. The structure lives in the gap index as a quadratic plus a leading-binary-digit read.

The classification runs on the inverse supply, in four cells rule

The top offset obeys o(d) = (o(d − 1) + M(d) − M(d − 1)) mod d at every populated window, an empty one carrying it over without the mod. (i) Quasi-polynomial M, the critical band, is tame by offset compression with a parity: at M = dc with c odd the decrement is +1 mod d, the offset freezes at d − 2 at the canonical start, other starts moving the constant, and the landing set is exactly polynomial with no geometric content; with c even the decrement is −1 mod d, proved at every even c, and the checked even powers carry the sqrt pole's marks 3·2n seed included, while the scaled line M = ⌊d²/2⌋ carries 3n + 1 — the ratio a fingerprint of M and never a universal constant. (ii) Slow supplies, those repeating some value coprime to L at least L times, are tame by the coverage lemma: the window of a value coprime to L, walked in steps of that value at least L times, crosses all of Z/L, so every class is hit by that window's top and reachability is decided from a finite prefix. (iii) Its contrapositive, the starvation lemma, places the oracle: keeping any class empty forces every coprime high-multiplicity value out of the supply, so the planted-bit track — all values ≡ 0 mod L — is maximal starvation and the oracle's design was forced; value-starved supplies stay untame at any rate. (iv) The super-critical band is the open cell, next.

Scope. The coverage and starvation lemmas are proved, as is the decrement's parity at every power; the freeze and the marks are rule at the checked families (c = 3, 5; c = 2, 4, 6, 8; the scaled line), the recurrence bisimulated against the tick rider on every supply run and the coverage bound never exceeded across 61 supply–modulus pairs.

Above c = 2 every window is attained, and the first-hit bound is computed from M alone rule

At M = ⌊dc⌋ with c > 2 every window is attained and multiplicity is unbounded, the width w(d) ≥ c(d − 1)c−1 − 1 exceeding d from an explicit d0, so the coverage lemma decides the residue question with a bound T(L) = top(d*), d* the first value coprime to L at or above the threshold c(d − 1)c−1 − 2 ≥ Ld, growing as Lc/(c−2). Inside the band c ∈ (1, 2) multiplicity is at most 1 beyond an explicit d1, so the lemma has no hypothesis at any L: the open cell is exactly that wide.

Scope. Rule for the bound, verified at c = 5/2, 7/3, 9/4 against every L ≤ 60; the cell's width is derived.

The open cell is exactly c in (1, 2), and its question is a coverage theorem on the offset observation

A computable first-hit bound B(L) is the same statement as every class being hit — the first appearance is then computable by enumeration — so the bound's size (L·HL in range, the coupon-collector law the word's own shuffle also pays) is not what is asked, and the change of currency from equidistribution to coverage restates the cell in the decider's own terms and leaves it as hard. The step a proof cannot take elementarily is named: a landing's residue is its offset's, tn+1 = top(dn) − on, the offset moved by an exact countdown-and-refill recurrence and being a fresh digit of the orbit's position at resolution 1/w(dn) with w → ∞, so a proof needs top(dn) − on mod L to take every value along the orbit's own sparse set of windows — equidistribution input on selected windows, not a counting statement. It cannot come from the block structure: inside a maximal run of constant gap difference δ the landings are tj = t0 + j·d0 + δ·j(j + 1)/2 exactly, and a block continued jitter-free is a quadratic missing (p − 1)/2 classes mod every odd prime p ∤ δ, so coverage where it holds is carried by the jitter, the offset's choice. Measured, the offset is equidistributed — the normalized offset uniform on [0, 1) to Kolmogorov distance 0.003 over 105 landings — and it has a closed form: the normalized offset is 1 − frac((tn + 2)1/c) to first order, a Weyl word along the landing orbit, uncorrelated at every lag 1 to 8, with star discrepancy falling as N−0.58 at c = 3/2 and N−0.37 at c = 7/4 over the decade to 106, a random source's rate or worse and never a rotation's. The census stands at four powers inside the cell (c = 4/3, 3/2, 5/3, 7/4) by every L ≤ 60 by every class with no obstruction anywhere, the last class hit at landing 363, 410, 365, 450; and a YES has a short certificate somewhere, the best-position window at most 3L over every L ≤ 60 — a fact about the word and not about a decider that cannot start where the certificate is.

Scope. Observation inside the band: the census at four powers, positive-controlled on the two supplies whose exclusions the sqrt pole and the scaled line own; the block law checked on 51,150 runs with no exception and the jitter-free miss on 14,809 pairs; the closed form is a property of top(d) = ⌊dc⌋ − 2, mean deviation under 3·10−4 at c = 3/2 and 7/4. What stays open is the coverage theorem on c ∈ (1, 2): that a window top's residue less its landing's offset takes every value mod L along the orbit's selected windows.
