The delay line

A growing pool of windows holds nothing but the operation stream played back mod the supply, and on every unbounded supply that is enough for two counters. How a counter survives on a slow supply where the counters built on a fixed grow schedule cap, what the normal form reduces halting to, how far that residual can be certified, and what a decider sound on a fragment of programs still has to ask the supply.

The machine has r registers, each holding one residue at every window of a pool of windows Z/m, the column at a window being the r residues held there, with componentwise add, subtract, multiply and write-constant; one global zero-test, the AND of a register's per-window zero bits; grow, which appends a fresh window born at zero; and the frontier singleton, the register reading 1 at the freshest window and 0 at every older one, the only handle a program has on a window. The supply is the sequence mg, the modulus of the g-th window grown; its rate is o(g) below linear and Ω(g) at or above, a supply swinging between the two being neither, and the canonical sublinear supply mg = max(2, ⌈√g⌉) is the sqrt pole. A bounded supply leaves the machine finite-state and every unbounded one makes it universal (the supply law), the second by the doubling counter. A window is pointed when a register saved as its frontier singleton singles it out, and a rider is a counter held as a residue in one pointed window at the frontier; its zero-test fires when the value wraps to 0. A register is faithful when its value tracks its increment count. Universality is Minsky's, cited.

Capacity: every unbounded supply carries two counters

The doubling counter times its own grows rule

The value V sits on one pointed window of modulus mP, its pointer P the frontier singleton saved while that window was the freshest, so V + P adds one there and nowhere else, V < mP holds exactly, and the zero-test is truthful. When an increment is due and V + P, computed into a scratch register, tests zero, the counter first migrates: it grows one window, saves the new frontier singleton F, and compares mF against 2mP by stepping a residue counter on each of the two windows in lockstep — F outlasts when P's counter wraps twice before F's wraps once, a wrap count bounded by 2 being all the control the compare needs, the two scratch counters written back to 0 before the next compare — and repeats until F outlasts; then it moves the value across one unit at a time, decrementing V by P and incrementing a second register by F until V tests zero, takes the second register as V, re-points P to F, and applies the increment. The compare is exact and runs after every grow, so a migration ends exactly when some later window has modulus above 2mP, which is the supply being unbounded and nothing more; any factor above 1 would carry the value, and 2 keeps the migrations to one per doubling of the modulus. Compared only once per block of mP grows, the block counted by a counter on P's window, the migration still ends on a nondecreasing supply, but not on one whose large moduli avoid the block ends. Two such counters keep independent pointers, a grow for one being born 0 in the other, so a two-counter machine runs on one pool. The grows are timed by counters on windows the program already points at, each faithful below its own modulus, never by a counter larger than the one being built; a counter with no pointer at all is the clock counter.

Scope. Rule: the invariant and the termination are argued from the construction, and the programs, written in machine operations, are checked window by window against a second engine that stores every register at every window. Printed, with no miscount after any operation: 0 → 2000 → 0 on the sqrt pole with the compare once per block (9,424,910 grows to reach 2000); 0 → 200 → 0 with the compare after every grow on mg = g at even g and 2 at odd g, where the block compare stops at value 1 with its grow budget spent, and on mg = g at powers of two and 2 elsewhere; B := 3A then A := B from A = 300 ending at (900, 0) on the sqrt pole and from A = 100 ending at (300, 0) on the alternating supply; with every modulus 2 the grow budget runs out at value 1.

Positional counters growing once per increment cap below the linear rate, and the crux is re-basing, not carry rule

A carry across two addressed windows, each singled out by a saved frontier singleton, is within the machine: save one per digit, and carry when the low digit wraps by increment-then-zero-test on the low register, nonzero at its one window. But a positional value Σ digiti·Wi, Wi the product of the bases below digit i, has the moduli as its bases, and migrating a digit-window to a fresher modulus rescales the higher weights by the unknown ratio m′/m, a multiplication by an unknown the machine cannot perform; only the top digit migrates cleanly, and that obstruction is re-basing; a counter moving only its top digit is top-migratable. The cap lemma: on a nondecreasing supply such a counter's faithful value stays below W·mfrontier, with W the frozen product of the lower bases, a constant the program chooses. Growing once per increment it survives without bound only when some fixed W keeps W·mg abreast of g at every count, which is mg = Ω(g); on the sqrt pole, counting from no windows grown, the top-migratable counter first miscounts at exactly W². The other fixed schedules fail too, a fresh window carrying no value information: the single rider, from three windows grown, first miscounts on the sqrt pole at count 3; the CRT counter, value as residues and exact while it is below the lcm, caps by lcm freeze, a grown window being born 0 and not the value's residue; and the unary support counter has no decrement, being the growth machine. A grow schedule set by the value looks like a regress — on the sqrt pole, value 100 with a top weight of 3 needs 1,090 grows before counting, as if timing the grows needed a counter larger than the one held — and the doubling counter is the way out of it.

Scope. Rule for the named schemes, verified at mg = ⌈g/3⌉ with the carry exact at bases 3 and 4 over counts 0 to 11; the sqrt pole's first miscounts at counts 4, 9, 16, 25, measured at W = 2 to 5; at ⌈g/3⌉ a base-4 counter runs 600 increments uncapped. The linear rate is the line of this schedule, not of the machine.

Bandwidth is per operation, and caps three schemes on a nondecreasing sublinear supply only at boundedly many operations per increment rule

The componentwise operations never move value between windows, so value crosses windows only through the global zero-test, one bit out, and the frontier singleton, one unit in, O(1) per operation: the bandwidth principle. On the sqrt pole the machine is neither finite-state nor well-structured by monotonicity — 20 distinct moduli after 2,000 steps and rising, so no finite column alphabet, and registers that reset — and one program exhibiting both still gets its correct verdict. What bandwidth caps, on a nondecreasing sublinear supply, is a named scheme spending boundedly many operations per increment. There the top-migratable counter at base 4 has a faithful top digit, observed, of 3 at horizons 200 and 800 on the sqrt pole, where it grows 50 to 200 on the linear successor supply, and a firing period is a program constant: the CRT register's zero-test is a pulse that fires only once growth stops, at the lcm of the frozen set (60, 420, 4,620 on windows of moduli 3, 4, 5 with none, a 7, or a 7 and an 11 grown before the freeze). A cross-window product materializes at no window, and blind pre-growth of G0 windows buys a capacity of order √G0 (faithful to 10, 20, 30 at 100, 400, 900, the first lie one above ⌊√G0⌋). The doubling counter spends unboundedly many grows and steps per migration, so its value and the firing periods of its compare counters are unbounded while every operation still moves O(1).

Scope. Rule for the principle and for three schemes, the CRT pulse, the cross-window product and blind pre-growth; the top digit's constancy is measured at two horizons, and that no scheme at bounded operations per increment holds an unbounded addressed value on such a supply is conjectured. The machine's being neither finite-state nor well-structured is shown on one program.

The normal form, and the reduction it buys

Born at zero is a theorem: the suffix-evaluation normal form theorem

Write the op word for the sequence of operations a run has executed. Along any run, register i at a window born at time τ with modulus m holds Ei(the op word since τ) mod m, the free evaluation over Z from the all-zero state. The induction on the op word is short: reduction mod m is a ring homomorphism, so the componentwise operations commute with it; write-constant writes one integer; a grow never touches an old window; and the frontier singleton is 1 − ONES, where ONES is a register set to 1 and re-set at every grow, both moves native. So the registers carry no information beyond the op word; at each step a window's column is fixed by its birth time and its modulus; and the configuration abstracts to (control, the grow count, the set of pairs (column, m)) with every operation broadcast and grow inserting the zero pair — a reset-broadcast structure, for every program on every supply, with no rate hypothesis. The window pool is a delay line of the op stream sampled mod the supply: it holds what the program wrote and nothing else, and the doubling counter's values are written into it. One sharpening: a window under the shared op word realizes any fixed integer polynomial recurrence in its age, not only a constant or the age mod its own modulus. Fibonacci runs as a growing-window clock, its zero-set at modulus m the multiples of the rank of apparition, the least index at which m divides a Fibonacci number, and the global zero-test fires as a period-60 pulse at frozen moduli 2, 3, 4, 5 — the lcm of the ranks 3, 4, 6, 5, a different computation from the CRT pulse's lcm 60 at moduli 3, 4, 5.

Scope. Proved by induction on the op word; 5,571 window-checkpoints over 60 random programs on three supplies are the cross-check, 0 mismatches, never the proof. The Fibonacci witness is observation at moduli 2, 3, 4, 5.

Halting reduces to semi-deciding that the run grows forever rule

After a run's last grow the whole configuration lives in Q × ∏(Z/mk)r and the machine reads no input between grows, mk the modulus of the k-th window, so the plain decider — simulate, HALT on halt, LOOP at the first repeat of the full configuration with no grow between — is complete for every run with finitely many grows, by pigeonhole; the exact repeat is sound and exponentially patient, where the first repeat of a compressed summary of the configuration is fast and wrong (below). If every forever-growing program admits a finite certificate, halting is decidable, halt, loop and certificate searched in parallel; every unbounded supply being universal, each carries forever-growing programs no certificate family covers, so a certificate scheme reaches a fragment. "Grows forever" is co-semi-decidable for free, its complement being halt-or-loop.

Scope. The reduction is derived from the normal form; the plain decider is a rule on the programs tested — HALT and a certified LOOP far inside its pigeonhole bound, the two positive controls, passed first, and no certificate at the step limit on a forever-growing program, as designed.

The growth certificate

A sound certificate exists: a segment whose tests are forced rule

A certificate is a segment of the run between two equal program counters holding at least one grow; it repeats forever exactly when every test inside it reads the same bit at every repetition, and the scheme's job is to force those bits, sufficient and not necessary. Every window at a test is old (in the pool when the segment started), young-same (born at a grow in the same repetition) or young-earlier (born in a previous one), and the normal form gives each its content. The forcings. LOCK-ONE: the last write to the tested register inside the segment is the constant 0, so every window reads 0 at any age. LOCK-ZERO-C: the last write is a constant κ ≠ 0 and one pool window has m ∤ κ. LOCK-ZERO-F: the window born at the segment's last grow before the test holds a fixed nonzero integer E with m > |E|, on a nondecreasing supply so that every later repetition's window is as large — the one lock that spends the supply, which a supply bounded by |E| cannot carry, witnessed by a program that certifies on the sqrt pole and prints no certificate on a bounded one. LOCK-STATIC: the register is written nowhere in the segment, so the bit is constant and equal to the one read, the scan over the whole segment, since a backward scan from the test mislabels 13 of 46. And for old windows the segment's orbit on (Z/m)r is eventually periodic, so "reads 0 at this position forever" is decided per window and the same orbit pays a ZERO lock. Young-same is one fixed integer per grow position, derived-necessary and shadowed by LOCK-ZERO-F on the census; young-earlier is the free integer action of the segment word iterated over Z, decided whenever it reaches a fixed point or a cycle. Every lock is reachable and fires alone on a program built for it.

Scope. Soundness and supply-specificity are derived from the normal form, with 102 certified programs replayed to 600 grows apiece, 0 unsound, every test's bit checked at every position of a cyclic walk — positions and not instructions, one test executing twice in a segment at two positions with two different last writes. Coverage is the next block's, an observation.

The forced-one wall fell, and the residue on one draw is one program observation

Forcing a test to read false needs one persisting witness; forcing it to read true needs every window the machine owns, unborn ones included, and that looked like a wall. On a draw of 600 random programs, 109 grow-heavy — the run reaching 120 grows inside the step limit, an observation and not a decision that it grows forever — the three original locks (LOCK-ONE, LOCK-ZERO-C, LOCK-ZERO-F) certified 52, 49 of them holding no test at all (a branch-free cycle repeats because it has no branch), so lock-carried coverage was 3 and never a rate; across seeds it runs 0 to 3 per cell. With LOCK-STATIC and the three-family reading (the old, young-same and young-earlier contents read off the normal form) attached the same draw certifies 102, lock-carried coverage is 53, the branch-free share falls from 94% to 48% with the 49 unchanged, and LOCK-STATIC carries the plurality and not the bulk (32 against 20 by the three-family reading, the two overlapping on 3 and four more carried by an older lock, the order reversing at program length 20). Of the 7 left, 6 hold a test whose bit takes both values on replay, so that segment cannot repeat with the same answers, and the certifier's cut — the first repeated program counter holding a grow — is its own choice: retried at repeats 2 to 7, 6 of the 7 certify. The survivor is the route limit: its free integer action neither stabilises nor cycles. Over the 34 integer actions the census reached, 32 hit a fixed point, 2 neither, and a proper cycle never appears; the survivor's read value settles while its state does not. None of this bears on capacity, which the doubling counter settles: what is bounded is a scheme, and a program can be built to defeat any arm.

Scope. One census cell of 600 random programs at one seed, the segment choice swept 11 repeats deep, seed and program length swept one dial at a time; the stable form across the sweep is 48 to 62% of grow-heavy programs carried by LOCK-STATIC and the three-family reading, counted per reading, so a program both carry counts twice (52 of 109 at the census cell).

The decision procedure

A fire is pending while the rider's next true zero-test lies in the future, and the fire's time is a landing. A pass is one tick of a rider: grow a fixed number K of windows, the grows-per-pass, move the value to the fresh frontier window in unary, then increment and zero-test. The start p is the number of windows grown before the first pass, so pass t sees frontier index Kt + p; the canonical start is p = 2. A supply is tame at a grows-per-pass and a start when one procedure decides, given r and L, whether some landing is ≡ r mod L: the questions the decider below extracts.

A pending fire lands exactly when the modulus falls behind the rider, and the first-repeat rule is phase-blind criterion

A pending fire lands exactly when the modulus falls behind the rider: on a nondecreasing supply, a fire armed at pass t₀, the rider reading 0 after that pass, lands at the least t > t₀ with m(Kt + p) ≤ t − t₀ and hangs when there is none. Until it wraps the value is t − t₀, and it was below the previous pass's modulus, which is at most this pass's, so the add wraps exactly when t − t₀ reaches m(Kt + p) and no wrap is skipped; on a computable supply the time is computed by evaluating it. So every fire lands exactly when t − m(Kt + p) is unbounded above, which every nondecreasing o(g) supply gives at every fixed K, and sublinearity is sufficient, not necessary. On mg = ⌈g/a⌉ (a modulus 1 reading every value as 0) every fire lands exactly when K < a, at t = max(t₀ + 1, ⌈(p + at₀)/(a − K)⌉), the landings growing geometrically by a/(a − K), and at K ≥ a at most one fire lands, at t = 1 from start 0; the successor supply mg = g + 1 at one grow per pass keeps its fire pending forever, a faithful counter never re-zeroing. At slope one the sublinear correction decides: mg = max(1, g − ⌊√g⌋) at one grow per pass from start 0 fires at 1, 2, 4, 16, 256, each landing from 2 on the square of the last. The first-repeat heuristic — LOOP on a repeated (control, zero-vector) signature — is doubly unsound: a frozen lcm pulse of period 60 draws a false LOOP at step 15 against a true halt at 250, and a riding counter's growing gaps (a top-migratable counter at base 3 on the sqrt pole fires at 9, 24, 45, 72, 105, 141) draw one at unboundedly late steps. The phase ladder: bounded hidden phase (frozen, recovered by tracking the frozen residues), growing but computable phase (riding, recovered by the landing), phase pushed into the supply (not recoverable machine-side).

Scope. Criterion for the landing, proved by that inequality and checked against the rider's own recurrence in 480 of 480 cells (ten nondecreasing supplies, K = 1 to 8, p = 0 to 5), the closed form at ⌈g/a⌉ matched to 20,000 passes for a ≤ 6, the successor supply's hang exhibited over 4,000 passes. The first-repeat rule's unsoundness is a rule, exhibited on its two killers.

The three-verdict decider is sound on its fragment, and a supply tame where its riders run decides the fragment rule

The fragment is the programs made of a setup followed by one loop, whose zero-tested registers are each touched in the loop body only by rider increments, by adds of singletons saved before the loop, by adds of one constant at every window in a loop that grows, or not at all, with resets allowed on fire branches, a rider's increment with its move to the frontier counting as one operation; the doubling counter, saving other singletons inside its loop, is outside it. Verdicts HALT(t), LOOP(certificate), SUPPLY-ARITHMETIC(extracted question). A register's signal is the stream of its zero-test bits, classified riding, frozen (on windows saved before the loop), growing-uniform (touched only by a constant at every window) or constant; predictions are bisimulation-checked against the concrete machine tick for tick, and a walk of the control over the predicted bits decides halts, with a fire branch that consults another signal's phase having its question extracted rather than guessed, so wherever the extracted questions are decidable the fragment is. Zero false verdicts over the programs tested, both naive killers included; a program outside the fragment gets no verdict. The supply oracle closes the other face: the machine reads one bit per landing about the supply's fine structure — on a supply whose every modulus is ≡ 0 mod 6 every wrap lands at phase 0 of a frozen clock mod 6; one tail switch to 1 mod 6 and the first post-switch wrap trips the detector — so over the computable o(g) supplies encoding "machine e halts" in a tail switch, a fragment program's halting is undecidable in e. Inside the fragment a nondecreasing sublinear supply forces every rider's fire to land and the supply's arithmetic is the one remaining input channel; outside it, on an unbounded supply, the machine is universal.

Scope. Rule for the fragment: soundness argued from the certificates' construction, every prediction bisimulation-checked per call, zero false verdicts over the programs tested; that every fragment program receives one of the three verdicts is shown over those programs only. The supply oracle is a refutation by construction.

Which supplies are tame

The rest reads a rider at one grow per pass. The inverse supply is M(d) = max{g : m(g) ≤ d}. A landing enters by the gap d from the landing before it (from time 0 for the first), the modulus it wraps, and leaves by the gap to the next; one entered by d has its frontier index in the span of d, (M(d − 1), M(d)], with M(1) = 0 when every modulus is at least 2, on a nondecreasing unbounded supply, and the span top in time is top(d) = M(d) − p. A landing's offset is top(d) less the landing, d the gap it enters by; the offset o(d) of a gap value d is top(d) less the last landing at or before top(d), the last landing entered by d when there is one. A gap value is attained when some landing enters by it, its multiplicity the number that do, and its width is w(d) = top(d) − top(d − 1).

The sqrt pole is solved: the wrap word has a closed form, and the residue questions are decided by a finite check rule

With s(v) the largest 3·2n ≤ v over n ≥ −1, so that s(2) = 3/2, at the canonical start every landing that leaves by v is v² − 2s(v) − 1 or v² − v − 2s(v − 1) − 1, the two coinciding at v = 3·2n. The proof is the offset recurrence: the offset of v counts down by one per value and resets at zero, o(v) = o(v − 1) − 1 where o(v − 1) ≥ 1 and o(v) = v − 1 where o(v − 1) = 0, because the landings that leave by v, each v before one entered by v, lie in (f(v − 1), f(v)] with f(v) = top(v) − v = v² − v − 2, an interval of length 2v − 2 walked in steps of v, and top(v − 1) + 1 is never a landing; the gap values that landings leave by once rather than twice are exactly the powers-of-two multiples of 3, the sequence's whole non-polynomial content, and multiplicity is at most 2. The recurrence never reads the start, so the doubling is the supply's while the value its doubling run starts from is the start's, counting the landings each value enters (3 at start 0, 2 at start 1, 5 at start 5). At the canonical start the landings mod 6 are v² − 1 and v² − v − 1, which never take the value 4, so a program halting on a landing ≡ 4 mod 6 loops forever, decided — but the exclusion belongs to the start and not to the supply: over fourteen starts (0 to 11, 17 and 40) the canonical one is the only one excluding class 4, starts 0, 4, 7 and 11 exclude classes 0, 2, 5 and 1 instead, and the other nine hit all six. The extracted class is decided for every L by a split check: below the point where a block [3·2n, 3·2n+1) first covers L values the landings are enumerated, above it the reachable residues follow from v² and v² − v over v mod L against the tail set of 6·2n mod L.

Scope. Proved by the offset recurrence and checked on 199,999 landings to about 1010, and from the first landing, t = 2, on every rider landing to 106, each inside its span in frontier and in time; the split check equals enumeration for every L ≤ 60 and is load-bearing at every L divisible by 4, 22 of 60 classes excluded at L = 60. The structure lives in the gap index as a quadratic plus a leading-binary-digit read.

The classification runs on the inverse supply, in four cells rule

The offset o(d) of a gap value d obeys o(d) = (o(d − 1) + M(d) − M(d − 1)) mod d at every attained d, an unattained one carrying it over without the mod. (i) Quasi-polynomial M is decided by offset compression with a parity at the checked powers and the scaled line below, and conjectured tame at every quasi-polynomial M: at M = dc with c odd the step M(d) − M(d − 1) is ≡ 1 mod d, the offset freezes at d − 2 at the canonical start, other starts moving the constant, and the landing set is exactly polynomial with no geometric content; with c even the step is ≡ −1 mod d, proved at every even c, and the checked even powers carry the sqrt pole's marks 3·2n seed included, while the scaled line M = ⌊d²/2⌋ carries 3n + 1 — the ratio a fingerprint of M and never a universal constant. (ii) Covering supplies, those attaining for every L some gap value coprime to L with multiplicity at least L, are tame by the coverage lemma: that value's landings, L or more in steps of the value, cross all of Z/L, so every class is hit by a landing at or before that span's top and reachability is decided from a finite prefix. (iii) Its contrapositive, the starvation lemma, places the oracle: keeping any class empty forces every coprime value of multiplicity at least L out of the supply, so the oracle's track — all values ≡ 0 mod L — is maximal starvation, the extreme of the designs the lemma allows; a starved supply can plant bits at any rate, and one that plants none is not thereby untame. (iv) M = ⌊dc⌋ at non-integer c: above 2 decided by coverage, and in (1, 2) the open cell, next.

Scope. The coverage and starvation lemmas are proved, as is the step's parity at every power; the freeze and the marks are rule at the checked families (c = 3, 5; c = 2, 4, 6, 8; the scaled line), the recurrence bisimulated against the tick rider on every supply run and the coverage bound never exceeded across 61 supply–modulus pairs.

The open cell is exactly c in (1, 2), and its question is a coverage theorem on the offset observation

Every class being hit already gives a computable first-hit bound B(L) — the first appearance is then computable by enumeration — so the bound's size (L·HL in range, HL the L-th harmonic number, the coupon-collector law the word's own shuffle also pays) is not what is asked, and the change of currency from equidistribution to coverage restates the cell in the decider's own terms and leaves it as hard. The step a proof cannot take elementarily is named: a landing's residue is fixed by its span top and its offset, tn = top(dn) − on, dn the gap tn enters by and on its offset, the offset moved by the exact recurrence and being a fresh digit of the orbit's position at resolution 1/w(dn) with w → ∞, so a proof needs top(dn) − on mod L to take every value along the orbit's own sparse set of spans — equidistribution input on selected spans, not a counting statement. It cannot come from the block structure: inside a maximal run of constant gap difference δ the landings are tj = t0 + j·d0 + δ·j(j + 1)/2 exactly, and a block continued jitter-free is a quadratic missing (q − 1)/2 classes mod every odd prime q ∤ δ, so coverage where it holds is carried by the jitter, the offset's choice. Measured, the offset is equidistributed — the normalized offset on/w(dn) uniform on [0, 1) to Kolmogorov distance 0.003 over 105 landings — and it has a closed form: the normalized offset is 1 − frac((tn + 2)1/c) to first order, a Weyl word along the landing orbit, uncorrelated at every lag 1 to 8, with star discrepancy falling as N−0.58 at c = 3/2 and N−0.37 at c = 7/4 over the decade to 106, against −0.52 to −0.58 measured for a random source: a random source's rate or worse, and never a rotation's. The census stands at four powers inside the cell (c = 4/3, 3/2, 5/3, 7/4) by every L ≤ 60 by every class with no obstruction anywhere, the last class hit at landing 363, 410, 365, 450; and a YES has a short certificate somewhere, the shortest run of consecutive landings hitting every class, placed at its best position, at most 3L long over every L ≤ 60 — a fact about the word and not about a decider that cannot start where the certificate is.

Scope. Observation inside the band: the census at four powers, positive-controlled on the two supplies whose exclusions the sqrt pole and the scaled line own; the block law checked on 51,150 runs with no exception and the jitter-free miss on 14,809 pairs; the closed form is a property of top(d) = ⌊dc⌋ − 2, mean deviation under 3·10−4 at c = 3/2 and 7/4. What stays open is the coverage theorem on c ∈ (1, 2): that a span top's residue less its landing's offset takes every value mod L along the orbit's selected spans.
