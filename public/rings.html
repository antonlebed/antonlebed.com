Which rings walk

Every law on the clock page prices a lone item against its own ladder, and a ring enters only as a supply. A greedy walk in a real ring seats places off a MENU, and there the product of norm and gap names the wrong place: the head is why. What the winner's kind decides, where a tie crosses kinds, how much of a place's column a supply has to carry, and what the crossing to elements costs.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient. A place is a prime of the ring with an integer degree (the log-norm over a number ring); seating one is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move. A rational prime splits into distinct places, stays inert as one place of higher residue degree, or ramifies as one place repeated, the multiplicity being the ramification index e, tame when e < p and wild otherwise; the residue degree f sets the norm pf, and a unit sits at level i when it is 1 modulo the i-th power of the place. A place's standard column is the one the local arithmetic generates with no head. A place's column is λ read across its depths, its ladder the depths where the column jumps, and its door the least climb that raises λ from where it stands — a lone door against its own column, a populated door against the state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated λ's, which is what a ring charges. A ladder is headed when a leading stretch of gaps exceeds its eventually constant tail. The menu is the priced set of a state's admissible moves, the void menu the first state's, a tie a menu with several minima, and the paid sequence what the walk pays move by move. A walk locks when it stops opening for good, the move it repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle, and a coordinate left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again is a strand. In the ideal world a move seats one place power; in the element world it must be a single element and seats a core, the place power aimed at, plus a rider, the cheapest product of places whose ideal class cancels the core's, read off the class group's minimal representatives; a place's colour is its (degree, class), and the supply matrix counts items per colour. The whole vocabulary is bound at The pricing schedule; what one place hands over is The clock.

Which places a void walk ever seats

The winner-kind dichotomy: the paid direction falls, holds or rises with the kind of the void's first winner rule

An unseated place's door never falls as the invariant grows (The certificate), so what decides is whether the void winner's own column ever pauses. At every unramified winner except norm 2 the column climbs strictly at every residue degree — the p-th power map carries exact level i to i + 1, and level 1 to 2 because x + x² is nonzero somewhere on the residue field once it has more than two elements — so λ is (pf − 1)·pa−1, the door stays 1, the menu minimum is a constant from the first move, and every rival lost the void at or above it: the walk seats exactly one place EVER. Z[√−5] and Q(√−23)'s ring are this at 3 on a split place of norm 3; the inert-2 winners of discriminants −19, −43, −115 at 4. At norm 2 the residue field is F2, where x + x² vanishes identically, so the column holds one flat step 1 2 2 4 and the minimum FALLS: the winner opens at door 2 for 4, pays 4 once more, and recurs at 2, the family's floor, below every rival forever (−7, −31, −55); its conjugate ties at exactly 4 and the tie is benign, the loser's column being the winner's own. A RAMIFIED winner hands the walk a climbing transient, and one cheap ramified place can catch another: Z[√−30], ramified at 2, 3, 5 with least split norm 11, pays 3 3 5 5 17 …, seats the place over 3 as the cheapest opening and the place over 5 left UNSEATED at step 3, pinned at 5 until something of norm ≥ 8 is bought. Which ramified places can be caught is a residue-characteristic dichotomy: every place of odd norm has λ = N − 1 even, so every seating off the characteristic-2 fibre feeds the 2-part of L and a characteristic-2 ramified door climbs in lockstep with the menu (0 of 753 seeded seatings), while an odd-characteristic one widens only when its next rung divides L. Past a lock no strict seating is possible, a flat floor against nondecreasing prices meeting only at a tie, and none was observed. So a flat support is never a law about ramification but the arithmetic of the winner's kind.

Scope. The unramified half a theorem at every ring of integers, up to a void tie; its witnesses and the ramified half rules in range: eleven quadratic rings walked from the void, 3,109 ramification-free seeded walks, five in-situ monotonicity rigs at 0 falls over 2.5 million consecutive cost pairs.

Beyond the quadratic shape the void tie crosses kinds, and the tie-break chooses the world rule

A cubic ring whose 2 factors at residue degrees 1 and 2, with no norm-3 place, ties the void at 4 between the norm-2 and norm-4 places, nothing conjugating one into the other. The tree ends three ways: the norm-2 place twice, paid 4 4 2 2 2 … and the norm-4 place never seated; the two mixed orders merging into one state with both seated, paid 4 4 4 2 2 … and the norm-4 place stranded at exponent 1; the norm-4 place twice, paid 4 forever. Exactly one coordinate runs away in each, so the greedy limit is MULTIVALUED at the void, the same ring and law running different signatures by the break alone. The tie can cross kinds AT ONE NORM: a cubic ring staging 5 as a tame PQ² with 2 and 3 inert ties the void at 5 across the unramified/ramified seam, and the break chooses between the dichotomy's rows. Seat the unramified member and the paid holds at 5 forever. Seat the ramified one and the paid rises for good, but the runaway is the MENU's to sell: the inert 2 prices its place at 8 against every lcm(4, 5k), so the risen branch is undercut at 8 or cheaper wherever the ring holds a degree-1 place over 7, 13, 17, 19 or 23 — 9,450 of 9,478 polynomials — which takes the runaway at its own norm while both tie members end stranded or unseated, the deep place a VOID LOSER; the ramified winner reclaims it at its norm-squared plateau, paid 5 5 8 25 25 …, only at the 28 polynomials over five discriminants with no such place. The tree has no merge, the tie never recurs, and the break is binary at every walked void. A cubic ring also presents the cross-norm same-prime tie and a width-3 opening no quadratic ring can; the width law is The greedy image's.

Scope. A rule: the branch arithmetic proved from the columns for every ring meeting each signature, walked at 92 cubic polynomials over 17 discriminants, six positive, and at 1,132 of 9,478 polynomials over at least 2,577 rings.

What a head does to a walk

The product prices the tail while the walk pays the head, and a ring with no head anywhere has no walk to speak of rule

The product of norm and gap is what a place charges once its ladder is periodic, and the head comes first. Z[i] was chosen because its product ordering favours the ramified place — 4 against a least unramified norm of 5 — and the walk seats it as the void's cheapest opening, takes its one cheap move to exponent 3, is charged 32 there where the law says 4, and locks on the inert place of norm 9. The law has two quantifiers, a minimum over SEATED items and its wider use to predict which place is seated at all: Q(√−23)'s ring breaks only the wide one — its two split places over 2 carry product 2 against a lock at 3 and are never seated — and Z[i] breaks both. Two mechanisms pass a cheap place over, read at the DECIDING state where the walk first took the vehicle it kept: HEADED, the place's own ladder pricing its door above the product (the norm-2 splits at 4 rather than 2, their first rung trivial since N − 1 = 1; Z[i]'s ramified place at 32); or COVERED, the state widening the lone door into a populated one (Z[i]'s norm-5 splits, 1 → 2, price 25). All five fall in exactly one, mechanically, since a state can only widen a lone door. Without a head every standard-column place has door 1 at every depth and is priced at its bare norm forever, so the walk locks on the cheapest such place: at the −23 field the norm-5 place, touched and nothing else; at the doubly ramified ring Q[x]/(x³ − 2) the norm-2 place's first entry divides every invariant, so its door starts at 2 and rises with slope 3 in the 2-part of L — the ring's cheapest norm is walk-INVISIBLE — and the wild norm-3 place is seated twice and stranded. The −23 field is where the two head criteria first disagree and an engine decides: e = 1 at its place over 2 predicts a head under "p − 1 ≤ e", f = 3 fails the residue clause, and the ladder carries none. The first cubic field with class number above 1 — discriminant −283, Q[x]/(x³ + 4x + 1), class number 2, every field of smaller |d| certified class number 1 — carries its head at its cheapest place by the unramified shape, and it walks: five ideal basins over the norm ≤ 40 belt, the head holding 11 of 44 seeds at 2 per move, and the element void locking ON the head at 4 per move, paying 2 per ladder rung as the ideal lock does. Heads decide walks at degree 3 and above class number 1 together: which rings walk is a question about heads, not class groups.

Scope. A rule in range: Z[i] walked from the void with its whole norm ≤ 30 universe scanned at every state; the headless cubic rings of discriminant −23 and Q[x]/(x³ − 2); the first classed-and-headed cubic.

The ladder column a supply cannot carry

The cost monoid crosses the characteristic, and the price of the crossing is a second column the supply cannot carry rule

Degrees add over a function field and norms multiply over a number ring, so the rider's cost becomes a least norm PRODUCT — a different minimum from the budget's, this one over the multisets a rider could be — and nothing breaks, because the shortest path deriving it and the bare-door lemma need only a minimum over a monotone monoid. With the rider's cost and the minimal representatives recomputed from the supply matrix as a shortest path in the (min, ×) semiring and the door handed over from the ring's λ, the abstract walker's element menu is the ring engine's at every state — 344 and 347 entries, of which 103 and 307 are bundles. What the hand-over costs is the LADDER column: a function field's items share one ladder, a number ring gives each its own, and the matrix, a count of items per colour, carries no per-place ladder. A colour plus a gap gives (N − 1)·rad(N)⌈(a−1)/gap⌉, which is exactly the exponent of the unit group wherever e/(p − 1) < 1 — the prime-to-p part N − 1 at every depth, the p-part the exponent of the principal units — so the supply end and the local unit group reach one expression. It misses at three columns of the three quadratic engines, every one a place over 2 with residue field F2, while both tame ramified columns there are reproduced: the precondition is e ≥ p − 1 with f = 1, and inside it wild ramification shifts the staircase's PHASE, a doubling zone at a tame place shifts its LEVEL, a residue field of size two drops the level, and a plateau wider than e is a head. At 23 = P·Q² two places share the colour 23 and differ only in the gap, their columns parting from depth 3 (11,638 against 506): the ladder is a column the supply genuinely needs, which over a quadratic ring could not be shown, two places over one prime there being conjugate and priced alike forever.

Scope. A rule in range: two quadratic rings, 675 element states, every seed of both norm-40 belts walked twelve moves and the branch tree to four, 0 menus differing; 551 places of the cubic field of least |discriminant| to depth 14.

The ladder is not primitive and not eliminable; at a seated place the door buys one residue layer while the walk pays for r rule

λ(Xa) is the exponent of the unit group of O/Xa, so the ladder is one filtration's exponent sequence with the gap its asymptotic step and the head its transient — not primitive. But the sequence is not a function of the colour q = pf, nor of (p, e, f), the head reading the completion's torsion — not eliminable, 23 = P·Q² the witness. The gap being e is The clock's theorem, so the 12 sequences read here are a control: gap = e at all 8 places of zero excess. At a seated place the L-torsion index at the door is exactly N(X) at 162 of 162 readings with q − 1 dividing L — at the least depth with ⌈(a−1)/e⌉ one above the p-part of L, exactly f elementary divisors reach the next p-power — while the price is N(X)r, a PREMIUM of N(X)r−1 with two sources, a head or a populated invariant (a split place of gap 1 going from door 1 to 6 beside its conjugate). Z[√−5] over 2 from exponent 3 pays 16 to open an index of 2. At an opening, the 8 readings without q − 1 dividing L, the door is forced by the prime-to-p part and the index need not divide the price.

Scope. A property for the reduction; a rule in range for the readings: 175 doors recomputed off enumerated residue rings by a predicate consulting no λ, 90 with the invariant recomputed too, 0 off.

The price is the additive filtration's index, which matters at the opening move and nowhere else, with one sign per substrate rule

N(X)r is the congruence index |Ue+r|/|Ue| exactly at a seated place and exceeds it by N(X)/(N(X) − 1) at an unseated one. Over a number ring re-pricing openings multiplicatively moves 4 of 9 trajectories, all 4 ties the norm ordering breaks on coincidences like 2¹·1 = 3⁰·2 — the multiplicative price is less separating there and undercuts nothing. Over one residue base q it is more separating: qa − qb determines (a, b), so no two openings tie, and every additive cross-degree tie, the void tie included, breaks toward the smaller degree. What goes with the void tie is everything resting on it — the two-branch census reads a singleton, the ceiling at twice the least degree is never attained, the wide strand vanishes — while the void-menu law, the stop comparison and the image's per-opening factor are price-invariant. The swap's one new tie sits at q = 2 in the climb-against-degree-1 form; an engineered two-item supply reaches it in one move and branches permanently, no standard supply reaching it. Which filtration the schedule means is a real choice with a known blast radius, the tie-fed laws of The surviving openings.

Scope. A rule in range: 347 price readings over four rings; 314,928 comparator pairs and 60 sweep rows over six function-field supplies.

The element lock's recurrent price is flat because the price factors rule

The ideal argument is about one place's valuation and an element move seats several; it does not have to reach them. The recurrent vehicle's form is the core to the r times the minimal representative of the cancelling class, so the price factors — the first factor flat by the valuation argument verbatim, the second a table lookup on a finite group fixed once the door is — and the flat element price is the ideal mechanism composed with the rider being a price.
