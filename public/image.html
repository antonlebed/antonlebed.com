The greedy image

Off the integers the cheapest move is a class of moves, one per way of breaking each tie, and the set of limits that class reaches is one formula: a product over the walk's surviving tied openings of how many ways each can be taken. What that multiplicity is, which kinds of tie there are, and the one certificate that proves two branches never meet.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient. A place is a prime of the ring with an integer degree (the degree of the irreducible over F2[x], the log-norm over a number ring); seating one is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move, which advances the tick T every price is read against, and the exponent it lands on is set by the place's door, the least climb that raises λ against the state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated places' λ's. A fresh move is an opening, paid at its degree's discount, and a vehicle is the divisor a move seats. The menu is the priced set of admissible moves, a tie a menu with several minima, a branch one way of breaking it; a walk locks when it stops opening for good and the move it then repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle. A coordinate carried up without bound is a runaway, one left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again a strand. In the ideal world a move seats one place power; in the element world it must be a single element and seats a core, the place power aimed at, plus a rider, the cheapest product of places whose ideal class cancels the core's, read off the class group's minimal representatives. The whole vocabulary, and the schedule family the laws are stated in, is The pricing schedule.

The greedy image is the set of limits the minimal-move policy class reaches from a seed, a limit being the formal product a trajectory converges to (The limit). A tied opening is surviving when the branches it opens reach distinct limits. The rider's reach is how far a minimal representative can carry a place — the genus over a curve, the Minkowski bound over a number ring. A place's colour is its (degree, class).

The multiplicity at an opening

The multiplicity is an orbit count, which takes the ring out of that factor rule

Strip the ring and the items of one degree are interchangeable, so every permutation preserving what the price READS is an automorphism of the dynamics, and the reachable configurations of a shape are one orbit. A block of the readable colouring holding n items, k seated with an exponent multiset, contributes n!/((n−k)!·∏ mult(e)!), and the image is the SUM over reachable shapes of the PRODUCT of those — a multinomial read off the supply, the dynamics run only to enumerate the shapes, one or two per ring. The per-opening product is the corner where every opened degree carries one item at exponent 1. Three things the orbit reading decides: a degree seated m times contributes C(n, m) and not the falling factorial, the m! seating orders reaching one divisor, so a trajectory count is not a limit count wherever a degree opens twice; the strand a fast clock leaves (the strand) multiplies as a free coordinate; and the sum over shapes overcounts limits, a clock move on the runaway taking two shapes to one reading, 487 configurations to 461 readings at F2[x]. The supply is PRICE-BLIND: both filtrations price the menu key and not the item, so the factor is invariant under the additive/multiplicative swap. The class colouring refines the supply from a vector to a matrix rather than leaving the schedule, the refined multinomial holding where the colour-blind one fails.

Scope. A tie set is not one kind of thing, and what a decline is depends on which datum the members differ in rule

λ reads (degree, depth) and the element world reads the class, so a tie splits into species. Different degrees with one member fresh is a REORDERING: a fresh move multiplies λ by an odd 2d − 1 and a clock move raises only the 2-adic part, so neither disturbs the other's door and both orders reach one divisor — not always in two moves, a clocked member leaving its own place a cheaper door that invites moves before the orders meet. The same degree in different CLASSES is a CHOICE, and the line is the rider's reach: whichever core is taken, the other is unseated at a degree now carrying a seated place, so its vehicle stays dearer forever, and beyond the reach — the genus over a curve, the Minkowski bound over a number ring — no minimal representative carries it back as a passenger; below the line a declined core is a loan the rider repays, above it a loss. Two clock moves at different degrees is a choice too, each raising the clock and making the other dearer. And where a decline is neither, it is the rider's discount, the loser out-competed rather than dominated.

One certificate separates branches, and starvation is it at exponent zero rule

Separating two branches' limits takes a proof that some coordinate's future is determined. Read a state through its door prices: with T the tick, a place of degree d at exponent e prices its door at d·(T + 1 − e), and a clock move raises every door count alike while resetting the mover's. If some place of degree at most P's is cheaper by more than the genus, and neither it nor P can be seated fresh, and no rider can carry P, then P is never a minimal move again: a clock move changes the gap by (dP − dQ) (T′ − T) ≥ 0, and clocking the witness resets it to the bottom, widening the gap; a place still seatable fresh has a door of 1 and jumps the moment it is taken, so a witness that can jump is no witness. P's exponent is frozen and the limit reads it finite. Starvation is the lemma with the witness the declined core's seated sibling, a place of its own degree; the freeze is the lemma with an exponent pinned where it stands. What is proved is that the LOCK is unnecessary: a sprawling world determines coordinates, all but about one of the 8 to 17 places the lemma speaks about at a walked state.

Scope. Proved for the ideal world; a rule in range with the genus as slack in the element world, 60,972 of 60,972 cores the starvation certificate covers subsumed, six rings; the determined-coordinate census is over reached states and not the limit.

Why the count factors

Lockstep is a theorem, and the type the rings were measured with is the type that factors theorem

Everything the engine reads is a function of a place's (degree, class), so any permutation of places preserving that colouring is an automorphism of the whole dynamics, and two tie members of one REFINED type — the colouring plus the state depth — are carried one to the other by a permutation fixing the state; the subtrees are isomorphic, every later opening carries the same multiplicity, and the count FACTORS at every ring. The measured type drops the state depth, and the gap could only open through a core exponent above its door, a route the bare-door lemma (The pricing schedule) closes at any genus; the rider route needs two distinct places of one degree and one class within the rider's reach, and the least degree at which two places share a class is 1, 2, 5, 5, 6, 6 against genus 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, clearing it by one at both ends of the ladder and by four or more between. What carries the formula's risk is the reading of a cross-type decline as a reordering, and that reading is right for the cross-degree half and wrong for the same-degree cross-class one, which is a choice above the genus. The width the formula multiplies is bought by depth, not geography: exhaustively over shallow states the within-type width is at most 2 and exactly 1 at nearly every element tie state, while the same rings' trajectories reach widths in the hundreds and thousands across the elliptic ladder.
