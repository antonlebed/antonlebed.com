The clock dial

How many clocks does the family run? One every item shares, one per item, or a ring's own middle — one tick per residue characteristic with a ladder per place. The dial decides two different things, a ring sits at a cell the family never ran, and what a head's transient leaves behind is a formula in both halves.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient. A place is a prime of the ring with an integer degree; seating one is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move, which advances the tick every price is read against. A place's ladder is the set of depths where its λ jumps, the gap the least climb that raises λ, and the door its gap at the depth it stands, read against the state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated λ's. A ladder is headed when a leading stretch of gaps exceeds its eventually constant tail; the head's seat is e/(p − 1), its width the largest gap less the tail, its splice the step where the ladder leaves its ramp, the rungs pj + 1 it climbs first; a ψ ladder is one the local arithmetic generates, the orbit of ψ(i) = min(p·i, i + e), the head's law on The clock. A cell is settled when its counts no longer move with the move budget. A walk locks when it stops opening for good, the move it repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle, and the budget is what that vehicle pays. A coordinate carried up without bound is a runaway, one left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again a strand. The schedule family — items of integer degree, a price f(degree, door) read against a clock, a fresh discount on each degree's first openings, the born-covered degrees having none — is bound at The pricing schedule; a global clock gives every item one tick, a per-item clock each item its own, and a block clock gives every item over one residue characteristic one tick and every item its own ladder. What one place hands any of them is The clock.

One tick, or one each

The clock is two dials, a ring sits at a cell the family never ran, and what collapses a limit to one deep place is the order on prices rule

A seated place's door reads vp(L) and nothing else (The door a state charges), so a state's price structure is the vector of vℓ(L) over the rational primes, with each place's ladder attached to its own coordinate. "Global against per-item" moves two things on one dial — who shares a tick and who shares a ladder — and a ring separates them: places over one rational prime share a tick and not a ladder. Run at that cell, the limit's shape splits by the recurrent cost. At every bounded gap exactly ONE item runs away however finely the partition is cut, at all 20 cells, while at a growing ladder the runaways number the blocks, 6 of 6: a mover lands on its own next rung, so a bounded ladder's recurrent cost is FLAT and one global minimum wins forever, while a climbing cost makes the minimum ROTATE and hands every block a runaway. One deep place is the flat-cost corner of a one-per-block law, collapsed by the total order on prices and by nothing p-adic. The budget survives untouched, 0 of 20 off; what the partition dials is the count above exponent 1 — 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 at gap 5 against a runaway count of 1 — so every coordinate a refinement adds is a strand. The degree ceiling is per-block, the state's deepest degree a maximum over blocks. A partition whose two supplies are isomorphic still gives one runaway by tie-break; and the ring's own shape — one tick, two ladders — puts the runaway on the narrow item and never carries the wide one above exponent 1, the product of norm and gap pricing a wide place out with no ring in it, the head included at all 7 widths: what a block clock strands, it strands ACROSS blocks and never inside one.

Scope. A rule in range: 6 ladders × 5 partitions, 120 moves a cell, the dial's ends certified against the family's two clocks move for move.

verifiers: explore_block_clock.py, explore_headed_block.py

The rotating regime is arithmetically unreachable, and a head buys only a transient of it rule

Rotation needs a climbing recurrent cost and more than one block. A function field's ladder climbs and it has one residue characteristic; a number ring has many blocks and its ladders are eventually constant-gap. So "one deep place" is one theorem over the schedule family and two unrelated accidents over the objects, and no ring anyone can build would show the second regime. A ramp climbs, so a headed place rotates for its own length; every ψ ladder flattens at its splice, and exactly one item rises at all 50 cells, every runaway past its crossing depth, the budget its price at the tail gap. The finiteness of the residue field is the whole of what stands between the law and its second regime, and it is the hypothesis the door identity is stated under.

Scope. A derivation on the two rules it rests on; a rule in range for the head, 10 ψ ladders × 5 partitions at 120 and 400 moves.

verifier: explore_headed_block.py

What the transient leaves

Where a strand rests is the ramp's, and how many there are is a census asked of the schedule with no walk rule

WHERE a strand rests: one above a ramp rung pj + 1, at or below the seat, 67 of 67, the seat setting the depth set and refinement filling it top-down, deepest rung first and never given up. A strand CLIMBS to its rest, 63 of the 67 moving. HOW MANY: an item strands if and only if its entry and every climb door price strictly under the runaway's splice price P = f(1, e + w), resting at the first rung whose exit door prices at or above P — exactly A(w) = 5, 6, 8, 9, 12, 15, 21, 27, 39 at widths 1 to 24 at every per-item cell, seed-invariant, moved by a third item per degree by exactly the census's own delta. The block dial's own limit, the diagonal partition, strands one item per losing block up to the count of degrees d ≥ 2 with f(d, 1) < P; the budget f(1, e) is the wrong threshold, width-blind at 4 while the cap runs 5 to 39. The head is two coordinates — the seat grades the depths, the width prices the admissions — which is why no scalar dial could find either; a wide TAIL decides whether there is any population at all, Q2's tail gap of 1 making its budget the least price and stranding nothing. At a second and third price the resting rungs and the top-down fill are unmoved and the count is the census recomputed from the price in force: a degree exponent shrinks the admitted set, the additive price widens it. Every count is identical at 400 and 1,200 moves.

Scope. A rule in range: 67 strands over 10 ψ ladders × 5 partitions; nine widths × ten partitions at (p, e) = (2, 4), the census certified against all seven recorded arithmetic cells first; both halves re-run at f = d²σ and f = d + σ.

verifiers: explore_headed_block.py, explore_splice_cap.py, explore_rung_schedule.py

The census's scope is three hypotheses, two of them the price's rule

The price must be nondecreasing in the degree, nondecreasing in the DOOR, and a tie at the bar must go to the lowest key; that is the list. Doors nondecreasing in time is a property of the landing rule — a tick is a ladder member and the next member is monotone — and not a hypothesis on the ladder. A fourth condition added on a witness, the born opening not undercut by a fresh discount, grades only the runaway's NAME on the exact ladder, where the census is empty: wherever a ladder carries a door of width 2 at all, a rival opened at the discount cannot pass it before degree 1 opens, and degree 1 then draws level and is cheaper or first at every shared rung (the overtaking lemma) — the riser — the item that runs away — reads (1, 0) at all 104 settled cells on the thirteen wide-door ladders, and the born set moves the census only through the entry branch the formula already reads. The bar is the door of the LAST step wider than the tail, e + w being ψ's one-overshoot case; on a ladder with two, reading the first undercounts at 6 of 9 prices. Three of the derivation's five steps close outright under a per-item clock, each certified at zero over 113 settled cells; the coarser-clock cap is derived as a CONTAINMENT, a shared tick only raising a door and shrinking the admitted set, no coarser cell seating an item the per-item census excludes at 144 settled cells. Delete degree-monotonicity and the riser is not the runaway and the climb runs into the tail, the formula undefined rather than wrong; delete door-monotonicity and the identity breaks at 7 of 10 cells, every failure the walk seating an item the census excludes; reverse the tie order and 8 of 9 crossed cells break the same one-sided way. Eight prices no closed form generates reproduce the census at 78 of 80 cells, the other two unreadable at the move budget. Unboundedness in the door is not needed.

Scope. A rule in range: nine prices × fourteen ladders, four of them ladders ψ cannot produce, each incompleteness and each necessity proved by a witness.

verifiers: explore_price_hypotheses.py, explore_census_theorem.py, explore_born_set.py

What the per-item end determines

A reading forgets the runaway's exponent and names the runaway as the last item to move the clock, which is sound only under a global clock; under a per-item clock most final states determine no reading at all, and a head is what gives that image a domain — every least-cost continuation of a headed cell locking on one reading where a constant gap of 2 or wider determines nothing. Both are The surviving openings. What a ring hands each ladder is The clock; what a run converges to when a limit does exist is The limit.
