Chained units

What a network of readers pays: two units in series against the one fused unit that sees every stream, a longer chain against its pairwise gains, and what a comparator costs inside a unit or on its output.

A stream is a numeral in radix b over a digit set D = {−a−..a+}, read from its most significant digit; its first n digits name a cell of values, of depth n, the interval of what the unseen digits can still spell, of width ω/bn with ω = M− + M+, where M± = a±/(b − 1) are the ends of the window [−M−, M+] a stream ranges over and M̂ = max(M−, M+). The set's slack ρ = a− + a+ + 1 − b counts the digits it holds beyond the radix; at slack 1 or more the cells of one depth overlap and a value inside the window has several writings, the two ends one apiece. A digit system is one (radix, digit set), written radix b over {−a−..a+}. A unit is a reader of one map of streams, emitting the map's value as a stream over the same digit set: it is read at lookahead c when its output's first t digits are a function of its inputs' first t + c, the least such c is the map's delay, and where the output runs led by o positions, its first digit weighing bo times an input's, L = c + o is the lookahead in the output's own scale; a map is representable at a system when that system's window holds that map's own range, a condition on the pair and never on the system alone: the product asks M̂² ≤ M+ and holds at 13 of the 20 systems of radices 2..5, while the multiply–add, led by one position, holds at all 20. The reader has no strategy: at any output position the inputs' prefixes name a box, the map sends the box to an interval, and the unit is dead exactly when that image strictly contains an overlap zone, the intersection of two consecutive output cells, a condition on the streams alone; a death below is certified by the game, an exhaustive search over the streams' prefixes, or by a scan of the boxes, except where a scope line names it the law's word, and a survival is the search running clean to a stated depth. A smooth map's exact lookahead in the output's own scale is the margin law's L*, the least L with bLρ ≥ Λ(a− + a+) for Λ, the box width rate, the sup over the window of the gradient's L¹ norm, and that L* is the unit's floor (the many-stream theorem, with the product, the reciprocal and the divider each unit here is built from). Below, the units are composed: a producer's stream fed to a consumer, and a comparator set inside a unit or on its output.

Chained units

The chained delay criterion

A chain is a product reader that sees x and y and emits a stream P = xy over the same digit set at lookahead L₁, which it can do only where the product is representable, feeding a sum reader that sees P and z, aligned with P, and emits xy + z at lookahead L₂; it reads the inputs at L₁ + L₂, and the fused unit is the multiply–add on Polynomial maps, one reader seeing all three streams. The product reader picks its digit by any rule wherever two are legal, and the sum reader may know the rule. What it then knows of xy is the fibre of the prefix it has seen, the union of the product images of every (x, y) prefix the rule sends there, and a cell holds the fibre plus z's box iff it holds the fibre's hull, an interval inside P's own cell, inset by α and β from the cell's ends in cell units. So the sum reader is dead at L₂ iff an integer lies in the two-sum's lattice interval with its ends drawn in, ((bL₂ − 2)M+ + β, bL₂ − (bL₂ − 2)M− − α), and its escape cost is the least α + β that empties the interval. The product reader pays for insets out of its slack: every image at level n + L₁ must sit in some hull, and consecutive hulls, at unit spacing, must overlap by the image width, so α + β ≤ (ω − 1) − 2M̂ω/bL₁, its slack budget, nonnegative exactly from the product's own floor on; the narrowed reader, one into cells inset by (α, β), reads at the product's law with the slack ρ − (b − 1)(α + β). The escape law: the chain survives at (L₁, L₂) iff the escape cost fits the budget. At every system of radices 2..5 it fits only where the interval is already empty, so no aligned chain reads below L₁* + L₂*, and where P can be written that sum is the chain's floor, the two units' own floors, whatever the multiplier does with its digits. The composition rule is the hardware field's own — "for a series of online operations, the overall latency is the sum of online delays" (Usman, Ercegovac and Lee, 2023) — and it is here a floor: no selection rule for the multiplier lowers the aligned sum. The threshold-miss law property

A product-type consumer multiplies the producer's stream, of top A in cell units, by a fresh stream z: for the product A = M̂², for the sum, emitted at lead 1, A = 2M̂/b. Its box width rate is Λ₂ = A + M̂, and with the producer's hulls inset by (α, β) it is alive at L₂ iff Aω + M̂(ω − α − β) ≤ bL₂(ω − 1), so its escape cost is E₂(L₂) = max(0, Λ₂ω − bL₂(ω − 1))/M̂: the miss of the consumer's own margin law's threshold, read in the producer's coordinate by dividing by the consumer's gradient M̂ along that stream. At slack 1, b(ω − 1) = ω, so the consumer's floor is at most 2 at every radix and 2 exactly when Λ₂ > 1; the threshold root is the M̂ with Λ₂(M̂) = 1, the only pair that can read below the floors' sum is (L₁*, L₂* − 1), and by the escape law the chain reads there iff E₂(1) = ω(Λ₂ − 1)/M̂ fits the producer's slack budget S(L₁*): 0 < Λ₂ − 1 ≤ (M̂/ω)S(L₁*), the threshold missed from above by at most the budget, itself O(1/b). The root is the consumer's own. Product-fed, Λ₂ = M̂ + M̂² and the root is 1/φ: with S(2) = (ω/b)(1 − 2M̂/b) the law reads b(M̂ + 1 − 1/M̂) ≤ 1 − 2M̂/b, the golden band, and the slack-1 values of M̂, j/(b − 1), landing in that window above an irrational root to radix 40 are the golden convergents from above and their mediants, nine of the 37 radices 4..40. Sum-fed, z(x + y), Λ₂ = M̂(1 + 2/b) and the root is b/(b + 2), rational and moving with the radix; the sum's images at depth n + L₁ − 1 sit on a grid of pitch 1/bL₁ in cell units, so its budget is (ω − 1) − (2ω − 1)/bL₁, a pitch above the dense producer's, and under either the law gains at j = b − 2, which is the least j/(b − 1) inside the window from radix 5 on and below that is not above the root at all: radix b over {−2..b − 2} and its reversal, at (2, 1), at every radix from 5 to 40 and at no other slack-1 system, M̂ above the root by 0.0568 at radix 9 falling to 0.0220 at 40, while below radix 5 the nearest slack-1 system, radix 4 over {−1..3}, misses by 7/16 under the lattice budget. So the convergents are the grid j/(b − 1) meeting an irrational root's O(1/b) window and nothing more, and a chain built to gain takes the least grid point above its consumer's root, checked against the budget. No system of slack 2 or more gains for either consumer to radix 40. The sum-fed call has its first support at radix 5 over {−2..3} at (2, 1), where the covering search finds an assignment of the sum's images to the producer's hulls at every depth to 4, while (2, 0), which the law calls dead, dies at depth 1 and (1, 1), the producer below its floor, at depth 2.

The matching law property

The chain of two units lengthens to a chain of n units: a head, the product xy or the sum x + y, feeding n − 1 product-type consumers in series, unit j ≥ 2 multiplying unit j − 1's stream by a fresh stream aligned with it, the chain's delay the sum of the units' lookaheads. At slack 1 every M̂ is at most 1, so the tops fall along the chain; the head's own map must be representable besides, which at slack 1 is a separate condition on the system and not a consequence of the slack — radix b over {−(b − 1)..1} has M̂ = 1 and a window that does not hold M̂². Unit j's input has top, the largest value it can carry in cell units, A₁M̂j−2, with A₁ = M̂² under the product head and 2M̂/b under the sum, so its box width rate Λj = M̂ + A₁M̂j−2 and its miss Λj − 1 never increase along the chain. Read with its input's hulls inset by I = α + β, unit j is alive at L iff Λjω − M̂I ≤ bL(ω − 1); its floor L*j is the least alive L at I = 0, and it keeps for its own consumer the leftover Sj(L, I) = (ω − 1) − (Λjω − M̂I)/bL, the slack budget's hull-overlap argument carried past the first link. The head's leftover S₁(L) is its own slack budget, (ω − 1) − 2M̂ω/bL for the product and the lattice budget (ω − 1) − (2ω − 1)/bL for the sum. Every leftover, so every inset, is below ω − 1, and every verdict and every leftover rises with the inset. A link (j − 1, j) carries a pairwise gain when 0 < ω(Λj − 1) ≤ M̂Sj−1(L*j−1, 0): the threshold-miss law read on that link alone, the upstream unit at its floor over an uninset input.

The law: at slack 1 the chain's least delay is the sum of its units' floors less ν, the largest number of gain-carrying links no two of which share a unit, outside the two windows below. Comparators

The comparator delay theorem

The margin law never grants lookahead 0 to a map whose Lipschitz constant is 1 or more, and the field's table gives max and min on-line delay 0 (Ercegovac and Lang, Digital Arithmetic, 2003, Table 9.1); the reason is the lattice. Every cell of depth n, the cell of an n-digit prefix, has its lower end on (Z − M−)/bn and width ω/bn, the output cells included, and a cell of depth n meets a cell of depth n − 1 inside one of the latter's children, the depth-n cells whose lower ends run through the parent's lattice points from its own lower end up, the last ending at the parent's top (the cell lemma). So a cell-valued map, one whose image of every box of prefixes lies inside some cell of the same depth, reads at lookahead 0 at every system: the committed cell, the one the reader's emitted digits name, holds the image, the image lies in a cell, the cell meets the committed one inside a child, and that child is a legal digit. max and min of any number of streams and their compositions are cell-valued, the image of a box under max lying in the input cell with the larger top: the clamp min(max(x, y), z), the rectifier max(x, 0), whose image is the point 0, inside the committed output cell, when the INPUT cell lies below 0, and the identity as the case of one stream. |x| is cell-valued iff its mirror, the cell reflected through 0, is a cell, (b − 1) | (a+ − a−), which within the radix means a symmetric set; at every asymmetric system tried the reader dies at 0 in one round, and every system survives at 1, the margin's grant. The comparator is free. A reader's death is a property of the image alone: an image inside the committed cell fails every child iff it strictly contains an overlap zone, and a cell strictly contains no zone. A fused unit G(w, max(y, z)), one reader seeing every stream, has each box image inside G's image of a box of its own inputs at the same depth, the cell of y or of z with the larger top standing for the comparator, so a fused death is a death of G; and on the diagonal y = z the adversary hands the reader G's own game, so the fused unit reads no earlier. For every map G of streams the delay of G(w, max(y, z)) equals the delay of G(w, y), min likewise. A comparator on an output, max(G(x), z) with z a stream the reader sees beside G's inputs, costs exactly G's delay too. At most, since its image lies inside G's or inside z's cell. At least, because the constant stream of the lowest digit −a− spells the window's bottom −M− exactly, and fed as z it makes max(G(x), z) equal to G(x) wherever G's range lies inside z's window [−M−, M+], so a reader of the comparator is a reader of G at the same lookahead: every map at a representable system read aligned with its inputs, and any map whose partner is read at the output's own scale; min is the mirror case, the constant top stream. The sum led by one position with its partner at the input scale is the case left, z sitting inside the sum's range and the comparator fed the bottom a clamp of the sum rather than the sum. There a death at a box is a property of the sum of the two prefixes as integers alone, and adding bc+1 to that sum, legal while the prefixes stay in range, carries the death one overlap zone up, so every death has a translate whose zone lies above −M− at every system with slack 1 or more (the translation lemma), and there the clamp's image is the sum's and the clamp dies with it. max(x, y) + z reads at the lookahead criterion's floor for x + z and x·max(y, z) at the product's. A datapath's delay table is its arithmetic skeleton's with the comparators deleted: a digit-serial network's rectifier and pooling cost no lookahead, fused into the multiply–add or not, on an output as inside a unit.
