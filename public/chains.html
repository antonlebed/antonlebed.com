Chained units

What a network of readers pays: two units in series against the one fused unit that sees every stream, and what a comparator costs inside a unit or on its output.

A stream is a numeral in radix b over a digit set D = {−a−..a+}, read from its most significant digit; its first n digits name a cell of values, the interval of what the unseen digits can still spell, of width ω/bn with ω = M− + M+, where M± = a±/(b − 1) are the ends of the window [−M−, M+] a stream ranges over and M̂ = max(M−, M+). The set's slack ρ = a− + a+ + 1 − b counts the digits it holds beyond the radix; at slack 1 or more the cells of one depth overlap and a value has several writings. A digit system is one (radix, digit set), written radix b over {−a−..a+}. A unit is a reader of one map of streams, emitting the map's value as a stream over the same digit set: it is read at lookahead c when its output's first t digits are a function of its inputs' first t + c, the least such c is the map's delay, and where the output runs led by o positions, its first digit weighing bo times an input's, L = c + o is the lookahead in the output's own scale; a map is representable at a system when the window holds its range at its lead. The reader has no strategy: at any output position the inputs' prefixes name a box, the map sends the box to an interval, and the unit is dead exactly when that image strictly contains an overlap zone, the intersection of two consecutive output cells, a condition on the streams alone. A smooth map's exact delay is the margin law's L*, the least L with bLρ ≥ Λ(a− + a+) for Λ, the box width rate, the sup over the window of the gradient's L¹ norm, and that L* is the unit's floor (the many-stream theorem, with the product, the reciprocal and the divider each unit here is built from). Below, the units are composed: a producer's stream fed to a consumer, and a comparator set inside a unit or on its output.

Chained units

The chained delay criterion

A chain is a product reader that sees x and y and emits a stream P = xy over the same digit set at lookahead L₁, feeding a sum reader that sees P and z, aligned with P, and emits xy + z at lookahead L₂; it reads the inputs at L₁ + L₂, and the fused unit is the multiply–add on Polynomial maps, one reader seeing all three streams. The product reader picks its digit by any rule wherever two are legal, and the sum reader may know the rule. What it then knows of xy is the fibre of the prefix it has seen, the union of the product images of every (x, y) prefix the rule sends there, and a cell holds the fibre plus z's box iff it holds the fibre's hull, an interval inside P's own cell, inset by α and β from the cell's ends in cell units. So the sum reader is dead at L₂ iff an integer lies in the two-sum's lattice interval with its ends drawn in, ((bL₂ − 2)M+ + β, bL₂ − (bL₂ − 2)M− − α), and its escape cost is the least α + β that empties the interval. The product reader pays for insets out of its slack: every image at level n + L₁ must sit in some hull, and consecutive hulls, at unit spacing, must overlap by the image width, so α + β ≤ (ω − 1) − 2M̂ω/bL₁, its slack budget, nonnegative exactly from the product's own floor on; a reader into cells inset by (α, β) reads at the product's law with the slack ρ − (b − 1)(α + β). The escape law: the chain survives at (L₁, L₂) iff the escape cost fits the budget. At every system of radices 2..5 it fits only where the interval is already empty, so the chain's floor is L₁* + L₂*, the two units' own floors, whatever the multiplier does with its digits. The composition rule is the hardware field's own — "for a series of online operations, the overall latency is the sum of online delays" (Usman, Ercegovac and Lee, 2023) — and it is here a floor: no selection rule for the multiplier lowers the aligned sum. The fused unit sits below the chain wherever the product's margin absorbs the addition outright, its floor the product's own, and at radix 2 over {−1..1}, where it pays one for z and the adder alone pays two; the two are level where the product's floor is 1 and both pay one for z:

The law composes. A product consumer (xy)·z, a second multiplier fed the first's stream, reads the box width rate with one side narrowed: alive at L₂ iff M̂²ω + M̂(ω − α − β) ≤ bL₂(ω − 1), so its escape cost ω(1 + M̂) − bL₂(ω − 1)/M̂ is continuous where the sum's was a lattice gap, and need and budget bind at the same corner |xy| = M̂². A pole's lead propagates by depth, not by digit choice. Comparators

The comparator delay theorem

The margin law never grants lookahead 0 to a map whose Lipschitz constant is 1 or more, and the field's table gives max and min on-line delay 0 (Ercegovac and Lang, Digital Arithmetic, 2003, Table 9.1); the reason is the lattice. Every cell of depth n, the cell of an n-digit prefix, has its lower end on (Z − M−)/bn and width ω/bn, the output cells included, and a cell of depth n meets a cell of depth n − 1 inside one of the latter's children, the depth-n cells whose lower ends run through the parent's lattice points from its own lower end up, the last ending at the parent's top (the cell lemma). So a cell-valued map, one whose image of every box of prefixes lies inside some cell of the same depth, reads at lookahead 0 at every system: the committed cell, the one the reader's emitted digits name, holds the image, the image lies in a cell, the cell meets the committed one inside a child, and that child is a legal digit. max and min of any number of streams and their compositions are cell-valued, the image of a box under max lying in the input cell with the larger top: the clamp min(max(x, y), z), the rectifier max(x, 0), whose image is a point inside the committed cell when the cell sits below 0, and the identity as the case of one stream. |x| is cell-valued iff its mirror, the cell reflected through 0, is a cell, (b − 1) | (a+ − a−), which within the radix means a symmetric set; at every asymmetric system tried the reader dies at 0 in one round, and every system survives at 1, the margin's grant. The comparator is free. A reader's death is a property of the image alone: an image inside the committed cell fails every child iff it strictly contains an overlap zone, and a cell strictly contains no zone. A fused unit G(w, max(y, z)), one reader seeing every stream, has each box image inside G's image of a box of its own inputs at the same depth, the cell of y or of z with the larger top standing for the comparator, so a fused death is a death of G; and on the diagonal y = z the adversary hands the reader G's own game, so the fused unit reads no earlier. For every map G of streams the delay of G(w, max(y, z)) equals the delay of G(w, y), min likewise. A comparator on an output, max(G(x), z) with z a stream the reader sees beside G's inputs, costs exactly G's delay too. At most, since its image lies inside G's or inside z's cell. At least, because the constant stream of the lowest digit −a− spells the window's bottom −M− exactly, and fed as z it makes max(G(x), z) equal to G(x) wherever G's range lies inside z's window [−M−, M+], so a reader of the comparator is a reader of G at the same lookahead: every map at a representable system read aligned with its inputs, and any map whose partner is read at the output's own scale; min is the mirror case, the constant top stream. The sum led by one position with its partner at the input scale is the case left, z sitting inside the sum's range and the comparator fed the bottom a clamp of the sum rather than the sum. There a death at a box is a property of the sum of the two prefixes as integers alone, and adding bc+1 to that sum, legal while the prefixes stay in range, carries the death one overlap zone up, so every death has a translate whose zone lies above −M− at every system with slack 1 or more (the translation lemma), and there the clamp's image is the sum's and the clamp dies with it. max(x, y) + z reads at the lookahead criterion's floor for x + z and x·max(y, z) at the product's. A datapath's delay table is its arithmetic skeleton's with the comparators deleted: a digit-serial network's rectifier and pooling cost no lookahead, fused into the multiply–add or not, on an output as inside a unit.

Scope. Theorem for the cell-valued maps and the free comparator inside a unit and on an output, at every radix and contiguous digit set with slack 1 or more, the output aligned with the inputs and led by one position for the map with an additive part; the exhaustive search is the check, 80 systems over five maps surviving at 0 uncertified (max, min and the rectifier at the 20 systems of radices 2..5, max of three and the clamp at the 10 of radices 2..4), the fused sum at the two-sum's floor at 10 of 10 systems of radices 2..4 and the fused product at the product's at the 7 representable systems, certified one lookahead below wherever a lookahead below exists (the 6 sums with floor 2, all 7 products). The |x| clause is a criterion at the 13 representable systems of radices 2..5, a− ≤ a+: the 6 symmetric survive at 0 by the theorem, the 7 asymmetric are certified dead at 0, and all 13 survive the search at 1. On an output, with the adversary confined to the constant bottom or top stream, the certificate comes at the bare unit's own round at all 7 representable products aligned with their inputs (radices 2..4) and at the 10 sums led by one position with floor 2 (radices 2..5), the partner read at the output's scale or the input's, and the full search survives at the floor at every one.
