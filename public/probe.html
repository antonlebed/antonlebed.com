The sighted probe

A probe that reads a grown state only through whether a move leaves its period fixed sees one number — and every move keeps an exact ledger on it.

The worlds here are grown by a growth law: extend the modulus N by the least m ≥ 2 meeting a structural demand; a prime the state carries is one of its windows, seated at the exponent it holds. An observer inside such a world holds its genesis posterior (the inside view). A second percept sits beside it and is far poorer. Read each state through its period λ(N), the exponent of the unit group of Z/N — the least e with ae ≡ 1 for every unit a — and offer it a move: does the move leave the period fixed? The wall W(L) is the largest modulus whose period divides L: 2 when L is odd, 2v₂(L)+2 when L is even, times pvp(L)+1 over the odd primes p with (p−1) | L, the doors of L. A modulus divides its own wall and the wall never falls, so a move m is transparent — leaves λ unchanged — iff Nm divides W(λ(N)): the transparent moves from N are exactly the divisors of the transparency headroom V(N) = W(λ(N))/N. That one number is the probe's entire reading of a state, and it is a second percept rather than a second object: 855 and 2565 share the prime support {3, 5, 19} and split the headroom (161616 against 53872), while 10 and 11 share the headroom 24 across the supports {2, 5} and {11}, so neither reading determines the other. Where the reading merges two states, what telling them apart costs and how often it happens is the blind spot.

The exponent identity theorem

The percept is the machine's register file. For an odd prime l whose door is open, (l−1) | λ(N) — which every seated l satisfies — the slack δl = vl(odd λ(N)) − vl(N) + 1 equals vl(V) exactly, the prime 2 carrying its own formula (v₂(V) = v₂(λ) + 2 − v₂(N) at even λ). At a shut door the two part company, vl(V) = 0 while δl ≥ 1, because pushing an unseated l moves λ at the primes of l−1 rather than at l. The slack vector is the counter vector of the growth machine — the counter machine the growth walk realizes, one counter per seated odd prime (the reading/hosting square) — so a Minsky machine's counters and an observer's eye read the same numbers: the counters are the seated odd primes, and the probe sees the prime 2 besides.

Scope. One line of algebra once both definitions are unfolded, and checked on 1973 open-door and 4007 shut-door cells over states to 599 at ten odd primes, 1998 at the prime 2 to 1999.

The headroom ledger theorem

Every move splits into what it spends of the percept and what it opens:

V(Nm)  =  V(N)gcd⁡(m, V(N))⋅G′,G′∈Z>0.V(Nm) \;=\; \frac{V(N)}{\gcd(m,\,V(N))}\cdot G' , \qquad G' \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0} .

The wall's gain at each prime covers whatever the move fails to pay out of V, which is what makes the opening factor G′, the premium, an integer, and the two halves are one quotient once N cancels: G′ is the wall gain W(λ(Nm))/W(λ(N)) divided by the unpaid part m/gcd(m, V), so "G′ is an integer" says exactly that the unpaid part divides the gain. A move can never destroy more headroom than it spends: the burn is gcd(m, V), truncated subtraction on the exponents, and V rises exactly when G′ > gcd(m, V). A rise needs λ to move, but λ moving does not buy one — a move that also burns transparency comes out behind (N = 3 under m = 16 sends V from 8 to 5).

Scope. Proved prime by prime for all N and m, and confirmed over 23084 moves (N < 400, m < 60), 13134 of them with more than one prime in the move.

verifiers: explore_headroom.py, explore_premium.py

The premium's closed form theorem

A move touches only its own primes. With el = vl(m) and the seated depth cl = vl(W(λ)) − vl(V), the new period is lcm(λ, lcm{λ(lcl+el) : l | m}) and

G′  =  W(lcm⁡(λ, lcm⁡{λ(l cl+el):l∣m}))W(λ)⋅∏l∣ml el−min⁡(el, vl(V)),G' \;=\; \frac{W\bigl(\operatorname{lcm}(\lambda,\ \operatorname{lcm}\{\lambda(l^{\,c_l+e_l}) : l \mid m\})\bigr)} {W(\lambda)\cdot\prod_{l \mid m} l^{\,e_l-\min(e_l,\,v_l(V))}} ,

so the state enters through its λ and one coordinate of the percept per prime of the move, and through nothing else. On a move coprime to the state every cl vanishes and the ascent is a function of λ alone — which the probe does not have; it reads V. And only there: λ(3) = λ(8) = 2, yet the move 3 pays a premium of 7 at one state and 1 at the other, because V(3) = 8 and V(8) = 3 sit at v₃ = 0 and 1. The primes of a move interact: at λ = 1 the move 35 opens the door 13, which neither 5 nor 7 opens alone, since 13 − 1 = 12 draws its 4 from λ(5) and its 3 from λ(7). So a move's premium is not assembled from its factors' premiums, and the reason is that a door reads a divisibility of the whole λ.

Scope. Proved for all N and all m; the coordinates vl(V) over l | m are tight (no coordinate is spare, checked per prime) and the coprime case is a theorem on 1218 cells. The fresh-import special case shows the wall's 2-part 2v₂+2 is load-bearing: the odd-prime form of the formula fails on 85 of 18291 imports, all at q = 2.

verifiers: explore_composite_move.py, explore_premium.py

Only the quotient is path-free theorem

The wall gain is a ratio of endpoint values, so it telescopes along a split m = ab, and the unpaid part does not: G′(N, ab) = G′(N, a)·G′(Na, b)· gcd(ab, V)/(gcd(a, V)·gcd(b, V(Na))). Since V(Nab) is one state either way, an inflated opening half arrives with an equally inflated spending half — the gross flows are path-dependent and only their quotient is not. The correction is the reciprocal of an integer every time, so the premium is submultiplicative along every factorization: a split move never undercounts what the move opened, with equality iff at every l | b the first step opens no l-door below the exponent the second step pays at. What a move spends and what it opens are properties of the move together with the granularity it is watched at; the wall gain alone is a state function of the endpoints.

Scope. Proved prime by prime over 52536 splits, the correction exactly 1 on 97.0% of them; the equality condition holds with 0 disagreements.

verifier: explore_composite_move.py

The wall map is a closure operator theorem

N ↦ W(λ(N)) is monotone, inflationary and idempotent, so a transparency episode — a run of transparent moves — is a walk inside the interval [N, W(λ(N))], the divisor lattice of V, and its fixed points are V = 1, the closed states with no transparent move left — the tombstones of the mortal fate, the growth fate that halts (the three fates). Above 2 every closed state is divisible by 24, for an elementary reason: λ is even from 3 up, so the wall carries 2v₂+2 ≥ 8 and (3−1) | λ admits the 3; the state 2 escapes as the only closed state with an odd period. The jump straight to the closure, m = V, is admissible from every live state (V > 1) and priced by the one coordinate the probe holds, so a transparency episode is a capped walk whose coordinate descends to 1 (48, 24, 12, 6, 3, 1 off seed 5), and mortality is the absorbing branch of that walk rather than an exception to it: the fate that dies is the one whose cap runs out of coordinate.

Scope. Proved outright; idempotence and inflation checked below 3000, monotonicity below 400 against every divisor; the jump's admissibility and the descent measured at 13 states off seeds 3, 5, 7.

verifiers: explore_headroom.py, explore_standing_move.py
