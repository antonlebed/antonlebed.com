Growth

What the tower's demands grow when nothing chooses: three fates, one per demand, what growth buys that choice does not, what of the tower is authored by size, and the structure a growth law needs beneath the ring.

  • Thermal and density — the hot limit, the zeta measure and the density theorem

A growth law is a structural demand plus a greedy move: extend the modulus N by the least m ≥ 2 meeting the demand. No demand mentions primes. Throughout, λ(N) is the exponent of the unit group, the least L with aL = 1 for every unit a. A prime's window is its slot in the state, OPENED by a move that seats it and DEEPENED by one that raises its exponent, and the door at a prime is the least move there that raises λ against the whole state at once. A trajectory's seed is the state it starts from. One character recurs through everything below: the archimedean place deleted by the construction (The Object). It is the cost axis inside "least m"; softened, it becomes a temperature (Thermal and density); read as an operation, it is the borrow whose absence makes growth's depth face decidable (Computation).

The three fates

The three fates rule

Greedy growth laws trichotomize by demand. Breadth: demanding independence, that the extension split as Z/Nm ≅ Z/N × Z/m, forces the pick to be the least prime not dividing N, since every smaller candidate is built from primes N carries (the least-new lemma): every new channel is a field and squarefree-ness never breaks, with neither demanded. From seed 1 the trajectory is the primorial tower; from any seed the picks are the missing primes in increasing order (the healing rule: a seed's excess powers persist, healing adds windows and never removes them), so every trajectory's window set converges to all primes. Demanding new idempotents grows the same tower, and so does the rate optimizer, idempotents per bit, whose unique argmax at every primorial is the next prime: three demands, one attractor. Depth: demanding new dynamics, that λ grow, collapses onto one prime's column (the lock-prime law). Mortality: demanding transparent growth, capacity with λ frozen, halts: λ is monotone, the rings with λ(n) | L are finitely many with a maximum W(L), the wall, and the greedy cannot stall below it, since while N < W the least prime factor of W/N is a legal move, so it dies at exactly W(λ(seed)). Every trajectory's limit is a supernatural number, a formal product over the primes with exponents in {0, 1, …, ∞}, and the fates are three PROPERTIES of it, not a partition: every prime seated, some prime at infinite depth, a finite integer. The greedy laws realize exactly one each, and that purity is greed's: a free policy under the same demand reaches a limit carrying breadth and depth at once (The limit). The tower's axiom is thereby an output: the primorial tower is the attractor of exactly one demand, independence, which is the Chinese-remainder splitting read as a growth demand.

Scope. Proved, verified exhaustively to the range each clause names: the least-new lemma and the idempotent demand N ≤ 105, the healing rule 100 seeds × 25 steps, the realized orders exactly λ's divisors N ≤ 1200, the wall at seeds 2 to 100 (seed 73 climbs to W(72) = 20,174,525,280 in 13 steps and dies), the optimizer k ≤ 6 at horizon 1000. Threshold-greedy laws over extensions of Z/N; additive moves and non-cyclic ambients are open.

The wall is Adams' number rule

"λ(m) | L" and "aL ≡ 1 (mod m) for every unit a" are one condition, so W(L) is the number m(L) = gcdk kN(kL − 1) of Adams (On the groups J(X) II, 1965), and its closed form at even L is his theorem: W(L) = denominator(BL/2L), BL the Bernoulli number: 24, 240, 504, 480, 264, 65520 at L = 2, 4, …, 12, the orders of the image of the J-homomorphism in the stable homotopy groups of spheres, by the same definition and not by coincidence; nothing here uses the homotopy. The squarefree kernel of W(L) is the von Staudt–Clausen denominator ∏ p over (p−1) | L, the largest n with aL+1 ≡ a for all a; for odd L, W = 2. That the wall has this depth is Z's, not mortality's. Over F2[x] the same corner is elementary,

W(L)=∏d: (2d−1)∣L(∏deg⁡P=dP)2v2(L),W(L)=\prod_{d:\,(2^d-1)\mid L}\Bigl(\prod_{\deg P=d}P\Bigr)^{2^{v_2(L)}},

collapsing at L = (2n − 1)·2v to (x2n + x)2v: at v = 0 the wall ring is the product of every finite field whose degree divides n, and at v > 0 that polynomial's power, no longer a product of fields, the exponent being what the clock's 2-part buys. The wall is generic; only its value is arithmetic-specific.

Scope. The gcd form is verified at every L ≤ 200 and the closed form against Bernoulli numbers computed from scratch at every even L ≤ 200; the F2[x] form is proved and brute-verified at 44 values of L over every monic of degree ≤ 10.

The lock-prime law rule

Under dynamics-greed the minimal λ-growing move is always a prime power qr, and per prime the door is closed form: at odd q, deepening from depth e ≥ 1 costs qvq(λ) − e + 2, and opening costs q when (q − 1) ∤ λ and qvq(λ)+2 otherwise; q = 2 runs the same scan with contributions 1, 2, 2d−2. Door costs are powers of distinct primes, hence distinct: the walk is deterministic with no tie-break axis. Every minimal move is a deepening, a fresh opening, or a ghost opening, a prime q | λ with (q − 1) ∤ λ, a period already haunting the dynamics with no window of its own, a cheap door that never binds. A deepening or fresh opening establishes the recurrence invariant vq(λ) = eq − 1 (eq − 2 at q = 2), which prices q's door at q forever while every rival door is nondecreasing and never equal, so the first non-ghost pick locks the trajectory: all later picks deepen that prime, the one hiccup λ(4) = λ(8) = 2 buying a 2-lock one cost-4 double step. Ghosts strictly increase and divide λ(seed), so the lock is certain within ωodd(λ(seed)) + 1 picks and the basin map, seed to lock prime, is decidable at the first non-ghost move. A ghost's dowry, the factors of q − 1 joining λ, closes cheaper destinies behind it: seed 11 ghosts 5 and locks 7; seed 71 ghosts 5, 7 and locks 17, ghost 7's dowry 6 blocking the 9-door that would have locked 3; seed 20231 ghosts 5, 7, 17 and locks 19, the bound tight. From the void, seed 1, greedy dynamics grows 1, 3, 9, 27, …: 2 is invisible to greed at birth.

Scope. Proved; the menu verified exhaustively N ≤ 20,000, the lock over 2,000 seeds × 30 post-lock steps, the census to 20,000. A mixed-characteristic privilege: in equal characteristic the same demand sprawls (the local module law). Whether the ghost wander is unbounded over seeds is open.

Every prime is some seed's lock rule

For any prime q, the seed q·P with P a prime ≡ 1 mod B and ≢ 1 mod q, for the enriched modulus B = lcm(lcm{p − 1 : p ≤ q}, rcr for primes r < q), locks q at its first move: every door below q is blocked past q by construction, every door above costs more, no ghost fires, and Dirichlet supplies P. In range one blocker P = 55441 serves q = 29, 31, 37, 41 and 43, because B is the tower's own λ at the ring Z/q# and 31 to 43 are a run of primes whose arrival leaves λ standing: the tower manufactures basins for its own rungs, its k-th rung being Z/pk#.

The cold doors rule

From every N ≥ 3 opening the 2-window costs at least 16 and the 3-window at least 9, and the floors are unique to 2 and 3: every prime from 5 up opens at face value q from some state, a λ that q − 1 does not divide, and λ = 2 serves them all. Dynamics-greed marks up exactly the two primes independence buys first; the void is the exception that proves the scope, its first move buying 3 at face value.

Scope. Proved; the face-value states asserted q ≤ 47.

The two-adic prices rule

The prime 2 pays three separate-looking premiums: the cold door at 16 where 3's is 9; the double step λ(4) = λ(8), so a 2-locked trajectory jumps its 2-part from 4 to 16 in one cost-4 move; and the extra exponent a 2-door buys, one move able to raise the exponent of 2 in λ by two where an odd prime's rises by one (the budget inequality). They are two facts wearing three names. The splitting: Z2× ≅ {±1} × (1 + 4Z2) where every odd Zp× is procyclic, its finite shadow λ(2e) = 2e−2 for e ≥ 3. The evenness: −1 has order 2 in every odd residue field, so λ is even wherever an odd prime divides N. A counterfeit 2-channel wearing the odd pattern λ*(2e) = 2e−1 drops the door to 2v+2 and kills the double step and the two-step rise at once, seed 4's moves running 2, 2, 2, … in place of 4, 2, 2, …, while 3's floor stands at 9 on the evenness alone. The cold door is their conjunction: on an opening state the 2-door is 2v+3 at v = v2(λ), the +3 the splitting's exponent and the floor v = 1 the evenness's, and the markups compound, both tables agreeing at door 4 at a hypothetical v = 0. Floor 16 real, 8 counterfeit, 3's unmoved at 9. The two facts are the two valuations of −1 − 1 = −2, an order and a depth read at disjoint places, of the one torsion unit Z owns.

Scope. The splitting and the evenness are proved; the price recomputation in both worlds is exhaustive over odd N ≤ 20,000, with the cyclic control at every odd p ≤ 13 to exponent 5 and the double step run from seed 4.

Growth vs choice

Point the demand at a capability instead of structure and compare two designers: design-by-choice picks the cheapest modulus carrying the capability outright; design-by-growth pays for it in moves. Patient growth, one move straight to the capability, is choice by definition. Growth never beats choice on size-cost, its terminal modulus being one of choice's candidates; what it buys is procedure, bounded local scans in place of a global search over moduli it would have to already understand.

The myopia gap rule

Impatient growth, every move making progress, overpays on order-m capabilities, that m divide λ, at 126 of the 139 multi-part m ≤ 200, mean ratio 4.2 and worst 33 at m = 190, where the one prime 191 covers every part but the greedy has bought two parts cheaply and pays for 191 anyway: a combined move, a prime ≡ 1 mod several parts or the right prime power, buys several parts at once and is invisible to progress reward. Single-part capabilities never gap, the first progress step being the jump. The 13 ties are trajectories whose cheap steps multiply out to the optimum, deepening a prime power or buying the optimal prime pair one part at a time.

Scope. The single-part rule is proved; the gap is a sweep over m ≤ 200 and the tie mechanism an observation on the 13.

The emergent designed tower rule

"Keep √−1, grow independently" picks exactly the missing admissible primes, 2 and p ≡ 1 mod 4, in increasing order: the least coprime capability-keeping move is a prime from the admissible set, since an admissible composite is a product of at least two new admissible primes and so at least the square of the least one, and constrained healing follows by the healing rule's induction. What size authors

Every law above prices by size. Between candidate windows the only canonical structure is ring maps, and a unital map Z/a → Z/b exists iff b | a: the window world orders moves by divisibility, distinct primes are an antichain, and "least prime" is external data. Costless independence admits every coprime move, composites included (seed 2 may pick 9; seed 3 may run 5, 7, 11, … with 2 never entering), so fields, squarefree-ness and healing are authored by the selection. Replace "least m" by an arbitrary cost c(m) and a tie-break, a rule choosing among the cheapest admissible moves, and ask which outputs survive.

The properness criterion criterion

If c is strictly monotone under strict divisibility, as the prime-factor count Ω is, every pick is a prime under every tie-break, a composite coprime to the state having a prime divisor that is coprime too and strictly cheaper: fields and squarefree-ness are window-purchasable. The destination asks for one thing more. A prime p is guaranteed to open its window under EVERY tie-break iff its sublevel set {q prime : c(q) ≤ c(p)} is finite: while p is admissible every pick costs at most c(p) and consumes one member of that set permanently, so a finite set leaves p the eventual unique minimum, while an infinite one feeds a tie-break that starves p forever, Ω being the specimen, every prime at one cost and an odd-preferring tie-break keeping 2 unpicked indefinitely. Healing holds under every tie-break exactly when the cost is proper, every sublevel set finite. Of the deleted place, "least m" uses properness and one linear extension, and nothing else about size is load-bearing for where a breadth trajectory ends.

Scope. Both directions proved; fields for free verified across 3 tie-break policies × 6 seeds × 20 steps, the starvation specimen at 30 steps. The demand throughout is independence.

The route is the deleted place's rule

What size does author is the route, the order the primes arrive in: any enumeration of the primes whatever is the route of some proper divisibility-monotone cost, so entry order, and with it the rung identities, the order the primes enter λ and the runs where it stands still, is the deleted place's own contribution. The one natural window-visible arrival constraint, every prime factor of q − 1 already resident, is exactly the order of Pratt's primality certificates, the parents of q being the primes of q − 1: it forces 2 first and then any Fermat prime, and the increasing order is one of its linear extensions among 8.5·1012 at 20 primes. In bits, the first 20 primes carry about 61 bits of route, the Pratt order supplies about 18 of them and the archimedean choice the rest. The tower as a limit is window-authored; the tower as a sequence is authored by the deleted place.

Scope. Rule in range: three scrambled proper costs entering all 46 primes in range, each at its own rank and all at one destination; the extension counts by exact enumeration over the 20-prime order. Whether Pratt's is the strongest window-visible arrival demand still compatible with the all-primes destination is open.

What a growth law needs beneath it

Every law on this page is stated over a ring. Take the ring away and ask what each fate used: the theorems survive, and they stratify by structural floor. A monoid is a set with an associative multiplication and a 1; it is cancellative when am = bm forces a = b, and free, a unique-factorization world, when every element is a product of atoms in exactly one way as a multiset. A demand is up-closed when anything above an admissible move is admissible too. The crystal is the squarefree limit: every atom entered once, at depth 1, none deeper.

The selection ladder rule

To DIE a law needs only order; to REACH EVERYTHING it needs multiplication; to BE ARITHMETIC, independent windows, squarefree, the crystal, it needs unique factorization. Floor 0, pure order. Any demand confining the trajectory to a finite region absorbs, under every policy, within that region's height. For the interval demand, admissible = everything above the state in the region, mortality's own shape, the grave is a no-successor element, unique exactly when the reachable region has a maximum; the tower dies at ONE wall because the states above its seed with λ frozen, the multiples of the seed dividing W(λ(seed)), have one. A region with several maximal elements leaves the grave to the policy: greedy dies at the cheapest, thermal splits between them, the multi-tombstone freedom, a degree of freedom the tower never shows. Floor 1, a cancellative commutative monoid with a summable multiplicative weight. Up-closed nonempty demands reach the top of any such monoid: the injection m ↦ am is cancellativity, so the admissible mass inside the multiples of a is at least w(a) times the whole, uniformly in the state and for every element rather than only for atoms, so cofinality needs no atomicity and countability comes free from summability. The machinery reading a world's genesis from inside it (The inside view) runs at this floor. Floor 2, freeness. Each atom enters once, its entry depth geometric, so the limit is the crystal with probability ∏g(1 − w(g)) = 1/ζM by the Euler product: the partition function of thermal breadth is the zeta function OF THE MONOID, Riemann for the integers and rational for F2[x].

Scope. Each floor's law is proved at its own structure; the multi-tombstone freedom is a rule over a 240-region census, 30 policy-dependent, minimal specimen one element below two incomparable ones. Cancellativity is floor 1's hypothesis rather than a proved necessity. Instance worlds: the integers, monic F2[x], and the numerical monoid below.

The coprimality collapse rule

Freeness is necessary. In the numerical monoid ⟨x², x³⟩, cancellative and not free, every xn with n ≥ 5 carries both atoms, so every independence trajectory dies by its second move while the same world's up-closed demands still reach its top: without unique factorization breadth collapses into mortality, and breadth's immortality is a unique-factorization privilege. The partition identity breaks with it, the Dirichlet sum 13/12 against the Euler product 1024/945 at t = 1/4, first diverging at x6 = x²x²x² = x³x³, one element and two multisets.

Scope. Proved, the 2-move census exhaustive (four maximal paths).

Between floors 1 and 2 there is room, the mezzanine, and one ring straddles it. The elements of Z[√−5] form a monoid that is cancellative but not free, since 6 = 2·3 = (1+√−5)(1−√−5), so they sit on the mezzanine; its ideals are free, so they sit at floor 2. The class group, the ideals modulo the principal ones, those a single element generates, of order h, is what separates them, and thermal growth sees the gap three ways.

The sensor criterion criterion

Two elements are coprime iff the class sequence of their ideal gcd is zero-sum-free, no non-empty sub-sequence of classes summing to the trivial class, and the sensor is exact, element-coprime matching ideal-coprime for all pairs, exactly when h = 1. Element-coprimality is class-blind, and what it misses is budgeted: a hidden common divisor carries at most D(Cl) − 1 primes, D the Davenport constant of the class group, one prime for C₂, the minimal specimens 2 and 1 ± √−5 silently sharing the prime above 2.

Scope. The zero-sum-free law is proved in any Dedekind domain and the h > 1 witness is constructive in any ring of integers; verified on all 3240 pairs of norm ≤ 120 in Z[√−5], with Z[i] the h = 1 control. The static combinatorics is the block-monoid theory of non-unique factorization; the growth reading is what is claimed.

The class gap rule

Statics. The ideal world's partition function is the Dedekind zeta ζK, the sum of N(I)−β over ideals, crystal probability 1/ζK(2) = 0.5389, so the family is three deep, Riemann, rational, Dedekind. The element world's is (ζK + L(χ−4)L(χ5))/2, matched coefficient by coefficient to n ≤ 104, so the gap between the two partition functions is a class-group L-function. Dynamics. The element crystal is dead rather than leaky: the pure-square move stays admissible until depth 2, so a run inside a finite universe of primes absorbs only with every nonprincipal prime at depth ≥ 2 and every principal prime entered, and the squarefree fraction is 0. Thermometer. ζprinc/ζK runs 0.6743 at β = 2 and 0.5063 at 1.05, converging to 1/h at the pole: heat both worlds toward it and their mass ratio reads the class number off. To be arithmetic at the ideal level needs only the mezzanine; at the element level, the crystal and independent windows, it needs class number 1, and the class group measures how far a world sits from its own arithmetic.

Scope. Z[√−5], h = 2. The partition split is genus theory and the pole ratio is residue equidistribution, both classical; the absorption profile law is proved in-universe and the crystal probabilities are exact.

Shallow and bottomless columns rule

In the infinite world the question is how fast each prime's exponent in the state, its column, grows, and the class group splits the columns in two. The SHALLOW half is a property: once a principal prime has entered, any later move divisible by it puts the trivial class into the gcd's class sequence, so a principal column is written by at most one move and frozen thereafter, over any class group; what is bounded is the writes and not the depth, a fresh prime's square writing depth 2 in one stroke. At h = 1 this covers every prime. The BOTTOMLESS half is a rate. Past depth 1 every move raising a nonprincipal P carries a fresh nonprincipal factor, the leading such move P·Q, so with a, b the prime-zeta tails, the sums of N(P)−β over the fresh principal and fresh nonprincipal primes, and K the nonprincipal mass already spent, the one-step raising probability is N(P)−β·b/(a + Kb), and each nonprincipal column grows linearly at N(P)−β/(ρβ + Cβ), Cβ the same sum over all nonprincipal primes and ρβ the trajectory's own tail ratio a/b: 0.2152 predicted against 0.2148 measured at the prime above 2, β = 2. The static tails are balanced, each class holding half the primes, but the trajectory's ratio runs 0.64 to 0.93, a nonprincipal prime being spent only with a partner, so the balanced denominator gives a lower bound on the rate rather than the rate. The split is about a class being trivial, not parity: intact at Cl = C₃.

Scope. The shallow half is proved; the rate identity is derived and measured within 4.3% at move-norm caps of 2000, 8000 and 32000, β = 1.5 and 2, five seeds each, the agreement improving with the cap, at Z[√−5]; the shallow half over 39 runs at h = 1, 2, 3, Z[i] the control. What is measured and not derived is that ρβ stays bounded, which the rate being positive rests on.

Read as a machine, the growth apparatus splits along a knife edge: its depth face, moduli and their monotone growth, sits one borrow short of Turing-complete, its element face with growth is universal bare, and every door has a named price (Computation).

Growth over other fields

Replace Z and the depth fate splits on one local invariant: at a place of ramification e and residue degree f the 1-unit exponent climbs one λ-tick per e of depth at the eventually flat price pef in mixed characteristic and at unbounded prices in equal characteristic, so the lock is the finite-rank case and the sprawl its rank-∞ limit, seating at most one irreducible per degree. Every ideal tie is a conjugate pair, ghosts are edited by class field theory, and above class number 1 the fate runs once per currency, ideals reproducing Z's geography while elements pay the class group in price. Heat the same laws over F2[x] and everything selection authored melts while the diverging cost does not: Growth over other fields. Whether a lock must EXIST at all reduces to an infinite carrier ladder, closed at every odd characteristic below 1000 and inhabited beyond any bound: The cascade boundary. Which fields close cheaply is arithmetic of its own, the class group and the unit rank deciding it at degree 2 and 3 (Places).

The inside view

An observer holding only the finished ring can compute its own genesis posterior exactly, and what that posterior retains splits by fate: a breadth world's evidence for a cold origin is capped at ζ(β) : 1 forever while a depth column's grows without bound, a world can be built unreadable at exactly the temperature it was grown, and a hand reaching in to re-lock or revive one has an exact price list: The inside view. A probe reading such a world only through whether a move leaves its period fixed sees one number, and every move keeps an exact ledger on it: The sighted probe. The price of telling two merged states apart has a floor the probe computes from its reading alone, and the largest blind class is proved infinite and a fixed share of the primes: The blind spot. How much of a genesis is beyond recovery is an exact quantity with a critical temperature, one clock at every finite place: Irreducibility.

Thermal growth

Soften every law's argmin into a Gibbs selection and the blueprint grades by how much of the selection each property needs: the wall needs none, the destination only full support, and what only the argmin enforced, the route, squarefree-ness and the lock, melts, hot dynamics reaching the full profinite completion from every seed while the partition function of the hot breadth limit is ζ(β) itself. The asymptotic underneath, the fraction of the first k primes whose arrival leaves λ unchanged approaching 1, is untouched by temperature: Thermal and density.
