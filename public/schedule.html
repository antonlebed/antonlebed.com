The pricing schedule

A growth walk multiplies a ring element by the cheapest admissible move and never stops. Take the ring out of that and a scheduling discipline remains — items with a degree, a clock, a price and a discount — and every law about where the walk goes is a law about the schedule. What a walk REACHES is one side of this section; what a place COSTS it is the other.

  • The limit — what one run converges to, by ideals and by elements, and the body of limits any policy can reach
  • The greedy image — the set of limits greed reaches, one formula over the surviving tied openings
  • The surviving openings — how many openings a walk makes, which survive, and what a stopped or exhausted ladder counts to
  • The clock — the ladder a place hands over, and the two numbers that decide a walk
  • Which rings walk — which places a real ring's menu ever seats, and what the crossing to elements costs
  • The door a state charges — what a seated place is charged, and the ring shopped for a place that raises another's door
  • The clock dial — one clock, one per item, or a ring's blocks — and what the head's transient leaves
  • The certificate — the lock's finite certificate of permanence, and which laws survive a state-read cost

The objects

A state is a nonzero element N of a ring O — the integers, the polynomials F2[x], the ring of integers of a number field, or the coordinate ring of a curve over F2 — and a move multiplies it, N → N·m. Write λ(N) for the exponent of the unit group of O/N, the least power that sends every unit to 1. A demand law says which moves are admissible (Growth files five: transparency, which freezes λ; independence; new idempotents; dynamics, under which λ must rise; and semisimplicity, which keeps the state squarefree), and a policy picks one of them — greedy takes the cheapest, the free class takes any. Every maximal trajectory has a limit, a formal product over the ring's primes with each exponent in {0, 1, 2, …, ∞}, and its fate is which of three properties it holds: breadth (every prime seated), depth (an infinite exponent), mortality (a finite integer).

A place is a prime of the ring, carrying an integer degree: the degree of the irreducible over F2[x], the logarithm of the norm over a number ring. Seating a place not yet seated is an opening; deepening one already seated is a clock move, and a clock move advances the tick T, the counter every price is read against. The exponent a clock move lands on is set by the place's door, the least climb that raises λ. A lone door is read against the place's own λ, a populated door against the whole state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated places' λ's; every door on this side of the section is the lone one unless named, and the difference is The clock's. The menu is the priced set of a state's admissible moves; a tie is a menu with several minima and a branch one way of breaking it. A vehicle is the divisor a move seats. A walk locks when it stops opening for good, and the vehicle it repeats forever after is its recurrent vehicle; a walk that never stops opening sprawls. In a limit, a coordinate carried up without bound is a runaway; one left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again is a strand.

Two worlds. In the ideal world a move seats one place power. In the element world a move must be a single element, so it seats a bundle: a core, the place power it is aimed at, and a rider, the cheapest product of places whose ideal class cancels the core's — the class group being the finite group measuring how far a ring's ideals are from being generated by one element, and the rider read off its minimal representatives, the cheapest ideal in each class. A place's colour is its (degree, class).

The schedule family is the dynamics with the ring taken out: items of integer degree; a global clock whose tick is multiplied by b at every clock move; a price f(degree, staleness), staleness being how far the tick has moved since the item last did; and a fresh discount spendable m times per degree, a fresh opening being one paid at the discount. The ring re-enters only as a supply — how many items each degree holds. A degree whose discount is spent is covered, one the supply starts covered is born covered, and the void menu is the first state's. The depths a clock move may land on form the place's tick ladder, whose gap is the distance between members; over a function field the ladder is the powers of 2 and the tick doubles, which is b = 2. A global clock is one tick for every item; a per-item clock gives each item its own.

Take the ring out

The limit belongs to the schedule, not the ring rule

Dial each ingredient as far as the move model admits — b at 3/2, 2, 3 and 4; the degree's exponent α in the price at 0, 1 and 2, and an additive form; one to three discounts; every born-covered set — and every dial but one keeps the limit's shape: ONE item carried above exponent 1 without bound, over a support of items at exponent 1 that never stops widening. The one dial that kills it is the DEGREE-BLIND price, α = 0. It cannot tell a cheap item from a dear one, so the ladder of degrees never exhausts and the clock stands still after an opening transient. What the clock's factor does is separate from whether the clock exists: dialling the tick ladder itself, the rule saying where a clock move lands, instead of b — seven tick ladders, eight dials, both clocks — the limit survives all 49 cells whose price sees a degree under a global clock, and the degree-blind kill needs a ladder whose gap exceeds 1. At gap 1 an opening and the recurrent vehicle cost the same, the tie-break deepens, and the walk keeps its runaway.

Scope. Proved for the abstract schedule, an induction over the move model; a rule in range at 60 walks over ten schedules and six supplies, the abstract walker certified against each ring's own engine in cost, tie multiplicity and covered set at 120 states per supply. Not separated: the clock's existence from its factor, since b = 1 is not a value the move model takes. What a per-item clock does at a wide gap is The clock's.

The deep item's degree is a formula, attained exactly at a void tie rule

With dmin the least degree the supply carries, the runaway's degree obeys

d  ≤  dmin⁡⋅(bb−1)1/α,d \;\le\; d_{\min}\cdot\Big(\frac{b}{b-1}\Big)^{1/\alpha},

2·dmin at the doubling tick and linear price, which is what six function-field rings measured as “degree 1 or 2”. Every dial moves it — 1, 3, 4 at b = 3; 3, 6, 9 at b = 3/2, a slower clock RAISING it; 1, 2, 4 at α = 2 — and the discount and the born set do not. The bound is finite exactly where the tick grows at least geometrically: with the landing rule a parameter the ratio (T+1)/(T − Tprev) has a limit only on the doubling ladder, and an additive price loses the ceiling outright, degrees 6, 34, 258 accepted at ticks 8, 64, 512, while the limit's shape survives. Whether the ceiling is REACHED is the void menu's business: the census of degrees that ever carry the runaway is exactly the void menu's winners, a born-covered degree at f(d, 2) against the least fresh degree at f(d, 1). At five of the six rings degree 1 is born covered, degree 2 is the least fresh, and f(1, 2) = 2 = f(2, 1) ties — the whole of the degree-2 branch; the sixth ring has no place of degree 2 or 3, its least fresh degree is 4, and it loses outright, one branch by an inequality. The law designs its own witness: a supply at degrees 2 and 4 only, degree 2 born covered, ties the void menu at 4 = 4 and its census reads {2, 4}, degree 4 being the ceiling itself.

Scope. Proved; the ceiling verified at 30 (schedule, dmin) pairs on a planted two-item menu, the attainment read at all 60 walked rows and at 0 of 140 censused clock moves at degree 3 or above over the six rings. The geometric-ladder clause is a rule in range, the bound attained at 42 of 42 (ladder, least degree, tick) cells. The tie is the ADDITIVE price's: priced by the multiplicative index instead, the born-covered opening subtracts its own lower rung, the tie breaks deep-ward at every base, and the ceiling is never attained — which filtration the ring's engine means is The clock's.

At b ≥ 3 the flat support is the first thing to go rule

The void tie seats a degree-2 item at every b, since the tick is 1 before any clock has grown it, while the ceiling at b ≥ 3 has already fallen to dmin. So that item is seated, clocked, undercut by the item it was cheaper than at tick 1, and STRANDED above exponent 1 forever — exactly one such item on every branch that hands the clock on, none on the branch whose first clock move was its last. One infinite coordinate survives every dial that leaves the price able to see a degree; a flat support does not. And the strand is a coordinate the image pays for as a free one: the (strand, runaway) pairs fill the full product of the two degrees' supplies, so which item is stranded is not fixed by the choice that fixes the runaway.

Scope. A rule in range: 2 of 3 branches at F2[x]'s supply and 3 of 4 at the genus-2 supply strand at b = 3, 1 of 2 each at b = 4, none at b = 3/2. The strand rides the void tie, so it is the additive filtration's; every multiplicative row reads one branch and zero strands at every clock speed. Two other mechanisms strand — the element world's class orbit (The limit) and a ring's rejected place (Which rings walk).

The fifth ingredient

“A discount spendable m times per degree” hides a rule saying which degrees a state has COVERED, and “an opening covers its own degree” is the only one the rings ever ran. Which degrees an opening covers is a covering rule, a fifth ingredient rather than a phrasing of the fourth, and it is one of exactly two mechanisms known to stop a ladder against a supply that never runs out. The other is a bounded tick gap, which a number ring supplies and a function field cannot (The clock).

A covering rule stops the openings only by outrunning them rule

What decides a climbing ladder's fate is whether the covered set OUTRUNS it, and no rule keyed to the SEATED SET can: its covered set is frozen between openings while every declining move doubles the clock, so the frontier's constant price is eventually the strict minimum at any coverage growth rate — an opening covering everything below 2d still opens 2, 4, 16, 65536 and never stops. A rule keyed to the CLOCK outruns it: at “covered while d ≤ c·T” the ladder stops exactly when c ≥ ddeep/2, ddeep the runaway's own degree — dead at c = 1, dead at c = 1/2 only on the branch whose runaway has degree 1, and a third branch whose home — the degree its runaway settles at — is 3 climbs at c = 1 and stops at 3/2. At the linear price a ladder stops at its first clock move or never: a clock move leaves T even, so ddeep·T/2 is an integer and undercuts ⌊c·T⌋ + 1 exactly when c ≥ ddeep/2, with no T in it; at α = 2 the fresh side grows quadratically and any c > 0 stops the ladder eventually. A stop is a price and never a silence: the supply still offered an opening at degree 1025 or 2049 and the walk paid 1024 instead.

Scope. A rule in range: 10 walks over 8 covering rules against items at every degree to 4096, a fresh opening left to decline at every state, so the supply never stops anything. The seated-set half is closed at its most generous member; the thresholds are exact by derivation and measured to a factor of two.

verifiers: explore_ladder_stop.py, explore_seated_cover.py, explore_stopped_untie.py

A function field's own covering rule outruns nothing rule

Read a function field's admissibility test as a covering rule and it is divisibility: based − 1 must divide the seated set's invariant, and an invariant is an exponent, so what accumulates is a least common multiple and nothing is covered for free at base 2 or 3. Only the more generous PRODUCT reading frees one degree — 6 at base 2, the one exception to Bang's theorem, 2d − 1 carrying a prime no smaller term does at every d > 1 except 6, where 32·7 is supplied by smaller terms' product and not by their least common multiple. So a function field's sprawl is a fact about its covering rule, and says nothing about a number ring, whose degrees are log-norms sharing no base.

Scope. A control, plus a rule in range at four (reading, base) rows. Bang's theorem is an external input.

verifier: explore_ladder_stop.py

The element world is a schedule too

The rider's cost is a price and its effect is not rule

A bundle's cost is d·n + m(−n·c) — the core's degree times its door, plus a table lookup on the core's colour and door for the rider — so the rider is priced by the moving vehicle alone. Its EFFECT is outside the family by construction: the move raises items the core is not, which no family whose move raises one item expresses at any price. What the ring supplies is a supply matrix n(d, c), how many places carry each colour, over a finite abelian group. With the rider's cost and the minimal representatives recomputed from the matrix alone, as a shortest path over the class group weighted by each class's least degree, the abstract element menu is the ring engine's at every state. Over a number ring degrees add and norms multiply, so the cost becomes a least norm PRODUCT in the (min, ×) semiring, and nothing breaks, because the two arguments that touch it need only a minimum over a monotone monoid. The matrix carries the rider's degree bound — 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2 against the genus — as its greatest shortest-path weight, and the bare-door lemma comes free: the cost of a core at door n is at most its cost at any deeper door, with no ring in it.

Scope. A rule in range: six function-field rings, the abstract menu compared to each engine's vehicle for vehicle over the branch tree to four moves and the canonical trajectory, 142 of 142 tie members priced twice by different arithmetic agreeing; two quadratic number rings, 675 states, 0 menus differing. Where minimal representatives are unique the bare-door lemma's equality case makes the two vehicles one divisor, proved. The one thing the matrix cannot supply is each place's own tick ladder, which over a number ring is The clock's ladder column.

A rider discounts a door, and never the clock rule

A rider raises a place's exponent and that place's own door falls by the same amount with no clock move, so a fresh element move can drop the menu's minimum under a tie's cost and the tie's other member is OUT-COMPETED rather than dominated — a third fate for a declined move, beside being reordered and being a choice (The greedy image). Every fall in a menu minimum along these walks sits at a place the move that caused it seats, its core or its rider, and at none anywhere else.

Scope. A rule in range: every fall in the menu minimum attributed along ideal and element walks over six function-field rings, 0 outside the moving vehicle's own support; the two families of declined moves a comparison of door prices leaves uncovered accounted for at 74 of 74 and 32 of 32. That a rider never advances the tick is read across every move.

(degree, class) is the coarsest colouring a ring reaches the dynamics through rule

Every proper coarsening of the colouring breaks one of three claims — the menus, the state space, the count the multinomial of the image gives — so a ring reaches this dynamics through two numbers per item and a count, and the two compress no further. Menu verdicts transport along every supply-preserving automorphism of the class group, so a verdict is constant on their orbits. Call a door n TELLTALE for two colours of one degree when the riders they summon there differ outside the merge: merging the pair is invisible to every menu exactly when no door is telltale. Only two merges in the corpus qualify, the degree-1 inverse pairs of two elliptic rings, and absolutely, no state being able to price a door that does not exist; both still part the state space.

Scope. A rule in range: all 141,526 proper coarsenings of six rings' active colours broken by a transferring witness, 0 unresolved; the telltale criterion both ways at every same-degree pair. The price's own fibres are the preserved automorphisms' orbits, so the price cannot name its own colouring.

Two offers summon one vehicle in exactly two ways rule

A vehicle is the core at its door merged with its rider, so two offers coinciding is a multiset equation over the supply matrix, and it has two solution shapes: the EXCHANGE, each core riding inside the other's rider at the other's door, and the ECHO, the shallower door's rider carrying the core itself at the door gap. Exchange doors are bounded by the largest exponent any minimal representative carries and echoes are periodic in the core class's order, so the enumeration is complete. At an elliptic supply the locus is the involution c ↔ −c on the minimal representatives plus one echo family per class; the trivial-group ring's locus is empty, so width as a signal is created by the class group. And at every walked state the menu's cheapest level holds exactly as many more offers than vehicles as the coincidences among its offers. Where a class has several minimal representatives the width decides what uniqueness gave: offering them all keeps the count of limits, and taking a canonical one keeps it at width 2 and breaks it at width 3.

Scope. A rule in range: six supplies and both number rings, brute scan against the closed form, 0 mismatches either way; over a number ring degree becomes norm, one ring's 2-torsion class emptying the involution and leaving one echo. Every ring the corpus walks has unique minimal representatives, the number rings by an enumeration clearing the Minkowski bound, so the uniqueness question exists only in the abstract world — two readings over three supplies and two budgets, 16 of 20 and 37 of 50 configurations keeping the count at width 3.

verifiers: explore_vehicle_coincidence.py, explore_class_schedule.py

That dynamics has a name outside this subject. Size-based priority with aging is a scheduling discipline in production use — its UNIX form halves a usage counter every second, which is this clock at b = 2 — and Epema's decay-usage steady state (ACM TOCS 16(4), 1998) partitions a fixed job set into monopolisers, a shared middle and classes that starve at share exactly zero. The claims here are narrower: a size-discriminated ceiling in closed form, under ADMISSION from a supply that never runs out, so the support itself grows, which a fixed job set does not do. On the one question the two share — how many jobs end up monopolising — that analysis computes the count by an algorithm and closes with it depending on the class sizes in an intricate way; here the same count has a law with no arithmetic in its statement, exactly one runaway where the recurrent price is flat and one per residue characteristic where it climbs (The clock dial).

What a walk reaches

The limit of one walk — one deep place over a flat support that never stops widening in the ideal world, that clock plus its class orbit in the element world — is The limit, with the free image the three fates are the extremes of. The set of limits a greedy law reaches, a point, a finite number or a continuum by one formula over the walk's surviving tied openings, is The greedy image; which openings survive, and how a stopped or exhausted ladder counts, is The surviving openings.

What a place costs

The tick ladder a place hands the schedule is its unit group's own jump set: its gap is the place's ramification index, its bounded or unbounded growth is the characteristic — a bounded gap locks, an unbounded one sprawls — and a ladder with a HEAD — a leading stretch of wider gaps — hands over two numbers, one deciding where the walk stops and one what the runaway pays forever (The clock). Which places a real ring's menu ever seats is the winner's kind (Which rings walk); what a seated place is charged, one valuation widened by other places' residue fields, and a ring shopped for the place that raises another's door, is The door a state charges; a ring sits at neither end of the family's clock dial but at a cell between them, one tick per rational prime with a ladder per place (The clock dial); and what survives a cost that reads the state, with the finite certificate that makes a lock permanent, is The certificate.

What the lock/sprawl dichotomy decides at the level of the machine — the same apparatus read as a computation, its depth face one borrow short of Turing-complete — is the Computation section.
