The limit

What one greedy walk converges to: one place carried to infinite depth over a support that stays flat and never stops widening, proved with no ring in the argument; that clock plus its class orbit when the walk grows by elements; and the body of limits any policy at all can reach, whose extremes are the three fates.

A growth walk multiplies a ring element by admissible moves and never stops. A limit is the formal product a maximal trajectory converges to, one exponent in {0, 1, 2, …, ∞} per place; its reading is the limit with the runaway's exponent forgotten; an era is the stretch between two doublings of the tick.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient. A place is a prime of the ring with an integer degree (the degree of the irreducible over F2[x], the log-norm over a number ring); seating one is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move, which advances the tick T every price is read against, and the exponent it lands on is set by the place's door, the least climb that raises λ against the state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated places' λ's. A fresh move is an opening, paid at its degree's discount, and a vehicle is the divisor a move seats. The menu is the priced set of admissible moves, a tie a menu with several minima, a branch one way of breaking it; a walk locks when it stops opening for good and the move it then repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle. A coordinate carried up without bound is a runaway, one left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again a strand. In the ideal world a move seats one place power; in the element world it must be a single element and seats a core, the place power aimed at, plus a rider, the cheapest product of places whose ideal class cancels the core's, read off the class group's minimal representatives. The whole vocabulary, and the schedule family the laws are stated in, is The pricing schedule.

The limit of one walk

The ideal limit is one deep place over a flat support that never stops growing theorem

The limit of a greedy dynamics trajectory in the ideal world over F2[x] or a curve is

∞⋅C  +  one place at exponent 1 at each degree the walk opens,\infty\cdot C \;+\; \text{one place at exponent 1 at each degree the walk opens,}

C the one runaway, every other seated place at exponent 1 forever, the support unbounded. Four facts make it. The tick doubles exactly at a clock move and an opening leaves it alone, so every exponent written is 0, 1, or a tick plus 1, and no price falls under d·T/2. A clocked place of degree 1 is therefore permanent, every rival's price rising at least as fast at every later clock move, and it settles at its FIRST clock move with no transient. Degree 1 is never admissible as an opening — 21 − 1 divides everything — so a rational place is seated only by a clock move, while the fresh ladder opens 2, 3, 4, … one place per degree forever, 2d − 1 carrying a prime no smaller one does (Bang's theorem, exceptions d = 1 and d = 6, where 32 does the work). C is itself one of the opened degrees' places exactly when its degree is 2. The one deep place is the flat-cost CORNER of a wider law: at the clock a ring actually carries, one tick per residue characteristic with a ladder per place, the runaway count is the number of characteristics whose recurrent cost climbs, and no realizable ring supplies a climbing cost and a second characteristic together (The clock dial).

Scope. Proved for the ideal world, an induction over the move model with no computation in it; a rule in range at six rings, 11 branches walked 300 moves each with every move's tick and exponent read. The degree the runaway can hold, and why it is 1 or 2 at these rings, is the degree ceiling.

The ideal continuum's points are choice functions on degrees rule

Which place of a degree is seated at an opening is free and permanent: nothing later in the ideal dynamics reads a place's identity, which is why a walker carrying no places is the engine exactly. So the limit set is the choice of the runaway times a product over degrees of place counts, the first ten openings alone offering of order 1011 ways at F2[x] and 1016 at the largest elliptic supply. That turns the cardinality of the greedy image (The greedy image) from a count into a description of the object counted.

Scope. A derivation on the ideal limit, inheriting its scope, with the factors computed and every opening's width asserted to be the degree's whole unseated population. The element world reads a class at every opening as well, so this is not its description.

The element limit is the clock plus its class orbit rule

A rider raises an exponent with no clock move and so voids the ideal limit's exponent ceiling — but greedy will not pay a rider it can avoid. A fresh opening costs the degree for a principal core, one whose place is itself a single element, and strictly more otherwise, and every degree past a handful has principal places to spare, so the fresh rider flow dries up by step 9 at every ring, and the only source left is the clock's own move, one per era against an era that doubles. After the last non-principal opening the only places a rider reaches are the minimal representatives the clock's class γ summons. The limit is ∞·C, plus at most the places of γ's orbit gaining about one unit an era, plus one place at exponent 1 at each opened degree; it collapses to the ideal shape when the orbit is empty (γ = 0) or is C itself (γ of order 2), and gains coordinates otherwise — three at the class-5 elliptic ring, three to six at genus 2. The ideal world's degree-2 clock survives where the clock is principal, since reaching exponent 2 at a rational place takes a rider that never arrives. Permanence does not survive: at genus 2 a rational place carries the first three doublings as a rider and then loses the clock, so the clock changes hands inside the opening stretch or not at all.

Scope. Proved for the steady state; a rule in range over six rings walked 300 moves on every branch of a complete tie sweep, 20 branches at the class-5 ring and 120 at genus 2. The element world has no reordering lemma — a declined minimal type is still minimal at the successor at only 466 of 719 types at genus 2 — so its shapes are read over every branch.

Those coordinates are unbounded, and how many is arithmetic on the clock's increments rule

No walk settles it, five more eras costing 32× the moves. The recursion does: a clock move takes r = T + 1 − e, lands at T + 1 where the tick turns over, and summons the minimal representative of −r·γ, w of whose units land back on the clock, so

T′=2T,r′=T−w,w=rep⁡[−rγ][C],T' = 2T,\qquad r' = T - w,\qquad w = \operatorname{rep}[-r\gamma][C],

w reading r only through r mod h. The state (T mod h, r mod h) is finite, so the summoned classes are eventually periodic: a cell — a (degree, class) pair of places — in the CYCLE is fed forever and is unbounded, a cell only in the PRE-PERIOD stops at a computable exponent. The verdicts: the clock alone at four rings, and three unbounded coordinates at the class-5 ring and at genus 2 on every branch; one ring's w = 0 is absorbing, so its rider income cannot restart. Lifted off the ring the recursion is a map on (Z/d)2 with d the order of γ, and ESCAPE — a pre-period residue the cycle never repeats — is decided by (d, the w-pattern, the seed). But the seed is the state at the first clock move past the opening stretch, not algebra, so the species cannot be read off a ring before walking one: 77 of genus 2's 120 branches carry a bounded coordinate and the rest do not, over one ring and one class group. The tick's period dividing the cycle is the one law; the escaping classes' cells, less the cycle's and the clock's, are the bounded coordinates at all 150 branches. So the two worlds differ by a logarithmically deep coordinate and never by the headline.

Scope. Proved for the steady state; a rule in range at every branch of six rings, 964 clock moves reproduced era for era, every branch closing its cycle at least once. Every pre-period read is 0 or 1 and every cycle 1, 2 or 4, range facts and not laws: the same recursion over arbitrary groups reaches cycles at 9× the tick's period and pre-periods of 83, over 4.9 million swept orbits.

The free image

A law and a seed s, the state a walk starts from, have an image Im(L, s), the limits reachable from s under the whole free policy class — every admissible move allowed, not only the cheapest.

The fate image is the body the three fates bound rule

The five filed demands have five images, and four form a strict chain under inclusion at a squarefree seed: semisimplicity reaches s·∏p∈S p over the infinite prime sets disjoint from the seed's support, every exponent 1; independence the same shape from any seed with exponents free but finite and the seed's frozen; new idempotents every multiple of s of infinite support with ∞ allowed; dynamics every infinite multiple of s. Transparency sits outside, reaching a single finite point. Every image parameterizes as a SUPPORT choice then an EXPONENT choice on it, and the fates are its extremes: breadth pins the support maximal, depth pins an exponent maximal, each a face extremal in one coordinate, while mortality pins both minimal and is the only corner. That asymmetry is why a trajectory can sit at the depth face and still differ from another that does, by where it lies in the free coordinate; why only mortality excludes the others; and why fate purity fails over Z — 2∞·∏(odd primes) carries breadth and depth at once. A generic member is interior and holds no fate. Two things greed could not see: a free dynamics policy reaches 2∞ from the void (push 4, not 2), so 2's exponent being invisible to greed at birth is greed's and not the law's; and an image is not determined by its fates, new idempotents and dynamics agreeing on all three and differing on everything else. Redrawn over F2[x] the five laws give the same five shapes, the same chain and the same extremes: no mechanism was ever about the integers. What the ring moves is the mortality corner's value, the transparency wall, the one finite point transparency reaches (Growth).

Scope. A rule: the closure half exhaustive over a state × move battery, the reach half by construction plus a stated continuation argument, 0 mismatches over five laws over Z and again over F2[x].

verifiers: explore_fate_image.py, explore_fate_image_ff.py

The image reads the demand square coordinate by coordinate observation

The two coordinates are the two axes of the reading/hosting square that decides universality (Computation). HOSTING decides whether an infinite exponent is reachable, by two mechanisms: a law hosting no decrement never revisits a prime (independence, semisimplicity), while the law hosting no increment revisits constantly and is stopped by a finite reachable set (transparency). READING decides whether a run may stop widening: a demand blind to λ can only be met by new support, one that reads λ by re-pushing a seated prime. The reading axis is not a theorem about blindness — non-semisimplicity is blind by the same test and admits moves that seat nothing — and the square does not decide the image, independence and semisimplicity sharing a cell.

Scope. An observation over the five laws, both coordinates read off witnesses.

Mortality is the corner, and the square does not reach it rule

A limit of finite support and finite exponents is a finite integer, and every move multiplies by at least 2, so a (law, seed) pair is mortal exactly when its image holds that corner. The cell of the square that looks nearest — a λ-reading law hosting increments alone — does not force it: FRESH-DYNAMICS, which admits m iff m is a prime not dividing N and λ rises, is a live law in that cell, seating primes at exponent 1 forever and never every prime. Liveness belongs to the (law, seed) pair: semisimplicity dies from seed 12 and lives from seed 1.

Scope. A rule; the witnesses are explicit laws and seeds.

Where greed names a single point, that point goes interior rule

Over Z greedy dynamics is extremal in both coordinates, its support minimal at one prime's column and its exponent maximal; over F2[x] it stays exponent-maximal with its support interior, infinite and of density zero. Both hold the lone depth fate, and only the other coordinate separates them — which the fate names cannot carry and the coordinates can. Determinism was a rational accident for the greedy RULE: a tie-break is needed wherever distinct moves share a cost, which is off Q always. What that costs is the greedy image's question — nothing over Z, a bounded factor of the width per surviving tie over a number ring, everything over F2[x] (The greedy image).

Scope. A rule; the sprawl's density is Growth's.

The set of limits

Off Z the greedy policy is a class, one member per way of breaking each tie, and the set of limits it reaches is the product, over the walk's surviving tied openings, of the tie multiplicity at each — a point, a finite number or a continuum, with nothing countable between. The multiplicity at an opening, the species a tie can be and the certificate that separates two branches' limits are The greedy image; how many openings there are, which survive the recurrent vehicle, and what an exhausted or stopped ladder counts are The surviving openings; how wide a menu can be is the coincidence locus.

What prices all of it

Take the curve and the ring out and four ingredients remain — items carrying a degree, one global clock, a price read against it, and a discount on each degree's first openings — with the ring re-entering only as a supply. One infinite coordinate survives every dial of that family but one, the flat support does not survive as far, and a fifth ingredient the four hide is the second mechanism that stops the openings: The pricing schedule. What the clock itself hands over — the ladder, the head, the door a state charges — is The clock.
