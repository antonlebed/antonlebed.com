The surviving openings

The second and third factors of the greedy image: how many openings a walk makes — endless where the ladder climbs, finite where it locks, and what an exhausted or stopped ladder counts to — and which of the choices made at them the recurrent vehicle later swallows. The count is a point, a finite number or a continuum, and nothing in between.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient. A place is a prime of the ring with an integer degree (the degree of the irreducible over F2[x], the log-norm over a number ring); seating one is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move, which advances the tick T every price is read against, and the exponent it lands on is set by the place's door, the least climb that raises λ against the state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated places' λ's. A fresh move is an opening, paid at its degree's discount, and a vehicle is the divisor a move seats. The menu is the priced set of admissible moves, a tie a menu with several minima, a branch one way of breaking it; a walk locks when it stops opening for good and the move it then repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle. A coordinate carried up without bound is a runaway, one left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again a strand. In the ideal world a move seats one place power; in the element world it must be a single element and seats a core, the place power aimed at, plus a rider, the cheapest product of places whose ideal class cancels the core's, read off the class group's minimal representatives. The whole vocabulary, and the schedule family the laws are stated in, is The pricing schedule.

The greedy image is the set of limits the minimal-move policy class reaches, a product over the walk's surviving tied openings of the tie multiplicity at each (The greedy image). With the ring taken out, the schedule family has items of integer degree, a price read against the tick, a fresh discount per degree — a degree whose discount is spent is covered, one that starts so is born covered — and the ring as a supply of items per degree; the void menu is the first state's, a home is a degree that wins it, so the runaway can settle there, and a degree's width is how many items the supply holds at it. The depths a clock move may land on are the place's tick ladder, its gap the distance between members; a global clock is one tick for every item, a per-item clock one each. A reading of a state is the state with the runaway's exponent forgotten. Sibling starvation is a declined place priced out forever by the seated place of its own degree.

How many openings there are

A number ring's ideal image is exactly 2t; a function field's is the continuum rule

F2[x] never locks, so openings recur forever and each choice is made permanent by sibling starvation: one limit per choice function, the continuum. The number rings lock, by price and not by coverage: one world's door returns to 1 forever while the other must climb to the next power of two — the lock/sprawl dichotomy is the shape of λ's p-part, linear against logarithmic in the depth (The clock) — and at the deepest locks 6% and 12% of the two universes are covered, hundreds of door-1 escapes sit unspent, and the walk declines every one because an escape costs its norm and the walk never gets richer. So the openings stop and the ideal image is FINITE, of size 2t for t the tie count — 2 the quadratic width, the general base the width per tie — branch-independent by lockstep — policies differing only in which tie member they take run identically (The greedy image) — and unmerged because a seated place's own deepening door strictly undercuts its unseated conjugate's at every state: conjugate places share one ladder, a seated place's first rung already divides the invariant, so the level the invariant next needs sits two rungs up and the seated place has climbed one. The greedy image is a Galois orbit only where t ≤ 1: two independent conjugate ties compose into four limits, more than the Galois group's 2, while other seeds reach a single Z-like point. What decides which is geography and not the class group — t ≥ 2 needs the pre-lock wander to open a split pair, and the class-3 ring wanders only through ramified places, which are Galois-fixed and cannot tie.

Scope. A rule in range: two quadratic rings × two seed belts to norm 250, 0 branch merges at every seed, the sibling starvation proved and instanced 2308 times; the flat recurrent price at 131 locking seeds.

The exhausted image is a sum over the runaway's homes of a product over the other openings rule

Give the schedule a supply with a top degree and past it the walk is deterministic, the runaway re-clocking forever, so the set of LIMITS is constant while the set of STATES keeps changing. Only a tied opening multiplies, so a supply of one item per degree has an image of 2 however long its ladder runs — the tie left is between DEGREES, which a within-degree count cannot see — which is also why stopping is not needed to make a count finite. The exhausted image is nh·∏nd, derived from the void-menu law (The pricing schedule) with only reachability measured: for n items at each of degrees 1 to D it reads nD−1·(n + 1), the two terms the two HOMES the void menu ties between, degree 1 beside one flat item at each degree above, or degree 2 with degree 1 empty. Break the tie — gap the supply, steepen the price to α = 2, or raise the floor so the fresh side wins — and the sum has one term: 128, 64, 64 at width 2, 243 and 81 at width 3, pure powers of the width, and 48 = 2·3·2·4 where the widths differ per degree, a product and not a power. The product runs over the OPENABLE degrees: a born-covered degree above the home is empty at every reachable state, the image reading 24 against the 25 a count of present degrees would give.

Scope. Proved for the corner schedule; a rule in range at two supplies at four and three budgets past exhaustion, seven (width, top degree) rows, and nine families across three dials, 0 off the closed form. The multiplicative filtration breaks the same tie deep-ward at every base, confirming the home half from a lever the rig never touches. Exhaustion is unavailable to a number ring: a degree there is a log-norm, the degree set is unbounded, and the ladder stops on a flat recurrent price instead.

Under a stopped ladder the product runs over the homes and over nothing else rule

Run the three tie-breaks above — gap the supply, steepen the price, raise the floor — against a supply that never runs out under a covering rule that stops the ladder (The pricing schedule), and the image is

∏homes(nh+1)  −  1,\prod_{\text{homes}} (n_h + 1) \;-\; 1,

the minus one the state with no runaway. It is a closed form only at a rule that stops EVERY branch: at a tie the two homes are b born covered and 2b fresh, with thresholds c ≥ b/2 and c ≥ b, and a c between them leaves the image infinite on the second branch. At most two homes ever, one born covered and one fresh, since the price rises with the degree at either door. At the linear price a ladder stops at the first clock move or never, so every opening a stopped walk makes happens at tick 1, and a degree opened there attained the void menu's minimum, which is what a home is: breaking the tie removes the second home and its optional opening in one move, because they are the same degree. The widths above the homes are irrelevant — tail widths 1 and 5 both read 11 = (2+1)(3+1) − 1 — so a stop makes an image SMALL, bounded by (n + 1)2 − 1 whatever the supply, set from the void menu before the walk begins. The power-of-2 reading does not travel: the tied control reads 8 at width 2, and what separates the regimes is width 3, 15 against 3.

Scope. Proved at the linear price; a rule in range over eleven families, six untied across three dials at two widths and five tied controls, matched shape by shape, at one discount per degree and a doubling clock.

At a bounded gap the image settles, and two counts disagree about whether a wider gap buys a bigger one rule

Read at the move budget rather than cumulatively, the reading set of every constant-gap ladder stops moving by budget 8 while every growing ladder's is still moving at 12. Over F2[x]'s supply the settled sets have 3, 6, 4 and 4 members at gaps 1, 2, 3 and 5 — 5, 28, 90 and 12,396 with the item identities put back — and over the class-5 elliptic supply 1, 1, 1, 2. The shape count is not monotone in the gap; the identity count, the one that holds the ring's places, rises at every gap. Two of the four shape counts are not powers of 2, evidence about the ring's own count. A settled set is a limit set; a moving one is reported only as evidence that nothing settles.

Scope. A rule in range: the readings of the states at move budgets to 12, branching on every tie.

The reading that builds that set is a global-clock law rule

A reading forgets the runaway's exponent and identifies the runaway as the LAST CLOCK MOVER, which is sound at a global clock for a reason the identification does not carry: a clock rises only where the landing exceeds the tick, so a clock mover sits above exponent 1, and under a global clock exactly one coordinate is recurrent — a corollary of the ideal limit theorem (The limit). Under a per-item clock the last clock mover is the recurrent item at 4.6% of states, and rebuilt to follow what the walk actually carries to infinity, a state's reading still moves with a tie yet to be broken at most of them: what fails is the object, not the instrument.

Scope. A rule in range: 46,571 live states over 11 ladders at two supplies and both clocks, 557 of 557 global-clock states with one recurrent coordinate.

A head is what gives the per-item image a domain at all rule

A head is a leading stretch of a place's tick ladder whose gaps exceed the eventually constant tail gap; its sup is the largest gap an item lands on (The clock). At twelve of fourteen headed cells every final state carries a determined limit — every least-cost continuation locks on ONE reading, exhaustively — where the constant ladder of the same sup gap determines NOTHING at gap 2 or wider, its every candidate parting under full branching; the exact ladder, gap 1, keeps its 25; and the two cells outside the twelve are the widest head, sup 12, whose stop sits past the old budget and which, walked to 84 moves, reads 4432 and 9202 determined against zero undetermined. WHY is a derivation: door = tick + 1 − e, so on a constant-gap ladder every landed item's door is the gap forever, two landed siblings of one degree are priced identically at every step, and a continuation running one away has an equal-cost mirror running the other, the readings parting exactly where the siblings' kept exponents do; a ramp, the head's climbing stretch, prices position and closes the fork — no determined state carries such a swappable pair, and at the elliptic supply's cells of gap 2 through 8 the fallen states carry it almost to the last, while every polynomial-ring fall carries none and parts by a fork not named here. So a head does not give the image a different size, it gives it a domain. At a global clock no tie appears under branching, and the head parts from its constant-ladder control on size and members instead — the settled size agreeing at 10 of 14 cells and the set at 8 — the ramp's cheap door spent on depth and on openings at once.

Scope. A rule in range: every candidate state's continuation branched over every minimal-cost tie, 0 of 747 global-clock states undetermined; the mirror argument a derivation.

What the recurrent vehicle carries

A choice's permanence in the state is not its visibility at the limit, and the honest count is 2t − e rule

Branches never rejoin in any measured run; what happens instead is that two permanently distinct states reach one limit when the lock's recurrent vehicle swallows both members of a tied pair, sending them to infinity together. An ideal move seats one place and can never do it; an element move seats a bundle and does — at the class-3 ring the basin — the seeds whose walks lock on one place — of the split prime over 2 erases, the basin of an inert place swallows nothing. And e counts the ties whose core the RIDER can carry, at every seed, because that vehicle is the product of the two minimal representatives and so swallows exactly the rider-eligible places. Erasure and readmission are one route — a declined core returning as a passenger — differing only in whether the vehicle carrying it recurs. So the cardinality is a point, a finite number and a continuum because the surviving openings are none, finitely many and endless: Z's walk opens constantly and not one of its openings is a tie. The formula covers the (law, ring) PAIR: greedy independence over F2[x] ties at its openings as dynamics does and still reaches the polynomial primorial, every irreducible seated once in turn, under every tie-break, because coprimality readmits the declined sibling a move later — the tie is a reordering, permanence fails, no opening survives, one point.

Scope. A rule in range: 53 element seeds over two rings; e derived from the minimal representatives.

verifiers: explore_greedy_image_nf.py, explore_class_species_nf.py

No greedy image is countably infinite rule

A surviving opening is a tie, so its multiplicity is at least 2, and a walk's openings are countable: finitely many surviving give a finite product, countably many give at least 2ℵ₀ and at most ℵ₀ℵ₀, the same cardinal. The lower-bound correction passes straight through, a finite sum of finite products being finite and any term reaching 2ℵ₀ being enough. So the space between the finite images and the continuum is empty — though the finite ones being powers of 2 is the sample's accident, both number rings run being quadratic, and the settled counts 3, 6, 4, 4 at bounded gaps say so a second way.

Scope. A derivation from the formula, inheriting its scope.

Both halves of surviving have a budget, which is stronger than the gap it defends rule

Falsifying the empty middle needs endless openings whose choices endlessly fail to survive. ERASURE cannot deliver it: it needs a recurrent vehicle, a recurrent vehicle is a lock, and a lock stops the openings. PERMANENCE was the live half, a bundle readmitting a declined choice as coprimality does, and the cell that decides it — equal characteristic with a class group, where openings never stop and bundles exist — is the coordinate ring of an elliptic curve, its class group the curve's points. Readmission is REAL there and needs class number at least 3: two rational points share a degree and tie, greedy declines one, and a later core's class summons it back as a free rider. But every nonzero class of such a ring contains a place of degree 1, so the free rider is always a rational point — proved, a starved place of degree d ≥ 2 sitting under every seated same-degree neighbour's door by d·e, which no completion — the rider that makes a core principal — closes — and a curve has h − 1 of them; depths never fall, so each is lost once and only the openings before its finite seating can fail. Permanence fails at finitely many openings, so that cell sits at the continuum too. The budget is not the curve's nor Riemann–Roch's: the class group is finite and each cost level holds finitely many ideals, so every class has a least cost attained by finitely many, the rider lies in a fixed finite set, and the permanence half closes for every Dedekind ring with those two properties. Genus 2 pays it: the rider does stop being rational, completions of degree 0, 1 and 2 in all three shapes a degree-2 one can have, and the bound survives untouched, every returning decline of degree 1 — and since every nonzero class there has exactly one minimal affine representative, no earlier choice decides a later summons.

Scope. A rule in range over the complete characteristic-2 elliptic ladder, class numbers 1 to 5, in two worlds, the class-1 control reproducing the F2[x] void move for move: 0, 0, 3, 6, 5 starved declines returned, none in any ideal world, the worst walk losing 2. Genus 2 (y2 + y = x5 + x over F2, class number 15): 16 of 786 starved declines returning, each of degree 1. The rational-rider claim and the unique-representative claim are proved.
