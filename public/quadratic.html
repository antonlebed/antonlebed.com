Quadratic windows

The window every quadratic irrational carries, and what its gate reads.

A window is a nested grid of cells refining toward a point, and it reads a map at lookahead c when the output cell at depth t is a function of the input cell at depth t + c; cmin(t) is the least lookahead that works at depth t, its values over t a map's column, and a map is gated when no bounded lookahead works at all (Reading). The window that counts by Fibonacci numbers rather than by powers — the Zeckendorf window, on Completions — is one member of a family with a member for every irrational.

For irrational α = [0; a1, a2, …] with convergents pk/qk (q0 = 1, q1 = a1, qk = ak qk−1 + qk−2), the Ostrowski digits of n write n = Σ bk qk greedily. Legality — what makes the writing unique — caps b0 at a1 − 1 and bk at ak+1, and forces a zero below any digit sitting at its cap; the lowest admissible digit is position 0 when a1 ≥ 2 and position 1 when a1 = 1, the only position legality caps at 0. At α = 1/φ = [0; 1, 1, 1, …] these are the Zeckendorf digits. The window is the trailing one: the depth-t cell of n is the set of nonnegative integers agreeing with n on their t low-order digits, cells partitioning at each depth and nesting across depths. A string is read through its star Σ bk θk, where θk = qkα − pk is the convergent remainder, |θ| falling geometrically with alternating signs: an integer n sits on the circle at nα mod 1, and its low digits read off that point.

What the nested cells define in the limit is the window's completion, and a map is readable at bounded lookahead exactly when it extends to a Lipschitz self-map of that object (the Lipschitz gate). Every Ostrowski completion is an odometer: its points code circle points, the cells being the arcs a rotation coding cuts, and the odometer's cut points — the circle points with two codings — are −tα for t ≥ 1. On the odometer the successor extends and addition does not, which is what leaves the gate something to say about multiplication itself. A quadratic α supplies two more things: its continued fraction is eventually periodic, and shifting the digit string by one period is the action of the fundamental unit ε of α's order Z[α]. A scan whose witness depth tracks the range it imposes, rather than settling, is cap-shaped, and a scan cell is one (window, map) pair. The flip is the lowest position at which the images of a pair of ever-deeper-agreeing inputs differ, and its address is that position.

The gate

The quadratic Ostrowski gate rule

At four windows — the golden 1/φ; the silver √2 − 1 = [0; 2, 2, …]; the bronze [0; 3, 3, …]; and √3 − 1 = [0; 1, 2, 1, 2, …] — the completion is an odometer at every one and the readings are exact in the limit: the lookahead a map needs at depth t over the integers below N can only rise with N, so it has a limit at every depth, and at a periodic window a carry automaton prints it — a relation between an integer and its image leaves a bounded carry read digit by digit, so the relation is a finite automaton (The lookahead limit). The successor n + 1 needs lookahead exactly 1 at every depth from one above the lowest admissible digit on, never returning to 0; the shift by the period — the unit's action — needs none, the shifted string being legal; dropping the low period's worth of digits needs the period or the period plus one, alternating; and every ×m and ⌊n/m⌋ at m = 2..7 is unbounded from the lowest admissible digit on — the column infinite from the depth that first covers that position, one above it. The larger alphabet {0, …, a} does not rescue integer multiplication: carry direction wins. The gate tracks the unit group of Z[α], not membership in it, which every integer has; and the roof — the per-depth cell count, the counting shadow of the per-digit scale — carries nothing: the number of depth-t cells is the denominator qt itself at every window, its growth by the unit at a periodic window the ratio qt+l/qt and no information about units at all.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope, exhaustive to 3·105 per window, the limit columns decided by the automaton at 108 cells — the four windows and four arbitrary-period windows at m = 2..7, and ×a, ⌊n/a⌋ at [0; 1, 1, a] for a = 2..7 — every one unbounded from one depth above the lowest admissible digit. The failing half is a theorem at every irrational window, the tear — the discontinuity — at a cell endpoint (Completions), and the depth it grows from is the flip's address, a theorem at every irrational window by the containment principle. The roof count a rule at every window and depth measured.

The down-borrow

The mechanism behind the tear at the constant-a windows is one Zeckendorf does not have: the down-borrow a·qk = qk+1 − qk−1, the recurrence read backwards, a subtraction rewriting lower positions where Zeckendorf's mechanism was an additive down-carry. Run along a comb — an input whose digits sit on evenly spaced positions — the borrow either telescopes in one identity or, where its span and the comb's spacing disagree, chains through the rewrites between; either way it leaves a pair of inputs agreeing to unbounded depth whose images do not.

The comb telescopes theorem

At every constant-a window [0; a, a, …] with a ≥ 2, ×a has no continuous extension to the completion: the even comb YK = q2 + q4 + ⋯ + qK telescopes under ×a to qK+1 − a, bottom digit 0, while its top-raised partner YK + qK lands on qK+1 + a·qK − a, whose legal string has bottom digit 1 — the pairs agree to depth K and the images differ at the lowest digit, unconditionally in K and a, one comb and no parity stripe. The degeneracy is a = 1, where legality caps the bottom digit at 0 and squeezes the mixed string out, exactly where Zeckendorf's parity-striped comb takes over. An alternating alphabet moves the flip's address and not the shape: at every period-2 window [0; 1, a, 1, a, …] with a ≥ 2 the borrow holds at every odd position, the positions whose cap is a, so the odd comb q1 + q3 + ⋯ + qK telescopes under ×a to qK+1 − 1 with b1 = a, while the top-raised partner lands on qK+1 + a·qK − 1 with b1 = 0 at odd K ≥ 3: the flip sits at b1, the lowest admissible digit, a1 = 1 pinning b0 at 0 as Zeckendorf's alphabet does, and no stripe appears. And when the borrow's span mismatches the comb's spacing the discontinuity survives with a new carrier: at [0; 1, 1, 2, …] the large quotient's positions sit three apart while its borrow spans two, so no single identity telescopes and the intermediate quotients' rewrites chain — every extension of the comb uM = q3 + q6 + ⋯ + q3M cascades to the bottom, landing 2uM on one of two legal strings by the parity of M, Zeckendorf's stripe one storey up, the flip still at b1.

Scope. Theorem at every constant-a window and every period-2 window [0; 1, a], both for every a ≥ 2, and at the period-3 window [0; 1, 1, 2] for ×2; the combs checked by greedy extraction at a = 2..7 and M ≤ 26. Silver's a = 2 member at K = 12 is the gate scan's measured extremal pair.

The absorption lemma theorem

Across the period-3 family [0; 1, 1, a] the parity of a splits the comb's fate. The two unit quotients give qK+2 = 2qK + qK−1 at K ≡ 0 mod 3, so a top charge c·qK−1 absorbs the next comb extension's a·qK in place, everything below untouched, iff 2c = a − 1. Odd a conserves the half-charge (a − 1)/2, which climbs two positions per extension: the image bottom freezes at (0, 1, (a + 1)/2) and the comb is read, a freeze surviving every alternative comb scanned. Even a's best integer charge a/2 − 1 leaves one unit per extension, and the bottom alternates between two closed forms, b2 striping a/2 against a/2 + 1 by the parity of M. The split is a fact about the comb and never about the gate, which the boundary family below witnesses at every a without consulting its parity.

Scope. Theorem at every a ≥ 3 (a = 2 is the cascade above), the closed forms checked at a = 3..9, M ≤ 26.

The boundary family

A comb built for one window does not carry to the next, and where the comb freezes there is no witness to build at all. What replaces it takes nothing from the period: the legal string of qK − 1 at each depth, and a cut point, where the completion has two names for one point.

The maximal string theorem

Greedy on qK − 1 = aK qK−1 + (qK−2 − 1) recurses in steps of two, so the largest integer supported below position K writes as the alternating cap-filling — digit aK−2i at position K − 1 − 2i, plus b0 = a1 − 1 at odd K — supported on one position parity and flipping whole with the parity of K: bottoms (0, a2, 0) against (a1 − 1, 0, a3) from K ≥ 2, the flip at the lowest admissible digit. The two parities are the two codings of the cut −α below depth K.

Scope. Theorem at arbitrary tail, periodicity never used; verified K = 1..40 at four period-4 and period-5 windows.

The boundary family rule

Dividing near-denominator combinations qK + u·qK−r − t by m inside one both-parity residue class of the convergent pair (qK, pK) mod m — chosen so the input point misses the cut orbit while the image point sits on it; m | t with the input on the orbit is the cut case and is excluded — yields one convergent input family whose images land beside the cut on the side the convergent's sign (−1)K dictates, alternating between the two codings of one point forever. Class existence, the one leg measured per cell, is a theorem at every measured cell by a three-number certificate: the class condition at K is a function of the finite state (K mod the period, the convergent-pair matrix mod m), which evolves invertibly and so is purely periodic, so one even member, one odd member and the state period certify infinitely many of each parity. The excluded class is its own specimen: an input family landing on the cut is the maximal-string family one level down, non-convergent by the same parity flip. And existence is not general — the golden window is provably empty at m = 3, 5, 7, where the address needs a different witness (the apparition criterion).

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: the gate witnessed at every a = 2..7 at [0; 1, 1, a] (classes to K = 400, depths to 140) and at four arbitrary-period windows (periods 4 and 5, mixed alphabets, a1 to 3) at every m = 2..7, all 24 cells (K ≤ 400, depths to 225), the flip at b0 or b1 everywhere and the successor at lookahead 1 beside it; class existence certified at all 30 cells, 29 at r = 1 and one ([0; 1, 1, 1, 2] at m = 5) at r = 3; the limit column at every cell decided by the carry automaton, unbounded from exactly that flip.

The address at every window

Neither witness is needed for the address. The depth-d cells are the arcs the circle is cut into at −tα for 1 ≤ t ≤ qd (no cut at all while qd = 1), so a map tears at any input off the cut orbit whose image lands on it, and whether some input does is a question about where the map sends the cuts — answered at every irrational window, the period never consulted.

The containment principle theorem

The two codings of the cut −tα part at pt = min{p : qp+1 ≥ max(t, 2)}, and −α's at the lowest admissible digit. A shift-free relation mo·w = mi·n + c between an integer n and its image w sends the input's circle point x to (mix + cα + j)/mo with j = miP mod mo, P the integer part of nα, and every offset j occurs inside any deep cell because consecutive convergents are unimodular — qkpk+1 − qk+1pk = ±1, so (qk, pk) and (qk+1, pk+1) span (Z/m)² at every k. The lookahead at depth t is the least ĉ with every output cut's preimages among the depth-(t + ĉ) input cuts, infinite exactly when some preimage is not a cut: a non-cut point is interior to a cell at every depth and the lattice of integer points accumulates at it from both sides. Member by member: at mo = 1 the preimages of −sα are (−(s + c)α + j)/mi, cuts only at j = 0 with mi | s + c, so ×m + c is unbounded from the lowest admissible digit for every c; n + c at c ≥ 0 has every preimage a cut and is finite with the exact column min{ĉ : qt+ĉ ≥ qt + c} (0 while qt = 1), which at c = 1 is the constant 1 from the lowest admissible digit's depth on — the successor's lookahead 1, a theorem at every irrational window; and the decrement n − c at c ≥ 1 sends −α back to the lattice point (c − 1)α, never a cut, so the decrement borrows where the increment does not. At mo ≥ 2 the images of one converging input accumulate at mo points 1/mo apart, a jump the sweep of the offset carries across some cut, so every such relation — the floors ⌊n/m⌋ included, as the union over c — is unbounded from the lowest admissible digit. Every shift-free relation is unbounded from the lowest admissible digit except n + c at c ≥ 0, whose column is exact.

Scope. Theorem at every irrational window, periodicity used nowhere. Checked against the carry automaton at 189 periodic cells over nine windows — n + c for c = 2..5 equal to the formula entry for entry, the decrement, ×m + c and six mo ≥ 2 relations unbounded from exactly one depth above the lowest admissible digit — and in exact integers at e − 2, ∛2 − 1 and five windows designed so that qK ≡ 1 mod m never occurs, 12,150 readings, 0 off; the decrement's two codings of −α (qK − 1 for the two parities of K) at 1,350 readings, 0 off.

The unimodular family theorem

The witness the principle writes down, at −α for ×m: YK = m·qK + a·qK+4 + b·qK+5 with (a, b) ∈ [0, m)² the one solution of a·(q, p)K+4 + b·(q, p)K+5 ≡ (1, 0) mod m, and nK = (YK − 1)/m. The images mnK sit beside −α on alternating sides within (2m − 1)|θK|, parting at exactly p1, while the inputs converge to −α/m, never a cut. The raised-top family that proved the periodic case first — (qK − 1)/m against its top raised by one cap, the crossing ratio |θK+2/θK| < (m − 1)/m — is the sub-case a = b = 0 with qK ≡ 1 mod m, and its need for that residue to recur was the price of one convergent where two are unimodular. At the golden window's obstructed cells (m = 3, 5, 7, where no boundary class exists and no comb is written) the automaton's own witness is of the raised-top kind, its images the two codings of −2α parting at b1 for ×3 and ×5 and of −α for ×7.

Scope. Theorem at every irrational window; read at e − 2 and ∛2 − 1 for m = 2..7 and at five designed tails on which qK ≡ 1 mod m never occurs, every K = 8..56, parting exactly p1 at all 833 readings; the raised-top sub-case at all 54 (window, m) cells the automaton reads.

The shifted members are not addresses theorem

A relation read after the input string is moved r positions has for its image point the pseudo-star Σ dkθk+r, a function of the string and of no circle point; which strides read is the question of Digit shifts. The drop by l — the string moved down l positions — has no address: the single-digit inputs j·qD and (j + 1)·qD drop to points stepping by θD−l, and where aD+1 ≥ qD−l+1 + qD−l the sweep wraps the circle and a consecutive pair straddles −α, parting at p1 with the inputs agreeing to depth exactly D. A window with that inequality at infinitely many D — a designed Liouville-type tail — has the drop by 1 unbounded from the lowest admissible digit, while the four quadratic windows of the gate read the drop by their period finite with peak l + 1. The drop's column is a Diophantine property of the window's quotient growth, and between the two ends it is open: at e − 2, whose quotients grow but sit far below its denominators, a big digit's drop reaches exactly two positions and the finite-range column reads flat across a decade of range, while ∛2 − 1's rises.

Scope. Theorem at the unbounded end (the straddling pair found within 2 of its predicted crossing at every D = 4, 8, …, 24), a rule at the four periodic cells of the finite end, the middle open.

verifiers: explore_relation_address.py, explore_limit_maps.py

How common the certificate is

The apparition criterion theorem

At a constant-a window a common value across parity forces a fixed vector of an odd power of the quotient matrix, whose determinant is −1, so its trace — the Lucas number Ln(a) — must vanish mod m at an odd n; via U2n = UnLn the criterion closes. Let z(m), the rank of apparition, be the first index at which m divides the recurrence's Lucas-type sequence U0 = 0, U1 = 1, Un+1 = a·Un + Un−1. At an odd prime m a both-parity class exists iff z(m) ≡ 2 mod 4, and at m = 2 always. The golden window is empty at m = 3, 5, 7 — Ln² − 5Fn² = ±4 kills m = 5 outright — and every odd prime dividing the discriminant a² + 4 is obstructed at its window.

Scope. Theorem; the iff is period 1's — at every window the trace form stays sufficient, and the (1, 1, 1, 2) window's r = 3 cell shows it is not necessary.

The apparition density theorem

Writing ε for the window's unit, z(m) ≡ 2 mod 4 iff the multiplicative order of ε mod m has 2-adic valuation at most 1, so a prime inert at the window (a² + 4 not a square mod m) never carries the class while a split m ≡ 3 mod 4 always does, and the density of qualifying odd primes is 1/3 at a generic window and 7/24 on the Pell family a² + 4 = 8·square (a = 2, 14, 82, …). The split m ≡ 1 mod 4 half is Chebotarev equidistribution in the 2-power Kummer tower over Q(√(a² + 4)), whose degrees never collapse because the unit's norm −1 is not a square in a real field (Hasse's method; Lagarias 1985 at the golden window, Moree 1996 at every negative-norm-unit window), the one collapse being cyclotomic, √2 ∈ Q(ζ8) at the Pell kernel. Windows whose units are odd powers of one unit share v2(z) at every prime, so the Pell rows and the golden pair a = 1, 4 print identical columns. Obstruction is the two-thirds majority, and nothing waits on it: the address is the containment principle's everywhere, and what the criterion measures is how often this witness exists beside it.

Scope. Theorem throughout; the per-layer fingerprint — among split m ≡ 1 mod 4 with j = v2(m − 1), the layers v2(z) = 0, 1, e arriving with probabilities 21−j, 21−j, 2e−j for 2 ≤ e ≤ j − 1 — checks within 3σ at every cell of 12 windows over primes to 10⁶.

At every window here — each of which has a unit family for the gate to track — the gate tracks the unit group of the order the window's arithmetic lives in, and the family sits in the odometer row of the completion atlas. The tear is a theorem at every irrational window, and what a window's own engine adds is the address, the flip at the lowest admissible digit: by a proved comb at the Zeckendorf, constant-a, [0; 1, a] and [0; 1, 1, 2] windows; at [0; 1, 1, 3] through [0; 1, 1, 7] and the arbitrary-period windows by the certified boundary family; and at every irrational window, for every shift-free relation, by the containment principle — no comb, no certificate, no period. The carry automaton is its check wherever a period exists, the unbounded column starting one depth above the lowest admissible digit at all 108 cells read. Nothing stays open on the address.

Beside that gate rather than under it, these strings carry the shifts, moving every digit up by a fixed stride: nobody's unit action off the period, and readable or not by the stride. Across a family of windows built to carry one large quotient per period the verdict is a function of the stride modulo the period, its gated half derived from combs landing on cuts and its bounded half decided by a cycle criterion whose lattice avoidance is a theorem at both parities of the period — Digit shifts. The lookahead a shift needs past its depth is a column with an exact limit at every periodic window, a finite automaton printing it, and the half-period stride of a window with two large quotients gates exactly where they differ — The lookahead limit. And a wider output alphabet separates the two tears above: raise any digit cap and ×m has a bounded reader on the completion at every irrational window, while ⌊n/m⌋ stays gated at every widening — the tear was the coding's, not the map's — with the reader on the integers priced exactly, the flush the whole price — The widened output.
