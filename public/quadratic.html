Quadratic windows

The window every quadratic irrational carries, and what its gate reads.

A window is a nested grid of cells refining toward a point, and it reads a map at lookahead c when the output cell at depth t is a function of the input cell at depth t + c; cmin(t) is the least lookahead that works at depth t, its values over t a map's column, and a map is gated when no bounded lookahead works at all; the gate is the line between the maps a window reads and the maps it does not (Reading). The window that counts by Fibonacci numbers rather than by powers of a base — the Zeckendorf window, on Completions — is one member of a family with a member for every irrational.

For irrational α = [0; a1, a2, …] with convergents pk/qk (q0 = 1, q1 = a1, qk = ak qk−1 + qk−2), the Ostrowski digits of n write n = Σ bk qk greedily. Legality — what makes the writing unique — caps b0 at a1 − 1 and bk at ak+1 for k ≥ 1, and forces a zero below any digit sitting at its cap; the lowest admissible digit is position 0 when a1 ≥ 2 and position 1 when a1 = 1, the only position legality caps at 0. At α = 1/φ = [0; 1, 1, 1, …] these are the Zeckendorf digits, position k carrying Fk+1; position 0 is capped at 0 there, so a depth on this page is one more than the same cell's depth on Completions. The window is the trailing one: the depth-t cell of n is the set of nonnegative integers agreeing with n on their t low-order digits, cells partitioning at each depth and nesting across depths. A string is read through its star Σ bk θk, where θk = qkα − pk is the convergent remainder, |θ| falling geometrically with alternating signs. The star is nα minus an integer, so it is the circle point nα mod 1, and a string's low digits read off that point.

What the nested cells define in the limit is the window's completion, and a map is readable at bounded lookahead exactly when it extends to a Lipschitz self-map of that object (the Lipschitz gate). Every Ostrowski completion is an odometer, the adding machine on the legal digit strings: its points code circle points, the cells being the arcs into which those points cut the circle, and the odometer's cut points — the circle points with two codings — are −jα for a cut index j ≥ 1. On the odometer the successor extends and addition does not, so multiplication cannot be read off addition and has to be asked on its own. A quadratic α supplies two more things: its continued fraction is eventually periodic with period l, and shifting the digit string by one period is the action of the fundamental unit ε of α's order — the multiplier ring of Z + Zα, the numbers carrying that module into itself. A scan cell is one (window, map) pair. The flip is the lowest position at which the images of a pair of ever-deeper-agreeing inputs differ, and its address is that position.

The gate

The quadratic Ostrowski gate rule

At four windows — the golden 1/φ; the silver √2 − 1 = [0; 2, 2, …]; the bronze [0; 3, 3, …]; and √3 − 1 = [0; 1, 2, 1, 2, …] — the completion is an odometer at every one and the readings are exact in the limit: the lookahead a map needs at depth t over the integers below N can only rise with N, so it has a limit at every depth, finite or not, and at a periodic window a carry automaton prints it — a relation between an integer and its image leaves a bounded carry read digit by digit, so the relation is a finite automaton (The lookahead limit). The successor n + 1 needs lookahead exactly 1 at every depth from one above the lowest admissible digit on, never returning to 0; the shift by the period — the unit's action — needs none, the shifted string being legal; dropping the low period's worth of digits needs the period or the period plus one, alternating — at the golden window not the same map as division by φ, the dropped low digit b0 being always 0 there; and every ×m and ⌊n/m⌋ at m = 2..7 is unbounded from the lowest admissible digit, its column infinite from the first depth that covers that position. The larger input alphabet {0, …, a} does not rescue integer multiplication: carry direction wins. The gate tracks the unit group of α's order, not membership in the order, which every integer has; and the roof — the per-depth cell count — carries nothing: the number of depth-t cells is the denominator qt itself at every window, so the roof is the continued fraction over again: its growth at a periodic window, the ratio qt+l/qt, is the eigenvalue of the period matrix, the product of the period's quotient matrices, and is the same number the period shift already gave.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope, exhaustive to 3·105 per window, the limit columns decided by the automaton at 108 scan cells — the four windows above and the four arbitrary-period windows [0; 1, 1, 1, 2], [0; 2, 1, 3, 1], [0; 1, 2, 1, 1, 3] and [0; 3, 1, 2, 2, 1], each at m = 2..7, and ×a, ⌊n/a⌋ at [0; 1, 1, a] for a = 2..7 — every one unbounded from the lowest admissible digit. The unbounded half is a theorem at every irrational window, the tear — the discontinuity — at a cell endpoint (Completions), and the depth it grows from is the flip's address, a theorem at every irrational window by the containment principle. The roof count is a property; the cells' cuts are proved with the drop's column.

The down-borrow

The mechanism behind the tear at the constant-a windows is one Zeckendorf does not have: the down-borrow a·qk = qk+1 − qk−1, the recurrence read backwards, a subtraction rewriting lower positions where Zeckendorf's mechanism was an additive down-carry. Run along a comb — an input whose digits sit on evenly spaced positions — the borrow either telescopes in one identity or, where its span and the comb's spacing disagree, chains through the rewrites between; either way it leaves a pair of inputs agreeing to unbounded depth whose images do not.

The comb telescopes theorem

At every constant-a window [0; a, a, …] with a ≥ 2, ×a has no continuous extension to the completion: the even comb YK = q2 + q4 + ⋯ + qK telescopes under ×a to qK+1 − a, bottom digit 0, while its top-raised partner YK + qK lands on qK+1 + a·qK − a, whose legal string has bottom digit 1 — the pairs agree to depth K and the images differ at the lowest digit, unconditionally in K and a, one comb and no striping of the image by the comb's length M. The degeneracy is a = 1, where legality caps the bottom digit at 0 and squeezes out the string that mixes the two position parities, exactly where the Zeckendorf window's comb — whose image does stripe by length — takes over. An alternating alphabet moves the flip's address and not the shape: at every period-2 window [0; 1, a, 1, a, …] with a ≥ 2 the borrow holds at every odd position, the positions whose cap is a, so the odd comb q1 + q3 + ⋯ + qK telescopes under ×a to qK+1 − 1 with b1 = a, while the top-raised partner lands on qK+1 + a·qK − 1 with b1 = 0 at odd K ≥ 3: the flip sits at b1, the lowest admissible digit, a1 = 1 pinning b0 at 0 as Zeckendorf's alphabet does, and no stripe appears. And when the borrow's span mismatches the comb's spacing the discontinuity survives with a new carrier: at [0; 1, 1, 2, …] the large quotient 2 sits at every third position while its borrow spans two, so no single identity telescopes and the rewrites at the positions between chain — every extension of the comb uM = q3 + q6 + ⋯ + q3M cascades to the bottom, landing 2uM on one of two legal strings by the parity of M, the same alternation displaced upward, the flip still at b1, the lowest admissible digit where a1 = 1.

Scope. Theorem at every constant-a window and every period-2 window [0; 1, a], both for every a ≥ 2, and at the period-3 window [0; 1, 1, 2] for ×2; the combs checked by greedy extraction at a = 2..7 and M ≤ 26. Silver's a = 2 member at K = 12 is the pair the gate rule's scan met at its deepest K.

The absorption lemma theorem

Across the period-3 family [0; 1, 1, a] the parity of a splits the comb's fate. The window's two quotients equal to 1 give qK+2 = 2qK + qK−1 at K ≡ 0 mod 3, so a top charge c·qK−1 — the digit c placed at position K − 1 — absorbs the next comb extension's a·qK in place, everything below untouched, iff 2c = a − 1. Odd a conserves the half-charge (a − 1)/2, which climbs two positions per extension: the image's three lowest digits (b0, b1, b2) freeze at (0, 1, (a + 1)/2) and the comb stops witnessing anything — the map reads it, a freeze surviving every alternative comb scanned. Even a's best integer charge a/2 − 1 leaves one unit per extension, and the three lowest digits alternate between two closed forms, b2 striping a/2 against a/2 + 1 by the parity of M. The split is a fact about the comb and never about the gate, which the boundary family below witnesses at every a without consulting its parity.

Scope. Theorem at every a ≥ 3 (a = 2 is the cascading case above), the closed forms checked at a = 3..9, M ≤ 26.

The boundary family

A comb built for one window does not carry to the next, and where the comb freezes there is no witness to build at all. What replaces it takes nothing from the period: the legal string of qK − 1 at each depth, and a cut point, where the completion has two names for one point.

The maximal string theorem

Greedy on qK − 1 = aK qK−1 + (qK−2 − 1) recurses in steps of two, so the largest integer supported below position K writes as the alternating cap-filling — digit aK−2i at position K − 1 − 2i, plus b0 = a1 − 1 at odd K — supported on one position parity and flipping whole with the parity of K: the three lowest digits are (0, a2, 0) against (a1 − 1, 0, a3) from K ≥ 2, the flip at the lowest admissible digit. The two parities are the two codings of the cut −α below depth K.

Scope. Theorem at arbitrary tail, periodicity never used; verified K = 1..40 at four period-4 and period-5 windows.

The boundary family rule

The inputs are the near-denominator combinations qK + u·qK−r − t, divided by m: the integer r ≥ 1 is how far below K the second convergent sits, u its coefficient and t the amount subtracted, the three of them the knobs a cell is scanned over. A both-parity class is a residue class mod m of the pair (qK + u·qK−r, pK + u·pK−r) attained at an even K and at an odd one. Taking the class so that the input point misses the cut orbit while the image point sits on it — m | t with the input already on the orbit is the cut case and is excluded — yields one input family that converges, its images landing beside the cut on the side the convergent's sign (−1)K dictates, alternating between the two codings of one point forever. Class existence, the one thing a cell measures rather than proves, is certified rather than merely measured at every cell scanned, by a three-number certificate: whether the class is attained at K depends only on the finite state (K mod the period, and mod m the convergent-pair matrix whose columns are (qK, pK) and (qK−1, pK−1)), which evolves invertibly and so is purely periodic, so one even member, one odd member and the state period certify infinitely many of each parity. The excluded class is its own specimen: an input family landing on the cut is the maximal string one position lower, whose inputs do not converge, by the same parity flip. And existence is not general — the golden window is provably empty at m = 3, 5, 7, where the address needs a different witness (the apparition criterion).

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: the gate witnessed at every a = 2..7 at [0; 1, 1, a] (classes to K = 400, depths to 140) and at the four arbitrary-period windows above at every m = 2..7, all 24 scan cells (K ≤ 400, depths to 225), the flip at b0 or b1 everywhere and the successor at lookahead 1 beside it; class existence certified at all 30 scan cells, 29 at r = 1 and one ([0; 1, 1, 1, 2] at m = 5) at r = 3; the limit column at every cell decided by the carry automaton, unbounded from exactly that flip.

How common the certificate is

The apparition criterion theorem

At a constant-a window the recurrence carries two sequences. Let z(m), the rank of apparition, be the first index at which m divides U0 = 0, U1 = 1, Un+1 = a·Un + Un−1, and write Ln(a) for its companion L0 = 2, L1 = a, Ln+1 = a·Ln + Ln−1. At a constant-a window a value common to both parities forces a nonzero vector mod m fixed by an odd power of the quotient matrix [[a, 1], [1, 0]], whose determinant is −1, so the power's trace — which is Ln(a) — must vanish mod m at an odd n; via U2n = UnLn the criterion closes. So at a constant-a window and an odd prime m a both-parity class exists iff z(m) ≡ 2 mod 4; at m = 2 one always exists. The golden window is empty at m = 3, 5, 7 — Ln² − 5Fn² = ±4 kills m = 5 outright — and every odd prime dividing the discriminant a² + 4 is obstructed at its window.

Scope. Theorem; the iff is the constant-a case's — at every window the trace condition stays sufficient, and the [0; 1, 1, 1, 2] window's r = 3 cell shows it is not necessary.

The apparition density theorem

Writing ε for the window's unit, z(m) ≡ 2 mod 4 iff the multiplicative order of ε mod m has 2-adic valuation at most 1, so a prime inert at the window (a² + 4 not a square mod m) never carries the class while a split m ≡ 3 mod 4 always does, and the density of qualifying odd primes is 1/3 away from the Pell family a² + 4 = 8·square and 7/24 on it (a = 2, 14, 82, …). The split m ≡ 1 mod 4 half is Chebotarev equidistribution in the 2-power Kummer tower over Q(√(a² + 4)), whose degrees never collapse because the unit's norm −1 is not a square in a real field (Hasse's method; Lagarias 1985 at the golden window, Moree 1996 at every negative-norm-unit window), the one collapse being cyclotomic: √2 ∈ Q(ζ8), which is where the Pell family's own collapse comes from. Windows whose units are odd powers of one unit share v2(z) at every prime, so the Pell windows print one column between them, and so do a = 1 and a = 4. Obstruction is therefore the majority — two odd primes in three away from the Pell family — and nothing waits on it: the address is the containment principle's at every window, and what the criterion measures is how often this witness exists beside it.

Scope. Theorem throughout; the per-layer fingerprint — among split m ≡ 1 mod 4 with ν = v2(m − 1), the layers v2(z) = 0, 1, f arriving with probabilities 21−ν, 21−ν, 2f−ν for 2 ≤ f ≤ ν − 1 — checks within 3σ at every cell of 12 windows over primes to 10⁶.

At every window here the gate tracks the unit group of the multiplier ring the window's arithmetic lives in, and every completion here is an odometer. The tear is a theorem at every irrational window, and what a window's own arithmetic adds is the address, the flip at the lowest admissible digit: by a proved comb at the Zeckendorf, constant-a, [0; 1, a] and [0; 1, 1, 2] windows; and at [0; 1, 1, 3] through [0; 1, 1, 7] and the arbitrary-period windows by the certified boundary family. The carry automaton is its check wherever a period exists, the unbounded column starting one depth above the lowest admissible digit at all 108 scan cells read. Neither witness is needed for the address itself, which is fixed by the cuts alone at every irrational window (The flip's address).

What that costs a window's own arithmetic is everything: the relations tying an image to its input by one linear equation — ×m, the floors ⌊n/m⌋, n + c — have their columns decided by whether every output cut's preimages are themselves cuts a bounded depth below, which leaves every one of them unbounded from the lowest admissible digit except n + c at c ≥ 0, whose column is exact and whose c = 1 member is the successor's lookahead 1. The drop by g — the whole string moved down g positions — is not of that form and takes no address from the principle; its column is read off cuts a pair of inputs straddles instead, infinite at e − 2 and at a bounded non-periodic window, open in general (The flip's address).

Beside the gate rather than under it stand the shifts, which are nobody's unit action: whether one reads is a function of its stride modulo the period (Digit shifts), and the lookahead it needs past its own depth has an exact limit at every periodic window (The lookahead limit). And a wider output alphabet separates the two tears above: raise any digit cap and ×m has a bounded reader on the completion at every irrational window, while ⌊n/m⌋ stays gated at every widening — the tear was the coding's, not the map's (The widened output).
