Ceilings

Evaluations whose best possible score is derived rather than estimated: what the optimum is, on which members of the family the evidence is worth nothing, and what attaining the optimum costs — every value a closed form, on families designed so that a closed form exists.

An eval is a task family with a prior over its instances, an evidence channel the solver is shown, and a score. Its ceiling is the Bayes-optimal expected score, what a solver that knew the generating distribution would get; its floor is the best score with the evidence severed, the prior's own best constant answer, computed by summation rather than assumed to be one half. One member of the family is a cell; a cell is dead when ceiling and floor coincide, so the evidence buys nothing there, and interior when the ceiling strictly beats the floor without reaching certainty. A ceiling is tight when a procedure of stated cost meets it, the achiever; the Bayes posterior meeting the Bayes optimum is a definition, so the content of each block is the closed form together with the achiever's price. Two scores are asked: 0-1 accuracy, which pays for a correct answer, and log-loss, which reads the probability assigned to the truth and whose optimum is a least conditional entropy. Under either score “ceiling” means that score's optimum.

The families are cut from the archimedean deletion. Fix N squarefree — a product of distinct primes, at least two — and draw from Z/N uniformly: one element x, or in two of the families below an ordered pair or triple of distinct elements. A channel is the residue modulo one prime dividing N. The solver is shown a nonempty proper subset of the channels, whose product is the read modulus M, and the product of the unread ones is the unknown cofactor c = N/M. What the shown residues pin is a fiber: the c elements sharing them, an arithmetic progression of step M, one progression per point when a pair or triple is drawn. The question asked is always about size or order, which residues do not carry (the hiding lemma), and it is one bit until the last block widens it to k parts. A cell is one pair (ring, shown subset), and a dial is the arithmetic condition naming which cells are dead.

Every ceiling below is derived from its design, exact in rational arithmetic, and confirmed cell by cell across the stated range. That is also the scope: rings of at most a few tens of thousands of elements, and no number here is a ceiling for a benchmark built by anyone else.

The anatomy

The sign bit and the parity dial rule

Ask for the sign bit [2x ≥ N], which half of the ring x fell in. The 0-1 ceiling is exactly (c+1)/2c when the unknown cofactor is odd and exactly 1/2 when it is even. The fiber is a progression of step M, so its count of elements below N/2 is ⌈c/2 − r/M⌉ at shown residue r: c/2 for every r at even c, and one of two values at odd c. As N is squarefree, c is even exactly when 2 divides N and channel 2 is unread, so the dial is one arithmetic bit: a cell is dead exactly when its ring has a channel 2 and the solver is not shown it. At odd N every proper subset lifts off the floor. The log-loss ceiling is the same dichotomy: the binary entropy H2((c+1)/2c) at odd c and log 2 at even. The achiever costs a constant per query — combine the shown residues into r = x mod M by the Chinese remainder theorem, then answer “below” iff 2r < M — and is tight at every cell.

A single channel obeys the same law one level coarser, since a solver reading one channel is the same eval at read modulus p: at even N only channel 2 earns anything, 1/2 + 1/N, and at odd N every channel p earns 1/2 + p/2N. So the family prices its own cheaper solvers as a difference of two of its own closed forms. At N = 2310 with channels {2, 3, 5} shown, the joint ceiling is 39/77, the best single channel is worth 578/1155, and the price of reading one channel instead of three is 1/165.

Scope. Proved for every squarefree N and proper divisor M; exhaustive over the 602 cells of the 57 rings built from the first six primes, largest N = 30030, in exact rationals with entropies to 1e-12 nats. 211 cells sit at the floor and all 211 are the cells with channel 2 unread. The achiever was enumerated on the 362 cells with N ≤ 2310. Uniform prior; 0-1 and log-loss scores. Toy scale.

The threshold law rule

The sign bit is the predicate [x ≥ t] at the midpoint, so ask it at every threshold t; a cell together with a threshold is a slice. Write s for t mod M. A fiber's count below t is ⌊t/M⌋ + [r < s], so a cell has exactly two fiber types, and the 0-1 ceiling is the floor max(t, N−t)/N everywhere except where c is odd and t lies strictly inside the middle fiber window (mM, (m+1)M) with m = (c−1)/2, where it is (c+1)/2c independent of t. The even half of the parity dial survives at every threshold; the odd half deforms from a parity condition into a window condition. The lift over the floor is the tent min(s, M−s)/N, largest at the midpoint: the sign bit is the extremal member of its own family. The achiever is unchanged and stays tight.

Scope. Proved for every squarefree N, proper divisor M and threshold; exhaustive on the 40,914 slices of the 194 cells over the 37 rings with N ≤ 500, all 2,697 interior slices carrying the tent exactly, plus slices sampled at window edges, midpoints and ends up to N = 2310. Uniform prior. Toy scale.

Which channels pay

The orientation ceiling and the inverted dial rule

Draw a uniform ordered triple of distinct elements, show all three residues mod M, and ask for the triple's cyclic orientation, the direction one travels around the ring to meet the three in order. Translating the first point to 0 leaves a pair of differences (a, b) with orientation positive iff a < b as integers, and the fiber posterior is uniform on a product of two c-element progressions, so the count of positive completions is a staircase count on a c-by-c grid: rung by rung of one progression, how many rungs of the other lie beyond it. A fiber whose two shown differences are distinct and nonzero leans by exactly (c+1)/2c toward the order the residues show; a fiber where they agree or one is zero is exactly balanced. Weighting the fiber classes,

12  +  c (M−1)(M−2)2 (N−1)(N−2),\tfrac{1}{2} \;+\; \frac{c\,(M-1)(M-2)}{2\,(N-1)(N-2)},

off the floor exactly when M ≥ 3. No fiber ever decides the orientation — the fraction of triples the shown channels determine is 0 on every cell (cyclic orientation is totally hidden) — and the lean inside that total hiding is real and priced. The dial inverts against the sign bit's: the only dead subset is {2}, the one channel the sign bit lived in. A single channel obeys the formula at M = p, worth zero at p = 2 and increasing in p, so the best single channel is the largest prime read. Which channels pay is a property of the task, not of the ring.

Scope. Proved for every squarefree N and proper divisor M; exhaustive on the 194 cells over the 37 rings with N ≤ 500, where the 19 floor cells are exactly the subset-{2} cells; the reduction to difference pairs confirmed against raw triples at N = 30. The achiever, two residue subtractions and one comparison, is tight. Uniform prior. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_ceiling_anatomy.py

The comparison ceiling and the constant dial rule

Draw a uniform ordered pair of distinct elements, show both residues mod M, and ask [x < y]. A fiber with unequal shown residues is a product of two full progressions and leans by (c+1)/2c toward the residue order; a fiber with equal ones is balanced, as many distinct pairs ascending as descending. Weighting them, the cofactor cancels:

12  +  M−12 (N−1)\tfrac{1}{2} \;+\; \frac{M-1}{2\,(N-1)}

against a floor of exactly 1/2, with no dead cell at all, M = 2 included. A third dial shape — constant, where the sign bit's was parity and orientation's was inversion — and the value depends on the ring and the read modulus alone, not on how much is hidden. The achiever combines both residues, orders them and answers a fixed way on a tie; the best single channel is again the largest prime read. The same rings admit an exact comparator, which buys exactness by importing an extra coordinate and reading every channel (the exact comparator and its cost law); this is the other end of that question, what a proper subset is worth with nothing imported.

Scope. The ceiling, the floor and the log-loss form proved; exhaustive on the 362 cells of the 49 rings with N ≤ 2310, the achiever enumerated on the 116 cells with N ≤ 210, the entropy strictly below log 2 on every cell. Uniform prior on the pair. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_ceiling_dials.py

The skew unit observation

Three task shapes, three dial shapes, one number underneath. Every interior value above reduces to a staircase count on a c-element progression, and the per-fiber skew (c+1)/2c is what such a count yields: the sign bit's whole ceiling, the threshold family's interior value, the lean of an orientation fiber before dilution by the fraction of fibers that lean at all, and the lean of a comparison fiber before ties. The dials differ in which cells are alive; what a live cell is worth keeps reducing to the same unit.

Scope. An observation across the four instances, each of whose ceilings is separately proved at the range its block states. Toy scale.

Whether the evidence is worthless

Deadness is score-relative rule

At the midpoint the two scores agree about which cells are dead, both naming the even-c ones; across the threshold family they part. Call the set of thresholds at which a cell is dead its floor set, one per score. The log-loss floor set is the thin set of thresholds divisible by M, where every fiber is the same type and the posterior equals the prior. The 0-1 floor set is everything outside the odd-c middle window: there the posterior moves but never crosses one half, so a decision score cannot spend what a log score is paid for. At any threshold outside the window and not divisible by M the two scores disagree outright about whether the evidence is worth anything. What survives the choice of score is the structural core, the thresholds divisible by M.

Scope. Proved, and exhaustive on the threshold family's 40,914 slices: 4,599 in the log-loss floor set against 38,217 in the 0-1 floor set, entropies to 1e-12 nats. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_ceiling_anatomy.py, explore_ceiling_dials.py

The window law and the resonance criterion rule

Replace the uniform prior by the geometric tilt, P(x) proportional to θx for a positive real θ, uniform at θ = 1, and re-derive the threshold family under it. Within a fiber the weights form a geometric ladder in q = θM, and every value — ceiling, floor, conditional entropy — stays closed-form. A fiber's mass below the threshold exceeds one half exactly when its count below exceeds

Q∗=log⁡q1+qc2,Q^{*} = \log_{q} \frac{1 + q^{c}}{2},

so a cell's interior thresholds are exactly one fiber window of M−1 thresholds, located at ⌊Q*⌋, and the window is empty exactly when Q* is an integer B. Call such a tilt resonant. Resonance is algebra: Q* = B says q is a root of qc − 2qB + 1, monic with constant term 1, whose only rational roots are ±1, so no rational tilt off uniform is ever resonant. Each 0 < B < c with 2B ≠ c carries exactly one resonant tilt, an algebraic irrational, below 1 iff 2B < c, the tilts of B and c−B reciprocal to each other. At 2B = c the only root is q = 1, a double root: the uniform prior is that family collapsed, and parity deadness is Q* = c/2 landing on an integer. Every non-resonant tilt revives every even-c cell. What does not move with the prior is the structural core: a threshold divisible by M leaves every fiber the same type at every θ, so those slices stay dead at every tilt and remain the whole log-loss floor set. Only the core is prior-robust — the same test the strongest grade of certified forgetting applies to a forgotten datum, flatness at every reweighting of the prior rather than at a named one (the four grades).

Scope. The window law and the resonance criterion proved for every M and c; the criterion confirmed at c ≤ 12 by an exhaustive rational scan to numerator and denominator 60, no rational resonance among 145,332 candidates, with the count, the side and the reciprocal pairing holding at all 66 (B, c) pairs. The window law exhaustive on the 116 cells with N ≤ 210 at every threshold across a nine-point θ grid, plus spot points to N = 2310; the θ = 1 point reproduces the uniform law exactly, and all 39 even-c cells are interior at each of the eight off-uniform grid tilts. Only the threshold family is re-derived under tilt; the orientation and comparison evals stand at the uniform prior. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_ceiling_dials.py, explore_tilt_resonance.py

A fixed threshold is prior-brittle; the family self-repairs rule

The sign bit's even-c half is dead at every tilt, its midpoint threshold sitting on a fiber boundary. Its odd-c half is alive only while q stays strictly between the one root ρ in (0, 1) of the death boundary 2qm = 1 + qc, m = (c−1)/2, and that root's reciprocal — the resonant tilt of B = m, whose root at c = 3 is the reciprocal golden ratio. The threshold family keeps one live window at every tilt, sliding toward the bottom fiber as q → 0 and the top as q → ∞, and only the resonant tilts ever close it, none of them rational off uniform. A fixed threshold is the brittle object; the family is not.

Scope. Proved from the window law; every sign-bit cell is dead at each of the eight off-uniform grid tilts, all of which sit outside the live band. Toy scale.

The k-ary ceiling and the live arc rule

Ask which k-th of the ring x fell in: the magnitude class Y = ⌊kx/N⌋, the sign bit at k = 2. Write c = ak + b with 0 ≤ b < k. Within a fiber the classes occupy consecutive runs of a or a+1 elements, exactly b of them the longer, the heavy classes; which are heavy rotates with the shown residue while their count does not, and at b ≥ 1 the first heavy class is ⌊kr/bM⌋. At uniform the ceiling is ⌈c/k⌉/c at every fiber against a floor of ⌈N/k⌉/N, and guessing the first heavy class attains it. A cell is dead at uniform iff k | c or saturation, M(k−b) < k, possible only at k > M, where class 0 is heavy at every fiber and the evidence never moves the guess.

Under the tilt the runs stand still and only their masses move, each class weighing a difference of two powers of q, so convexity leaves at most four classes ever optimal at a fiber: 0 and the first heavy below uniform, the last heavy and k−1 above. Below uniform, a fiber whose first heavy class is not 0 disagrees with the floor's guess iff q exceeds the one root in (0, 1) of qL+a+1 − qL − qa + 1, L = a·⌊kr/bM⌋, and the mirror x ↔ N−1−x carries that above uniform. So every cell is one of three: dead everywhere (k | c or saturated); alive on one open arc (qlo, qup) of tilts with algebraic ends; or alive at every tilt (a = 0, unsaturated, some fiber's class-0 run empty). Saturated cells have qlo = 1, dead at uniform and revived by any upward tilt, because the floor in ⌊kx/N⌋ breaks the mirror symmetry. Whenever M > k and a ≥ 1 the arc is the band (ρa, 1/ρa), ρa the root in (0, 1) of the resonance polynomial with B = a and c = 2a+1: a function of a = ⌊c/k⌋ alone, reciprocal-golden at a = 1. The sign bit's death boundary is this law's k = 2 row.

Scope. The run law, the uniform ceiling, floor, achiever and dial, and the arc law proved; exhaustive in exact rationals at 428 cells with M ∈ {2, 3, 6, 15, 35}, every integer cofactor 2 ≤ c ≤ 12 — the run law reads only the fiber's progression, so N = Mc is not held squarefree — and k ≤ 9, 144 cells dead at uniform, 44 of them by saturation; the arc law verified at 1,602 (cell, tilt) evaluations over a nine-point grid, sample tilts on each side of every finite arc edge, 44,191 checks. Uniform and tilted priors; 0-1 score. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_kary_ceiling.py

Together the score and the prior make “remaining headroom” exact. Headroom is a gap between a ceiling and a score, and the ceiling moves with the score asked and the prior assumed — enough, in these families, for two scores to disagree about whether the evidence is worth anything and for a family's dead cells to be an artifact of one prior. Where the family is designed, both dependencies are written down: the dial, the window and the boundary each fails at.

Scoring an estimator

A closed form for the optimum is also a measuring device: an estimator of that optimum can be scored against the answer instead of against another estimate. Four standard Bayes-error estimators run on these families all break somewhere, and none of the breakages is visible from inside the estimator carrying it — one interval never repairs however much data arrives, one contains the truth less often as data arrives, one is correct everywhere and too weak to certify the evidence is worth anything, and one's statistic the evidence does not determine at all. What sets the direction each failure takes is the statement rather than the estimator: stated uncertainty.
