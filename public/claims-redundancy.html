The widened output

  • The tear survives at zero excess theorem — With the below-a-cap rule dropped and every cap kept the range is exactly [−α, 1 − α] and ×m's tear keeps its address at every irrational window: dropping the rule alone buys ×m nothing. — explore_redundant_ostrowski.py
  • The completion's reader exists at any positive excess theorem — Raise any cap, so the excess is positive, and ×m has a bottom-up reader on the completion at every irrational window under the overlap bound c = O(log(2m/(1 + s))), position 0 raised by one being enough: the tear was the writing's. — explore_redundant_ostrowski.py
  • The floors stay torn at every slack theorem — ⌊n/m⌋ stays gated at every irrational window and every slack, its tear the map's, x ↦ x/m being m-valued on the circle with branch n mod m; the successor reads at lookahead 0 once the below-a-cap rule is dropped. — explore_redundant_ostrowski.py
  • That reader is the completion's, not the integers' property — The completion's reader is not the integers': an integer input must flush to residual 0, so the integer reader carries the alive offsets in a residual game on the finite automaton, its least winning lookahead cint. — explore_redundant_ostrowski.py, explore_lookahead_band.py
  • The universal reader over bounded quotients rule — In the frame of two consecutive convergents the game at every window with quotients at most A is one finite game with a universal reader: ×2 at slack (1, 1) reads at lookahead 2 over quotients at most 3, ×3 at 3 over quotients at most 2. — explore_universal_reader.py, explore_reader_box.py, explore_reader_runs.py
  • The flush floor: lookahead 0 or at least 2, never 1 rule — cint is 0 when s ≥ (m − 1)amax, whatever s0, and at least 2 otherwise, never 1: from the flushed state a late cap digit's image breaks the cap below the digitwise line, spilling into two positions a reader at lookahead 1 has written. — explore_flush_floor.py
  • The price of the flush, and the law it reads observation — The flush's price cint − csaf is 0 at 91 of the grid's 120 nonzero-slack cells, 1 at 28 and 2 at one, and at every cell decided cint = max(csaf, L*), the released reader's lookahead against the clairvoyant's from a flushed state. — explore_flush_price.py, explore_flush_law.py, explore_lookahead_band.py
  • The flush is the whole price theorem — At every purely periodic window, m ≥ 1 and slack with s0 ≤ s + 1, the completion's reader's least lookahead equals the released reader's: offsets, box and frame cost nothing, the whole distance to the integer reader being the flush's price. — explore_flush_theorem.py, explore_completion_reader.py, explore_completion_lift.py
  • What a proof of the price law must supply observation — The winning set at the winning lookahead is exactly the flushable states at every grid cell, and at every losing lookahead one round of removal annihilates it; the lemma a proof owes depends on the lookahead, not on the state alone. — explore_flush_closure.py, explore_flush_seed.py, explore_flush_depth.py, explore_flush_order.py
