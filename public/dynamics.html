Eigenvalue Cost

Each CRT channel has a cost: c(p) = 4sin²(π/p). Cheaper channels activate last in the hierarchy. mod 11 is cheapest; mod 8 and mod 9 are most expensive.

Total cost = 7.452. The hierarchy builds expensive channels first (mod 8, mod 9) and cheap channels last (mod 11). This connects spectral theory to the activation order.
