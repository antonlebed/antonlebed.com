Death and 1,576,576

CRT(1,576,576) = (0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1)

The Mathematics

The Projector (PROVED)
2^420 mod 12,612,600 = 1,576,576. This number is an idempotent: 1,576,576^2 = 1,576,576. Its CRT decomposition is (0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1). The mod-8 channel is annihilated. The remaining five channels (mod 9, mod 25, mod 49, mod 11, mod 13) are preserved exactly. Multiplying any element n by 1,576,576 projects away its mod-8 component.

The identity 1 decomposes as 1 = e + P, where e is the mod-8 idempotent and P = 1,576,576 is the projector. Every element is the sum of its mod-8 projection and its five-channel projection. P preserves five of six channels; e preserves only the mod-8 channel.

mod-8 channel: annihilated

The projector sets the mod-8 channel to 0. Any information encoded in n mod 8 is lost. This is the only channel destroyed.

mod-9, mod-25, mod-49: preserved

The three inner channels survive projection intact. n mod 9, n mod 25, n mod 49 are unchanged.

mod-11, mod-13: preserved

The two boundary channels also survive. n mod 11 and n mod 13 pass through unchanged.

490 Split (PROVED)
490 = 2 x 5 x 7^2. In Z/12,612,600: 490^420 has CRT = (0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1) = the projector. The channels for {2, 5, 7} project to 0, while {3, 11, 13} project to 1. 490 splits the ring into inner and boundary. Z/12,612,600 has 64 idempotents; the projector's image has 32.

Philosophical Reflection

The algebra above is proved. What follows is interpretation, not derivation. The CRT projector annihilates one channel while preserving five others. If you read the mod-8 channel as physical substrate and the remaining channels as pattern and structure, then the projector describes a dissolution where pattern outlasts substrate. Whether this says anything about actual death is a philosophical question, not a mathematical one. The ring gives the structure. The meaning is yours.

Seven Channels

TRANS Projector (PROVED)
2^1680 mod 214,414,200 = 26,801,776. CRT = (0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1). The same mechanism in the seven-channel ring: mod-8 dies, but now six channels survive instead of five. The mod-17 channel passes through unchanged. Idempotent: 26,801,776^2 = 26,801,776.

In Z/12,612,600, five of six channels survive projection. In Z/214,414,200, six of seven. The structure is the same -- the mod-8 channel is always the one that dies, because 2^3 = 0 mod 8 and every higher power of 2 stays at 0. Every other channel's modulus is odd, so powers of 2 cycle forever and never reach 0.

mod-8: always annihilated

2^3 = 0 mod 8. The mod-8 channel dies regardless of how many channels the ring has. This is the only channel destroyed.

mod-9 through mod-13: preserved

The five channels from the six-prime ring all survive. Their projector values are identical: each sees residue 1.

mod-17: also preserved

The seventh channel. 2^1680 mod 17 = 1 (since 1680 is divisible by phi(17) = 16). The escape channel survives the projection.

Complement: 214,414,200 - 26,801,776 + 1 = 187,612,425, with CRT = (1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0). The same orthogonal split: one projector keeps everything except mod-8, the other keeps only mod-8. Their sum is 1. Their product is 0.
