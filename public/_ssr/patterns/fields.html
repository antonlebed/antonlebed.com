Z[i]: |1+2i|^2 = 5 splits

The chain primes {2,3,5,7,11} live in Z, but their shadows extend into number fields: Gaussian integers Z[i] (disc = -4), Eisenstein integers Z[omega] (disc = -3), imaginary quadratic fields Q(sqrt(-d)). 5 is the ONLY chain prime that splits in Z[i] -- the prime that fails to divide 24 is the one that decomposes.

Two quadratic fields partition ALL key constants. The Gaussian field (disc = -4) generates 13, 41, 137. The Eisenstein field (disc = -3) generates 7, 19, 67, 163. Together they organize the entire chain.
