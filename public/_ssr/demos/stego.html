CRT Steganography

Steganography patents. CC0 alternative.

CRT channels are algebraically independent. Replace mod-8 and mod-9 channels with secret data -- the remaining 5 channels (mod 25, 49, 11, 13, 17) are untouched. The stego value is still a valid ring element. The secret is invisible to anyone who doesn't know which channels to read.

How It Works

CRT Steganographic Principle
Any element of Z/214,414,200 has 7 independent CRT residues. Modifying mod-8 (3 bits) and mod-9 (~3.2 bits) channels embeds ~6.2 bits per carrier value. The other 5 channels carry the original signal undistorted. CRT independence means the hidden data is algebraically invisible in the mod-25, 49, 11, 13, 17 subspace.
