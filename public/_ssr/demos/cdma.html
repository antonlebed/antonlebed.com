CRT Spread Spectrum

CDMA patents. CC0 alternative.

CDMA: multiple users share ONE channel. CRT makes each coprime key a unique algebraic spreading code. 7 independent channels, each with its own modular inverse. Encode = multiply by key. Decode = multiply by inverse. phi(214,414,200) = 38,707,200 simultaneous users at O(1) cost.

How It Works

CRT Spreading Principle
Every integer coprime to N = 214,414,200 is invertible in the ring. Coprime k spreads data d to d*k per channel. The receiver despreads with k^(-1) per channel: each modulus m has a unique inverse. CRT independence means interference from other users vanishes per-channel. 7 moduli = 7 orthogonal despreading dimensions.
