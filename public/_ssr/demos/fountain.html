CRT channels ARE fountain drops. Each value decomposes into 7 independent residues. Lose ANY one channel and reconstruct from the remaining 6. No Luby transform, no random graphs, no degree distributions. The ring structure IS the erasure code. 7 drops, 6 sufficient.

How It Works

CRT Erasure Principle
By the Chinese Remainder Theorem, any value in Z/214,414,200 is UNIQUELY determined by its 7 residues mod {8,9,25,49,11,13,17}. These residues are algebraically independent. Losing one channel leaves 6 residues. We can trial the missing channel (at most 49 trials for mod-49) to find the unique value matching all 6 known residues. Recovery is guaranteed with probability 1.
