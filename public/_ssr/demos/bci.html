CRT BCI Signal

E30: Neuralink/BrainGate. CC0.

EEG has 6 standard frequency bands: Delta(0.5-4Hz), Theta(4-8Hz), Alpha(8-13Hz), Beta(13-30Hz), Gamma(30-80Hz), High-Gamma(80-150Hz). CRT decomposes neural signals into exactly 6 algebraically independent channels. L=11 = artifact rejection for free. The ring IS the BCI filter bank.

How It Works

CRT Neural Decomposition
An EEG sample in Z/12612600 decomposes into 6 bands: D(mod 8)=Delta, K(mod 9)=Theta, E(mod 25)=Alpha, b(mod 49)=Beta, L(mod 11)=Gamma, G(mod 13)=High-Gamma. 490 split: DEAD={Delta,Alpha,Beta} = neural content. ALIVE={Theta,Gamma,HiGamma} = neural integrity. L=11 artifact detection: ~92% for fat channels, 100% with dual L+GATE parity.
