ARC-AGI Solver

32 primitives vs 175 billion parameters

ARC-AGI: 800 abstract reasoning puzzles. Each has training examples showing input grids mapped to output grids. Given a new input, predict the output. No two puzzles use the same rule. This page runs a real solver in your browser, using canvas to render the colored grids.

The Pipeline

Structure vs Scale
The scaling approach: train a billion-parameter model on millions of examples. Cost: hundreds of millions in compute. The axiom approach: 32 simple primitives + composition search. Cost: zero. Runs in your browser. No training data. Current score: 61/800 (7.6%). CRT decompose+recombine alone gets 77% of eval correct tasks.
StageMethodSearch Space
1. SANDPILE32 primitives, depth-2~1024 compositions

This demo implements Stage 1 with 7 primitives (flip, rotate, tile, mirror, color map). Tasks 5-6 show the solver's limits: they need specialized transforms not in our basic set.
