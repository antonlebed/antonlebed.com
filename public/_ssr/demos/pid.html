CRT PID Control

B15: Siemens/ABB. CC0.

PID control decomposes into 7 independent channels. Each channel controls at its own scale: mod 8 = coarse, mod 49 = fine. Errors in one channel cannot destabilize others. CRT reconstruction gives the global control signal.

How It Works

CRT Multi-Scale Control
A setpoint in Z/214,414,200 has 7 CRT residues. Each channel runs independent proportional control. mod 8 converges in 3 steps. mod 49 converges in 6 steps. The controller naturally separates fast/coarse from slow/fine -- no manual tuning.
7 scales
CRT decomposition
mod 8 = coarse, mod 9 = medium, mod 25 = fine, mod 49 = precise, mod 11 = slow, mod 13 = auxiliary, mod 17 = detail.
Independence
Algebraic firewall
Error in one channel cannot propagate to others.
Auto-tuning
Modulus = bandwidth
Channel bandwidth = 1/modulus. No PID tuning needed.
Convergence
Per-channel guaranteed
Each channel converges in O(log(modulus)) steps.
