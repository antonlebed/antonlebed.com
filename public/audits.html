Deletion audits

Two published deletion audits run against a target known exactly: what each gets right, what its stated uncertainty covers, the estimator of one of them taken apart root by root, what an auditor holding no truth can compute about the stability of their own verdict, and how good any statement of that kind can be.

The target comes from a family of small grown worlds (the amnesia certificate is what they were built for). A state is a positive integer, a move multiplies it by a member of that state's menu, and a route is the sequence of moves from the start state 1. The world used throughout is the depth column, whose menu is {2, 3} at every state, so 2a3b is reached by every arrangement of a twos and b threes, all equally likely. The fiber of a state is the set of routes reaching it, and the posterior of a function of the route is its conditional distribution on that fiber. The function audited is the first move, whose posterior at 2a3b is exactly (a/(a+b), b/(a+b)): the count leak, a state's own exponents naming what it still tells about how it started. A tool that estimates that posterior from samples is scored on its bias and on what its stated uncertainty covers, the number it aims at being a rational fixed in advance, and the exponents place that number wherever a run wants it.

An inference-style deletion audit is such a tool. It holds samples of a system's behaviour with the audited datum present (the member class) and absent (non-member), and estimates from them what the state under audit still holds. Two published audits from two estimator families were scored. The population family models the audited set as a mixture of the two classes and reports the non-member weight π, the forgetting rate, with a bootstrap band, the {5%, 95%} quantiles of the estimate over resamples of the audited set. The per-example family scores one sample at a time: it fits a Gaussian to each class's scores from shadow runs, fresh replicates with the datum planted or withheld, and states a per-sample membership probability, deciding member above ½. Both targets are exact here: the fiber splits by first move into the two classes, so the mixing weight is the count leak, and the class-conditional distributions of the audit feature — the scalar a tool reads off each sampled route, here a count of 2-moves over three positions after the first — are exact hypergeometric rationals. Every miss is the estimator's.

The population audit

The flip and the band observation

Fresh member, non-member and audited samples each replicate, 200 replicates per cell — one feature design and one target placement — at audit sizes 50, 200 and 1000, the exponents placing the target at 1/2, 1/5 and 4/5; the two off-centre placements are the world cells. The estimator fits the audited set's variance against the mixture's predicted variance and drops its own model's mean equation. Where the two class variances are equal the prediction is invariant under π ↔ 1 − π, so the objective has twin minima no sample size separates: on equal-variance Gaussian cells the estimate lands nearer 1 − π than π in about half the replicates at every size. In the world cells the class variances differ, in ratio 0.727 and 1.750, and the audit is identified; the flip persists anyway, 28% of replicates at one placement at audit size 200 and 10% at 1000. The bootstrap band covers the exact rational at 69–86% across all three placements and every size, never near the 90% its quantiles suggest, and narrows with data while the coverage stays.

Scope. Observation: one tool's low-order-moment estimator, one seed stream, scalar features, one world family. The mixture model holds exactly at fiber inputs by construction; nothing here scores an audit whose feature model must be taken on faith. Toy scale.

No solution in the box, at a rate a margin sets observation

Write A for the audited set's variance less the member class's, B for the squared gap between the class means and G for the class-variance gap plus B. The audit minimizes (A − πG + π²B)² over the box [0, 1], and only A moves with the audited set. The equation has a solution in the box exactly when the measured A lies in the range A(p) = Gp − Bp² sweeps over the box; when it does not, the tool returns the box-constrained minimizer with no solution behind it, silently. At one placement that happens in 0.525, 0.400 and 0.237 of replicates at the three sizes, and in 0.564, 0.431 and 0.301 of the resamples the band is built from. The rate is a normal tail at the distance from A to the nearer edge of that range over the spread of the measured A, coefficients only, no term for the feature: it reproduces the world cells' rates from population coefficients at the two larger sizes, 0.431 and 0.301 against 0.450 and 0.287. Coarsening the feature acts through the margin alone: quantizing a continuous feature to three levels shrinks the margin 22-fold and reproduces the world cells' plateau, a rate that stays high across the sizes where the continuous dial's falls to 0.002. Two failures answer to the name: complex roots, where the fallback lands on the objective's turning point G/2B, 99–100% of events at every quantized cell, and real roots both outside the box, 16–65% at the world cells, where it lands on a box endpoint, at both world cells the endpoint nearest the truth.

Scope. Observation over 18 of 21 rows, a row being one cell at one audit size: two settings of a continuous Gaussian dial, that feature quantized to 2, 3 and 4 levels, and the two world cells, 200 replicates and 200 resamples per replicate. Largest deviation of the tail formula 0.125 at the smallest size and 0.069 from size 200 up. Controls: the exact hypergeometric moments reproduce the world cells' population solutions with zero difference, and the samplers recover the class variances to 0.0022. Toy scale.

The tangent case property

The three rows left out are the ones with a binary feature. There the objective has a double root at the truth exactly when the audited class is balanced, which a median cut forces; that balance also maximizes the audited variance, so the fluctuation that can leave the range is one-sided, and the measured no-solution rate is 0.010 where a two-sided tail says 0.500. The margin law's scope is the non-tangent case, and inducing a tangency is the only other route coarsening has, which is why the rate is not monotone in the level count: 0.010, 0.246, 0.121 at two, three and four levels.

Scope. Derived; the tangent rows are named and not modelled, no cell dialling through a tangency. Toy scale.

The flip is the level set at the half-box value rule

Whenever a replicate's own turning point G/2B lies outside the box, A(p) is monotone on it, the estimate is a monotone function of the measured A alone carrying both no-solution fallbacks as its endpoint values, and it lands on the far side of 1/2 exactly at A < G/2 − B/4, the direction reversing with the side of the box the turning point sits on and again with the side of 1/2 the truth sits on. No solution and the flip are two cuts of one scalar, at a range edge and at the half-box value, which lies strictly inside the range, so the two overlap only beyond the far edge: 1.8% of replicates at size 200 and 16.5% at size 50. A flip is the in-box solution drifting past 1/2, not the silent fallback, the solution's share among flips 0.929 and 1.000 at the two larger sizes. The rate follows with nothing fitted, a normal tail off the exact fourth moments: 0.387, 0.283, 0.099 against the audit's own 0.385, 0.280, 0.100.

Scope. Derived algebraically and verified with zero exceptions on all 2,174 covered replicates of 2,400 at the two world cells and three sizes; where B is exactly 0, a real event at size 50, there is no turning point and the direction is the sign of G alone. Toy scale.

Inside the box the law is replaced by a case split rule

Where the turning point enters the box, A(p) is concave there and the estimate is read off the candidates the estimator already tries: the turning point itself, whose side of 1/2 is the sign of G − B; an endpoint, 0 or 1; a single in-box solution, which keeps the level set on its own branch; or two in-box solutions, which both zero the objective, the choice between them floating-point residue and candidate order. On the first three the side of 1/2 is fixed by the two class samples alone and the audited sample never enters: 138 of the 226 replicates that drift into this regime at the world cells sit there, so the instrument changes which sample decides rather than degrading. The two-solution case is a coin, the upper solution winning 0.42–0.51 of the time.

Scope. Rule, zero exceptions on the 222 determined of the 226 replicates set aside because their turning point entered the box; the turning point enters the box in 70, 15, 0 and 111, 29, 1 replicates of 400 at the two world cells across the sizes, and the set-aside flips at the scored population's rate, 0.443 against 0.448 and 0.333 against 0.363. Toy scale.

The self-report

Three of the cut's four terms are available from inside property

G and B come from the class samples and A from the audited one; the fourth term is the cut's direction, which turns on the side of 1/2 the truth sits on, precisely what an audit is for. What survives is a statement about the sign of one scalar, D = A − (G/2 − B/4): two replicates land on opposite sides of 1/2 exactly when their signs of D disagree. So an auditor can state the probability that a fresh sample's estimate would sit on the other side of 1/2 from the number in hand, an instability of their own verdict, which equals the flip risk when the verdict is on the truth's side and its complement when it is not.

Scope. Derived from the level set; holds wherever it does. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_flip_risk.py

The spread is solved and the form is not rule

Scored as a forecast of that event, the naive statement Φ(−|D|/sd(A)), sd(A) the spread of the measured A, has its spread right and its form wrong. The per-replicate fourth-moment plug-in matches a resampled sd(A) to 1.5% at the worst of nine rows and under 0.7% at the other eight, against a form error reaching a factor of 5.4. Two corrections are owed: the denominator, since D reads all three samples with the member variance entering it and A with opposite signs, so sd(D) is narrower at one world cell and wider at the other; and the predictive √2, the auditor centring on their own D. The √2 earns the gain, +0.058 against the swap's −0.026 at one cell at size 1000 and +0.031 against +0.008 at the other, the two fighting at one cell.

Scope. Rule at three cells and three sizes, 300 replicate pairs per row, the corrections measured one at a time. Toy scale.

The compression law prices every plug-in form, and the floor is a trade rule

Write μ for |D| over sd(D) at the population. Write D̂ for D computed from the sample in hand. Any statement Φ(−|D̂|/s) has mean E[Φ(−|μ + Z|/s)] with Z standard normal, the folded convolution, within 0.023 of measurement at every row as s sweeps 0.90 to 1.72; dropping the fold gives Φ(−μ/√(s²+1)), which misses by 0.147 at μ = 0.37 and under 0.010 at μ ≥ 2.47. It subsumes the corrected statement's √3 as s = √2 and the naive √2 as s = 1, and holds at the designed cell, one built to put the population turning point inside the box, where no derivation covers a replicate: what a statement says is the law's, whether it is right is the derivation's. Against the normal-model pair rate 2Φ(−μ)Φ(μ) every plug-in form's ratio rises across μ = 0.37 to 2.47, 0.6 to 1.8 at the sharpest inherited scale and 0.7 to 5.1 at the corrected form, because noise in the estimated μ flattens any tail exp(−aμ²/2) to coefficient a/(1+a) < 1. Sharpening, the pair form 2p(1−p) at p = Φ(−2|D̂|/sd(D)), holds a 1.9× band against 7.7× and does not over-warn at any world row, but states 0.205 against a realized 0.457 at the small-μ end and reaches the true exponent only at s = 0, where the statement is 0 everywhere. No member is right at both ends, and the least-wrong one fails toward confidence: all six of its world ratios sit below 1, since 2p(1−p) is pinned at ½ where the truth is ½ and sharpening can only push it down.

Scope. Derived and confirmed at 36 arm-rows, three cells at three sizes, 300 replicate pairs per row, the folded convolution read at four scales on each. The trade's range figures are observations at the six world rows; the designed cell extends nothing there, every ratio at it being read against a model that cell breaks. Toy scale.

The binned diagram slopes on the sign property

Conditional on the replicate in hand the pair rate takes exactly two values, Φ(−μ) when D̂ fell on the majority side of the cut and 1 − Φ(−μ) on the minority side, its magnitude irrelevant; the statement reads |D̂| alone, which carries no sign and is not independent of it. So binning on the statement mixes the two in bin-dependent proportions: at one world cell at size 1000 the minority share runs 0.000 to 0.301 across the bins and the realized rate 0.061 to 0.325. A perfectly calibrated mixture exists and needs μ, hence the truth; a truth-free statement is scored on the per-row mean.

Scope. Derived and checked at the world rows. Toy scale.

The statement ships its own applicability test property

The whole derivation assumes the population turning point outside the box, which is the condition that the two class variances differ by no more than the squared gap between their means. The replicate's own G/2B in [0, 1] needs no truth to compute, reads the boundary conservatively — at equality the turning point sits on an endpoint where the level-set law still holds — and fires on 95–100% of the designed cell's replicates against 0–29% of the world cells', standing down as the audit grows. Where it fires the estimate is a coin: two in-box solutions in 6–24% of replicates.

Scope. Firing rates observed at the three cells and three sizes. Toy scale.

Split-half wins where no derivation holds observation

One instrument reads no derivation. Split-half cuts all three samples, runs the estimator on each half, counts halves landing on opposite sides of 1/2, and pools over re-splits, available to a single auditor as a bootstrap is. Three costs: it answers at half the audit size, tracking the model at μ/√2 within 0.05 at five of six world rows, so the bridge back to full size re-imports the parametric assumption; one split is a single yes-or-no; and complementary halves are anticorrelated by construction, lifting the crossing rate 0.027–0.053 above the half-size model at five of six rows. Bridged, it is better centred than the corrected form, band 0.85 to 2.71 against 0.66 to 5.11, and not than the sharpened one, and its ratio still rises with μ at both cells: the divergence survives the move outside the plug-in family. At the designed cell it wins outright, absolute error 0.058 and 0.000 against 0.137 and 0.090 at the two smaller sizes.

Scope. Observation, three cells at three sizes, 300 replicate pairs and 32 re-splits per row. The designed-cell verdict rests on the two smaller sizes; at the largest the gap of 0.027 sits inside a standard error of 0.015 and an independent measurement reverses it. Halves with B exactly 0 are never filtered and move the pooled rate by 0.001. The divergence past the measured sweep is argued, not proved. Toy scale.

The per-example audit

The exact call and the miscalibrated probability observation

Run against the same world at the same placements with the same feature, at shadow counts 8, 32 and 128 per class, the last its own published setting, 400 replicates per cell. Its two outputs come apart. The membership call, scored as balanced accuracy, matches the exact best achievable accuracy to within 4·10−4 in every cell at shadow count 128. The stated probability converges not to the exact posterior but to the limit of the Gaussian fits: 0.830 stated where the posterior is 11/15, and 0.951 where it is 1 — overconfident at interior cells, underconfident exactly where the truth is certain. The limit is deterministic, so the calibration error settles at a positive floor, and the fraction of audited mass stated more extreme than its posterior rises with shadow data, 0.57 to 0.81 at one placement, more data concentrating every replicate onto the miscalibrated limit. The mechanism needs no world: on a feature carrying exactly two values the fit states 0.985 where the posterior is 0.9, a Gaussian tail pricing the far value at its squared distance where the truth holds linear mass.

Scope. Observation: one tool of the per-example likelihood-ratio family, run as its own algorithm states, scalar features, one world family, one seed stream. A positive control with the Gaussian model well-specified calibrates before anything else is read. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_membership_ruler.py

The two tools' stated uncertainties fail in one direction: the stated certainty outruns the accuracy behind it, a band narrowing with data while its coverage stays low, a probability more extreme than the posterior over most of the audited mass. Their mechanisms are unrelated and the direction belongs to neither. Both state a fit's own uncertainty, which counts only error the fit's model admits, and a fit is the one statement kind whose omitted term carries no sign (the sign law; what changing only the statement kind does to this audit's own ambiguity). The silent no-solution regime is not a direction at all: a verdict with no equation behind it, reported in the format of one that has.

A third published kind states its uncertainty as a guarantee rather than a number. Scored against cells whose posteriors are set by hand, the guarantee holds exactly as advertised while the coverage on one subpopulation alone is zero, the cheapest rule meeting it handing that subpopulation the empty set; and where subpopulations carry unequal weight, the rule built to meet the guarantee cheaply is not always the cheapest, its excess bounded, and sometimes proved zero, by its own coverage overshoot: Prediction sets.
