The Sieve

The Eratosthenes sieve finds primes by elimination: remove multiples of 2, then 3, then 5, then 7… The survivors are the primes. Each step of that process is a projection onto a prime field — and this gives the sieve an algebraic form, powered by the Chinese Remainder Theorem.

Each step is a channel

“Remove multiples of p” is equivalent to “check the residue mod p.” If the residue is 0, the number is a multiple — sieve it out. If nonzero, it survives this step. The CRT decomposes a number into these residues simultaneously, one per prime.

At k = 7 (the demo rung throughout this site): N = 510,510 = 2 × 3 × 5 × 7 × 11 × 13 × 17. Every number maps to a unique 7-tuple of remainders. Every valid 7-tuple maps back to exactly one number. Type one and see:

Independence

The CRT doesn't just decompose — it gives you independence. Knowing a number's remainder mod 2 tells you nothing about its remainder mod 3, or mod 5, or any other channel. Zero mutual information. This is a theorem, not an approximation — and it's why the sieve steps don't interfere.

Watch independence in action

Click +1 repeatedly. Channels highlight when they wrap (roll from max back to 0). Mod 2 wraps every 2 steps, mod 3 every 3 — they wrap at different times. No channel's wrap affects any other.

Adding 1 in the full ring is just adding 1 in each channel independently. Multiplying two numbers is multiplying channel by channel. The ring's arithmetic decomposes into 7 tiny, parallel, non-interfering computations.

Instead of one problem with 510,510 possibilities, you have 7 problems with at most 17 possibilities each. Total positions: 2 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 11 + 13 + 17 = 58. That's an 8,802× reduction.

One prime at a time

The tower grows by adding one prime per rung. Each prime adds one independent channel. Every channel at every level is a prime field — no zero divisors, every nonzero element invertible.

What this gives you

Seven independent channels, by construction, yield three things for free:

Error correction. Three parity channels are algebraically determined by the four data channels. Corrupt any single channel and the parity detects, locates, and corrects it. The code is MDS (distance 4) — optimal. Rate improves as the tower grows: 4/7 → 5/8 → 6/9.

Parallel computation. Any operation on the full ring decomposes into 7 independent operations on tiny channels. Matrix multiplications become block-diagonal. Gradients don't cross channels.

Prime prediction. Sub-rings built from fewer primes have an Euler characteristic that names primes they've never met. The tower predicts the primes it hasn't yet included.
