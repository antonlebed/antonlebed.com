pentanomial.py

Checks: COLLIDE: The row lemma theorem; COLLIDE: The least free size off a line theorem; COLLIDE: Mann's bound known; COLLIDE: The size-5 reduction theorem.

To run it: save pentanomial.py; needs sympy.

"""pentanomial.py -- five terms: a seed with a factor cyclotomic in a
monomial carries it negative, a free seed can be mixed, and a seed free
of every negative torsion zero is a question about one variable.

QUESTION. The torsion split (rooted.py) calls a seed FREE when some
negative factor of its core vanishes at no point of roots of unity,
MIXED when a free seed's core also carries a torsion-rooted factor, and
FREE OUTRIGHT when no factor of its core is torsion-rooted. Sizes 3 and
4 are never free outright (rooted.py, quadrinomial.py), so size 5 is the
first place it can occur. Off a line, can a size-5 seed be free at all,
what shape must it have, and is its freedom outright a new question or
the line's?

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine). P is a 0/1 core with five
terms. By the half-size law (halfsize.py) a factorization needs Newton
dimension at most 2, so P lies on a line or in a plane.
  (1) FIVE IS PRIME. P(1, 1) = 5 is the product of its factors' values,
      so one factor has value +-5 and every other +-1. A non-monomial
      factor with no negative coefficient has value at least 2, so at
      most ONE factor is nonnegative, and a reducible core is a seed.
  (2) THE ROW LEMMA. Let P, off a line, have a factor Phi_n(w) with w a
      primitive monomial. In coordinates with w = x (a unimodular map)
      Phi_n(x) divides P exactly when it divides every row P_j(x), the
      terms of P with y-exponent j. A one-term row never vanishes at a
      root of unity and P has at least two rows, so the rows have sizes
      2 and 3. A binomial row x^a (1 + x^s) vanishing at a primitive n-th
      root zeta makes zeta^s = -1, so n is even. A trinomial row makes
      three unit numbers sum to 0, which forces an equilateral triangle
      (|1 + z| = 1 with |z| = 1 puts z at a primitive cube root), so the
      two ratios are the primitive cube roots and 3 | n. So 6 | n, n is
      no prime power, Phi_n(1) = 1, and Phi_n, with constant and leading
      coefficient 1, has a negative coefficient. EVERY FACTOR OF A
      NON-COLLINEAR FIVE-TERM CORE THAT IS CYCLOTOMIC IN A MONOMIAL IS
      NEGATIVE AND TORSION-ROOTED, of order divisible by 6, and the
      rows in its direction have sizes 2 and 3.
  (3) SIZE 5 CAN BE MIXED. P = 1 + x^3 + y (1 + x^4 + x^8), rows 1 + x^3
      = Phi_2 Phi_6 and 1 + x^4 + x^8 = Phi_3 Phi_6 Phi_12, is Phi_6(x)
      times R = (1 + x) + y Phi_3(x) Phi_12(x), which is linear in y with
      coprime coefficients, so irreducible, and negative. At a torus
      point |y| = 1 needs |1 + x| = |Phi_3(x) Phi_12(x)| with x = e^(i theta);
      with t = 2 + 2 cos theta, |1 + x|^2 = t, |Phi_3|^2 = (t - 1)^2 and
      |Phi_12|^2 = (t^2 - 4t + 1)^2, so t is a root of S = (t - 1)^2 (t^2
      - 4t + 1)^2 - t. At a root of unity x, t is a totally real
      algebraic integer, and an irreducible S with a non-real root has
      no totally real root. So R has no torsion zero and the menu {2,
      16, 6, 96, 1536}, core P, is a free seed, mixed beside Phi_6.
  (4) MANN'S BOUND. In a minimal vanishing sum of t roots of unity
      (no proper subsum vanishes) every ratio is a root of unity of
      squarefree order with every prime factor at most t (H. B. Mann,
      Mathematika 12 (1965) 107-117). The argument, for the record: put
      the roots in mu_N with one of them 1 and p | N. If p^2 | N, the
      powers 1..rho^(p-1) of a primitive N-th root rho are a basis of
      Q(rho) over Q(rho^p), so each coset of mu_(N/p) sums to 0 and
      minimality leaves one coset: N drops to N/p. If p || N, the only
      relation among a primitive p-th root's powers over Q(zeta_(N/p))
      is their sum, so the p coset sums are equal; zero forces one coset
      as before, and nonzero needs all p cosets filled, p <= t. At t = 5
      every ratio in a minimal part is a 30th root of unity, and a
      vanishing five-term sum is minimal or a pair beside a triple.
  (5) THE DETERMINANT BOUND. At a zero (zeta, zeta^K) of P, zeta a root
      of unity, write A_u = u . (1, K) for a difference u of two
      exponent vectors of P. Within a minimal part zeta^(30 A_u) = 1
      for each of its differences. If two of the constrained differences
      u, u' are not parallel, zeta^(30 g) = 1 with g = gcd(A_u, A_u'),
      and g divides u'_2 A_u - u_2 A_u' = det(u, u'): so zeta is one of
      at most 30 |det(u, u')| roots, however large K is. Otherwise every
      constrained difference lies along one primitive w: the pair and
      the triple each sit on a line parallel to w, the rows of (2) in
      that direction, and omega = zeta^(w . (1, K)) is a common root of
      the binomial row B(W) and the trinomial row T(W). Then gcd(B, T)
      is a non-constant product of cyclotomic polynomials and gcd(B,
      T)(w) divides P. So IF P HAS NO FACTOR CYCLOTOMIC IN A MONOMIAL,
      THE ROOTS OF UNITY zeta WITH P(zeta, zeta^K) = 0 NUMBER AT MOST
      30 SUM |det(u, u')| OVER NON-PARALLEL PAIRS, FOR EVERY K.
  (6) THE SIZE-5 REDUCTION. Let P be a size-5 seed off a line none of
      whose NEGATIVE factors has a torsion zero. By (2) P has no factor
      cyclotomic in a monomial. Choose coordinates in which no factor's
      support lies on a horizontal line; for K past the x-width of P, y
      -> x^K is injective on P's support and on each factor's, so P_K =
      P(x, x^K) is a 0/1 pentanomial and every factor stays
      non-monomial. A negative factor's image has no cyclotomic factor,
      at any K. With two negative factors the non-cyclotomic part of
      P_K is reducible. Otherwise P = G H with G nonnegative by (1). G_K
      has G's t terms and degree span at least K minus a constant, and
      a nonzero root of a t-term polynomial has multiplicity at most t -
      1 (quadrinomial.py, Hajos's bound), so if G_K had only roots of
      unity for roots it would have at least (K - c)/(t - 1) distinct
      ones, each a zero of P at (zeta, zeta^K), against (5). So for all
      large K, G_K has a non-cyclotomic factor beside H_K, and P_K's
      non-cyclotomic part is reducible. A FREE-OUTRIGHT size-5 seed
      needs none of this: it has no torsion zero at all, so P_K has no
      cyclotomic factor and is reducible. Conversely a reducible 0/1
      pentanomial with no cyclotomic factor is itself a free-outright
      seed on a line, by (1). SO A FREE-OUTRIGHT SEED OF SIZE 5 EXISTS
      IN SOME NUMBER OF VARIABLES EXACTLY WHEN SOME 0/1 PENTANOMIAL IN
      ONE VARIABLE IS REDUCIBLE WITH NO CYCLOTOMIC FACTOR, and a seed
      off a line free of every negative torsion zero exists only when
      some 0/1 pentanomial's non-cyclotomic part is reducible, a NO to
      the question Filaseta and Solan left open (Math. Scand. 84 (1999)
      5-10, read at source); on a line 1 + x + x^3 + x^4 + x^7 = Phi_5
      (1 - x^2 + x^3) is such a seed with no NO in it. Their Theorem 1, that such a pentanomial with its
      irreducible RECIPROCAL factors removed is 1 or irreducible, puts a
      non-cyclotomic reciprocal factor in any such example.

DESIGN. Polynomials are sympy's; a factor is cyclotomic by sympy's own
test.
  CONTROL: P of (3) factors as Phi_6(x) times R; 1 + x + x^2 + y is
     irreducible; 1 + x + x^3 + x^4 + x^7 = Phi_5(x) (1 - x^2 + x^3), a
     mixed seed on the line. The rotated exhibit (x -> x y) has a factor
     cyclotomic in x y, and its zeros (zeta, zeta^K) number 2(K + 1),
     growing with K: the positive control for (5)'s count.
  MANN: every multiset of five N-th roots of unity with one of them 1,
     N = 60 and 84, summing to 0: the minimal ones have every ratio a
     30th root of unity; every pair part is antipodal and every triple
     part equilateral.
  EXHIBIT: S irreducible over Z with a non-real root; R irreducible and
     negative; no zero of R at a pair of N-th roots, N <= 60.
  BOX: every 5-subset of [0, 8]^2 with least coordinates 0, not
     collinear, through the Gao-Lauder polygon filter (a lattice
     polygon is a Minkowski sum of two polygons, neither a point
     (S. Gao and A. G. B. Lauder, Decomposition of polytopes and
     polynomials, Discrete Comput. Geom. 26 (2001) 89-104),
     exactly when some 0 <= l'_i <= l_i, neither all 0 nor all l_i,
     has sum l'_i v_i = 0 over its primitive edge directions v_i of
     lattice length l_i), one per orbit of the square's eight
     symmetries, factored. Per seed: a factor cyclotomic in a
     primitive monomial, the rows in its direction, its order and
     sign; the other factors' torsion zeros decided (below);
     the split rooted / mixed / free outright.
     THE TORSION TEST for a factor R = A(x) + v B(x), v a monomial
     with positive y-exponent in coordinates where the cyclotomic
     factor is Phi_n(x): R has a torsion zero exactly when some root
     of unity omega has B(omega) != 0 and -A(omega)/B(omega) a root of
     unity, since v then takes that value at a torsion point. Such an
     omega is a root of A(x) A(1/x) - B(x) B(1/x), and in Q(omega) of
     order m the ratio is a root of unity only if its 2m-th power is 1:
     the candidates exact, the power read numerically at each, within
     10^-7. Where A(x) A(1/x) - B(x) B(1/x) vanishes identically every
     omega is a candidate and the bracket is left unresolved.
  BOUND: for every orbit representative that is irreducible, take
     every 997th, the zeros (zeta, zeta^K) with zeta of order at most
     400, at K = 20, 40, 60, against 30 sum |det(u, u')|.
  LINE: every 1 + x^a + x^b + x^c + x^n, n <= 30: the non-cyclotomic
     part 1 or irreducible; the non-reciprocal part 1 or irreducible
     (Filaseta-Solan Theorem 1); seeds rooted / mixed / free outright.
  SPECIALIZE: every seed of the box at K = 9..12, each a 0/1
     pentanomial, its non-cyclotomic part 1 or irreducible.

PREDICTIONS (fixed before the engine).

  P2 MANN: every minimal part's ratios in mu_30; pairs antipodal,

read. A minimal five-term sum with a ratio outside mu_30 contradicts (4)

  P1 The exhibit factors as x^2 - x + 1 times x^6 y + x^5 y - x^3 y + x

  P4 5,263,020 cores, 5,262,736 non-collinear, 1,228,232 through the

Tiers: the row lemma and the size-5 reduction are theorems, proved

def poly(points):
    return Poly.from_dict({p: 1 for p in points}, X, Y)


def factors(p):
    """Non-monomial Z-irreducible factors with multiplicity, leading
    coefficient made positive."""
    _, fl = p.factor_list()
    out = []
    for q, e in fl:
        if len(q.terms()) < 2:
            continue
        if q.LC() < 0:
            q = -q
        out += [q] * e
    return out


def negative(q):
    return any(c < 0 for c in q.coeffs())


def cross(u, v):
    return u[0] * v[1] - u[1] * v[0]


def collinear(pts):
    p0 = pts[0]
    u = (pts[1][0] - p0[0], pts[1][1] - p0[1])
    return all(cross(u, (p[0] - p0[0], p[1] - p0[1])) == 0 for p in pts[2:])


def hull(pts):
    """Vertices of the convex hull, counterclockwise, no collinear ones."""
    ps = sorted(set(pts))
    lo, hi = [], []
    for p in ps:
        while len(lo) >= 2 and cross((lo[-1][0] - lo[-2][0],
                                      lo[-1][1] - lo[-2][1]),
                                     (p[0] - lo[-2][0], p[1] - lo[-2][1])) <= 0:
            lo.pop()
        lo.append(p)
    for p in reversed(ps):
        while len(hi) >= 2 and cross((hi[-1][0] - hi[-2][0],
                                      hi[-1][1] - hi[-2][1]),
                                     (p[0] - hi[-2][0], p[1] - hi[-2][1])) <= 0:
            hi.pop()
        hi.append(p)
    return lo[:-1] + hi[:-1]


def decomposable(pts):
    """Gao-Lauder: the hull is a sum of two lattice polygons, neither a
    point."""
    h = hull(pts)
    edges = []
    for i in range(len(h)):
        a, b = h[i], h[(i + 1) % len(h)]
        dx, dy = b[0] - a[0], b[1] - a[1]
        g = gcd(abs(dx), abs(dy))
        edges.append((dx // g, dy // g, g))
    states = {(0, 0, False, False)}
    for vx, vy, g in edges:
        states = {(sx + j * vx, sy + j * vy, u or j > 0, left or j < g)
                  for sx, sy, u, left in states for j in range(g + 1)}
    return (0, 0, True, True) in states


SYMS = [lambda a, b: (a, b), lambda a, b: (b, a), lambda a, b: (-a, b),
        lambda a, b: (a, -b), lambda a, b: (-a, -b), lambda a, b: (-b, a),
        lambda a, b: (b, -a), lambda a, b: (-b, -a)]


def canonical(pts):
    best = None
    for s in SYMS:
        q = [s(a, b) for a, b in pts]
        mx, my = min(a for a, _ in q), min(b for _, b in q)
        key = tuple(sorted((a - mx, b - my) for a, b in q))
        if best is None or key < best:
            best = key
    return best


def adapt(w):
    """A unimodular matrix sending the primitive vector w to (1, 0)."""
    p, q = w
    # extended gcd: a p + b q = 1
    a0, a1, b0, b1, r0, r1 = 1, 0, 0, 1, p, q
    while r1:
        k = r0 // r1
        a0, a1, b0, b1, r0, r1 = a1, a0 - k * a1, b1, b0 - k * b1, r1, r0 - k * r1
    if r0 < 0:
        a0, b0 = -a0, -b0
    return ((a0, b0), (-q, p))


def apply(m, pts):
    return [(m[0][0] * a + m[0][1] * b, m[1][0] * a + m[1][1] * b)
            for a, b in pts]


def monomial_cyclotomic(q):
    """(w, n) when q is Phi_n(w) for a primitive monomial w, else None."""
    pts = [e for e, _ in q.terms()]
    if len(pts) < 2 or not collinear(pts):
        return None
    p0 = min(pts)
    u = [(a - p0[0], b - p0[1]) for a, b in pts]
    a, b = next(d for d in u if d != (0, 0))
    g = gcd(abs(a), abs(b))
    w = (a // g, b // g)
    if w[0] < 0 or (w[0] == 0 and w[1] < 0):
        w = (-w[0], -w[1])
    coeff = {}
    for (e, c) in q.terms():
        d = (e[0] - p0[0], e[1] - p0[1])
        k = d[0] // w[0] if w[0] else d[1] // w[1]
        coeff[(k,)] = int(c)
    lo = min(k for (k,) in coeff)
    uni = Poly.from_dict({(k - lo,): c for (k,), c in coeff.items()}, X)
    if uni.LC() < 0:
        uni = -uni
    if not uni.is_cyclotomic:
        return None
    for n in range(1, 400):
        if Poly(cyclotomic_poly(n, X), X) == uni:
            return w, n
    return None


def rows_along(pts, w):
    m = adapt(w)
    q = apply(m, pts)
    rows = {}
    for a, b in q:
        rows.setdefault(b, []).append(a)
    return m, rows


def torsion_free_bracket(pts, w):
    """Test on R = F / (its part cyclotomic in w): with F's two rows
    along w, A = row0 / G and B = row1 / G, G their gcd. Returns (free,
    reason)."""
    m, rows = rows_along(pts, w)
    if len(rows) != 2:
        return None, "rows"
    (_, r0), (_, r1) = sorted(rows.items())
    def row(r):
        lo = min(r)
        return Poly.from_dict({(a - lo,): 1 for a in r}, X)
    a, b = row(r0), row(r1)
    g = a.gcd(b)
    a, b = a.quo(g), b.quo(g)
    da, db = a.degree(), b.degree()
    arev = Poly.from_dict({(da - k,): c for (k,), c in a.terms()}, X)
    brev = Poly.from_dict({(db - k,): c for (k,), c in b.terms()}, X)
    d = (a * arev * Poly(X ** db, X) - b * brev * Poly(X ** da, X))
    if d.is_zero:
        return None, "identical moduli"
    for f, _ in d.factor_list()[1]:
        if f.degree() < 1 or not f.is_cyclotomic:
            continue
        n = next(k for k in range(1, 2000)
                 if Poly(cyclotomic_poly(k, X), X) == (f if f.LC() > 0 else -f))
        for k in range(n):
            if gcd(k, n) != 1:
                continue
            om = cmath.exp(2j * cmath.pi * k / n)
            bv = b.eval(om)
            if abs(bv) < 1e-9:
                continue
            r = -a.eval(om) / bv
            if abs(r ** (2 * n) - 1) < 1e-7:
                return False, f"zero at order {n}"
    return True, "no omega"


def torsion_zero(q, top):
    terms = q.terms()
    for n in range(1, top + 1):
        roots = [cmath.exp(2j * cmath.pi * k / n) for k in range(n)]
        for i in range(n):
            for j in range(n):
                v = sum(int(c) * roots[(i * e[0] + j * e[1]) % n]
                        for e, c in terms)
                if abs(v) < 1e-9:
                    return n
    return None


def section_control():

    line = Poly(1 + X + X ** 3 + X ** 4 + X ** 7, X)
    lf = factors(line)
    ok_line = (sorted(q.degree() for q in lf) == [3, 4]
               and sum(q.is_cyclotomic for q in lf) == 1
               and any(negative(q) and not q.is_cyclotomic for q in lf))
    print(f"    exhibit: {[q.as_expr() for q in fs]}")
    print(f"    1 + x + x^2 + y irreducible: {irr}; 1 + x + x^3 + x^4 + x^7:"
          f" {[q.as_expr() for q in lf]}")
    rot = [(a, a + b) for a, b in [(0, 0), (3, 0), (0, 1), (4, 1), (8, 1)]]
    counts = [zeros_on(rot, K, 400) for K in (20, 40, 60)]
    print(f"    rotated exhibit, zeros (zeta, zeta^K) at K = 20, 40, 60: "
          f"{counts}")
    control("P1 controls", ok_ex and irr and ok_line
          and counts == [2 * (K + 1) for K in (20, 40, 60)])


def section_mann():
    section("MANN -- vanishing sums of five roots of unity")
    ok = True
    for n in (60, 84):
        roots = [cmath.exp(2j * cmath.pi * k / n) for k in range(n)]
        vanish = minimal = bad = split = badsplit = 0
        for rest in combinations_with_replacement(range(n), 4):
            ks = (0,) + rest
            if abs(sum(roots[k] for k in ks)) > 1e-9:
                continue
            vanish += 1
            parts = [c for r in (2, 3) for c in combinations(range(5), r)
                     if abs(sum(roots[ks[i]] for i in c)) < 1e-9]
            if not parts:
                minimal += 1
                if any((ks[i] - ks[j]) * 30 % n for i in range(5)
                       for j in range(5)):
                    bad += 1
                continue
            split += 1
            for c in parts:
                if len(c) == 2:
                    badsplit += (ks[c[0]] - ks[c[1]]) % n != n // 2
                else:
                    d = sorted((ks[i] - ks[c[0]]) % n for i in c)
                    badsplit += d != [0, n // 3, 2 * n // 3]
        print(f"    N = {n}: {vanish} vanishing, {minimal} minimal ({bad} "
              f"with a ratio outside mu_30), {split} split ({badsplit} "
              f"parts neither antipodal nor equilateral)")
        ok &= (vanish > 0 and (minimal > 0) == (n % 5 == 0)
               and bad == 0 and badsplit == 0)
    check("P2 Mann's bound at five terms", ok)


def section_exhibit():
    section("EXHIBIT -- the mixed seed {2, 16, 6, 96, 1536}")
    s = Poly((T - 1) ** 2 * (T ** 2 - 4 * T + 1) ** 2 - T, T)
    sf = s.factor_list()[1]
    nreal = len(real_roots(s))
    r = Poly((1 + X) + Y * (1 + X + X ** 2) * (X ** 4 - X ** 2 + 1), X, Y)
    rirr = len(factors(r)) == 1
    z = torsion_zero(r, 60)
    print(f"    S = {s.as_expr()}; factors {len(sf)}, real roots {nreal} of "
          f"{s.degree()}; R irreducible {rirr}, negative {negative(r)}, "
          f"torsion zero at N <= 60: {z}")
    check("P3 the exhibit is free", len(sf) == 1 and sf[0][1] == 1
          and nreal < s.degree() and rirr and negative(r) and z is None)


def enumerate_box(box):
    grid = [(a, b) for a in range(box + 1) for b in range(box + 1)]
    col0 = [i for i, p in enumerate(grid) if p[0] == 0]
    cores = noncol = passed = 0
    reps = set()
    for i0 in col0:
        p0 = grid[i0]
        for rest in combinations(range(i0 + 1, len(grid)), 4):
            pts = [p0] + [grid[i] for i in rest]
            if min(b for _, b in pts):
                continue
            cores += 1
            if collinear(pts):
                continue
            noncol += 1
            if not decomposable(pts):
                continue
            passed += 1
            reps.add(canonical(pts))
    return cores, noncol, passed, sorted(reps)


BOX = {}


def section_box():
    box = 5 if REHEARSE else 8
    section(f"BOX -- every non-collinear five-term core in [0, {box}]^2")
    t0 = time.time()
    cores, noncol, passed, reps = enumerate_box(box)
    t1 = time.time()
    print(f"    {cores} cores, {noncol} non-collinear, {passed} through the"
          f" filter, {len(reps)} orbit representatives [{t1 - t0:.1f} s]")
    seeds, irred = [], []
    nonseed = 0
    for pts in reps:
        fs = factors(poly(pts))
        if len(fs) < 2:
            irred.append(pts)
            continue
        if not any(negative(q) for q in fs):
            nonseed += 1
        seeds.append((pts, fs))
    print(f"    {len(seeds)} reducible, {nonseed} with no negative factor "
          f"[{time.time() - t1:.1f} s]")
    split = {"rooted": 0, "mixed": 0, "outright": 0}
    noshape = badfactor = unresolved = 0
    reasons = {}
    for pts, fs in seeds:
        cyc = [(q, monomial_cyclotomic(q)) for q in fs]
        cyc = [(q, wn) for q, wn in cyc if wn]
        if not cyc:
            noshape += 1
            print(f"    NO FACTOR CYCLOTOMIC IN A MONOMIAL: {pts} "
                  f"{[q.as_expr() for q in fs]}")
            continue
        for q, (w, n) in cyc:
            _, rows = rows_along(list(pts), w)
            sizes = sorted(len(r) for r in rows.values())
            if sizes != [2, 3] or n % 6 or not negative(q):
                badfactor += 1
                print(f"    ROW LEMMA FAILS: {pts} {q.as_expr()} n={n} "
                      f"rows {sizes}")
        others = [q for q in fs if not monomial_cyclotomic(q)]
        negs = [q for q in others if negative(q)]
        if not negs:
            split["rooted"] += 1
            continue
        free, why = torsion_free_bracket(list(pts), cyc[0][1][0])
        why = why if free is None else ("free" if free else "zero found")
        reasons[why] = reasons.get(why, 0) + 1
        if free is None or len(others) > 1:
            unresolved += 1
            print(f"    UNRESOLVED: {pts} {[q.as_expr() for q in fs]}")
            continue
        split["mixed" if free else "rooted"] += 1
    BOX.update(box=box, seeds=seeds, irred=irred)
    print(f"    seeds: rooted {split['rooted']}, mixed {split['mixed']}, "
          f"free outright {split['outright']}; without a factor cyclotomic"
          f" in a monomial {noshape}; row lemma failures {badfactor}; "
          f"unresolved {unresolved}; torsion test outcomes {reasons}")
    check("P4 every reducible core is a seed", seeds and nonseed == 0)
    check("P4 every seed has a factor cyclotomic in a monomial, so none is "
          "free outright", noshape == 0)
    check("P4 the row lemma: rows 2 and 3, order divisible by 6, negative",
          badfactor == 0)
    check("P4 every negative bracket decided", unresolved == 0)
    if box == 8:
        check("P4 the split (transplant: 130 / 33 mixed / 97 rooted)",
              (len(seeds), split["mixed"], split["rooted"])
              == (130, 33, 97))


FRACS = {}


def zeros_on(pts, K, top):
    """Roots of unity zeta of order <= top with sum zeta^(a + K b) = 0."""
    if top not in FRACS:
        FRACS[top] = [(k, n) for n in range(1, top + 1) for k in range(n)
                      if gcd(k, n) == 1]
    count = 0
    exps = [a + K * b for a, b in pts]
    for k, n in FRACS[top]:
        v = sum(cmath.exp(2j * cmath.pi * ((k * e) % n) / n) for e in exps)
        if abs(v) < 1e-9:
            count += 1
    return count


def det_bound(pts):
    diffs = [(p[0] - q[0], p[1] - q[1]) for p, q in combinations(pts, 2)]
    return 30 * sum(abs(cross(u, v)) for u, v in combinations(diffs, 2)
                    if cross(u, v))


def section_bound():
    section("BOUND -- zeros (zeta, zeta^K) of irreducible pentanomials")
    sample = BOX["irred"][::997]
    over = rising = 0
    worst = [0, 0, 0]
    for pts in sample:
        c = [zeros_on(list(pts), K, 400) for K in (20, 40, 60)]
        b = det_bound(pts)
        over += any(x > b for x in c)
        rising += c[2] > c[0]
        worst = [max(w, x) for w, x in zip(worst, c)]
    print(f"    {len(sample)} representatives; most zeros at K = 20, 40, 60: "
          f"{worst}; above the bound {over}; rising {rising}")
    least = min(det_bound(pts) for pts in sample)
    print(f"    the least bound over the sample: {least}")
    check("P5 no count above its bound (the frozen kill of (5))",
          sample and over == 0)


def section_line():
    top = 16 if REHEARSE else 30
    section(f"LINE -- 1 + x^a + x^b + x^c + x^n, n <= {top}")
    total = red = badcyc = badrec = 0
    split = {"rooted": 0, "mixed": 0, "outright": 0}
    for n in range(4, top + 1):
        for a, b, c in combinations(range(1, n), 3):
            total += 1
            fs = factors(Poly(1 + X ** a + X ** b + X ** c + X ** n, X))
            if len(fs) < 2:
                continue
            red += 1
            noncyc = [q for q in fs if not q.is_cyclotomic]
            if len(noncyc) > 1:
                badcyc += 1
                print(f"    FILASETA-SOLAN WITNESS: {(a, b, c, n)} "
                      f"{[q.as_expr() for q in fs]}")
            nonrec = [q for q in fs if Poly(list(reversed(q.all_coeffs())),
                                            X) not in (q, -q)]
            badrec += len(nonrec) > 1
            negs = [q for q in fs if negative(q)]
            if all(q.is_cyclotomic for q in negs):
                split["rooted"] += 1
            elif any(q.is_cyclotomic for q in fs):
                split["mixed"] += 1
            else:
                split["outright"] += 1
    print(f"    {total} pentanomials, {red} reducible; non-cyclotomic part "
          f"reducible {badcyc}; non-reciprocal part reducible {badrec}; "
          f"seeds rooted {split['rooted']}, mixed {split['mixed']}, free "
          f"outright {split['outright']}")
    check("P6 the non-cyclotomic part is 1 or irreducible", red and badcyc == 0)
    check("P6 Filaseta-Solan Theorem 1 holds", badrec == 0)
    check("P6 free outright 0", split["outright"] == 0)


def section_specialize():
    box = BOX["box"]
    section(f"SPECIALIZE -- every seed of the box at K = {box + 1}..{box + 4}")
    pairs = shape = bad = one = 0
    top = 0
    for pts, _ in BOX["seeds"]:
        for K in range(box + 1, box + 5):
            pairs += 1
            terms = {}
            for a, b in pts:
                terms[(a + K * b,)] = terms.get((a + K * b,), 0) + 1
            p = Poly.from_dict(terms, X)
            top = max(top, p.degree())
            shape += len(terms) == 5 and set(terms.values()) == {1}
            noncyc = [q for q in factors(p) if not q.is_cyclotomic]
            one += not noncyc
            if len(noncyc) > 1:
                bad += 1
                print(f"    FILASETA-SOLAN WITNESS: {pts} K={K}")
    print(f"    {pairs} (seed, K) pairs, {shape} five-term 0/1 images, "
          f"degree at most {top}; non-cyclotomic part 1 at {one}, "
          f"reducible at {bad}")
    check("P7 every non-cyclotomic part 1 or irreducible", bad == 0)
