"""clock.py -- what one place charges a growth walk: the tick ladder of a
place of a Dedekind ring with finite residue field, read as the orbit of
one map, with one overshoot and one number beside it.

QUESTION. A growth walk raising lambda pays, at a place P, the norm of
the least climb in depth that raises lambda's p-part: N(P)^r at door r
(module_law.py). The depths where that p-part jumps are the place's TICK
LADDER. module_law.py proves the ladder's tail: past a threshold it
steps by e in mixed characteristic, and it is the powers of p in equal
characteristic. What is the WHOLE ladder, head included? Is it the orbit
of the level map psi(i) = min(p i, i + e) from level 1, so that one map
prices every tick a place ever charges, and if not, what else does a
place hand the walk?

THE OBJECTS. K is the completion of the ring at P, a complete discrete
valuation field with residue field F_q, q = p^f, ramification index e
over p (e is infinite in equal characteristic, where p = 0 in K), pi a
uniformizer, U_i = 1 + pi^i O_K. E(a) is the exponent of U_1/U_a, a
power of p, and lambda(P^a) = (q - 1) E(a) (module_law.py). The LADDER
is the set of depths a >= 1 with E(a + 1) > E(a). The BEND is
s = e/(p - 1). Write p = u pi^e with u a unit and u_bar its residue.
psi(i) = min(p i, i + e) (p i when e is infinite); for i < s it is p i,
for i > s it is i + e.

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine).
  (1) THE LEVEL STEP. Let z be in U_i but not U_(i+1), z = 1 + x,
      v(x) = i. In (1 + x)^p - 1 = p x + sum_(1<j<p) C(p, j) x^j + x^p
      the middle terms have valuation at least e + 2i > e + i, the
      term p x has valuation e + i and x^p has p i. So v(z^p - 1) =
      psi(i) WHENEVER i != s, and v(z^p - 1) >= p s = s + e when
      i = s (only possible when (p - 1) | e). At i = s write x = c pi^s
      + (deeper); the coefficient of pi^(ps) in z^p - 1 is
      c^p + u_bar c in F_q. The map c -> c^p + u_bar c is additive on
      F_q, with kernel of size 1 or p (the roots of c^(p-1) = -u_bar,
      with 0). A class at the bend overshoots iff its c is in the
      kernel.
  (2) A LEVEL-1 UNIT ATTAINS THE EXPONENT. U_1/U_a is an abelian
      p-group, so its exponent is the largest order of an element. If y
      has the largest order p^k and v(y - 1) > 1, take any z with
      v(z - 1) = 1: if z's order is below p^k then z y has order p^k and
      v(z y - 1) = 1. So E(a) = max over units z with v(z - 1) = 1 of
      the order of z in U_1/U_a, which is p^(k_z(a)) with
      k_z(a) = least k having t_k(z) >= a, t_k(z) = v(z^(p^k) - 1).
  (3) THE TRAJECTORY OF A LEVEL-1 UNIT. t_0 = 1 and, by (1), t_(k+1) =
      psi(t_k) at every k with t_k != s. The orbit of 1 below the bend
      is 1, p, p^2, ..., so the trajectory meets the bend iff s = p^n
      for some n >= 0, i.e. e = (p - 1) p^n. Otherwise every level-1
      unit has the one trajectory psi^k(1), k(a) is the least k with
      psi^k(1) >= a, and E(a + 1) > E(a) iff a is on the orbit: THE
      LADDER IS THE ORBIT OF 1 UNDER psi. When s = p^n every level-1
      unit reaches the bend at step n, with bend coefficient c^(p^n),
      c its level-1 coefficient; as c runs over F_q^x so does c^(p^n).
      Put A = the least t_(n+1)(z) over level-1 units z, the ARRIVAL,
      and w = A - s - e >= 0, the WIDTH. Past the bend every step is
      + e exactly, so the slowest level-1 unit is one attaining A
      (trajectories past the bend never cross: they add e each step),
      and THE LADDER IS 1, p, ..., p^n, A, A + e, A + 2e, ... .
  (4) THE HEAD CRITERION. w >= 1 iff EVERY bend coefficient c^(p^n),
      c != 0, lies in the kernel of (1), i.e. the kernel is all of F_q,
      which needs q - 1 <= p - 1, so f = 1, and then c^p = c on F_p
      makes the map c (1 + u_bar), zero iff u_bar = -1. So:
          w >= 1  iff  f = 1, e = (p - 1) p^n, and u_bar = -1.
      Given (p - 1) | e, the third clause is the statement that -u_bar
      is a (p - 1)-th power in F_p, which is mu_p inside K (K contains
      mu_p iff it contains a (p - 1)-th root of -p, since
      Q_p(zeta_p) = Q_p((-p)^(1/(p - 1))), and a unit is a (p - 1)-th
      power iff its residue is, p - 1 being prime to p). Its e are the
      ramification indices of Q_p(zeta_(p^(n+1))).
  (5) THE TWO NUMBERS. The gap at a landing depth a (one past a ladder
      member) is next(a) + 1 - a with next(a) the least member >= a.
      Along the ladder the gaps are p^j (p - 1) below the bend, each
      less than e; at most e at the step crossing it; e + w at the one
      splice p^n -> A; e forever after.
      So the sup of the gaps a walk can land on is e + w, the tail
      gap is e, and nothing else of the ladder varies: a place hands
      the walk (p, e, f) and the one number w. In equal characteristic
      e is infinite and psi(i) = p i: the orbit is the powers of p,
      module_law.py's log clock, and there is no bend.
  Tier, if the engine agrees: (1)-(3) and (5) are a theorem, (4) a
  criterion; w's VALUES are observations per place.

DESIGN. Standard library only; one process. The local rings are
module_law.py's (O_K = Z_p[x]/(g), g Eisenstein or irreducible mod p;
E by the generators 1 + (a residue lift) pi^i, controlled there
against the whole group). For an Eisenstein g = x^n + ... + c_0, pi^n = -c_0 (1 + ...),
so u_bar = -(c_0/p)^(-1) mod p and u_bar = -1 iff c_0/p = 1 mod p; at
Z_p (unramified, n = 1) u_bar = 1.
  C  POSITIVE CONTROL, before any verdict: (a) at every place and every
     a with q^(a-1) <= 20,000, E(a) by generators against the whole of
     U_1/U_a; (b) at the same (place, a), (2)'s claim: the largest
     order among units with v(z - 1) = 1 equals E(a).
  L  THE LADDER at each place to depth D = ceil(s) + 5e + 8 (D >= 12):
     the measured ladder against (3)'s prediction. Where s is not a
     p-power, the ladder must equal psi's orbit of 1 below D. Where
     s = p^n, the ladder must begin 1, p, ..., p^n, its next member A
     is READ, and every later member below D must be A + je.
  H  THE HEAD: w = A - s - e >= 1 exactly at the places the criterion
     of (4) names, and w = 0 at every other place with s = p^n.
  W  THE WIDTH VALUES, printed per place.
  Q  EQUAL CHARACTERISTIC: F_2, F_3, F_4, F_9 [[t]] ladders to depth
     40 are the powers of p (the orbit of psi with e infinite).
  The places, g low degree first (32 of them):
     p = 2: Z_2; unramified of degree 2 and 3; x^2 + 2, x^2 - 2,
       x^2 - 2x + 2 (Q_2(i), i = sqrt -1 in a field's name),
       x^2 - 2x + 6 (Q_2(sqrt -5)),
       x^2 + 2x - 2 (Q_2(sqrt 3), the field of sqrt -5 again, since
       -15 is a square in Q_2), x^2 - 10, x^2 + 10, x^3 + 2, x^4 - 2,
       x^4 + 2, x^6 - 2, x^8 - 2; with x^2 + 2, x^2 - 2 and Q_2(i)
       these are all six ramified quadratic fields over Q_2;
     p = 3: Z_3; unramified of degree 2; x^2 + 3, x^2 - 3, x^2 - 6,
       x^3 + 3, x^4 - 3, x^6 - 3, x^6 + 3;
     p = 5: Z_5; x^2 + 5, x^4 + 5, x^4 - 5, x^4 + 10;
     p = 7: x^2 + 7, x^6 + 7, x^6 - 7.

PREDICTIONS, fixed before the run.
  C  (a) and (b) hold at every tested (place, a).
  L  every ladder is (3)'s shape to depth D at all 32 places.
  H  heads (w >= 1) at exactly: Z_2, the ten Eisenstein places over
     2 with e in {2, 4, 8}, x^2 + 3, x^2 - 6, x^6 + 3, x^4 + 5, x^6 + 7
     (16 places). The bend is a p-power and there is no head at: the
     unramified places over 2 (f >= 2, s = 1), x^6 - 3, x^4 - 5,
     x^4 + 10, x^6 - 7, x^2 - 3.
  W  TRANSPLANTED from the records this script replaces: w = 1 at Z_2,
     x^2 + 2, x^2 - 2 and x^2 + 3; 2 at Q_2(sqrt -5) and x^4 - 2; 3 at
     Q_2(i); 4 at x^8 - 2. Q_2(sqrt 3): 2, since K(i)/K is unramified
     there and at e = 2 over Q_2 the width is 1, 2, 3 as K(i)/K is
     ramified, unramified or split (also a transplant); by the same
     rule 1 at x^2 - 10 and x^2 + 10, where K(i) = K(sqrt -d) is
     ramified. x^2 - 6 at 3 is the field of x^2 + 3, so 1. The rest
     are read.
  Q  the powers of p, at all four.
KILLS, as printed observables: any C line off (the instrument fails); any
place whose measured ladder parts from (3)'s shape below D, or any place
with w >= 1 outside (4)'s list or w = 0 inside it (the argument fails).
A width value off its transplant is a finding to read, never a kill: the
argument does not fix w.

FINDINGS. Every prediction landed: 40/40 checks PASS, no kill fired.
  C  generators against the whole group at 346 (place, a), 0 off; a
     unit of level exactly 1 attains E(a) at the same 346, 0 off.
  L  all 32 ladders have the argument's shape to depth D (14 to 56).
     Where s is not a p-power the ladder is psi's orbit: 1, 2, 4, 7,
     10, ... at x^3 + 2; 1, 2, 4, 8, 14, 20, ... at x^6 - 2, where
     psi(4) = 8 is still the multiplying branch and the next step is
     + 6. Where s = p^n it is 1, ..., p^n, A, A + e, ...: 1, 2, 7, 9,
     ... at Q_2(i); 1, 2, 4, 8, 20, 28, ... at x^8 - 2; 1, 3, 12, 18,
     ... at x^6 + 3.
  H  heads at exactly the sixteen places of the criterion; the bend a
     p-power and no head at the seven others: both unramified places
     over 2 (f = 2, 3), x^2 - 3, x^6 - 3, x^4 - 5, x^4 + 10, x^6 - 7.
  W  w = 1 at Z_2, x^2 + 2, x^2 - 2, x^2 - 10, x^2 + 10, x^2 + 3,
     x^2 - 6, x^4 + 5, x^6 + 7; 2 at Q_2(sqrt -5), Q_2(sqrt 3), x^4 - 2,
     x^4 + 2; 3 at Q_2(i) and x^6 + 3; 4 at x^8 - 2. Every transplant
     landed; Q_2(sqrt 3) and Q_2(sqrt -5), one field under two
     polynomials, agree. Over the six ramified quadratic fields of Q_2
     the width is 1, 2, 3 as K(i)/K is ramified (four fields),
     unramified (sqrt -5) or trivial (Q_2(i)).
  Q  the ladder is 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 over F_2 and F_4, and 1, 3, 9, 27
     over F_3 and F_9.
  Tiers: the ladder theorem (1)-(3) and the two numbers (5) are a
  theorem, the head criterion (4) a criterion, their proofs the
  argument above; the widths are observations per place, and at e = 2
  over Q_2 a rule, exhaustive over the six fields (since proved at
  every head: tame.py at odd p, wild.py at 2 for e >= 2).

RUN RECORD. 40/40, 25.3 s wall, 9.0 MB peak commit under a memory guard.
After the run the Kummer level e/(p - 1) was renamed the BEND, the least
level p-th powers of level-1 units reach past it the ARRIVAL A, and the
exponent of the bend n, to keep seat, L and T free for the seated
places, the invariant lambda and the tick; the rerun printed the same
40/40 and no number moved.

def bend_power(loc):
    """n with e = (p - 1) p^n, or None."""
    e, p = loc.e, loc.p
    if e % (p - 1):
        return None
    s, n = e // (p - 1), 0
    while s % p == 0:
        s //= p
        n += 1
    return n if s == 1 else None


def u_bar_is_minus_one(loc):
    if loc.kind == "unr":
        return loc.p == 2
    return (loc.g[0] // loc.p) % loc.p == 1


def criterion(loc):
    return (loc.f == 1 and bend_power(loc) is not None
            and u_bar_is_minus_one(loc))


def psi(loc, i):
    return min(loc.p * i, i + loc.e)


def depth(loc):
    return max(12, -(-loc.e // (loc.p - 1)) + 5 * loc.e + 8)


def ladder(loc, D):
    E = [None] + [loc.E_gen(a) for a in range(1, D + 2)]
    return [a for a in range(1, D + 1) if E[a + 1] > E[a]]


def orbit(loc, D):
    out, i = [], 1
    while i <= D:
        out.append(i)
        i = psi(loc, i)
    return out


def section_control():
    section("C  POSITIVE CONTROL: generators, the whole group, level 1")
    tested = bad_a = bad_b = 0
    for loc in PLACES:
        a = 1
        while loc.q ** (a - 1) <= LIMIT:
            tested += 1
            full = loc.E_full(a)
            bad_a += full != loc.E_gen(a)
            N = loc.prec(a)
            lvl1 = [z for z in loc.reps(a, False)
                    if loc.val([z[0] - 1] + z[1:], N) == 1]
            best = max(loc.order_exp(z, a, N) for z in lvl1) if lvl1 else 0
            bad_b += loc.p ** best != full
            a += 1
    check("C(a) E(a) by generators = E(a) over the whole of U_1/U_a",
          bad_a == 0, f"{tested} (place, a), {bad_a} off")
    check("C(b) a unit of level exactly 1 attains E(a)",
          bad_b == 0, f"{tested} (place, a), {bad_b} off")


def section_ladder():
    section("L  THE LADDER: psi's orbit of 1, one overshoot at the bend")
    rows = {}
    for loc in PLACES:
        D = depth(loc)
        lad = ladder(loc, D)
        n = bend_power(loc)
        if n is None:
            ok, w = lad == orbit(loc, D), None
        else:
            s = loc.e // (loc.p - 1)
            head = [loc.p ** j for j in range(n + 1)]
            ok = lad[:n + 1] == head and len(lad) > n + 1
            if ok:
                A = lad[n + 1]
                w = A - s - loc.e
                ok = w >= 0 and lad[n + 1:] == list(range(A, D + 1, loc.e))
            else:
                w = None
        rows[loc.name] = (ok, n, w)
        print(f"  {loc.name:20s} p={loc.p} e={loc.e} f={loc.f} "
              f"n={n} D={D} w={w} ladder {lad[:9]}"
              f"{' ...' if len(lad) > 9 else ''}")
        check(f"L {loc.name}: the ladder has (3)'s shape to depth {D}", ok)
    return rows


def section_head(rows):
    section("H  THE HEAD CRITERION: w >= 1 iff f = 1, e = (p-1)p^n, "
            "u_bar = -1")
    got = {n for n, (ok, _, w) in rows.items() if w}
    by_crit = {loc.name for loc in PLACES if criterion(loc)}
    zero = sorted(n for n, (ok, _, w) in rows.items() if w == 0)
    print(f"  heads measured: {len(got)}; by the criterion: {len(by_crit)}")
    print(f"  bend a p-power, no head: {zero}")
    check("H the measured heads are exactly the criterion's places",
          got == by_crit, f"off: {sorted(got ^ by_crit)}")
    check("H the criterion's places are the slate's sixteen",
          by_crit == HEADS, f"off: {sorted(by_crit ^ HEADS)}")


def section_width(rows):
    section("W  THE WIDTH VALUES")
    for n, (ok, _, w) in rows.items():
        if w:
            note = WIDTH.get(n)
            print(f"  {n:20s} w = {w}"
                  + ("" if note is None else
                     f"   (transplant {note}{'' if note == w else ' OFF'})"))
    off = {n: rows[n][2] for n in WIDTH if rows[n][2] != WIDTH[n]}
    print(f"  widths off their transplant: {off}")


def section_equal():
    section("Q  EQUAL CHARACTERISTIC: the orbit of p i")
    for loc in EQUAL:
        E = [None] + [loc.E_gen(a) for a in range(1, 42)]
        lad = [a for a in range(1, 41) if E[a + 1] > E[a]]
        pw = [loc.p ** j for j in range(8) if loc.p ** j <= 40]
        print(f"  {loc.name:9s} ladder {lad}")
        check(f"Q {loc.name}: the ladder is the powers of p to 40",
              lad == pw)


def main():
    section_control()
    if not all(CHECKS):
        print("  control failed: run stopped")
        raise SystemExit(1)
    rows = section_ladder()
    section_head(rows)
    section_width(rows)
    section_equal()
    print()
    print(f"{sum(CHECKS)}/{len(CHECKS)} checks passed")
    raise SystemExit(0 if all(CHECKS) else 1)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
