rationed.py

Checks: LEARNER: The layers of a holding set (theorem); LEARNER: Under an income the rank is common (theorem); LEARNER: On the eights row, patience with no bank lags for the whole run (rule); LEARNER: Destination universality (rule).

Cited on: LEARNER.

To run it: learner.py beside it; standard library only.

"""rationed.py -- a reader of the straddle cover paid a fixed income of
rank per step: the income decides how deep it reads, and the policy
decides only which of the incomparable members at that depth it holds.

QUESTION. learner.py's reader commits, at step n, a member of the
straddle cover holding the image J_n, and with no limit on its moves
the best reader commits the BOTTOM of J_n, a function of the image
alone. Here the reader is RATIONED: at each step it receives an
INCOME of B rank units, a move costs the rank it gains (a tree move 1,
a chain move from index k to k' the difference k' - k, a chain entry
at a tile its index), and a chain move is truncated to what is left,
which stays sound because the straddles at one vertex nest. Unspent
units go to a BANK of capacity W, overflow lost, and a DRAWDOWN
coordinate delta caps a step's spending at B + delta (INF draws
whatever is banked). A policy is learner.py's four coordinates and
delta. Does the optimum now depend on the stream, on the resources
(B, W), or on neither; and where, in the cover, can a difference
between two readers sit?

THE LOSSES. The DEFICIT loss is learner.py's: the product of the
committed lengths over the counted steps n = 8 .. N - 1, infinite when
a committed interval is unbounded. The LAG loss reads the committed
interval on the image's own clock: tau(n) counts the steps m <= n with
|J_m| >= |C_n|, and the LAG at step n is n + 1 - tau(n), the number of
steps the reader sits behind the stream; the lag loss sums it over the
counted steps. The COMPOSITE order compares lag losses and breaks ties
by the deficit. The sum of scale shortfalls, scale(J_n) - scale(C_n),
would be no new loss: its first term does not depend on the policy, so
it is the logarithm of the deficit loss shifted by a constant. The lag
quantizes the shortfall by the stream's own rate, which is why it can
order two readers differently.

THE SLATE. learner.py's eight rows and a ninth, y -> 2y on the stream
whose continued-fraction digits are the Fibonacci word over {1, 2}
(the fixed point of 1 -> 12, 2 -> 1): bounded digits, never periodic,
so a value that is not a quadratic irrational. Horizon N = 120.
Settings: B in 1, 2, 3, 4 and W in 0, 2, 4, 8. The drawdown axis at
cap W is the values 0, 1, 2, 4 below W and INF, since delta >= W draws
exactly what INF draws (the bank never holds more than W); at W = 0
the axis is INF alone. So the policy space has 100, 300, 400 and 500
members at W = 0, 2, 4, 8.

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine).
  (A) THE LAYERS. Fix an image J that is a bounded interval of positive
      length held by the root. Its path tiles T_0 (the root) ... T_h
      (= I*) sit at depths 0 .. h, k_i* is the largest index whose
      straddle at T_i holds J, and the members
      holding J are the (T_i, k) with 0 <= k <= k_i* (learner.py's (D)
      shows the tile of a holding member holds J). The rank of (T_i, k)
      is i + k. CLAIM: if a holding member M' lies strictly inside a
      holding member M, then rank(M') > rank(M). So two distinct
      holding members of one rank are incomparable, every rank from 0
      to r*(J) = h + k_h* carries a holding member (T_R for R <= h,
      (I*, R - h) above), and the bottom is the only member of rank
      r*(J).
      Proof. Write M = (T_i, k), M' = (T_j, k'). If j = i the straddles
      nest and k' > k. If j < i: M lies inside T_i, which lies inside
      a child tile of T_j and so misses T_j's vertex v_j, while M'
      either is T_j itself (not inside M, being larger) or, k' >= 1,
      straddles v_j; either way M' is not inside M. So j > i, and if
      k = 0 the rank rises since j > i. If k >= 1, J lies inside
      S_k(v_i) but its closure misses v_i (else no tile below T_i
      holds J, and T_j would not exist), so J sits in one child tile
      of S_k(v_i), say (l + k v_i, v_i), and the path below T_i runs
      T_(i+m) = (l + (m - 1) v_i, v_i) for m = 1 .. k + 1. For
      i < j <= i + k the tile T_j has its left end l + (j - i - 1) v_i
      outside S_k(v_i), whose left end is l + k v_i, and a straddle at
      T_j contains v_j = l + (j - i) v_i in its interior, a point at
      or left of that end; so M' is not inside M. Hence j >= i + k + 1
      and rank(M') >= j > i + k = rank(M). The mirror side is the same.
  (B) THE COMMON RANK. Let a rationed reader have both patiences 0 and
      delta = INF. Every legal move holds J_n, so it only ever holds
      members holding the current image. Above the bottom a move is
      always legal (learner.py's (E)) and affordable, a tree move
      costing 1 and a chain move truncating to what is left; so the
      loop stops exactly when the rank reaches r*(J_n) or the step's
      units run out. Every move adds its cost to the rank, so the rank
      gained is route-free: rho_n = min(r*(J_n), rho_(n-1) + B +
      b_(n-1)), and the bank b_n = min(W, B + b_(n-1) - (rho_n -
      rho_(n-1))) follows the same recursion, with rho_(-1) = 0 at the
      root and b_(-1) = 0. So the four greedy spend-all readers share
      one rank trace and one bank trace, computed without running any
      of them, and by (A) at every step their committed intervals are
      EQUAL or INCOMPARABLE. Whatever separates them, the stream
      included, enters only as a choice between incomparable members
      of one layer.
  (C) THE START DELAY. On (identity, eights) the first image is
      (0, 1), which no member holds, its closure meeting 0, so the
      greedy reader first moves at step 1, and a reader of tree
      patience p, with chains refused, first moves at step p + 1. The
      demand there is
      about eight ranks a step, so at an income of 1, 2 or 4 both
      readers spend their whole income every step and the rank gap
      is B p from step p + 1 on, not closing over the run with no bank
      (W = 0, the setting run): patience costs a lag the run never
      closes once the income binds and nothing is banked. With tiles alone the
      holding members form a chain (learner.py's (F)), so the patient
      reader's tile contains the greedy one's, and the loss rises
      strictly with p.
  (D) A TAUTOLOGY, NAMED BEFORE IT PRINTS. A policy in every row's
      argmin set minimizes every product over rows, so when such a
      policy exists the loss lost by training on one set of rows and
      testing on another is zero. A zero transfer gap is a corollary
      of a common argmin member, never a second finding.

PREDICTIONS (fixed before the engine ran).
  P0 [controls, read first] (i) With no income limit the rationed
     reader commits what learner.py's reader commits, all 100 policies
     on all eight rows. (ii) The Fibonacci word's first twelve digits
     are 1,2,1,1,2,1,2,1,1,2,1,1 and its share of 2s over 10^4 digits
     lies in [0.375, 0.390]. (iii) The unlimited greedy reader's
     counted lag is at most 6 on every row. (iv) At income 1 on
     (identity, eights) the greedy reader's lag rises over the counted
     steps at a slope in [0.80, 0.95] (about 7/8: one rank earned of
     about eight demanded). (v) The ln-deficit losses of the four
     greedy spend-all readers on (identity, eights) at (B, W) = (4, 2)
     print as the published ones: sigma = (0,0) and (0,1) at -14920.6,
     (1,1) at -14909.1, (1,0) at -14901.0, each to 0.1.
  P1 [(A), the layers] At every step n <= 20 of every row, over the

     distinct members of one rank are nested, every strictly nested pair

     r*(J_n) - 1 and r*(J_n) carry a member (the first run's walk to r*
     hung; see the run record), and the bottom is the only member of

  P2 [(B), the common rank] At every setting and row the four greedy

  P3 [(C), the start delay] On (identity, eights) at W = 0 and B = 1,

  P4 [destination universality, the principal] At every one of the
     16 settings, under the deficit order and under the composite

     policy U = (1, 1, 0, 0, INF), chain first at tiles and tree first

     patiences 0 with delta in {1, INF}. CONTROL: the intersection
     routine prints empty on a planted pair of disjoint argmin sets,
     and the per-row argmin sets are not all equal. Added after the

  P5 [the inversion named] At (4, 2), U and V = (0, 0, 0, 0, INF)
     commit equal or incomparable intervals at every step (from P2),
     V's loss is below U's on (identity, eights) and equal to it on

     printed with both intervals, their common rank and their lengths.

KILLS (observables).
  K0 any P0 control misses: nothing below is read.
  K1 a P1 or P2 failure: (A) or (B) is false in this cover.
  K2 P4 prints an empty common set at some setting, under either order:
     the optimum depends on the stream there.
  K3 P3 prints a gap other than B p: (C)'s demand reading is wrong.

FINDINGS (entered after the run; 26 of 26 checks pass).

def fib_word(count):
    w = "1"
    while len(w) < count:
        w = "".join("12" if ch == "1" else "1" for ch in w)
    return [int(ch) for ch in w[:count]]


def tiles_of_digits(digits):
    p2, q2, p1, q1 = 0, 1, 1, 0
    out = []
    for a in digits:
        p, q = a * p1 + p2, a * q1 + q2
        e1, e2 = (p, q), (p + p1, q + q1)
        out.append((e1, e2) if lt(e1, e2) else (e2, e1))
        p2, q2, p1, q1 = p1, q1, p, q
    return out


def images9(horizon):
    out = {}
    for m, s in ROWS9:
        f = MAPS[m]
        if s == "fib":
            tiles = tiles_of_digits(fib_word(horizon))
        else:
            tiles = input_tiles(s, horizon)
        row = []
        for lo, hi in tiles:
            a, b = f(lo), f(hi)
            row.append((a, b) if lt(a, b) else (b, a))
        out[(m, s)] = row
    return out


def rank(c):
    return c[3] + c[2]


# the rationed reader

def run_rationed(Js, pol, B, W):
    """Committed members and bank levels at every step. B = None is the
    unlimited reader, which ignores W and delta."""
    s_tile, s_str, pt, pc, dl = pol
    C = ROOT
    bank = 0
    out, banks = [], []
    for n, J in enumerate(Js):
        rt = Js[n - pt] if pt is not INF and n - pt >= 0 else None
        rc = Js[n - pc] if pc is not INF and n - pc >= 0 else None
        if B is None:
            rem = None
        else:
            avail = B + bank
            spend = avail if dl is INF else min(avail, B + dl)
            rem = spend
        while rem is None or rem > 0:
            l, r, k, d = C
            v = add(l, r)
            lo, hi = span(C)
            tree = chain = None
            if rt is not None:
                for ch in ((lo, v, 0, d + k + 1), (v, hi, 0, d + k + 1)):
                    if holds(ch, rt):
                        tree = ch
                        break
            if rc is not None:
                k2 = index_max(l, r, rc)
                if rem is not None:
                    k2 = min(k2, k + rem)
                if k2 > k:
                    chain = (l, r, k2, d)
            if tree is None and chain is None:
                break
            prefer_chain = (s_tile == 1) if k == 0 else (s_str == 0)
            prev = rank(C)
            if chain is not None and (tree is None or prefer_chain):
                C = chain
            else:
                C = tree
            if rem is not None:
                rem -= rank(C) - prev
        if B is not None:
            bank = min(W, avail - (spend - rem))
        if not (le(span(C)[0], J[0]) and le(J[1], span(C)[1])):
            raise AssertionError("a commitment lost the image")
        out.append(C)
        banks.append(bank)
    return out, banks


def image_len(J):
    return (J[1][0] * J[0][1] - J[0][0] * J[1][1], J[0][1] * J[1][1])


def lags(Js, trace):
    """lag(n) = n + 1 - #{m <= n : |J_m| >= |C_n|} at every step."""
    jl = [image_len(J) for J in Js]
    out = []
    tau = 0
    for n, C in enumerate(trace):
        c = length(C)
        if c is None:
            out.append(n + 1)
            continue
        tau = min(tau, n + 1)
        while tau <= n and jl[tau][0] * c[1] >= c[0] * jl[tau][1]:
            tau += 1
        while tau > 0 and jl[tau - 1][0] * c[1] < c[0] * jl[tau - 1][1]:
            tau -= 1
        out.append(n + 1 - tau)
    return out


def lag_loss(Js, trace):
    return sum(lags(Js, trace)[N0:])


def ln_loss(t):
    return ln_int(t[0]) - ln_int(t[1]) if not t[2] else float("inf")


def d_axis(W):
    return [d for d in (0, 1, 2, 4) if d < W] + [INF]


def space5(W):
    return [p + (d,) for p in policies() for d in d_axis(W)]


def argmin(space, key_cmp):
    best = [space[0]]
    for p in space[1:]:
        c = key_cmp(p, best[0])
        if c < 0:
            best = [p]
        elif c == 0:
            best.append(p)
    return set(best)


def common(sets):
    out = None
    for s in sets:
        out = set(s) if out is None else out & s
    return out


def relation(a, b):
    if a == b:
        return "equal"
    if inside(a, b):
        return "inside"
    if inside(b, a):
        return "contains"
    return "incomparable"

def section_controls(imgs):
    section("P0 CONTROLS")
    bad = 0
    for row in ROWS:
        for p in policies():
            bad += run(imgs[row], p) != run_rationed(imgs[row], p + (INF,),
                                                     None, 0)[0]
    check("P0 unlimited rationed reader equals the learner's reader",
          bad == 0, f"{bad} of 800 traces differ")
    fw = fib_word(10 ** 4)
    dens = fw.count(2) / len(fw)
    check("P0 Fibonacci word prefix and share of 2s",
          fw[:12] == [1, 2, 1, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1]
          and 0.375 <= dens <= 0.390, f"share {dens:.4f}")
    worst = 0
    for row in ROWS9:
        tr, _ = run_rationed(imgs[row], (0, 0, 0, 0, INF), None, 0)
        worst = max(worst, max(lags(imgs[row], tr)[N0:]))
    check("P0 unlimited greedy counted lag at most 6 on every row",
          worst <= 6, f"max {worst}")
    tr, _ = run_rationed(imgs[EIGHTS], (0, 0, 0, 0, INF), 1, 0)
    lg = lags(imgs[EIGHTS], tr)
    s = (lg[-1] - lg[N0]) / (len(lg) - 1 - N0)
    check("P0 income-1 greedy lag slope on (identity, eights) in "
          "[0.80, 0.95]", 0.80 <= s <= 0.95, f"slope {s:.4f}")
    pub = {(0, 0): -14920.6, (0, 1): -14920.6, (1, 1): -14909.1,
           (1, 0): -14901.0}
    got = {}
    for sg in pub:
        tr, _ = run_rationed(imgs[EIGHTS], sg + (0, 0, INF), 4, 2)
        got[sg] = ln_loss(loss(tr))
    check("P0 greedy spend-all ln-losses on (identity, eights) at (4, 2) "
          "match the published ones",
          all(abs(got[k] - pub[k]) <= 0.1 for k in pub),
          ", ".join(f"{k} {got[k]:.1f}" for k in pub))


def holding_members(J):
    tiles = path_tiles(J)
    out = []
    for i, t in enumerate(tiles):
        K = index_max(t[0], t[1], J)
        ks = set(range(0, min(K, 40) + 1)) | {k for k in (K - 1, K)
                                              if k >= 0}
        out += [(t[0], t[1], k, t[3]) for k in sorted(ks)]
    return out


def section_layers(imgs):
    section("P1 THE LAYERS")
    same_rank_nested = rank_drop = gaps = not_sole = wide = steps = 0
    pairs = 0
    for row in ROWS9:
        for n, J in enumerate(imgs[row][:21]):
            ms = holding_members(J)
            if not ms:
                continue
            steps += 1
            b = bottom(J)
            top = rank(b)
            ranks = {rank(m) for m in ms}
            want = set(range(min(top, 40) + 1)) | {top - 1, top}
            gaps += any(R not in ranks for R in want if R >= 0)
            not_sole += sum(1 for m in ms if rank(m) == top and m != b)
            by = {}
            for m in ms:
                by.setdefault(rank(m), []).append(m)
            wide += any(len(g) >= 2 for g in by.values())
            for a in ms:
                for c in ms:
                    if a is c or a == c:
                        continue
                    pairs += 1
                    if inside(a, c):
                        if rank(a) == rank(c):
                            same_rank_nested += 1
                        elif rank(a) < rank(c):
                            rank_drop += 1
    check("P1 no two distinct members of one rank are nested",
          same_rank_nested == 0, f"{pairs} ordered pairs, "
          f"{same_rank_nested} off")
    check("P1 a strictly inner member has the higher rank",
          rank_drop == 0, f"{rank_drop} off")
    check("P1 every rank to 40, the bottom's and the one below carry a "
          "member",
          gaps == 0, f"{gaps} steps with a gap, over {steps}")
    check("P1 the bottom is the only member of its rank",
          not_sole == 0, f"{not_sole} off")
    check("P1 control: some layer holds two or more members",
          wide > 0, f"{wide} of {steps} row-steps")


def recursion(Js, B, W):
    rho, bank, out, banks = 0, 0, [], []
    for J in Js:
        b = bottom(J)
        top = rank(b) if b is not None else 0
        new = min(top, rho + B + bank)
        bank = min(W, B + bank - (new - rho))
        rho = new
        out.append(rho)
        banks.append(bank)
    return out, banks


def section_common_rank(imgs, traces):
    section("P2 THE COMMON RANK OF THE GREEDY SPEND-ALL READERS")
    trace_off = rec_off = nested = differ = 0
    for (B, W), per in traces.items():
        for row in ROWS9:
            quartet = [per[row][sg + (0, 0, INF)]
                       for sg in ((0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 0), (1, 1))]
            rks = [[rank(c) for c in tr] for tr, _ in quartet]
            bks = [bk for _, bk in quartet]
            trace_off += any(r != rks[0] for r in rks)
            trace_off += any(b != bks[0] for b in bks)
            rr, rb = recursion(imgs[row], B, W)
            rec_off += rr != rks[0] or rb != bks[0]
            for n in range(len(imgs[row])):
                ms = [tr[n] for tr, _ in quartet]
                for i in range(4):
                    for j in range(i + 1, 4):
                        rel = relation(ms[i], ms[j])
                        if rel != "equal":
                            differ += 1
                            nested += rel != "incomparable"
    check("P2 one rank trace and one bank trace per setting and row",
          trace_off == 0, f"{trace_off} off")
    check("P2 they equal the recursion run with no reader",
          rec_off == 0, f"{rec_off} off")
    check("P2 differing intervals are incomparable, never nested",
          nested == 0, f"{differ} differing pair-steps, {nested} nested")
    check("P2 control: the quartet differs somewhere", differ > 0)


def section_start_delay(imgs):
    section("P3 THE START DELAY ON (identity, eights)")
    Js = imgs[EIGHTS]
    off = []
    for B in (1, 2, 4):
        for sg in ((0, 0),):
            g = [rank(c) for c in run_rationed(Js, sg + (0, INF, INF),
                                               B, 0)[0]]
            for p in (1, 2, 3):
                h = [rank(c) for c in run_rationed(Js, sg + (p, INF, INF),
                                                   B, 0)[0]]
                if any(g[n] - h[n] != B * p for n in range(N0, len(Js))):
                    off.append((B, sg, p))
    check("P3 tile-only rank gap is B p at every counted step",
          off == [], f"off at {off}")
    bad = []
    steps = []
    for a in (0, 1):
        for b in (0, 1):
            for pc in (0, 1, 2, 3):
                ls = [loss(run_rationed(Js, (a, b, pt, pc, INF), 1, 0)[0])
                      for pt in (0, 1, 2, 3)]
                if not all(cmp(ls[i], ls[i + 1]) < 0 for i in range(3)):
                    bad.append((a, b, pc))
                steps += [ln_loss(ls[i + 1]) - ln_loss(ls[i])
                          for i in range(3)]
    check("P3 at B = 1 loss rises strictly with tree patience", bad == [],
          f"off at {bad}; ln step {min(steps):.1f} .. {max(steps):.1f}")


QUARTET = [sg + (0, 0, INF) for sg in ((0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 0), (1, 1))]


def settings_tables(imgs):
    """Per setting: every policy's (deficit loss, lag loss) per row, and
    the traces of the greedy spend-all quartet, the rest discarded."""
    tables, traces = {}, {}
    for B in INCOMES:
        for W in CAPS:
            tab, keep = {}, {}
            for row in ROWS9:
                tab[row], keep[row] = {}, {}
                for p in space5(W):
                    tr, bk = run_rationed(imgs[row], p, B, W)
                    tab[row][p] = (loss(tr), lag_loss(imgs[row], tr))
                    if p in QUARTET:
                        keep[row][p] = (tr, bk)
            tables[(B, W)], traces[(B, W)] = tab, keep
    return tables, traces


def fmt(p):
    return "(" + ",".join("I" if x is INF else str(x) for x in p) + ")"


def section_universality(tables):
    section("P4 DESTINATION UNIVERSALITY")
    planted = common([{(1, 0)}, {(0, 1)}])
    check("P4 control: the intersection of disjoint argmin sets is empty",
          planted == set())
    empty = []
    greedy_missing = []
    u_off = []
    at42 = {}
    every = {}
    unequal = 0
    for (B, W), tab in tables.items():
        space = space5(W)
        for order in ("deficit", "composite"):
            sets = []
            for row in ROWS9:
                Ls = {p: tab[row][p][0] for p in space}
                if order == "deficit":
                    def key(x, y, Ls=Ls):
                        return cmp(Ls[x], Ls[y])
                else:
                    Gs = {p: tab[row][p][1] for p in space}

                    def key(x, y, Ls=Ls, Gs=Gs):
                        c = (Gs[x] > Gs[y]) - (Gs[x] < Gs[y])
                        return c if c else cmp(Ls[x], Ls[y])
                sets.append(argmin(space, key))
            unequal += any(s != sets[0] for s in sets)
            com = common(sets)
            every[order] = com if order not in every else every[order] & com
            if not com:
                empty.append((B, W, order))
            if not any(p[2] == 0 and p[3] == 0 and p[4] is INF for p in com):
                greedy_missing.append((B, W, order))
            if (U in com) != ((B, W) != (4, 2)):
                u_off.append((B, W, order))
            if (B, W) == (4, 2):
                at42[order] = com
    check("P4 the nine rows share an argmin member at every setting, "
          "both orders", empty == [], f"empty at {empty}")
    check("P4 a greedy spend-all policy is always common",
          greedy_missing == [], f"missing at {greedy_missing}")
    check("P4 U is common at every setting but (4, 2)", u_off == [],
          f"off at {u_off}")
    want = {(a, b, 0, 0, d) for a, b in ((0, 0), (0, 1))
            for d in (1, INF)}
    for order, com in at42.items():
        check(f"P4 at (4, 2) the common set is sigma (0,0), (0,1) greedy, "
              f"delta 1 or INF ({order})", com == want,
              " ".join(fmt(p) for p in sorted(com, key=str)))
    check("P4 control: per-row argmin sets are not all equal",
          unequal > 0, f"{unequal} of 32 setting-orders")
    for order, com in every.items():
        print(f"  common at all 16 settings ({order}): "
              + (" ".join(fmt(p) for p in sorted(com, key=str)) or "none"))
    check("P4 no policy is common at every setting, either order",
          not any(every.values()))


def section_inversion(imgs, tables, traces):
    section("P5 THE INVERSION AT (4, 2) NAMED")
    tab, per = tables[(4, 2)], traces[(4, 2)]
    worse = []
    for row in ROWS9:
        lu, lv = tab[row][U][0], tab[row][V][0]
        if row != EIGHTS and cmp(lv, lu) != 0:
            worse.append(row)
        print(f"  {row}: ln loss U {ln_loss(lu):.2f}, V {ln_loss(lv):.2f}")
    tu, tv = per[EIGHTS][U][0], per[EIGHTS][V][0]
    lu, lv = loss(tu), loss(tv)
    check("P5 V below U on (identity, eights), equal to it on the eight "
          "other rows", cmp(lv, lu) < 0 and worse == [],
          f"V off U on {worse}")
    diff = [n for n in range(N0, len(tu)) if tu[n] != tv[n]]
    rels = {relation(tu[n], tv[n]) for n in diff}
    check("P5 U and V differ only by incomparable intervals",
          rels <= {"incomparable"}, f"{len(diff)} counted steps differ")
    if diff:
        n = diff[0]
        for name, c in (("U", tu[n]), ("V", tv[n])):
            ln = length(c)
            print(f"  step {n}: {name} holds {span(c)}, rank {rank(c)}, "
                  f"k {c[2]}, length {ln[0]}/{ln[1]}")
        print(f"  image at step {n}: {imgs[EIGHTS][n]}")


def main():
    imgs = images9(N_MAIN)
