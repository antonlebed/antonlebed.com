carrier.py

Checks: CLOCK: A ramified quadratic place over 2 reads its width off δ mod 8 (known); CLOCK: A carrier bears on a place down to a ladder member (theorem).

Cited on: CLOCK.

To run it: save carrier.py, with clock.py, module_law.py beside it; standard library only.

"""carrier.py -- what a place over another prime does to a place's door:
how deep a carrier's jump of the count reaches down a ladder, what it
moves the door to there, and the ramified quadratic place over 2 read
through it, whose width is a function of delta mod 8.

QUESTION. A place P over p, seated at depth a, has door m(V + 1) - a,
where V = v_p(L) is the COUNT and m(j) = x_j + 1 is the least depth
whose column reaches j, x_1 < x_2 < ... being P's ladder
(door.py; clock.py). A CARRIER is a place G over another prime whose
opening lifts the count: it puts N(G) - 1 into L, so the count becomes
at least j = v_p(N(G) - 1), whatever G's own depth. Three questions.
  (a) At which depths of P does a carrier move P's door, when the rest
      of L's p-part is P's own column, and to what?
  (b) What does that read at the ramified place over 2 of a quadratic
      field Q(sqrt delta), against a carrier jumping the count to 2?
  (c) What is the least norm of a carrier jumping the count to j, and
      what does a residue degree of 3 change about it?

THE OBJECTS. c(a) = v_p(E(a)) is P's column: the number of ladder
members below a, since E jumps by exactly p at each member (clock.py).
The state is {P at depth a} with, or without, a carrier supplying j; the
carrier's prime-to-p part never touches P's door, whose prime-to-p part
N(P) - 1 is paid while P is seated. So with the carrier L's p-part is
max(c(a), j) and without it c(a).

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine).
  (1) THE DEPTHS A CARRIER BEARS ON. Without the carrier the count is
      c(a) and the door m(c(a) + 1) - a; with it the count is
      max(c(a), j). The two differ iff j > c(a), m being strictly
      increasing, and c(a) < j iff fewer than j members lie below a iff
      a <= x_j. So a carrier jumping the count to j moves P's door at
      exactly the depths 1..x_j, the j-th LADDER MEMBER, and there moves
      it from m(c(a) + 1) - a to m(j + 1) - a = x_(j+1) + 1 - a. At
      depth 1 the door goes from 1 to x_(j+1).
  (2) THE STAIRCASE IS ONE LADDER SHAPE. The form a <= e (j - 1) + 1
      is the case x_j = 1 + (j - 1) e: the ladder 1, 1 + e, 1 + 2e, ...
      By the ladder theorem (clock.py) that is psi's orbit exactly when
      psi(1) = 1 + e, i.e. p >= 1 + e, AND no step overshoots, which
      fails only at a head. So the staircase holds iff e <= p - 1 and
      the place has no head; at e = p - 1 the head criterion decides
      (f = 1 and u_bar = -1, n = 0). Everywhere else the depths and the
      door are read off the ladder's other shape: at Z_2 (e = 1, a
      head, w = 1) the ladder is 1, 3, 4, 5, ..., so x_j = j + 1 for
      j >= 2, one deeper than the staircase; at Q_2(i) it is 1, 2, 7, 9,
      so x_2 = 2 against the staircase's 3, then 7 against 5; at x^3 + 2
      over 2 (e = 3 >= p, no head) it is 1, 2, 4, 7, so x_2 = 2 against
      4.
  (3) THE RAMIFIED PLACE OVER 2, READ AS A DOOR. Let P be the place
      over 2 of Q(sqrt delta), delta squarefree, 2 ramified: e = 2,
      f = 1, bend s = 2 = 2^1, so the ladder is 1, 2, A, A + 2, ... with
      A = s + e + w = 4 + w (clock.py), and by (1) its door at depth 1
      against a carrier jumping the count to 2 is x_3 = 4 + w.
      Independently, the column of P at depth b is v_2 of the exponent of
      a 2-group of order 2^(b-1), and the count 2 means 4 | L, so the
      door is the least r with some unit eta having eta^4 != 1 mod
      P^(1+r): the door is MIN over units eta with eta^4 != 1 of
      v_P(eta^4 - 1). For a ramified place of a quadratic field,
      v_P = v_2 o N, and
          N(eta^4 - 1) = (N - Tr + 1)(N + Tr + 1)((N - 1)^2 + Tr^2),
      N = N(eta), Tr = Tr(eta), from N(eta - 1), N(eta + 1) and
      N(eta^2 + 1). Write eta = sigma + tau sqrt delta and i =
      v_P(eta - 1), its level.
        i >= 3: v_P(eta + 1) = v_P(2) = 2, and eta^2 + 1 = (eta^2 - 1)
          + 2 with v_P(eta^2 - 1) >= 5, so v_P(eta^2 + 1) = 2: total
          i + 4 >= 7.
        i = 2: v_P(eta + 1) >= 3 (eta + 1 = (eta - 1) + 2, two terms of
          valuation 2), v_P(eta^2 + 1) = 2: total >= 7.
        i = 1, delta = 2 gamma with gamma odd: a unit has sigma odd, and
          level 1 needs tau odd. Both outer factors are (sigma -+ 1)^2 -
          2 gamma tau^2, valuation 1; N - 1 = (sigma^2 - 1) - 2 gamma
          tau^2 has valuation 1, so the third factor is
          4 (((N - 1)/2)^2 + sigma^2), two odd squares, whose sum is
          2 mod 8: valuation 3. Total 5, for every odd gamma.
        i = 1, delta = 3 mod 4: a unit has sigma + tau odd, and level 1
          needs sigma even, tau odd. Both outer factors are odd^2 -
          delta odd^2 = 1 - delta mod 8, valuation 1. With sigma =
          2 sigma', N - 1 = 4 sigma'^2 - (delta tau^2 + 1), and the
          third factor is 16 (((N - 1)/4)^2 + sigma'^2).
          delta = 3 mod 8: (delta tau^2 + 1)/4 is odd, so (N - 1)/4 and
            sigma' have opposite parities: one odd and one even square,
            valuation 4, total 6.
          delta = 7 mod 8: (delta tau^2 + 1)/4 is even, so (N - 1)/4
            has sigma''s parity. sigma' odd gives two odd squares,
            valuation 4 + 1, total 7; sigma' even gives two even ones,
            valuation >= 6, total >= 8. The least is 7.
      So the door is 5 at delta = 2, 6 mod 8, 6 at delta = 3 mod 8 and
      7 at delta = 7 mod 8, and w = door - 4 is 1, 2, 3: THE WIDTH IS A
      FUNCTION OF delta mod 8. It reads delta through 1 - delta mod 8
      and delta tau^2 + 1 mod 8, and of gamma only that it is odd,
      which is why the six ramified quadratic extensions of Q_2 give
      four residues' worth of widths: Q_2(sqrt 2) and Q_2(sqrt 10) share
      w = 1 as clock.py measured.
  (4) THE CARRIER'S NORM. A carrier jumping the count to j has
      p^j | N(G) - 1 and N(G) > 1, so N(G) >= p^j + 1, the FLOOR,
      attained iff p^j + 1 is a prime power. At p = 2 that is 3, 5, 9,
      17 at j = 1..4 (9 = 3^2) and not 33 at j = 5. A residue degree of
      3 over ell has N(G) - 1 = (ell - 1)(ell^2 + ell + 1), so it carries
      the primes p = 1 mod 3 dividing ell^2 + ell + 1 from a small
      ell: 7 from norm 8, where residue degree <= 2 first carries 7 at
      norm 29. Since ell^2 + ell + 1 is odd, it never cheapens a carrier
      for p = 2, though ell - 1 supplies 2 as at any odd ell.

TRANSPLANTS. The door m(V + 1) - a is door.py's theorem and the ladder's
shape clock.py's; both are imported rather than restated, and (1)'s
depths are measured below from a WHOLE-GROUP column (module_law.py's
E_full), not from the ladder they are compared with. The unit brute of
(3) is written here from nothing and reads no ladder at all.

REPRESENTATIVES. In (3) units are sigma + tau sqrt delta with
(sigma, tau) mod 2^K. Moving sigma or tau by 2^K moves eta^4 - 1 by a
multiple of 2^K, of valuation at least 2K at P, so any valuation below
2K read off a representative is true; a reading at or above 2K is
CENSORED and counted, never used.

PREDICTIONS, fixed before the run.
  C  CONTROL. E_gen equals E_full (the whole group) at every place of
     the consumer set to its depth; v_P(x) = v_2(N(x)) on a sample at
     every ramified field of |delta| <= 60; the norm identity of (3) holds
     as integers at every sample; the unit brute's column of Q(i)'s
     place over 2 is v_2 = 0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3 at b = 1..9, which
     is c(b) off the ladder 1, 2, 7, 9.
  B  At every place of the consumer set and j = 1..4, the depths where
     seating a carrier supplying j moves the door (brute lcm doors off
     the whole-group column) are exactly 1..x_j, and the moved door is
     x_(j+1) + 1 - a, wherever x_(j+1) lies inside the column read.
  S  The staircase x_j = 1 + (j - 1) e holds at every j read exactly at
     the places with e <= p - 1 and no head: Z_3, Z_7, the unramified
     place of degree 2 over 2, x^2 - 3 over 3 (e = 2 = p - 1, no mu_3);
     and departs at some j <= 4 at Z_2, x^2 + 2, x^2 - 2, Q_2(i),
     Q_2(sqrt -5), x^2 + 3 (headed) and x^3 + 2 (e >= p).
  D  Over every squarefree delta, |delta| <= 2000, with 2 ramified: the unit
     brute's least v_P(eta^4 - 1) is 5, 6, 7 at delta = 2 or 6, 3, 7 mod 8,
     none censored [ruled: no door censored]; it equals x_3 of the Eisenstein
     place built from delta (x^2 - delta, or x^2 + 2x + 1 - delta at delta = 3
     mod 4); and the least over units of level >= 2 is at least 7 at every
     field.
  F  The least prime power q = 1 mod p^j, for p < 24 and j <= 6, read to
     2 000 000: at p = 2 it is 3, 5, 9, 17 at j = 1..4, each the floor,
     and 97 at j = 5. Of the nine primes p < 24, residue degree 3
     carries p more cheaply than residue degree <= 2 at exactly 7
     (8 against 29) and 13 (27 against 53).
KILLS, as printed observables: any C line off (the instruments fail,
nothing below is read); a place and j where B's printed depths or door
differ from the ladder's; an S place on the wrong side; a D field off
its residue, censored, or off x_3, or a level >= 2 minimum below 7 (the
case analysis missed a seat); an F row off. [Ruled on audit: B reads
(1)'s corollary back off the whole group, so it fires only on an engine
fault.]

FINDINGS. Every prediction landed: 11/11 checks PASS, no kill fired.
  C  E_gen equals the whole group's E_full at all 11 consumer places to
     depth x_5 + 1; v_P = v_2(N) at 8232 elements over the ramified
     fields of |delta| <= 60, 0 off; the norm identity at 2888 samples, 0
     off; the unit brute reads Q(i)'s column 0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3.
  B  At all 11 places and j = 1..4 a carrier moves the door at exactly
     depths 1..x_j and sets it to x_(j+1) + 1 - a, 0 (place, j) off, 0
     depths censored. This is (1)'s arithmetic read back off a
     whole-group column, a check of the corollary and not separate
     evidence: the content is the ladder theorem's. The depths reach
     x_j = 3 at Z_2 for j = 2, 7 at Q_2(i) for j = 3, 4 at x^2 + 3 for
     j = 2, and 2 at x^3 + 2 for j = 2. The staircase holds at Z_3, Z_7,
     the unramified place of degree 2 over 2 and x^2 - 3, and departs at
     the six headed places and at x^3 + 2, whose ladder is 1, 2, 4, 7
     against 1, 4, 7, 10.
  D  Over the 1619 squarefree delta, |delta| <= 2000, with 2 ramified:
     the door is 5 at all 404 fields with delta = 2 mod 8 and all 404
     with delta = 6, 6 at all 405 with delta = 3 and 7 at all 406 with
     delta = 7; it equals the ladder's x_3 at all 1619; units of level
     >= 2 never go below 7. So w = 1, 1, 2, 3 by residue. 64800 unit
     readings, eta^4 = 1 exactly among them, sat at or above v = 10 and
     were set aside; every door is below it.
  F  At p = 2 the least carrier norms for j = 1..6 are 3, 5, 9, 17, 97, 193:
     the floor p^j + 1 at j = 1..4, not at 5. At p = 3 they are 4, 19, 109,
     163, ..., at p = 7 they are 8, 197, ...; eleven cells, at p >= 11 and the
     top j, print None, no prime power below 2 000 000 qualifying. Of the nine
     primes below 24, residue degree 3 is cheaper at exactly 7
     (norm 8 against 29) and 13 (27 against 53); at 19 it first carries at 343
     against 191.
  Tiers: (1) and (2) a theorem, corollaries of the door theorem and the
  ladder theorem, their proof the argument; (3) a theorem, its proof the
  case analysis, which is finite and complete for every delta; (4) a
  property for the floor, the table a rule in range.

RUN RECORD. 11/11, 12.6 s wall, peak commit 21.9 MB under a memory guard.
After the run the proof's letters were traded into ones the clock
family leaves free (delta for the field, eta = sigma + tau sqrt delta
for a unit, i for its level, gamma, ell), since d, r, c, T, u and x_j
are spent there; the rerun printed the same 11/11 and no number moved.

import os
os.environ.setdefault("OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS", "1")

from math import isqrt

from module_law import Local, check, section, CHECKS, vp
from clock import criterion, ladder

K = 5                  # units sigma + tau sqrt delta, (sigma, tau) mod 2^K
CENSOR = 2 * K
BOUND = 2000           # the range of d in section_door
SMALL = 60             # the range of d in the valuation control
SCAN = 2_000_000       # the prime-power scan of section_floor
JMAX = 4               # counts a carrier is read at

CONSUMERS = [
    Local(2, [0, 1], "unr", "Z_2"),
    Local(3, [0, 1], "unr", "Z_3"),
    Local(7, [0, 1], "unr", "Z_7"),
    Local(2, [1, 1, 1], "unr", "unramified f=2 at 2"),
    Local(2, [2, 0, 1], "eis", "x^2 + 2"),
    Local(2, [-2, 0, 1], "eis", "x^2 - 2"),
    Local(2, [2, -2, 1], "eis", "Q_2(i)"),
    Local(2, [6, -2, 1], "eis", "Q_2(sqrt -5)"),
    Local(3, [3, 0, 1], "eis", "x^2 + 3"),
    Local(3, [-3, 0, 1], "eis", "x^2 - 3"),
    Local(2, [2, 0, 0, 1], "eis", "x^3 + 2"),
]
STAIRCASE = {"Z_3", "Z_7", "unramified f=2 at 2", "x^2 - 3"}
DOOR_BY_RESIDUE = {2: 5, 6: 5, 3: 6, 7: 7}
QI_COLUMN = [0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3]


# ------------------------------------------------------------- a place's door
def members(loc, need):
    """The first `need` ladder members x_1 < x_2 < ... (clock.py)."""
    D = 8
    while True:
        lad = ladder(loc, D)
        if len(lad) >= need:
            return lad[:need]
        D *= 2


def full_column(loc, D):
    """lambda(P^b) for b = 0..D off the WHOLE group U_1/U_b."""
    lam = [1]
    for b in range(1, D + 1):
        lam.append((loc.q - 1) * loc.E_full(b))
    return lam


def door(lam, a, L):
    """Least r >= 1 with lambda(P^(a + r)) not dividing L, or None past
    the column read."""
    for b in range(a + 1, len(lam)):
        if L % lam[b]:
            return b - a
    return None


def section_control():
    section("C. CONTROL: the column off the whole group, v_P, the identity")
    off = 0
    for loc in CONSUMERS:
        D = members(loc, JMAX + 1)[-1] + 1
        for b in range(1, D + 1):
            if loc.E_gen(b) != loc.E_full(b):
                off += 1
    check("E_gen = E_full at every consumer place to its depth", off == 0,
          f"{off} off over {len(CONSUMERS)} places")
    bad = seen = 0
    for d in ramified(SMALL):
        loc = eisenstein(d)
        for x in range(-6, 7):
            for y in range(-6, 7):
                n = x * x - d * y * y
                if n == 0 or vp(n, 2) >= 12:
                    continue
                z = [x, y] if d % 4 == 2 else [x + y, y]
                seen += 1
                if loc.val(z, 8) != vp(n, 2):
                    bad += 1
    check("v_P = v_2(N) at every sampled element, |delta| <= 60",
          bad == 0,
          f"{seen} elements, {bad} off")
    bad = seen = 0
    for d in (-1, 2, 3, -5, 7, -6, 10, -21):
        for x in range(-9, 10):
            for y in range(-9, 10):
                a, b = x, y
                for _ in range(2):
                    a, b = a * a + d * b * b, 2 * a * b
                n4 = (a - 1) ** 2 - d * b * b
                N, T = x * x - d * y * y, 2 * x
                seen += 1
                if n4 != (N - T + 1) * (N + T + 1) * ((N - 1) ** 2 + T * T):
                    bad += 1
    check("N(eta^4 - 1) = (N - Tr + 1)(N + Tr + 1)((N - 1)^2 + Tr^2)",
          bad == 0, f"{seen} samples, {bad} off")
    col = unit_column(-1, 9)
    print(f"  unit brute column of Q(i) over 2, b = 1..9: {col}")
    check("Q(i)'s column is 0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3", col == QI_COLUMN)


def section_bears():
    section("B. THE DEPTHS A CARRIER BEARS ON, AND THE DOOR IT SETS")
    wrong = censored = 0
    for loc in CONSUMERS:
        x = members(loc, JMAX + 1)
        D = x[-1] + 2
        lam = full_column(loc, D)
        p = loc.p
        rows = []
        for j in range(1, JMAX + 1):
            bears, doors = [], []
            for a in range(1, x[j - 1] + 3):
                own = door(lam, a, lam[a])
                with_q = door(lam, a, lam[a] * p ** j // gcd_p(lam[a], p, j))
                if own is None or with_q is None:
                    censored += 1
                    continue
                if own != with_q:
                    bears.append(a)
                    doors.append(with_q)
            want = list(range(1, x[j - 1] + 1))
            want_doors = [x[j] + 1 - a for a in want]
            ok = bears == want and doors == want_doors
            wrong += not ok
            rows.append(f"j={j}: 1..{max(bears) if bears else 0}"
                        f"{'' if ok else ' OFF'}")
        print(f"  {loc.name:22s} ladder {x}  " + "  ".join(rows))
    check("the carrier bears on exactly 1..x_j, door x_(j+1) + 1 - a",
          wrong == 0, f"{wrong} (place, j) off, {censored} depths censored")
    print()
    print("  the staircase x_j = 1 + (j - 1) e against the ladder:")
    off = 0
    for loc in CONSUMERS:
        x = members(loc, JMAX)
        stair = [1 + (j - 1) * loc.e for j in range(1, JMAX + 1)]
        holds = x == stair
        print(f"    {loc.name:22s} e={loc.e} p={loc.p}  ladder {x}  "
              f"staircase {stair}  {'holds' if holds else 'departs'}")
        want = loc.e <= loc.p - 1 and not criterion(loc)
        off += holds != want or want != (loc.name in STAIRCASE)
    check("the staircase holds exactly at e <= p - 1 without a head",
          off == 0, f"{off} places on the wrong side")


def gcd_p(n, p, j):
    """The p-part of n that p^j already covers, so lcm(n, p^j) is exact."""
    return p ** min(vp(n, p), j)


# --------------------------------------------- the ramified place over 2
def squarefree(n):
    n = abs(n)
    for f in range(2, isqrt(n) + 1):
        if n % (f * f) == 0:
            return False
    return True


def ramified(bound):
    """Squarefree delta != 1, |delta| <= bound, 2 ramified in Q(sqrt delta)."""
    return [d for d in range(-bound, bound + 1)
            if d not in (0, 1) and d % 4 in (2, 3) and squarefree(d)]


def eisenstein(d):
    """The place over 2 of Q(sqrt delta): pi = sqrt delta at delta = 2
    mod 4, pi = sqrt delta - 1 at delta = 3 mod 4."""
    g = [-d, 0, 1] if d % 4 == 2 else [1 - d, 2, 1]
    return Local(2, g, "eis", f"Q_2(sqrt {d})")


def unit_readings(d):
    """(least v_P(eta^4 - 1), least of it over level >= 2, censored)
    over units eta = sigma + tau sqrt delta, (sigma, tau) mod 2^K."""
    best = best2 = None
    cens = 0
    for x in range(2 ** K):
        for y in range(2 ** K):
            N, T = x * x - d * y * y, 2 * x
            if N % 2 == 0:
                continue
            j_num = (x - 1) ** 2 - d * y * y
            n4 = (N - T + 1) * (N + T + 1) * ((N - 1) ** 2 + T * T)
            if n4 == 0:
                cens += 1
                continue
            v = vp(n4, 2)
            if v >= CENSOR:
                cens += 1
                continue
            best = v if best is None else min(best, v)
            if vp(j_num, 2) >= 2:
                best2 = v if best2 is None else min(best2, v)
    return best, best2, cens


def unit_column(d, D):
    """c(b) for b = 1..D: the least t with v_P(eta^(2^t) - 1) >= b at
    every unit eta, off exact powers of representatives mod 2^6."""
    need = [0] * (D + 1)
    for x in range(64):
        for y in range(64):
            if (x * x - d * y * y) % 2 == 0:
                continue
            vals, a, b = [], x, y
            for _ in range(5):
                n = (a - 1) ** 2 - d * b * b
                vals.append(12 if n == 0 else min(12, vp(n, 2)))
                a, b = a * a + d * b * b, 2 * a * b
            for bb in range(1, D + 1):
                t = next(t for t, v in enumerate(vals) if v >= bb)
                need[bb] = max(need[bb], t)
    return need[1:]


def section_door():
    section("D. THE RAMIFIED PLACE OVER 2: THE DOOR IS x_3 = 4 + w")
    fields = ramified(BOUND)
    tally = {r: [] for r in DOOR_BY_RESIDUE}
    off_res = off_x3 = cens = low_seat = 0
    for d in fields:
        best, best2, c = unit_readings(d)
        cens += c
        r = d % 8
        tally[r].append(best)
        off_res += best != DOOR_BY_RESIDUE[r]
        x3 = members(eisenstein(d), 3)[2]
        off_x3 += best != x3
        low_seat += best2 is not None and best2 < 7
    for r in (2, 6, 3, 7):
        vals = sorted(set(tally[r]))
        print(f"  delta = {r} mod 8: {len(tally[r]):4d} fields, doors {vals}")
    top = max(v for vs in tally.values() for v in vs)
    print(f"  {len(fields)} fields, {cens} unit readings censored at "
          f"v >= {CENSOR}; every door below it: {top < CENSOR}")
    check("the door is 5, 6, 7 at delta = 2 or 6, 3, 7 mod 8", off_res == 0,
          f"{off_res} of {len(fields)} off")
    check("the unit brute's door equals the ladder's x_3", off_x3 == 0,
          f"{off_x3} of {len(fields)} off")
    check("units of level >= 2 never go below 7", low_seat == 0,
          f"{low_seat} fields below")
    widths = {}
    for d in fields:
        x = members(eisenstein(d), 3)
        widths.setdefault(d % 8, set()).add(x[2] - 4)
    print("  w = x_3 - 4 by residue: "
          + ", ".join(f"{r}: {sorted(widths[r])}" for r in (2, 6, 3, 7)))


# ------------------------------------------------------ the carrier's norm
def prime_powers(n):
    sieve = bytearray([1]) * (n + 1)
    sieve[0] = sieve[1] = 0
    for i in range(2, isqrt(n) + 1):
        if sieve[i]:
            sieve[i * i::i] = bytearray(len(sieve[i * i::i]))
    primes = [i for i in range(n + 1) if sieve[i]]
    out = set()
    for r in primes:
        q = r
        while q <= n:
            out.add(q)
            q *= r
    return primes, sorted(out)


def section_floor():
    section("F. THE CARRIER'S NORM: THE FLOOR, AND RESIDUE DEGREE 3")
    primes, pps = prime_powers(SCAN)
    small = [p for p in primes if p < 24]
    least = {}
    for p in small:
        for j in range(1, 7):
            m = p ** j
            least[p, j] = next((q for q in pps if q % m == 1), None)
    for p in small:
        print(f"  p = {p:2d}: least carrier norm at j = 1..6: "
              + ", ".join(str(least[p, j]) for j in range(1, 7)))
    floors = [least[2, j] for j in range(1, 6)]
    check("p = 2: 3, 5, 9, 17 at j = 1..4, each p^j + 1, then 97",
          floors == [3, 5, 9, 17, 97])
    cheaper = []
    for p in small:
        lo = min(r ** f for r in primes[:2000] for f in (1, 2)
                 if (r ** f - 1) % p == 0)
        three = min(r ** 3 for r in primes[:200] if (r ** 3 - 1) % p == 0)
        tag = "degree 3 cheaper" if three < lo else ""
        print(f"  p = {p:2d}: residue degree <= 2 from {lo}, "
              f"degree 3 from {three}  {tag}")
        if three < lo:
            cheaper.append((p, three, lo))
    check("degree 3 is cheaper exactly at 7 (8 < 29) and 13 (27 < 53)",
          cheaper == [(7, 8, 29), (13, 27, 53)], f"{cheaper}")
