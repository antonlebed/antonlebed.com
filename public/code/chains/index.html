chains.py

Checks: REDUNDANT: The relative-gradient law (property; rule); REDUNDANT: The chained delay (rule); REDUNDANT: The threshold-miss law (rule); REDUNDANT: The matching law (rule).

Cited on: REDUNDANT.

To run it: manystream.py, poles.py, redundant.py beside it; standard library only.

"""
chains.py -- what a chain of on-line units pays: the escape law, the
threshold-miss law, the matching law, and the led stream spent in one
hop.

QUESTION. manystream.py prices ONE reader of signed-digit streams:
a map f is read at the least L with b^L rho >= Lam |I|. A datapath
wires readers in series, the first unit's output digits being the
next unit's input stream. The literature prices a series by adding the
units' on-line delays (Usman, Ercegovac and Lee, Low-Latency Online
Multiplier with Reduced Activities and Minimized Interconnect for
Inner Product Arrays, J. Signal Processing Systems 95, 2023, section
4.2.2: "for a series of online operations, the overall latency is the
sum of online delays of the corresponding operation"). Four
questions. Can the first unit's choice among legal digits buy the
second a lower delay, or is the sum a floor? Where the sum is beaten,
by what law? What does a chain of many units pay? And when the first
unit runs AHEAD (poles.py: the reciprocal emits before it reads),
does the lead pass down the chain?

THE SETTING. Radix b, digits D = {-a-, ..., a+}, a+- >= 1, slack
rho = a- + a+ + 1 - b >= 1, M+- = a+-/(b - 1), w = M- + M+,
w - 1 = rho/(b - 1), Mh = max(M-, M+). Every stream is a D-stream of
value in [-M-, M+]; after n digits it is confined to a BOX of width
w/b^n. The PRODUCER is x y read at lead 0. Its range is
[-M- M+, max(M-^2, M+^2)], which the root tile at lead 0 holds iff M-^2 <= M+,
that is a-^2 <= a+ (b - 1): the REPRESENTABLE cells. Its floor L1* is the
least L with b^L (w - 1) >= 2 Mh w (manystream: Lam = 2 Mh). The
CONSUMER reads the producer's stream U and a fresh stream z. Units
in series are ALIGNED: the consumer reads digit n of U and of z
together, and a chain at (L1, L2) pays L1 + L2 in the inputs.

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine).
  (1) THE HULL. The consumer sees a prefix of U. What it knows of
      x y is the FIBRE: every value the producer's selector sends to that
      prefix. Its output tiles are intervals, so a tile holds the
      fibre plus a z box iff it holds the fibre's HULL plus that box.
      The hull is an interval inside U's own box, INSET by alpha from
      the box's bottom and beta from its top (in units of the box's
      level). So the producer's selector reaches the consumer only through
      its insets. The step uses that a tile is an interval and
      nothing else; it is the floor's whole scope.
  (2) THE INSET CLAUSE. By manystream's tile lemma the sum consumer
      U + z at delay L2 is dead iff some image strictly contains a
      zone. With U's hull shortened by (alpha, beta) the k = 2 clause
      becomes: dead iff an integer lies in
          ((b^L2 - 2) M+ + beta,  b^L2 - (b^L2 - 2) M- - alpha).
      Its ESCAPE COST E(L2) is the least alpha + beta that empties
      the interval: 0 when it is empty, and otherwise, with the
      integers j_1 < ... < j_r inside, the least over splits of
      (j_i - lo) + (hi - j_(i+1)).
  (3) THE BUDGET. At level n + L1 the product images of the (x, y)
      prefixes have widths up to, and dense below, 2 Mh w / b^L1 in
      level-n units, at every position, and each must sit inside the
      hull its prefix is sent to. Hulls sit at unit spacing, so
      consecutive hulls overlap by at least the widest image:
          alpha + beta <= S(L1) = (w - 1) - 2 Mh w / b^L1,
      nonnegative exactly from L1* on.
  (4) THE ESCAPE LAW. The chain is dead at (L1, L2) when
      E(L2) > S(L1). The deaths are certified exactly by a COVERING
      SEARCH: no assignment of the level-(n + L1) product images to
      level-n hulls, each hull its box inset by an option that empties
      the clause, puts every image inside its hull. A survival needs
      no search where E = 0: both units at their own floors.
  (5) THE NARROWED READER (sufficiency, by the tile lemma). A reader
      into tiles [q - M- + alpha, q + M+ - beta] is a reader on the
      tiles narrowed by alpha below and beta above, with the same integer digits,
      whose children still cover their parent since
      a+- >= (b - 1)(M+- - inset). Its zones have length
      w - 1 - alpha - beta, so it reads f wherever
          b^L (w - 1 - alpha - beta) >= Lam w,
      the input boxes unchanged. A producer so narrowed hands its
      consumer a hull inset by (alpha, beta).
  (6) A PRODUCT CONSUMER z U, U of TOP tau = sup |U| (tau = Mh^2
      behind a product, 2 Mh/b behind a sum read at lead 1), reading
      U through a hull
      inset by eta in total: its image of a z box times a hull is at
      most (tau w + Mh (w - eta))/b^n wide, so by (5) it reads at L2
      wherever tau w + Mh (w - eta) <= b^L2 (w - 1), and keeps for the
      next unit the LEFTOVER
          S2(L2, eta) = (w - 1) - (Lam2 w - Mh eta)/b^L2, Lam2 = tau + Mh.
      Its escape cost is its own threshold's miss read in the
      producer's coordinate: E2(L2) = max(0, Lam2 w - b^L2 (w - 1))/Mh.
  (7) THE THRESHOLD-MISS LAW. At slack 1, w - 1 = w/b, so the
      consumer's floor is 1 when Lam2 <= 1 and 2 when Lam2 > 1, and
      the only pair that can beat the sum is (L1*, L2* - 1). The chain
      reads one below the sum iff
          0 < Lam2 - 1 <= (Mh / w) min(S(L1*), room),
      the threshold missed by at most the producer's budget, bounded
      by its range's room (the audit note below; corrected after
      the run). The
      survival side is (5) and (6), a proof. The death side is (3):
      argued, and certified by (4)'s search where it runs. The root
      Lam2(Mh) = 1 is 1/phi behind a product (Lam2 = Mh + Mh^2) and
      the rational b/(b + 2) behind a sum (Lam2 = Mh (1 + 2/b)); the
      sum head's budget is (w - 1) - (2w - 1)/b^L1, its images on a
      lattice of pitch 1/b^L1.
  (8) THE MATCHING LAW. A chain of units, a head reading fresh streams
      alone, then product consumers numbered from 2, each consumer j
      of top tau_(j-1) = tau_1 Mh^(j-2) and Lam_j = Mh + tau_(j-1),
      priced by (6) link by link: a consumer
      below its floor spends its producer's leftover, and one at its
      floor passes on its own. The least total over every assignment
      is an UPPER bound on the chain's floor, proved by (5); its lower
      half is (3) applied link by link, argued and not certified past
      two units. The hand-attack at slack 1: Lam_j never rises along
      the chain; a unit below its floor sits at L = 1 and keeps less
      than (Mh (w - 1) - w (Lam_j - 1))/b, so two adjacent units both
      below their floors need the second's miss under about Mh^2/b^2
      (the ADJACENT range); and an inset crossing two links changes a
      verdict only inside a range O(1/b^3) wide (the CROSSED range). Outside
      those ranges the least total is the naive sum less a MAXIMUM
      MATCHING of the path's pairwise gains: gains on links sharing no
      unit add, gains sharing a unit do not.
  (9) A LED STREAM IS SPENT IN ONE HOP. Let a unit multiply a fresh
      stream z by a stream r >= 0 (corrected after the run) whose
      range has top tau > 0 (so the map's range is [-M- tau, M+ tau]
      and its scale K = tau). The least lead o has b^(o - 1) < tau,
      so a level-1 cell, of length w b^(o - 1), is shorter than the
      range's w tau. At a negative lookahead the first digit is
      committed before any digit of z,
      and must hold the whole range: dead. So such a unit reads at
      c >= 0 whatever r's producer did, and its own output is an
      ordinary stream. The reciprocal's lead is not lost, though: a
      consumer reading r as deep as the reciprocal runs ahead, d =
      -c1* digits past z, sees r's box b^d times narrower, and by (5)
      reads wherever (tau + Mh b^-d) w <= b^L (w - 1), against
      (tau + Mh) w for the aligned consumer.

THE DESIGN, frozen before the engine.
  CA THE CONTROLS. (i) The inset clause at zero insets equals
     manystream.clause_dead at k = 2 at the 20 cells of radices 2..5
     (manystream.census) and L2 = 0..3. (ii) The inset clause against
     an exact zone scan: U's hulls nested (a prefix's hull its own
     inset box cut to its parent's), every (U, z) prefix pair to
     depth 4 at radices 2..4, L2 in {1, 2}, insets on a grid of
     sixths and tenths of the slack w - 1; the scan's death verdict
     equals the clause's at every triple. (iii) The narrowed reader
     (5) at zero insets reproduces manystream's product floor at the
     representable cells.
  CB THE ESCAPE LAW at the 20 cells. Per cell: L1*, L2* (the k = 2
     clause), the naive sum, the escape law's least L1 + L2 over
     L1 in [L1*, L1* + 5], L2 in [0, 5], and the FUSED floor of
     x y + z (manystream's law, Lam = 2 Mh + 1, at its own lead). The
     covering search at every pair with L1 >= L1* and L1 + L2 below
     the naive sum, radices 2..4, n = 1..3.
  CC THE THRESHOLD-MISS LAW at every slack-1 cell of radices 2..40,
     both consumers, each at its representable cells (the product
     consumer where Mh^2 fits the root tile at lead 0; the sum-fed where 2 Mh^2
     <= b M+ and its mirror): the least L1 + L2 by (6) over
     L1 in [L1*, L1* + 5], L2 in [0, 5], against the criterion (7).
     And every cell of slack 2 or more to radix 40 read the same way.
  CD THE MATCHING LAW. A dynamic programme over a chain's units,
     keeping the largest leftover at each (total, below-floor run)

  CE THE LED STREAM at every reciprocal pair of poles.py (the 20

PREDICTIONS.

     consumers; the product-fed gains at 17 cells, all at (2, 1):
     (4,2,2), (9,4,5), (12,5,7), (17,7,10), (22,9,13), (25,10,15),
     (30,12,18), (33,13,20), (38,15,23) and the mirrors of all but
     the symmetric first; the sum-fed gains at (b, 2, b - 2) and its
     mirror at every radix 5..40 and nowhere else; no cell of slack 2
     or more gains to radix 40.
  PD outside both ranges the programme reads naive - nu at every
     cell; the five-unit sum-fed chain at (9, 2, 7) reads 7 against
     the matching's 8, an adjacent pair; the sum-fed four-unit chain
     at (b, 2, b - 2) reads naive - 2 at every radix 8..40.
  PE c2 >= 0 at every pair; the eager L below the aligned at 25 of
     the 40 pairs; the fused divider at or below the chain at all 40.

KILLS, as what the script prints.
  K1 a CA line off: the clause or the scan is wrong; nothing below is
     read.
  K2 the escape law's floor below the naive sum at some cell, or the
     covering search finding an assignment at n = 3 at a pair the law
     calls dead: the sum is not the aligned chain's floor there.
  K3 a slack-1 cell where the search and the criterion disagree: (7)
     is wrong.
  K4 a cell outside both ranges where the programme is not
     naive - nu: (8) is wrong.
  K5 c2 < 0 at a pair: (9) is wrong.

POSITIVE CONTROL: CA, read before any verdict line.

FINDINGS (entered after the run; every number is a print).
  - PA holds: the inset clause at zero insets is the k = 2 clause at

import math
import time
from fractions import Fraction as Fr

import manystream as ms
import poles

CHECKS = []
IMG_CAP = 400_000


def check(name, ok, detail=""):
    CHECKS.append(bool(ok))
    tail = f"  ({detail})" if detail else ""
    print(f"  {'PASS' if ok else 'FAIL'}  {name}{tail}")


def cells(bmin, bmax, slack=None):
    for b in range(bmin, bmax + 1):
        for am in range(1, b):
            for ap in range(1, b):
                rho = am + ap + 1 - b
                if rho >= 1 and (slack is None or rho == slack):
                    yield ms.Cell(b, am, ap)


def representable(cell):
    return cell.am ** 2 <= cell.ap * (cell.b - 1)


# ---- (2) the inset clause and its escape cost ----

def clause_ends(cell, L2):
    B = Fr(cell.b) ** L2
    return (B - 2) * cell.Mp, B - (B - 2) * cell.Mm


def clause_dead(cell, L2, alpha, beta):
    lo, hi = clause_ends(cell, L2)
    lo, hi = lo + beta, hi - alpha
    return math.floor(lo) + 1 < hi


def escape_options(cell, L2):
    """The minimal (alpha, beta) emptying the clause; [(0, 0)] when it
    is already empty."""
    lo, hi = clause_ends(cell, L2)
    ints = [j for j in range(math.floor(lo) + 1, math.ceil(hi))
            if lo < j < hi]
    if not ints:
        return [(Fr(0), Fr(0))]
    opts = []
    for i in range(len(ints) + 1):
        beta = ints[i - 1] - lo if i > 0 else Fr(0)
        alpha = hi - ints[i] if i < len(ints) else Fr(0)
        opts.append((alpha, beta))
    return opts


def escape_cost(cell, L2):
    return min(a + c for a, c in escape_options(cell, L2))


def sum_floor(cell):
    L = 1
    while clause_dead(cell, L, 0, 0):
        L += 1
    return L


# ---- (3) the budgets, (5) the narrowed reader ----

def budget_xy(cell, L1):
    return cell.w - 1 - 2 * cell.Mh * cell.w / Fr(cell.b) ** L1


def budget_sum(cell, L1):
    return cell.w - 1 - (2 * cell.w - 1) / Fr(cell.b) ** L1


def floor_xy(cell):
    L = 0
    while budget_xy(cell, L) < 0:
        L += 1
    return L


def narrowed_L(cell, lam, inset):
    """(5): least L with b^L (w - 1 - inset) >= lam w, or None."""
    z = cell.w - 1 - inset
    if z <= 0:
        return None
    L = -40
    while Fr(cell.b) ** L * z < lam * cell.w:
        L += 1
    return L


# ---- the zone scan of the inset clause ----

def zone_scan_dead(cell, L2, alpha, beta, nmax=3):
    """Every nested hull of U to depth nmax plus every z box over a
    period of prefix values: does an image strictly contain a zone?"""
    b, Mm, Mp = cell.b, cell.Mm, cell.Mp
    D = range(-cell.am, cell.ap + 1)
    level = {(0, (-Mm + alpha, Mp - beta))}
    period = b ** L2
    for n in range(1, nmax + 1):
        s = Fr(1, b ** n)
        new = set()
        for p, (plo, phi) in level:
            for e in D:
                q = b * p + e
                lo = max((q - Mm + alpha) * s, plo)
                hi = min((q + Mp - beta) * s, phi)
                if lo < hi:
                    new.add((q, (lo, hi)))
        level = new
        if n < L2:
            continue
        hulls = {h for _, h in level}
        umin = -cell.am * poles_repunit(b, n)
        for (hlo, hhi) in hulls:
            for u in range(umin, umin + period + 1):
                img = (hlo + (u - Mm) * s, hhi + (u + Mp) * s)
                if cell.kills(img, n, L2):
                    return True
    return False


def poles_repunit(b, n):
    return (b ** n - 1) // (b - 1)


# ---- (4) the covering search ----

def product_images(cell, m):
    s = Fr(1, cell.b ** m)
    R = poles_repunit(cell.b, m)
    boxes = [((u - cell.Mm) * s, (u + cell.Mp) * s)
             for u in range(-cell.am * R, cell.ap * R + 1)]
    out = set()
    for x0, x1 in boxes:
        for y0, y1 in boxes:
            c = (x0 * y0, x0 * y1, x1 * y0, x1 * y1)
            out.add((min(c), max(c)))
    return sorted(out)


def covering_exists(cell, L1, L2, n):
    """True, False, or None past the cap: can the level-(n + L1)
    product images be assigned to level-n hulls, each its box inset
    by an escape option, every image inside its hull?"""
    m = n + L1
    if (cell.am + cell.ap + 1) ** (2 * m) > IMG_CAP:
        return None
    imgs = product_images(cell, m)
    opts = sorted(set(escape_options(cell, L2)))
    s = Fr(1, cell.b ** n)
    qs = list(range(math.floor(imgs[0][0] / s - cell.Mp) - 1,
                    math.ceil(imgs[-1][1] / s + cell.Mm) + 2))

    def walk(i, rest):
        if not rest:
            return True
        if i == len(qs):
            return False
        q = qs[i]
        if rest[0][0] < (q - cell.Mm) * s:
            return False
        for a, c in opts:
            hl, hr = (q - cell.Mm + a) * s, (q + cell.Mp - c) * s
            left = [iv for iv in rest if not (hl <= iv[0] and iv[1] <= hr)]
            if len(left) < len(rest) and walk(i + 1, left):
                return True
        return walk(i + 1, rest)

    return walk(0, sorted(imgs))


# ---- (6), (7) a product consumer ----

def consumer_escape(cell, A, L2):
    lam2 = A + cell.Mh
    miss = lam2 * cell.w - Fr(cell.b) ** L2 * (cell.w - 1)
    return max(Fr(0), miss) / cell.Mh


def xy_head(cell):
    """(the largest magnitude tau_1, the head's floor, its budget
    bounded by its room, its range) behind a product."""
    rng = (-cell.Mm * cell.Mp, max(cell.Mm, cell.Mp) ** 2)
    r = room(cell, rng)
    return (cell.Mh ** 2, floor_xy(cell),
            lambda L: min(budget_xy(cell, L), r), rng)


def sum_head(cell):
    rng = (-2 * cell.Mm / cell.b, 2 * cell.Mp / cell.b)
    r = room(cell, rng)
    return (2 * cell.Mh / cell.b, sum_floor(cell),
            lambda L: min(budget_sum(cell, L), r), rng)


def room(cell, rng):
    """The most a narrowed reader may inset in total and still hold
    the range [lo, hi] in its root tile at lead 0."""
    return (cell.Mm + rng[0]) + (cell.Mp - rng[1])


def times_z(cell, rng):
    c = [z * p for z in (-cell.Mm, cell.Mp) for p in rng]
    return (min(c), max(c))


def sum_fed_ok(cell):
    return 2 * cell.Mh ** 2 <= cell.b * cell.Mp


def two_unit(cell, head):
    """(naive, least, the consumer's Lam2, the head's floor budget)."""
    A, L1s, S, _ = head(cell)
    lam2 = A + cell.Mh
    L2s = 0
    while consumer_escape(cell, A, L2s) > 0:
        L2s += 1
    least = L1s + L2s
    for L1 in range(L1s, L1s + 6):
        for L2 in range(0, 6):
            if consumer_escape(cell, A, L2) <= S(L1):
                least = min(least, L1 + L2)
    return L1s + L2s, least, lam2, S(L1s)


# ---- (8) the chain programme ----

def chain_units(cell, head, n):
    A1, L1s, S, rng = head(cell)
    lams = [cell.Mh + A1 * cell.Mh ** (j - 2) for j in range(2, n + 1)]
    rooms = []
    for _ in lams:
        rng = times_z(cell, rng)
        rooms.append(room(cell, rng))
    return L1s, S, lams, rooms


def leftover(cell, lam, L, inset):
    scale = Fr(cell.b) ** L
    return cell.w - 1 - (lam * cell.w - cell.Mh * inset) / scale


def unit_floor(cell, lam):
    L = 0
    while leftover(cell, lam, L, 0) < 0:
        L += 1
    return L


def pareto(states):
    """Drop every (total, leftover) another beats on both."""
    out, best = {}, Fr(-1)
    for t in sorted(states):
        if states[t] > best:
            out[t] = best = states[t]
    return out


def programme_all(cell, head, nmax):
    """For every chain length n = 3..nmax, the prefix of one chain:
    {n: (naive, matching, least total, floors, Lams)}. A chain of n
    units is the first n units of the chain of nmax, so one pass
    prices every length."""
    L1s, S, lams, rooms = chain_units(cell, head, nmax)
    floors = [unit_floor(cell, lam) for lam in lams]
    states = {}
    for L1 in range(L1s, L1s + 3):
        if S(L1) >= 0:
            states[L1] = max(S(L1), states.get(L1, Fr(-1)))
    states = pareto(states)
    naive, up = L1s, S(L1s)
    take, skip = -1, 0
    out = {}
    for j, (lam, Ls) in enumerate(zip(lams, floors)):
        new = {}
        for t, v in states.items():
            for L in range(max(0, Ls - 1), Ls + 3):
                r = leftover(cell, lam, L, min(v, cell.w - 1))
                if r >= 0:
                    r = min(r, rooms[j])
                if r >= 0 and r > new.get(t + L, Fr(-1)):
                    new[t + L] = r
        states = pareto(new)
        g = Ls >= 1 and leftover(cell, lam, Ls - 1, up) >= 0
        up = min(leftover(cell, lam, Ls, 0), rooms[j])
        take, skip = (skip + 1 if g else -1), max(take, skip)
        naive += Ls
        n = j + 2
        if n >= 3:
            out[n] = (naive, naive - max(take, skip), min(states),
                      floors[:j + 1], lams[:j + 1])
    return out


def programme(cell, head, n):
    return programme_all(cell, head, n)[n]


def in_ranges(cell, lams, floors):
    """Does the chain carry an adjacent-range pair or a crossed link?"""
    w, Mh, b = cell.w, cell.Mh, cell.b
    for j in range(len(lams) - 1):
        l1, l2 = lams[j], lams[j + 1]
        keep = Mh * (Mh * (w - 1) - w * (l1 - 1)) / b
        if l1 > 1 and l2 > 1 and w * (l2 - 1) < keep:
            return "adjacent"
    for j in range(1, len(lams)):
        lo = leftover(cell, lams[j - 1], floors[j - 1], 0)
        hi = leftover(cell, lams[j - 1], floors[j - 1], w - 1)
        if Mh * lo < w * (lams[j] - 1) < Mh * hi:
            return "crossed"
    return None

def section_ca():
    print("CA  the controls")
    off = 0
    for cell in cells(2, 5):
        for L in range(1, 5):
            off += clause_dead(cell, L, 0, 0) != ms.clause_dead(
                cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap, 2, L)
    check("CA(i) the inset clause at zero insets is the k = 2 clause",
          off == 0, f"80 (cell, L), {off} off")
    fr = [Fr(0), Fr(1, 10), Fr(1, 6), Fr(1, 3), Fr(1, 2)]
    tot, off, where = 0, 0, []
    for cell in cells(2, 4):
        for L2 in (1, 2):
            for fa in fr:
                for fb in fr:
                    a, c = fa * (cell.w - 1), fb * (cell.w - 1)
                    tot += 1
                    if zone_scan_dead(cell, L2, a, c) != clause_dead(
                            cell, L2, a, c):
                        off += 1
                        where.append((cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap, L2,
                                      str(fa), str(fb)))
    check("CA(ii) the zone scan equals the inset clause", off == 0,
          f"{tot} triples, {off} off {where[:4]}")
    off = 0
    for cell in cells(2, 5):
        if representable(cell):
            lam, o, Ls = ms.law_of(cell, ms.MAPS[2])
            off += narrowed_L(cell, 2 * cell.Mh, 0) != Ls - o
    check("CA(iii) the narrowed reader at zero insets is the product's "
          "floor", off == 0, f"{off} off")


def section_cb():
    print("CB  the escape law at the 20 cells")
    below, fused_cmp, rep, capped, found = [], {}, 0, [], []
    pairs = 0
    for cell in cells(2, 5):
        L1s, L2s = floor_xy(cell), sum_floor(cell)
        naive = L1s + L2s
        least = naive
        for L1 in range(L1s, L1s + 6):
            for L2 in range(1, 6):
                if escape_cost(cell, L2) <= budget_xy(cell, L1):
                    least = min(least, L1 + L2)
        if least < naive:
            below.append((cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap))
        lam, o, Lf = ms.law_of(cell, ms.MAPS[4])
        tag = "rep" if representable(cell) else "led"
        rep += tag == "rep"
        rel = "below" if Lf < naive else (
            "equal" if Lf == naive else "above")
        fused_cmp.setdefault(tag, {}).setdefault(rel, 0)
        fused_cmp[tag][rel] += 1
        print(f"    ({cell.b},{cell.am},{cell.ap}) {tag}: L1* {L1s}, "
              f"L2* {L2s}, naive {naive}, escape law {least}, fused "
              f"{Lf} (lead {o})")
        if cell.b > 4:
            continue
        for L1 in range(L1s, naive):
            for L2 in range(1, naive - L1):
                if escape_cost(cell, L2) <= budget_xy(cell, L1):
                    continue
                pairs += 1
                verdict = None
                for n in (1, 2, 3):
                    verdict = covering_exists(cell, L1, L2, n)
                    if verdict is not True:
                        break
                if verdict is None:
                    capped.append((cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap, L1, L2, n))
                elif verdict:
                    found.append((cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap, L1, L2))
                elif n > 1:
                    print(f"      ({cell.b},{cell.am},{cell.ap}) at "
                          f"({L1}, {L2}): no covering at n = {n}")
    print(f"    fused against the chain: {fused_cmp}; representable "
          f"{rep}; law-dead pairs searched {pairs}, capped {capped}")
    check("CB the escape law never below the naive sum", not below,
          f"below at {below}")
    check("CB no covering at any law-dead pair",
          not found and not capped,
          f"found {found}, capped {capped}")


def section_cc():
    print("CC  the threshold-miss law, radices 2..40")
    off, gains, deep = [], {"xy": [], "sum": []}, []
    bound = {"xy": 0, "sum": 0}
    unfunded = 0
    for cell in cells(2, 40):
        for name, head, ok in (("xy", xy_head, representable),
                               ("sum", sum_head, sum_fed_ok)):
            if not ok(cell):
                continue
            naive, least, lam2, S0 = two_unit(cell, head)
            if S0 < 0:
                unfunded += 1
                continue
            _, L1s, _, rng = head(cell)
            raw = (budget_xy if name == "xy" else budget_sum)(cell, L1s)
            if room(cell, rng) < raw:
                bound[name] += 1
            if cell.rho >= 2:
                if least < naive:
                    deep.append((name, cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap))
                continue
            law = 0 < lam2 - 1 <= cell.Mh / cell.w * S0
            if law != (least < naive):
                off.append((name, cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap))
            if least < naive:
                gains[name].append((cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap))
    for name, g in gains.items():
        more = " ..." if len(g) > 12 else ""
        print(f"    {name}-fed: {len(g)} gaining cells at "
              f"{len({c[0] for c in g})} radices: {g[:12]}{more}")
    print(f"    the range's room below the budget at the head's floor: "
          f"{bound}; heads with no budget at their floor, skipped: "
          f"{unfunded}")
    check("CC the search equals the criterion at every slack-1 cell",
          not off, f"off at {off[:6]}")
    want = [(4, 2, 2)]
    for b, am, ap in ((9, 4, 5), (12, 5, 7), (17, 7, 10), (22, 9, 13),
                      (25, 10, 15), (30, 12, 18), (33, 13, 20),
                      (38, 15, 23)):
        want += [(b, am, ap), (b, ap, am)]
    check("CC the product-fed gains are the 17 predicted cells",
          sorted(gains["xy"]) == sorted(want),
          f"read {len(gains['xy'])}")
    want = sorted([(b, 2, b - 2) for b in range(5, 41)]
                  + [(b, b - 2, 2) for b in range(5, 41)])
    check("CC the sum-fed gains are (b, 2, b - 2) and mirrors, 5..40",
          sorted(gains["sum"]) == want, f"read {len(gains['sum'])}")
    check("CC no cell of slack >= 2 gains", not deep, f"at {deep[:6]}")


def section_cd():
    print("CD  the matching law")
    t0 = time.time()
    outside, inside, reads, unending = [], {}, 0, 0
    for cell in cells(3, 40, slack=1):
        for name, head, ok in (("xy", xy_head, representable),
                               ("sum", sum_head, sum_fed_ok)):
            if not ok(cell):
                continue
            A1, L1s, S, _ = head(cell)
            if S(L1s) < 0:
                continue
            ns = set(range(3, 13))
            if cell.Mh == 1:
                unending += 1
            elif cell.b <= 24:
                run, top = 0, A1
                while cell.Mh + top > 1:
                    run, top = run + 1, top * cell.Mh
                ns.add(max(3, run + 2))
            every = programme_all(cell, head, max(ns))
            for n in sorted(ns):
                naive, match, least, floors, lams = every[n]
                reads += 1
                if least != match:
                    win = in_ranges(cell, lams, floors)
                    key = (name, cell.b, cell.am, cell.ap, n)
                    if win is None:
                        outside.append(key + (naive, match, least))
                    else:
                        inside[key] = (win, match - least)
    print(f"    {reads} chains read, {time.time() - t0:.1f} s; whole run "
          f"unread at {unending} (cell, head) with Mh = 1; off the "
          f"matching inside the ranges at {len(inside)}: "
          f"{sorted(set(inside.values()))}")
    check("CD naive - nu outside both ranges", not outside,
          f"off at {outside[:4]}")
    naive, match, least, *_ = programme(ms.Cell(9, 2, 7), sum_head, 5)
    print(f"    (9, 2, 7) sum-fed, five units: naive {naive}, matching "
          f"{match}, least {least}")
    check("CD the witness reads 7 against the matching's 8",
          (match, least) == (8, 7))
    off = []
    for b in range(8, 41):
        for cell in (ms.Cell(b, 2, b - 2), ms.Cell(b, b - 2, 2)):
            naive, match, least, *_ = programme(cell, sum_head, 4)
            if least != naive - 2:
                off.append((b, cell.am, cell.ap, naive, least))
    check("CD the sum-fed four-unit chain reads naive - 2, radices 8..40",
          not off, f"off at {off[:4]}")


def section_ce():
    print("CE  the led stream")
    rows = []
    for cell, h in poles.pairs():
        u = poles.Recip(cell, h)
        c1 = u.law_L() - u.lead()
        if c1 >= 0:
            continue
        R = Fr(1) / (Fr(cell.b) ** u.lead() * h)
        o2 = poles.least_pow(cell.b, R)
        La = narrowed_L(cell, R + cell.Mh, 0)
        Le = narrowed_L(cell, R + cell.Mh / Fr(cell.b) ** (-c1), 0)
        dv = poles.Divider(cell, h)
        cd = dv.law_L() - dv.lead()
        rows.append((La - o2, Le - o2, cd))
    eb = sum(1 for a, e, _ in rows if e < a)
    fz = sum(1 for _, e, c in rows if c <= e)
    fe = sum(1 for _, e, c in rows if c == e)
    print(f"    {len(rows)} pairs; eager below aligned at {eb}; the fused "
          f"divider at or below the eager chain at {fz}, equal at {fe}")
    check("CE 40 pairs, eager below aligned at 25",
          len(rows) == 40 and eb == 25, f"read {len(rows)}, {eb}")
    check("CE the fused divider at or below at every pair",
          fz == len(rows))


def main():
    t0 = time.time()
    section_ca()
    if not all(CHECKS):
        print("\nCONTROL FAILED: nothing below is read.")
        raise SystemExit(1)
    section_cb()
    section_cc()
    section_cd()
    section_ce()
    print(f"{sum(CHECKS)}/{len(CHECKS)} checks passed, "
          f"{time.time() - t0:.1f} s")
    raise SystemExit(0 if all(CHECKS) else 1)


if __name__ == "__main__":
