tower_growth.py

Checks: TOWER: The density theorem (theorem; observation); TOWER: The serial ledger (rule).

Cited on: TOWER.

To run it: crt.py beside it; standard library only.

"""tower_growth.py -- how lambda grows along the primorial rungs: the
density of transparent rungs, the ledger of lambda's jumps, and the
serial law that each jump raises one base.

QUESTION. lambda(k) = lcm(p_1 - 1, ..., p_k - 1) is the exponent of the
unit group of the rung Z/p_k#. A rung k >= 2 is TRANSPARENT when
lambda(k) = lambda(k - 1), i.e. (p_k - 1) | lambda(k - 1). Does the
share of transparent rungs tend to 1, how fast, and what does a jump of
lambda look like when one happens?

THE PROOF THE CHECKS ARE BUILT AROUND (by hand; x = p_k throughout).
Write L(x) = log lambda(k) = sum over primes q of a_q log q, a_q the
largest exponent of q dividing some p - 1 with p <= x.
  (A) Exponents above 1 need q^2 < x, so they cost at most
      pi(sqrt x) log x = O(sqrt x).
  (B) The primes q <= x/log x cost at most theta(x/log x) ~ x/log x.
  (C) A prime q > x/log x dividing p - 1 < x is P+(p - 1), the largest
      prime factor, with cofactor m = (p - 1)/q < log x, so this part
      costs at most log x times #{p <= x : P+(p - 1) > x/log x}. That
      set is covered by the pairs (q, mq + 1) both prime with
      m < log x, and the upper-bound sieve for prime pairs counts it
      as << x loglog x/(log x)^2 (the sum of 1/phi(m) over m < log x
      gives the loglog, the singular series averaging to a constant).
      Both primality conditions are needed; either alone leaves x loglog x.
  So L(x) << x loglog x/log x.
  THE WRITE-ONCE BOUND. Every non-transparent rung raises the exponent
  of at least one prime q (an EVENT at q). Exponents only climb and
  q^a | p - 1 < x, so q hosts at most floor(log x/log q) events; an
  event at q > y adds at least log y to L. Hence, for every y,
      N_nt(k) <= sum_{q <= y} floor(log x/log q) + L(x)/log y,
  N_nt(k) being the non-transparent rungs among 2..k. With
  y = x/(log x)^3 the first term is O(x/(log x)^3) and the second is
  << x loglog x/(log x)^2, so N_nt(k)/k << loglog x/log x -> 0.
  THE CYCLICITY RATIO. Each step adds at most log(p - 1) to L
  (the new lambda divides the old one times p - 1), so
  L <= N_nt log x; with log phi(p_k#) = sum log(p - 1)
  = theta(x) - O(loglog x) and k log x/log phi -> 1, alpha = L/log phi
  is at most N_nt/k (1 + o(1)). Both ratios are nonnegative and
  O(loglog x/log x), so N_nt/k - alpha = O(loglog x/log x) as well, and
  says no more than that both vanish. The write-once bound caps N_nt
  from above and gives no lower bound on alpha.

THE SERIAL LAW'S CHARACTERIZATION (by hand). Let f(m) be the least
prime = 1 (mod m). The prime p raises base q iff v_q(p - 1) exceeds
v_q(p' - 1) for every prime p' < p, iff no prime below p is 1 mod
q^h with h = v_q(p - 1), iff p = f(q^h); and p = f(q^j) for any j >= 1
forces p = f(q^h) (q^j | q^h). So p raises q iff p lies in the RECORD
SET R_q = {f(q^j) : j >= 1}, and "every jump raises exactly one base"
holds iff the record sets are pairwise disjoint. A DOUBLE BIRTH, a
prime in two record sets, has witnesses m1, m2 of distinct bases with
m1 m2 | p - 1, so the smaller is at most sqrt(p - 1): a search that
takes every prime power m <= X, sets p = f(m), factors t = (p - 1)/m
and tests p against f(r^{v_r(t)}) for each prime r | t other than m's
base is complete for every double birth with p - 1 < X^2. The ratio
p / f(r^{v_r(t)}) is the MARGIN; margin 1 is a double birth.

DESIGN. Standard library and crt.py.
  W  one walk over every prime p <= 10^7 in order (k to 664,579),
     factoring p - 1 by trial division and keeping lambda as a map of
     running maximum exponents. Per step: the events, the increment
     of L, log(p - 1). At rung milestones k = 50, 200, 1000, 2000,
     10^4, 10^5 and the last: the transparent share over rungs 2..k,
     alpha, N_nt/k and their gap, the write-once bound at
     y = x^(1/2), x^(2/3), x^0.9 and x/(log x)^3 wherever y > 2 (the
     cell y = x/(log x)^3 at k = 50 has y = 1.43 and is skipped), and
     the events at
     q <= sqrt x. At x milestones 10^4, 10^5, 10^6, 10^7: L/x, the
     rate r = L log x/(x loglog x), and the step (C) census
     #{p <= x : P+(p - 1) > x/log x} against x loglog x/(log x)^2.
     The ledger: L at x = 10^5, the running sum of the increments read
     off the factorizations, against log lcm{p - 1 : p <= 10^5} computed
     by lcm directly; steps raising two or more bases counted. (That an
     increment is at most log(p - 1) holds by construction and prints.)
  S  the serial law. POSITIVE CONTROL first: the same search with 5
     and 7 deleted from the primes must report a double birth at 13
     (13 is then the least prime = 1 mod 3 and the least = 1 mod 4).
     Then a direct walk to
     k = 2000 against the record test, rung by rung. Then the search
     to X (argument; default 10^6, complete to p < 10^12; the record
     run takes X = 10^8, complete to 10^16), printing the 20 smallest
     margins. Primality by Miller-Rabin with bases 2..17, exact below
     3.4 * 10^14; the search asserts it stays under that.

PREDICTIONS, fixed before the run (the W numbers at k <= 10^4 and x <=
10^7 are those of the scripts this one replaces; the rest are new).
  W1 transparent share 23/49 at k = 50, 1531/1999 at 2000, 8013/9999
     at 10^4, rising at every rung milestone and between 0.82 and 0.88
     at the last.
  W2 N_nt/k and alpha: gap positive and falling at every milestone;
     alpha(10^4) = 0.1651, N_nt(10^4) = 1986.
  W3 the write-once bound holds at every (milestone, y) cell; at
     k = 10^4 and y = sqrt x it reads 176 + 2982.4.
  W4 events at q <= sqrt x: 117 of 1986 at k = 10^4, the share falling.
  W5 L/x falls at every x milestone; r lies in [0.74, 0.80] at each;
     census/scale in [0.45, 0.60].
  W6 the ledger identity to 1e-9 relative; every increment at most
     log(p - 1); no step raises two bases, x <= 10^7.
  S1 the control reports a double birth at p = 13.
  S2 walk and record test agree at every rung to 2000: 438 jumps bring
     a new prime and 30 raise an old one.
  S3 no double birth to X; the smallest margin 7/3 at p = 7; the 20
     smallest margins all sit at p < 600, the 20th at 34.6.

FINDINGS. Every prediction landed: 11/11 checks PASS (12/12 after
the audit in RUN RECORD).
  W1 transparent 23 of 49 rungs at k = 50, 1531 of 1999 at 2000, 8013
     of 9999 at 10^4, 83339 at 10^5 (0.8334) and 565513 of 664578 at
     k = 664,579, p_k = 9,999,991 (0.8509).
  W2 N_nt/k against alpha: 0.5200 / 0.2963 at k = 50, 0.1986 / 0.1651
     at 10^4, 0.1491 / 0.1318 at the last; the gap falls 0.2237,
     0.1218, 0.0613, 0.0509, 0.0335, 0.0221, 0.0173.
  W3 the bound holds in every cell. At the last rung, y = sqrt x gives
     1001 + 163434.5 against N_nt = 99065 (ratio 1.66), y = x^(2/3)
     the tightest cell (1.29), y = x/(log x)^3 gives 1.72: the proof's
     choice of y is not the tightest in range, only the one whose
     small term provably vanishes.
  W4 events at q <= sqrt x: 15 of 26 at k = 50, 117 of 1986 at 10^4,
     667 of 99065 at the last.
  W5 L/x 0.1866, 0.1653, 0.1456, 0.1317 at x = 10^4..10^7; r 0.7740,
     0.7787, 0.7660, 0.7637; census/scale 0.485, 0.523, 0.542, 0.557.
     The census ratio creeps upward; the proof needs it bounded, and
     in range it is.
  W6 L = 1317126.096 at x = 10^7, and at x = 10^5 equal to the log of
     the lcm computed directly; no increment above log(p - 1); no step
     raises two bases.
  S1 the control prints DOUBLE BIRTH at 13 (m1 = 4, m2 = 3).
  S2 438 new-prime jumps and 30 raises of an old base to k = 2000,
     walk and record test agreeing at every rung.
  S3 X = 10^6: 78,726 record primes over 78,498 bases, no double
     birth, complete for p - 1 < 10^12. X = 10^8: 5,762,848 record
     primes over 5,761,455 bases, no double birth, complete for
     p - 1 < 10^16; the largest cofactor is 284 (p = 20,829,174,293).
     Smallest margins 7/3 at p = 7, then 11/3, 29/5, 19/3; the 20th is
     34.6, at p = 173.
  Tiers: the density theorem and the cyclicity ratio are
  theorems, the checks a finite chart of their ingredients. The record
  characterization is a criterion, proved. The serial law is a rule,
  verified for every p < 10^16 and open beyond: ruling out a double
  birth at every scale is beyond a search, and no proof that the
  record sets of distinct primes are disjoint is known.

RUN RECORD. Default X = 10^6: 11/11, 2.5 s, 77 MB peak commit under
a memory guard. X = 10^8: 11/11, 89 s, 157 MB peak commit. An audit
replaced W6's telescoping identity with the lcm at x = 10^5
(L = 16526.250 both ways) and asserted W3's 176 + 2982.4: 12/12, 3.1 s,
76 MB peak commit under a memory guard, default X.

Standard library and crt.py.

    python tower_growth.py [X]
"""

import sys
from math import isqrt, lcm, log

from crt import primes_up_to

CHECKS = []


def check(name, ok, detail=""):
    CHECKS.append(bool(ok))
    print(f"  [{'PASS' if ok else 'FAIL'}] {name}" + (f" -- {detail}" if detail else ""))


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ W

X_WALK = 10 ** 7
K_MILES = (50, 200, 1000, 2000, 10 ** 4, 10 ** 5)
X_MILES = (10 ** 4, 10 ** 5, 10 ** 6, 10 ** 7)


def floor_log(x, q):
    """The largest a with q^a <= x."""
    a, v = 0, q
    while v <= x:
        a, v = a + 1, v * q
    return a


def walk():
    primes = primes_up_to(X_WALK)
    trial = primes_up_to(isqrt(X_WALK) + 1)
    k_miles = set(K_MILES) | {len(primes)}
    expo = {}                     # q -> running max exponent in lambda
    L = logphi = 0.0
    n_nt = multi = over = 0       # over: increments above log(p - 1)
    events = []                   # (k, q), one per raised base
    big = []                      # P+(p - 1) per prime, for the census
    rows, xrows = [], []
    xi = 0
    for k, p in enumerate(primes, 1):
        m, fac = p - 1, {}
        for q in trial:
            if q * q > m:
                break
            if m % q == 0:
                e = 0
                while m % q == 0:
                    m //= q
                    e += 1
                fac[q] = e
        if m > 1:
            fac[m] = fac.get(m, 0) + 1
        big.append(max(fac) if fac else 1)
        inc, raised = 0.0, 0
        for q, e in fac.items():
            old = expo.get(q, 0)
            if e > old:
                inc += (e - old) * log(q)
                expo[q] = e
                raised += 1
                events.append((k, q))
        if raised:
            n_nt += 1
            multi += raised > 1
            over += inc > log(p - 1) + 1e-9
        L += inc
        if p > 2:
            logphi += log(p - 1)
        if k in k_miles:
            rows.append((k, p, n_nt, L, logphi))
        while xi < len(X_MILES) and (k == len(primes) or primes[k] > X_MILES[xi]):
            x = X_MILES[xi]
            cut = x / log(x)
            xrows.append((x, L, sum(1 for b in big if b > cut)))
            xi += 1
    return rows, xrows, events, L, over, multi


def section_w():
    print("W -- the density, the ledger and the write-once bound")
    rows, xrows, events, L, over, multi = walk()
    print(f"  {'k':>6} {'p_k':>8} {'trans':>7} {'share':>7} {'N_nt/k':>7} "
          f"{'alpha':>7} {'gap':>7}")
    shares, gaps = [], []
    for k, p, n_nt, Lk, lphi in rows:
        share = (k - 1 - n_nt) / (k - 1)
        gap = n_nt / k - Lk / lphi
        shares.append(share)
        gaps.append(gap)
        print(f"  {k:>6} {p:>8} {k - 1 - n_nt:>7} {share:>7.4f} {n_nt / k:>7.4f} "
              f"{Lk / lphi:>7.4f} {gap:>7.4f}")
    print("  write-once bound: small + large against N_nt")
    cells_ok = True
    for k, p, n_nt, Lk, lphi in rows:
        for label, y in (("x^1/2", p ** 0.5), ("x^2/3", p ** (2 / 3)),
                         ("x^0.9", p ** 0.9), ("x/log^3", p / log(p) ** 3)):
            if y <= 2:
                continue
            small = sum(floor_log(p, q) for q in primes_up_to(int(y)))
            large = Lk / log(y)
            cells_ok &= n_nt <= small + large
            if label == "x^1/2" or k == rows[-1][0]:
                print(f"  k={k:>6} y={label:>8}: {small:>6} + {large:>9.1f} = "
                      f"{small + large:>9.1f} vs {n_nt:>6} "
                      f"(ratio {(small + large) / n_nt:.2f})")
    small_ev = []
    for k, p, n_nt, Lk, lphi in rows:
        s = sum(1 for kk, q in events if kk <= k and q * q <= p)
        small_ev.append(s)
        print(f"  k={k:>6}: events at q <= sqrt x: {s} of {n_nt}")
    at = {r[0]: r for r in rows}
    k4 = at[10 ** 4]
    check("W1 transparent share 23/49 at 50, 1531/1999 at 2000, 8013/9999 at 10^4",
          (at[50][0] - 1 - at[50][2], at[2000][0] - 1 - at[2000][2],
           k4[0] - 1 - k4[2]) == (23, 1531, 8013))
    check("W1 share rises at every milestone, last in (0.82, 0.88)",
          all(a < b for a, b in zip(shares, shares[1:])) and 0.82 < shares[-1] < 0.88,
          f"last {shares[-1]:.4f} at k = {rows[-1][0]}")
    check("W2 gap positive and falling; alpha(10^4) = 0.1651, N_nt = 1986",
          all(g > 0 for g in gaps) and all(a > b for a, b in zip(gaps, gaps[1:]))
          and round(k4[3] / k4[4], 4) == 0.1651 and k4[2] == 1986)
    check("W3 write-once bound at every (milestone, y) cell with y > 2",
          cells_ok)
    p4 = k4[1]
    small4 = sum(floor_log(p4, q) for q in primes_up_to(int(p4 ** 0.5)))
    check("W3 at k = 10^4, y = sqrt x: 176 + 2982.4",
          small4 == 176 and round(k4[3] / log(p4 ** 0.5), 1) == 2982.4,
          f"{small4} + {k4[3] / log(p4 ** 0.5):.1f}")
    shares_ev = [s / r[2] for s, r in zip(small_ev, rows)]
    check("W4 117 of 1986 events at q <= sqrt x at 10^4, share falling",
          small_ev[K_MILES.index(10 ** 4)] == 117
          and all(a > b for a, b in zip(shares_ev, shares_ev[1:])))
    print(f"  {'x':>9} {'L':>11} {'L/x':>7} {'r':>7} {'census':>7} {'/scale':>7}")
    lx, rs, cs = [], [], []
    for x, Lx, census in xrows:
        r = Lx * log(x) / (x * log(log(x)))
        scale = x * log(log(x)) / log(x) ** 2
        lx.append(Lx / x)
        rs.append(r)
        cs.append(census / scale)
        print(f"  {x:>9} {Lx:>11.1f} {Lx / x:>7.4f} {r:>7.4f} {census:>7} "
              f"{census / scale:>7.3f}")
    check("W5 L/x falls; r in [0.74, 0.80]; census/scale in [0.45, 0.60]",
          all(a > b for a, b in zip(lx, lx[1:])) and all(0.74 <= r <= 0.80 for r in rs)
          and all(0.45 <= c <= 0.60 for c in cs))
    L5 = next(Lx for x, Lx, _ in xrows if x == 10 ** 5)
    lam5 = lcm(*(p - 1 for p in primes_up_to(10 ** 5)))
    print(f"  ledger: L = {L:.3f} at x = 10^7; at 10^5 {L5:.3f} against "
          f"log lcm {log(lam5):.3f}; increments over log(p - 1): {over}; "
          f"steps raising two bases: {multi}")
    check("W6 the ledger at x = 10^5 equals log lcm{p - 1}; no step raises "
          "two bases", abs(L5 - log(lam5)) <= 1e-9 * L5 and multi == 0)


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ S

MR_BASES = (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17)
MR_LIMIT = 341_550_071_728_321       # the bases above are exact below this


def is_prime(n):
    if n < 2:
        return False
    for a in MR_BASES:
        if n % a == 0:
            return n == a
    d, s = n - 1, 0
    while d % 2 == 0:
        d, s = d // 2, s + 1
    for a in MR_BASES:
        x = pow(a, d, n)
        if x == 1 or x == n - 1:
            continue
        for _ in range(s - 1):
            x = x * x % n
            if x == n - 1:
                break
        else:
            return False
    return True


class Least:
    """f(m), the least prime = 1 (mod m), with a set of primes treated
    as absent."""

    def __init__(self, deleted=()):
        self.deleted = set(deleted)
        self.cache = {}

    def __call__(self, m):
        if m in self.cache:
            return self.cache[m]
        c = m + 1
        while not is_prime(c) or c in self.deleted:
            c += m
        assert c < MR_LIMIT
        if m <= 10 ** 5:
            self.cache[m] = c
        return c


def small_factor(t):
    out, q = {}, 2
    while q * q <= t:
        while t % q == 0:
            out[q] = out.get(q, 0) + 1
            t //= q
        q += 1
    if t > 1:
        out[t] = out.get(t, 0) + 1
    return out


def search(X, f):
    """Every prime power m <= X: p = f(m), and p tested against every
    other base of p - 1. Returns bases, records, double births, the
    largest cofactor and the 20 smallest margins."""
    sieve = bytearray([1]) * (X + 1)
    sieve[0:2] = b"\x00\x00"
    for i in range(2, isqrt(X) + 1):
        if sieve[i]:
            sieve[i * i::i] = bytearray(len(range(i * i, X + 1, i)))
    bases = records = 0
    doubles, margins, tmax = [], [], (0, 0, 0)
    for q in range(2, X + 1):
        if not sieve[q]:
            continue
        bases += 1
        m, last = q, 0
        while m <= X:
            p = f(m)
            if p != last:
                last = p
                records += 1
                t = (p - 1) // m
                if t > tmax[0]:
                    tmax = (t, p, m)
                for r, v in small_factor(t).items():
                    if r == q:
                        continue
                    p2 = f(r ** v)
                    if p2 == p:
                        doubles.append((p, m, r ** v))
                    margins.append((p / p2, p, m, r ** v))
                    if len(margins) > 400:
                        margins = sorted(margins)[:20]
            m *= q
    return bases, records, doubles, tmax, sorted(margins)[:20]


def section_s(X):
    print("S -- the serial law")
    _, _, doubles, _, _ = search(100, Least(deleted=(5, 7)))
    for p, m1, m2 in doubles:
        print(f"  control: DOUBLE BIRTH p = {p}, m1 = {m1}, m2 = {m2}")
    check("S1 control: with 5 and 7 deleted, a double birth at 13",
          any(p == 13 for p, _, _ in doubles))
    f = Least()
    expo, agree, new, bump = {}, True, 0, 0
    for p in primes_up_to(20000)[:2000]:
        fac = small_factor(p - 1) if p > 2 else {}
        by_walk = sorted(q for q, e in fac.items() if e > expo.get(q, 0))
        by_record = sorted(q for q, e in fac.items() if f(q ** e) == p)
        agree &= by_walk == by_record
        if len(by_walk) == 1:
            new += by_walk[0] not in expo
            bump += by_walk[0] in expo
        for q in by_walk:
            expo[q] = fac[q]
    check("S2 walk = record test at every rung to 2000; 438 new, 30 raised",
          agree and (new, bump) == (438, 30), f"{new} new, {bump} raised")
    bases, records, doubles, tmax, margins = search(X, f)
    for p, m1, m2 in doubles:
        print(f"  DOUBLE BIRTH p = {p}, m1 = {m1}, m2 = {m2}")
    print(f"  X = {X}: {records} record primes over {bases} bases; "
          f"double births {len(doubles)}; complete for p - 1 < {X * X}")
    print(f"  largest cofactor t = {tmax[0]} at p = {tmax[1]}, m = {tmax[2]}")
    print("  20 smallest margins (margin, p, m, m2):")
    for mg, p, m1, m2 in margins:
        print(f"    {mg:8.3f} {p:>6} {m1:>4} {m2:>4}")
    check("S3 no double birth to X", not doubles)
    check("S3 smallest margin 7/3 at p = 7; the 20 all at p < 600, 20th 34.6",
          margins[0][1] == 7 and abs(margins[0][0] - 7 / 3) < 1e-12
          and all(mg[1] < 600 for mg in margins) and round(margins[-1][0], 1) == 34.6)


def main():
    X = int(sys.argv[1]) if len(sys.argv) > 1 else 10 ** 6
    section_w()
    section_s(X)
    print(f"{sum(CHECKS)}/{len(CHECKS)} checks passed")
    raise SystemExit(0 if all(CHECKS) else 1)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
