dial.py

Checks: CLOCK: A block clock keeps at most one runaway per block (theorem); CLOCK: Two blocks of a number ring never both run away (theorem).

Cited on: CLOCK.

To run it: limit.py, stop.py beside it; standard library only.

"""dial.py -- the block clock: how many runaways a greedy walk keeps when
items share a notch in blocks, and what decides which blocks run.

QUESTION. A number ring's places over one rational prime read the state
through one p-adic valuation, so they share a notch; places over different
primes do not. That is a BLOCK clock, strictly between the family's two
ends: the GLOBAL clock (one block) and the PER-ITEM clock (every item its
own block). Measured before: at a constant gap one item runs away however
the items are cut into blocks, at a growing ladder one item per block. Is
that stop.py's per-item theorem with the block as the item, and the
chain of limit.py read inside each block? And is "one runaway at a flat
cost" a law, or a tie-break's?

THE SCHEDULE is limit.py's with the notch made per block. An item is (d,
slot), of degree d; a partition names its BLOCK b(d, slot), and a ladder
function names its LADDER. Block b carries a notch T_b, starting at 1. A
CLOCK move on an item of block b at exponent a has door T_b + 1 - a,
lands at T_b + 1, and moves T_b to next(T_b + 1) along the MOVER's own
ladder; an unseated item of a covered degree enters by a clock move from
a = 0. An OPENING has door 1, lands at 1, and moves no notch. The degree's
covering is global (born {1}, nu = 1). One block is limit.py's global
clock; singleton blocks are stop.py's per-item clock. The HOLDER of a
clock move is the item it moves; an item's ENTRY DOOR r0 is the door of
its first move; the HIGH-WATER PRICE H is the largest price a walk pays.
A block is SINGLE-HOLDER when one item makes all its clock moves. Its
CROSSING PRICE is H_b = kappa(d, max(r0, sup)), d and r0 its holder's and
sup its holder's ladder's largest gap: the dearest price on the holder's
path, paid where the path crosses the head.

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine).
  (1) THE CHAIN, PER BLOCK. limit.py's proof reads only the one notch the
      two holders share and the exponents under it. Take a clock move of
      Y in block b whose block's previous clock move had holder Z != Y. Z
      landed at t + 1 above the notch t it met, the notch went to
      next(t + 1), and since only openings and other blocks' moves came
      between -- neither touches T_b or raises an exponent of b above 1 --
      Y's door is at least next(t + 1) + 1 - max(1, t), one more than Z's
      next(t + 1) - t. Z was on the menu, so with kappa(d', r') > kappa(d, r)
      whenever d' >= d and r' > r, d_Y < d_Z. The ladders need not agree:
      the notch moved along Z's. So every change of holder inside a block
      falls in degree, and a block clocked infinitely often has exactly
      one runaway.
  (2) A FROZEN BLOCK ONLY CHEAPENS. While b is not clocked, T_b and b's
      exponents stand still; an opening in b adds an item at exponent 1,
      door T_b, and a newly covered degree adds an entry at door T_b + 1.
      So b's least pending price never rises while b is idle, and it is
      finite once b has a seated item or a covered one.
  (3) THE SINGLE HOLDER'S PATH (stop.py (2)). A single holder pays its
      entry door and then its own ladder's consecutive gaps: its notch
      stands at next(its landing), the landing one past a member. So its
      pending price is at every step one its path pays, and every price
      it pays is at most H_b.
  (4) BOUNDED GAPS: THE CROSSING PRICE ADMITS, THE TAIL DECIDES. Let every
      ladder's gaps be bounded and kappa(d, 1) tend to infinity. Some block is
      clocked infinitely often (limit.py), its runaway's prices are
      bounded, so H is finite and finitely many items ever move. Past the
      last opening every block's pending price is fixed except the
      blocks being clocked, and a runaway past its bend pays kappa(d, tail)
      forever on a ladder whose gaps settle (on bounded gaps that never
      settle it pays several prices forever). Let B be the largest price
      paid infinitely often; greed pays the least price on the menu at
      every step, so every idle block's pending price is at least B. If
      every block is single-holder, (3) gives H = min_b H_b over the blocks
      ever clocked: the runaway's path never prices above its H_b and is
      always on the menu, and a block whose path must pass a dearer
      price is left waiting at it. So a block whose TAIL is the cheapest
      can be priced out by its HEAD: its crossing price above another
      block's leaves it frozen at its bend. Two blocks run away only at
      an exact tie of recurrent prices, and whether a tied block runs is
      then the tie-break's.
  (5) CLIMBING GAPS: EVERY BLOCK RUNS. Let the gaps be unbounded and kappa
      unbounded in the door. A block clocked finitely often keeps a
      pending price at most some P once it holds a seated item, by (2),
      so every later price paid is at most P; a block clocked infinitely
      often pays gaps without bound; and if no block were, infinitely
      many openings would be paid at prices without bound. So every
      block that ever holds a seated item is clocked infinitely often,
      and by (1) holds exactly one runaway. Every opening is made
      (stop.py), so every block with a supplied item runs: one runaway
      per block.
  (6) THE RING. A number ring's blocks are its rational primes and each place's
      ladder steps by its finite e past the bend (clock.py), so its gaps are
      bounded; but a ring's block ticks in counts and an opening can jump it,
      which (4) does not model, so the ring rests on the lid (lid.py): a block
      clocked forever becomes lidded, finitely many openings follow, and past
      the last no count jumps. Its price is N(P)^r, so two blocks' recurrent
      prices p^(fe) and q^(f'e') are powers of different primes, by unique
      factorization, and never tie: a number ring keeps ONE runaway if the walk
      locks and none otherwise (a walk that never locks clocks every block
      finitely often, lid.py), and no tie-break is consulted across blocks
      [ruled on audit (F7): proved between blocks only; within one prime the
      chain is door.py's]. A function field's places all lie over p: one block,
      the global clock, one runaway by (1). Two runaways at a climbing ladder
      need two blocks with unbounded gaps, two characteristics each of equal
      characteristic, which no domain has (F_2[t] x F_3[t] has them).
  (7) THE WIDE PLACE. Two items of ONE degree in one block, on a gap-1
      and a gap-h ladder, tie at their entry; by (1) whichever enters
      first holds the block's clock forever, the other never passing
      exponent 1. So "the narrow place is the runaway" at equal degree
      is the tie-break at the entry, not a price: the wide item runs on
      the branch where it enters first. At a real ring the two places
      have different norms and no tie.
      [Ruled since (F6): false; x^3 + 2x + 1 holds two places of norm 59
      on gaps 1 and 2.]

HAND-ATTACK, at d r with two items per degree and the slot partition:
  (a) gap 3 in block 0 against (2,2,3) = 1, 2, 4, 9, 11, ... in block
      1. The block-0 rational item enters at door 2 and then pays 3
      forever. The block-1 rational item pays 2, 1, 2 and then faces 5
      at its bend: H_0 = 3 < H_1 = 5, so block 1 freezes there with its
      tail price 2 cheaper than the runaway's 3. H = 3.
      [Ruled after the run (F2): (2,2,3) is 1, 2, 7, 9, 11, ...; the item
      pays 2, 1 and then faces 5.]
  (b) gap 5 against (2,2,3): H_0 = 5 = H_1; both cross, the recurrent
      prices 5 and 2 part, and block 1 runs. H = 5.
  (c) gap 3 in both blocks: both rational items enter at 2 and bid 3.
      Lowest exponent first, they leapfrog and both run; highest first,
      the one ahead stays ahead and one runs.
  (d) ring shape, one block, gap 1 and gap h for the two slots of every
      degree: the rational slots tie at door 2 at notch 1. Wide first: it
      lands at 2, the notch goes to 1 + h, it re-clocks at h and the
      narrow item's entry costs h + 2. Narrow first: the notch stays at
      2, it re-clocks at 1.
  (e) the doubling ladder in a finite partition: a holder's price
      doubles per clock and the price of the opening at the horizon
      (the least uncovered supplied degree) grows about one per move,
      so the last half of a walk sees each block
      clocked about once; the doubling ladder is the marginal case of
      PR5, the squares ladder the comfortable one.

# a cell: a price, a partition into blocks, and a ladder per item

class Cell:
    def __init__(self, name, price, block, ladder):
        self.name, self.price = name, price
        self.block, self.ladder = block, ladder
        self.S = L.Sched(name, None, price)       # born {1}, nu 1


PARTS = {"ONE": lambda d, s: 0, "SLOT": lambda d, s: s,
         "MOD2": lambda d, s: (d + s) % 2, "MOD3": lambda d, s: (d + s) % 3,
         "ITEM": lambda d, s: (d, s)}


class St:
    """T: block -> notch; seat: item -> (exponent, entry door); last: block
    -> the item its last clock move moved."""
    __slots__ = ("T", "seat", "opens", "nseat", "last")

    def __init__(self):
        self.T, self.seat, self.last = {}, {}, {}
        self.opens, self.nseat = Counter(), Counter()

    def copy(self):
        s = St()
        s.T, s.seat, s.last = dict(self.T), dict(self.seat), dict(self.last)
        s.opens, s.nseat = Counter(self.opens), Counter(self.nseat)
        return s

    def key(self):
        return (tuple(sorted(self.T.items(), key=repr)),
                tuple(sorted(self.seat.items())),
                tuple(sorted(self.opens.items())),
                tuple(sorted(self.last.items(), key=repr)))


def notch(C, st, it):
    return st.T.get(C.block(*it), 1)


def moves(C, st, sup, b_only=None):
    """Every move on the menu, (price, type), a type (kind, d, a, r0, slot).
    With b_only, the clock moves of that block alone, openings excluded."""
    out = []
    for (d, s), (a, r0) in st.seat.items():
        if b_only is not None and C.block(d, s) != b_only:
            continue
        r = notch(C, st, (d, s)) + 1 - a
        out.append((C.price(d, r, False), ("c", d, a, r0, s)))
    for d in sup:
        if b_only is None and out and C.price(d, 1, True) > min(out)[0]:
            break
        for s in range(sup[d]):
            if (d, s) in st.seat:
                continue
            if b_only is not None and C.block(d, s) != b_only:
                continue
            if not L.covered(C.S, st, d):
                if b_only is None:
                    out.append((C.price(d, 1, True), ("o", d, 0, 1, s)))
            else:
                r = notch(C, st, (d, s)) + 1
                out.append((C.price(d, r, True), ("u", d, 0, r, s)))
    return out


def menu(C, st, sup):
    ms = moves(C, st, sup)
    best = min(p for p, _ in ms)
    return best, [t for p, t in ms if p == best]


def apply(C, st, t):
    """(state after, door paid, block moved or None, item)."""
    kind, d, a, r0, s = t
    n = st.copy()
    it = (d, s)
    if kind == "o":
        n.seat[it] = (1, 1)
        n.opens[d] += 1
        n.nseat[d] += 1
        return n, 1, None, it
    b = C.block(d, s)
    T = st.T.get(b, 1)
    r = T + 1 - a
    if kind == "u":
        check(r == r0, "an unseated item's entry door")
        n.nseat[d] += 1
    n.seat[it] = (T + 1, r0 if kind == "c" else r)
    n.T[b] = C.ladder(d, s).next(T + 1)
    n.last[b] = it
    return n, r, b, it


def canon_low(types):
    return min(types, key=lambda t: (t[0] == "o", t[1], t[2], t[3], t[4]))


def canon_high(types):
    return min(types, key=lambda t: (t[0] == "o", t[1], -t[2], t[3], t[4]))


def step(C, st, t, price, log):
    s2, r, b, it = apply(C, st, t)
    check(C.price(t[1], r, t[0] != "c") == price, "the menu's price")
    prev = st.last.get(b) if b is not None else None
    log.append((t[0], t[1], r, price, b, it, prev))
    return s2


def run(C, sup, n, st=None, log=None, choose=canon_low):
    st = st if st is not None else St()
    log = [] if log is None else log
    for _ in range(n):
        best, types = menu(C, st, sup)
        st = step(C, st, choose(types), best, log)
    return st, log


def branches(C, sup, k, n, choose=canon_low, cap=6000):
    front = {St().key(): (St(), [])}
    for _ in range(k):
        nxt = {}
        for st, log in front.values():
            best, types = menu(C, st, sup)
            for t in types:
                lg = list(log)
                s2 = step(C, st, t, best, lg)
                nxt.setdefault(s2.key(), (s2, lg))
        front = nxt
        check(len(front) <= cap, "the branch cap")
    return [run(C, sup, n, st, list(log), choose) for st, log in
            front.values()]

def chain_faults(log):
    """(changes of holder inside a block, those not falling in degree)."""
    ch = bad = 0
    for kind, d, r, p, b, it, prev in log:
        if b is not None and prev is not None and prev != it:
            ch += 1
            bad += not d < prev[0]
    return ch, bad


def late(log, frac):
    """block -> {item: [prices]} over the clock moves of the last part."""
    out = {}
    for kind, d, r, p, b, it, prev in log[int(len(log) * (1 - frac)):]:
        if b is not None:
            out.setdefault(b, {}).setdefault(it, []).append(p)
    return out


def lemma_faults(C, st, sup, H):
    bad = 0
    for d in sup:
        if not sup[d] or d in C.S.born:
            continue
        p1 = C.price(d, 1, True)
        if p1 < H and not L.covered(C.S, st, d) and st.nseat[d] < sup[d]:
            bad += 1
        if p1 > H and st.opens[d]:
            bad += 1
    u = min(d for d in sup if sup[d] and not L.covered(C.S, st, d))
    bad += C.price(u, 1, True) < H
    return bad


def holders(log):
    """block -> list of distinct clock movers in order, with each one's
    entry door (the door of its first clock move, or 1 if opened)."""
    out, first = {}, {}
    for kind, d, r, p, b, it, prev in log:
        if kind == "o":
            first.setdefault(it, 1)
        if b is None:
            continue
        first.setdefault(it, r)
        hs = out.setdefault(b, [])
        if not hs or hs[-1] != it:
            hs.append(it)
    return out, first


def crossing(C, it, r0):
    s, _ = SP.sup_tail(C.ladder(*it))
    return C.price(it[0], max(r0, s), False)


def bounded_reading(C, st, log, sup):
    """The PR3 observables for one branch."""
    H = max(x[3] for x in log)
    lt = late(log, 0.25)
    prices = {p for b in lt for ps in lt[b].values() for p in ps}
    B = max(prices)
    two_price = len(prices) > 1
    idle_below = 0
    for b in {C.block(*it) for it in st.seat}:
        if b in lt:
            continue
        pend = [p for p, _ in moves(C, st, sup, b_only=b)]
        idle_below += bool(pend) and min(pend) < B
    hs, first = holders(log)
    single = all(len(set(v)) == 1 for v in hs.values())
    off = None
    if single:
        Hb = {b: crossing(C, v[0], first[v[0]]) for b, v in hs.items()}
        off = (H != min(Hb.values())
               or any(Hb[b] != H for b in lt))
    two_late = sum(len(v) > 1 for v in lt.values())
    headed = sum(crossing(C, hs[b][-1], first[hs[b][-1]]) > B for b in lt)
    return dict(H=H, B=B, two_price=two_price, idle_below=idle_below,
                single=single, off=off, two_late=two_late, late=lt, hs=hs,
                first=first, headed=headed)

def common(lad):
    return lambda d, s: lad


def by_slot(l0, l1):
    return lambda d, s: l0 if s == 0 else l1

def section_control():
    print("S0  CONTROLS")
    moves_read = 0
    for lad in L.LADDERS:
        S = L.Sched(lad.name, lad, L.price_power(1))
        for part, glob in (("ONE", True), ("ITEM", False)):
            _, ref = SP.run(S, SUP, glob, N)
            C = Cell(lad.name, L.price_power(1), PARTS[part], common(lad))
            _, mine = run(C, SUP, N)
            check([(t[0], t[1], r, p) for t, r, p, *_ in ref]
                  == [x[:4] for x in mine],
                  f"{part} parts from stop.py at {lad.name}")
            moves_read += len(ref)
    print(f"  ONE matches stop.py's global walk and ITEM its per-item walk "
          f"move for move at {len(L.LADDERS)} ladders, {moves_read} moves")
    planted = [("c", 1, 2, 2, 0, (1, 0), None),
               ("c", 2, 1, 2, 0, (2, 0), (1, 0))]
    check(chain_faults(planted) == (1, 1), "the chain checker did not fire")
    C = Cell("gap 3", L.price_power(1), PARTS["SLOT"],
             common(L.ladder_gap(3)))
    st, log = run(C, SUP, N)
    check(not bounded_reading(C, st, log, SUP)["two_price"],
          "a clean walk's last quarter")
    log2 = list(log)
    k, d, r, p, b, it, prev = log2[-1]
    log2[-1] = (k, d, r, p + 1, b, it, prev)
    check(bounded_reading(C, st, log2, SUP)["two_price"],
          "the equal-price checker did not fire")
    print("  the chain checker fires on a planted rising change; the "
          "equal-price checker on a planted second price")


def section_bounded():
    print(f"S1  BOUNDED (PR1-PR3): 2 items per degree to 400, born {{1}}, "
          f"ties branched over {K} moves, {N} more LOW")
    print("  price  ladder    part  branches lemma chg/rise 2late | "
          "2price idle<B single off  >1 late block")
    tot = Counter()
    for pname, price in PRICES:
        for lad in bounded_ladders():
            for part in PARTS:
                C = Cell(lad.name, price, PARTS[part], common(lad))
                row = Counter()
                for st, log in branches(C, SUP, K, N):
                    rd = bounded_reading(C, st, log, SUP)
                    row["br"] += 1
                    row["lemma"] += lemma_faults(C, st, SUP, rd["H"])
                    ch, bad = chain_faults(log)
                    row["chg"] += ch
                    row["rise"] += bad
                    row["2late"] += rd["two_late"]
                    row["2price"] += rd["two_price"]
                    row["idle"] += rd["idle_below"]
                    row["single"] += rd["single"]
                    row["off"] += bool(rd["off"])
                    row["multi"] += len(rd["late"]) > 1
                    row["headed"] += rd["headed"] > 1
                tot += row
                print(f"  {pname:6s} {lad.name:9s} {part:5s} {row['br']:5d} "
                      f"{row['lemma']:5d} {row['chg']:4d}/{row['rise']:<3d} "
                      f"{row['2late']:4d}  | {row['2price']:5d} "
                      f"{row['idle']:6d} {row['single']:6d} {row['off']:3d}"
                      f"  {row['multi']:4d}")
    print(f"  totals: {tot['br']} branches; lemma faults {tot['lemma']}; "
          f"{tot['chg']} changes of holder, {tot['rise']} not falling; "
          f"{tot['2late']} blocks with two late items; {tot['2price']} "
          f"two-price quarters, {tot['idle']} idle blocks below B; "
          f"{tot['single']} single-holder, {tot['off']} off min H_b; "
          f"{tot['multi']} branches with more than one late block, "
          f"{tot['headed']} with two headed late blocks")
    for key in ("lemma", "rise", "2late", "2price", "idle", "off",
                "headed"):
        check(tot[key] == 0, f"PR1-PR3: {key}")


def describe(C, st, log):
    rd = bounded_reading(C, st, log, SUP)
    parts = []
    for b, hs in sorted(rd["hs"].items(), key=repr):
        it = hs[-1]
        s, tl = SP.sup_tail(C.ladder(*it))
        Hb = crossing(C, it, rd["first"][it])
        nl = sum(len(v) for v in rd["late"].get(b, {}).values())
        parts.append(f"block {b}: holder {it} H_b {Hb} tail price "
                     f"{C.price(it[0], tl, False)} late moves {nl}")
    return rd, parts


def section_admission():
    """PR4, killed as frozen: the census of every branch, with the clause
    the theorem does claim -- H = min H_b on a single-holder branch --
    checked on each."""
    print("S2  THE ADMISSION CELLS (PR4): d r, SLOT, LOW, every branch")
    psi = SP.place_ladder(2, 2, 3)
    for c in (3, 5):
        fired = 0
        C = Cell(f"gap {c} | (2,2,3)", L.price_power(1), PARTS["SLOT"],
                 by_slot(L.ladder_gap(c), psi))
        seen, shown = Counter(), {}
        for st, log in branches(C, SUP, K, N):
            rd, parts = describe(C, st, log)
            if rd["single"]:
                check(not rd["off"], f"H = min H_b at gap {c}")
            fired += rd["H"] != c or (1 if c == 3 else 0) in rd["late"]
            k = (rd["H"], tuple(sorted(rd["late"], key=repr)), rd["single"])
            seen[k] += 1
            shown.setdefault(k, parts)
        print(f"  gap {c} | (2,2,3): {sum(seen.values())} branches; PR4 "
              f"KILLED as frozen on {fired}")
        check(fired == PR4_KILLED[c], f"PR4's frozen kill as recorded, "
              f"gap {c}")
        for k, n in sorted(seen.items(), key=repr):
            print(f"   H {k[0]}, late blocks {list(k[1])}, single-holder "
                  f"{k[2]}: {n} branches")
            for line in shown[k]:
                print("      " + line)


def section_climbing():
    print(f"S3  CLIMBING (PR5): d r, {NC} moves, blocks with a seated item "
          f"and those not clocked in the last half")
    for lad in climbing_ladders():
        for part in PARTS:
            C = Cell(lad.name, L.price_power(1), PARTS[part], common(lad))
            miss, blocks, br, per_late = 0, 0, 0, 0
            for st, log in branches(C, SUP, K, NC):
                br += 1
                seated = {C.block(*it) for it in st.seat}
                lt = late(log, 0.5)
                blocks += len(seated)
                if part == "ITEM":
                    per_late += len(lt)
                    continue
                miss += len(seated - set(lt))
                check(all(len(v) == 1 for v in lt.values()),
                      "two late items in a block, climbing")
            if part == "ITEM":
                print(f"  {lad.name:8s} ITEM  {br} branches, items clocked "
                      f"in the last half {per_late} of {blocks} seated")
                continue
            if lad.name == "squares":
                check(miss == 0, f"PR5 at squares {part}")
            print(f"  {lad.name:8s} {part:5s} {br} branches, blocks seated "
                  f"{blocks}, not clocked late {miss}")


def section_tie():
    print("S4  THE TIE (PR6): SLOT, one ladder in both blocks, d r")
    fired = 0
    for lad in (L.ladder_gap(2), L.ladder_gap(3), L.ladder_gap(5),
                SP.place_ladder(2, 2, 3)):
        C = Cell(lad.name, L.price_power(1), PARTS["SLOT"], common(lad))
        out = {}
        for nm, ch in (("LOW", canon_low), ("HIGH", canon_high)):
            cnt = Counter()
            for st, log in branches(C, SUP, K, N, ch):
                rd = bounded_reading(C, st, log, SUP)
                nb = len(rd["late"])
                both = (1, 0) in st.seat and (1, 1) in st.seat
                cnt[(nb, both)] += 1
                check(rd["headed"] <= 1, f"two headed runaways {lad.name}")
                if nm == "LOW" and lad.bounded and lad.name.startswith("("):
                    fired += both and nb == 1
                    continue      # PR6 killed here: the head breaks the tie
                if nm == "LOW":
                    check(not (both and nb == 1), f"PR6 LOW at {lad.name}")
                else:
                    check(nb == 1, f"PR6 HIGH at {lad.name}")
            out[nm] = dict(cnt)
        print(f"  {lad.name:8s} (late blocks, both rational seated): "
              f"LOW {out['LOW']}  HIGH {out['HIGH']}")
    print(f"  PR6 KILLED as frozen at (2,2,3) under LOW on {fired} branches")
    check(fired == 8, "PR6's frozen kill as recorded, (2,2,3) LOW")


def section_two_chars():
    print("S5  TWO CHARACTERISTICS (PR7): SLOT, doubling | tripling, d r")
    C = Cell("2 | 3", L.price_power(1), PARTS["SLOT"],
             by_slot(L.ladder_b(2), L.ladder_b(3)))
    cnt = Counter()
    for st, log in branches(C, SUP, K, NC):
        nb = len(late(log, 0.5))
        check(nb == 2, "PR7: one block clocked late")
        cnt[nb] += 1
    print(f"  late-half blocks per branch: {dict(cnt)}")
    C = Cell("2", L.price_power(1), PARTS["ONE"], common(L.ladder_b(2)))
    cnt = Counter(len(late(log, 0.5)) for _, log in branches(C, SUP, K, NC))
    print(f"  one block, doubling: late-half blocks per branch {dict(cnt)}")


def section_wide():
    print("S6  THE WIDE PLACE (PR8): ONE block, slot 0 on gap 1, slot 1 on "
          "gap e, d r")
    for e in (2, 3, 5):
        C = Cell(f"1 | {e}", L.price_power(1), PARTS["ONE"],
                 by_slot(L.ladder_gap(1), L.ladder_gap(e)))
        cnt = Counter()
        for st, log in branches(C, SUP, K, N):
            (items,) = late(log, 0.25).values()
            check(len(items) == 1, f"PR8 two late items at e = {e}")
            (it,) = items
            cnt[(it, max(x[3] for x in log))] += 1
        check(any(it[1] == 1 for it, _ in cnt), f"PR8 wide never runs, {e}")
        print(f"  e = {e}: (runaway, H): branches {dict(cnt)}")
