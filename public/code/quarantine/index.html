quarantine.py

Checks: COLLIDE: A witness-free tie is a regrouping or passes a seed (property); FORGET: The quarantine theorem (theorem); FORGET: Recycled worlds need no witness (property).

Cited on: COLLIDE, FORGET.

To run it: no other script; standard library only.

"""quarantine.py -- which grown worlds can hide their route at every
temperature, and what the answer needs of the menus.

QUESTION. A grown world (forget.py's convention) moves from state N to
N*m with probability m^-beta / Psi, Psi the sum of n^-beta over the
menu's moves n; a history is a ROUTE from seed 1 and a DATED FIBER
fixes the endpoint and the age. A fiber is ROBUST for the route when every route in it
carries the same posterior at every beta. A world is COPRIME when
every menu is coprime to its state: no menu along a route offers a
prime the route has already used. Is a coprime world ever robust on a
fiber holding two routes or more? Which hypothesis does the answer
use: that menus are functions of the state, or that they are coprime
to the whole state rather than to the last move? And what does a
world that RECYCLES primes buy instead?

THE ARGUMENT (written before the engine).
  (A) ROUTE WEIGHTS. On a dated fiber every route's numerators
      multiply to N^-beta, so the route posterior is proportional to
      1 / D, D the product of the normalizers at the states the route
      passes before each move, seed included (the route-weight
      cancellation). Each Psi is a Dirichlet polynomial with one
      coefficient 1 per menu element, so Psi determines its menu, and
      two routes tie at every beta iff their D are equal as formal
      Dirichlet polynomials: finite sums of n^-beta over distinct n
      are linearly independent functions on any interval of beta.
  (B) THE QUARANTINE THEOREM. In a coprime world two distinct routes
      of one dated fiber never tie at every beta. Proof by induction
      on the age t. At t = 1 the endpoint is the move. At t >= 2 let
      the routes tie. Both D share the seed's Psi; the Dirichlet
      polynomials form a domain, so the products over the later
      states agree. Their coefficients are nonnegative, so nothing
      cancels, and the support is the set of products of one element
      per menu, whose primes are the union of the menus' primes: both
      routes' later menus use one prime set S. QUARANTINE: every later
      state is divisible by the first move m, and a coprime menu shares
      no prime with its state, so m has no prime in S; so does the
      other route's first move m'. VALUATION: every move after the
      first is drawn from a later menu, so its primes lie in S; the
      endpoint N is m times those moves, so m is the part of N off S,
      and so is m'. So m = m', the routes share their first state and
      its menu, that factor cancels, and the induction runs on from
      the common state. Hence a coprime world holds no dated fiber
      robust for the route at any age, and, a block of a cure being a
      fiber piece whose routes tie, no refinement of the dated state
      cures the route robustly either.
  (C) WHAT THE PROOF USES. The valuation step reads only the endpoint
      and the prime set of the later menus; it never asks where a menu
      came from. The cancellation step needs the two routes' menus to
      agree once their prefixes do, which holds for any menu that is a
      function of the route so far. So the theorem holds verbatim with
      HISTORY menus, a function of the prefix and coprime to the
      state. Coprimality to the whole state is the load: the
      quarantine step needs the first move barred from every later
      menu, not only the next. A LAST-MOVE world, each menu coprime to
      the last move only, escapes by hand: menu() = {2, 3},
      menu(2) = {3, 5}, menu(3) = {2, 5}, menu(2, 3) = {2, 5},
      menu(3, 2) = {3, 5}, menu(2, 5) = menu(3, 5) = {7}. Its fiber
      (30, 3) holds exactly 2,3,5 and 3,2,5, whose later menus are
      {3, 5}, {2, 5} against {2, 5}, {3, 5}: equal multisets, robust,
      and the menu at state 6 depends on the route, since the last
      move does.
  (D) THE ROGUE WORLD, recycled and witness-free. menu(1) = {2, 32},
      menu(2) = {2, 4, 8}, menu(16) = {2, 16}, menu(32) = {2, 4},
      menu(128) = {2, 8, 32}. The fiber (256, 3) closes at 2,8,16 and
      32,4,2 (hand closure: a first move 2 then 2 or 4 lands on states
      with no menu toward 256 at age 3; 8 then 16 is the one way). With
      x = 2^-beta, Psi(2) Psi(16) = (x + x^2 + x^3)(x + x^4) and
      Psi(32) Psi(128) = (x + x^2)(x + x^3 + x^5); both are
      x^2 + x^3 + ... + x^7, so the fiber is robust, while the
      normalizer multisets differ: the symmetry witness is absent.
      The identity is the two pairings of (1 + x)(1 + x + x^2)
      (1 - x + x^2), whose last factor has a negative coefficient.
      AGE 3 IS LEAST: at age 2 each route has one later state, so a
      tie is one Psi equal to another, the same menu, the witness.
  (E) THE COARSE WORLD, a witness-free tie put to work. Every move a
      power of 2: menu(1) = {2, 4, 8, 16}, menu(2) = menu(4) = {4, 8},
      menu(8) = {8}, menu(16) = {8, 16}, menu(64) = {2, 4},
      menu(128) = {2}. The fiber (256, 3) holds P = 2,8,16,
      Q = 4,4,16, A = 8,8,4 and B = 16,8,2. P and Q tie with equal
      multisets (Psi(2) = Psi(4)); A and B tie witness-free, x^3 (x + x^2)
      = (x^3 + x^4) x. P's product is A's times x (1 + x), which is 1
      only at x = (sqrt 5 - 1) / 2, beta = log2 of the golden ratio.
      So the dated state leaks the route at every other beta, and the
      blocks {P, Q} and {A, B} are spread and flat at every beta: a
      strict cure bought by menu design in an order-carrying world.
  (F) THE TUNED WORLD of forget.py is coprime and flat for the route
      at (6, 2) at beta = 1 only: tuning is the one way a coprime
      world hides a route.

DESIGN. A world is a function menu(prefix, state). Routes to a given
age are walked breadth first, each carrying the menus it passed; D is
formed as an exact integer Dirichlet polynomial {n: coefficient}, and
two routes TIE when their D agree, WITNESSED when their multisets of
menus agree as well. Fraction weights at beta = 1, 2, 3 cross-check the
formal ties on the hand worlds. The ARMS, menus drawn at random at each
state (STATE) or each prefix (HISTORY) as 1 or 2 elements of the
allowed moves, dated fibers of age 2 and 3, 3000 worlds per arm, fixed
seeds, on the small universe US = {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15}:
  COPRIME-STATE and COPRIME-HISTORY, menus coprime to the state;
  LAST-HISTORY, menus coprime to the last move (the removed
  ingredient); FREE-STATE, no demand (the scan's own control).
Two larger coprime arms run on the 7-smooth moves 2 .. 36, menus of 1
to 3 elements, ages 2 to 4, 1000 worlds each. The EXHAUSTIVE arm takes
every coprime world on {2, 3, 4, 5, 6} with a nonempty menu wherever
one is allowed, at ages 2 and 3: menus read the state, at the ten
states those moves reach with a nonempty allowed menu, and two tables differing only at a state one
of them never reaches count as two worlds, their fibers counted
apart. A FIRST-MOVE print reads, on the
coprime arms' fibers of at most eight routes meeting two first moves
or more, whether the first move's class masses agree at every beta,
by the exact identity sum over a class of L / D, L the product of all
D.

PREDICTIONS (fixed before the engine).
  P1 controls: the tuned world is coprime, flat at (6, 2) at beta = 1
     and at odds 117/70 at beta = 2; the perturbed rogue world,
     menu(128) = {2, 8, 16}, keeps two routes at (256, 3) and ties at
     no grid beta.
  P2 the rogue world: fiber (256, 3) is exactly the two routes, their
     D agree formally and their Fraction posteriors are 1/2 at
     beta = 1, 2, 3, and the multisets differ.
  P3 the coarse world: fiber (256, 3) is exactly P, Q, A, B; P, Q
     witnessed, A, B witness-free; P's D equals A's times x (1 + x);
     the dated state is not flat at beta = 1, 2, 3 and both blocks are.
  P4 the last-move world: fiber (30, 3) is exactly 2,3,5 and 3,2,5,
     tied and witnessed; the world is last-move coprime and not
     coprime.
  P5 zero ties in the exhaustive arm and in all four coprime random
     arms, STATE and HISTORY alike.
  P6 at least one tie in FREE-STATE and in LAST-HISTORY.
  P7 [TRANSPLANT from the two-route case] no coprime fiber hides its
     first move at every beta. A hit kills nothing: the theorem is
     about the route, and a hit would be flatness failing to pass up
     under coarsening, printed as a specimen.

KILLS. A tie in the exhaustive arm's or a coprime random arm's check
kills the quarantine theorem, STATE, or its history form, HISTORY. P6's
two tie controls gate the arms: if one fails the scan cannot see what
it looks for and no coprime arm is checked, though every arm's totals
still print. P1 to P4 are controls on the hand worlds, read first.

FINDINGS. Every prediction held and no kill fired.
  P1 the tuned world is coprime, flat at (6, 2) at beta = 1 and at odds
     117/70 at beta = 2; the perturbed rogue fiber keeps 2,8,16 and
     32,4,2 and ties at no grid beta.
  P2 the rogue fiber is exactly its two routes; both later-state
     products are x^2 + ... + x^7 (the terms 4, 8, ..., 128), the
     multisets differ, and the posterior is 1/2 at beta = 1, 2, 3.
  P3 the coarse fiber is exactly P, Q, A, B; P, Q witnessed, A, B
     witness-free, P's D is A's times x (1 + x), the dated state leaks
     at every grid beta and both blocks are flat.
  P4 the last-move fiber (30, 3) is exactly 2,3,5 and 3,2,5, tied with
     the witness, in a world last-move coprime and not coprime.
  P5 no tie: COPRIME-STATE 575 multi-route fibers, COPRIME-HISTORY 548,
     the large arms 1030 each, an equal count from distinct draws
     (the two keyed and seeded differently), 3183 in the four,
     EXHAUSTIVE 141216 fibers over 87885 worlds.
  P6 LAST-HISTORY ties once, FREE-STATE twice, every tie witnessed: a
     random draw makes equal menus, never a witness-free tie, which
     only the designed worlds of (D) and (E) carry.
  P7 no coprime fiber hides its first move at every beta: 0 of 313,
     312, 277 and 252 reads in the random arms and 0 of 119232 in the
     exhaustive one, 0 of 120386 in all, so no specimen prints.

RUN RECORD. python quarantine.py: 20 of 20 checks, 7.5 s, peak commit
14.0 MB. The first-move hit count is printed beside its totals, and the coprime
arms are read only after the two tie controls pass, both added on
review.
"""

import itertools
import random
import sys
from collections import defaultdict
from fractions import Fraction
from math import gcd

BETAS = (1, 2, 3)
CHECKS = []


def check(name, ok, detail=""):
    CHECKS.append(bool(ok))
    tag = "ok  " if ok else "FAIL"
    print(f"  [{tag}] {name}" + (f"  ({detail})" if detail else ""))


def section(title):
    print()
    print(title)


# ------------------------------------------------- Dirichlet polynomials

def dmul(a, b):
    out = defaultdict(int)
    for n, c in a.items():
        for m, d in b.items():
            out[n * m] += c * d
    return dict(out)


def dadd(a, b):
    out = defaultdict(int, a)
    for n, c in b.items():
        out[n] += c
    return {n: c for n, c in out.items() if c}


def dkey(a):
    return tuple(sorted(a.items()))


def zpoly(menu):
    return {m: 1 for m in menu}


def product(menus):
    d = {1: 1}
    for mu in menus:
        d = dmul(d, zpoly(mu))
    return d


# ----------------------------------------------------------- the worlds

def walk(menu, age):
    """Every route of the given age: (moves, endpoint, menus passed)."""
    out = [((), 1, ())]
    for _ in range(age):
        nxt = []
        for r, n, ms in out:
            mu = menu(r, n)
            for m in mu:
                nxt.append((r + (m,), n * m, ms + (mu,)))
        out = nxt
    return out


def fibers(menu, age):
    fib = defaultdict(list)
    for r, n, ms in walk(menu, age):
        fib[n].append((r, ms))
    return fib


def by_state(table):
    return lambda prefix, n: table.get(n, ())


def by_prefix(table):
    return lambda prefix, n: table.get(prefix, ())


def weight(route, menus, beta):
    w = Fraction(1)
    for m, mu in zip(route, menus):
        w *= Fraction(1, m ** beta) / sum(Fraction(1, k ** beta)
                                          for k in mu)
    return w


def masses(items, beta):
    return [weight(r, ms, beta) for r, ms in items]


def ties(items):
    """Tied pairs in a fiber: (witnessed, witness-free)."""
    wit = free = 0
    keyed = [(dkey(product(ms)), tuple(sorted(ms))) for _, ms in items]
    for (k1, s1), (k2, s2) in itertools.combinations(keyed, 2):
        if k1 == k2:
            if s1 == s2:
                wit += 1
            else:
                free += 1
    return wit, free


def first_move_robust(items):
    """Whether the first move's class masses agree at every beta."""
    ds = [product(ms) for _, ms in items]
    classes = defaultdict(list)
    for i, (r, _) in enumerate(items):
        classes[r[0]].append(i)
    sums = []
    for idx in classes.values():
        s = {}
        for i in idx:
            rest = {1: 1}
            for j, d in enumerate(ds):
                if j != i:
                    rest = dmul(rest, d)
            s = dadd(s, rest)
        sums.append(dkey(s))
    return len(set(sums)) == 1


def is_coprime(menu, age):
    return all(gcd(m, n) == 1 for r, n, ms in walk(menu, age - 1)
               for m in menu(r, n))


TUNED = by_state({1: (2, 3), 2: (3, 5, 15), 3: (2, 10)})
ROGUE_TABLE = {1: (2, 32), 2: (2, 4, 8), 16: (2, 16), 32: (2, 4),
               128: (2, 8, 32)}
ROGUE = by_state(ROGUE_TABLE)
PERTURBED = by_state({**ROGUE_TABLE, 128: (2, 8, 16)})
COARSE = by_state({1: (2, 4, 8, 16), 2: (4, 8), 4: (4, 8), 8: (8,),
                   16: (8, 16), 64: (2, 4), 128: (2,)})
LAST = by_prefix({(): (2, 3), (2,): (3, 5), (3,): (2, 5),
                  (2, 3): (2, 5), (3, 2): (3, 5), (2, 5): (7,),
                  (3, 5): (7,)})


# ------------------------------------------------------------- sections

def section_controls():
    section("CONTROLS: the tuned world and the perturbed rogue world")
    fib = fibers(TUNED, 2)[6]
    w1, w2 = masses(fib, 1), masses(fib, 2)
    check("the tuned world is coprime", is_coprime(TUNED, 3))
    check("tuned fiber (6, 2) flat at beta = 1, odds 117/70 at 2",
          w1[0] == w1[1] and max(w2) / min(w2) == Fraction(117, 70),
          f"beta 2 odds {max(w2) / min(w2)}")
    fib = fibers(PERTURBED, 3)[256]
    flat = [len(set(masses(fib, b))) == 1 for b in BETAS]
    check("perturbed rogue: two routes, tied at no grid beta",
          len(fib) == 2 and not any(flat) and ties(fib) == (0, 0),
          f"routes {[r for r, _ in fib]}")


def section_rogue():
    section("THE ROGUE WORLD: a robust fiber with no witness")
    fib = fibers(ROGUE, 3)[256]
    routes = sorted(r for r, _ in fib)
    check("fiber (256, 3) is exactly 2,8,16 and 32,4,2",
          routes == [(2, 8, 16), (32, 4, 2)])
    d = [product(ms[1:]) for _, ms in fib]
    print("    later-state products:", [dkey(x) for x in d])
    check("the two D agree formally, multisets differ",
          ties(fib) == (0, 1))
    post = [masses(fib, b) for b in BETAS]
    check("posterior 1/2 at beta = 1, 2, 3",
          all(p[0] == p[1] for p in post))


def section_coarse():
    section("THE COARSE WORLD: a witness-free tie funds a strict cure")
    fib = dict(fibers(COARSE, 3)[256])
    names = {"P": (2, 8, 16), "Q": (4, 4, 16), "A": (8, 8, 4),
             "B": (16, 8, 2)}
    check("fiber (256, 3) is exactly P, Q, A, B",
          sorted(fib) == sorted(names.values()),
          f"routes {sorted(fib)}")
    get = {k: (v, fib[v]) for k, v in names.items() if v in fib}
    if len(get) < 4:
        return
    check("P, Q witnessed; A, B witness-free",
          ties([get["P"], get["Q"]]) == (1, 0)
          and ties([get["A"], get["B"]]) == (0, 1))
    dp, da = product(get["P"][1]), product(get["A"][1])
    check("P's D is A's times x (1 + x)",
          dkey(dp) == dkey(dmul(da, {2: 1, 4: 1})))
    items = list(get.values())
    whole = [len(set(masses(items, b))) == 1 for b in BETAS]
    blocks = [len(set(masses([get[a], get[b]], beta))) == 1
              for a, b in (("P", "Q"), ("A", "B")) for beta in BETAS]
    check("the dated state leaks at every grid beta, both blocks flat",
          not any(whole) and all(blocks))


def section_last():
    section("THE LAST-MOVE WORLD: coprime to the last move is not enough")
    fib = fibers(LAST, 3)[30]
    check("fiber (30, 3) is exactly 2,3,5 and 3,2,5",
          sorted(r for r, _ in fib) == [(2, 3, 5), (3, 2, 5)])
    check("the two routes tie with the witness", ties(fib) == (1, 0))
    last_ok = all(not r or gcd(m, r[-1]) == 1
                  for r, n, ms in walk(LAST, 2) for m in LAST(r, n))
    check("last-move coprime, not coprime",
          last_ok and not is_coprime(LAST, 3))


def random_world(rng, universe, allowed, keyed, sizes):
    cache = {}

    def menu(prefix, n):
        k = prefix if keyed == "prefix" else n
        if k not in cache:
            opts = [m for m in universe if allowed(m, n, prefix)]
            size = min(rng.randint(*sizes), len(opts))
            cache[k] = tuple(sorted(rng.sample(opts, size)))
        return cache[k]
    return menu


def coprime(m, n, prefix):
    return gcd(m, n) == 1


def last_move(m, n, prefix):
    return not prefix or gcd(m, prefix[-1]) == 1


def free(m, n, prefix):
    return True


def scan(menus, ages, first_move=False):
    """Totals over worlds: multi-route fibers, witnessed and
    witness-free tied pairs, robust fibers, first-move reads."""
    t = defaultdict(int)
    for menu in menus:
        t["worlds"] += 1
        for age in ages:
            for items in fibers(menu, age).values():
                if len(items) < 2:
                    continue
                t["fibers"] += 1
                wit, fr = ties(items)
                t["witnessed"] += wit
                t["free"] += fr
                if len({dkey(product(ms)) for _, ms in items}) == 1:
                    t["robust"] += 1
                if first_move and len(items) <= 8 and \
                        len({r[0] for r, _ in items}) >= 2:
                    t["first read"] += 1
                    if first_move_robust(items):
                        t["first robust"] += 1
                        print(f"    SPECIMEN first move hidden: "
                              f"{[r for r, _ in items]}")
    return dict(t)


US = (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15)
UL = tuple(m for m in range(2, 37)
           if all(p in (2, 3, 5, 7) for p in
                  [q for q in range(2, m + 1) if m % q == 0
                   and all(q % s for s in range(2, q))]))


def exhaustive_worlds():
    universe = (2, 3, 4, 5, 6)
    states = (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20)
    options = []
    for n in states:
        allowed = [m for m in universe if gcd(m, n) == 1]
        options.append([c for k in range(1, len(allowed) + 1)
                        for c in itertools.combinations(allowed, k)])
    for choice in itertools.product(*options):
        yield by_state(dict(zip(states, choice)))


def section_arms():
    section("THE ARMS: ties by demand on the menus")
    arms = [("COPRIME-STATE", US, coprime, "state", (1, 2), (2, 3), 3000),
            ("COPRIME-HISTORY", US, coprime, "prefix", (1, 2), (2, 3),
             3000),
            ("LAST-HISTORY", US, last_move, "prefix", (1, 2), (2, 3), 3000),
            ("FREE-STATE", US, free, "state", (1, 2), (2, 3), 3000),
            ("COPRIME-STATE large", UL, coprime, "state", (1, 3),
             (2, 3, 4), 1000),
            ("COPRIME-HISTORY large", UL, coprime, "prefix", (1, 3),
             (2, 3, 4), 1000)]
    out = {}
    for seed, (name, uni, allowed, keyed, sizes, ages, count) in \
            enumerate(arms):
        rng = random.Random(seed)
        menus = (random_world(rng, uni, allowed, keyed, sizes)
                 for _ in range(count))
        t = scan(menus, ages, first_move=allowed is coprime)
        out[name] = t
        print(f"    {name}: {t}")
    t = scan(exhaustive_worlds(), (2, 3), first_move=True)
    out["EXHAUSTIVE"] = t
    print(f"    EXHAUSTIVE: {t}")
    controls = ("LAST-HISTORY", "FREE-STATE")
    seen = []
    for name in controls:
        t = out[name]
        seen.append(t.get("witnessed", 0) + t.get("free", 0) > 0)
        check(f"{name} finds a tie", seen[-1])
    if not all(seen):
        print("    a positive control failed: the coprime arms are not read")
        return
    for name, t in out.items():
        if name in controls:
            continue
        tied = t.get("witnessed", 0) + t.get("free", 0)
        check(f"{name}: no tie in {t.get('fibers', 0)} multi-route "
              "fibers", tied == 0 and t.get("fibers", 0) > 0)
    rand = [t for name, t in out.items()
            if name not in controls and name != "EXHAUSTIVE"]
    print(f"    the four coprime random arms: "
          f"{sum(t.get('fibers', 0) for t in rand)} multi-route fibers")
    co = rand + [out["EXHAUSTIVE"]]
    print(f"    first move hidden at every beta: "
          f"{sum(t.get('first robust', 0) for t in co)} of "
          f"{sum(t.get('first read', 0) for t in co)} reads over the "
          f"coprime arms")


def main():
    section_controls()
    section_rogue()
    section_coarse()
    section_last()
    section_arms()
    print()
    n, ok = len(CHECKS), sum(CHECKS)
    print(f"{ok} of {n} checks pass")
    return 0 if ok == n else 1


if __name__ == "__main__":
    sys.exit(main())
