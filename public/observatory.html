Observatory

In a totally ramified extension of Q2, a unit approximating a root of unity lands, one squaring past the Kummer seat, at a depth that spells the digits of 2/πe, π a uniformizer — a proved staircase, and, run backwards, a design kit: a field built to spell any chosen word.

Fix a prime p and a finite extension K/Qp that is totally ramified of degree e, so its residue field is Fp itself (residue degree f = 1): on this page, a window. Write π for a uniformizer (an element of least positive valuation), v for the valuation normalized by v(π) = 1, and OK for the integers of K. A 1-unit is a unit u with v(u − 1) ≥ 1, its level is v(u − 1), and Ui = 1 + πiOK is the unit filtration. The Kummer seat, below just the seat, is the level i* = e/(p−1), where ζp − 1 would sit if K held a primitive p-th root of unity; it is assumed integral throughout. An Eisenstein polynomial xe + Σ p·bixi − p·d (p ∤ d) defines such a window with its root as π, and every window arises so.

The clock

The two-gear clock theorem

Raising a 1-unit of level i to the p-th power sends it to level ψ(i) = min(p·i, i+e) — the Frobenius gear ×p below the seat, the pump gear +e above it — exactly, for every unit, at every level but the seat itself. In (1 + x)p − 1 the terms p·x and xp sit at levels i+e and p·i and every other binomial term sits above the smaller, so the valuation is their minimum wherever the two differ. At the seat the gears tie and can cancel; the orbit of a unit — its levels under repeated p-th powering — departs from the two-gear law nowhere else.

Scope. Off the seat, a proof for every local field. That cancellation happens nowhere but the seat is a rule in range: exhaustive over 14 mixed-characteristic fields and 2 equal-characteristic rings.

A cancellation at the seat is what this page reads. An arrival class (c, m), with c·pm = i*, is the set of 1-units u = 1 + ρπc (ρ a unit) whose m Frobenius steps carry them onto the seat; such a unit is an approximant of a primitive pm+1-th root of unity, and a window carries one class per cyclotomic layer. Its landing L(u) = v(upm+1 − 1) ≥ p·i* is the level one step past the seat, and the class's arrival spectrum is the set of its landings. Expanding (1 + ρπc)pm+1 term by term, each monomial enters at a level fixed by its exact multinomial p-content, and the landing is the first level whose summed digit is nonzero; call this the game. Below a certain level the summed digits depend on ρ not at all, only on the digits of the unit w = 2/πe (−p/πe at odd p), so a class whose game stops there stops at the same level for every member: its spectrum is a single value, and the class is rigid. The freedom rung is the first level a choice of ρ reaches. Levels are counted from the floor p·i* as rel: L = p·i* + rel. No unit's game passes every level unless the window contains the root it approximates: a stopless game converges to a primitive root of unity.

Since f = 1 the Teichmüller digits w0, w1, … of w are canonical and idempotent (digitp = digit; literal bits at p = 2), and the window word is their in-window vector — (w1, …, w3e/2−1) at p = 2, (w0, …, we) at odd p. The claims below say the spectra are a readout — of the word, digit for digit, and of the ramification of the p-power cyclotomic tower over the window — lawful enough to prove and invertible enough to design.

The ramification reading

An arrival class plays for the primitive pm+1-th roots of unity, and its landing decomposes as a sum of forced approaches to the individual roots, priced by the ramification of K(μpm+1)/K (μn the n-th roots of unity).

The constellation law rule

Where the seat cancels — f = 1 and ζp ∈ K — every unit that starts on the seat lands at exactly p·i* + 1, while a class-1 arrival — a unit of level 1, the class with c = 1 — lands deeper by an extra that reads the field: 0 at Q2(√±2), 1 at Q2(√−5), 2 at Q2(i), 5 at Q2(ζ8). The extra has a classical name: the landing is the avoidance value of the torsion constellation — i* plus the sum of v(u − ζ) over the primitive roots ζ, each approach forced exactly as deep as the window allows (integer levels, Fp-rational residues) and ending where K(ζ)/K is ramified (a fractional level), unramified (a non-rational residue), or where the torsion is in K and the roots' own pairwise distances branch — a tree game. At the μ4 floor — the layer m = 1, playing for the fourth roots of unity — over all six ramified quadratics of Q2 the extra is 0 / 1 / 2 iff K(i)/K is ramified / unramified / split, that letter computed independently of any orbit; the two fields √10 and √−10 were called from √2's chain before they were measured, and measured so. In-field torsion plays the tree: the achieving class-1 unit at ζ8 approaches the four primitive eighth roots at distances {2, 2, 3, 3}, and the four landings of the ζ16 tower (17, 18, 20, 28) were called from the tree before that census ran.

Scope. Rule in range: exhaustive over the units of Q2, its six ramified quadratics, x3 − 2, x6 − 2 and ζ8; sampled at ζ16 and ζ9; ramification labels certified by residue tests and constructed torsion, independently of every orbit.

The tame face rule

At odd p the alphabet is thinner. Arrival classes exist only when p(p−1) | e, and the μp floor never shows the ramified letter: K(ζp) = K((−p/πe)1/(p−1)) is a tame extension by a unit root, always unramified (Wilson's theorem), so at that floor the only question is whether the seat cancels. Whether it does is a residue pigeonhole: with ζp ∈ K the p − 1 torsion directions exhaust Fp× and the seat cancels; without it the residue that would carry ζp lies outside Fp and every orbit runs the bare two-gear law, every spectrum rigid at p·i*. F2 has one nonzero residue, which is why every p = 2, f = 1 seat cancels. The ramified letter returns one layer up, at the μp2 cutoffs below.

Scope. Rule in range at p = 3, 5: exhaustive at the two ramified quadratics of Q3, sampled at five e = 6 fields (p = 3) and two e = 20 fields (p = 5); the p = 2 half of the pigeonhole is the constellation law's census.

The cutoff law rule

Where the seat cancels, the depth of the forced approach is a Kummer defect: δ = maxy v(yp − ζp), how nearly ζp is a p-th power inside K. The pm primitive roots lying over ζp have product upm − ζp exactly, so their approach sum never leaves the window, and each per-root approach ends at the level δ/p (at m = 1). The arrival ceiling is (p−1)·i* + δ, and δ ranges over {ℓ ∈ (i*, p·i*) : p ∤ ℓ} ∪ {p·i*} ∪ {∞}. The letter is the defect read at its three ranges: ramified δ < p·i*, a fractional cut — one value exists at e = 2, which is why all four ramified quadratics of Q2 whose letter is ramified share the cut 3/2, and three e = 6 fields of Q3 share δ = 7, cut 7/3; unramified δ = p·i*, a residue cut at the integer i* — a letter that exists at odd p, since the Artin–Schreier map xp − x on Fp has image {0}: the sextic x6 + 6x5 + 6x4 + 3x3 + 12 measures δ = 9, and ζ9 lies in its unramified cubic lift; split δ = ∞, the torsion in-field. Classically the defect is the Kummer conductor: the ramification break of K(ζp2)/K is t = p·i* − δ, and the textbook break ladder 2k − 1 of Q2(ζ2n) is the case δ = 1. At m ≥ 2 the ceiling reads the pm-power defect, which descends a storey (the storey descent): x18 + 3 has D2 = 21, cut 21/9 = 7/3 again.

Scope. The product reduction and the ceiling identity are derivations general in (p, m); the value menu is derived at m = 1; the field readings are a rule in range over seventeen fields at p = 2, 3, 5 (e ≤ 20, m ≤ 2), the defect and the ceiling measured by independent climbs, the breaks checked by direct Galois action at ζ9 and ζ8.

The storey descent theorem

Write Dm(K) = maxy v(ypm − ζp) for the pm-power defect, so D1 = δ, and eK for the degree of the window K. A storey is a totally ramified step K/C of degree p between windows with ζp ∈ C: K = C(b1/p) with b a uniformizer of C, a radical storey, or b a 1-unit of level l with i*C < l < eC and p ∤ l, a unit-root storey. Every p-th power in K is an element of C to the level L = eK (radical) or eK + p − (p−1)l (unit-root): writing y = Σj<p cjπKj with cj ∈ OC, yp ≡ Σ cjp πKpj mod p, πKp is an element of C to level L, and the map is onto OC mod πKL. Hence ypm ≡ cpm−1 mod πK(m−1)eK + L, and for m ≥ 2, Dm(K) = p·Dm−1(C) whenever p·Dm−1(C) < (m−1)eK + L, which holds at odd p off torsion and off the boundary Dm−1(C) = p·i*C, and at p = 2, m = 2 off the boundary D1(C) = 2i*C. The first rung is Herbrand's: for K ≠ C(ζp2) the compositum K(ζp2)/C is bicyclic with upper breaks the storey's, p·i*C − l, and the cyclotomic, p·i*C − D1(C), and the break of K(ζp2)/K is Herbrand's ψK/C of the cyclotomic one, so D1(K) = D1(C) + eK when the storey's break sits above it (every radical storey, whose break is p·i*C itself) and D1(K) = p·D1(C) + eK − (p−1)l when below. A storey adds eK to the first defect and multiplies every higher one by p; up a radical tower over Qp(ζp) the whole ladder is Dm(Cn) = pm−1·D1(Cn−m+1) down to the floor pn = i*: x18 + 3 reads 25, 21 = 3·7, 9; x54 + 3 reads 79, 75 = 3·25, 63 = 3·21; x16 − 2 reads 31, 30, 28, 24; the unit-root storeys l = 4 over x6 + 3 and l = 8 over x12 + 3 read 25, 21 and 50, 42; and the break of K(ζp2)/K is invariant up every tower built. An unramified extension of K leaves every Dm ≠ p·i* in place, the digit equations below the Artin–Schreier level being rational over the residue field, and moves only the boundary value: the sextic of the cutoff law reads 9 and its unramified cubic lift 16. So the per-root cutoff of a radical tower's storey-m game is the first cut of the floor m − 1 storeys down.

Scope. Theorem: the collapse and the lift are derivations for every storey with (p−1)L ≥ eK (at p = 3, l ≤ 3eC/4 + 1), the first rung Herbrand's for K ≠ C(ζp2); the unramified clause is a derivation checked at three lifts. Checked at thirteen rungs and thirteen first-rung storeys over p = 2, 3, twenty-six fields, every m ≥ 2 value named before its climb ran and the first rung read off the towers, then named at six fresh storeys before theirs; a constructive witness reaches the law's value at every rung. The second branch of the first rung is unrun: the family x12 + 3c, c ≡ 1 (mod 3), reads D1 = 14 at all 81 members below 35, and no storey the root construction seats (l < 3eC/4) has its break below the cyclotomic one.

The odd-p face

The tame readout theorem

Odd p, any window with integral seat, any class (c, m). One formula: L = p·i* + min(δw, pm), where δw = v(w − 1) is the window defect of w = −p/πe. The sign matters: by Wilson's theorem the leading digit w0 is 1 iff ζp ∈ K, so the tame face's thinness is the zeroth digit, the gate: gate closed (w0 ≠ 1, δw = 0) every class is rigid at p·i*; gate open, the next pm − 1 digits stop the game rigidly and freedom arrives at the truncation pm. The window defect is not the Kummer defect: the sextic above has δ = 9 and δw = 3, spectrum minimum 9 + min(3, 3) = 12. δw moves with the choice of π; its pm-truncation does not, and is exactly the invariant content of w − 1. The wildness criterion c(p−1)2 < p, that the first deeper-squaring constant undercuts freedom, holds exactly at the p = 2 top class, the class with c = 1, where the always-open gate, the 3e/2 readout and the skeleton constants below all live; the p = 2 staircases with c > 1 are this formula verbatim.

Scope. Proved twice: by the enumeration, general in (p, c, m, e), and as a corollary of the rung theorem with the second point from the digits. Field verification is a rule in range at p = 3, 5 over twenty fields (e ≤ 20, m ≤ 2), designed defect ladders δw = 1, 2, 3, 5 landing on the formula.

The readout theorem

The readout theorem theorem

K/Q2 a window of degree e (so i* = e), any class (c, m) with c·2m = e. Every spectrum is rigid below its freedom rung, and its minimum is a staircase on the digits of w = 2/πe. A class with c > 1 reads the digits w1 … w2m−1: L = 2e + min(j1, 2m), j1 the position of the first nonzero digit. The window's own top class (c = 1, forcing e a 2-power) reads w1 … w3e/2−1, half again the window's depth: the game stops at the first rel r whose effective digit is nonzero, that digit being wr everywhere except at the two skeleton positions r = e/2 and 5e/4 (one position at e = 2), where it is wr + 1, the added 1 the binomial tail of the seat's basis monomial (1 + π)e (the second point from the digits); freedom at 3e/2. The proof prices every monomial's entry level by its exact 2-content: six enter below 3e/2, the class's own index digit enters twice at rel e and cancels in pairs, and no free digit enters before 3e/2. Corollaries: w1 = 1 locks every class rigid at 2e + 1; the skeleton positions are blind rungs, where the window's own digit is silent and a field whose digits vanish there stops anyway (the pure fields xe − d stop at rel e/2); and there is one digit vector with no stop at all, nonzero exactly on the skeleton, carried by ζ2e at every 2-power depth: the digits of 2/(ζ2e − 1)e are the skeleton. The top readout grows linearly with the window — 2, 5, 11, 23, 47 digits at e = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32.

Scope. Proved for every window of Q2 and every class; f = 1 is essential (digit idempotence). The enumeration lemma is brute-checked to e = 64. The e = 32 face was computed after the proof, the digit counts 1, 3, 7, 15, 47 of its five classes and ζ64's vector {16, 40} written down before the census ran.

The designed readout

The readout runs backwards, and completely: an Eisenstein polynomial's coefficients, written in base-p digits, are the digits of w in another coordinate. Write bi = Σ βi,kpk and d = d0 + Σ δkpk with digits in {0, …, p − 1}; the digit βi,k enters w at level i + ke and δk at level ke, so every level r ≥ 1 carries exactly one coefficient digit, the one at (i, k) = (r mod e, r div e).

The coefficient triangle theorem

Since πe = p(d − Σ biπi), w = −1/(d − Σ biπi), and in the ring where p = −wπe each coefficient digit enters as a monomial at its own level times a power of w, while every other reading of w lands above its entry level — a carry born at level s lands wj at s + e + j, a product's level-r digit reads its factors below r — so wr is ± the level-r coefficient digit plus a function of the digits below. The map from the coefficient digits to the digits of w is unitriangular over Fp at every level (triangular, every diagonal entry ±1), hence a bijection from the Eisenstein polynomials with the open gate (d ≡ −1 mod p; every d at p = 2) onto the digit strings with w0 = 1: the digit string is a complete invariant of the Eisenstein polynomial, and every string is printed by exactly one reduced design, the polynomial with the deeper digits zeroed, which a greedy level-by-level solve finds without disturbing the prefix. Its truncations are the readouts. At levels 1 … 3e/2 − 1 it is the p = 2 window word read off the sub-leading bits (bi mod 2 below e, d mod 4 at e, ⌊bi/2⌋ mod 2 at e + i for i < e/2): the window word is the defining equation's sub-leading bits re-encoded, and designed fields print their words in live spectra, at every class the sampled minimum the staircase on the placed word. At levels 1 … Me, M = vp(i*), it is the digits that fix the jump set of the field, Pagano's invariant of the 1-units (the digit precision), so the jump set is a function of the first Me coefficient digits and of no fewer. The reduction law is its triangularity: a coefficient digit at level ≥ 3e/2 moves no digit of the word, so word(F) = word(reduce(F)) for reduce(F) the reduced design carrying F's first 3e/2 − 1 bits, Eisenstein windows sort into exactly 23e/2−1 word classes, and word(F) = word(G) iff reduce(F) = reduce(G). The cyclotomic anchor Φ2e(x+1), the window Q2(ζ2e), reduces in closed form: by Kummer's binomial parities, reduce(Φ2e(x+1)) = xe + 2xe/2 + 4xe/4 − 6 at every 2-power e ≥ 4, the unique reduced design carrying ζ2e's skeleton word; at e = 32 that three-term field runs its top class stopless to the freedom rung and lands at 112, ζ64's landing, a cyclotomic mimic exact modulo the invisible bits. Past the affine head the rows are polynomials in the digits: at e = 4, w4 = β1,0 + β2,0 + δ1 + β1,0β2,0 and w8 has degree 3; at e = 8 the degree reaches 3 at w11.

Scope. Theorem at every p and every level, using neither a 2-power nor the parity of e; its instances rules: the bijection exhaustive over the 256 Eisenstein polynomials at e = 4 read modulo their first eight coefficient digits (bi mod 4, d mod 8) and the 729 with the open gate at (p, e) = (3, 6) (bi mod 3, d mod 9), and over the 32 / 256 / 2048 reduced designs at e = 4, 6, 8 on the word; the diagonal at 1,136 (polynomial, level) changes to level 80 at e = 16 and at p = 3, 5, each leaving the digits below fixed and running the level's digit through Fp; placement at e = 16 (27 target words); 628 non-reduced fields (e ≤ 16, deep-bit perturbations and a random Eisenstein zoo, negative coefficients included) each printing their reduction's word; the anchor's closed form by direct binomial bits and by the Kummer count at e = 4 … 64; the live-spectrum tie a rule in range at e = 8, 16; the rows records of the computation at e = 4 and 8.

The odd-p triangle theorem

At odd p the whole game reads depth ≤ e, so the in-window word (w0, …, we) is the coefficient triangle's first e + 1 levels: a function of (b1 … be−1 mod p; d mod p2) alone — no second diagonal, the deeper digits first acting at level e + 1. The map is unitriangular with diagonal −w02, hence a bijection, and the greedy solve designs any word: x6 + 6x4 − 15 realizes the landing 15 in the class c = m = 1 at p = 3, a value no other censused field of that class takes.

Scope. The diagonal pricing and the unitriangularity are general in (p, e), seat-free; the bijection is exhaustive at (p, e) = (3, 4), (5, 3), (3, 6) — 162 / 500 / 1458 words, all distinct; the designed landings are a rule in range at p = 3, 5.

The spectrum is the odd-p invariant rule

At odd p the word is a chart, not a field invariant: a change of uniformizer π ↦ π(1 + aπk) moves w at depth pm·k exactly, mixing digit r into r + k with coefficient r·a, and the word is invariant only from k > c(p−1). The spectrum is the invariant, and a field's set of chart-words is exactly the solution variety of its forced-digit laws: Q3(ζ9) has three words, all realized, and the designed word [1, 0, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0] is Q3(ζ9) in another chart. And at m = 1 a game that passes the whole window is a torsion detector: every level above e carries a free digit, the climb is constructive past the precision at which Hensel's lemma lifts a root, and no window without ζp2 passes — so the designed x6 − 3x4 + 3 does not mimic the cyclotomic anchor, it generates Q3(ζ9). The p = 2 mimic above is a wildness privilege.

Scope. Rule in range at p = 3, 5 for the chart action; at every field where the detector fires the root of unity is certified by Hensel's lemma.

The telescope

The telescope rule

The two gears are Herbrand's two slopes: ψ = p·φseat identically, with φseat the function of slopes 1 and 1/p breaking at the seat. At m ≥ 2 the game's monomials sort into the tower's storeys by j = vp(A), A the monomial's part total, and scaling sizes by p with offsets fixed carries the (c, m−1) game onto storey j ≥ 1 verbatim — multinomial valuations and digit values preserved (Wilson's theorem in blocks) — with onset relj(c, m) = pj · rel0(c, m−j), the latter taken in that class's own window of degree e/pj. Every storey opens as a copy of the game one window down. The only m-dependence left is a sign, so gate-open the shifted digits are pinned at every storey: wrelj = (−1)m+1−j and wrelj+pj = (−1)m−j·k1, the fork digit k1 = wpm returning at every storey with alternating sign. In one digit vector: Q3(ζ81) has k1 = 2 and pins (2, 2, 1, 1) at rels 84, 87, 136, 137, and six single-coefficient perturbations of Φ81(x+1) stop at 81 plus the violated rel, exactly. The filtration is the classical one: with σa: ζ ↦ ζa the Galois action, v(ζa − ζ) on Q3(ζ81) is the seat ladder {c·pj}, lower breaks one below ({0, 2, 8, 26}), upper numbering equally spaced. The game reads the cyclotomic tower's ramification filtration through the unit filtration; the words, pins and designed stops are digit-level fine structure the classical objects do not carry.

Scope. The Herbrand identity is exact, swept p ≤ 7; the scaling bijection is a derivation, its finite face verified exhaustively in range; the pin ladder is a rule in range at (p, c, m) = (3, 1, 3), gate-open, the m = 3 sign fixed as the falsification point before the run and held, and Q5(ζ125)'s pins called before they were read. The ζ81 filtration is a measurement; the two slopes and the cyclotomic break ladder are classical (Serre).

Related work

Krasner's lemma and its sharp forms (Yoshida's ultrametric on Eisenstein coefficients, Keating's perturbation congruences, Fontaine's property (Pm)) price coefficient proximity; the Ore–Newton polygon and residual polynomial ladder of the Pauli school builds invariants and realization templates from leading coefficient data; the ramification filtrations of normal towers are classical (Serre, Sen; the Lubin–Tate torsion breaks). The canonical Eisenstein forms — Monge's reduced polynomials, and the forms of Pauli–Roblot, Greve–Pauli and Pauli–Sinclair — cut coefficients at ramification-dependent levels and yield reduced sets of polynomials naming each extension, never a bijection onto a word, and none inverts a prescribed arrival sequence to a polynomial: the triangle's inverse is new there. The invariant the literature does own is Pagano's jump set of the field, the subject of The jump set.

What the jump set holds

The 1-units of a window are a filtered module free on one generator per level of T* modulo one relation, and the Pareto frontier of that relation's coordinates is the field's jump set. Every arrival class's landing spectrum lies in a bracket the jump set fixes, both ends attained: its floor e* + pm (7e/2 at the wild top class) against the first frontier weight of order at most m, so every class minimum is the rung under its own floor. The second frontier weight is the window defect read up to the top class's truncation, e* + min(δw, X) with X = pM, e/2 at the wild top class, at every p, which makes the tame readout, the p = 2 staircases and the readout theorem's top-class law corollaries of one structure theorem; and over Haar-random windows the jump set's frequency is a closed form, derived from the coefficients through the triangle, the rung's geometric. The jump set holds the three results with their derivations.

What these claims assemble to: the finite-time transient of the p-power map on a window's units is an exact digit channel. The classical filtration is its coarse structure, the jump set its middle, the word its fine structure — and the channel runs in both directions: measured, it reads the field; designed, it prints any chosen word.
