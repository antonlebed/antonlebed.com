Observatory

In a totally ramified extension of Q2, a unit approximating a root of unity lands, one squaring past the Kummer seat, at a depth that spells the digits of 2/πe, π a uniformizer — a proved staircase, and, run backwards, a design kit: a field built to spell any chosen word.

Fix a prime p and a finite extension K/Qp that is totally ramified of degree e, so its residue field is Fp itself (residue degree f = 1): on this page, a window. Write π for a uniformizer (an element of least positive valuation), v for the valuation normalized by v(π) = 1, and OK for the integers of K. A 1-unit is a unit u with v(u − 1) ≥ 1, its level is v(u − 1), and Ui = 1 + πiOK is the unit filtration. The Kummer seat, below just the seat, is the level i* = e/(p−1), where ζp − 1 would sit if K held a primitive p-th root of unity; it is assumed integral throughout. An Eisenstein polynomial xe + Σ p·bixi − p·d (p ∤ d) defines such a window with its root as π, and every window arises so.

The clock

The two-gear clock theorem

Raising a 1-unit of level i to the p-th power sends it to level ψ(i) = min(p·i, i+e) — the Frobenius gear ×p below the seat, the pump gear +e above it — exactly, for every unit, at every level but the seat itself. In (1 + x)p − 1 the terms p·x and xp sit at levels i+e and p·i and every other binomial term sits above the smaller, so the valuation is their minimum wherever the two differ. At the seat the gears tie and can cancel; the orbit of a unit — its levels under repeated p-th powering — departs from the two-gear law nowhere else.

Scope. Off the seat, a proof for every local field. That cancellation happens nowhere but the seat is a rule in range: exhaustive over 14 mixed-characteristic fields and 2 equal-characteristic rings.

A cancellation at the seat is what this page reads. An arrival class (c, m), with c·pm = i*, is the set of 1-units u = 1 + ρπc (ρ a unit) whose m Frobenius steps carry them onto the seat; such a unit is an approximant of a primitive pm+1-th root of unity, and a window carries one class per cyclotomic layer. Its landing L(u) = v(upm+1 − 1) ≥ p·i* is the level one step past the seat, and the class's arrival spectrum is the set of its landings. Expanding (1 + ρπc)pm+1 term by term, each monomial enters at a level fixed by its exact multinomial p-content, and the landing is the first level whose summed digit is nonzero; call this the game. Below a certain level the summed digits depend on ρ not at all, only on the digits of the unit w = 2/πe (−p/πe at odd p), so a class whose game stops there stops at the same level for every member: its spectrum is a single value, and the class is rigid. The freedom rung is the first level a choice of ρ reaches. Levels are counted from the floor p·i* as rel: L = p·i* + rel. No unit's game passes every level unless the window contains the root it approximates: a stopless game converges to a primitive root of unity.

Since f = 1 the Teichmüller digits w0, w1, … of w are canonical and idempotent (digitp = digit; literal bits at p = 2), and the window word is their in-window vector — (w1, …, w3e/2−1) at p = 2, (w0, …, we) at odd p. The claims below say the spectra are a readout — of the word, digit for digit, and of the ramification of the p-power cyclotomic tower over the window — lawful enough to prove and invertible enough to design.

The ramification reading

An arrival class plays for the primitive pm+1-th roots of unity, and its landing decomposes as a sum of forced approaches to the individual roots, priced by the ramification of K(μpm+1)/K (μn the n-th roots of unity).

The constellation law rule

Where the seat cancels — f = 1 and ζp ∈ K — every unit that starts on the seat lands at exactly p·i* + 1, while a class-1 arrival — a unit of level 1, the class with c = 1 — lands deeper by an extra that reads the field: 0 at Q2(√±2), 1 at Q2(√−5), 2 at Q2(i), 5 at Q2(ζ8). The extra has a classical name: the landing is the avoidance value of the torsion constellation — i* plus the sum of v(u − ζ) over the primitive roots ζ, each approach forced exactly as deep as the window allows (integer levels, Fp-rational residues) and ending where K(ζ)/K is ramified (a fractional level), unramified (a non-rational residue), or where the torsion is in K and the roots' own pairwise distances branch — a tree game. At the μ4 floor — the layer m = 1, playing for the fourth roots of unity — over all six ramified quadratics of Q2 the extra is 0 / 1 / 2 iff K(i)/K is ramified / unramified / split, that letter computed independently of any orbit; the two fields √10 and √−10 were called from √2's chain before they were measured, and measured so. In-field torsion plays the tree: the achieving class-1 unit at ζ8 approaches the four primitive eighth roots at distances {2, 2, 3, 3}, and the four landings of the ζ16 tower (17, 18, 20, 28) were called from the tree before that census ran.

Scope. Rule in range: exhaustive over the units of Q2, its six ramified quadratics, x3 − 2, x6 − 2 and ζ8; sampled at ζ16 and ζ9; ramification labels certified by residue tests and constructed torsion, independently of every orbit.

The tame face rule

At odd p the alphabet is thinner. Arrival classes exist only when p(p−1) | e, and the μp floor never shows the ramified letter: K(ζp) = K((−p/πe)1/(p−1)) is a tame extension by a unit root, always unramified (Wilson's theorem), so at that floor the only question is whether the seat cancels. Whether it does is a residue pigeonhole: with ζp ∈ K the p − 1 torsion directions exhaust Fp× and the seat cancels; without it the residue that would carry ζp lies outside Fp and every orbit runs the bare two-gear law, every spectrum rigid at p·i*. F2 has one nonzero residue, which is why every p = 2, f = 1 seat cancels. The ramified letter returns one layer up, at the μp2 cutoffs below.

Scope. Rule in range at p = 3, 5: exhaustive at the two ramified quadratics of Q3, sampled at five e = 6 fields (p = 3) and two e = 20 fields (p = 5); the p = 2 half of the pigeonhole is the constellation law's census.

The cutoff law rule

Where the seat cancels, the depth of the forced approach is a Kummer defect: δ = maxy v(yp − ζp), how nearly ζp is a p-th power inside K. The pm primitive roots lying over ζp have product upm − ζp exactly, so their approach sum never leaves the window, and each per-root approach ends at the level δ/p (at m = 1). The arrival ceiling is (p−1)·i* + δ, and δ ranges over {ℓ ∈ (i*, p·i*) : p ∤ ℓ} ∪ {p·i*} ∪ {∞}. The letter is the defect read at its three ranges: ramified δ < p·i*, a fractional cut — one value exists at e = 2, which is why all four ramified quadratics of Q2 whose letter is ramified share the cut 3/2, and three e = 6 fields of Q3 share δ = 7, cut 7/3; unramified δ = p·i*, a residue cut at the integer i* — a letter that exists at odd p, since the Artin–Schreier map xp − x on Fp has image {0}: the sextic x6 + 6x5 + 6x4 + 3x3 + 12 measures δ = 9, and ζ9 lies in its unramified cubic lift; split δ = ∞, the torsion in-field. Classically the defect is the Kummer conductor: the ramification break of K(ζp2)/K is t = p·i* − δ, and the textbook break ladder 2k − 1 of Q2(ζ2n) is the case δ = 1. At m = 2 the ceiling reads the p2-power defect: x18 + 3 has D2 = 21, cut 21/9 = 7/3 again; whether that defect descends to a subfield in general is open.

Scope. The product reduction and the ceiling identity are derivations general in (p, m); the value menu is derived at m = 1; the field readings are a rule in range over seventeen fields at p = 2, 3, 5 (e ≤ 20, m ≤ 2), the defect and the ceiling measured by independent climbs, the breaks checked by direct Galois action at ζ9 and ζ8.

The odd-p face

The tame readout theorem

Odd p, any window with integral seat, any class (c, m). One formula: L = p·i* + min(δw, pm), where δw = v(w − 1) is the window defect of w = −p/πe. The sign matters: by Wilson's theorem the leading digit w0 is 1 iff ζp ∈ K, so the tame face's thinness is the zeroth digit, the gate: gate closed (w0 ≠ 1, δw = 0) every class is rigid at p·i*; gate open, the next pm − 1 digits stop the game rigidly and freedom arrives at the truncation pm. The window defect is not the Kummer defect: the sextic above has δ = 9 and δw = 3, spectrum minimum 9 + min(3, 3) = 12. δw moves with the choice of π; its pm-truncation does not, and is exactly the invariant content of w − 1. In-window the odd-p game is two monomials and nothing else — no constants, no blind digits. The wildness criterion c(p−1)2 < p, that the first deeper-squaring constant undercuts freedom, fails at every odd p and every p = 2 class with c > 1, and holds exactly at the p = 2 top class, the class with c = 1, where the always-open gate, the 3e/2 readout and the skeleton constants below all live; the p = 2 staircases with c > 1 are this formula verbatim.

Scope. The enumeration proof is general in (p, c, m, e); field verification is a rule in range at p = 3, 5 over twenty fields (e ≤ 20, m ≤ 2), designed defect ladders δw = 1, 2, 3, 5 landing on the formula.

The readout theorem

The readout theorem theorem

K/Q2 a window of degree e (so i* = e), any class (c, m) with c·2m = e. Every spectrum is rigid below its freedom rung, and its minimum is a staircase on the digits of w = 2/πe. A class with c > 1 reads the digits w1 … w2m−1: L = 2e + min(j1, 2m), j1 the position of the first nonzero digit. The window's own top class (c = 1, forcing e a 2-power) reads w1 … w3e/2−1, half again the window's depth: the game stops at the first rel r whose effective digit is nonzero, that digit being wr everywhere except at the two skeleton positions r = e/2 and 5e/4 (one position at e = 2), where it is wr + 1, the added 1 a constant the binomial game contributes itself; freedom at 3e/2. The proof prices every monomial's entry level by its exact 2-content: six enter below 3e/2, the class's own index digit enters twice at rel e and cancels in pairs, and no free digit enters before 3e/2. Corollaries: w1 = 1 locks every class rigid at 2e + 1; the skeleton positions are blind rungs, where the window's own digit is silent and a field whose digits vanish there stops anyway (the pure fields xe − d stop at rel e/2); and there is one digit vector with no stop at all, nonzero exactly on the skeleton, carried by ζ2e at every 2-power depth: the digits of 2/(ζ2e − 1)e are the skeleton. The top readout grows linearly with the window — 2, 5, 11, 23, 47 digits at e = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32.

Scope. Proved for every window of Q2 and every class; f = 1 is essential (digit idempotence). The enumeration lemma is brute-checked to e = 64. The e = 32 face was computed after the proof, the digit counts 1, 3, 7, 15, 47 of its five classes and ζ64's vector {16, 40} written down before the census ran.

The designed readout

The readout runs backwards. Over Z2 take F(x) = xe + Σ 2bixi − 2d, d odd, e even, π a root of F.

The triangle theorem

Order the sub-leading coefficient bits of F by level: bi mod 2 at level i (i = 1 … e−1), d mod 4 at level e, ⌊bi/2⌋ mod 2 at level e + i (i = 1 … e/2−1): 3e/2 − 1 bits, the word length exactly. The map bits → window word is unitriangular over F2 — digit r equals the level-r bit plus a function of strictly earlier bits — hence a bijection: every word in {0,1}3e/2−1 is printed by exactly one reduced design (an F whose coefficients carry only the listed bits), and a greedy level-by-level solve places any chosen word without disturbing its prefix. Designed fields print their words in live spectra: at every class the sampled minimum is the staircase on the placed word. The window word is the sub-leading Eisenstein data, re-encoded bijectively.

Scope. Unitriangularity is proved for every even e, nowhere using a 2-power; the bijection is exhaustive at e = 4, 6, 8 (32 / 256 / 2048 designs, every word realized), placement at e = 16 (27 target words); the live-spectrum tie is a rule in range at e = 8, 16.

The reduction law theorem

Every deeper coefficient bit of F feeds a level ≥ 3e/2, so word(F) = word(reduce(F)), reduce(F) the reduced design carrying F's triangle bits. With the bijection: the word is a complete invariant of the sub-leading bits, Eisenstein windows sort into exactly 23e/2−1 word classes, and word(F) = word(G) iff reduce(F) = reduce(G). The cyclotomic anchor Φ2e(x+1), the window Q2(ζ2e), reduces in closed form: by Kummer's binomial parities, reduce(Φ2e(x+1)) = xe + 2xe/2 + 4xe/4 − 6 at every 2-power e ≥ 4, the unique reduced design carrying ζ2e's skeleton word. At e = 32 that three-term field runs its top class stopless to the freedom rung and lands at 112, ζ64's landing: a cyclotomic mimic, exact modulo the invisible bits.

Scope. The level pricing is the triangle's own, general in even e; the closed form is verified by direct binomial bits and by the Kummer count independently at e = 4 … 64; 628 non-reduced fields (deep-bit perturbations and a random Eisenstein zoo, e ≤ 16) each print their reduction's word.

The odd-p triangle theorem

At odd p the channel is thinner and closes at the window edge: for F = xe + Σ p·bixi − p·d, the in-window word (w0, …, we) is a function of (b1 … be−1 mod p; d mod p2) alone — no second diagonal; digit k of bi feeds level i + ke, so the deeper digits first act at level e + 1. The map is unitriangular with diagonal −w02, hence a bijection, and the greedy solve designs any word: x6 + 6x4 − 15 realizes the landing 15 in the class c = m = 1 at p = 3, a value no other censused field of that class takes.

Scope. The diagonal pricing and the unitriangularity are general in (p, e), seat-free; the bijection is exhaustive at (p, e) = (3, 4), (5, 3), (3, 6) — 162 / 500 / 1458 words, all distinct; the designed landings are a rule in range at p = 3, 5.

The spectrum is the odd-p invariant rule

At odd p the word is a chart, not a field invariant: a change of uniformizer π ↦ π(1 + aπk) moves w at depth pm·k exactly, mixing digit r into r + k with coefficient r·a, and the word is invariant only from k > c(p−1). The spectrum is the invariant, and a field's set of chart-words is exactly the solution variety of its forced-digit laws: Q3(ζ9) has three words, all realized, and the designed word [1, 0, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0] is Q3(ζ9) in another chart. And at m = 1 a game that passes the whole window is a torsion detector: every level above e carries a free digit, the climb is constructive past the precision at which Hensel's lemma lifts a root, and no window without ζp2 passes — so the designed x6 − 3x4 + 3 does not mimic the cyclotomic anchor, it generates Q3(ζ9). The p = 2 mimic above is a wildness privilege.

Scope. Rule in range at p = 3, 5 for the chart action; at every field where the detector fires the root of unity is certified by Hensel's lemma.

The telescope

The telescope rule

The two gears are Herbrand's two slopes: ψ = p·φseat identically, with φseat the function of slopes 1 and 1/p breaking at the seat. At m ≥ 2 the game's monomials sort into storeys by j = vp(A), A the monomial's part total, and scaling sizes by p with offsets fixed carries the (c, m−1) game onto storey j ≥ 1 verbatim — multinomial valuations and digit values preserved (Wilson's theorem in blocks) — with onset relj(c, m) = pj · rel0(c, m−j), the latter taken in that class's own window of degree e/pj. Every storey opens as a copy of the game one window down. The only m-dependence left is a sign, so gate-open the shifted digits are pinned at every storey: wrelj = (−1)m+1−j and wrelj+pj = (−1)m−j·k1, the fork digit k1 = wpm returning at every storey with alternating sign. In one digit vector: Q3(ζ81) has k1 = 2 and pins (2, 2, 1, 1) at rels 84, 87, 136, 137, and six single-coefficient perturbations of Φ81(x+1) stop at 81 plus the violated rel, exactly. The filtration is the classical one: with σa: ζ ↦ ζa the Galois action, v(ζa − ζ) on Q3(ζ81) is the seat ladder {c·pj}, lower breaks one below ({0, 2, 8, 26}), upper numbering equally spaced. The game reads the cyclotomic tower's ramification filtration through the unit filtration; the words, pins and designed stops are digit-level fine structure the classical objects do not carry.

Scope. The Herbrand identity is exact, swept p ≤ 7; the scaling bijection is a derivation, its finite face verified exhaustively in range; the pin ladder is a rule in range at (p, c, m) = (3, 1, 3), gate-open, the m = 3 sign fixed as the falsification point before the run and held, and Q5(ζ125)'s pins called before they were read. The ζ81 filtration is a measurement; the two slopes and the cyclotomic break ladder are classical (Serre).

Related work

Krasner's lemma and its sharp forms (Yoshida's ultrametric on Eisenstein coefficients, Keating's perturbation congruences, Fontaine's property (Pm)) price coefficient proximity; the Ore–Newton polygon and residual polynomial ladder of the Pauli school builds invariants and realization templates from leading coefficient data; the ramification filtrations of normal towers are classical (Serre, Sen; the Lubin–Tate torsion breaks). The canonical Eisenstein forms — Monge's reduced polynomials, and the forms of Pauli–Roblot, Greve–Pauli and Pauli–Sinclair — cut coefficients at ramification-dependent levels and yield reduced sets of polynomials naming each extension, never a bijection onto a word, and none inverts a prescribed arrival sequence to a polynomial: the triangle's inverse is new there. The invariant the literature does own is Pagano's jump set of the field (arXiv:1810.09975). The 1-units form a filtered module over Zp, and it is quasi-free: free on one generator 1 + πa at each level a of T* = {i < p·i* : p ∤ i} ∪ {p·i*}, modulo one relation, the torsion. The orbit of that relation vector under the filtered automorphisms is classified by a pair (I, β), I ⊂ T* and β strictly decreasing with the weights ψβ(a)(a) strictly increasing: a coordinate at level a divisible by p exactly b times has weight ψb(a), the level it reaches after b powerings, and (I, β) is the Pareto frontier of the vector's coordinates under (divisibility, weight). His coefficient readout of the jump set is stated for regimes that exclude p = 2 with e even, the triangle's regime. The two objects have now been printed side by side.

The rung is the jump set's second weight rule

Call the rung of a window of Q2 its class-1 landing minimum, the top class's staircase value. At every field the first frontier point of the relation vector is (1, log2 e + 1) at weight 2e, the seat's image under the pump gear, and the rung is the weight of the second point — the first level above the seat's image at which the torsion relation is essential. The cyclotomic anchors ζ2e have I = {1} alone, no second point, and their stopless walk lands at the freedom rung, 7e/2 = 7, 14, 28. The three objects nest strictly, word → (I, β) → rung, each arrow many-to-one: at e = 4, 17 words, 8 jump sets, 6 rungs, and at e = 8, 16 jump sets against 9 rungs. Read this way, the seat cancellation of the two-gear clock is the relation vector's frontier past its first point; the word is finer than the jump set, and what the frontier points past the second read is open — the landings of the intermediate classes are the first suspect.

Scope. Rule in range over 120 fields at e = 2, 4, 8: the six ramified quadratics, the 48 Eisenstein quartics and ζ8, 64 octics and ζ16. Controls: at the 55 fields of e = 2, 4 the frontier's last order equals the 2-power torsion order (Pagano's Proposition 3.42) and the frontier is unchanged under two changes of uniformizer, the latter also at the pure octics; the hand value at x2 − 2 prints verbatim. The mechanism is a sketch, not a derivation.
