The recovery chart

The knife edge run backwards: every re-import that buys the deleted move back, one door per row, what each door actually sells, and the surface where decidability turns out to live at the interface JOINTLY, in what the boundary exposes and what the machine can time.

The machine is the growth apparatus of the Growth section — the tower, the integers modulo the product of the first primes, growing one prime at a time — and it has two faces; p and q name primes throughout. On the depth face the state is a modulus N read as a vector of exponents — each prime p a window carrying the depth vp(N) — every move multiplies N, and a test reads presence, whether p divides N. On the element face the machine holds registers in Z/N over a set of windows that grows: channel-local ring operations (add, subtract, multiply, write a constant), the pseudo-inverse, inverting each nonzero residue and keeping zero at zero, channel reads, and one global zero-test, the AND of the per-window zero bits; a window adjoined mid-run is born at zero. Base extension sets a new window from the lift of the others, the integer their residues jointly name; a window is synced when its residue is the lift's. Universality means Minsky's — two counters with increment, decrement and an exact zero-test — and needs a borrow, a move that lowers a counter while a test reads the value it descends through; the depth face has none, the borrow being the archimedean place — the size of an integer — turned into a move, which is what leaves that face one move short (the knife edge). A door is a read the construction deleted, re-imported as a primitive. A read is a ratchet when it flips at most once per run and re-readable when it can be consulted again with a new answer.

The grading question under every row: what does the re-import let the machine read of a value as it moves? The candidate invariant "universal exactly when the value a down-move destroys is read at the archimedean place" is false at the first row, where universality is bought with no size read and no value destroyed. What survives as the currency is a derived down-move, a readable quantity some move lowers while a test reads it, which need not be stored anywhere: it can be synthesized as the difference of two rising ladders, or manufactured natively by a sparse residue under a moving pointer. And the answer splits by face: the depth face's door buys possibility, its bare class being decidable; the element face is universal with no import, so its doors sell per-step exactness and never possibility.

Row 1: the second ruler

The door: prime-power depth read at two or more places at once, the machine layer of the ruler ladder, which grades arithmetic over addition by how many independent scales its predicates read. The model is the growth machine with tests upgraded from presence reads to three-way depth comparisons sign(α·vp − β·vq − γ); moves still multiply, depths never fall, nothing archimedean is imported. The price starts at the reads: depth beyond 1 means local rings and not residue fields, so the door re-imports the depth axis the tower's squarefree moduli drop.

The purchase is quantized: one comparison buys nothing, two independent comparisons buy everything rule

Every test reading one depth difference v2 − v3, at any offsets, plus presence reads elsewhere, makes the machine bisimilar to a one-counter machine, so halting stays decidable (one-counter automata, Valiant–Paterson 1975): the growth machine's monotone decidability proof dies here, a comparison flipping back to false as the depths leapfrog, but the one-counter argument survives. Two independent comparisons, X = v2 − v3 and Y = v3 − v5 with equality reads, run a two-counter Minsky machine exactly, every macro move a multiplication. The borrow is synthesized: no ladder is lowered, but on the derived counter v2 − v3 the move INC3, raising v3 by one, is a value-reading down-move — two increments plus a comparison equal one borrow. The threshold is the second independent comparison at any window count from two: across three or more as two differences, and at exactly two as a second ratio on one pair (two ratios). The mirror: exact-configuration reachability stays decidable, monotone runs to a fixed target being bounded, while halting goes undecidable — the exact reverse of reset nets, where reachability is undecidable and termination decidable (Dufourd–Finkel–Schnoebelen 1998).

Scope. The one-comparison bisimulation is proved, every move and test factoring through the difference, and verified on a three-program battery with one-counter decidability cited; the two-comparison simulation is verified step-exact with Minsky cited. Never an exhaustive claim over programs.

Two places at two ratios are universal, and the miss was the gadget's rule

At exactly two places the linear route is closed: two monotone depths' moves span a two-generator cone in any linear encoding, at most a half-plane, which cannot carry both directions of two counters. Take windows p, q with tests at the ratios 1:1 and α:β in lowest terms, α > β; the register is the common depth u at home, vp = vq = u; a c-step raises vp, a d-step raises vq, and a test fires when its form α·vp − β·vq − γ reads zero. The one-walk gadget, one descent onto the α:β line and one climb back home, multiplies by α/β only when β | u and lands at ⌈αu/β⌉ otherwise, an affine miss off the multiplicative lattice that destroys any exponent encoding. But a cyclic pattern of a c-steps and b d-steps drops the α:β form by a constant per cycle and lands home at an affine image per residue class, so a pattern is a generalized-Collatz instruction whose multiplier is chosen. The read: j repetitions of cβ−1dα−1, then c-steps HOME until the 1:1 form fires — the leg that restores vp = vq, which the pattern by itself never does — fire for every u and land at α·u, the firing substep reading u mod j — a residue read whose only cost is the junk factor α, a multiplier harmless to any exponent encoding that avoids the primes of α. The multiplier: the block a = rβ − sα, b = α(r − s), walked home the same way, lands at (r/s)·u exactly on s | u for every r/s ≥ α − β + 1 with s coprime to αβ (below that threshold ×9/5 compiles by luck and ×11/7 does not), the offset zeroed by a finite table. A FRACTRAN program — a list of fractions, each step multiplying by the first whose denominator divides the register, halting when none does; Turing-complete (Conway) — over primes coprime to αβ, each fraction padded to the threshold by a power of a junk prime, one coprime to the program and to β whose stripped power leaves the program's own state, then runs step-exact: Conway's multiplication program in 25 steps on two places at 3:2, the trace stripped of 3s equal to the reference interpreter's at every step.

Scope. The cone lemma and the cyclic-pattern and multiplier laws are proved and verified at five ratios; the compile is verified step-exact; FRACTRAN universality is cited.

Row 2: the remembering borrow

Answered upstream, on the Computation landing, and the comparison is between two machines' ONLY down-move, never between a machine and itself plus a primitive: a plain decrement beside the native zero-test, the remembering borrow, is exactly Minsky, while a machine whose only down-move is the forgetful borrow — reset, clearing a window and reading nothing on the way down — is a cliff down to finite-state: increment, reset and zero-test are the reset corner (Computation). The row is the chart's calibration: the danger is the reading, not the lowering.

Row 3: the height cut

The door: the one place the tower natively re-imports the archimedean. The ring's error-correcting code is a height cut: its codewords are the elements whose lift lies below D, the product of the data windows, the low primes the value is stored in, so the remaining parity windows are fixed by the data and a check re-derives them (The Object). Ring operations do not preserve the cut, so state evolution must re-derive parity per step, which is base extension. The model is the element machine over a growing squarefree window set, no depth axis, with base extension as the door primitive.

Parity per step is a borrow, and without it the zero-test lies in both directions rule

On elements the down-move x − 1 and the zero-test are both native; what is missing is exactness. A window adjoined mid-run is born at zero rather than at the lift's residue, and the unsynced zero-test reads a false zero at the wrap and a false nonzero from the frozen offset — window 7 born at count 4 holds 3 when the counter truly returns to 0. Base extension is the missing sync: grow-and-extend before each increment keeps every register's lift equal to its counter, and a two-counter machine runs step-exactly, its lift below N through 60 grown increments. The row reduces to row 2: parity per step turns the ring's wrapped subtraction into the remembering borrow, the height certificate being what remembers. The freeze lemma prices the offset exactly: a window p born at count c0, when the product of the multipliers by units applied so far was M0, reads rp = (c − (M/M0)·c0) mod p under state-independent adds and unit multipliers, M being that product now, so repair from a synced window is exact under the cut and a reset re-syncs at zero alone.

Scope. The simulation is verified step-exact on seeds 0 to 8 and both lies exhibited; Minsky universality cited. With the bare class universal (the frontier rider), what the door sells is per-step exactness, an O(1) lift certificate against the rider's unary time.

The keystone lemma: base extension from a growing source is non-ring-computable, and two codes part on what the dynamics computes rule

Every state-independent composite of ring operations is channel-local by the Chinese remainder theorem, so on a window born with a constant it yields a constant, while the target lift(x) mod p is not constant in x: CRT independence itself forbids the door at that layer, the residue number system's own engineering fact — base extension is the one non-channel-local operation, the wrap count the archimedean bit (Szabó–Tanaka 1967). Scoped honestly, a program can rebuild a lift residue from any source of windows it can name, so bounded-source extension is native; what no fixed program reaches is extension from an unboundedly growing source, the addressing wall: finitely many window names against unboundedly many windows. A syndrome is the read a code checks membership by. Beside the height cut sits the dictionary code of Meaning, whose codewords form a subgroup of the units, and lying wholly below the cut does not tell the two apart, since a coset of such a subgroup can: {31, 101} = 31·{1, 101·31−1}, an order-2 coset below D = 210 in Z/2310. What does is what the dynamics computes: the height-cut syndrome is the predicate [lift < D], aperiodic and non-ring-computable to re-derive, while the dictionary syndrome is channel-local and survives multiplication by a unit, the move that code runs on. Checking a codeword is free; re-deriving where the state sits against the cut is the whole tax. The same fact at the pseudo-inverse: to test whether q divides a count c, q must be one of the windows; to divide by q it must not be, q then a unit, and the pseudo-inverse divides exactly while writing 0 into window q, burning that window's sync.

Scope. The lemma is proved for state-independent ring-op composites, any polynomial map and any register count, and exhausted at N = 2310; the division-sync tension checked on 42 inputs, the quotient's residue in window q lost on 33 of them.

Row 3 without the door: the bare class and its allocator

The frontier rider: the bare element class is universal rule

The door-free element machine — growth, ring operations, channel reads, no base extension — is Turing-universal. The construction is sparse: a counter lives as a residue in one pointed window and as literal 0 everywhere else, the pointer an idempotent register re-seated on the growth frontier at every increment. One increment is one grow, so the pointed prime — the (3 + g)-th after g grows, at least g + 4 — always exceeds the count: the value stays inside the first period of every read that touches it and the zero-test never gets the chance to lie. Born-at-zero, the no-door clause itself, is the encoding's free sync, every unpointed window's intended content being 0. The lemma "periodic reads cannot assemble an aperiodic borrow" is false as stated: a periodic read is exact below its period, and growth mints periods faster than any native count climbs. The rider breaks no wall: its one read across all windows is the zero-test, and the keystone lemma, about state-independent composites, is untouched: the rider moves its count by a state-dependent unary loop.

Scope. Step-exact on the Minsky battery at seeds 0 to 10 and on a 2,000-operation random schedule, machine-checked at every executed step, the construction program-uniform, the pointed prime exceeding the count by at least 6 over the battery; Minsky cited. A wrap control exhibits the periodic lie the pointer discipline prevents.

The door sells efficiency, never possibility: a quadratic factor, tight rule

An increment at value v costs v transfer passes, the unary steps that carry the value into the fresh window one unit at a time, so counting one register to T costs T(T − 1)/2 against the door's T, one synced write per increment: bare ≤ door², and the quadratic is tight because that cost is counted and not bounded, a value never exceeding its increment count. The coarse classes — decidable, P, NP, PSPACE — are closed under a polynomial factor, so the door crosses no class boundary; a quadratic factor is still a real polynomial cost, though no lower bound over all bare programs is proved. Two cost layers stack and only the lower is the door's: the encoding blow-up of a two-counter simulation of a Turing machine (Schroeppel 1972) is inherited equally by door machine and bare rider, and the door removes only the carrier-sync layer. The literature's exponential unary gaps live on other axes — the number of counters (Fischer–Meyer–Rosenberg 1968) or the input encoding's length (Berman 1978) — never the per-operation sync cost of one counter.

Scope. The price is measured directly, counting to T bare against door; the tightness is proved; the class facts are cited.

The minimal carrier FOR THE RIDER: the borrow is a fresh-register supply rule

The rider runs on the even tower (moduli 4, 6, 8, …: every window composite, every pair sharing a factor, no field, no single-ring CRT reading) and on the successor tower (moduli 2, 3, 4, …, the slowest strictly increasing supply — the sequence of moduli the fresh windows are minted at — with the pointed modulus a constant 4 above the count). A supply capped at 8 lies at count exactly 8. So the rider's carrier is an unbounded supply of fresh exact writable finite cyclic registers born at zero, each larger than the count it must hold — a substrate-free fact one level below the tower, the prime staircase pn > n being one instance of that condition and not its source. The headroom is this construction's requirement and not universality's: the supply law buys the same on every unbounded supply, registers smaller than the count included. Primality, coprimality, the fields and the CRT reading all go unused. The growth machine grows too but never mints a writable fresh register; what growth must mint for universality is exact writable capacity.

Scope. The identical protocol is step-exact on both carriers, and the cap control lies exactly where derived.

The supply law: bounded is finite-state, unbounded is universal rule

Write mg for the modulus of the g-th window minted, the supply as a sequence. At the bit supply, every window Z/2, the configuration is (control, the set of tuples of register bits that some window holds), a sound and complete bisimulation quotient, so the machine is finite-state — a sibling of the growth machine and not a step below it, decidable by boundedness where that one is decidable by monotonicity, its registers resettable; the argument uses only a bounded alphabet, so any supply with mg ≤ C is finite-state. On an unbounded supply the doubling counter keeps its value on one pointed window and, when that window is full, grows until a window of more than twice its modulus appears, found by comparing moduli with residue counters after every grow, then moves the value across in unary; the search ends because the supply is unbounded, so two such counters run faithfully on every unbounded supply, monotone or not, and the machine is universal, Minsky cited. In capacity a rate enters only for counters growing once per increment: a positional counter, whose digits sit in windows with the moduli as bases, can move only its top digit to a fresh window, so the product Wd of its lower bases stays a program constant, and on a nondecreasing supply it survives exactly when Wd·mg ≥ g, which is mg = Ω(g): the supply mg = ⌈g/3⌉ suffices, where the single rider, which needs mg > g, wraps.

Scope. Bounded ⇒ finite-state verified through an exact bisimulation quotient on a program battery at window sizes 2, 3, 4. Unbounded ⇒ universal argued from the construction; with the modulus comparison after every grow it counts 0 → 200 → 0 on a supply alternating g and 2 and on one large only at powers of two, two counters exact, and 0 → 60 → 0 on mg = max(2, ⌈√g⌉), where the comparison once per block of grows reaches 0 → 2000 → 0. The once-per-increment counter is exhibited uncapped at ⌈g/3⌉.

Row 4: the interactive hand

The door: observer interventions. Do pushes from outside — multiplications of the modulus, as every growth move is, or on the element face writes into the registers — change the machine class, or only the basin, the outcome a run lands in: whether it stops, and where its growth settles? The model is the handled machine — the growth or element machine plus an external hand applying pushes between steps — graded by the hand's read set: blind, a fixed schedule; machine-grade, the machine's own read kinds; door-grade, anything determining the true virtual value, the integer each register is meant to hold: depth comparisons, lift reads, or full watching since birth. A read atom is one elementary test a program makes, on the depth face whether a depth has reached a fixed threshold.

Pushes are inert on the depth face: the ratchet law rule

Depths never decrease, so every depth-face read atom flips at most once per run: a program with k atoms receives at most k flips from any hand, omniscient or uncomputable, the map from hands to fates — whether the run stops, seats every prime at exponent 1, or sends one exponent to infinity, the three fates of Growth — factors through a finite branch tree, and both game questions — does some hand, does every hand make it halt — are decidable on the capped-depth quotient, the control state with each depth cut off at the largest threshold a test reads, played as a two-player game. The comparison door cannot be smuggled through move-only pushes: the hand can know the comparison stream and cannot tell it. This ratchet wall is the read-side twin of the addressing wall: a fixed lexicon of one-shot readables against an unbounded outcome stream.

Scope. The game decider on the capped-depth quotient agrees with exhaustive schedule enumeration over 11,957 runs on four machines, the flip budget never exceeded; on ratchet-only read surfaces the inertness holds for any hand whatsoever.

verifier: explore_interactive_hand.py

On the element face the hand's read is the whole purchase rule

A hand that writes each zero-test's verdict into its own one-shot flag runs exactly the seeds (starting values) whose runs make at most k tests, given k flags, and stalls when the flags run out; the same protocol through one re-readable mailbox register runs every seed. A door-grade hand runs full universality through a door-free machine: the watching hand syncs each grown window by one native add whose parameter carries its knowledge — base extension performed by the intervener, row 3's door with the non-channel-local computation moved into the hand — and, the bare class being universal, it buys row 3's re-price, O(1) exactness and not possibility. Below the door nothing moves: a blind hand mints no exactness the machine lacks, the freeze lemma holding verbatim under external adds and multipliers by units, and a finite-state machine-grade hand is absorbed into one product control.

Scope. The publishing split witnessed at k = 2, 4, 8 and the mailbox step-exact; the watching hand's simulation step-exact with Minsky cited; the blind hand checked over three 200-operation schedules and the machine-grade hand absorbed over 120 steps.

The repertoire, graded: basin, not class rule

The intervention repertoire of the inside view — the scar, the extra depth a push writes into the state; healing, the growth law absorbing a push and seating new primes as before; the rudder, a push that steers where growth settles; the phoenix, a push of 2 at each halt — is move-only and class-inert: a fresh scar flips exactly one read atom, a deeper second scar none. The basin stays programmable. A prime's column is the fate that sends that prime's exponent to infinity; a world is one run of the growth law from its seed, and it is locked onto a column when no rival move can undercut it again. With λ(N) the Carmichael exponent, the least e with xe ≡ 1 mod N for every unit x, pushing 2r to depth max(3, v2(λ(N)) + 2) makes the pushed state's v2(λ) its 2-depth minus 2, which keeps 2 itself the price of deepening 2 forever, the least move there is, so any world is one push from 2's column; and one push by a composite times a prime from Dirichlet's theorem on primes in progressions steers a world onto a target prime q's column, checked to q = 13.

Scope. The one-push lock onto 2's column is proved and checked at 200 of 200 states, the steering at 16 of 16 pairs with targets q ≤ 13.

Across all four rows the class is set by the composite read set, machine plus hand, never by who moves or what is pushed — with the one exception the read surface names, where the same read set gives two classes because the machine reading the boundary can time a flag's flip; and the frontier rider split the chart's currency by face. On the element face the bare class is universal, so possibility was never for sale: the element doors are read imports that buy O(1) per-step exactness where the bare machine pays unary time. The depth face still sells possibility: it is monotone, its native read atoms are ratchets, its bare class stays decidable, and even one comparison buys nothing, so the second independent comparison is the genuine universality purchase, and no hand can leak one across a ratchet-read surface to a machine that tells apart only finitely many flip timings. The element face owns subtraction and, with growth, a truthful unbounded counter bare; its reads are periodic and re-readable, and re-readability is the read-side difference.

The read surface

With universality native to the bare element face there is no door to keep thin, and the dividends, what the deletions pay back, split into two layers. The static ones — canonical forms, decidable equivalence, self-certification (the static dividend) — are facts of the ring and stand everywhere. The runtime ones — decidable halting, decidable hand-games — are properties of the interface AND of what the machine behind it can time, with a measured scope.

The composite probe: undecidability confined behind k ratchet flags for a timing-blind reader, leaked whole through a mailbox, and the crossing toll rule

Couple a finite-control bulk, the machine on the reading side of the boundary, to a genuinely universal core — a two-counter program on the frontier rider — through k ratchet flags: at most k boundary events ever cross, every realized history lands on the branch tree, and both boundary-quantified fate questions are answered by enumerating the tree with zero core steps, while which leaf the core realizes is the core's own reachability, undecidable. Through a re-readable mailbox the same core leaks whole: a two-state bulk mirrors the core's parity stream, one boundary event per step, 200 in 200 against at most 2 on the ratchet side. Carrying value v across the boundary bare costs exactly v transfer passes, t(t − 1)/2 of them over t increments and t(t − 1)/2 + t operations in all, against the door's one synced write per step: the crossing costs the value carried unless the door is bought.

Scope. Rule for flag-word-driven bulks, the schedule enumeration exhaustive over the four battery composites, the toll checked at every increment.

The interface conjecture is false: the flip-timing channel theorem

The candidate theorem — any bulk with decidable autonomous fate questions behind a ratchet-only boundary keeps boundary-quantified questions decidable — fails: one ratchet flag, one counter and a clocked simulator suffice. A waiting counter transcribes the flip's timing; a budgeted simulation converts it into a halting-witness check; some-halt equals the halting problem while every fate question with the flag held frozen stays decidable. One ratchet flag carries unbounded information, the timing, into any bulk unbounded enough to store it. The confinement results keep their scope, bulks of finite timing resolution, whose run between boundary events passes through finitely many states and so tells apart only finitely many flip timings: finite-control (the ratchet law's branch tree), flag-word-driven (timing-blind, the composite probe), and well-structured bulks, monotone over a well-quasi-order, for upward-closed targets, a class whose product with the finite monotone flag lattice stays well-structured (Finkel–Schnoebelen 2001) — a move frame and not a read principle. Well-structured systems carve decidability by monotone moves over a well-quasi-order (Schmitz–Schnoebelen), and safety games are undecidable with both players monotone (Abdulla–Bouajjani–d'Orso 2003). So decidability lives at the interface, jointly: questions posed through a ratchet-only boundary by a machine of finite timing resolution stay decidable whatever sits behind it; a boundary exposing re-readable reads carries everything; re-readability alone does not force the loss, the forgetful borrow's re-readable sign read staying finite-state; and the read kinds alone do not close the questions.

Scope. A theorem given Minsky's halting theorem, by an explicit reduction; the mechanics verified exhaustively at probe scale, 33 checks.

The kernel reading: a scheduler on these reads is a vector addition system once a task may be delayed a step rule

Build the smallest scheduler and message channel on presence over a fixed enumeration of task slots, the seats, a zero-guard that makes a task wait, a residue read of the tick and a one-shot equality on a rung, a tick count reached exactly once, in a module whose syntax tree is asserted free of order comparison (29 equality nodes, one subtraction, no less-than and no min). With its bounds removed it is a vector addition system — counters that rise and fall with no branch on zero — with a finite-quotient core — seat table, position, tick residue — once deterministic scheduling is relaxed to "a task may be delayed a step": the deterministic kernel breaks the compatibility axiom of well-structured systems — a state holding extra tokens can match every step of the state without them — at every blocked receive, 22 of 1,600 pairs, and the relaxation restores it. Its safety is then coverability, whether a marking at least as large as some target is reachable, and the Karp–Miller tree decides it: on a producer–consumer with a credit channel back it decides the channel's reach — data at the credit count coverable, one more not, an undrained timer channel unbounded — and the credit protocol makes a drop-on-full queue lossless with no comparison anywhere. Two system calls reach Minsky machines and none of the workload's waits does, read by whether one more token ever changes a task's stutter-free control path rather than its timing: a try-receive (one tested channel is a one-counter machine, two are Minsky) and a branch on the tick's residue downstream of a wait, the flip-timing channel inside a kernel. A residue wait is harmless, and a rung the tick has passed is a ratchet. The class landing is the asynchronous-programs theorem (Ganty–Majumdar 2012: a multiset task buffer under a nondeterministic scheduler has safety equal to Petri-net coverability, EXPSPACE-complete, and a test for a handler's absence makes it undecidable) and its real-time extension (Ganty–Majumdar 2009: a clock that advances only when no zero-delay task is pending simulates zero tests). What the kernel reading adds is which calls keep the class, and that the clock's residue keeps it as a wait and loses it as a branch.

Scope. Rule at the verifier's scale; the class claims stand on the cited theorems, a kernel on a finite machine being finite-state whatever it compares. The verifier hosts a program halting exactly on even inputs on one channel counter; the relaxation is clock-preserving under an oblivious round robin and a skipping one with a per-round tick, and under no scheduler that skips against a per-step tick.
