Learning

Learning as exact descent in reader space — no gradients, no floats, every comparison a big-integer comparison — where such a descent bottoms out, what it is blind to, and the two doors through which the data can enter what it finds.

  • Nesting — the floor, which moves nest, and the geometry that decides it
  • The substrate door — the machinery grown on the data instead of fixed
  • Forgetting — when a working state can certify it no longer holds a datum
  • Menu collisions — how wide the one door to robust forgetting actually is
  • Collision mechanisms — what the second factorization at a collision is, and what decides that there is one
  • Menu seeds — which menus carry a negative factor, and the shape their cores can have

A stream is the continued-fraction expansion of a real number, arriving digit by digit, and a map is a function applied to it. After n digits the value is pinned to a cylinder In, and what a reader is certain of about the output is the image interval Jn = f(In); these nest and shrink as digits arrive. A row is one (map, stream) pair, and a slate of rows stands in for training data throughout.

What a reader commits to are cells drawn from one fixed family, the cover: the tree cells, the Stern–Brocot cells, each the set of positive reals whose expansion opens with a given run of digits, so that the root cell is all of them; and the straddle chains standing at their vertices — a cell's vertex being the mediant of its two endpoints, the fraction whose numerator and denominator are their sums, and a chain a nested sequence of intervals each holding its vertex in the interior. Both kinds are graded by rank, which steps by one along every child relation (the redundant cover). A reader is a commitment policy on that cover: at each step it may commit cells containing the current image, and a commitment is a ratchet, never undone and permanently sound, since a cell containing Jn contains every later image. Within one input step a reader multi-commits, taking commit moves for as long as any remains available. What is left to choose is which route to take past a vertex, the tree child or the chain, and how long to wait before committing, so a policy is four coordinates: a route preference at tree cells, a route preference at straddles, and two patiences, one per cell kind. A kind at patience p commits a candidate only once it has contained the image for p+1 consecutive steps, so what it reads is the image from p steps back, that kind's reference. Patience 0 is greedy, committing on sight; patience ∞ refuses the kind outright, and the policy refusing both is the refuser.

Two exact rulers price a policy over a counted window, a fixed span of input steps, the same for every policy compared. Writing a cell's scale for ln(1/length), the deficit at a step is the scale the reader is short — the image interval's scale less the committed cell's, the quantity the commitment bound keeps bounded for a greedy reader. Summed over the counted steps it orders policies exactly as the product of their committed cells' lengths does, which is how it is compared, and it is infinite for as long as a committed cell is. The clock loss is the lag, in whole steps, of the committed scale behind the stream's own demand — how many images ago the reader's cell was the current one — with the deficit as tiebreak. Neither is order-isomorphic to the other, since the clock quantizes scale by the stream's local rate, and the two weigh a slate differently: the clock is rate-invariant in aggregation where the deficit overweights fast rows. Both compare by cross-multiplying big integers, so no float and no derivative enters any decision. Descent is the matching finite-graph notion: neighbouring policies differ by one notch in one coordinate, or by both route preferences flipped together; a move is taken only when it strictly lowers the loss; and a stall is a policy with no strictly better neighbour. A landscape is one loss surface, a row and a resource setting fixed.

Resources enter as settings rather than as policy coordinates: a rank budget B of cover-rank units per input step, and a bank of capacity W holding what a step does not spend, deposits automatic and overflow lost. What the policy gains under them is one coordinate for spending, the drawdown schedule, capping what a single step may withdraw — spend-all at one end, withholding at the other. The pair (B, W) is the reader's metabolism, spent against the row's demand, the rank per input step that row asks for: about one per digit at the golden ratio, about eight at [0; 8, 8, …].

The object

The quotient design law rule

Descent runs on the behavioural quotient — policies identified when their committed-cell traces over the counted window agree — built at design time, and its classes are what descent moves between. Two policies with identical counted traces cannot be ordered by any loss over that window, so such a tie is structural, and coordinates left unquotiented manufacture stalls the quotient does not have. Two were carried as landscape features before the quotient was built. The scarcity trap, a class a binding budget seemed to strand under the unthrottled optimum, is two single moves through a flat of behavioural twins and dissolves on the quotient with no cure move at all; the degenerate ridge a full bank builds — behaviourally identical policies tied into a flat strict descent cannot cross — is a quotient fibre by construction. Collapse facts provable by hand shrink the space before any engine runs: schedule values past the cap are behaviourally equal, and a bank never drawn flattens the schedule axis.

Scope. The tie statement is a property of the window; the two dissolutions are exhaustive at the resourced arena — the scarcity trap at budgets 1, 2, 4, 8 and unthrottled, the ridge at budgets 1 through 4 with bank caps 0, 2, 4, 8 — over the eight quadratic rows, horizon 120 counted from step 8. Toy scale.

The landscape

The funnel and its one blind corner rule

With no budget in force the deficit is a perfect loss over the committing policies: monotone strict descent converges from every one of them to the exact global optimum, greedy in both kinds, tied exactly across every route preference. The sole stall is total refusal, and it sits on a flat plateau of infinite loss: every single-coordinate neighbour prices infinite too, because a reader confined to the tree cells freezes at the wall row — the squaring map on √2, which sends the stream onto a vertex, where a tree cell can never refine past rank 1 — and a reader confined to the chains freezes at the root on every row whose image never approaches one, so no deficit comparison carries signal there. The corner is a trap and not a shallow basin: a frozen reader's shortfall grows quadratically in the horizon. The chains cure the wall itself (the wall criterion): a greedy reader on them commits exactly rather than boundedly there, its deficit falling to zero. Designed streams and short counted windows carry more stalls than this; those are the stall census's.

Scope. Exhaustive over 100 policies — the patience axis 0, 1, 2, 3, ∞ — on eight rows whose streams are quadratic irrationals, their expansions eventually periodic, horizon 120 counted from step 8. Toy scale.

The cure separation rule

Two cures for the blind corner, both built from the window's own prices, both work at the small space and separate when the patience axis grows. The signal cure refines the comparator lexicographically: finite deficit beats infinite, and two infinite policies compare by their committed-scale shortfall, the finite part of the infinite loss. It converges from every start at any plateau width, for a reason that does not depend on width — the shortfall orders the plateau however far it extends. The move cure keeps the plain comparator and widens the move set with the patience diagonals, moves lowering or raising both patiences at once; it is radius-bounded, stalling exactly where the whole neighbourhood is infinite, since the blind region grows with the axis while the cure's reach stays one notch on each patience however long the axis. A signal cure scales; a move cure of fixed reach is sized to the plateau it was designed against. Whether a move set whose reach grows with the axis — the full patience clique of the move-set law — cures a corner whose whole neighbourhood is infinite is untested: that law is about finite-loss stalls.

Scope. Exhaustive at both spaces, 100 policies on the patience axis 0, 1, 2, 3, ∞ and 324 on the axis 0…7, ∞, the eight quadratic rows, horizon 120. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_bootstrap_cures.py

The bottom lemma theorem

Fix a bounded nondegenerate interval. The cover cells containing it form a sub-poset with an inclusion minimum, and greedy multi-commit reaches that minimum in any preference order: the containing cells are finitely many, every commit move strictly raises rank, and local confluence holds in three cases, so Newman's lemma applies. The minimum has a closed form: the deepest tree cell strictly containing the interval, carried along the chain at that cell's vertex to the deepest element still containing it — a function of the interval alone, computable with no policy, no route and no commit loop, and equal to the greedy run's committed cell at every one of 17,892 census steps. Two corollaries. Confluence: at greedy patience the committed cell sequence is preference-free, which is why the funnel's optimum is route-free. Pointwise global optimality: every policy's committed cell contains its references, which contain the current image, whose minimum sits inside every cell containing it — so greedy patience dominates every policy at every step, in cells and not merely in loss, at every route preference, in any policy space of this family. The unresourced optimum therefore carries nothing stream-specific, and nothing about it can be learned from data. The lemma's single-reference hypothesis is tight: at mixed patience the cell the multi-commit settles at can depend on preference, one frontier policy pricing infinite at tree-first and finite at chain-first. In the partition cover — the tree cells alone, where the containing cells form one nested chain — loss is instead pointwise monotone in patience, strictly where the compared losses are finite, with total refusal worst. Global here means best in this policy family, not best conceivable; what the best conceivable solver could score is an eval's ceiling.

Scope. The lemma, its corollaries and the partition valley proved for this cover and move set; the closed form is proved and checked against the run at 17,892 of 17,892 census steps; the engine checks the containment at 169,082 cells and the domination at 1,789,200 of 1,789,200 policy-steps; the tightness witness is one policy at the funnel's slate and horizon.

verifiers: explore_bootstrap_cures.py, explore_minimal_cell.py

What the optimum depends on

The throttle: the start-delay and catch-up laws rule

Pointwise optimality consumes unbounded multi-commit, so a rank budget breaks its proof and prices the coordinates that proof made free. The four greedy policies, one per pair of route preferences, split: one pair wins, and which pair wins is stream-independent. The start-delay law: a patience-p reader opens about B·p ranks behind greedy, and under a binding budget that gap is never erased — what erased it unthrottled was exactly the multi-commit the budget removes — so patience costs a permanent lag rather than a transient one, and the patience valley is strict wherever a row lags. The catch-up thresholds: greedy at budget B matches the unthrottled loss exactly from a per-row threshold on — the ceiling of the row's mean demand where its fluctuation is bounded and the warm-up debt, the rank its first large digit asks for before the budget can supply it, is coverable — and never on the wall row, whose exponential demand leaves a throttled reader a per-step deficit growing linearly in the step, so no finite budget catches up. A threshold read on a coarse budget grid is an interval and not a value: one row's 4 on the grid 1, 2, 4, 8 refines to 3 on a grid that includes 3.

Scope. The split and the lag exhaustive at budgets 1, 2, 4, 8 and unthrottled over the eight quadratic rows, horizon 120; the start-delay mechanism hand-derived; the thresholds an observation over those eight rows, the finer grid the bank's, budgets 1 through 4. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_throttled_reader.py, explore_banking_reader.py

The bank rule

Banking moves exactly one threshold on the slate, and by warm-up funding rather than by smoothing: a row whose greedy reader waits while the first digits arrive banks that step's income, which later repays the leading digit's debt, taking that row's threshold from 2 to 1. At the optimum the schedule axis is degenerate, every drawing schedule tying in the argmin classes; off it route-locking is real — 83 strict wins for a withholding schedule over spend-all, each an early cheap commit steering a preference-fixed route past a better later cell, every one strictly off the optimum. The interior-income criterion: a bank cycles, drains recurring, only if its income sits strictly inside the set of per-step demands the stream attains — with no surplus step nothing is banked after warm-up, with no deficit step nothing drains — and it does cycle when that income also clears the catch-up threshold, one drain per period on each of six periodic spike streams with demand {1, s}; below the mean the reader spends its surplus on a growing lag and the bank runs dry after a transient. Periodicity is not what cycling needs: seven of the eight quadratic rows attain demands of spread at most 1, which no integer income can sit inside, and the wall row outruns every income. An event's size is the run of deficit past income, capped by W, so a heavy-tailed event needs a run of deficit steps, which no isolated-spike stream produces.

Scope. The threshold move and the route-locking count exhaustive at budgets 1 through 4 and caps 0, 2, 4, 8 over the eight quadratic rows, horizon 120; the criterion's necessity proved, its sufficiency with the threshold a rule over six designed periodic streams. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_banking_reader.py, explore_bank_period.py

Destination universality rule

Each row's argmin class is the set of behaviours minimizing that row's loss. At every setting one universal policy sits in every row's argmin class, and both transfer gaps — the excess a policy picked on one ensemble of rows pays on another — are exactly zero: under the deficit on the eight quadratic rows at every budget and cap, and under the clock loss, taken with the deficit as tiebreak and taken alone, on all nine rows, the one aperiodic non-quadratic row included. What the optimum does depend on is the resource environment: at exactly one setting, (B, W) = (4, 2), the route order inverts and the universal set shifts, verbatim under both losses. That the shift is identical under both tests two layers for stream dependence at once — the allocation layer (route, patience, schedule) and the loss layer (scale units against whole steps) — and finds it in neither. A reader adapts to its metabolism, never to its data; what descent learns here is how to spend, not what the stream says. Read as forgetting, that independence is a certificate at the strongest of the four grades, flat at every weighting of the data rather than tuned to one, inherited from the destination being a function of metabolism alone (the four grades).

Scope. Exact and exhaustive at the stated arena and nowhere beyond it: this policy space, these nine rows, horizon 120, these two losses. Outside this family of exact readers universality can fail, and the two laws of when it fails are the data door and the substrate door. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_throttled_reader.py, explore_banking_reader.py, explore_scale_clock.py

Stalls and nesting

The funnel's blind corner is one of five stall species, and the other four live at settings its arena does not visit: resource abundance and binding budgets, a second exact ruler beside the deficit, and counted windows short enough to end inside a trade. Every one of them is a fact about a move set that lets a reader change patience one notch at a time — over the ten landscapes the census draws its specimens from, a reader allowed three notches stalls nowhere — and every finite-loss stall found sits off the spine, with one patience pinned to the present: stalls. What decides whether a lowered patience improves anything is whether the two runs' committed cells nest. A greedy reader commits the floor of the current image, which sits inside every cell any policy commits, so a strictly better class exists from every class off the bottom; nesting can die only where the commit loop had a route preference to consult; and for a run preferring the chain at both kinds it is forced, under the identity map, by two inequalities on the indices of the references read at the one step where a run can leave a chain: nesting.

The data door

Where does training data enter an exact adapted system, and what does holding it cost? One frozen arena for the four blocks below: the behavioural quotient with no budget in force, 100 policies in 38 classes, nine rows, horizon 120.

The door opens at the loss rule

Destination universality is a property of (space, loss) jointly, and the loss is the axis that breaks it. Relax exactly what the loss reads — a decision-valued prediction score, the committed state alone guessing which side of its cell's vertex the stream will land on and charged for a miss — and universality dies: the rows' argmin classes stop intersecting under both orders, the score alone and the score with the deficit beneath it as tiebreak; greedy leaves the argmin on six of nine rows; and eight rows are perfectly predictable in-family, but by different policies. Two hand lemmas locate the door. Collapse: any local proper score — one a truthful prediction minimizes — read against the flat belief on the committed cell telescopes back into the family of losses that see only the committed-cell trace, so leaving that family takes a discrete decision. Coarsening: the ratchet state is coarser than the public past, so the honest score is a function of the state alone. The optimum set becomes the row's fingerprint: readability of the row from the optimum set completes to fully row-discrete under the score alone, while a single adapted state stays almost never row-exclusive — the ensemble reads the data and an individual optimum barely does. In the grades of the amnesia certificate the break reads per consumer: the individual state map loses its strongest witness at the first scored bit, and the set map never factored row-free to begin with.

Scope. The hand lemmas proved; everything else exact and exhaustive on the frozen arena. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_prediction_door.py

The door has no width rule

Each counted step carries one future bit, which side the stream in fact took. Every one of the 112 of them, taken alone as the top order with the full deficit beneath it, already empties the universal intersection, while zero bits restore it: a step at zero rather than a threshold. So the price of data is not conserved across bandwidth and reaches zero — the exchange rate inverts as the coupling thins until, at a single scored bit, a row's specialist ties the pooled winner's miss total outright and pays only the deficit tiebreak. Free adaptation exists at six of eight grid points: a strict specialist advantage beside a merged readability block, the row's data entering the optimum without the row becoming readable from it. The anchor is not blank either: the deficit's own argmin sets already split the rows into three blocks, one split stream-driven, so a stream-reading loss completes set-level readability rather than creating it.

Scope. Exact and exhaustive on the frozen arena over the 112 single-bit tiebreaks and an eight-point bandwidth grid. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_bandwidth_dial.py

“Reads the stream” is not the law pattern

The obvious criterion fails from both sides. Sufficiency fails at the value level: losses consulting future bits only in their values keep universality whenever the per-row preorders they induce never move, and at this scope they never do — 467 of the 475 within-row pairs tying in value are identical in trace, so a tiebreak sitting below the deficit has nothing to act on. Necessity fails outright: 146 losses that read nothing of the stream at all — indicators of a band of deficit values, row-uniform functions of the deficit alone — break universality, as does mixing two keepers with incompatible witnesses. What survives is a pattern over the species tried: every non-degenerate loss whose top order consults future bits breaks — delayed horizons, the next-digit class, the perverse direction included, so no universal anti-predictor exists either.

Scope. Exact and exhaustive on the frozen arena over the loss species tried, which is what makes the surviving statement a pattern and not a rule. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_score_criterion.py

The keep law rule

Emptiness is the generic case, so the thing needing a law is the keep. Order classes by containment of their committed cells, step by step and row by row; a loss whose optimum is pulled monotonically toward one extreme of that order keeps universality, and both extremes exist. The greedy class is pointwise-tightest, its committed cell contained in every class's cell at every counted step on every row — a two-step corollary of the bottom lemma, holding against a cell family rich in overlaps where no chain argument applies — and the refuser is pointwise-coarsest by construction. All four monotone loss species tried keep, each with its pole as the sole universal optimum. For the losses that reward landing in a band of deficit values the criterion is exact and proved. Pool the deficit values of every (class, row) pair into one sorted order, 117 distinct ranks at scope; a class's hull is the interval its rank in that order spans across the rows; a band is clean when on every row it catches some class and not all; and a clean band keeps if and only if it contains some class's hull, the class it then hands every row at once. The hull spectrum is bimodal, and the gap is the mechanism of generic emptiness: the refusing corner has widths 0 to 2, at the top of the spectrum on every row, while every adapted class's hull spans at least 85 of the 117 ranks — adaptation makes a class's standing violently row-dependent and only refusal is row-stable. The law is sufficiency-only: 213 keepers built from two disjoint bands exist, 201 of them with the whole universal optimum set avoiding both poles, a keep handed out by where the values fall and not by monotone structure.

Scope. The coarse pole, the hull criterion and the two-band construction proved; the tight pole proved from the bottom lemma and engine-confirmed on the frozen arena; the loss-species results exhaustive there. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_keep_law.py, explore_score_criterion.py

What holding data costs rule

Data enters exactly through what the evaluator can witness, and within a row the committed geometry exhausts the visible: no loss tried separates any of the 475 within-row pairs of classes whose committed cells agree in length, step for step — the same 475 pairs the value ties above pick out. So data content is a cross-row quantity, witnessed only where two rows collide in geometry, and the canonical site is the golden near-twin pair: the identity and squaring maps on φ, which share identical geometry columns on 32 of the 38 classes because φ² = φ + 1 acts as a translation of the cover tree — where a side-reading loss scores twin classes differently and no function of the geometry can. The price is structural rather than statistical: the optimum set becomes the row's fingerprint, per-row advantage costs pooled loss wherever it is not free — 4 to 15 pooled misses per miss of advantage with every future bit scored — and no adapted optimum keeps its cross-row standing.

Scope. Exact and exhaustive on the frozen arena over the loss species tried. Toy scale.

The substrate door

The second door lets the distinguishing machinery grow on the data instead of holding it fixed — a set of distinct primes, extended whenever two inputs it cannot tell apart are supposed to be told apart, by the smallest prime that separates something still confused. Growth is then a covering problem over the differences the supervision asks to have separated, and demanding every distinction at once forces the grown set to be a counter, the first k primes, its depth set by how fine a resolution is asked for and its content by nothing. Structure in the data moves the set without picking it, because redundant small primes still cover: sets grown on the multiples of different primes transfer to each other near-intact, and so does natural grammar, which carries the one kind of difference that could have opened the door. What a set grown on language tracks is the encoding it was handed, and an embedding that holds word order gives up the transform its words could otherwise share: the substrate door.

What a reader holds can also be asked to leave. When a still-working state must certify what it no longer contains, the certificate's price is prime recycling — forgetting — and how much room recycling gives is a question about unique factorization: menu collisions, what the second factorization is made of in collision mechanisms, and which menus can take part in one at all in menu seeds. Turned outward, the same exactly-known worlds score published deletion audits against a posterior known in closed form — deletion audits — and grade what four standard estimators do when the optimum they estimate is exact: stated uncertainty.
