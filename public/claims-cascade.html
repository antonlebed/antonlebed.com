The cascade boundary

  • The escape's conjunction rule — An escaping trajectory's move costs must diverge, and every rank-1 characteristic's door caps them at pv+2 alone and at every stage, so every such exponent must run away at once through carriers of norm 1 + m·pv+1, m ≤ p − 1. — explore_module_law.py, explore_element_cascade.py
  • The standing-move dichotomy rule — A greedy walk paying at most the least of a family of standing prices, each a nondecreasing function of one state coordinate, either locks, some coordinate staying bounded, or has every coordinate run away at once. — explore_standing_move.py, explore_standing_recipe.py
  • The excess prices the door rule — Over Z the door at an odd q is priced by the excess xq = vq(λ(N)) − vq(N), exactly qx+2 when q divides N, the excess never below −1, and the lock is that coordinate pinned at its floor. — explore_standing_move.py
  • All three fates sit in the schema rule — Depth locks with its excess at the floor, mortality is the bounded branch with the wall jump's coordinate driven to 1, and breadth is the growing branch, one standing recipe per residue class with every coordinate diverging. — explore_standing_move.py, explore_standing_recipe.py
  • The budget inequality rule — A door cheap enough to afford is too cheap to buy the exponent that keeps the ladder climbing: V rises at most one per move at odd p and two at p = 2, so a characteristic-zero trajectory with a rank-1 place over 2, 3 or 5 locks. — explore_ghost_wander.py, explore_module_law.py
  • The characteristic sweep rule — At the pinned cap every one of the 167 odd primes below 1000 reaches a death rung, erratic in p, so every characteristic-zero ring with a rank-1 characteristic below 1000 has its boundary closed. — explore_cascade_chars.py
  • The loosened ladder rule — Charging the door on a generator widens the multiplier by the class factor τ and delays the ladder without saving it: all 167 odd characteristics below 1000 still die at τ = 2, and the factor is 1 at every principal place. — explore_element_cascade.py
  • The inhabited residual theorem — For d the product of the primes up to P, every prime up to P ramifies in Q(√d), so its least rank-1 characteristic exceeds P: the residual has members at every bound, and a uniform close is a theorem's job. — explore_cascade_residual.py
  • The target is density zero rule — A ring closes as soon as one rank-1 characteristic does, and those carry positive density in every field, so a density-zero set of undying primes already closes every characteristic-zero ring. — explore_cascade_residual.py
  • The entry price rule — Over every fundamental discriminant to 108 the 15 champions reach a least rank-1 characteristic of 127 at |D| = 46,305,413, log|D| ≈ ln 2·π(L), and only D = −67 and −163 buy in with no ramified prime below L. — explore_cascade_residual.py
  • The shape of a death rung theorem — A carrier qa at rung V has a the order of q mod pV+1, so a death is a finite check plus one prime-existence question, and the rung is invisible to every small-prime sieve, the sifted count carrying no V. — explore_cascade_theorem.py
