The multipliers

At the 83 charted cells where the pure products lose, what wins is a trade: one cyclotomic block of a pure product given up for a non-cyclotomic factor of the same degree. The factors that appear, the members, form a short list, five residual classes on the chart and seven members across every cell a walk has reached, minted one cell at a time with nothing predicting the next. Their scarcity is the lattice's and not their class's, which holds an infinite family at its lowest degree; six of the seven sit on the unit circle and the fifth leaves it; and from height 4 up every minimiser's residual takes the value 1 at −1, a rule with an exact threshold that the seventh member, minted off the chart, breaks.

A vector of integers c0, …, cM−1 is the polynomial P(x) = Σ cixi; its width is M, its height the largest |ci|, and its depth J the multiplicity of the root 1. Write h(M, J) for the least height of a nonzero vector of width M and depth at least J, computed exactly at each cell of a chart of 695 cells with 4 ≤ M ≤ 40 and 2 ≤ J ≤ 30; a minimiser is a vector attaining it. Every vector of a cell is a cofactor q times (x−1)J, q of degree below the rank M − J. A pure product is a product of factors xd − 1 with at least J of them and degree below M; the pure bound is the least height over the pure products fitting a cell, and a cell fails when h sits strictly below it. A cyclotomic polynomial Φd is the minimal polynomial of a root of unity of exact order d, and xd − 1 is the product of Φe over the divisors e of d, so a pure product is a product of cyclotomics. Dividing a cofactor by every cyclotomic factor and every power of x it carries leaves its residual. A reciprocal polynomial reads the same backwards; a monic one has leading coefficient 1; a primitive one has coefficients sharing no common factor. A failing rank's first failing depth is the low edge of its band. Where the failing cells sit is on Where the products lose; how h is computed is on Flattening.

The trade

The residual is the failure flag, and a class of five residuals beats the products on the chart rule

Divide each of the 695 exhibited minimisers by every cyclotomic factor it carries: five residuals survive, 1 at 612 cells, B = 2 + 3x + 2x² at 51, A = 2 + 4x + 5x² + 4x³ + 2x⁴ at 29, the product A·B at 2 and C = 3 + 5x + 3x² at 1. The cyclotomic part is a pure product at every one of the 695, and the 22 witnesses carrying a power of x lose nothing by dropping it, the coefficient multiset and the flattening surviving at a lower degree. So every cell has a minimiser that is a pure product times one of the five, and the four non-trivial residuals cover 51 + 29 + 2 + 1 = 83 cells, exactly the failing set: one direction proved, residual 1 making the witness a pure product and so h a pure height, the other the count. A·B is no new member, the class being closed under multiplication; its two cells are the first place one member does not suffice. C is genuine, divisible by neither A nor B, with leading coefficient 3. Least height over (pure product)·A and over (pure product)·B, swept over the same multiset enumeration as the pure family, beats the pure bound at exactly the 83 failing cells and attains h at 80, improving without attaining at precisely the three cells whose residual the pair cannot make.

Scope. Rule, exhaustive over the 695 cells, every witness split and multiplied back; the residual named at any one cell is that of the ONE minimiser the reduction returns, and a cell can carry others (attainment against minimisers). Toy scale.

The swap is a construction, and it builds the exhibited minimiser itself rule

At each failing rank the clean cell one depth below the low edge has a minimiser of residual 1, and the first failing cell's minimiser is that object with one cyclotomic block traded for an equal-degree member. The smallest specimen: at rank 5, depth 18, width 23, the cheapest pure product has cofactor (1+x)²(1+x+x²) and height 4420 against the cell's h of 3638, whose minimiser has cofactor (1+x)²·B, the factor 1+x+x² gone and B, of the same degree, standing where it was. Read against the cheapest pure product at the cell the match is partial, the minimiser's cyclotomic multiset a subset of the argmin's at 78 of the 83 and the dropped block matching the residual's degree at 73, the commonest trades Φ2² → B at 36 cells, Φ5 → A at 15 and Φ3 → B at 13. Read against EVERY admissible pure cofactor it is exact: the swap family ranges over every pure cofactor fitting the cell, every sub-multiset of its cyclotomic factors and every product of members of equal degree, the empty trade included, and its least height hs equals h at all 83, with no lattice reduction anywhere in it; at all 83 the vector the reduction returns is a member of the family up to sign, 54 carrying a negative leading coefficient and none a monomial factor. The ten cells the argmin reading misses are reached from a different parent, the distance between a description and a construction. The members are not a degree accident: three polynomials carrying their degrees, heights and values at 1, and not reciprocal, equal the pure bound at all 83 and beat it at none. Below its rank's low edge the minimiser IS the pure argmin up to sign and monomial at 230 of 230 cells. And since h ≤ hs ≤ pure bound always, hs strictly below the pure bound CERTIFIES a cell as failing with no reduction in it, while hs equal to it certifies nothing; along the rank-22 column the reduction runs from 0.03 to about 7 s across widths 30 to 52 while the family holds near 2.

Scope. Rule over the chart's rectangle for hs = h at the 83 and the pure argmin at the 230; the partial-match counts upper bounds by at most six, six cells offering several cheapest pure products that classify differently; the timing an observation. Toy scale.

The members sit in the unit-circle class, which Kronecker makes a class, and E leaves it property

Every residual the chart exhibits is reciprocal with every root on |z| = 1: in y = x + 1/x, A, B and C are 2y² + 4y + 1, 2y + 3 and 3y + 5, with real roots −1 ± √2/2, −3/2 and −5/3 inside (−2, 2), the interval y = 2cos θ carries onto the circle; D, F and G pass the same exact Sturm test. A monic integer polynomial with nonzero constant term and all roots on the circle is a product of cyclotomics (Kronecker), so the only way out of the cyclotomic products is to be non-monic, and every member has leading coefficient at least 2; what carries the cyclotomic part the rest of the way to a PURE product is the census, which no theorem supplies, Φ3 being cyclotomic and not a pure product. E is the exception: its descent 1 + 4y + 12y² + 11y³ + 3y⁴ has two real roots in (−2, 2) out of four, so four of E's eight roots lie off the circle, and what survives outside the chart is only that the cofactor splits as cyclotomics times a residual. The ambient class is infinitely generated for a cheap reason: Wc = c + (2c−1)x + cx² is reciprocal, primitive, of discriminant 1 − 4c < 0 and so irreducible, non-monic for c ≥ 2, and descends to cy + 2c − 1 with root −(2c−1)/c in (−2, 2); W2 = B and W3 = C, the Wc are pairwise non-associate, and degree 1 admits no member at all. So the scarcity of members is the lattice's and never the class's, and the walk is not enumerating its class in order: W4 = 4 + 7x + 4x², of height 7, has never been exhibited. Six of the seven are irreducible over Q by descent: on the circle every irreducible factor is self-reciprocal, an odd-degree one would vanish at −1 where no member does, and the even ones descend to factors of the y-polynomial, linear at B and C, of non-square discriminant at A (8) and G (12), and without a rational root or an integer quadratic split at D and F.

Scope. Property for the class, the family and the six irreducibilities, proved; observation for which members the lattice exhibits. E's four roots off the circle leave its irreducibility measured rather than argued. Toy scale.

The end values

Every minimiser's residual is 1 at −1 from height 4 up, and not below rule

Holding the enumeration radius fixed at h√M collects every vector of least sup norm instead of the one the reduction returns. Over the 695-cell chart every cell of height at least 4 closes, 442 at a 50,000-node cap and the last 20 at 10⁶, two of them costing 79,247 and 251,834 nodes, which is why an earlier reading put the line at 5; and at every one every minimiser's residual takes the value 1 at −1. One step lower it fails: sixteen cells carry a residual with another value, every one at h = 1, 2 or 3, and the value takes eleven values there, every integer from −5 to 5 except 0, which the split forbids, and 7. The class count is the wrong denominator: 121 of the 163 offending classes come from the single cell M = 11, J = 2, h = 1, whose sphere holds 232 classes by itself, so the low cells report degeneracy and not a spread. Since R(1) and R(−1) differ by twice the odd-index coefficient sum, the value forces R(1) odd. And the value is independent of the circle: the minimiser sets leave the unit-circle class at 24 cells of heights 1, 2, 3 and 6, above the h = 3 top of the cells that lose the value, so the h = 6 cell leaves the circle and keeps the value, and E, off the circle with E(−1) = 1, is the first instance of that separation and not an exception.

Scope. Rule, exhaustive over the 462 chart cells with h ≥ 4 and over every minimiser of each, proved at none; observation for the sixteen failing cells and the 24 that leave the circle, read off the pass that closes 105 of the 233 cells below height 4. Toy scale.

Both end-value patterns died at the seventh member, and the value at 1 could never have been a law observation

A(1) through F(1) are 17, 7, 11, 71, 193 and 157, all prime, and G(1) = 26; A(−1) through F(−1) are 1 and G(−1) = 2, minted at h = 67,323 off the chart. Every bound this subject owns comes from evaluating P at a root of unity, where |P(z)| ≤ Mh forces |Q(z)| ≤ Mh/|z−1|J; at z = 1 the flattening is the constraint and P(1) = 0 says nothing about the cofactor. The ambient family had already killed primality as a law about the class, Wc(1) = 4c − 1 being composite at eleven of the twenty-three c from 2 to 24, first at W4, 15. What the rule above needs is a derivation that CONSULTS THE HEIGHT AND THE RECTANGLE: it is false at h ≤ 3 on the chart and false at one off-chart cell of height 67,323, so no hypothesis in the height alone can state it, which rules out both height-free handles, the minimiser's primitivity and the (x+1)-side bound |Q(−1)| ≤ Mh/2J, discharged by the cyclotomic part alone wherever it bites.

Scope. Observation; the multiplicativity of the value and the oddness forcing are properties. Whether the value is forced on the chart at h ≥ 4 is open. Toy scale.

The law is about attainment, and the collected set adds one polynomial observation

That h is attained inside the class holds at all 695 cells, read off the exhibited minimiser. That everything attaining h is inside is false: over the 550-cell chart's fixed-radius pass, 405 cells close, 57 cap where a small height meets a wide lattice and the ball swallows everything, 88 at h = 1 are never reached, and at 28 of the 405 two minimisers of one cell have different residuals, at 24 some minimiser leaves the class, only at heights 1, 2, 3 and 6, so no unclosed cell can move the top. Uniqueness is not a height phenomenon: 246 closed cells have h ≥ 10 and 51 carry more than one minimiser up to sign, the largest holding nine. Over the 442 cells at h ≥ 5 of the 695-cell chart, every one closed and the 145 added by the widening running from h = 700 to 155,117,520, the whole minimiser sets exhibit SIX residual classes, five of them the census's own, 1, A, B, C and A·B, and one the collected set adds:

monic, reciprocal, cyclotomic-free, 1 at −1 and 19 at 1, at exactly one cell, M = 18, J = 8, h = 6, the cell that leaves the circle and keeps the end value; its roots are off the circle, which Kronecker forces for a monic cyclotomic-free polynomial. S is irreducible over Z: by the descent split on a least-degree factor, its cubic y³ + 2y² + y + 1 has no rational root and a non-self-reciprocal factor drags its own reciprocal in, which at degree 3 forces T(−1)² = −1; and the rig divides every monic integer polynomial of degree 1, 2 and 3 inside the Mignotte bounds, 2,357,161 candidates, and none divides. Being monic puts it outside every product of members at once. It is not a minted member, a member being minted where the class in hand cannot reach h where S is read off a minimiser the reduction never returned, its cell's witness splitting to 1, and it has not recurred in 442 cells where E stopped being an accident on its second sighting.

Scope. Observation over the two passes named, the 405-cell counts the narrower chart's and the six-class census the wider one's at h ≥ 5; the irreducibility of S a property. Whether the class the lattice exhibits is finitely generated, and whether the members can be characterised at all, is open. Toy scale.
