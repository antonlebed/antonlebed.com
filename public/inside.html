The inside view

An observer holding only the finished ring, asking what it can know of how it grew — and what a hand reaching in can buy.

The worlds here are grown by a growth law: a structural demand plus a greedy move, extend the modulus N by the least m ≥ 2 the demand admits. Each demand's greedy policy realizes one fate — breadth seats every prime in turn and stays squarefree, depth collapses onto one prime's column, mortality halts — and softening the argmin into a thermal selection, an admissible m picked with probability proportional to m−β at a temperature parameter β > 1, makes each fate a distribution over histories. The crystal is the cold limit β = ∞ from seed 1, the state a world starts from: the squarefree run 2, 6, 30, 210, …. A prime the state carries is one of its windows; the state's whole percept is its depth profile, the exponent of every window, and nothing else. A route from a seed to a state is an ordered factorization of their quotient into admitted moves, and each intermediate world's menu is every move the demand admitted there, with normalizer Z the menu's total thermal weight Σ m−β. The Boltzmann numerators cancel between routes — every route to N from a seed s has the same product of moves, so the same weight (N/s)−β — and the posterior over routes given the state is proportional to the product of the reciprocal interior normalizers: all route information lives in what each intermediate world could have done otherwise, never in what it did. Every posterior below is therefore exact. What is then knowable splits by fate.

What the state remembers

The bounded-evidence law rule

The breadth world is the amnesiac fate. An observer in a squarefree world testing the crystal against thermal genesis at β has, over the windows up to P, the likelihood ratio ∏p ≤ P (1 − p−β)−1, increasing to ζ(β): the whole infinite world supplies ζ(β) : 1 odds for the crystal and never more — 1.64 : 1 against β = 2, 94% of it from the windows below 10. The asymmetry is total: one window at depth 2 refutes the crystal outright, since a crystal never deepens, while no finite observation refutes hot growth. Cold genesis is falsifiable and never confirmable.

Scope. Proved; the ratio checked monotone over cutoffs to 10⁵ and within the Euler tail bound of ζ at β = 1.5, 2, 3. The crystal side needs the seed convention: without it, β = ∞ explains any state as a defective seed.

The finite thermometer rule

The state's information about its own temperature is finite. The depth profile is a family of independent geometric variables, one per window, so the Fisher information about β — the curvature of the log-likelihood, whose reciprocal bounds the variance of any unbiased estimate — is a convergent sum, 0.885 in total at β = 2: any unbiased inside estimate of β = 2 has standard deviation at least 1.06, forever. And the thermometer reads defects: a squarefree sample has positive score at every β, so its maximum-likelihood temperature is the crystal, and 63% of β = 2 worlds hand their observer a false crystal reading. The anthropic weight is capped the same way: conditioning a temperature prior on squarefreeness reweights it by 1/ζ(β), so over β ∈ [1.1, 4] the largest Bayes factor it buys one temperature over another is ζ(1.1)/ζ(4) ≈ 9.8.

Scope. The convergence is proved by an integral bound and the total bracketed to [0.8844, 0.8859]; the false reading share is measured at β = 2; the anthropic cap is a worked posterior over that prior range.

The unbounded-memory law rule

The depth world remembers. On a column 3t grown from seed 1 the probability of visiting depth t has a closed form, and the log-odds for cold genesis against temperature β grow linearly in depth, with slope β log 3 − log(1 + 1/Zcol(β)) > 0 for every β > 1, Zcol the normalizer of a deep column state, the same at every level — 0.69 nats per deepening at β = 2, against the breadth world's eternal total log ζ(2) = 0.50. One deepening out-earns the squarefree world's whole infinite lifetime, and thermal genesis, never refutable at finite depth, becomes confirmable in the limit. At zero temperature the past is a near-unique fossil chain, at most 14 pasts for any state below 20000 against breadth's 32 on the same range. The trade is exact: breadth buys channels and pays with its history; depth buys memory and one re-imported absolute value |·|q and pays with every window but one.

Scope. Proved for every β > 1, the brackets checked at five temperatures; the fossil census is exact to 20000.

The seed–history confound rule

Amnesia survives the removal of temperature. Freeze the breadth world at zero temperature, where the law is deterministic and the route is the increasing one, and the state still fails to determine its own past. The pairs (seed, age) whose greedy trajectory passes through N are exactly the subsets of D(N) = {p : p divides N exactly once, and every prime below p divides N}: the subset is the set of primes the run picked, the seed is N divided by their product, and every such pair is a run. So a state supports 2|D(N)| pasts — N = 210 has 16 — at the one temperature where the law leaves nothing to chance. The ambiguity is over where the run began, never over the order it went in. What buys uniqueness is the genesis convention, seed 1, and nothing the world holds.

Scope. Proved, and checked exhaustively at every N ≤ 2000. At β < ∞ every ordered coprime factorization is a candidate history and the route posterior above is what sorts them.

Memory lives in witnessing rule

Every thermal greedy law is an exponential family in β with sufficient statistic −log m, so watching a world grow is sampling: each watched move carries Fisher information Var(log m) over that state's menu, bounded below along the trajectory in both fates. Watched information grows linearly in moves while the finished state's total is capped, and the gap has an exact location — the witness gap: the information a watched path carries over the dated state, the state read with its move count, is exactly the Fisher information of the route posterior, Ipath = Idated + I(route). The same normalizers that carry all route information carry all the surplus temperature information. In mutual-information form, with G the generator — the law and the temperature — the dated state being a function of the path gives I(G; path) = I(G; dated) + I(G; path | dated) exactly. Along the breadth trajectory one late watched move out-informs the whole infinite state at β = 2 by the 10⁵-th prime.

Scope. The exponential-family form and both gap identities are proved (the law of total variance and the chain rule), the Fisher identity checked to 10−15 on a truncated world; the per-move floor is bucket-certified in range; the crossover lies between the 3.3·10⁴-th and the 10⁵-th prime.

The fossil paradox rule

The fate that remembers its generator perfectly forgets its history maximally, and necessarily so. A one-window column's menu never changes, so its route posterior is uniform and its state keeps every generator bit the path held — the witness gap is exactly zero — while the route entropy given the dated state sits at the uniform maximum. Breadth is the reverse: a positive witness gap and a route posterior skewed below uniform. Zero menu drift — the menus never changing along the run — forces the route posterior uniform, and uniformity is at once the proof of the perfect fossil and the maximum of amnesia. Memory for the generator and memory for the past are different resources, and the chain identity locates the relation: what the state loses about the generator is precisely what the route posterior carries.

Scope. Proved; the column's identity checked to 10−17 and its route entropy to the uniform maximum exactly, breadth's gap 0.013 nats with route-entropy ratio 0.737, on the truncated world (twelve moves, ages ≤ 4).

The induction ceiling rule

Fix a finite class of generators — three demands nested by what they admit (semisimplicity, independence, new idempotents) at three temperatures — mix the ensemble over age, and read it through the nested channels state ⊂ dated state ⊂ watched path: the ladder is strict at every row, and the coordinates split by channel. The law lives in the state — the laws' reachable state sets differ, and dating adds 0.4% — while the temperature lives in the clock, a rate, so dating adds 28% and watching more; at zero temperature the law is invisible to any watcher, and two admissibility probes — offer q², offer 2q — buy the whole log 3 nats. The mnemonic advantage has a crossover: a young breadth world out-informs a young column, depth winning from age 3. The exact Bayes posterior attains the chart while a smoothed plug-in pays 200% more, so grown worlds are induction benchmarks that know their own optimum, scoring a compression-driven inducer against the literal Bayes value — and the test is lopsided, the generator log₂ 3 bits forever while the unrecoverable route entropy crosses it by age 3 and grows about a bit per move. The instrument built from that property is the benchmark ceiling.

Scope. Every number truncated-menu exact at twelve moves, ages ≤ 4, seed 1; the ladder inequalities are proved by data processing and their strictness measured; the split and the crossover are observations at that truncation.

The one-way design

Can a demand offering many moves at every state — every admissible move coprime to the state, so that no window is ever reused — hide exponentially many histories? The route posterior at a dated endpoint is uniform iff the product of interior normalizers is the same along every route, so the design target is normalizer balance, and the answer is a trichotomy: impossible at every temperature, designable at one, and nearly free at height.

The quarantine theorem rule

In any coprime demand no dated endpoint with two or more routes has a uniform route posterior at every β, at any age: the leading move of a route is quarantined from every later menu's primes, the support of the normalizer product pins both routes to one prime set, and the valuations then force the leading moves equal, induction running down the route. So β-free perfect amnesia forces a single route in many-window growth. The escape is prime recycling, and it is the exact boundary: a constant menu — {2, 3} at every state, its primes reused forever — hides exponentially many routes at every β, which is the depth column's own privilege and the fossil paradox's other face; and a designed world free to reuse its primes holds both routes to a two-route endpoint equiprobable at every β with menus that differ, so flatness at every temperature does not force the constant menu outside the coprime class.

Scope. Proved at every age for coprime demands, cross-checked by a proof-independent scan (0 coprime realizations of a flat two-route fibre across 4849 candidate endpoints, against 4805 with recycling allowed); the recycling world is an exact construction closing its fibre at two routes.

The tuned amnesiac rule

At the world's own temperature the balance criterion is a finite system of mass equations, solved exactly by Egyptian-fraction junk menus — admissible moves that lead off the endpoint's divisor lattice and contribute weight only. At age 2 both two-route endpoints are exactly uniform at β₀ = 1, detuning to 117 : 70 odds at β = 2; at age 3 all 3! routes of one endpoint are exactly equiprobable. The tuned world keeps nonzero menu drift and a positive witness gap while its routes sit uniform at β₀: it forgets its past without becoming a fossil, and the equivalence the fossil paradox rests on holds β-free only inside the coprime class, where the quarantine theorem makes it vacuous. History-hiding in many-window growth is thermally tuned — a grown world can be built unreadable exactly at the temperature it was grown.

Scope. Exact constructions at ages 2 and 3, in rational arithmetic; general ages argued, not proved. The witness gap of the tuned world is 0.003 nats over the {1, 2} temperature ensemble at ages ≤ 2.

The footprint law observation

Plain breadth hides nearly for free at height. In the coprime demand the route posterior's only leak is the used primes' menu-mass footprint — a model weighting each route by the product over its interior states of 1/(Zempty − the mass its used primes removed), Zempty the seed's normalizer, captures all but 0.04% of the leak from support {11, 13, 17, 19} up, the mode always heavy-footprint-first, small-prime worlds overlap-dominated. Endpoints on high primes hide their history at every tested temperature, and the breadth route-entropy ratio 0.737 above is a small-prime artifact.

Scope. Measured across a temperature grid and tested supports on the truncated world.

How much of a genesis is beyond recovery is itself an exact quantity with a critical point the thermodynamics cannot see — one clock at every finite place, the deep clock reading four separable layers of the field, and on a breathing column a staircase of modes: Irreducibility.

A second percept sits beside the genesis posterior and is far poorer: read a state only through whether an offered move leaves its period fixed, and the whole reading is one number, the transparency headroom. Every move keeps an exact ledger on it — what it spends, what it opens, and a closed form for the opening from the period and one coordinate per prime of the move — and the wall map behind it is a closure operator whose fixed points are the mortal fate's tombstones: The sighted probe. Two states with one headroom are one state to that probe; what telling them apart costs, which primes it cannot see arrive, and how coarse it is — the largest blind class its census finds proved infinite by a sieve and a fixed share of the primes through the Bernoulli denominators: The blind spot.

The price of intervention

Give the observer a hand: a push is a multiplier injected from outside the law, and what it moves is read through the state's period λ(N), the exponent of the unit group of Z/N. In a deep world one push re-locks the growth and a composite push steers it to any chosen prime. In a mortal world the hand buys time, and the price list is exact. In the breadth world the hand buys neither steering nor time: what it buys is inscription, and nothing it writes can ever be erased.

The scar ledger rule

Acting is fate-graded opposite to memory. In the breadth world healing absorbs every push — the demand goes on seating new primes and the destination is push-proof — but multiplication never removes, so every scar, the extra depth a push writes into the state, is permanent. In the amnesiac fate the only fossils are therefore artifacts, and artifacts forge: a crystal dressed in scars with geometrically distributed depths is state-indistinguishable from thermal genesis by construction, and a single 2-scar — one squared prime on an otherwise squarefree state — moves the crystal's temperature reading from infinity to β̂ = 1.88. The deniability split sorts pushes by who can call them: a gcd-scar, a push sharing a prime with the state, is a probability-zero move under the thermal law — signed, certainly artificial to any watcher of the path — yet the state it leaves is thermally reachable, so it is deniable in the state; a coprime scar, a push the law itself could have made, is deniable even to the watcher. States can always be forged; only watched paths can be signed, and only gcd-scars sign.

Scope. Permanence is proved and held over 200 thermal continuations of an injected 2²; the forgery readings and the single-scar estimate are measured at β = 2 through the maximum-likelihood temperature read from the state alone; the deniability split's two specimens are exact routes.

The universal rudder rule

The depth fate is one push from any destination. Pushing 2 to depth max(3, v₂(λ) + 2) establishes the invariant v₂(λ) = depth − 2, which prices the door of 2 at the least possible move, 2, forever, so no rival door can undercut it: any world re-locks onto the 2-adic column, a 17-column six deep included. For a general prime q, one composite push raising q's depth to vq(λ) + 2, times a prime P ≡ 1 modulo a modulus that raises every rival door above q and P ≢ 1 mod q, steers the growth onto q's column; Dirichlet supplies P. Which lock a seed reaches was decidable; interactively it is programmable.

Scope. The re-lock at 2 is proved and held on 200 states; general targets are a rule in range, 16 of 16 (state, target) pairs locked, the construction argued through Dirichlet.

The phoenix criterion rule

Give mortality minimal life support: at each death — the state then is the wall W(λ) — inject the least move, 2. The push bumps only the 2-part of λ and every move the law makes between deaths is transparent, so the genome D = odd(λ(seed)), the odd part of the seed's period, is frozen for the whole afterlife, and the windows the phoenix ever opens are exactly {p : p − 1 | 2t·D}, each at the frozen depth vp(D) + 1, t counting the revivals — a designed supernatural number programmed by the seed. From seed 1 those windows are the Fermat primes at their own exponents (3, 5, 17, 257, 65537 at λ = 2, 4, 16, 256, 65536), and whether the phoenix ever opens a sixth window is the Fermat-prime question: the cheapest escape from death grows the Fermat primes.

Scope. The frozen-genome and spectrum laws are proved at every genome, the seed-1 window list exact, the criterion the spectrum law read at D = 1; whether a sixth Fermat prime exists is open.

The price list and the exactness obstruction rule

Above the minimum the push writes the genome: after pushes the windows are {p : odd(p−1) | D} at depths vp(D) + 1 with D the odd part of the new λ, every window at exactly that ceiling. Opening a chosen prime p has a three-branch price: fiat (push p itself), depth (push le+1 for each prime power le exactly dividing odd(p−1)), or cover (push the least prime q* with odd(p−1) dividing odd(q*−1)). Cover routinely undercuts fiat — 97 opens via a push of 7 at 2.8 bits against 6.6, the price in bits being log₂ of the push, and across the primes below 50000 a third cost strictly less. The floor is the exactness obstruction: an exact genome edit, adding le with no odd side effect, needs a Proth prime le·2a + 1, whose least a is 1 to 8 for almost every le ≤ 59 but 583 for 47, and which does not exist at all for a Sierpiński le.

Scope. The window law is a theorem; the price list is a census over the primes below 50000; exact-edit feasibility is the Proth/Sierpiński question, open in general.

The genome's ledger theorem

For an odd prime l write cl for its exponent in the state and dl for its exponent in the odd part of λ; the slack δl = dl − cl + 1 ≥ 0 is a countdown of free pushes: a push of le moves the genome's l-exponent by exactly max(0, e − δl), so the same push 3² writes genome 3 on a virgin 3-column and 9 on a self-grown one, and over a genome D the coordinate runs through {0, …, vl(D) + 1}, the genome a function of the accumulated product and blind to the order of the pushes. A first push of l also enters odd(l−1), crediting the slack at every prime dividing l−1, while a repeat push credits nothing: credits are minted by the pushes that move the genome and spent by the transparent ones. Genomes are lcms of the atoms qa−1·odd(q−1) over the prime powers qa of the state, so realizing a genome is deepening where the predecessor allows it and otherwise importing a prime m·2a + 1 with m | D — a reduction to Proth. Every divisibility arrow between realizable genomes is traversable; the carving is on the one-push arrows out of the empty state, which are the Proth predicate exactly, and the first provably absent one is the Sierpiński number 78557, unreachable in one push by its covering set and reachable in two.

Scope. The slack law, the fibre box, the credit ledger and the atom decomposition are proved for every state and odd prime, with 0 violations over 40000 push triples and 2345 boxes; every odd genome ≤ 999 is realizable, the last holdout 383 needing a = 6393; all 468 observed genomes are one-push reachable.

The bill doubles over F2[x] rule

Over F2[x], where a bounded alphabet makes every thermal world mortal (the mortality split), minimal life support tops the cheapest column to within its native reach of the frontier and buys exactly one native move per revival before the world re-dies one clock tick deeper, so the bill obeys billk+1 = 2·billk + D — 2, 10, 26, 58, 122 at D = 6 — the module law's diverging cost read as a price the operator pays. Over Z the rank-1 2-column crosses every frontier at constant cost 2, bill 0: the immortal witness needs no phoenix.

Scope. Proved and asserted along the D = 6 afterlife at β = 2; the afterlife is deterministic (menu size 1 at every revival), so the temperature is irrelevant to it.
