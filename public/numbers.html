Number systems

Coordinates, bases, and towers built to fit a machine word.

A rung of the tower is Z/N with N the product of the first k primes, the residue mod p its channel at p, every channel a field. The question Walls asks of an operation, through which residue window can it be read, is asked here of a coordinate system. Recoding each channel by a discrete logarithm turns multiplication into addition, and the wall that meets the new hard operation is a different kind of wall from the one it left. A positional base, and any divisibility-chain mixed radix, turns out to be exactly where two readings of one digit string coincide. And the towers designed to fill a machine word put the whole blueprint inside the ring the internet checksum runs in, with a characteristic-2 mirror beside them where the size wall keeps its core and loses everything archimedean.

The second log: index coordinates

Napier's log, one map turning × into +, is the first of three. The second is that map made exact over a residue window, and it exists only through the CRT split: the unit group is non-cyclic at every rung k ≥ 3, its exponent λ = lcm(p − 1) being smaller than its order φ = ∏(p − 1), so Z/N has no primitive root, while every channel, being a field, has one.

The index transform rule

Per-channel discrete logarithms to a primitive root compose with CRT into U(Z/N) ≅ ∏p Z/(p − 1), under which multiplication is channel-wise addition, powering is index scaling, multiplicative order is a per-channel gcd read, e-th-root existence and count are per-channel divisibility, and quadratic residuosity is the index parity bit; λ is the index ring's additive exponent. Non-units extend canonically: xλ+1 = x for all x, so the multiplicative monoid is a commutative Clifford monoid, the Boolean lattice of supports, the set of channels where an element is nonzero, carrying the sub-rings' unit groups as grades. The full coordinate is x ↔ (supp x, indices on supp x): multiplication intersects supports and adds indices, the meadow inverse, the pseudo-inverse x′ with x·x′·x = x and 0′ = 0 that makes division total on a squarefree rung, is index negation, the collapse xλ to the support idempotent is the grading map, and log 0 = −∞ is realized as grade drop.

Scope. The per-field facts are standard; what is stated is their tower-wide composition, exhaustive at Z/510510.

The Zech wall rule

The hard operation in index coordinates is addition. It costs one unary table Z(n) = ind(1 + gn) per channel, g the channel's primitive root and ind its discrete logarithm, and the wall lives within the channel: Z is incompatible with every nontrivial proper quotient of Z/(p − 1), every p ≥ 5, every divisor 2 ≤ q ≤ (p − 1)/2, composite q included, hence not polynomial. The proof is a linear-fraction count: indices mod q sort the nonzero residues into the cosets of the index-q subgroup C, compatibility says (1 + ωx)/(1 + x) ∈ C for every ω ∈ C and admissible x, and for fixed ω ≠ 1 that equation is linear in x, so exactly |C| − 2 of the p − 3 required x comply and every other yields a violating pair. A structure obstruction, the table resisting every compression, where the size wall is an information one: the index map recodes each channel independently, so the locality criterion is blind to it and addition stays channel-local.

Scope. Proved, independent of the choice of primitive root; checked over 263 (p, q) pairs, 5 ≤ p < 200.

The rigidity dichotomy rule

The coordinate systems in which + is channel-local are exactly the coprime factorizations of N, at Z/30 the four nontrivial partitions of {2, 3, 5}, coordinates counted up to relabeling within a channel since channel-local bijections compose and invert channel-locally; demanding × as well changes nothing. Multiplication alone is floppy: U(30) has four direct decompositions, three crossing tower channels. No coordinate hosts (+ local, × additive) over the whole ring. On the units the index coordinate is exactly that pair, and the zero grading stops it: a full-log value is an index or −∞, × extends only as monoid addition with −∞ absorbing, and the coordinate leaves the group world at the vanishing locus. That stop is the logarithm's pole at zero, one wall at both poles: a leading window reading sign, exponent and digits is the same log shape at the archimedean place, where the vanishing is a limit and prints as the equality wall's blind spot (The dual pole), while the finite ring affords it exactly as a Boolean grade with the zero test kept. And none hosts (+, ×, size) together: (+, ×) is CRT, (×, powering) is the index, (+, size) is positional, and all three is nowhere.

Scope. Exhaustive at k = 3 plus standard ingredients, primary decomposition for the coordinate census.

The third log: where Napier's ladder ends

The ladder suggests a third move: a super-logarithm, a coordinate turning powering into × the way the index turns × into +. It does not exist, for reasons different in kind from the Zech and size walls. Two terms. The index ring of a channel p is Z/(p − 1), where that channel's indices live; powering by e reads there as multiplication by e, so a super-log would be an index transform applied to the index ring, the same two moves one level down. And tetration x^^h is powering iterated h times, the ladder step above powering, which such a coordinate would linearize.

Powering leaves the ring rule

Powering is not a ring operation to begin with: xy is ill-defined on residue pairs, 348 of 870 witness pairs at Z/30 and at one field exactly p − 2 residues witnessing, and is well-defined only as Z/N × Z/λ(N) → Z/N, and there only for exponents ≥ 1, since xa+λ = xa holds for every x at a ≥ 1 and at a = 0 on the units alone. So the hyperoperation ladder leaves the ring at its third step, and each further level lives one ring down.

Scope. Exhaustive at Z/30 and Z/210, sampled at Z/510510.

The three grades of the third log rule

Existence of the coordinate comes in three strengths. A single super-log exists iff the index ring's unit group is cyclic (Gauss: primitive roots exist mod m iff m ∈ {1, 2, 4, qk, 2qk} for q an odd prime); inside Z/510510 the channels 13 and 17 already fail, and the passing primes thin toward density zero, 0.100, 0.071, 0.055 at 104, 105, 106. One through a second CRT split exists iff 8 ∤ p − 1, since U(2k), k ≥ 3, is the only non-cyclic prime-power unit group: odd places never block, the obstruction is entirely 2-adic, density 3/4, and only p = 17 fails inside Z/510510. And the 2-adic patch U(2k) = ⟨−1⟩ × ⟨5⟩ always exists but is group-only, Z/2k being local with no ring split to carry it.

Scope. Classical ingredients composed; the densities a census to 106.

The finite ladder rule

Even where the coordinate is patchable, it runs out of object. Iterating λ gives a strictly descending chain, 510510 → 240 → 4 → 2 → 1, height 4, and each exponent level of a tetration lives one ring further down it, so x^^h mod N is constant once h reaches the chain height. A super-logarithm mod N would take a handful of values: the operation it would coordinate has run out. This third wall is an existence obstruction, where the size wall is information and the Zech wall structure; cryptography prices the absence as the double discrete logarithm's hardness (Stadler 1996).

Scope. Validated against exact integer towers up to 7(77), every sampled tower settling by depth 4; the chain heights are Martin–Pomerance and Harland's.

The blueprint recurs on the squarefree locus rule

The recursion is self-similar exactly where the index ring is squarefree: xλ+1 = x for all x in Z/m iff m is squarefree. When p − 1 is squarefree the index ring is itself an all-field tower on a prime set that skips primes rather than starting at 2, Z/6 and Z/10 being the index rings of 7 and 11, and the whole blueprint recurs; otherwise nilpotents appear, 2 in Z/4 and 6 in Z/12, and the graded extension dies. The density of self-similar channels is Artin's constant 0.3740 (Mirsky). Iterating the construction descends the Pratt certificate tree, the second-level split being Pohlig–Hellman in algorithmic form.

Scope. The characterization validated m ≤ 300; the density a census against Mirsky's theorem, 0.3745 observed.

What a base buys

The same question one level up: not which window reads an operation, but which windows a representation keeps readable. A numeration system is a digit map plus readers, graded per query: window-read, a function of O(1) digit positions placed uniformly relative to the queried position; scan-read, one pass with O(1) state, a finite automaton; or walled. By Ostrowski the readings on offer are the archimedean window and the finite places, each finite place offering its residue ladder and its valuation. Throughout, rad m is the product of the distinct primes dividing m.

The two-readings criterion criterion

For greedy numeration on a scale 1 = U0 < U1 < U2 < ⋯, the weighted low prefix, digits 0 to j − 1, equals n mod Uj for all n and j iff the scale is a divisibility chain, Uj | Ui for i ≥ j: greedy remainders shrink below Uj and every higher term is ≡ 0, and conversely n = Ui is a single digit with zero prefix, forcing Uj | Ui. Greedy buys order on every scale, chain or not: most-significant-first lexicographic order is numeric order. So the archimedean reading comes with greedy, the residue reading with the chain, and positional base b, with every divisibility-chain mixed radix, is exactly the coincidence of the two: one digit string legible through both windows. Zeckendorf keeps only the greedy half, its prefixes not residues and adding 1 able to rewrite the whole string; its scan-read repair is Frougny's Pisot transducer, classical.

Scope. Both directions proved; exhaustive over all 9139 scales (1, a, b, c) with c ≤ 40 and all 3876 length-5 scales to 20.

The readability chart rule
systembuyspays
positional base b size, order; addition scan-read; m | n window-read iff rad m | rad b, scan-read for every m multiplication and primality walled
CRT tuple (the tower) + and × window-read at depth 1 size, order, out-of-set divisibility walled
exponent vector ×, divisibility, gcd and lcm window-read addition walled outright
factorial or primorial chain the residue ladder through every prime an unbounded digit alphabet

Addition in base b is scan-read but never window-read, a carry being launchable from a position that dodges any fixed window; the divisibility cell is a proved criterion; multiplication and primality are walled classically (recognizable multiplication would make Büchi arithmetic decide it; primes are automaton-recognizable in no base, Minsky–Papert, Hartmanis–Shank). In the CRT row the pair (q, q + N) shares the whole window and disagrees at an out-of-set q. The exponent-vector row walls addition by witness: for any finite prime window a CRT-built family yt ≡ 2t − 1 mod 2t+1, ≡ 1 at the rest of the window, has a constant all-zero exponent vector on the window while v2(1 + yt) = t, so no fixed window computes any coordinate of a sum. The last row is the mixed radix on a divisibility chain through every prime: the factorial chain eventually window-reads every m | n, the primorial chain exactly the squarefree m. Every row buys a reading and pays: a number system is a choice of which wall to pay.

Scope. Rules with witnesses per cell, classical contacts as cited (Cobham–Semenov for the cross-base rigidity, Rényi for the non-chain greedy family); the boldface synthesis is an observation over this census, not a theorem. One window sits outside the chart's carriers, the continued-fraction window, whose cell mesh is not a function of digit depth (Reading).

Designed towers

The blueprint is not primorial-specific: any squarefree set of primes is a tower ring, all-field channels, meadow, idempotents, a free choice of places. One family fills machine words exactly, and it arrives from characteristic 2, from Conway's nim-field tower: the ordinals under nim-addition and nim-multiplication, each interval [0, 22n) the field F22n, stacked by quadratic extensions.

The nim tower inverts Napier's ladder rule

Every nim-rung is a field, its only idempotents 0 and 1, so the vertical move is extension rather than product and nothing plays channel at ring level. The channels live one level down: the unit group is cyclic of order 22n − 1 = F0 ⋯ Fn−1, the Fermat numbers, so the index ring of a nim-rung is a squarefree all-field designed tower, on the five Fermat primes for n ≤ 5. The vertical tower thus inverts the ladder: a perfect second log, cyclic units and one discrete logarithm with no CRT detour, which the primorial tower lacks at every k ≥ 3, against a maximally blocked third one, every Fermat channel ≥ 17 having 8 | p − 1 and only 3 and 5 passing the single-log grade. Pratt trees of Fermat primes are paths and the λ-chain decays two bits per step, height 10 at 232 − 1 against 4 at Z/510510, so tetration depth is a design knob.

Scope. Field structure exhaustive at F256; generators order-certified at F216 and F232. Beyond n = 6 the index tower rides the open question whether every Fermat number is squarefree.

The internet-checksum ring rule

The n = 4 member is Z/65535 = Z/(3·5·17·257). RFC 1071's end-around-carry addition is reduction mod 216 − 1 and its final complement is ring negation, so every packet checksum runs whole in the ring and splits by construction into four parallel field checksums, with collapse to the support idempotent in 8 squarings. RFC 1624's incremental update is the ring identity HC′ = HC + m − m′, splitting per channel, and the boundary bug it exists to fix lives entirely in the two representatives of the zero class.

Scope. The fold exhaustive over all 131071 single-add sums; the incremental split verified at n = 4, 5 and 6.

The machine-word tower rule

The n = 5 member Z/(232 − 1) = Z/(3·5·17·257·65537) fills one 32-bit word. The fold is add-with-carry at the word width, a datapath fact; every index modulus is a power of two, so unit multiplication is five parallel masked index adds of widths 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 packing a 31-bit index word exactly, the classic index-table multiplier with every channel a field; and λ = 216 collapses any x to its support idempotent in 16 squarings. Fletcher's checksum is the family's: its two running sums live in Z/255, Z/65535 and Z/(232 − 1), the members n = 3, 4, 5, and both split per channel. The family's standing poverty: 3 | 2w − 1 at exactly the even widths, so every member carries channel 3, the one channel whose field has a single element outside {0, 1}; odd widths dodge it.

Scope. The fold law exhaustive at the member; the index stack and the Fletcher split verified by demonstration.

The uint64 departure rule

At n = 6 the family leaves the all-Fermat spine (F5 = 641 · 6700417, Euler), and the uint64 tower Z/(264 − 1), seven prime channels in one 64-bit word, charts what survives. Verbatim: the fold, squarefree all-field, the incremental split. Split: the index stack, the five masked adds keeping their 31 bits while the two F5 channels need true mod-(p − 1) adds of 10 and 23 bits, a 64-bit index word against log2 φ ≈ 63.0, the first padding bit. Dead: the cheap collapse, λ = 216 · 3 · 5 · 17449 costing 33 squarings and 13 multiplies against 16 and none. Two structural prizes ride the width. The ECC payload is the checksum ring: under the tower split, the smallest channels carrying data and the largest parity, the data product is 3·5·17·257 = 65535, so the n = 4 member is the data space of n = 6, a 16-bit-word payload riding a 64-bit word at rate 4/7, minimum distance 4, MDS at the slack-free boundary, one extra payload value dropping the distance to 3. And the comparator's cost law clears at every member but sub-bit: Σ 1/p < 0.6 at each, proved for all n, since Lucas puts every prime factor of Fk at ≡ 1 mod 2k+2, so an unfactored Fk's reciprocal sum is under Hm/2k+2 with m ≤ 2k/(k + 2), a tail summing to 0.0009 from k = 12 while F0 to F11 give 0.5976 exactly; the limit sum is under 0.5986, a margin of 0.74 bits and never a whole one.

Scope. Rules verified per member, the ECC drop exhaustive over subsets; the reciprocal tail proved from Lucas's theorem in exact arithmetic.

The polynomial mirror

The horizontal mirror is F2[x]/f with f squarefree, the polynomial analogue of a primorial ring: one channel per irreducible factor of f, each a finite field F2e.

The mirror carries the full blueprint rule

CRT bijection, 2k idempotents, the identity aλ+1 = a with λ = lcm(2ei − 1), the meadow inverse aλ−1, φ = ∏(2ei − 1). The canonical rungs f = x2d + x, the product of all irreducibles of degree dividing d (Gauss), have channels in bijection with Frobenius orbits, λ = 2d − 1 Mersenne by construction, and collapse in d squarings. So the two characteristic-2 constructions realize the two designed-tower hardware families as index rings, the nim tower one Fermat tower and the mirror per-channel Mersenne rings, designed iff 2e − 1 is squarefree: the first failure is e = 6, prime exponents are clean to 40, and the general question is open, tied to Wieferich primes. The locality criterion transfers verbatim, channel-local, compatible and polynomial coinciding at the 4- and 8-element rings and the thin boundary recurring at F2[x]/x², 64 of 256: the criterion never used the integers.

Scope. Exhaustive at a 1024-element mirror ring; the criterion exhaustive at the 4- and 8-element rings.

The size wall softens exactly in its archimedean part rule

Degree is incompatible, with witnesses at every channel: the information core of the wall transfers verbatim. What disappears is everything archimedean, F2(x) having no archimedean place (Artin–Whaples). Sign is vacated, no characteristic-2 field admitting an order (Artin–Schreier); carries are gone, deg(ab) = deg a + deg b exactly; and the partial-fraction escape becomes exact-or-flagged, a t-term truncation reading the degree exactly or returning an all-zero self-flagging prefix, with zero silent failures where the integer escape fails silently near wraparound. Carries are the archimedean place's shadow in positional digits. Mixed radix stays triangular, and the diagonal escape collapses to an F2-linear map. The mirror's prime side is closed form, the Euler product over irreducibles equal to 1/(1 − 2t) coefficientwise, so the sieve questions trivialize there: the mirrors feed engineering, not the map.

Scope. The incompatibility and the exact-or-flagged reading exhaustive at the mirror ring; the vacated order and the missing place are classical.
