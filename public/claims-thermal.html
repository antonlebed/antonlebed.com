Thermal and density

  • The blueprint's grading rule — Mortality's wall absorbs at every temperature, breadth's destination needs only full support, and squarefree-ness melts to a law: each prime enters at most once, at a depth geometric with ratio p−β, independent across primes. — explore_thermal_growth.py
  • The lock melts rule — At any finite β a blocked rival opening costs a constant while λ's exponent there stands, so it fires almost surely: from seed 2, 100 of 100 runs at β = 2 open a second window within 40 steps; the argmin melts, the door price does not. — explore_thermal_growth.py
  • The hot-limit theorem rule — The dynamics demand's admissible set is upward-closed in divisibility, so P(p | pick) ≥ p−β uniformly and hot dynamics-greed reaches the full profinite completion almost surely for every seed and β > 1; the lock is a β = ∞ artifact. — explore_hot_limit.py
  • The closure trichotomy rule — Dynamics is upward-closed and co-finite, its shape forcing the top; independence is support-avoiding, its shape forcing entry-once; mortality is downward-closed and finite, its shape forcing nothing without its budget. — explore_hot_limit.py
  • The zeta measure rule — The hot-breadth limit has independent geometric depths of ratio p−β and is squarefree with probability 1/ζ(β), so the Riemann zeta function is the thermal tower's partition function; at β → 1⁺ the profile tends to Haar measure. — explore_thermal_growth.py
  • The radical and the tombstones rule — Cold independence grows the semisimple quotient Ẑ/J(Ẑ) and hot dynamics the full completion, temperature's gift exactly the radical; at the walls N = W(λ(N)) the thermal law is the free zeta law P(m) ∝ m−β. — explore_hot_limit.py
  • Transparency density approaches 1 theorem — The fraction of transparent primes among the first k approaches 1, log lcm{p−1 : p ≤ x} = o(x), by an elementary unconditional proof; the matching lower bound is Elliott–Halberstam-hard. — explore_lcm_shifted_primes.py, explore_shifted_prime_density.py, explore_asymptotic_density.py, explore_density_extended.py
  • Safe primes are never transparent rule — Every safe prime p = 2q + 1 is non-transparent with jump exactly q, no prime ≡ 1 mod q sitting below p; the twenty largest jumps among the first 500 primes are all safe primes. — explore_prime_gaps.py
  • The complexity ledger rule — λ grows only at non-transparent steps, each raising exactly one prime power, so log λ(pk#) < Nnt(k) log pk and transparency density → 1 forces the complexity ratio α = log λ/log φ → 0, measured 0.30 → 0.165. — explore_complexity_ledger.py
  • The collision equivalence theorem — α = 1 − collision/log φ exactly, and Nnt(k)/k − α(k) → 0 at rate O(log log x/log x), so α → 0 iff transparency density → 1; density → 1 being proved, lim α = 0 unconditionally. — explore_collision_equivalence.py, explore_ledger_threshold.py
