Growth over other fields

The same demands run over a number field or a function field, and what each ring charges: the local invariant deciding whether a demand locks or sprawls, what a ring's symmetry, its ghosts and its class group do to the lock, the sprawl's exact shape, and how much of all that temperature dissolves.

A growth law is a structural demand plus a greedy move: extend the state by the least admissible m ≥ 2, a tie-break settling any choice among equally cheap admissible moves. Over Z the state is a modulus N and the demands trichotomize into three fates, breadth, depth and mortality (Growth). Throughout, λ(N) is the exponent of the unit group, the least L with aL = 1 for every unit a; a prime's window is its slot in the state, OPENED by seating the prime and DEEPENED by raising its exponent; and the door at a prime is the least move there that raises λ against the whole state at once. The depth demand is dynamics-greed, which asks that λ grow; over Z it locks, collapsing within a bounded number of moves onto one prime's column forever with no rival door falling afterwards, and a law's basin map sends each seed to the place its trajectory ends on. Over a ring of integers a prime is replaced by a place, a prime ideal, with a norm (the size of its residue field), a ramification index e and a residue degree f; the state is either an ideal, the ideal world, or an element generating one, the element world, where only principal ideals are states and moves. A move's cost is its norm. Everything below replaces Z.

The price of depth

The local module law theorem

At a place with residue field Fq, q = pf, ramification e, write Ua for the units congruent to 1 modulo the a-th power of the place and E(a) = exp(U1/Ua) for the exponent of the 1-units read to depth a. In mixed characteristic U1 has Zp-rank ef and the p-power map is an isomorphism Ub → Ub+e past the wild threshold, so E(a+e) = p·E(a) eventually: a linear pump, one λ-tick per e of depth at the eventually constant price qe = pef = prank. In equal characteristic (1+u)pm = 1 + upm gives E(a) = p⌈logp a⌉ exactly, with rank infinite: a log clock whose tick prices are unbounded along any recurring column. The lock is the finite-rank case and the sprawl its rank-∞ limit.

Scope. Both cases are proved; instances brute-verified over full 1-unit grids at Z (the columns at 2, 3, 5), at the split, inert and ramified places of Q(√−23), and at F2[[t]], F3[[t]], F9[[t]], every mixed-characteristic threshold at most 3. The structure of the 1-unit group is classical (Neukirch, Algebraic Number Theory, II §5); what is read off it is the price.

The cross-field lock rule

Cold dynamics-greed locks in mixed characteristic and sprawls in equal characteristic. Every ideal trajectory censused over Q, Z[√−5], Q(√−23) and the cubic field of discriminant −283 locks, at the quadratic fields with at most two moves before the lock, and the recurrent price is prank on every trajectory: split 3-locks pay 3 a move, the ramified place above 5 in Z[√−5] pays 25, its place above 2, where 2 ramifies, pays 4, and a 2-lock over Q(√−23), where 2 splits into two places of residue degree 1, pays 2. Over F2[x] the same demand locks onto nothing: openings recur forever from every seed, under any tie-break, because a fresh door of cost at most ⌊log2 λ⌋ + 2 always exists while every deepening at the dyadic clock c, the exponent of 2 in λ, costs at least 2c−1 once that clears the seed's depths, exponential in the deepening count against a linearly growing alternative.

Scope. The sprawl is proved; the locks are a rule in range, 54 and 80 ideal seeds × 40 moves at the two quadratic fields and 44 at the cubic, 63 seeds × 60 moves over F2[x]. The price law is this census's; the module law above is what makes it general.

Crystal and absorption rule

Over any Dedekind ring with finite residue fields and finitely many places under any norm, ideal moves are single-place: the λ-moving part of any bundle moves λ alone and costs strictly less, so Z's door lemma survives in the ideal world at every class number and dies only in the element world. A place picked infinitely often at bounded cost absorbs the tail, and two such places are impossible, since equal constant prices would be equal prime powers of distinct primes, and a same-characteristic pair leapfrogs one out. So a bounded-cost tail IS a lock at the recurrent price prank, and in equal characteristic move costs diverge: the sprawl is general to every equal-characteristic Dedekind ring at the level of cost divergence. Doors are monotone while unmoved, but move costs are not: the ideal seed (7) over Q(√−23) pays 23, 23, 27 and then locks 3 at 3 a move, a catch-up followed by cheapness, which is why every proof here runs through boundedness and never through a staircase.

Scope. Proved; the cost drop is the census's specimen. What the transient of the pump reads classically, the ramification of the cyclotomic tower over the base, is the constellation law.

What the ring does to the lock

Ties are Galois orbits rule

Z's greedy needs no tie-break because prime powers of distinct primes are distinct integers. Over F2[x] the norm collapses onto degrees and ties are generic: the first dynamics move from the void, the seed 1, is a three-way tie at cost 2. Over a number field a door costs N(place)r, a power of one rational prime, so in the ideal world equal costs lie over one prime and only a split prime carries two places: every ideal tie is a pair of conjugate places, by proof, and the dynamics is conjugation-equivariant move by move, so the greedy must break the field's own symmetry (the void's first move over Z[√−5] is one place above 3 against its conjugate). Every field other than Q has split primes, so the greedy rule is complete over Q alone: determinism is a rational accident. Past degree 2 a tie need not be conjugate: the cubic field ties its rank-1 place above 2 at door 2 against its residue-degree-2 place above 2 at door 1, one prime, two norms, equal cost 4.

Scope. The ideal-world statement is proved. In the element world, where a move can straddle two rational primes, it is a measurement: 16 element ties at Z[√−5] and 12 at Q(√−23), every one a conjugate pair. It is a statement about single ties, and two independent ties compose into more limits than the group has elements (The surviving openings). The cubic tie sits in 3 of 453 census menus.

The ghost spectrum rule

A ghost is an opening at a prime q that already divides λ while q − 1 does not, a cheap door that never binds, whose dowry, the factors of q − 1 it adds to λ, closes cheaper destinies behind it (the lock-prime law). Over F2[x] the class is empty by type separation: the state lives in F2[x] and λ in Z, so "g divides λ" is untyped. In a number ring ghosts return and class field theory edits them. In Z[√−5] the seed at a place above 29 ghosts the tied pair above 7, whose dowry lifts the 3-door from 9 to 27 and hands the lock to the fresh pair above 23; a 5-ghost is impossible there, because every carrier of 5 drags 4 into λ (a split prime ≡ 1 mod 5 is ≡ 1 mod 20, an inert q has 8 | q² − 1, and the place above 5 carries 4 itself), where Z has them (seed 11); and the whole norm-40 belt of Q(√−23) fires none. Which hauntings a ring admits is field geography.

Scope. The emptiness over F2[x] and the impossibility of a 5-ghost in Z[√−5] are proved; the specimen and the two belt censuses are the record.

The class-group split rule

At class number h > 1 the depth fate runs once per currency. Growing by ideals, every censused field reproduces Z's geography: each void locks a norm-3 place at 3 a move, at the quadratic fields as a broken conjugate tie, Z[√−5]'s norm-40 belt locks at 2, 3, 7 and 23 plus a constructed lock at its ramified place above 5, Q(√−23)'s at 2, 3, 5, 13 and 29, and the cubic field's at norms 2, 3, 4, 5 and 17. Growing by elements, the world pays its class group in price, and the basin map is a norm race among the principal vehicles the seed's dowry leaves alive. Z[√−5] is a monobasin, every element seed riding to (2), the square of the place above 2, at 4 a move. Q(√−23) has two basins: 38 censused seeds to (2), the product of the two places above 2, at 4 a move, and three, the seed (5) and the two elements of norm 26, to the inert prime 5 at 25 a move, a pure principal column at the ideal price, because the seed's own dowry λ((5)) = 24 already covers every cheaper door. The cubic field has three: 17 seeds to the square of its cheapest place, 3 to a principal norm-17 place tax-free, and 1 to the rational (2) at 8 = 2³, the first tail to pay its ring's degree, because that seed's dowry covers the cheap square's every step and the degree-priced vehicle is the cheapest left. The monobasin is field geography, not a law of h > 1.

Scope. Rule in range: censused wall to wall at Z[√−5] (h = 2) and Q(√−23) (h = 3) and over the norm ≤ 40 belt of the cubic field of discriminant −283 (h = 2), both currencies, 42, 41 and 21 element seeds.

The element price pattern

An element tail pays its cheapest one-move principal vehicle still ticking, still moving λ against the state: Pm at norm pfm, m the place's class order, against the rational (p) at norm pn, n the ring's degree, so pmin(fm, n) a move wherever both tick. Z's columns pay p; Z[√−5]'s wild (2) pays 4, the power being the bundle; Q(√−23)'s (2) pays 4 with m = 3 and its inert (5) pays 25, the ideal price; the cubic field's square of its place above 2 pays 4, its norm-17 place 17, and one seed pays 8 = p³. The tax over the ideal price pef exists only at fm > n and is pure myopia: the tax-free cube of the place above 2 in Q(√−23) sits at norm 8 forever, three depths per purchase at 2 a tick, and a per-move greedy never picks it. And the tax need not buy depth at all. The cubic's taxed square pays 4 a move while λ quadruples, two doubling steps at the ideal world's own price of 2 a step, so where the vehicle squares a place whose column carries a flat step the tax buys granularity and never step price.

Scope. Pattern: two quadratic fields and one cubic, seven censused tail species, no proof. The still-ticking clause is load-bearing, the cubic's dowry edit being the counterexample without it; the pricing it rides is the module law.

The free-rider door and the passenger lift rule

A nonprincipal column's cheapest vehicle is the free-rider door: the ticking nonprincipal place bundled with a flat principal partner at the product norm, in Z[√−5] the norm-6 move that deepens the 3-window while carrying the place above 2 along at no tick. Z's door lemma dies here, the minimal principal move being no prime power. Every ride lifts the passenger one depth, so a trajectory that rides feeds the 2-column until its wild clock regularizes and (2) absorbs the walk: all 144 censused rider moves in Z[√−5] are flat at the place above 2 at ride time. The lift is real and conditional. Over Q(√−23) the three seeds that lock inert 5 never ride, and their 2-depths stay at seed level forever.

Scope. Rule in range: the mechanism is proved in part, that no principal ideal of norm 2 or 3 exists and that every ride feeds the 2-column, and that the lift forces the (2)-tail exactly on the trajectories that ride is the two censuses. The thermal clock split at Z[√−5], the element clock 1.3527 below the ideal 1.5492, is this cold asymmetry's thermal shadow (Irreducibility).

The wild seat holds only from depth rule

A place is wild when the residue characteristic divides its ramification index; in Z[√−5] the place P above 2 is wild, (2) = P², and its column's λ runs 1, 2, 4, 4, 4, 4, 8, 8, 16, …, a flat window at depths 3 to 6. In the ideal world the seed (2) grabs its own norm-2 door once, lands in that window and loses the lock to a fresh place above 7 at 7, while the seed P5, born past the window, locks 2 at 4 a move from its first move. In the element world every seed locks (2). The cubic field's place above 2, unramified, its column flat at depths 2 and 3, holds at every seeded depth in its census, the bare seat included, one door-2 jump clearing the window. Over F2(x) the seat inverts: every place ticks the same dyadic clock at the Frobenius log rate, and a wild-ticking clock holds no lock.

Scope. Rule in range: the two seeds over Z[√−5] and the 44-seed cubic census; the flat window is brute-verified over full unit grids to depth 13.

The sprawl

Over F2[x] a place is a monic irreducible g, its norm 2deg g, and λ factors as the least common multiple over seated places of (2deg g − 1)·2⌈log2 depth⌉: an odd part built from Mersenne numbers and a 2-part read off the dyadic clock c shared by every column. The door menu has three classes, with d = deg g and e the place's depth: a deepening at cost d(2c + 1 − e), a fresh opening at cost d where 2d − 1 does not divide the odd part of λ, and a clocked opening at cost d(2c + 1) where it does. Cost is the integer whose binary digits are the polynomial's coefficients, so "least" orders by degree first.

The sibling shadow and the sibling starvation rule

The first fresh opening at degree d puts 2d − 1 into λ and shuts every same-degree sibling out of the fresh door forever, the shadow. The starvation shuts the clocked door too: a sibling of a seated irreducible of degree d can enter only at d(2c + 1), where the seated one's own deepening costs d(2c + 1 − e), strictly cheaper at every clock. So greedy dynamics seats AT MOST ONE irreducible per degree, from any seed under any tie-break: the void run seats 1 of the 99,858 irreducibles of degree 21, x + 1 never opens, and the support has density zero among the irreducibles. That is infinite support, not breadth. The limit is a supernatural number with one infinite exponent and infinitely many finite ones, and the greedy sits interior in its support coordinate where over Z it is extremal in both (The limit).

Scope. Both laws are proved, tie-break- and seed-independent; the starvation is exercised 190 to 259 times per census run. Degree-1 places are born clocked, since 21 − 1 = 1, so a place with residue field F2 can never open fresh and enters only as depth fuel.

The void's closed form rule

From the seed 1 the sprawl is closed form: one clocked opening, x², then the dyadic warm-up on that column, then one fresh irreducible per degree frontier, the least degree whose fresh door is open, always the least irreducible of that degree, with the depth moves x2j firing exactly as the clock's power-of-2 frontiers are crossed, of degree 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32. The degree frontier advances at every degree: 2d − 1 carries a prime dividing no smaller 2d′ − 1 (Zsigmondy, with the one exception d = 6 checked directly), so a fresh door of degree d is open the first time the walk reaches it. The lex-least irreducible x wins every tie at a frontier, and its twin x + 1 starves forever.

Scope. Rule in scope, 60 moves, the degree frontier reaching 54; its advance is proved. The column carrying the tail's depth thread across the 63-seed census is always of degree at most 2.

The melt

Run the thermal law, an admissible m picked with probability proportional to |m|−β at a temperature parameter β > 1 rather than the argmin (Thermal and density), over F2[x], with |m| = 2deg m the norm at the infinite place of F2(x). The crystal is the squarefree limit a breadth trajectory reaches when every irreducible enters once, at depth 1, and none deeper.

The function-field melt rule

The apparatus transfers with closed forms. The partition function is rational, ζF2[x](β) − 1 = r/(1−r) with r = 21−β, the degree of a pick exactly geometric, and the crystal probability is exactly 1 − 21−β: 1/2 at β = 2 against Z's 1/ζ(2) ≈ 0.608, no transcendental content surviving. The hot-limit theorem transfers, so thermal dynamics reaches the full profinite completion almost surely at every β > 1, and the sprawl's geography is a β = ∞ artifact exactly as Z's lock is: the starved twin x + 1 opens in 96 of 100 runs at β = 2, both irreducibles of degree 3 open together in 7 of 100, and depth leaves the one column that carried it. Over Z temperature's gift to the dynamics demand was depth, the radical; here, where cold growth already runs one column's radical while starving breadth, the gift is breadth. Heat also dissolves the tie-break the cold walk required: equal-degree moves share weight democratically, and the thermal law takes no tie-break parameter at any finite β.

Scope. The closed forms are exact. The transfer is the hot-limit theorem's own argument run here: λ is divisibility-monotone over F2[x] too, so admissibility is closed upward, and the chance that a pick deepens a given irreducible g by at least k is at least |g|−βk whatever the state. The run counts are 100-trajectory Monte Carlo at β = 2.

The menu has two channels criterion

A move is admissible iff it fires the clock channel, some place, old or new, reaching a depth past the clock frontier 2c, or the odd channel, a fresh place g with 2deg g − 1 not dividing the odd part of λ; nothing else grows λ, 1-units being 2-groups in characteristic 2 and Mersenne numbers odd. The odd channel is a lattice: the degree-d fresh door is open iff no open degree is a multiple of d, uniformly for d ≥ 2, the Zsigmondy exception d = 6 covered because 9 divides 26 − 1 and no smaller 2d′ − 1. So the odd channel fires at most once per degree ever, opening a degree closes every unopened door dividing it, degree-1 places never fire it, and riders, a fresh door carrying a factor xr, creep a column toward the clock frontier for free within a total budget (D − 1)(D − 2)/2 at alphabet bound D. On the full menu the minimal admissible degree is the least of three named channels, the fresh open door, an old crossing and a fresh closed crossing, and the number of admissible moves of each degree is computed exactly by a generating-function count over the place menu. The two channels are the module law's rank split read as a menu partition.

Scope. Proved; asserted exhaustively over 20 states × every monic of degree ≤ 9, over all 4096 subsets of degrees {1, …, 12} for the lattice, and against brute counts at every degree on every battery state for the full-menu count.

The mortality split rule

What temperature never touches is the module law's diverging cost. Bound the alphabet, moves of degree ≤ D only, and every F2[x] thermal trajectory halts: λ at least doubles per move while the odd part is capped at one fresh Mersenne factor per degree ever, so the clock must climb about once a move, and holding a reading c needs some depth past 2c−1 where total degree grows only D a move, a linear budget against an exponentially receding clock frontier. The death is clock-authored: from a light seed, one starting well below the mode, its mode is c*(D) = ⌊log2 D⌋ + 2, the bit-length of the alphabet bound plus one, because a clock-frontier gap 2j is affordable iff 2j ≤ D and thermal picks land minimally. Only the mode is temperature-free. The tail is priced by the dyadic phase of D, its distance 2J+1 − D to the next power of 2: at phase 1 the mode is exceeded in 29 of 100 runs at β = 2, at phase 8 in none, and at D = 2 never, while at fixed D = 6 the exceedance runs 18.75%, 12.5%, 3.25% as β rises through 1.25, 2, 3. Heat can also strand the clock below the mode on an expensive column whose crossings the alphabet cannot pay. Riders set the reachable ceiling near 2 log2 D against the mode's log2 D, so thermal typicality reaches half the designed ceiling, and halt times obey T ≤ (D − 1) + (cfinal − c0), every admissible move raising the clock or spending a door. Z, by contrast, has an immortal bounded-alphabet witness: on the pure 2-column the move 2 rides the linear pump forever.

Scope. The halt is proved and the halt-time bound follows from the two channels, every move raising the clock or spending a door; the mode law is a rule for the mechanism, Monte Carlo at D ∈ {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12}, 60 to 100 runs each, every run halting, stated for the light seed x²; the ceiling is an exact count within the single-leader class, tight against its budget bound except at D = 5, 8, 12.

The escalator rule

Lift the degree bound and the forced march has a shape. Along thermal trajectories from the light seed the alphabet is linear, the fresh open door's degree tracking the move count, d*(T) ≥ T − 8 at β = 2 and 3 with d*/T → 1, while the clock stays logarithmic, at 5 or 6 after 26 moves. Pick degrees are unbounded, since an eventually bounded tail would be a bounded-alphabet trajectory from its own seed and would halt against an always-nonempty menu. The thermal roof sits a saturation gap above the naive geometric mean r/(1 − r): admissibility saturates a few degrees above the floor, almost every high-degree monic carrying a fresh open factor, so the mean overhead of a pick's degree over the floor is 2.7 at β = 2 and 1.1 at β = 3 against the geometric 1.0 and 0.33. The lattice tax, a skip that opens a multiple of a still-open door and kills it unused, is not zero but geometrically suppressed, about 2(1−β)d*, and vanishes as the window deepens.

Scope. The unbounded-degree corollary is proved; the structure is a rule and the distribution Monte Carlo, 26-move trajectories sampled exactly within a reported degree truncation whose relative tail is under 10−4.

The breathing column rule

The two ends of the module law show on a single column. Along the pure x-column the chance that the next pick leaves the column oscillates with the dyadic phase of the depth: exactly 1/2 at each clock frontier in the deep-window limit, measured 0.500 to 0.501 at depths 4, 8, 16, 32, and molten one step off it, in [0.9989, 1] at depth 33, since off the clock frontier the door recedes to 2c − a + 1. Sticky one move per window, melted the rest. Z's 2-column is instead a phase-constant band, the leaving probability in [0.312, 0.374] at every depth from 3 to 30, because the linear pump prices the door at 2 forever. A rank-finite pump is constant stickiness; a rank-infinite log clock breathes.

Scope. Rule in range, depths to 64 at β = 2, the inside mass an exact geometric series and the outside mass bracketed two-sided; Z's band is the menu enumerated exactly at the states 2a.

The melt's seat rule

In both characteristics, selection-authored structure, the geography, the route, the tie-breaks and the lock, melts at β = ∞ exactly, and module-authored structure, the cost divergence and the escalator, is temperature-invariant. No dynamical transition sits inside the thermal clocks' window: the limit object of thermal dynamics is constant, the full completion, on the whole of 1 < β < ∞, with rates moving smoothly (the fresh-pick fraction 0.753, 0.735, 0.720 at β = 1.5, 2, 3), so the critical structure the clocks read belongs to the zeta measure's genesis and not to the dynamics (Irreducibility).

Scope. The constancy of the limit is the hot-limit transfer; the rates are a three-point scan, an observation.

The cascade boundary

Every lock above is a census. Whether a lock must exist at all reduces to ruling out an infinite ladder of carriers, one at every characteristic carrying a rank-1 place, and the escape's demand is a conjunction over characteristics, so one characteristic closed closes the ring. A budget inequality closes 2, 3 and 5 outright, a direct walk closes every odd characteristic below 1000, still dying when the multiplier is widened by the factor an element move pays, and the residual is inhabited at every bound by the primorial field, which ramifies every prime below it, so a close covering every ring at once is a theorem's job: The cascade boundary. Which characteristics a field closes cheaply is decided by whether their places are principal, an arithmetic of its own on Places.
