Nesting

Whether lowering a patience improves anything comes down to whether the two readers' committed cells nest. The floor every reader's cells contain, what a failure of nesting costs, which single moves nest and which never do, the corner where nesting is proved, and the two inequalities at a single step that turn out to be that proof's hypothesis.

The reader being described is a commitment policy over a cover of intervals (descent in reader space); what stops one is stalls. A stream is the continued-fraction expansion of a real number, arriving digit by digit, and a map is a function applied to it. After n digits the value is pinned to a cylinder, and what the reader is certain of about the output is the image interval, that cylinder's picture under the map; these nest and shrink as digits arrive. Under the identity map the image is the cylinder itself; the other two maps censused are doubling and squaring.

What a reader commits to are cells drawn from one fixed family, the cover: the tree cells, the Stern–Brocot cells, each the set of positive reals whose expansion opens with a given run of digits, and the straddle chains standing at their vertices, a cell's vertex being the mediant of its two endpoints, the fraction whose numerator and denominator are their sums. Both kinds are graded by rank, which steps by one along every child relation (the redundant cover). A commitment is a ratchet, never undone. What a policy chooses is the route past a vertex — the tree child or the chain — and how long to wait before committing: four coordinates, a route preference at each of the two cell kinds and a patience for each. A kind at patience p commits a candidate only once it has contained the image for p+1 consecutive steps; patience 0 is greedy, committing on sight, and patience ∞ refuses that kind outright. Within one input step a reader multi-commits, taking commit moves for as long as any remains available. A kind's reference is the image interval its patience makes a candidate contain, the one from p steps back; a patience of 0 is pinned to the present image, doubly pinned when both are, and freshening a patience is lowering it by one. The spine is the policies whose two kinds share one reference: equal patience at both, no resource cap, every patience shorter than the counted window's start.

What a policy is priced by is the deficit over a counted window of input steps, the same for every policy compared, its length the horizon — writing a cell's scale for ln(1/length), the image interval's scale less the committed cell's, summed by cross-multiplying big integers so that no float enters a decision — with a second, coarser clock ruler counting that lag in whole steps. A row is one (map, stream) pair. Policies whose committed-cell traces over the window agree are identified, and the classes of that behavioural quotient are what a descent moves between; the members of a class are its policies, and its standing is its position in the landscape's loss order, counted from the bottom. Neighbouring policies differ by one notch in one coordinate, or by both route preferences flipped together; a run is one policy walked over one stream; and a stall is a policy with no strictly better neighbour. Where a resource cap is in force it enters as a setting: a rank budget B of cover-rank units per input step and a bank of capacity W holding what a step does not spend, against which the policy gains a fifth coordinate, its drawdown. A setting with neither is unresourced. A landscape is one loss surface, a row and a setting fixed. Three censuses recur below: the stall census, the ten landscapes holding the corpus's ten finite-loss stalls, 601 off-bottom classes and 1,362 members, resourced and unresourced; the wide census, unresourced, every off-bottom finite-loss class of the identity and doubling batteries at horizons 9, 10, 12 and 16, 9,401 identity classes and 11,935 doubling; and the digit scans, exhaustive over every stream on a fixed digit alphabet at a fixed length.

The floor theorem theorem

From any member of any off-bottom finite-loss class, unresourced, the doubly pinned policy at that member's own route preferences commits at every counted step a cell inside the member's, and wherever its class is distinct from the member's it strictly improves the deficit. The nesting half is the minimal cell read at a member: the doubly pinned run commits the floor of the current image — the inclusion minimum of the cover cells containing it, a closed form that reads the image and runs no policy — which sits inside every policy's cell at every step in any policy space of this family (the bottom lemma), so no counted window, route preference or class qualifier is needed for it. The improving half follows from nesting alone: distinct classes differ at some counted interval, and a nested interval that differs is strictly shorter. A strictly better class therefore exists from every class off the bottom at finite loss, the three unresourced stalls of the census included; what is measured rather than proved at those three is how far it is, never whether it is there.

Scope. Proved for this cover and move set with no resource cap in force, at any map, stream, horizon and patiences. Checked at 102,092 of 102,092 members of the wide census, none trivial: an unresourced member already doubly pinned stands at the bottom, which off-bottom excludes. The strictness is the deficit's.

verifiers: explore_pinned_freshening.py, explore_minimal_cell.py

The resource fence rule

"The floor's class is the bottom" holds unresourced — the four doubly pinned policies are one class, at standing 0 — and fails under a cap: at the seven resourced landscapes of the stall census they occupy 3, 4, 8 or 12 classes at standings 0 to 11, because a budgeted reader cannot always afford the floor, so the route and drawdown coordinates enter the run. What survives the fence is the conclusion, and in the composite order — the clock loss primary, the deficit its tiebreak — rather than only in the deficit the theorem proves: over all 601 off-bottom classes and 1,362 members, the floor's class stands strictly lower wherever it is distinct, 1,328 of 1,328, resourced landscapes included. The 34 remaining members are the doubly pinned policy, and their 30 classes are exactly the 30 no patience jump of any distance improves (the move-set law): a class holding its own floor has nowhere down the axis to go.

Scope. Exhaustive at the stall census. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_move_set.py

What one freshening cures rule

The residue the shift telescope leaves is every class with no member holding both patiences finite and positive — 2,800 identity and 4,215 doubling classes of the wide census, none frozen, more than five-sixths of them pinned to the present. Its one move is to freshen the other reference, and from some member that reaches a pointwise-nested strictly improving class at distance one at 2,546 of 2,546 identity tree-side classes, 597 of 597 doubling chain-side, and 2,535 of 2,955 doubling tree-side; the 420 that reach nothing at one step all sit at a pinned chain, and every one reaches such a class at two freshenings from a single member through a distinct intermediate class. The two sides are not symmetric: at a pinned tree, freshening the chain holds containment at every step in all 108 battery pairs, while at a pinned chain freshening the tree breaks it in 31 of 108 inside the counted window, the first of them under the identity map; sorted by route preference, the chain-preferring corner carries six of the 38 misses and every one of those is under doubling or squaring, none under the identity map, which is the scope the chain-preferring theorem says there can be none at. The three squaring-map stalls sit at a pinned chain, and of their strictly improving classes at distance two exactly one nests against the stall, reached by freshening the tree twice from the stall's own member at one pair of route preferences, the first freshening neither nesting nor improving. Nothing about two is load-bearing: at each of the 420 a witness sits at patience 2, so the second freshening is the walk to the floor, and the walk is no chain of nestings — nesting into the start turns on, off and on again along it — so no stepwise argument reaches the floor, and what does is the containing-cell minimum, which is not a route at all. That the loss is worse at one freshening and better at two is entailed by stallhood at escape radius 2 and names a definition, not a phenomenon.

Scope. Exhaustive over the pinned residue of the wide census; the asymmetry over the 108 battery pairs. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_pinned_telescope.py, explore_pinned_composite.py

The decision lemma rule

On the spine nesting is outright (the spine theorem), and every finite-loss stall the corpus has found sits off it. Off the spine nesting is conditional, and the condition is whether a route decision was available at all. Call a step decision-free when no iteration of its multi-commit had both a tree and a chain candidate at once — the one configuration in which the loop has a route preference to consult. Along a patience-down move, one lowering a single patience, committed cells nest at every step whose whole history is decision-free in both runs. From a common start the proof is complete: a smaller tree reference sits in the same tree child and a smaller chain reference gives a weakly longer chain, so the two loops walk the same branch sequence with the lower-patience one refining at least as far, and a candidate appearing for the smaller reference is itself a second candidate. What the engine carries alone is the induction across steps, where the two runs start from different cells — the monotone-fixed-point step the bottom lemma records as tight in its single-reference form. Of 741,888 patience-down (policy, step) pairs, the 188,928 decision-free in both runs have zero nesting exceptions, which puts all 71,023 non-nested pairs outside that set; the other 481,937 carry a decision and nest anyway, so the condition is necessary and not sufficient. Hence what any unresourced off-spine stall must satisfy: every patience-down neighbour is separated from it by an available decision, since a nested neighbour is strictly better while both traces are finite-loss — and at finite patience below the counted-window start that is every trace measured, none of 9,482 fully nested policy pairs having the finer one standing worse. That a decision alone can then buy a late gain with an early loss is the decision trade.

Scope. Exhaustive over 741,888 patience-down comparisons, unresourced, across the corpus's nine rows and their battery; the common-start half proved for this cover and move set, the induction across steps carried by the scans. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_stall_unresourced.py

The shift telescope theorem

Lowering both patiences by one is an exact one-step delay — the shifted reader's every cell is the parent's next-step cell — so it nests pointwise for free, its loss telescopes to the counted window's endpoints, and it strictly improves whenever it leaves the class. That puts a proved nested improver at distance 2 for every class holding a member with both patiences finite and positive whose window still refines past its start, and it avoids the routing-step case analysis outright: the two runs are the same run read one step apart, and the spine theorem's diagonal nesting is its equal-patience case. At the wide census it reaches 6,601 of the 9,401 identity classes and 7,718 of the 11,935 doubling, zero exceptions, the rest pinned or frozen, a class whose window no longer refines; the three squaring-map stalls are singletons at patiences (3, 0), (3, 0) and (2, 0), outside its reach and consistent with their stallhood. What nesting buys once it holds is a margin of at least one cover refinement, the one-cell wall.

Scope. Proved for this cover and move set, unresourced, at any map and stream; the reach counted at the wide census.

verifier: explore_shift_telescope.py

The patience-down monopoly and the seed rule

The nesting cure move at distance one is a patience-down every time: no flip, diagonal or patience-up move witnesses nesting at any of the 21,336 classes of the wide census, and the 632 doubling classes that dent the one-cell wall's domination condition — a one-move neighbour nested-or-equal at every counted step — are exactly its classes with no patience-down witness. The choice of member is load-bearing — a single tree-patience-down breaks pairwise nesting in 3,225 of 44,040 identity pairs, a chain one in 8,274 — while granting a reference where none existed nests at every counted step, zero failures in 14,680 pairs each way. Instrumented, the two parallel commit loops admit exactly two divergences from equal cells: a nested descent, or the seed, a tree-preferring cell where only the fresher reference opens a door while the chain is live. Under the identity map the seed fires only before the counted window and breaks no witness; the break path is nested drift after a nested divergence, 2,284 of the 3,225 counted failures carrying no seed and none of the 12,623 entries into a non-nesting state carrying one at entry. Under the squaring map the stall members' bad phase persists into the window instead.

Scope. Exhaustive at the wide census. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_shift_telescope.py, explore_seed_exclusion.py

The chain-preferring nesting theorem theorem

Under the identity map, a reader preferring the chain at both cell kinds has its tree-patience-down run nested pointwise at every step — across the whole chain-preferring slice, every chain patience against every tree patience, at every stream. At the wide census 14,680 such pairs show zero failures at any step, settling 5,774 of the 9,401 identity classes by selection; the other 3,627 witness nesting only with a tree preference at one or both kinds, clean inside the window because every bad phase sits before it. At the digit scans under the identity map, 3,696,980 pair runs at distinct coverage over twenty chain-preferring patience pairs show zero bad steps. Off the identity map the same readers drift, always in one configuration, the crossing: with the two runs the elder, at the higher patience, and the fresher, the elder's committed straddle has its near endpoint — near meaning on the value's side of the vertex — strictly interior to the fresher run's cell, never an inverted containment; and always in the stale regime, the fresher run's tree reference strictly staler than the shared chain reference, its complement the fresh regime showing zero failures under every map. The squaring map's 2,620 bad pair runs split 2,440 by a tree reference straddling the vertex without containing its cell, a shape no tree cell can take, and 180 with no tree reference at all; the doubling map's 1,006 all by a reference endpoint sitting exactly at the crossing vertex, the map aligning cylinder endpoints onto the vertex lattice. Neither shape occurs under the identity map on the away side, by an endpoint identity, and the near side never needs excluding, which is the ladder-entry lemma.

Scope. A theorem under the identity map at the whole chain-preferring slice, by the ladder-entry lemma below; the failure catalogue off it exact at the digit scans, up to {1, 2, 7, 25}8 and {1, 2}10.

verifiers: explore_seed_exclusion.py, explore_chain_persistence.py

The ladder-entry lemma theorem

Call the straddle chain at a vertex its ladder, the chain's cells its rungs, and a door the step at which a run leaves a ladder for a cell of that vertex's own family — from the tree cell into its near child, or out of a rung into the cell the rung sits in. Under the identity map a reference is a cylinder, and its index is the number of digits it pins, so the finer reference carries the larger index. Every cell that can cross a straddle holds that straddle's near ladder point interior, and every such cell is reached by a door. Three facts make that door unopenable in time. The vertex fact: a run's cell contains the value, so an occupied ladder's vertex is always a convergent — one of the best rational approximations the expansion produces — and the ladder runs exactly as many rungs as the expansion's next digit; at a vertex that is not a convergent the descent turns away and the ladder is never entered. The containment fact: the cylinders strictly inside any door's target cell are exactly those of index past the convergent's own. The maxed-ladder fact: a chain reference two or more indices past the convergent's own has already maxed the ladder, its near end lying strictly past the top rung's point. So a door needs a tree reference of index at least one past the convergent's, which in the stale regime forces a chain reference at least one step finer, one that has already maxed the ladder: a chain-preferring run either chains rather than dooring, where a chain move is still available, or leaves from the maxed rung itself, never below the index the other run will reach. Call the run at risk of falling behind the flank. In the fresh regime the flank's tree reference sits inside the chain reference and its door opens. The two arguments meet with no gap because the regime is read at the flank's own patience — the pair's fresh boundary case is the elder's stale one — so whichever run flanks, one argument reaches it, and the crossing's shape never enters. At the digit scans under the identity map the stale regime has zero doors from a tree cell into either child of a convergent vertex against 720,498 fresh, every one of its 177,186 straddle exits at the maxed index against 259,612 fresh every one below it, and zero violations of the exit index invariant — once a run doors out of a vertex's family at index k, the ladder there never grows past k — in 2,447,520 exits, against 626,722 in 7,263,472 fresh. Under the maps the invariant fails and the failures are the crossings: the doubling violators and crossings coincide as (stream, run) sets, 998 against 998, while under squaring every one of the 2,606 crossings has a violating flank and 1,504 violators carry no crossing — necessary, not sufficient.

Scope. Proved under the identity map on the chain-preferring slice, importing the crossing catalogue and the fresh-regime lemma of explore_chain_persistence.py; the counts are the digit scans' and confirm rather than carry it.

verifiers: explore_ladder_entry.py, explore_chain_persistence.py

The door inequalities rule

The cylinders are not the hypothesis. Take the reference family — the nested sequence of intervals a run's references are drawn from — as the design variable in place of the map, the maps' images set beside chains built by hand, and the three facts come apart. The vertex fact holds at all 681,632 occupied vertices of the full cross product, not one at a vertex that is not a convergent: it is the value's own descent and asks nothing of any lattice. The maxed-ladder fact is free too, zero failures in any family. The containment fact is keyed to the determinant of the reference that opened the door — |ad − bc| at endpoints a/b and c/d — and only one way: ±1 is sufficient, 568,195 doors and zero failures, while 2 and 3 merely permit failure, 14,939 and 18,735 doors respecting it. A determinant-1 family out of phase with the digits — every Stern–Brocot node rather than the subsequence the digits pick out, one no map can produce — keeps all three facts and breaks the conclusion anyway, 7,421 stale exit-index violations, because the step "the stale regime makes the chain reference one step finer" read a step along the family as a digit. Restated with a reference's index read off the reference itself, as the piece criterion defines it, rather than counted in the family's steps, the law returns whole at every determinant-1 family, that one included, and at every determinant-2 family besides; its 7,421 violations reclassify rather than vanish, every one outside the stale regime so read. The hypothesis, both inequalities read at the door itself: at a convergent vertex of index σ the tree reference has index at least σ + 1, and the chain reference's index is strictly larger. Where both hold a chain-preferring run's chain reference has maxed the ladder — 68,958 doors across all ten families, zero short — and the two conclusions run on different mechanisms: the tree-cell door that would leave early is empty because a chain move exists there and the preference takes it, while the straddle exits are not empty at all and every one leaves at the maxed rung, the only rung left, no chain move on offer since the commit loop requires a strict improvement. Where the first inequality fails, 2,356 of 4,511 doors fall short and all 1,583 surviving violations sit there. The geometry is preference-free and the law is not: on the tree-preferring slice the maxed ladder is handed over at all 88,932 such doors and 33,319 of those exits violate, against zero under chain preference. So the inequalities force the geometry and the preference converts it into the law, and the theorem is about decisions: not a policy a reader could execute, since reading one's own reference's index needs the digits, and not a property of a family, since a determinant-3 family carries decisions of both kinds. The chain/tree axis is the same fact from the reader's side. Both preferences chain only at convergents and walk them in strictly increasing index, so the axis is stride: in the stale regime the chain-preferring run never walks consecutive convergents, 0 of 5,859 sequences, and the tree-preferring run does in 1,646 of 5,875. Handed the two inequalities as an oracle — chaining exactly where both hold, preferring the tree elsewhere — a reader takes 59,982 doors where the oracle said chain and no chain move was on offer, violates the invariant at none, and still drifts at every family at least as much as the reader that always prefers the chain: the comparison buys nothing the preference did not. The tree's determinant-1 rigidity, the digit stream and phase are three sufficient conditions for the same two inequalities, which is why each in turn looked like the hypothesis.

Scope. Exhaustive over ten reference families, sixteen runs and both preference slices at the digit scans; the geometry at both slices, the law at the chain-preferring one. A rule at that scope and not a theorem over all families: what is proved is the identity-map case above. Toy scale.

verifiers: explore_reference_families.py, explore_five_prints.py

The piece criterion rule

The determinant was a proxy for a decomposition. Every reference is a finite union of maximal tree cells, one of which holds the value, and the pieces tile it, so its index — a tree cell's index being the length of the digit run that defines it — is the minimum over them, and the first door inequality holds exactly when the pieces the value does not occupy reach σ + 1. The occupied piece never attains the minimum, by a lemma: the cover's containment is strict and every door enters a child carrying the vertex as an endpoint, so the reference excludes the vertex, while the nodes on the value's path between one convergent and the next are exactly the semiconvergent intervals — the intermediate fractions the expansion passes through on its way to a convergent — every one with the vertex as an endpoint; so a determinant-1 interval on the value's path holding no vertex is no coarser than the next convergent's cell. That is the only step a run could have refuted, 738,910 doors and zero short, and the per-determinant cross-tabulation is a control on the decomposition, not evidence. Determinant ±1 is sufficient for the empty reason that such a reference is its own occupied piece. Determinant 2 is two cells glued at the half-mediant, the mediant with numerator and denominator each halved — at determinant 1 from both endpoints, and the interior fraction of least denominator — and it fails exactly when that second cell is too shallow, 73,354 of 88,293 doors. The criterion cuts across the determinant rather than refining it, determinant 3 being two pieces at 49,245 doors and three at 34,867, which is what makes it a restatement and not a relabelling. A finite digit stream names a cylinder and not a real, so which piece holds the value is read through the family's tail window and is undefined where a seam runs through it: 1,690 further doors, all at determinant 3, counted and excluded rather than assigned a side. No input to the door inequalities is keyed to a lattice fact any longer. Open, one named piece: the pre-window confinement of the 3,627 residue witnesses of the chain-preferring theorem, measured and not derived.

Scope. A rule at the geometry — ten families, both slices, 738,910 doors — and a theorem at its occupied half, which is the lemma. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_g2_separator.py

What the continued fraction already owned

The Stern–Brocot path to a value runs through the semiconvergents and turns exactly at the convergents, its runs as long as the digits, and every cell's endpoints are a determinant-1 pair — so a determinant-1 reference is an interval of the tree itself, which is half of the first door inequality's sufficient half; the vertex fact is the tree's as well, a biconditional about the value's own descent, which is why it survives every family; and a ladder's length is the classical complete-quotient identity |z − pσ/qσ| = 1/(qσ(ασ+1qσ + qσ−1)) rearranged. The cover — the straddle chains as a commitment family over the tree, the grading that makes rank the digit clock, the one-cell margin, the wall cure — is a design on that spine, and the piece decomposition and the half-mediant floor are derived in the verifier and controlled by brute force. What a preference does with the geometry it is handed is none of the continued fraction's: the same maxed ladder reaches both slices, and which convergents a slice skips is the policy's alone.
