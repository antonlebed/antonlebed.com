Every term

Every term the site's pages define in bold, in alphabetical order, each linked to the sentence that defines it and followed by the page that sentence stands on, with its section when it stands under one rather than above the page's first section. In running text a word in bold is at its definition: the site uses bold for nothing else there. A word that names different things on two pages is listed once for each, beside the page that defines it in that sense. A sense holds on its own page, on the pages of its section and on any page that points at its page; elsewhere the word means what it means in mathematics or in English. Where several senses are in reach, a page means its own; else, among the pages its opening paragraph points at, that of the first to bind the word; else the one bound near it (in its section, on the section's own page or on a page it lists) when only one is; and where several are and none is picked, the word means none of them. Where the opening paragraph of a page that states claims uses a term defined elsewhere, its first use there links to the sense that page means.
