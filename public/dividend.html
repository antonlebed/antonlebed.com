The static dividend

What the deletions pay back with no run at all: the exact perimeter of what the windows can say, the door out of it, and the algebra — reversible, canonical, decidable — that stands on both faces.

The tower at rung k is the ring Z/pk#, the integers modulo the product of the first k primes, read through k windows: an integer n is seen as its residue tuple (n mod p1, …, n mod pk), one residue field Fp per window, with the size of n — the archimedean place — deleted (The Object). The depth of a prime in n, its exponent, is read only as presence, each window being a residue field and not a local ring. A native predicate is any property of n computable from that tuple. The machine that grows the tower is on the Computation landing; everything here is static, true of the ring with no move made. Every decidability entry names the result that owns it.

What the windows can say

The expressiveness perimeter rule

Every native predicate — unit (sieve survivor), quadratic residue, idempotent support, error-correcting syndrome, the seed-flower's −χ, transparency — is a function of the residue tuple and nothing else, so the whole native repertoire is one class: unions of residue classes modulo a squarefree modulus, the purely periodic subsets of Z with squarefree period (the units of Z/30 are 8 classes at period 30; the k = 7 error-correcting code's valid set has period 510510). Above it sits a ladder of deletions. ⟨N, +, ×⟩ is undecidable (Gödel, Robinson); delete × for Presburger arithmetic, whose definable sets are the semilinear ones (Ginsburg–Spanier 1966), periods plus thresholds; delete the thresholds for the purely periodic sets; delete prime-power depth, residue fields Fp in place of Z/pj, for the tower's class. Both escapes are the tower's own deletions: the threshold {n ≥ t} has no period at all (witness n = t − 1), and {n ≡ 1 mod 4} splits the atom {1, 7} of ⟨mod 2, mod 3⟩, no residue-field window reading it. So "finite-window family = the Presburger-decidable fragment" is false as stated; the containment holds one way and is strict twice over, which is the stronger reading: the tower is more decidable than Presburger, and the two things it drops are its two deleted places — the archimedean axis and the depth axis, the latter re-imported at two windows being the ruler ladder's door.

Scope. Proved, with the periodicity facts and the two escaping witnesses run-verified; Presburger decidability, semilinearity and the undecidability of full arithmetic are cited. The class is the static one: the growth dynamics lift it to a decidable well-structured system and the archimedean borrow — a move that lowers a counter while a test reads the value it descends through, the one move the construction deletes — is the ceiling above that (the knife edge).

Native law against paid simulation: one layer of mod gates observation

A tower predicate is k parallel residue reads and a Boolean lookup, quantifier-free — one layer of MODp gates, the primitive of the circuit class ACC⁰ — where Presburger recovers n ≡ r (mod p) as ∃y. n = r + p·y. The structural contrast is verified; a cost edge over a general engine is not, because on the shared fragment the general engine already collapses to the same lookup: Cooper's quantifier elimination on an order-free conjunction bottoms out in a brute-force pass over residues, congruence-only systems convert to semilinear sets by the Chinese remainder theorem directly (Haase 2018), and in hardware the one-gate layer is the residue number system's standing small-modulus table practice. The dynamic lift is a three-tier ruler: static regular; growth dynamics a decidable well-structured system, with vector-addition reachability decidable but Ackermann-complete (Leroux–Schmitz 2019; Czerwiński–Orlikowski and Leroux 2021) and its reachability sets not always semilinear (Hopcroft–Pansiot 1979); the archimedean borrow the ceiling to undecidable.

Scope. The gate-layer form is run-verified; every complexity entry is cited. Observation tier because the comparison is structural and no cost is measured.

The second door

The perimeter places the tower inside Presburger, where everything is decidable. The question from the other side: starting at ⟨N, +⟩, what has to be added before decidability is lost again — which door, a read the construction deleted and re-imported as a primitive, opens? Call a set k-automatic if a finite-state machine reading base-k digits decides membership, and write Vk for the base-k depth ladder, the map sending n to the largest power of k dividing it.

The ruler ladder: undecidability over ⟨N, +⟩ is quantized rule

The rungs. R0, the periodic floor, what every base agrees on: decidable (Presburger 1929, Cobham 1969). R1a, one scale's tick marks — kN, the factorials, any sparse set: decidable (Semenov 1979/83). R1b, one scale's full ladder Vk, all k-automatic sets: decidable (Büchi 1960), with nothing between R1a and R1b in one base — a non-periodic k-automatic set either defines every k-automatic set or was already definable from the tick marks (Bell–Block Gorman–Schulz 2025). R2a, two independent scales' tick marks, ⟨N, +, 2N, 3N⟩: undecidable (Hieronymi–Schulz 2022) yet multiplication not definable (Schulz 2022), a no-man's-land between Presburger and Peano. R2b, two independent full ladders: multiplication returns (Villemaire 1992, Bès 1997). The non-scale doors all give full multiplication: one ladder with its own conversion x ↦ kx (Cherlin–Point 1986); mid-scale reads — the squares (Putnam 1957), any polynomial of degree ≥ 2 (Büchi); reads across unboundedly many primes — coprimality with + (Woods 1981), divisibility with successor (J. Robinson 1949), coprimality with successor alone undecidable with × open, tied to the Erdős–Woods conjecture.

Scope. Every decidable or undecidable entry is a cited theorem by others; what is run is the door mechanisms in the next block. Rule over its own measurements riding a cited chart.

Every door is a magnitude ruler getting read, and the mechanism is precision rule

Any non-Presburger predicate mints a ruler, an expanding set — one whose gaps grow without bound — definable from it (Michaux–Villemaire 1996). One ruler never opens the door: Semenov's compatibility criterion is exactly "no readable mid-scale ruler" (the squares fail because their gap probe 2x + 1 — the difference between consecutive members, definable from the set — is one), and Hansel's lemma is the matching impossibility, a set recognizable in two independent scales not being expanding. The door opens when two independent rulers are compared. Two bases are multiplicatively independent when neither is a rational power of the other, and then their joint ladder {2a3b} densifies without bound: the largest ratio between neighbouring members inside a decade falls from 1.50 to 1.039 by 1018, with the record-setting gaps landing exactly at the continued-fraction convergents of log2 3, while a dependent pair's ladder stays locked at ratio 2 forever. Multiplicative independence is unbounded measurement precision. The re-import has two grades, overhearing (R2a, enough coupling to interpret undecidable questions) strictly weaker than rebuilding multiplication (R2b): undecidability is not multiplication.

Scope. The densification is a rule at bases up to 2000 and the gap probes and the compatibility criterion are run-verified; the reading of every door as a ruler is a synthesis over the cited theorems and carries no tier of its own. The base-independence contact, Cobham–Semenov, is also what makes the readability chart per-base.

The carry tie: Pascal mod n is a carry detector, and the first modulus with two windows is the tower's second rung rule

Kummer's theorem — already at work in the binomial parities — says the exponent of p in the binomial coefficient C(x + y, x) counts the carries when x and y are added in base p, so the Pascal triangle mod n, taken as a bare relation Bn, is a carry detector at every prime window p | n. Ask what ⟨N, =, Bn⟩ can say, addition not assumed. One prime window, n = pj: decidable (Korec 1995, Bès 1997). Two windows: + and × are both definable from Bn alone (Korec 1993), and the first such n is 6 = 2·3, the first primorial that is not a prime power. The tower's carry-free componentwise addition is the deletion of exactly what B6 reads.

Scope. Kummer's theorem is classical and brute-verified here at p = 2, 3 for x, y ≤ 243; the definability results are cited. That the first two-window modulus is the second rung is an observation about which number it is, not a claim that the coincidence does work.

The tower's corner: all three truncations are load-bearing rule

The construction truncates three ways and each truncation closes a door. Depth ≤ 1: the residue tuple sees presence and never the rung — the same tuple mod 210 carries different V2 for exactly the even n. Breadth, the number of windows read, ≤ k: coprimality to a fixed support is pk#-periodic, rung R0, and the breadth door's size leak min{n > 1 : n ⊥ pk#} = pk+1 is the archimedean certificate of the sieve identity: unbounded breadth reads magnitude. Archimedean deleted: the machine's borrow and the mid-scale reads live at the same place. The necessity direction — that every undecidable ⟨N, +, X⟩ defines two independent expanding scales — holds inside the charted classes (Semenov for sparse sets, Hieronymi–Schulz with the dichotomy for the automatic class) and stands open in general, the counterexample shape named and no specimen known.

Scope. Verified at k = 4 for the depth and breadth truncations, the size leak checked for k ≤ 12; the charted-class necessity is cited and the general case is open.

The physics and the payoff

The erasure ledger: the native arithmetic is a reversible core rule

Under a uniform input the Landauer floor of a map is its entropy drop, zero exactly for a bijection. CRT encode and decode, add-a-constant and multiply-by-a-unit are permutations of Z/N factoring channelwise into per-field bijections: zero erasure, carry-free. Erasure enters at the many-to-one maps and for the additive homomorphisms its price is exact: multiply-by-m erases log2 gcd(m, N), the projection onto a set S of windows erases Σi∉S log2 pi. The window read is the erasing atom and the sieve's information loss rides the projections while the arithmetic loses nothing; the power map x ↦ xe is bijective on squarefree Z/N exactly when gcd(e, λ) = 1 and erases channel-locally otherwise. The ledger is symmetric across both deleted places: in Z/pk the fattening generator ×p is nilpotent and erases exactly log2 p while ×unit stays a bijection, so each place carries a reversible pole and a dissipative middle.

Scope. Entropy drops, kernel sizes and the bijection facts verified on Z/30 and Z/210. Landauer's principle is cited; thermodynamic dissipation is not measured, only the logical invertibility its floor is proportional to.

The witness gap is not Landauer erasure: two knobs at one seat rule

Two distinct non-injectivities. Landauer's is the one-step state map being many-to-one; the witness gap of the growth machine is the history-to-endpoint map being many-to-one. The growth machine has per-step erasure zero — every move is a translation of the vector of exponents — yet route entropy log2(k!) > 0: the route is never erased, only never encoded, an observer's inference deficit and not a thermodynamic cost, since the alternative histories are never instantiated. The two axes anti-align: reset, clearing a counter unconditionally, is maximally erasing yet leaves the machine finite-state, and the guarded decrement is a bijection with zero erasure yet the borrow that unlocks universality beside a branch on zero. Reversible universal machines exist (Bennett 1973) and erasing decidable ones do, so the deleted place hosts two independent knobs — the carry, forward, whose absence is reversible arithmetic, and the borrow, backward, whose absence is decidability for the machine whose moves only multiply — and the tower deletes the whole place and reaps both.

Scope. Per-step injectivity, route counts and the erasure ordering at the dial are run-verified; Bennett and the Minsky and reset facts are cited.

The decidable-equivalence dividend: three canonical forms by construction rule

A Turing system's artifact is the universal self-interpreter, paid for with undecidable program equivalence (Rice); a total language cannot contain its own interpreter (Turner 2004), and its artifact is the reverse, canonical forms and decidable equivalence. The tower owns three. The element form is the CRT tuple, unique and reversible, equality componentwise in O(k). The expression form is the concrete sibling of Rice: two polynomial expressions over Z/N are equal as functions exactly when per channel they agree in Fp[x]/(xp − x), so the canonical form is the tuple of per-field reduced polynomials with the exponent rule e ≥ 1 ↦ ((e − 1) mod (p − 1)) + 1, and x5 = x as a function on Z/30 though the polynomials differ. The predicate form is (least period, accepting residue set), the Myhill–Nerode minimal automaton of the periodic language, three spellings of unit(Z/30) collapsing to (30, 8 residues). All three deciders are total, so the native languages sit on the decidable-equivalence side and cannot host their own interpreters — and the forms are algebra, not runtime, standing on the universal element face too, since Rice constrains questions about what a program computes and never a ring's normal forms. Self-certification is the same dividend at the value level: a result carries its error-correcting syndrome, valid exactly when the syndrome is zero, checked in O(parity) with no trust in the producer.

Scope. The three forms, the x5 = x witness, the three-way predicate collision with inequivalent controls, the deciders' totality and a corrupted-channel correction verified on Z/30; Rice, Turner and Myhill–Nerode cited. Canonical forms and decidable equivalence are a commodity — minimal automata, Datalog, total proof assistants — and the CRT tuple is the residue number system's own defining representation, so where the tuple wins, add- and multiply-dominated work such as digital filters and double-CRT homomorphic encryption, the incumbent already runs on it; what residue codes add beyond it is compute-through, the syndrome surviving the arithmetic, exploited at memory-ECC scale.
