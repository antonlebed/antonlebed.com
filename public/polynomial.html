Polynomial maps

The delay of any smooth map of digit streams read through a signed digit set, the polynomial maps and the maps with a pole included, and the piecewise maps beside them.

A stream is a numeral in radix b over a digit set D = {−a−..a+}, contiguous — every integer between its ends a digit — unless a clause says otherwise, read from its most significant digit: the first t digits name a cell of values, an interval, and the cells refine toward a point as t grows. A map of streams is read at lookahead c when its output's first t digits are a function of the inputs' first t + c; the least such c is the map's delay, and a map with a finite delay is local at this end. The set's slack ρ = a− + a+ + 1 − b counts the digits it holds beyond the radix; at slack 1 or more a value has several writings and the cells overlap, which is what lets a product or a sum be local at all. Its reach is W = a− + a+, the width of what the unseen digits can still contribute. An operation injects a spread, the width of what its unseen digits can still move the output by; with c digits of lookahead an overlapping cover swallows whole any spread up to bcρ, and the Lebesgue margin is the least c at which that reaches the spread injected, a size-only count. The laws below read a map's exact delay against that margin; the slope and addition laws over the same sets, and the leading end's own vocabulary, are on Redundant arithmetic. A census below counts digit systems, one per (radix, digit set), written radix b over {−a−..a+}, and a table's system is one of them.

Maps of streams

The many-stream theorem theorem

Let f be a C² map of d streams over one contiguous set, the window [−M−, M+]d, M± = a±/(b − 1), the output aligned with the inputs and led by o positions when its range needs it (a sum's range doubles the window, so a map with an additive part runs led by one; the lead is the least o in Z whose root cell holds the range, negative where the range is narrower than the window). A polynomial's derivative is bounded on the window; a map may also carry a pole, a point where the derivative is unbounded, read as f(s + x) with s = M− + P and the pole distance P > 0 the argument's lower end over the window. Let Λ, the box width rate, be the sup over the window of the gradient's L¹ norm Σ|∂if|: a box of side ε maps into an interval at most εΛ wide, by the mean value theorem along the segment. With L the lookahead plus the lead, the margin law — the Lebesgue margin with the injected spread read as ΛW —

bLρ  ≥  Λ (a−+a+)b^{L}\rho \;\ge\; \Lambda\,(a^{-}+a^{+})

suffices for every C² map and, unless f is affine on the window, is the floor: the reader is dead at every L below, whatever it emitted before, so the delay is exactly the law's L*, whatever d, and nothing is asked of where the gradient peaks, of monotonicity on the window or of the curvature.

The reader has no strategy. In units of b−(t+1) at the emitted level, with ω = M− + M+ the reach at that scale, the output cells are [p − M−, p + M+] and the image of the input boxes is an interval inside the parent cell; a digit is legal iff its cell holds the image, so the reader is dead iff the image strictly contains an overlap zone [m + 1 − M−, m + M+] of two consecutive cells — a condition on the streams alone. The adversary fixes the streams in advance and any legal digit is as good as any other, so the greedy reader, any legal digit and no backtracking, survives wherever any reader does, and the adversary's certificate is the least output position at which some prefix box's image strictly contains a zone, found by scanning the excess region, the boxes whose width rate exceeds the law's threshold: the exhaustive game tree confined to that region loses no kill, a box outside it never straddling and a descendant's box lying inside its ancestor's, and matches the scan at every pair its depth reaches. Sufficiency: every prefix box lies in [−M−bn, M+bn]d, so its image is at most Λωb−L long, and the law says that is at most the zone's length ω − 1; the law covers a negative lookahead as written, the zone at an output position before the first input digit being no narrower than the zone at L.

Necessity is a count over a region, not a descent in depth. Death is a property of the image alone: a box whose image has lower end y and width w strictly contains a zone iff its excess g′ = w − (ω − 1) is positive and its phase {y + M−} lies in the dead arc (1 − g′, 1). Below L* the law's own excess g = Λω/bL − (ω − 1) is positive; let Λh be the midpoint of Λ and the law's threshold and C the component of {Σ|∂if| > Λh} holding the peak. The dichotomy. If the Hessian vanishes on C then the gradient is constant there, a boundary point of C interior to the window would read Λh, so C is the window and f is affine. Otherwise the Hessian is nonzero at a point of C, and a ball B round it, shrunk once per partial, has every partial one-signed or identically zero, an integer direction e with eTHe of one sign and |eTHe| ≥ κ > 0, and Σ infB |∂if| > Λh: every box in B has its image's ends at the two opposite corners the signs name and excess at least g/2, and along the lattice line u + ie of prefixes the lower end's phase z(i) = bn−L f(xu+ie) has second derivative eTHe/bn+L: van der Corput's inequality in its explicit form (Kuipers and Niederreiter, Uniform Distribution of Sequences, 1974, Theorem 2.7) bounds every harmonic of the phases by (hΔ + 2)(4 b(n+L)/2/√(hκ) + 3), Δ the range of ∇f·e over B divided by bL, and Erdős–Turán (Theorem 2.5) turns the harmonics into a bound on the discrepancy in the count N alone once bn is read as under (N + 1 + (ω + 1)/|e|∞)/X, X the line's chord through B. So there is an N₀, a function of (b, L, ω, g, X, κ, Δ) and never of the depth, past which some phase sits in the arc and the reader is dead at output depth n − c. For one stream the line is the prefix order itself and the ball a curved subinterval with |f″| ≥ κ; every interior peak of |f′| is a zero of f″, so an inflection at the peak is the curved case, not an exclusion. The proof's constant against its check: N₀ runs from 4.4 · 10⁴ to 8.0 · 10¹² over the certified pairs, while the line at the depth before a many-stream kill holds at most 15 boxes and the curved subinterval before a one-stream kill never more than 35 prefixes, so the proof's depth is never approached and the kills the scan finds are the curvature's own; the two inequalities, read at 595 depths of 10⁴ to 5 · 10⁴ phases, hold with the harmonics at most 0.12 of van der Corput's bound and the discrepancy at most 0.019.

The affine remainder splits by slope. A sum of k streams has its image endpoints on the lattice (s − kM−)/bL, s an integer, so it is dead at L iff an integer lies in

((bL−k) M+,    bL−(bL−k) M−),\bigl((b^{L}-k)\,M^{+},\;\; b^{L}-(b^{L}-k)\,M^{-}\bigr),

the lookahead criterion at k = 2, the identity's survival at lookahead 0 at k = 1 (the interval is (0, 1)), and the three-sum's floor at k = 3. The interval's length is the margin's excess kω − bL(ω − 1), so the margin is exact off the wedge — the systems where that length is positive and the interval holds no integer — and one digit high on it: the two-sum's wedge is addition's, the identity's is every system, the three-sum's is empty through radix 4 and first appears at radix 5 over {−4..4}. A sum of streams is a lattice map with a wedge, never a map that reads below the margin outright; a non-affine map's reachable endpoints fill the dead arc at the emitted scale, so its wedge is empty. One stream at a rational slope reads at the rational-slope delay criterion at the symmetric sets it covers and at the lattice clause at slope 1, and an irrational slope (x/√2) is dead below L* at all 20 systems of radices 2..5 with no lattice clause and no region theorem, an observation.

Off the contiguous sets the law outlives its proof. A digit set with a gap has tails filling an interval iff every gap is at most its hull width w = (dmax − dmin)/(b − 1), else a Cantor set: 30 of the 62 sets of at most five digits within [−3, 3] at radices 3 and 4 with two gap sizes. Its cells are the hull's translates by the representable prefixes and a parent's children overlap in zones of length w − g per gap g, so sufficiency survives with the least zone zmin = w − gmax in the zone's place, bL zmin ≥ Λw; the cell lemma does not: death is a property of the image iff every zone of one legal parent inside the parents' overlap contains a zone of the other, which fails at 23 of the 30 sets, where the reader has a strategy. The delay is that law's L* nonetheless: xy and the divider dead at L* − 1 and alive at L* at all 28 pairs of the 14 sets with zmin > 0, x² + y at all 14, and at the 16 touching sets, where the law admits no L, every pair dead at every L ≤ 3. In base β over {−a..a}, β a Pisot unit — the golden mean, the Narayana root, the silver mean, the tribonacci root, the plastic number — a prefix is an element of Z[β], the cells form no lattice and two legal parents' zones misalign aperiodically; the tails fill an interval iff β − 1 ≤ 2a, M = a/(β − 1), and the law in the base,

gives the delay of xy, x² + y and the divider at P = 1 at 27 of the 30 pairs of the five bases at a = 1 and 2, each dead at L* − 1 and alive at L* by the sufficiency, three pairs unreached at the tree's wall of six rounds. The kill round grows as the excess shrinks — 1 to 9 at the four wider bases and 5 to 12 at the plastic number, against 1 to 3 at the 60 contiguous pairs of radices 2..5 at the same maps and pole distance — and it is the adversary's search, not the reader's play: at a box whose image is wider than the zone the room is under one digit, so the legal digit is unique and the move is forced wherever a kill is possible, in base β as at an integer radix; a choice exists only at narrower images, and a reader with it removed dies at the same round at 23 of 24 decided pairs, one round earlier at one. The round falls as the excess's share of the zone grows, a share the base's grain caps at β − 1, 0.32 at the plastic number. The kill never escapes where the tree reaches, and one L lower every pair the wall left alive dies within six rounds. The confined tree above carries over, a box at or under the law's threshold having an image no wider than any zone, as has every descendant, so it loses no kill and no survival, and a round-12 kill costs twelve thousand nodes where the full tree survives two rounds at hundreds of thousands. Necessity off the contiguous sets is open: the proof's first step, any legal digit as good as any other, is false there, and the lattice line has no lattice to run on.

The piecewise theorem theorem

Let f be continuous on the window and C² on each of finitely many pieces, cut at kinks κ that are rational, an affine piece carrying a rational slope s = p/q and offset d. The reader has no strategy, so death is a property of the boxes alone: the delay is the least L at which no prefix box's image strictly contains an overlap zone, and a box either lies inside one piece or straddles a kink. With Λlaw = (ω − 1)bL/ω the law's threshold at L, the width rate at which the margin law holds with equality, three finite clauses decide. The curved clause: a non-affine piece kills iff the sup of |f′| over it exceeds Λlaw, the width bound one way and the many-stream argument with the piece as the window the other, since f′ is continuous and a piece affine on its excess region is affine outright. The piece clause: a box inside an affine piece has the phase of its image's lower end on a lattice of step gcd(p, qbL)/qbL with an offset d·bn−L mod 1 that cycles in the depth n, and kills iff that phase lies in the piece's own dead arc (1 − g, 1), g = |s|ω/bL − (ω − 1): one lattice period decides. The kink clause: a straddler is one of at most ⌈ω⌉ per depth, its image the hull of the map over it, and the containment lemma confines it to the shallow depths. At a depth where every affine piece holds a full lattice period, a killing straddler has a hull end on the lattice of an affine side above the threshold and its image inside a same-phase box of that piece, so that piece's clause already fires; a curved side above the threshold is the curved clause, and at an extremum kink, one end of the hull being f(κ) itself, a side below the threshold never attains the far end of a killing hull. So the map reads at the least L at which every clause is empty: at most the margin's L*, and below it exactly when every clause at L* − 1 is empty: a sup, a cycle and a shallow scan. A discontinuous map is dead at every L.

The specimens, one stream, the identity the right piece of each. x/2 on x ≤ 0 and x on x ≥ 0 has Λ = 1 and L* = 1 at every system and reads at 0 at 16 of 20, the slope-1/2 piece alone killing at the four systems whose digits both sit below b − 1 (radix 4 over {−2..2}, radix 5 over {−2..3}, {−3..2} and {−3..3}). Beside the identity a curved piece reads by its sup alone: 3x/4 + x²/4 on x ≤ 0 is dead at 0 and x/5 + x²/20 alive at 0 at every system, the second one below the margin. A C² map affine on its whole peak set, x − (x + M−/2)³ below −M−/2 and x above, is dead at 0 at every system by its curved remainder, the kill at input depth 1 to 3. The kink fires alone only at shallow depth: a valley kills with both pieces alive at all ten systems of radices 2..4, and a kink between slopes of one sign (1/2 beside 3/2, 2 or 2/3) at 8 of 20, each firing at input depth 0 or 1 and certified at a straddling prefix; at radix 5 neither search found a design, the valley's grid exhausted and the same-sign search budgeted. In two streams a piece reads at the many-stream theorem verbatim: |x|·y and max(x, y)·z, a product inside each piece, are dead at L* − 1 and alive at L* at 20 of 20 each.

The segment lemma. An affine piece of slope p/q with excess g > 0 at L is empty iff its lattice step δ = gcd(p, qbL)/qbL is at least g and its offset sits in a closed window of length δ − g, since an open arc of length g holds a point of every finer lattice whatever the offset; so a piece reads below the margin only through a simple slope. At L = 0, with W = a− + a+, every slope of magnitude at most ρ/W is free, above it exactly the p/q with 1 ≤ pW − qρ ≤ b − 1 may carry a piece, and none in the gap between 2/(2 + ⌈(b − 1)/ρ⌉) and 1 can. A table-and-interpolate unit, a curve sampled at breakpoints and joined by chords, sits at the margin unless its steepest chord has such a slope: the reciprocal 1/(s + x) joined by four uniform chords reads at 0 against L* = 1 at the four symmetric top systems of radices 2..5, where δ = g = 1/3 exactly, and its eight-chord refinement, its breakpoints on the cell lattice or off it, reads at L* at every system, 56 of the 60 designs at the margin; where a breakpoint sits changes no chord's slope, and decides a coarse-lattice piece at one depth only, the first at which the piece holds a box, the shallow kink clause on top. The robust floor. A map within ε of a curve, in the sup norm, inherits every curve kill whose two overhangs, the image's reach past the zone at either end, exceed ε, so no map within ε reads below the margin once ε is under εrob, the largest of the smaller overhangs over the curve's killing boxes at L* − 1, attained at a bounded depth. For the reciprocal it is attained at depth at most 3 and runs from 1/150 at radix 5 over {−4..4} to 1/12 at radix 4 over {−2..2}: from b−1.8 to b−4.1 at radices 3..5 and b−6.2 at radix 2 over {−1..1}, above b−4 at 17 of 20 systems, and at L* − 2 at least b−2 at 19. Above the floor the slopes that may carry a piece build the unit as a zigzag, slope 1 alternating with 1/2, 1/3 or 1/4 through the gap and the free slopes taking the shallow end, certified within 5 percent of the floor at 8 systems and within 50 percent at 11. A table's delay is bounded by its curve's floor, and a table budget buys at most the stretch between the margin and the floor.

Scope. The clause table against the game at every L read: 20 + 20 + 10 systems of radices 2..5 for the affine specimens and the valley designs, 20 + 20 + 20 + 8 for the curved specimens and the same-sign designs, no straddler killing at a depth past the lattices' period without an adjacent piece's clause firing there, and 0 disagreements; the two-stream pieces at 20 systems each.
