Polynomial maps

The delay of a polynomial map of digit streams read through a signed digit set, what a chain of two readers pays against one, and what a comparator costs inside a unit.

A stream is a numeral in radix b over a digit set D = {−a−..a+}, read from its most significant digit: the first t digits name a cell of values, an interval, and the cells refine toward a point as t grows. A map of streams is read at lookahead c when its output's first t digits are a function of the inputs' first t + c; the least such c is the map's delay, and a map with a finite delay is local at this end. The set's slack ρ = a− + a+ + 1 − b counts the digits it holds beyond the radix; at slack 1 or more a value has several writings and the cells overlap, which is what lets a product or a sum be local at all. Its reach is W = a− + a+, the width of what the unseen digits can still contribute. An operation injects a spread, the width of what its unseen digits can still move the output by; with c digits of lookahead an overlapping cover swallows whole any spread up to bcρ, and the Lebesgue margin is the least c at which that reaches the spread injected, a size-only count. The laws below read a map's exact delay against that margin; the slope and addition laws over the same sets, and the leading end's own vocabulary, are on Redundant arithmetic. A census below counts digit systems, one per (radix, digit set), written radix b over {−a−..a+}, and a table's system is one of them.

Polynomial maps

The polynomial delay criterion

Let f be a polynomial in streams over a contiguous set {−a−..a+} at radix b whose terms have disjoint variable sets, the output aligned with the inputs and led by o positions when its range needs it (a sum's range doubles the window, so a map with an additive part runs led by one), and let Lip₁ be the largest L¹ norm of its gradient over the window, in window units, which disjoint terms attain at the corner of largest magnitude: dM̂d − 1 for a monomial of degree d, 2M̂ + 1 for the multiply–add xy + z and for x² + y, k for a sum of k streams. With L the lookahead plus the lead, the margin law — the Lebesgue margin with the injected spread read as Lip₁W —

bLρ  ≥  Lip1 (a−+a+)b^{L}\rho \;\ge\; \mathrm{Lip}_{1}\,(a^{-}+a^{+})

suffices for every continuous map and is the floor wherever a term has degree 2 or more, the Lipschitz constant its only parameter: the square shares the product's delay, the three degree-3 shapes share one, the multiply–add and x² + y share theirs, one lookahead above the product's at radix 2 over {−1..1} (3 against 2). An affine map's floor sits on a lattice instead. In units of b−(t+1) at the emitted level, with M± = a±/(b − 1) and ω = M− + M+ the reach at that scale, a sum of k streams has its image endpoints on the lattice (s − kM−)/bL, s an integer, so it is dead at L iff an integer lies in

((bL−k) M+,    bL−(bL−k) M−),\bigl((b^{L}-k)\,M^{+},\;\; b^{L}-(b^{L}-k)\,M^{-}\bigr),

the lookahead criterion at k = 2, the identity's survival at lookahead 0 at k = 1 (the interval is (0, 1)), and the three-sum's floor at k = 3. The interval's length is the margin's excess kω − bL(ω − 1), so the margin is exact off the wedge — the systems where that length is positive and the interval holds no integer — and one digit high on it: the two-sum's wedge is addition's, the identity's is every system, the three-sum's is empty through radix 4 and first appears at radix 5 over {−4..4}. A sum of streams is a lattice map with a wedge, never a map that reads below the margin outright; a non-affine map's reachable endpoints are dense at the emitted scale (the stream correction on Reading), so its wedge is empty. The reader has no strategy. The output cells are [p − M−, p + M+] and the image of the input boxes is an interval inside the parent cell; a digit is legal iff its cell holds the image, so the reader is dead iff the image strictly contains an overlap zone [m + 1 − M−, m + M+] of two consecutive cells — a condition on the streams alone. The adversary fixes the streams in advance and any legal digit is as good as any other, so the greedy reader, any legal digit and no backtracking, survives wherever any reader does. Sufficiency: every prefix box lies in [−M−bn, M+bn], so by the mean value theorem every image is at most Lip₁ωb−L long, and the law says that is at most the zone's length ω − 1; with disjoint terms the corner width approaches that bound from below at every prefix length, or equals it when every term is affine, so the bound is the sup. Necessity is the excess placed on a zone: when the law fails by an integer excess E ≥ 1 the corner image exceeds the zone by a gap proportional to E/bL over a range of top prefixes, and the map's reachable endpoints sweep it. For the product, hold the streams' sum at 2U − 2I, U the prefix of n largest digits, and slide the split (U − I + i, U − I − i): the image keeps its length, its lower end falls by (2i − 1)/K a step with K = bn+c, and at I = ⌈√K⌉ the dead arcs of consecutive splits overlap and sweep a full unit. The square has no split: along uj = u0 − jbh with 2h ≥ n + c the phase of (u − M−)²/K is linear in j, its step 2r/((b − 1)bn+c−h) set by u0's low digits alone, and a large enough r makes the arcs sweep a unit once the step is under half the gap. The multiply–add is the first map where a lattice part and a dense part meet — z's endpoint on a lattice of step b−L, xy's sliding along the split line — and the union sweeps. On the diagonal the product row's right side is 4a² and its inequality is the delay condition of the on-line multiplier, the field's most-significant-first multiplier (Trivedi and Ercegovac, 1977, as restated in Frougny, Pavelka, Pelantová and Svobodová, DMTCS 2019, Corollary 2.2, an upper bound with optimality left open), read here as the floor for every reader and carried off the diagonal, up the degrees and into the multiply–add; that multiplier's rounding select is empty at five of the seven asymmetric systems of radices 2..5 and overpays at radix 5 over {−3..4}, reading 2 against the floor 1. The product's law is addition's bcρ ≥ 2W times M̂, the spread a bilinear map injects being the sum's scaled by the largest operand, and the digit is the second stream's: at radix 2 over {−1, 0, 1} the worst constant slope of magnitude at most 1 reads at 1 while two streams need 2, and at every system the worst slope reads one below the product's floor unless the floor is already 1. Two digits suffice at degree 2 (b² ≥ 4(b − 1)), and the degree-3 census reads 2 with 3 at radix 2 over {−1..1} alone. Chained units

The chained delay criterion

A chain is a product reader that sees x and y and emits a stream P = xy over the same digit set at lookahead L₁, feeding a sum reader that sees P and z, aligned with P, and emits xy + z at lookahead L₂; it reads the inputs at L₁ + L₂, and the fused unit is the multiply–add above, one reader seeing all three streams. The product reader picks its digit by any rule wherever two are legal, and the sum reader may know the rule. What it then knows of xy is the fibre of the prefix it has seen, the union of the product images of every (x, y) prefix the rule sends there, and a cell holds the fibre plus z's box iff it holds the fibre's hull, an interval inside P's own cell, inset by α and β from the cell's ends in cell units. So the sum reader is dead at L₂ iff an integer lies in the two-sum's lattice interval with its ends drawn in, ((bL₂ − 2)M+ + β, bL₂ − (bL₂ − 2)M− − α), and its escape cost is the least α + β that empties the interval. The product reader pays for insets out of its slack: every image at level n + L₁ must sit in some hull, and consecutive hulls, at unit spacing, must overlap by the image width, so α + β ≤ (ω − 1) − 2M̂ω/bL₁, its slack budget, nonnegative exactly from the product's own floor on; a reader into cells inset by (α, β) reads at the product's law with the slack ρ − (b − 1)(α + β). The escape law: the chain survives at (L₁, L₂) iff the escape cost fits the budget. At every system of radices 2..5 it fits only where the interval is already empty, so the chain's floor is L₁* + L₂*, the two units' own floors, whatever the multiplier does with its digits. The composition rule is the hardware field's own — "for a series of online operations, the overall latency is the sum of online delays" (Usman, Ercegovac and Lee, 2023) — and it is here a floor: no selection rule for the multiplier lowers the aligned sum. The fused unit sits below the chain wherever the product's margin absorbs the addition outright, its floor the product's own, and at radix 2 over {−1..1}, where it pays one for z and the adder alone pays two; the two are level where the product's floor is 1 and both pay one for z:

Comparators

The comparator delay theorem

The margin law never grants lookahead 0, and the field's table gives max and min on-line delay 0 (Ercegovac and Lang, Digital Arithmetic, 2003, Table 9.1); the reason is the lattice. Every cell of depth n, the cell of an n-digit prefix, has its lower end on (Z − M−)/bn and width ω/bn, the output cells included, and a cell of depth n meets a cell of depth n − 1 inside one of the latter's children, the depth-n cells whose lower ends run through the parent's lattice points from its own lower end up, the last ending at the parent's top (the cell lemma). So a cell-valued map, one whose image of every box of prefixes lies inside some cell of the same depth, reads at lookahead 0 at every system: the committed cell, the one the reader's emitted digits name, holds the image, the image lies in a cell, the cell meets the committed one inside a child, and that child is a legal digit. max and min of any number of streams and their compositions are cell-valued, the image of a box under max lying in the input cell with the larger top: the clamp min(max(x, y), z), the rectifier max(x, 0), whose image is a point inside the committed cell when the cell sits below 0, and the identity as the case of one stream. |x| is cell-valued iff its mirror, the cell reflected through 0, is a cell, (b − 1) | (a+ − a−), which within the radix means a symmetric set; at every asymmetric system tried the reader dies at 0 in one round, and every system survives at 1, the margin's grant. The comparator is free. A reader's death is a property of the image alone: an image inside the committed cell fails every child iff it strictly contains an overlap zone, and a cell strictly contains no zone. A fused unit G(w, max(y, z)), one reader seeing every stream, has each box image inside G's image of a box of its own inputs at the same depth, the cell of y or of z with the larger top standing for the comparator, so a fused death is a death of G; and on the diagonal y = z the adversary hands the reader G's own game, so the fused unit reads no earlier. For every map G of streams the delay of G(w, max(y, z)) equals the delay of G(w, y), min likewise; a comparator on an output, max(G(x), z), costs at most G's delay, its image lying inside G's or inside z's cell. max(x, y) + z reads at the lookahead criterion's floor for x + z and x·max(y, z) at the product's. A datapath's delay table is its arithmetic skeleton's with the comparators deleted: a digit-serial network's rectifier and pooling cost no lookahead, fused into the multiply–add or not.

Scope. Theorem for the cell-valued maps, the free comparator inside a unit and the bound on an output, at every radix and contiguous digit set with slack 1 or more, the output aligned with the inputs and led by one position for the map with an additive part; the exhaustive search is the check, 80 systems over five maps surviving at 0 uncertified (max, min and the rectifier at the 20 systems of radices 2..5, max of three and the clamp at the 10 of radices 2..4), the fused sum at the two-sum's floor at 10 of 10 systems of radices 2..4 and the fused product at the product's at the 7 representable systems, certified one lookahead below wherever a lookahead below exists (the 6 sums with floor 2, all 7 products). The |x| clause is a criterion at the 13 representable systems of radices 2..5, a− ≤ a+: the 6 symmetric survive at 0 by the theorem, the 7 asymmetric are certified dead at 0, and all 13 survive the search at 1. Whether a comparator on an output is exactly free is open.
