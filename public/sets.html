Prediction sets

A set-valued predictor promises coverage and delivers it, and the promise says nothing about the two things an auditor wants: how small the sets are, and whether any one subpopulation is covered at all. Where the smallest admissible set is a computation rather than an estimate, both are measurable, and what they show is that the rule built to keep the promise cheaply is the wrong SHAPE of rule off equal weights, by an amount its own overshoot bounds, an identity gives, and an arithmetic threshold sometimes proves zero with nothing searched.

Draw an integer x from 0 to N−1, under the uniform prior or a geometric tilt proportional to θx, with N = Mc, and show a solver only its residue modulo M. The residue is the atom; the fiber over it is the c integers sharing it; the label to be predicted is the magnitude class ⌊kx/N⌋, one of k. A cell is a finite set of weighted atoms, each carrying an exact posterior over the labels: a ring cell where this family supplies the posteriors, a bare cell where they are set by hand as rationals with no ring behind them. A rule gives each atom a set of labels; its cost is the weighted mean set size and its coverage the weighted mass of true labels it catches. The marginal constraint asks coverage of at least a bar T = 1 − α on average over the cell; the conditional one asks it at every atom. A pair is an atom r with a label y taken there, costing the atom's weight wr and covering wr·p(y|r). The operative level t* is the largest posterior value at which taking every pair at or above it still reaches the bar; the tied block is the pairs sitting exactly at it; and the certificate is the rule taking every pair strictly above the level plus the cheapest subset of the tied block that closes the coverage still owed. Its surplus D is the coverage it delivers past the bar, and D/t* its room. An optimum is a cheapest rule meeting the bar, computed exactly. A rule abandons an atom by giving it the empty set. The point-valued sibling of all this is stated uncertainty.

Two columns

The magnitude class is the one set-valued read property

The position within a fiber is independent of the residue under the uniform prior, and under the tilt the factor θr cancels, so any label that is a function of the fiber index alone has the same posterior at every atom and nothing conditional to measure. The magnitude class is a function of x, so at a fixed residue each class holds a contiguous block of fiber positions, of mass (qlo − qhi)/(1 − qc) with q = θM, and with c = ak + b the block lengths are a plus a Sturmian word of density b/k at phase r/M. So k dividing c is a flat cell, and b ≥ 1 moves the optimal set's identity with the residue while leaving its size in closed form. The tilt moves the weights and never the blocks.

Scope. Property, from the construction, at any M, c, k and θ; the block lengths checked in exact rational arithmetic on the cells below.

Coverage cannot rank two rules observation

Four cells, each scored against its exact optimum: fifteen atoms at c = 7 and k = 3, tilted at θ = 24/25 and uniform; the same atoms at k = c = 7, a flat cell; and 105 atoms at c = 11, k = 4, θ = 199/200. Split conformal, the standard construction, thresholds an estimated posterior at a level calibrated on held-out data. It holds its marginal guarantee everywhere, 0.7011 to 0.7272 against a bar of 0.70, while its exact coverage at the worst atom is 0.0000 at both live cells at every sample size, one atom receiving a set of true mass zero, and sixteen-fold more data does not move it. The same construction calibrated within each atom holds 0.7160 and 0.6530 at the two live cells, the Mondrian form, two numbers where the marginal one reports one, and pays in variance it buys back with sample, its mean size excess falling 0.52, 0.04, −0.02; its worst atom can sit under the nominal where an atom is thinly calibrated, 0.5714 at the flat cell at 2,000 draws and 0.7143 by 8,000, the guarantee being an expectation over the calibration draw. The two are indistinguishable on the guarantee each advertises and opposite on the one an auditor wants.

Scope. Observation over four designed cells at three sample sizes, 2,000, 8,000 and 32,000, forty trials each, truth exact in rational arithmetic, every coverage summed from true masses so that no held-out estimate enters the quantity the guarantee is about. The per-atom comparison is legitimate here because the observable is a residue, finitely many atoms of positive mass: the distribution-free impossibility of conditional coverage is stated for non-atomic marginals, and the conformal literature's parallel result carries the same hypothesis with its author's note that it is essential. Toy scale.

The penalty is on the form, not the practice observation

Split conformal thresholds an ESTIMATED posterior, whose ties sampling noise breaks, so it is not confined to the level sets of the true one. Its mean set size sits 1.00 label BELOW the exact threshold rule's, the form that takes the whole tied block or none of it, at the flat cell and 2.05 below at k = c, reliably smaller than the best rule of its own form, and 1.00 and 2.00 below on bare cells with no ring in them. A deployed predictor does not pay a label for a tie; what the finding says is that the theorem certifying its shape optimal does not hold on cells with ties, and that what rescues the practice is the estimation error the theorem idealizes away.

Scope. Observation, exact, on the four ring cells above and on hand-set bare cells, the same estimator code on both. Toy scale.

The tie and the certificate

What breaks the optimal form is a tie, and the ring is not load-bearing observation

Thresholding the posterior minimizes expected set size where the posterior has no ties: the constraint priced, the problem is pointwise and indifferent at the operative level, so a rule may take the tied labels there in any sub-collection, and only a rule forced to take the whole tied block or none of it can pay. Nothing in that argument mentions the law of x. A ring read through a modulus is heavy with ties, every atom of the flat cell carrying (3/7, 2/7, 2/7) so that the best threshold rule jumps from coverage 3/7 to 1, and the same estimator code on hand-set cells with no ring returns the same gaps, one label off the flat cell's masses and two off the dead cell's, at a cell with a SINGLE atom, where the conditional and marginal constraints coincide. The price of conditional coverage is non-negative at all eleven cells and set by integrality: at fifteen identical atoms the marginal constraint is an average, so 74 labels clear a bar that costs 75 per atom and the price is 1/15, while the same cell at three atoms cannot shave and prices at zero.

Scope. Observation, exact in rational arithmetic, at eleven cells, ring and bare. Toy scale.

The tie rule: multiplicity two is necessary at equal weights and sufficient nowhere rule

The tied block's multiplicity is the number of pairs it holds. At multiplicity one the certificate IS the thresholded posterior, so the two stand or fall together. At equal weights no single-pair cell pays: 0 of 21. At unequal weights 1,080 of 2,492 do, the worst by 3/10. Sufficiency fails at both weightings: 35 of 104 equal-weight cells with a wider block and 3,045 of 16,633 unequal ones pay exactly zero, a tied block whose width is exactly what the deficit needs costing nothing, a common shape and not a corner.

Scope. The necessary half at equal weights is a rule, proved; the counts are observations over a designed sweep of posterior menus crossed with weight vectors, every cell against an exhaustive optimum. Toy scale.

At equal weights the certificate is optimal; at unequal weights it is not, and densely rule

At equal weights every label costs the same, so minimizing cost is minimizing the number of labels, and for a fixed number the most coverage reachable is that many largest posteriors anywhere in the cell: the greedy by posterior, which is the certificate. 125 designed equal-weight cells find no disagreement, as they cannot. Off equal weights 5,447 of 19,125 designed cells price the certificate strictly above the optimum, by two overlapping routes: the tied fill overshoots, and the optimum spends the surplus by dropping a pair from strictly above the level outright (4,002 admit it) or by dropping a heavy tied pair and topping the coverage back up with a light label from strictly BELOW the level (2,463 have no optimum that avoids one); 1,147 are both and 129 neither. The specimen: weights 3/10, 3/10, 2/5, every row (2/5, 2/5, 1/5), level 2/5, bar 7/10; the certificate costs 2 and covers 4/5, the optimum takes sizes (2, 3, 1) for cost 19/10 and coverage exactly 7/10. What makes the cheap below-level label pay is that the surplus makes part of the coverage it replaces unnecessary.

Scope. Rule, proved, for the equal-weight optimality; the failure is proved by the exhibited cell, and its density is an observation over the designed sweep, exact in rational arithmetic with an exhaustive optimum at every cell. Toy scale.

The certificate's gap over the relaxation is the surplus over the level rule

With L the relaxation's value, L is the cost of the pairs above the level plus the coverage they leave owed divided by t*, and the tied fill covers t* per unit of cost, so cert − L = D/t* by one subtraction, using nothing about the fill's minimality. Three things follow. The surplus BOUNDS the inexactness: 0 ≤ cert − OPT ≤ D/t*, and D = 0 forces the certificate optimal. A rule costs Σ srwr over integer sizes, so cert − OPT lies in the cost lattice, the integer combinations of the weights, whose least positive element is g = gcd(nr)/Q for wr = nr/Q; hence D/t* < g proves exactness with no search. And at equal weights g is one weight while a minimal tied subset keeps D under that weight times t*, so the criterion fires everywhere and the proved equal-weight optimality is its special case, reached by a third route. The criterion fires at zero of the 5,447 overstating cells and decides 7,643 of the 13,678 exact designed unequal-weight cells, 3,326 of them by D = 0. The cap is nowhere tight: the share of D/t* the certificate actually overspends is 1 at none of the 15,882 cells with room, mean 0.1825, worst 0.9808.

Scope. Rule, proved for any finite set of weighted atoms, any labels and any bar; the machinery is complementary slackness for this program and the usual rounding step to an integral optimum, a feasible solution matching the relaxation's bound rounded to the cost lattice being the round-up certificate the cutting-stock literature studies as the integer round-up property, and what is new is the answer. The identity is exact in rational arithmetic at all 19,270 scorings over 19,269 cells; the firing and spend counts are observations over that sweep. Toy scale.

The anchored gap is a second rung, and even that is weight-shaped rule

The overspend is Σ (sr − s'r)wr over the certificate's own sizes and some optimum's, each size between 0 and k, so each coefficient lies in a box the certificate's sizes pin and the threshold rises to the anchored gap gA, the least positive value over that box, never below g, with the same proof. It decides 8,135 of the 13,678 exact cells, 59.5% against the lattice's 55.9%, still fires at none of the overstating ones, and equals g at every bare and equal-weight cell. On the geometric family the death is structural: along the walk holding the first cell's shape and moving θ over (n−1)/n, n = 3 to 1000, g collapses across thirty-six orders of magnitude while gA declines five and sits at least 4.8 orders under every rung's room. At the one rung whose certificate is exact the anchoring raises the threshold six orders, 1.259·10−15 to 1.852·10−9, against a room of 7.008·10−4. What holds gA down is difference vectors of small support, whose values the box admits whatever sizes the certificate carries; at the 105-atom cell, where the box is 5105, one width-6 witness at 1.832·10−7 against a room of 4.349·10−6 proves the miss outright.

Scope. Rule for the raised criterion and its monotonicity, proved; the box enumerated exactly by meet-in-the-middle at fifteen atoms; the decline and the witness are observations on the geometric family at nine rungs. Toy scale.

The flattening law property

Read a box vector as the polynomial P(x) = Σ crxr. Its value is P(θ) times the weights' normalizing factor (1−θ)/(1−θ15), and at θ = (n−1)/n expanding in powers of x − 1 gives P(θ) = Σj≥J mj(−1/n)j, J the multiplicity of the root 1, the vector's flattening. Past a crossover near n = 50 the anchored gap is the normalizing factor times min over J of AJ·n−J, AJ the least positive leading coefficient among admissible vectors of flattening J, an integer set by the certificate's sizes alone. From n = 175 on the minimizer is the coefficient vector of (x−1)(x²−1)(x³−1)², J = 4, m4 = 18, sliding one atom at a time as the size profile's 3s invade the tail; rungs 100 to 150 have J = 5 with A5 = 30, and the inversion in the decline is that family losing its last size window at 175. The size profile is constant at 111112222233333 from n = 2000 to 100000 and the gap is 18·n−4(1 + o(1)) in value units, o(1) at 3.5·10−5 by the top. The free box [−3, 3]15 reaches flattening exactly 6 with least leading coefficient 18, and flattening 7 is empty, so the anchoring is worth two orders of n: the certificate's sizes deny every flattening-6 vector and, past n = 150, every flattening-5 one. Below n = 25 the minimum is a cancellation across moments the law does not govern.

Scope. Property for the identity and its tail bound, algebra at any cell of this shape; the constants exact at every moment-regime rung of a seventeen-rung ladder, n = 3 to 100000. That the profile stays constant for all larger n is observed across four decades, not proved; the θ = 1 cell has the profile 111111123333333, its atom masses tying, so the profile at the limit is not the limit of the profiles. What that minimum over depths IS, the least height at a given flattening with nothing pinning the box, is Flattening's subject, and this walk borrows its answer. Toy scale.

The abandoned atom

The abandoned atom property

A bare cell of eight atoms at equal mass 1/8, seven peaked, one flat at (2/5, 8/25, 7/25). Against a bar of 0.70 the exact marginal optimum is seven singletons and the EMPTY SET at the eighth, the seven clearing the bar alone at 0.72625. At equal masses pairs enter the optimum in order of posterior, so the optimum abandons an atom precisely when its best label falls below the level, maxy p(y|r) < t*, a level set by the whole population and not by that atom: here t* = 4/5, the eighth atom peaks at 2/5 and the seventh at exactly 4/5 and is kept. The abandoned atom is not rare, not down-weighted and not adversarial; it is outnumbered. Split conformal has the threshold shape at equal masses, so its zero coverage at that atom is convergence to the optimum rather than a defect in reaching it.

Scope. Property, the equal-mass derivation holding at any number of atoms, labels and bar; the cell's optimum an exact computation confirmed against exhaustive search over per-atom sizes. Toy scale.

Off equal masses the condition fails in both directions rule

Call it the abandonment condition: an atom is abandoned by every optimum exactly when its best label sits under the level. Above the level it fails at 3,154 atoms of a designed sweep, each carrying a label strictly above t* and abandoned in every optimum; at the first witness the two served atoms cover 7/20·4/5 + 3/5·7/10 = 7/10 exactly and the third, peaking at 4/5, is simply not needed. Below it, 24 of the 634 atoms whose whole row sits under the level are served in every optimum, the optimum unique at all 24: at the first witness the heavy atom's label covers 17/25, short by 1/50, and closing the gap with the light atom's below-level label costs 1/20 where the remaining above-level atom costs 1/10. About nine cells in ten satisfy the condition throughout, which costs an iff nothing. Above-level failures land at a minimum-weight atom 87.5% and 92.6% of the time against base rates near 37%; the below failures' 24 of 24 is a base rate of 93.7%, an atom sitting wholly below the level only when the others are heavy enough to carry the bar alone.

Scope. The refutation is proved by the exhibited cells, exact rationals; the counts are observations over a designed sweep of 38,500 cells with an exhaustive optimum and every optimum enumerated. Toy scale.

No per-atom form replaces it rule

A predicate an auditor can run on one subpopulation reads that atom's own mass and posterior row against the operative level and nothing else. Of the 444 such readings the sweep produces, 55 carry an atom every optimum abandons in one cell and every optimum serves in another, 49 above the level and 6 below, with 12 more at the level excluded as indifferent. Witness: mass 1/20 with row (3/5, 1/5, 1/5) at level 1/5. Against weights (1/20, 1/20, 9/10) the heavy atom's two labels cover 18/25, past the bar, and the light atom is abandoned; against (1/20, 1/10, 17/20) they cover 17/25 and the cheapest top-up is the light atom's own best label, so the same atom is served. Min-cost covering stops being pointwise once purchases are lumpy, and what decides an atom is what the REST of the cell leaves owed. The relaxation's reduced cost w(t* − p)/t* orders the above half at 87.5% and 89.1% and does not beat the atom's weight alone: on the disagreements it splits 36 to 32 the weight's way on one menu and is uniquely right zero times against the weight's 38 on the other. What survives for an auditor is per-cell and never per-atom, its best form the crude weight reading, a tendency and not a law.

Scope. Rule, proved by the two exhibited cells, every value an exact rational and the optimum unique in both; the 55 readings and the reduced-cost scores are observations over the same sweep. Why the distance from the level fails to pay is open. Toy scale.

Flattening

What a box of bounded coefficients can flatten to at all, freed of the sizes that pin it, is a question about integer polynomials alone, with two inverse dials: the least height at a fixed width, and the least degree at a fixed height. Along both the cheapest polynomial is a product of factors xd − 1 wherever the charted corner reaches, and the height-1 row of the degree dial is the classical Borwein–Mossinghoff table; past width 12 the products lose cells, and past depth 20 two in five. The dials, the floors beneath them and the lattice that reads the minimum directly are on Flattening.

The swap

An atom that peaks above the level and is abandoned by some optimum is not, for that, abandoned by every one. An atom any rule meeting the bar abandons weighs at most 1 − T, which at weights in fifths and quarters confines abandonment to the lightest atom, and trading labels held on heavier partners for the light atom's top labels is an optimum exactly when the mass dropped equals the mass taken, a divisibility at one partner and a subset sum over several. That exchange rescues every abandoning optimum wherever the condition's above half holds, the zero-slack row being a theorem; below a band of the lightest mass every rescue takes more of some partner than it gives back, and the obstruction is arithmetic before it is coverage. The identity behind all of it, the mass bound that empties the condition's below half, and what the certificate's room decides, which is nothing, are on The swap.

The field's excess criterion measures set size against one because one is the only defensible stand-in when the optimum is unknown. Here the optimum is derived, and what it exposes is not a worse estimate but a worse shape of rule, and a fate no subpopulation can read off its own numbers.
