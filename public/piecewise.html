Piecewise maps

The delay of a map of one stream that is smooth on finitely many pieces, decided by three finite clauses, and the floor under every table built from a curve.

A stream is a numeral in radix b over a digit set {−a−..a+}, contiguous — every integer between its ends a digit and each end at most b − 1 — with slack ρ = a− + a+ + 1 − b at least 1, read from its most significant digit; the values a stream can name fill the window [−M−, M+], M± = a±/(b − 1), and its first n digits confine it to a prefix box, an interval the boxes of the next depth refine. A map f of one stream is read at lookahead c when its output's first t digits are a function of the input's first t + c; the least such c is the map's delay. In units of the digit being emitted, with ω = M− + M+ the reach at that scale, the output cells are [p − M−, p + M+], consecutive cells sharing an overlap zone of length ω − 1, and a digit is legal iff its cell holds the image of the input box; the reader is dead iff some box's image strictly contains a zone, a condition on the input alone, so it has no strategy and death is a property of the boxes: the game between the reader and an adversary fixing the stream is decided by scanning them. A box whose image has lower end y and width w kills iff its excess g = w − (ω − 1) is positive and its phase {y + M−} lies in the dead arc (1 − g, 1). With L the lookahead plus the lead, the positions the output runs ahead of the input where its range needs it, the margin law bLρ ≥ Λ(a− + a+), Λ the sup of |f′| over the window, is sufficient for every C² map — the reader survives at every L meeting it, the least being the margin L* — and is exact for every one not affine on the window (the many-stream theorem, over any number of streams); the excess region at L is the boxes whose sup of |f′| exceeds the rate at which the law holds with equality. A system is one (radix, digit set), written radix b over {−a−..a+}. The maps here are smooth only piece by piece, and the margin is a ceiling they can read below.

The piecewise theorem theorem

Let f be continuous on the window and C² on each of finitely many pieces, cut at kinks κ that are rational, an affine piece carrying a rational slope s = p/q and offset d. The reader has no strategy, so death is a property of the boxes alone: the map reads at the least L at which no prefix box's image strictly contains an overlap zone, and a box either lies inside one piece or straddles a kink. With Λlaw = (ω − 1)bL/ω the law's threshold at L, the width rate at which the margin law holds with equality, three finite clauses decide. The curved clause: a non-affine piece kills iff the sup of |f′| over it exceeds Λlaw, the width bound one way and the many-stream argument with the piece as the window the other, since f′ is continuous and a piece affine on its excess region is affine outright. The piece clause: a box inside an affine piece has the phase of its image's lower end on a lattice of step gcd(p, qbL)/qbL with an offset, a constant of the piece plus d·bn−L mod 1, eventually periodic in the depth n, and kills iff that phase lies in the piece's own dead arc (1 − g, 1), g = |s|ω/bL − (ω − 1): one period past its preperiod decides. The kink clause: a straddler is one of at most ⌈ω⌉ per kink and depth, its image the hull of the map over it, and the containment lemma, the rest of this clause, confines it to the shallow depths. At a depth where every affine piece holds a full lattice period, a killing straddler has a hull end on the lattice of an affine side above the threshold and its image inside a same-phase box of that piece, so that piece's clause already fires; a curved side above the threshold is the curved clause, and at an extremum kink, a kink at a local extremum of f, one end of the hull being f(κ) itself, a side below the threshold never attains the far end of a killing hull. So the map reads at the least L at which every clause is empty: at most the margin's L*, and below it exactly when every clause at L* − 1 is empty: a sup, a cycle and a shallow scan. A discontinuous map is dead at every L.

The specimens, one stream, the identity the right piece of each. x/2 on x ≤ 0 and x on x ≥ 0 has Λ = 1 and L* = 1 at every system and reads at 0 at 16 of 20, the slope-1/2 piece alone killing at the four systems whose digits both sit below b − 1 (radix 4 over {−2..2}, radix 5 over {−2..3}, {−3..2} and {−3..3}). Beside the identity a curved piece reads by its sup alone: 3x/4 + x²/4 on x ≤ 0 is dead at 0 and x/5 + x²/20 alive at 0 at every system, the second one below the margin. A C² map affine on its whole peak set, the points where |f′| attains Λ, x − (x + M−/2)³ below −M−/2 and x above, is dead at 0 at every system by its curved remainder, the kill at input depth 1 to 3. The kink fires alone only at shallow depth: a valley, a V-shaped kink between slopes of opposite sign, kills with both pieces alive at all ten systems of radices 2..4, and a kink between slopes of one sign (1/2 beside 3/2, 2 or 2/3) at 8 of 20, each firing at input depth 0 or 1 and certified at a straddling prefix; at radix 5 neither search found a design, the valley's grid exhausted and the same-sign search budgeted. Over several streams a piece reads at the many-stream theorem verbatim: |x|·y in two streams and max(x, y)·z in three, a product inside each piece, are dead at L* − 1 and alive at L* at 20 of 20 each.

The segment lemma. An affine piece of slope p/q with excess g > 0 at L is empty iff its lattice step δ = gcd(p, qbL)/qbL is at least g and its offset sits in a closed window of length δ − g, since an open arc of length g holds a point of every finer lattice whatever the offset; so a piece reads below the margin only through a simple slope, one whose lattice step is at least its excess. At L = 0, with W = a− + a+, every slope of magnitude at most ρ/W is free, above it exactly the p/q with 1 ≤ pW − qρ ≤ b − 1 may carry a piece, and none in the gap between max(1/2, 2/(2 + ⌈(b − 1)/ρ⌉)) and 1 can, slope 1/2 free or among those that may carry one at every system. A table-and-interpolate unit, a curve sampled at breakpoints and joined by chords, sits at the margin unless its steepest chord has such a slope: the reciprocal 1/(1 + M− + x) joined by four uniform chords reads at 0 against L* = 1 at the four symmetric top systems of radices 2..5, a− = a+ = b − 1, where δ = g = 1/3 exactly, and its eight-chord refinement, its breakpoints on the cell lattice or off it, reads at L* at every system, 56 of the 60 designs at the margin; where a breakpoint sits moves no chord design of the reciprocal off its reading, and decides a coarse-lattice piece at one depth only, the first at which the piece holds a box, the shallow kink clause on top. The robust floor. A map within ε of a curve, in the sup norm, inherits every curve kill whose two overhangs, the image's reach past the zone at either end, exceed ε, so no map within ε reads below the margin once ε is under εrob, the largest of the smaller overhangs over the curve's killing boxes at L* − 1, attained at a bounded depth. For the reciprocal it is attained at depth at most 3 and runs from 1/150 at radix 5 over {−4..4} to 1/12 at radix 4 over {−2..2}: from b−1.8 to b−4.1 at radices 3..5 and b−6.2 at radix 2 over {−1..1}, above b−4 at 17 of 20 systems, and its analogue at L* − 2 at least b−2 at 19 of 20. Above the floor the slopes that may carry a piece build the unit as a zigzag, slope 1 alternating with 1/2, 1/3 or 1/4 through the gap and the free slopes taking the shallow end, certified within 5 percent of the floor at 8 systems and within 50 percent at 11 of the 20. No table closer than εrob to its curve reads below the curve's margin, so a table budget buys at most the stretch between the margin and the floor.

Scope. Theorem for every continuous map of one stream that is C² on each of finitely many pieces, the affine pieces' slopes and offsets and the kinks rational, the sufficiency Lipschitz and the containment lemma's far-end clause scoped to extremum kinks; the kink firing alone a property at the systems searched. The clause table against the game at every L read: 20 + 20 + 10 systems of radices 2..5 for the one-piece maps x and x/2, the specimen and the valley designs, 20 + 20 + 20 + 8 for the curved specimens and the same-sign designs, no straddler killing at a depth past the lattices' period without an adjacent piece's clause firing there, and 0 disagreements; the several-stream pieces at 20 systems each. The segment lemma and the robust floor are properties; the slopes that may carry a piece agree with the clause table at 240 of 240 slope–system pairs, the table census is a rule at 60 designs and 160 breakpoint slides, the floor's values are a rule at the 20 systems, and the 95 zigzag designs are certified by the clause table and the game alike, the search reaching within 5 percent of the floor at 8 systems and exhausting its budget at the three systems with a+ < a− and a lead of 1, where the gap is the search's and not proved.
