Meaning

The exact ring put to work: concepts as codewords, the similarity the ring kept, and dynamics whose channels nothing in the ring can couple.

A rung is Z/N with N the product of the first k primes, one channel per prime, the residue mod p: every finite place kept and the archimedean one, size, order and magnitude, deleted (The Object). What that deletion costs a static operation is charted on Walls; the same ring run as a live medium pays and collects differently. On Z/510510, the seven-prime rung, the tower split makes the ring an error-correcting code over its seven windows, a window being the residue at one prime: a valid codeword carries one value below 210 on all seven, the data windows {2, 3, 5, 7} determining it and the parity windows {11, 13, 17} reading off that same value, at minimum distance d = 4, the largest a code of that size and length can have. That one number decides both what a symbol system built on the ring can do and what it provably cannot. Run the ring as dynamics and the deletion shows up as isolation instead: no rule made of ring operations lets one channel see another, so every coupling is a purchase with a price in bits.

Meaning as codeword

Let a meaning be a ring element and a concept a valid codeword of the tower split. The three properties folk semantics asks for, binding that undoes, wholes that snap back from damage, and a symbol that acts, are then theorems of the construction.

Exact binding property

bind(role, filler) = multiply; unbind = multiply by the meadow inverse, the pseudo-inverse x′ with x·x′·x = x and 0′ = 0, defined on the whole ring at once, which is what makes division total on a squarefree rung. Binding is exact for every unit role and every filler, and chained roles unbind in either order. The contrast: in holographic reduced representations and the semantic pointer architecture, circular convolution binds and correlation unbinds approximately, the unbound filler returning at cosine about 0.7 with random atoms and never 1, so every retrieval needs a cleanup memory and is probabilistic. Binary XOR binding is also exactly self-inverse; what the ring adds is the guaranteed cleanup below and the element-as-operator structure.

Scope. Follows from the meadow construction on any product of fields; exhaustive at Z/210, sampled at Z/510510.

Every element is both datum and operator property

opx = multiply-by-x, read back by opx(1) = x: the regular representation is faithful through 1, so a meaning is what it does and what it does determines what it is. The operator's reach is its window support, the image of opx having as many elements as the product of the primes where x is nonzero; a unit acts bijectively, and the meadow inverse undoes unit actions exactly and recovers the surviving windows for the rest, x′·(x·f) = esupp x·f with esupp x the idempotent that is 1 on x's support and 0 elsewhere.

Scope. An identity of the construction; the meadow laws and the reach exhaustive over all 210 elements of Z/210.

Snap-to-whole in three exact zones rule

A Gestalt is a codeword: d = 4 makes the perceptual basin of attraction a theorem. Any one corrupted window snaps back to the whole, and any three erased windows reconstruct it. Two corrupted windows are always detected and never mis-snapped: the view, the tuple as received, sits 2 from its own whole, and a mis-snap would need it within 1 of another, putting two wholes 2 + 1 < d apart. Three can produce an illusion, the view snapping to a different valid whole and never to garbage, but rarely. Basins are disjoint, exactly 52 tuples per concept, and beyond radius 1 the decoder refuses rather than hallucinates.

Scope. Proved from d = 4, which needs the sized split, the genesis ladder's d = 4 row; the censuses exhaustive, 210 concepts × 51 single corruptions and all 35 three-erasure patterns; the illusion rate 0.58% over 5,000 random three-corruption views.

The superposition wall and its cure rule

The bundle of the classical vector-symbolic architectures, an elementwise sum of bound pairs, carries no cleanup here, and for codeword fillers the failure is exact: unbinding a two-item sum returns the true filler plus a noise element sharing the second filler's support, units preserving support, and every nonzero codeword is nonzero on at least d = 4 windows, so whenever the second item is nonzero the query sits outside the true concept's radius-1 basin. Retrieval is 0, the one escape an empty second item, whose noise vanishes. The same d that guarantees the snap forbids the sum. What rescues the sum classically, quasi-orthogonality, bundle noise nearly invisible to a dot product in high dimension, is archimedean concentration of measure and not a ring resource: a wall of the deletion read at the representation level. The cure is ring-native: bundle = direct sum. Idempotent slots, roles as channel windows, retrieve every stored item exactly at a hard capacity, the channel partition, and multiplicative binding composes inside a slot.

Scope. The wall proved and verified at 0 of 2,000 retrievals, every noise weight at least 4; a ring-valued second item retrieves at the basin fraction, 52 of 510510; the cure a property of any CRT product. Capacity is hard on both axes, slots per bundle ending at the channel partition and the concept inventory at 210 for this rung, both raised only by rungs or designed towers. Codewords carry no archimedean similarity gradient; what the ring's own gradings still carry exactly is the similarity carrier and the readable hull.

What exactness buys, bounded observation

Exactness beats approximation only on axes with no concentration-of-measure rescue, and on exactly those axes the winning computation is generic exact arithmetic, table stakes for any exact computer; the tower's own structure is load-bearing only where a capacity, robustness or distribution property is wanted, which is the concentration regime. Two instances beside the wall above. Composition depth: approximate unbinding degrades with depth only for non-unitary atoms, atoms whose binding does not preserve length, or for novel intermediates; with unitary atoms the approximate substrate recovers at cosine 1 through depth 200 in a fixed register, and with known codebook atoms per-level cleanup resets its noise, so exactness wins a constant factor and never a scaling one, while with novel intermediates exact recovery survives to any depth as ordinary modular arithmetic and forfeits the corruption tolerance the approximate substrate exists for. Coupled inference: a coupling-blind residue split of a joint distribution is exact and cheap only when the distribution factors, where mean field is exact too, first errs at the same coupling mean field does, and needs brute force over the whole product once coupled.

Scope. Observations at the scopes run, depth to 1,000 and one Markov random field family at k = 3; the lemma itself is a conjecture from these instances and the binding rule of The substrate door.

The similarity carrier

The superposition wall's scope leaves graded similarity to a learned embedding. How much actually needs one is decided by two gradings the ring kept. Agreement depth grades a pair by how far down the tower it agrees: order the windows into levels, nested prefix by prefix, and let depth(x, y) be the deepest level through which x and y agree on every window. Congruences compose, so depth(x, z) ≥ min(depth(x, y), depth(y, z)): the strong triangle inequality, an ultrametric grading. The overlap ov(x, y) counts the windows on which x and y agree, in any order, and is not ultrametric. The shape that divides them is the chain: a near b, b near c, a far from c, the standard shape of graded co-occurrence neighbourhoods ("bank" near "river", "bank" near "money", "river" far from "money").

The similarity carrier rule

Agreement depth hosts tree-shaped similarity exactly. A rooted taxonomy embeds by path: give each level a block of windows, each branch an invertible digit on that block, and each leaf x the element φ(x) holding its path's digits. Then depth(φ(x), φ(y)) is exactly the depth of the leaves' lowest common ancestor, and one element carries the whole ancestor chain, each level read back by a partial congruence with no second embedding. A depth-d ball, everything agreeing with a given element through level d, is a congruence class mod the prefix's modulus, so a label constant on balls is read exactly from whichever stored exemplar agrees deepest with the query, any of them, no margin and no tie-break. Nearest-neighbour class readout, a heuristic in a metric embedding, is a theorem here. On a paradigm, a table of words by inflected forms, each form one transform of the word, whose word classes share no surface structure, the case that defeats the concatenative embedding, six words held out of the store complete every unseen form from tree placement and one attested form alone, at two class granularities from one embedding, where a per-word codebook covers none and a scrambled embedding scores nothing. What agreement depth refuses is the chain: the strong triangle forbids it under every assignment of ring elements. Trees are native; a learned metric is needed at most for the non-ultrametric residue.

Scope. The embedding identity, the ball-read and the chain refusal are proved, the refusal with an exhaustive control over all 27,000 triples of Z/30; the completion rates, 24 cells of 24 against 0 of 24 for both controls, verified at one 24-leaf taxonomy on Z/510510. Where an item's tree placement comes from is a data-side question outside the claim.

The component criterion and the readable hull criterion

The overlap represents the chain at its first opportunity, 0, 6, 16 in Z/30 overlapping pairwise 2, 2, 1, so the residue has a native carrier; the question is what retrieval can read of it. Store a set of elements and join two by an edge where their overlap reaches a threshold t, the threshold graph. Deepest-overlap readout, the stored exemplar of greatest overlap read as the answer, is exact and tie-break-free for every query and every admissible pool, one holding at least one neighbour of the query, iff the label is constant on that graph's connected components. One line each way: an exemplar at overlap ≥ t is itself a neighbour, hence in the query's component, and every tied exemplar likewise; a label non-constant on a component breaks across some edge, and querying one endpoint against the other alone is admissible, deepest, and wrong. The structure behind the criterion: components merge as t falls even though fixed-t neighbourhoods do not nest, so the component partitions form a nested family, an ultrametric u(x, y) = the largest, over paths from x to y, of the path's weakest overlap, single linkage, and the least ultrametric dominating the overlap. That is the readable hull: what deepest-overlap retrieval reads of any similarity grading is precisely its hull, and chains live in the grading–hull gap, a label that separates a chain's ends forcing an exact overlap tie between disagreeing labels behind some query. Component-grade readout is live, two words held out of the store completing every unseen form, and nothing finer exists. The overlap's readable classes are properties of the stored world and not of the ring: on the full ring the threshold graph is connected at every threshold below k, changing one window at a time walking anywhere, while agreement depth reads its congruence classes on the full ring as it stands. Exact readout is ultrametric-shaped no matter the carrier; the deleted metric is needed not to represent chains but for any reading finer than the hull.

Scope. The criterion is proved, necessary and sufficient, and exhaustively confirmed over all 243 labelings with up to three labels of a five-element store on six channels, every admissible pool for every query, and over 27 labelings at Z/30; the hull is its proved corollary, computed two ways and equal on that world and 20 seeded random worlds; the full-ring connectivity is proved for every k, exhaustive at Z/30; the completion rates, 6 of 6, verified at the one world.

The deletion as dynamics

Set the ring in motion: cells hold values of Z/N and update by a local rule, neighbour by neighbour. Conway's Life rule, a threshold on a count of alive neighbours, is a comparison, which the deletion walls; what a cellular automaton on the ring can do instead is settled by one law and priced by one menu.

The decoupling law rule

A rule built from ring operations, +, × and constants, hence every power map, every gate and the meadow inverse, is a polynomial, hence channel-local, its value mod p a function of the input mod p alone (the locality criterion in n variables), so the automaton is the direct product of k independent mod-p automata, channel p's entire future a function of channel p's initial plane alone. Conversely, any k-tuple of arbitrary per-channel rules is one polynomial rule, by Lagrange interpolation per channel with the coefficients CRT-glued degree by degree. Pure-ring automata are exactly the products of arbitrary per-channel automata: thresholds inside a channel come free, and no ring-op rule couples two channels, the meadow inverse included, which breaks measurement walls within a channel and never the locality wall between them.

Scope. The rule-equals-polynomial leg needs a squarefree modulus, failing at Z/4; locality implies the product on any product ring. Exhaustive at Z/6 with three-cell neighbourhoods; trajectory invariance at Z/30 over 64 steps, soups equal mod 5 keeping equal mod-5 planes at every step; the converse construction exact on all 900 inputs.

The coupling toll rule

Coupled dynamics is bought, not built, and the toll is priced in bits crossing the channel boundary per cell read. An aliveness bit, [x = 0] or any channel quantifier, costs 1; the support grade [|supp x| ≥ t], supp x the channels where x is nonzero, costs log2(k + 1); size comparison costs log2 N. No polynomial computes any of them, and the content never crosses, only quantifier bits do. Conway's threshold is bought at the support grade's price, and the toll is real: a graded Life on Z/30 paying one grade read per cell per step builds cross-channel mutual information from a channel-independent soup within 20 steps, while its decoupled twin never leaves the sampling floor. The fade window, keep channel p iff enough neighbours carry p, is a density dial where Conway has a point: a permissive fade freezes dense and a Conway-shaped window runs sparse.

Scope. The wall is the locality criterion with witnesses at Z/30 and carries at any composite modulus; the prices are the menu's; the Life runs are observations on a 48 × 48 torus, recorded in the script.

The exponent import rule

The tower-split code above survives no arithmetic: under a sum rule its minimum distance degrades as the running values climb rungs, 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 from 210 to 510510, and the repair is a per-step base extension paid every step. So make the invariant a dictionary closed under the dynamics instead, a set of words separated in the window metric, the number of windows where two elements differ, with update = binding and cleanup = snap to the nearest word. Binding-closed dictionaries of units are exactly the subgroups of the unit group; the channels being cyclic, a subgroup is a linear code over the exponent space ∏p Z/(p − 1), and window distance is exponent Hamming distance: classical coding theory imports wholesale through the index transform. The additive twin is trivial, additive subgroups being sub-products at minimum distance 1, so dictionaries live on the multiplicative side. Instances: order-2 exponents make ±1-per-channel words, so binary linear codes are binding-closed, and Hamming [7, 4, 3] on a designed tower over the seven odd primes 3 to 19 corrects any one window; a designed tower on places p ≡ 1 (mod 8) holds an order-8 unit whose powers sit at pairwise distance 7, correcting three of seven. This rung's own maximum-order unit generates a dictionary at minimum distance 1, two words 120 exponent steps apart differing in channel 17 alone and channel 2 never separating units at all, so a dictionary is constructed, never inherited.

Scope. Proved, and verified on the three instances; the negative over all 239 exponent differences of the rung's unit.

The snap-back automaton rule

Binding never spreads an error across windows, wrong(xy) ⊆ wrong(x) ∪ wrong(y) with wrong(x) the set of corrupted windows, and under pure binding a window-p fault stays in window p forever: the decoupling law re-read as fault containment. Two guarantees follow. Eager, snap each cell to its nearest word before binding: at most t corrupted windows per cell per step gives an exact trajectory forever, and failure is a single-step burst past the budget, never an accumulation. Lazy, correction waiting until readout: three stuck lanes, windows wedged at wrong values, open loop with zero maintenance, and one snap per cell reads out exactly. Affine maps x ↦ ux + c with u a unit preserve the window metric and are channel-local, so a single-window corruption stays single-window under them; the sum of two state values is not on that menu.

Scope. The containment proved; eager verified over 500 steps × 64 cells at the full 3-of-7 budget on the order-8 dictionary, where the value dynamics is the Z/8 linear automaton on exponents, and lazy after 200 steps; the isometry menu exhaustive at Z/30. The fault model is noisy value registers under reliable control, the algorithm-based fault-tolerance stance, with no threshold theorem. The dictionary-closed-under-a-group-action form with coset correction is Beckmann–Musicus and Hadjicostis–Verghese; universal computation on coded data at polylog overhead is Spielman. Binding is the classical transversal gate set, the decoupling law the matching wall, and in eager mode a symbol-table decode-update-reencode costs the same, so binding earns its keep in lazy mode and in the code design space.
