The split triple

The principal-place question one degree up, where it stops being one place at a time. A totally split prime of a cubic field carries three places whose ideal classes sum to zero and are otherwise free. Coverage is full after the lift, the class group deciding only the wait; the shortfall at small primes and the even spacing behind an early first hit both reproduce; and the one class number that read against the spacing turns out to be two populations under one label — the triples a field realizes filling either the whole sum-zero group or a diagonal inside it — with the shortfall itself located on the partially split primes.

A characteristic is a rational prime, and a greedy growth walk closes a ring once one characteristic's ladder of moves runs out (The cascade boundary); it closes cheaply at a principal place, one whose ideal is generated by a single element, because the price of a place runs over the powers of its ideal class, the ideal taken modulo the principal ones. The classes form the finite class group Cl(K), of order the class number h. Over a quadratic field that reading is capped by the units and not by the classes (Principal places); a cubic field has one more real place or one fewer, and the question is what survives.

The vocabulary of degree 3. A place has residue degree 1 when its residue field is the prime field, and a characteristic is rank-1 when it carries such a place unramified. In a non-Galois cubic field K an unramified prime is totally split — three degree-1 places, one sixth of the primes — partially split — one degree-1 place and one of degree 2, one half — or inert, one third; a cyclic cubic field has no partial type. A cubic field is complex when it has one real embedding and totally real when it has three, and every field read here is complex unless the signature is named, since the instrument that places a place in the class group reads complex fields only. Class numbers are the ordinary ones, read as a relation class number — the order of the group the relations found generate, which the true class number divides — where a rig says so. L1(K) is the least principal rank-1 characteristic over the odd unramified primes. The share is the fraction of primes carrying a degree-1 place at all that carry a principal one, quoted over its nominal, the value a uniform sorting of the classes would give; the first-hit model draws each such prime independently at that density and integrates to a predicted L1; the index of dispersion of a field's count of principal primes is its variance over its mean, 1 under independence and below 1 under regularity. M is the group of triples in Cl(K)³ summing to zero, of order h², and R the set of triples the totally split primes realize.

The lift

The coverage survives the lift whole, with the wait shorter observation

Over the 1103 cubic fields of |dK| ≤ 6000 — 888 complex, 215 totally real — read over the odd unramified primes to 1000, every stratum of signature and class number 1 through 8 is covered 100.0 per cent at the sweep bound 250, and coverage is a LOWER bound, the principality test being a positive certificate. The mean L1 runs 5.6 at complex h = 1 to 66.0 at complex h = 7, the totally real side 6.1 to 47.3, against the real quadratic side's 6.1 to 178.6 at the same prime cap. A non-cyclic cubic field reads two thirds of the primes rather than one half, with three chances at a totally split one; the twelve cyclic fields read one third. Below 250 the coverage grades sharply with h — 66.7 per cent at the bound 100 at complex h = 6 and 7 — so the absence of a floor is what makes coverage full, and the class group decides how long it takes.

Scope. Observation. The quadratic comparison is over a narrower |D| ≤ 4000, and the difference runs against the comparison, a wider discriminant range carrying larger class groups on average. Any instrument bias runs against the grading too: a larger class number means a smaller regulator — the volume of the unit lattice — at fixed discriminant, so a generator search reaches one sooner there.

The first-hit model is early at every class number above 1, part of it upstream of the class group observation

At the nominal Chebotarev density the ratio of measured to predicted L1 is exactly 1.000 at h = 1 on both signatures — an identity, every place principal there — and 1.17 to 3.21 above it. Part of that sits before the class group: the fraction of primes splitting completely runs 0.104, 0.121, 0.141, 0.158 over the bins to 1000 against a nominal one sixth, a lag a quadratic character has no analogue of. Against each stratum's own leave-one-out density, lag and share deficit divided out, the first hit is still early at every complex stratum of 18 fields or more, and clears its band only at the degenerate half of h = 3.

The mechanism reproduces at degree 3: short at small primes, under-dispersed at every class number but one observation

Both statistics count the places that are NOT principal, which a generator search cannot see. The instrument is a map placing a place in the class group against a lattice of known relations: triviality proves principality, and non-triviality proves the reverse once the lattice is saturated. Over the 236 fields of the box with h > 1, all 36,929 of their degree-1 places below 1000 placed, the share over nominal runs 0.64, 0.80, 0.41, 0.54, 0.12, 0.27 in the bottom bin at h = 2 through 7 and 0.97, 0.90, 1.04, 1.03, 0.88, 1.03 in the top — every stratum short at the bottom, the deficit deeper at larger h without being ordered by it, and within a few per cent of nominal by the top bin. The count is under-dispersed against each bin's own leave-one-out density, the one reference the share deficit cannot leak into: 0.325, 1.583, 0.461, 0.291, 0.364, 0.123 at h = 2 through 7. The index is a chi-square over n fields with null spread √(2/n) — 0.15 at h = 2's 94 fields, 0.58 at the six fields of h = 6 and 7 — so only h = 2 clears alone (z = −4.6), h = 5 is marginal (−2.1), and jointly the six combine by Stouffer to −2.9 with h = 3 pulling the other way inside it. No trend in h. Sizes compare only at h = 2: quadratic 0.142 on the same reference against cubic 0.325 — the direction ports, the size does not.

Scope. Observation on the complex fields, the totally real ones with h > 1 being too few to fill a stratum. The saturation gap is priced three ways: at 62 fields whose h = 1 is certified constructively the map calls nothing non-principal, 40 of its negative verdicts survive an exhaustive certificate, and 69,323 accepted elements pass the norm accounting. The row pools two splitting types and the deficit is not in both halves (the two-types block).

The triple, and why class number 3 looked exceptional

The realized set is an S3-stable subgroup, and at h = 3 it is M, a diagonal, or 0 rule

The three classes over a totally split prime sum to the class of (p), trivial, and that is the only constraint the class group imposes; the model every nominal density uses is that the triple is uniform on M, giving a split prime a principal place with probability qsplit = 1 − (h−1)(h−2)/h², which is 7/9 at h = 3. The triple is a Frobenius — the element of a Galois group a prime determines up to conjugacy. With N the Galois closure of K and H̃ that of its Hilbert class field H, the maximal unramified abelian extension, whose group over K is Cl(K), the totally split primes are those whose Frobenius in H̃/Q lies in Gal(H̃/N), and on that subgroup the triple of classes is a HOMOMORPHISM into M, each coordinate the restriction to one conjugate copy of H. Chebotarev fills Gal(H̃/N), so R is a subgroup of M, stable under permuting coordinates because conjugation permutes the copies, with every coordinate projection onto Cl. At h = 3, M is 2-dimensional over F3, the permutation module is not semisimple (3 divides the order of S3), the fixed space of the 3-cycle is the diagonal D = {(t, t, t)}, and any stable subgroup of order 3 lies in it by nilpotence of σ − 1. So R is M (the model), D (the DEGENERATE regime: every split prime carries one class three times, qsplit = 1/3, equal to the partial prime's) or 0, which forces h = 1. D is nontrivial exactly when 3 divides h, which is why the exception is class number 3's and no other's.

Scope. Proved; the subgroup statement holds at every cubic field and the trichotomy is its class-number-3 case. Nothing here computes in H̃. The general statement at every class number is the genus index law (The degenerate regime).

The partial density 1/h is derived, by an argument that does not port from the split side rule

A partially split prime's Frobenius is a TRANSPOSITION and lies in no subgroup on which the map to the classes is a homomorphism. It lies in one on which a different map is: the subgroup of Gal(H̃/Q) fixing the copy H, twice the size of Gal(H̃/N), every element of which fixes K pointwise, so restriction to H is a homomorphism to Cl(K). The degree-1 place is the Frobenius's fixed point, and Artin makes its class that restriction, independent of the prime chosen above p because Cl is abelian. So the realized classes are a COSET of S = Gal(H/(H ∩ N)), filled uniformly, with [Cl : S] = [H ∩ N : K] equal to 1 or 2 — and the index-2 branch, on which a partial place would NEVER be principal, is empty at every cubic field: at a cyclic field N = K, and at a non-cyclic one N/K is ramified, some prime ramifying in the quadratic resolvent inside N with inertia a transposition or all of S3, which doubles the ramification index of a place of K. The same argument gives the quadratic 1/h cheaper. So the two regimes at h = 3 differ only through qsplit, and their shares over the degree-1-carrying primes, (⅙·qsplit + ½·⅓)/(⅔), are 1/3 and 4/9 — a factor of 4/3 apart, the 7/3 of the q's damped by the partial primes.

Scope. Proved, at every cubic field; over a complex cubic field the real place settles the ramification alone.

The degenerate regime occurs at non-Galois fields, and the dichotomy is total observation

Of the 83 complex cubic fields of relation class number 3 to |dK| ≤ 6000, over their 2023 totally split primes, THIRTY-EIGHT carry one class at all three places over EVERY split prime they have — an equal-class fraction of exactly 1.000 on 17 to 27 primes each; the other 45 read 0.000 to 0.375, mean 0.2143, against the uniform model's one third. No field lies in [0.45, 0.95]. The saturation gap cannot manufacture that: two places read as one class only when their difference is PROVED to lie in the relation lattice, so an unsaturated lattice moves a field from D toward M and never the reverse, and the 38 are a positive certificate. The two groups' shares land on their derived values, 0.3232 and 0.4318 against 1/3 and 4/9. The cyclic case is the degeneracy's one classical instance — Gal(K/Q) permutes the places and the automorphism group of Z/3 has order 2, forcing the action trivial — and the content is that the same regime is reached with no Galois action on K at all.

Scope. Observation. Each share sits about 3 per cent under its value: at D the bottom-of-range deficit and nothing else, at M a net of opposing departures of the same size by coincidence (the two-types block). A split prime with no principal place has all three classes equal, three nonzero elements of Z/3 summing to zero being forced equal, and this holds at all 753 such primes. What sorts a field into its regime is read off the discriminant (The degenerate regime).

The h = 3 exception dissolves into the mixture, and the under-dispersion holds inside both halves observation

On the same leave-one-out reference the index of dispersion reads 1.583 over the whole stratum, 0.220 on the 38 and 0.252 on the 45: the excess was the gap between two groups' means. Splitting on anything correlated with the outcome lowers a within-group spread mechanically, and the answer is size and shape: removing a mixture carries an index down to 1 and no further, these land at 0.220 and 0.252, and a degenerate field's fraction is 1.000 with no cut fitted through a continuum. Class number 3 was never an exception to the regularity; it was two populations under one label.

The two mechanisms that pull opposite ways on the share sit on two splitting types observation

A pooled share of 0.432 against 4/9 made a principality shortfall look simultaneous with a coincidence suppression that RAISES it. Split by type: among the 1131 totally split primes of h = 3's generic half the carrying rate is 0.8603, ABOVE the model's 7/9 and above the 0.8556 the all-equal suppression alone predicts — the comparison telescopes to whether a third of the all-equal triples are all-principal, and 87 of 245 are (0.3551, z = +0.72), a deficit estimate in qsplit of −0.0047 at a spread of 0.0065, the wrong sign for a shortfall. The shortfall is the PARTIAL primes': 0.1911, 0.2594, 0.2455, 0.3311 across the bins against one third — the small-prime deficit located on a splitting type. A degree-1 place is more often principal over a totally split prime than over a partial one at equal norm, the worst cell +4.38σ at h = 3, where the split share sits at its model in every bin.

Scope. Observation. At h = 2 the split share is floored at one third by arithmetic and that row cannot carry it.

The partial shortfall is small-p, graded by the order of the class, and blind to the regime observation

Over 18,689 partial primes of 227 fields, against each field's own profile of class orders — the order of a class being the least power of it that is trivial — the principal cell runs 176/349.9, 510/627.8, 1444/1568.4, 4306/4449.7 across the bins at z = −12.1, −6.1, −4.1, −2.8 (−3.9 pooled once the measured dispersion is priced): shrinking at every step and still short at the top, a residual and not a bound, which does not touch the derivation, a count below 1000 having no purchase on a Chebotarev asymptotic. Low orders are short and the highest long: −2.36, −1.45, +3.30 at orders 1, 2, 4 (h = 4) and −2.12, −0.67, −0.19, +2.40 at orders 1, 2, 3, 6 (h = 6). The order cells of a stratum sum to its place count, so a short trivial class must inflate the others; the content is that the MIDDLE orders are short too, the surplus landing on the top order rather than spreading by cell size — the per-place form of the ladder the quadratic side grades (Principal places).

Scope. Observation. Written as levels and read against degree 2, the ladder is The generator ceiling's.

The early first hit is the spacing here too, and it is the degenerate half's pattern

The first hit is the count's survival function — the chance the count is still zero at each position, summed — so fed the index of dispersion at every index scale it returns the measured first hit at the one stratum whose early hit clears its band: 1.009 ± 0.07 at the degenerate half of h = 3 against 0.837 uncorrected, and 0.956 ± 0.045 at h = 2 against 0.929, which its 1.8 spreads cannot grade. The regime split does not move the stratum's mean (pooled and split derivations 1.005 and 1.002): the mixture's 1.583 is a cap-scale fact, and at the four scales where the first hit is decided the pooled stratum reads 0.90, 0.90, 0.56, 0.43. What the split decides is WHICH fields are early: the generic half sits within noise of independence and the degenerate half carries the whole of it, on an index curve — 0.96, 0.85, 0.51, 0.50, 0.46 at n = 1 to 5 — that falls as the quadratic h = 2 curve does to the same 0.46 over the same five scales, a split prime carrying one class three times being one draw as a quadratic split prime is one place.

Scope. The sign is a pattern over five strata, the landing an observation on the one stratum that grades it; the survival identity and the two-moment law are the quadratic page's (Principal places).

What the label holds

Degeneracy is a subgroup condition, the three classes agreeing modulo the cubes rather than being equal, of index 3 to the number of lines the field's conductor carries; above 3-rank 1 the word covers two regimes, and which one a field lands in is a congruence on the discriminant. The condition, the law, the sorter and the mechanism behind it, and the all-principal shortfall that deepens with the class number, are The degenerate regime's.
