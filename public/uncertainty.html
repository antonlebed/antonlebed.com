Stated uncertainty

What four standard estimators of a best-possible score do when that score is known exactly: where each one's stated interval breaks, which breakages are structural, the rate at which data cures a statement or does not, and what an auditor holding no truth can read of any of it.

The families are the ones whose optimum is derived (Ceilings). Fix N squarefree, draw x from Z/N uniformly, and show the solver the residues modulo a proper divisor M, leaving the unknown cofactor c = N/M. The shown residues pin a fiber, the c elements sharing them; one pair (ring, shown residues) is a cell. Read the task as classification: the label is [x ≥ t] at a fixed threshold t, ηr is the probability of label 1 on fiber r, at most two values across fibers, and the Bayes error R* is the least error rate any rule can reach, here a rational number. The error of the best constant answer is the no-evidence value; the gap between the two is the lift; a threshold is interior where the lift is positive and at the floor where it is zero; a cell's interior thresholds form one run, its window.

An estimator takes n draws and returns an estimate of R* with a stated 95% interval, half of whose width is its half-bar. Two things are then measurable against truth: its bias, the mean estimate minus R*, and its coverage, the fraction of trials whose interval contains R*. Four standard estimators run, in two families. The plug-in pair fits a majority rule to the sample and scores it, resubstitution on the same data and held-out on a withheld half. The bracket pair reports an interval from a theorem rather than a fitted rule: the 1-nearest-neighbour Cover–Hart bracket, from the nearest-neighbour error rate, and a divergence bracket, from the number of edges of a minimum spanning tree joining points of opposite label. Five slices are read, a slice being a cell with a threshold: three at the floor and two interior, at four sample sizes from 500 to 32,000, forty trials each. At the three floor slices every fiber carries the same ηr, 1/2, 4/7 and 6/11, the first two over M = 15 fibers and the third over M = 105, the widest. The first is the knife slice: at η = 1/2 exactly, every rule is optimal.

Four estimators against a fraction

The coverage map observation

Every slice sees at least one of resubstitution, held-out and the Cover–Hart bracket fail its nominal 0.95 at some sample size, and none of the failures is visible from inside: each interval is computed the same way whether it is right or wrong, and only the closed form says which. Which point estimator breaks is slice-dependent, and the ordering inverts. At the knife slice resubstitution covers 0.00–0.03 while held-out covers 0.93–0.97; at the widest floor slice resubstitution starts at 0.00 and recovers only at the largest n, while held-out falls 0.90, 0.50, 0.25, 0.15. Neither is simply the good one. Both brackets cover at 0.90 or better at every slice and every n, and buy it by containing the no-evidence value along with the truth.

Scope. Computed on the five slices at n = 500, 2000, 8000, 32000, forty trials each, one seed stream, 0-1 score, uniform prior. A positive control, a two-fiber Bernoulli problem with R* = 0.15, passes through the same sampler and estimator code before any slice is read. The two interior slices are no harder than the floor ones: the window moves the no-evidence baseline, not what an estimator does. Toy scale.

The resubstitution interval is inconsistent at the knife observation

Where every ηr is 1/2 exactly, resubstitution's deficit is the mean absolute deviation of a fair binomial, ½√(2M/πn), and its own half-bar is the binomial one, 1.96√(1/4n). Both shrink as 1/√n, so their ratio √(2M/π)/1.96 is free of n: 1.5766 derived, and −1.48, −1.55, −1.56, −1.59 printed across the four sample sizes, the sign recording an estimate below the truth. The point estimate is consistent, its bias running −0.0644 to −0.0087; the interval is not, and coverage stays at 0.00–0.03 at every sample size. More data never repairs it.

Scope. The closed form derived asymptotically and confirmed at four sample sizes, forty trials each. 0-1 score, uniform prior. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_ceiling_ruler.py

More data makes a held-out interval worse, for a while observation

At the widest floor slice held-out coverage falls 0.90, 0.50, 0.25, 0.15 across the four sample sizes, and only there: at the narrow floor slices the same estimator's coverage climbs with n. The estimator scores a fitted rule whose excess over the optimum decays with the probability that some fiber's empirical majority flips, while its reported precision decays as 1/√n; a tightening interval reads as improving evidence, and here it is not. The failure is transient: it lasts while the slice's 105 near-half fibers stay unresolved, and re-run at 400 trials the coverage turns back up inside the measured range, 0.863, 0.495, 0.152, 0.278.

Scope. The direction is the finding, not the rates: it survives all three tie-breaks of the fitted rule's empty-fiber choice, the two harsher ones falling further, while two random streams of one setting differ by 0.12, the scale of forty-trial noise. The turn back up is read at 400 trials on the second rig. 0-1 score, uniform prior. Toy scale.

Both failures are estimator facts, and the ring supplies placement rule

The same estimators through the same code on M bare Bernoulli fibers, with no ring anywhere, reproduce the knife ratio at −1.52 to −1.62 and the falling series at 0.88, 0.68, 0.23, 0.25. A dial the ring cannot turn, the distance δ of η from 1/2, locates the interval failure in the band 2δ√(n/M) ≲ 1: at n = 8000 a distance of 1/64 already lifts coverage from 0.00 to 0.85, and 1/32 restores it. What a designed family contributes is not the failure but the placement: a floor slice at η = 1/2 exactly, inside a band that narrow, with the truth a rational number.

Scope. The ring-free reappearance derived by hand and confirmed at the slices' own M, η and n; the band's location measured rather than derived, the crossing sitting nearer 1/2 than √(M/n). 0-1 score. Toy scale.

One bound read at two statistics rule

Once n ≫ M every test point has an exact residue match, so the nearest-neighbour rule returns a random same-fiber label and its error tends to Σr P(r)·2ηr(1−ηr): 1/2, 24/49 and 60/121 on the slices run, gaps at most 0.0007 at n = 32,000. The Henze–Penrose divergence the spanning-tree estimator targets has exact value E[(2η−1)²] here, and half of one minus it is the same quantity again, so the Cover–Hart and the divergence brackets are one bound read at two statistics; a second estimator family buys a different estimator and never a second opinion on the bound. The bound's equality case is this family's own window: the lower endpoint's shortfall is the Jensen gap of |2η−1|, zero iff t is divisible by M or the cell has odd cofactor with t in its middle window; off the window the endpoint is strictly conservative, by at most 0.0178 across the thresholds run.

Scope. Derived and confirmed in exact rational arithmetic on all 35 (slice, threshold) pairs the rig touches. Toy scale.

Correct and empty observation

The Cover–Hart bracket covers at every slice and n, and across the odd-cofactor window of the N = 105 cell it contains the no-evidence value at 14 of 14 in-window thresholds, on a family whose lift tents to a peak of 7/105; its width at n = 32,000 is wider than the whole tent. At the knife slice its 0.95–1.00 is its cap at 1/2, which no binary Bayes error can exceed, the truth being 1/2. Correct and informative are different properties, and only a ruler separates them. The plateau itself is recoverable: at n = 8000 both plug-in protocols track the flat 3/7 across fourteen independently sampled thresholds within 0.003.

Scope. Computed on the 30-threshold profile of the N = 105 cell at n = 8000, forty trials. Toy scale.

The divergence bracket covers for two wrong reasons observation

The spanning-tree bracket covers 0.95–1.00 everywhere, and neither source of that is accuracy: the truth sits at the exact bracket's lower endpoint on all five slices, so containing it costs nothing at the top, and its stated bar is binomial where a tree's edges are dependent, overstating the statistic's dispersion by 1.75 to 2.75 times at every slice and sample size. Even fed exact truth it certifies a lift at 2 of the 14 in-window thresholds, and at 0 once the bar is added.

Scope. Computed on the five slices at four sample sizes, forty trials, with the rows fed in an order that does not group the labels. Toy scale.

The tree the data does not fix observation

A spanning-tree estimator needs a distance, and the only one the evidence offers, the number of shown residues two points differ in, puts every pair in one fiber at distance zero. So any spanning tree of a fiber's points is a genuine minimum spanning tree, and the statistic is not a function of the sample. Fed the rows in class order, each fiber contributes one edge joining opposite labels and the statistic reads the fiber count alone: 0.0005 at n = 32,000 at every M = 15 slice, across-trial spread 0.0000, where the truth is 3/7 and where it is 1/2, and its bracket covers at 0.00. Shuffling the rows, which changes no datum, moves it to 0.4897. Nor are those the extremes: a fiber's zero-weight edges admit a spanning tree of the complete bipartite graph between its two label classes, every edge crossing, so the identified set, the values the data leaves possible as the estimator's arbitrary choices range, is the whole interval a binary Bayes error can occupy. The repair is a tree whose shape does not consult the labels: the labels are then exchangeable across its edges and the expected cross count is the population quantity whatever the tree. An exact Euclidean minimum spanning tree on a jittered embedding, label-blind by geometry, agrees with the shuffle.

Scope. Computed on the five slices at four sample sizes, forty trials, the mechanism and the identified set derived; the label-blind control is one configuration at n = 500, its agreement inside a conservative Poisson bound. Categorical evidence forces the non-identification: residues carry no order, so the tree has nothing to minimize. Toy scale.

The statement laws

The failures above share no mechanism. What they share is the object that fails: the statement, an interval computed from the estimator's own model, which can price only error that model has a coordinate for. Error outside the model is the uncounted term b, and it reaches the comparison against truth without entering the arithmetic. Write h for the stated half-bar and σ for the estimate's true dispersion across trials. A statement is a bound when its uncounted term has a known sign and a fit when it does not; a bound can only widen, so a fit is the one kind that can fail toward confidence (the sign law). The laws below run on designed worlds, fifteen equally likely fibers carrying stated rates: clear where no rate is nearer than 1/10 to 1/2, knife where every rate is 1/2, and mild where three rates sit just past 1/2 and twelve well away. One estimator with one statement kind is an arm; an arm on one world at one sample size is a case.

The rate law: three regimes on one dial observation

Whether data cures a statement is the trajectory of b against h, and one plug-in code shows all three. Where the model contains the truth, b/h falls 0.032 to 0.002 and coverage sits at nominal. At the knife world the two shrink at one rate, the ratio holds at 1.573–1.595, and coverage is flat at 0.015–0.035 whatever the sample size. Where the model cannot hold the truth — the mild world's fifteen fibers pooled into three groups of five — the ratio grows 0.259 to 1.781 while coverage falls 0.915, 0.875, 0.555, 0.045, the bias sitting from the second sample size on at the exact pooling gap 7/750, the distance from the pooled model's own limit to the truth. Nothing degrades except the claim. Coverage passes 1/2 where b equals h, which holds wherever h outruns σ, as it does at every fit arm here by a factor of 1.9 to 2.1.

Scope. Observation at 400 trials each, n = 500 to 32,000, over M = 15 fibers with exact rational targets; each arm's regime follows from its construction, its trajectory does not. The knife arm is the control, reproducing the constant √(2M/π)/1.96 = 1.5766 from a differently written estimator on a differently built world. The third regime is read on the small pooling gap because a large one leaves no trajectory: the same pooling on groups straddling 1/2 gives a gap twenty times wider and coverage 0.000 at every n. 0-1 score. Toy scale.

Coverage is a function of two ratios observation

Under approximate normality of the estimate, coverage is a function of b/σ and h/σ and nothing else; what is measured is that the approximation holds. Across the arms of the rate law, both statement kinds and three worlds, measured coverage tracks the pair alone, largest deviation 0.013 over 28 cases, and 0.023 on a wider grid of 32 where two more mechanisms land: a designed shrinkage weight and the held-out transient above. Four documented mechanisms — an omitted bias at the noise's own rate, a model class that cannot hold the truth, the smoothing of an atomic truth, a precision outrunning excess risk — differ only in where on the plane they put a statement.

Scope. Observation, 28 cases on the first rig and 32 on the second, 400 trials each. 0-1 score. Toy scale.

The corner is occupied, and visibility there splits the subsampling family observation

Fit every fiber, then pull each fitted rate toward 1/2 with a shrinkage weight decaying as n−1/4: a class that holds the truth and a bias that still moves at every n, yet decays slower than the half-bar. |b|/h runs 1.22 to 3.75 across a 64-fold n while coverage runs 0.255 to 0.000, more data strictly worsening the statement. That is the corner the knife and the cases whose term has settled onto a constant gap leave between them: divergent and still moving. The bootstrap term, the estimator's mean shift across resamples of its own sample, is blind to it by an exact identity, the pull being the same function of n on the sample and on any resample, so it cancels in estimator-minus-own-estimand. Only an instrument that reruns the estimator at another size sees it: the drift term, the estimate on the whole sample minus the estimate on half, detects 0.028 rising to 0.362 as the damage grows, and a two-size lever, 500 nested inside 32,000, reaches 0.522.

Scope. The cancellation is a property of the construction; the rates are observations at 400 trials, 150 resamples per trial, detector thresholds holding false alarms to 0.10 over the covering cases, the two-size lever one nested draw per trial. 0-1 score. Toy scale.

A non-identified statement is off the plane for its form observation

A published deletion audit (scored elsewhere against exact posteriors) estimates a mixing weight π by matching one sample's variance against a two-class mixture's, minimizing over the box [0, 1]. With γ the gap between the two class variances, the objective's second solution sits at 1 − π + γ/4, exact at every γ and matched to 1.1·10−16, competing with the true one until it leaves the box at γ = 4π. Identification is restored by the box and not by the data, and the dial prints it: at the smallest audit size the resampling weight on the spurious solution falls 0.54, 0.42, 0.34, 0.26 as it walks to the edge and is 0.00 once outside. Three repairs that move a coordinate all fail to put this statement on the plane — the plane averaged over the two solutions by their weights, coordinates read off the interval instead of the estimator, and the half-width's own randomness. What the plane assumes is the statement's shape, a point plus or minus a half-width with jointly near-Gaussian parts, and a quantile band off a two-peaked resampling distribution is not that shape.

Scope. Observation, 24 settings: a Gaussian sweep in γ at π = 1/5 plus the two world settings the audit was scored on, three audit sizes, 200 replicates per setting and 200 resamples per replicate. The second solution's formula and exit are properties of the objective. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_nonidentified_statement.py

A pair of bounds closes on the hull observation

A bound need not choose between the two solutions: the term it cannot count enters with a known sign and can only widen it, so a pair of one-sided bounds closes on the hull of the two solutions the population leaves possible, [0.2, 0.8] at the exactly non-identified setting, rather than on a point. The bracket runs [0.004, 0.997], [0.057, 0.946], [0.154, 0.845] across three audit sizes, its excess beyond the hull falling 0.196, 0.143, 0.046 at the low end and 0.197, 0.146, 0.045 at the high, the two agreeing within 0.003 though computed independently. No single arm carries this: whichever end of the box the truth sits nearest, that arm reports trivially and the other is tight. The bound's slack contracts to 0.051 where the parameter is identified and refuses to fall below 0.645 where it is not, while on the same resamples the fit band narrows from 0.809 to 0.671 with a coverage that does not rise, 0.855, 0.915, 0.820 against 0.90. Changing only the statement kind converts a tightening interval into a width that will not close.

Scope. Observation on the same 24 settings. The bound is one-sided at 0.95 against the fit band's two-sided 0.90, so the nominals are stated rather than compared. The bound's own shortfall sits entirely at the identified settings, 0.875–0.965, the resampling quantile's calibration and not the sign; at none of the 24 is the pair wrong together. Toy scale.

What can be seen from inside

An auditor holds the other side of the plane: an interval has been computed and there is no R* to score it against.

The observable coordinate carries no verdict observation

σ is estimable by resampling the one sample in hand, so h/σ is free from inside, and it is the wrong half of the plane. On the mild world at four sample sizes the per-fiber fit and the pooled fit print resampled h/σ of 2.21 against 2.01 down to 2.00 against 1.97, indistinguishable, while their coverages run 0.920 against 0.945 down to 0.920 against 0.065. Nor is the estimate faithful everywhere: the resampled σ runs 0.965–0.989 of the truth where the functional is smooth and 1.18–1.27 at the knife world, flattering the bar.

Scope. Observation, one world at four sample sizes, 400 trials, 150 resamples per trial. Toy scale.

verifier: explore_uncounted_inside.py

A converged term is invisible to both subsampling instruments property

The drift term reads b(n) − b(n/2) and differences the target away. The bootstrap term measures the estimator's bias against its own estimand, the number its arithmetic returns on the whole population, which for a model that cannot hold the truth is the model's limit and not R*. Split b into a decaying part and the gap between the model's limit and the target: the bootstrap reports the first and drops the second, the drift differences the second away. At the five cases where b has settled onto such a gap the true |b|/h is 4.33, 8.84, 17.71, 35.50 and 1.78 against inside readings of at most 0.15. What the kernel rests on is the model class: nothing computed inside a class names a target the class cannot express. Where the term still moves the inside view works, biased by a knowable factor: at the knife world the bootstrap reports √2 − 1 of the true term, 0.399–0.420 against 0.41421, flat across a 64-fold n.

Scope. A property of the two constructions, measured at five settled cases and four knife cases. Toy scale.

The verdict is no, and the failure is anti-monotone observation

A detector is a per-trial statistic with a threshold. At thresholds holding false alarms to 0.10 over the covering cases, the bootstrap term catches 0.432 and the drift term 0.196 of the non-covering cases' trials. The bootstrap's 0.432 is the four knife cases whole plus a residue at the two smallest sizes of one arm whose term had not yet settled onto its gap of 29/150; case by case it catches 1.000 at each knife case and then 0.302, 0.022, 0.000, 0.000 as the true |b|/h rises 4.33 to 35.50. Power falls as the damage grows, severity being bought by the convergence that empties the kernel. A goodness-of-fit test escapes the kernel by fitting a richer class and pays by detecting the model: on a world whose three groups each have every fiber on one side of 1/2, the pooled model is wrong about every fiber and exactly right about R*, the pooling gap is zero, coverage is nominal, and the within-group Pearson statistic climbs 8.0 to 548.5 in n. So the direction a stated uncertainty fails in is knowable from inside exactly where the uncounted term is still moving, the corner included, and not once it has settled.

Scope. Observation over 27 of 28 cases: 17 covering at 0.90 or above and 10 below 0.80, one at 0.828 excluded as neither; 400 trials per case. The fit test is read over the six non-covering cases that pool, 2,400 trials, where it catches 0.167. Every threshold is set over the covering cases' trials, the zero-gap world's among them, so the rates read detectors of the term and never of misspecification, which the raw statistic detects perfectly. Toy scale.

The repair reads the nested fits' difference in the functional observation

Read the same two fits in the target's coordinates rather than the model's: the pooled estimate of R* minus the per-fiber estimate, nonnegative on every sample by concavity, its mean the pooling gap itself. That statistic catches 0.979 of the failing converged-gap trials at false alarms 0.10 and stays silent, 0.033 and under, on the zero-cost world whose good trials collapsed the raw test to 0.167. It prices the term's size and not its lethality: on a case still covering at 0.935 and carrying the real gap 7/750 it alarms at 0.468, wrong as a verdict on coverage and correct at every size about what the model costs, h alone deciding when that cost turns fatal. An early warning, priced by h. What stays uncounted after both instruments is error outside every class the auditor can nest: the kernel narrows, it does not empty.

Scope. Observation on the second rig, eight arms at four sample sizes, 400 trials each, thresholds set as above. Toy scale.

A benchmark's claimed headroom is a function of its score and its prior, exact on these families, and the estimation move that makes a family worthless as a subject is what makes it an instrument. Direction is knowable from inside only where the uncounted term is still moving, and an exact-truth ruler is what says whether it is.
