The jump set

The 1-units of a totally ramified local field form a filtered module with one relation, and the Pareto frontier of that relation is the field's jump set. Every landing spectrum of the readout family lies in a bracket the jump set fixes, and the frontier's second point is the window defect read off the digits of one unit: the readout and the jump set are one invariant seen from two sides.

Fix a prime p and a totally ramified extension K/Qp of degree e with residue field Fp, on this page a window, with π a uniformizer, v the valuation normalized by v(π) = 1, and every window given by an Eisenstein polynomial xe + Σ p·bixi − p·d with its root as π. A 1-unit is a unit u with v(u − 1) ≥ 1 and its level is v(u − 1); raising a 1-unit of level i to the p-th power sends it to level ψ(i) = min(p·i, i + e) at every level but the seat i* = e/(p − 1), where the two gears tie, assumed integral throughout, and e* = p·i* is the level one step past it. Write w = −p/πe (2/πe at p = 2) for the unit the Eisenstein relation fixes, its digits w0, w1, … the canonical expansion in powers of π, and δw = v(w − 1) for the window defect: the gate is the zeroth digit, w0 = 1 exactly when ζp ∈ K (open) and δw = 0 otherwise (closed). Observatory defines the landing game these spectra come from and holds the readout theorems the bracket meets.

The module and its invariant

The invariant the literature does own is Pagano's jump set of the field (arXiv:1810.09975). The 1-units form a filtered module over Zp, and it is quasi-free: free on one generator 1 + πa at each level a of T* = {i < p·i* : p ∤ i} ∪ {p·i*}, modulo one relation, the torsion. The orbit of that relation vector under the filtered automorphisms is classified by a pair (I, β), I ⊂ T* and β strictly decreasing with the weights ψβ(a)(a) strictly increasing: a coordinate at level a divisible by p exactly b times has weight ψb(a), the level it reaches after b powerings, and (I, β) is the Pareto frontier of the vector's coordinates under (divisibility, weight). His coefficient readout of the jump set is stated for regimes that exclude p = 2 with e even, the regime of the observatory's triangle.

The rung theorem: every class's landing spectrum bracketed by the jump set theorem

Write the seat as i* = c0pM with p ∤ c0 and e* = p·i*. The arrival class (c, m), c = c0pM−m, is the units of level exactly c, each landing at v(upm+1 − 1); the class (c0, M) is the top class, c = 1 at 2-power e, and the rung of a window is its top-class landing minimum. At the closed gate there is no relation and every class lands rigidly at e*. At the open gate the first frontier point of the relation vector is (c0, M + 1) at weight e* at every field, the seat's image under ψ. A unit of the class raised to the pm+1-th power has its level-c0 coordinate at the relation's own order, and one choice of the relation's multiple cancels it; every coordinate whose order sits below m + 1 + na, na the powerings carrying level a up to c, is then fixed across the class at its own weight, every other one is the unit's to choose, and no level below c is ever fixed, its weight falling short of e*. So the class's landings lie in [min(Rc, Φc), Rc], both ends attained: Rc the weight of the first frontier point of order β ≤ m, infinite exactly when the class holds a primitive pm+1-th root of unity, and Φc the class's floor, e* + pm at every class of every odd p and at every p = 2 class but the top class with c0 = 1, whose floor is 7e/2: the level c + 1 pulls back to a coordinate other than c0 except when c0 + 1 = 2, so the top class of x12 − 2 sits at 24 + 4 = 28 and not 42, the readout theorem's wild floor being the c0 = 1 accident. Every class minimum is the rung under its own floor: the rung is the second frontier weight w2 capped at the floor, at 2-power e the floor 7e/2 = 7, 14, 28, where the cyclotomic anchors ζ2e, with I = {1} alone, land; the spectrum is rigid below the floor and graded from it. The frontier's points past the second are read by no minimum but by the intermediate classes' maxima, from the top down by order: the class (3, 1) of x18 + 3 tops at its third point, and at e = 8 over Q2 the class-4 top is unbounded exactly when i lies in the field, the class-2 top when ζ8 does. The second frontier weight is read off the digits of w in the next block. The three objects nest strictly, word → (I, β) → rung, each arrow many-to-one; the seat cancellation of the two-gear clock is the relation vector's frontier past its first point.

Scope. Theorem for every totally ramified window with integral seat at every p, given Pagano's Theorem 1.4, the module's structure; its instances rules at every field read: every Eisenstein cell at e = 2 to coefficient precision 26 and at e = 4 to 25, the exact class-1 landing enumerated at the 48 cells where the cap bites; 8192 Haar-random octics, the exact minimum of each class enumerated over its 32, 4 and 2 representatives and its maximum over the representatives that decide it, 0 off at every field and class; the 120-field census at e = 2, 4, 8; and the tame readout's twenty fields at p = 3, 5 with x12 − 2, every class's exact spectrum enumerated over 2 to 486 representatives, the bracket 0 off at 44 classes. Controls: the census quadratics and quartics, the pure octics x8 − d, ζ16 and the hand value at x2 − 2 print as read by formula, enumeration and ladder; at the 55 fields of e = 2, 4 the frontier's last order equals the 2-power torsion order (Pagano's Proposition 3.42), and the frontier is unchanged under two changes of uniformizer.

The second point from the digits theorem

Write B = (1 + πc0)d·pM for the seat's basis monomial, d the digit that carries the weight of ζp/B − 1 past i*, and τ for that weight's excess over i*, the tail of ζp past its leading monomial. The module reads the tail: a level strictly between i* and e* pulls back to a coordinate of order past M exactly when it is i* + jpM, a level a·pb with b < M having a > c0; so for τ < e the coordinate at weight i* + τ is undominated by the first frontier point exactly when pM ∤ τ, and then w2 = e* + τ, the second point the pullback of i* + τ at one order more; when pM | τ, w2 > e* + τ. The equation reads the tail off w: with ζp = 1 + y, y = dπi*(1 + t), the relation ζpp = 1 divided by y is (1 + t)p−1 = w(1 + ((p − 1)/2)·y + ⋯). At odd p the binomial tail of B enters no lower than i* + 4i*/3, so τ = δw below i* and τ = i* above it, the digit wi* = (p + 1)/2 deferring it at equality. At p = 2, where ζ2 − 1 = −wπe agrees with wπe to precision e*, B's own binomial tail enters at e/2 and 5e/4 and τ = δw below e/2, e/2 above it, deferred at equality. Hence min(w2 − e*, X) = min(δw, X) with X = pM, and X = e/2 at the wild class, the p = 2 top class with c0 = 1: the window defect is the second frontier weight's excess over e*. With the rung theorem this makes the tame readout at every class, the readout theorem's p = 2 staircases and its top-class law corollaries — its skeleton positions e/2 and 5e/4 are the binomial tail of (1 + π)e, and the stop at 2e + e/2 is that tail's first term whenever δw > e/2 — so the readout family and the jump set are one invariant seen from the digits and from the module.

Scope. Theorem for every totally ramified window with integral seat at every p, given Pagano's Theorem 1.4; its instances rules, 0 off at 1046 open-gate fields: fifteen of the tame readout's twenty at p = 3, 5, x12 − 2, the six ramified quadratics, 512 Haar-random quartics and 512 Haar-random octics. Controls: the hand values at x2 − 2 (τ = 1, second point (3, 1) at weight 5) and x6 + 3x + 3, and the pair x6 + 3x3 + 3 against x6 + 6x3 + 3, whose digit w3 = 2 against 1 reads τ = 4 against 3 at δw = i* = 3.

The frequency of a jump set over random windows rule

Put the Haar measure on the coefficients of an Eisenstein polynomial of degree e over Q2; read modulo 2k it is the uniform measure on 2e(k−1)−1 coefficient cells, and a cell is pure when its 2e children at precision k + 1 share its jump set. Under that measure the relation vector's coordinate orders ba are independent with P(ba = b) = 2−b, so the frequency of a jump set is the Haar measure of the order vectors whose Pareto frontier it is, conditioned on the frontier's least weight being 2e (Pagano's mass formula, Theorems 1.7 and 1.8, this its instance). The population reproduces the law class by class at every precision read, every cell pure: at e = 2 three classes at 1/2, 1/4, 1/4, which Serre's masses over the six ramified quadratics give by hand; at e = 4 ten classes, [1, 5] with β = (3, 1) at 1/2 down to [1] alone at 1/128, the 48-quartic census's 24, 6, 6, 3, 3, 3 being the first six masses times 48; at e = 8 the law has 44 classes, [1, 9] with β = (4, 1) at 1/2 down to [1] at 2−20, and 8192 Haar-random octics sit within 2.3 standard deviations of their masses at every one of the 22 classes the sample resolves. The rung's own frequency is geometric: every level above 2e is one coordinate's weight at one order, so the rung is 2e + 1 + j with probability 2−(j+1), the event that its coordinate's order is exactly its value given that the levels below did not fire, up to the floor, which takes the rest: 1/2 to 1/32 at rungs 9 to 13 and 1/32 at 14 at e = 4, 1/2 to 2−11 at 17 to 27 and 2−11 at 28 at e = 8, where the sample's rung counts halve 4146, 2080, 997, 472 from rung 17.

Scope. Rule, exhaustive over the cells named: at e = 2 the 128 cells at precision 25 and 512 at 26, at e = 4 the 2048 cells at 24 and 32,768 at 25, purity read directly at the lower precision of each. The law itself is Pagano's theorem; the closed form at e = 8 is the recursion's, its support the admissible set enumerated from the axioms, and the octic population is an observation over 8192 seeded draws, chi-square 26.2 on 22 degrees of freedom. The rung's geometric law is a property of the mass law.

What these claims assemble to: the readout family is the jump set read through one unit. The module fixes which coordinates a class can choose and which the relation fixes for it; the digits of w say where the relation's frontier sits; and what a class lands on is the frontier under its own floor.
