Irreducibility

How much of a grown world's genesis is beyond recovery from its endpoint is an exact quantity here, and it has a critical temperature: one clock at every finite place, reading the place and blind to everything finer.

A world is grown by a growth law: a structural demand on the modulus and a greedy move, extend the modulus by the least m ≥ 2 the demand admits. Thermal selection softens the argmin: at temperature β > 1 an admissible move m is picked with probability proportional to m−β, and a state's normalizer Z is the sum of m−β over its admissible moves. Each demand realizes one of three fates; two are on stage here. Breadth seats every prime, and depth — the period λ of the modulus must grow at every move — collapses onto one prime's column, the state a power of that prime forever. A prime whose arrival leaves λ where it stood is transparent to the column, and the column's transparent family, or transparent set, is the set of such primes. Because the Boltzmann weights multiply along a history, the posterior over the routes a dated endpoint could have taken is exact and every bit of route information sits in the normalizers of the intermediate states. What that posterior retains of the generator is the ledger of the inside view; this page reads what it loses — the route entropy, the irrecoverable share of a genesis — as a function of temperature.

The critical clock

The critical clock observation

On the depth column write Zcol(β) for the limit of the state normalizers down the column: with T(β) the transparent mass, the sum of m−β over the transparent moves, Zcol = ζ(β) − 1 − T(β). Two quantities split at its root, the clock βcol. The per-move route entropy, the entropy of the route posterior per move in the long-history limit, is the binary entropy of the coin 1/(1 + Zcol): an analytic function of β, a smooth crossover rising to its maximum log 2 at the clock with zero slope. Per move there is no transition. What marks the clock is the genesis mode: the set of depths whose normalizer is below 1 is a tail of the column, and its occupation by the posterior's mode jumps 0 → 1 at the clock, a discontinuous order parameter. The onset depth a* — how deep the origin stays uncertain — walks the transparent family one prime at a time as β descends to the clock, because each depth a has a clock of its own, the root of Za(β) = 1, and these accumulate at βcol with gaps set by the next family prime q as q−βcol (measured slope −1.499 against −1.496 on the 3-column, the column of the prime 3; R2 = 0.99997), so a* diverges as Θ(ln 1/(β−βcol)) exactly iff the family is infinite. An interior clock exists iff the fate's normalizer keeps a nonzero limit: depth grows one prime forever and its limit is of order 1; breadth grows every prime, its normalizer ζ·Π(1 − p−β) − 1 over the primes already seated → 0 by the Euler product, and its clocks pile up only at the pole β = 1.

Scope. The rate's analyticity and the existence criterion are exact statements about the closed-form normalizers; the accumulation law is measured on the 3-column (βcol ≈ 1.496, the family 2·3i + 1); the divergence is conditional on that family's infinitude, which is open.

The riser law rule

On the 3-column the onset depth is an exact staircase: between the clock gaps of consecutive family primes it equals jn + 1, jn the depth at which the n-th family prime enters, and the riser points (ln 1/gap, jn + 1) lie on a line of slope 1/(βcol ln 3), measured 0.6084 against 0.6085. A fit on a grid blind to the family reads staircase geometry instead and oscillates without settling; under the family's gap heuristic no pointwise asymptotic slope exists at all.

Scope. Rule on the censused family 2·3i + 1, nine risers.

The transition is invisible to prediction memory rule

Flattened to a symbol stream, the genesis process past the transient is independent coin flips with one causal state, so the ε-machine suite of computational mechanics — statistical complexity, excess entropy, entropy rate — reads (0, 0, analytic) identically through the clock, and its reconstruction from samples finds one state at every temperature including the clock. The same samples carry the transition: the share of sampled moves landing in the deep tail crosses 1/2 at the clock to grid resolution. The transition lives in the endpoint-conditioned posterior's mode geometry, an object a stationary prediction-memory formalism does not carry.

Scope. Rule for the one-state suite, proved from the route posterior's factorization law; observation for the reconstruction (a CSSR-shaped rebuild at 4,000 strings of 64 symbols).

The place spectrum

The place spectrum observation

Every prime q — every finite place of Q — carries its own depth column qa and its own clock, and the clock reads the place. The column's transparent moves are the prime powers pj with p − 1 dividing (q−1)q∞, up to exponent vp(q−1) + 1 (one more at p = 2): 2 is transparent for every q, 3 for every odd q ≠ 3, 5 iff q ≡ 1 (mod 4), 7 iff q ≡ 1 (mod 3) or q = 3, and a prime is uniquely blind to its own column. The finite places of Q map into the interval (1, β*), β* = 1.7286 the root of ζ(β) = 2, one crossing each; a richer transparent set pulls the clock lower, so coarse residues order the spectrum. The top is the 2-adic place: the 2-column cancels its own powers and sees only the Fermat primes {3, 5, 17, 257, 65537}, the poorest family, so its clock 1.6045 is the maximum over the tested family, with 1.4959 at q = 3 the maximum among odd q. The critical temperature of irreducibility is a valuation-theoretic invariant: it reads which place the column returned, and the set of clocks is Ostrowski's finite places read thermodynamically.

Scope. Observation; the closed forms cross-checked against a brute enumeration of each state's admissible moves over the tested family of columns.

The cross-field clocks observation

The reading is a fact about global fields, not about Q. Over F2[x] the clock is algebraic: the characteristic-2 Frobenius makes a degree-d column's transparent set the subfield lattice of its residue field, every member at unbounded exponent, and the clock equation clears to a polynomial in 2−β — the degree-1 clock solves 2−β = 1 − 1/√2 exactly (βcol = 1.7716), the analogue of β* is 2 exactly, same-degree places share a clock, and the lattice orders the spectrum by transparent mass rather than member count. Over Z[√−5], where ideals and elements part (class number 2), the clock splits by the class group: the clock equation of the walk on elements is the ideal walk's averaged over the class-group characters, and at the 3-column, whose transparent candidates are all non-principal, the element clock 1.3527 sits below the ideal clock 1.5492, the pair's ratio at the pole reading the class number 2. In every tested field the highest clock sits at the place whose residue field has two elements — q = 2 in Q, degree 1 in F2(x), the prime over 2 in Z[√−5] at 1.7040, where the Fermat candidates 17, 257 and 65537 are inert and gated out by the field's quadratic character.

Scope. Observation at three global fields; the transparency structure over F2[x] is a rule brute-verified in range and the class-group identity's components are proved or cited.

The deep clock

A column at a place of any global field has a ramification index e, the number of times the place's own uniformizer divides the rational prime beneath it, and past a finite transient its period rises once every e deepenings — a tick. The column breathes when e ≥ 2 and is breathless at e = 1. The rule the ticks follow is the two-gear clock of the Observatory: the p-th power map on the field's units.

The breathing law rule

In mixed characteristic the period breathes with eventual period exactly e after a finite transient; in equal characteristic the breath widens forever. Between ticks the entrants — the primes arriving transparently from outside the column — stand still while the column's own contribution to the transparent set shrinks, so the normalizer cycles through exactly e values indexed by the headroom, the depths left before the next tick — its pre-tick value times the partial sums 1, 1 + p−β, 1 + p−β + p−2β, … through e terms — and the column carries e clocks, the roots those masses give. The deep clock is the root at the states sitting immediately before a tick, the bracket clock the root at the just-ticked states. Four values over Q(ζ8); one over Q, whose 2-column has e = 1.

Scope. Rule in range, from the two-gear clock, over eight columns: Q, Q(√2), Q(√−2), Q(i), Q(√−5), Q(ζ3), Q(ζ8), Q(√3); the pre-tick normalizer equals its limit past the last entrant threshold, so convergence is exact rather than asymptotic.

The clock factorization rule

The deep clock is blind to the transient. An entrant's exponent cap is divisibility-monotone in λ and λ never falls, so the limit transparent set is path-free, and the deep clock reads it alone. Four layers, each reading a different datum of the field. The value reads the limit set — which primes eventually arrive transparently and at what exponent, fixed by the field's splitting law and the period tables of its ramified places. Q(√3) and Q(ζ3) carry columns with the same local data (p, e, f) = (3, 2, 1) — prime, ramification index, residue degree — and opposite fine structure, yet their clocks 1.4475 and 1.3336 are separated by the transparent set alone — the first admits the single entrant 2, the second a long family of split primes — and transplanting either field's unit chain, the sequence of levels the p-power map steps through on its units, onto the other moves neither root; Q(√2) and Q(√−2) share one chain and land at 1.4861 against 1.6318, parting on whether 3 splits. Same chain, different clock; same set, same clock. The accumulation set reads e, by the breathing law. The phase reads torsion: whether the field contains a p-th root of unity flips which deepenings the pre-tick states fall on. And the finite-time transient alone reads the fine structure of the p-power map on the units — the arrival-graded object the Observatory measures — visible in the normalizer at finite depth and nowhere at the limit.

Scope. The blindness is proved from the two limit facts, given that a state's transparent moves are the divisors of one integer, and inherits the tier of the period tables it is computed over; the layer readings are a rule in range over the same eight columns, each deep clock computed three ways — through the true unit chain, through a chain with no fine structure, and at the limit set — agreeing to 10−9. Residue degree above 1 and the non-abelian cubics are outside the census.

The mode staircase

The mode staircase rule

On a breathing column the genesis mode does not jump once: it quantizes into e geometric steps, one at each of the column's e clocks, which are strictly ordered with the deep and bracket clocks outermost. The occupation climbs 0, 1/e, …, 1, beginning at the bracket clock and completing at the deep clock: the pre-tick states join the mode last. In a mixed-characteristic column the depths that have joined between consecutive clocks form a residue stripe mod e, and the stripe's phase reads the field's unit chain: the twins Q(√3) and Q(ζ3), whose clock values the chain provably cannot move, carry complementary stripes (even against odd), and transplanting either chain onto the other flips the stripe while every step's clock holds to 10−9. The mode's geography reads the torsion that all e clock values are blind to. Each step's onset depth walks that step's own transparent family: constant at √3, whose family is provably finite (a bounded, first-order-like jump); the Fermat entry thresholds at the 2-adic columns (over Q the onset climbs 4, 6, 10, 18, the depths at which 5, 17, 257 and 65537 enter, bounded past 65537 iff the Fermat set is finite); and ζ3's family 2·3i + 1 exactly, the onset linear in the family index. So whether a column's transition is a sharp jump or long-ranged is pinned to its family's finiteness — settled at √3, an open prime-family question at the others, with the standard heuristics putting the 2-adic place (Fermat sum convergent: a sharp jump) and the ζ3 column (family sum divergent: long-ranged) on opposite sides.

Scope. The step ordering is proved from the accumulation set; the quantization, the stripes and the onset walks are a rule in range over √3, ζ3, ζ8 and the 2-adic columns, the constant of the ζ3 walk — e times the 3-column's — measured to under a percent at both headroom classes by the riser law's rig; the transition order is settled where the family is provably finite and open where its finiteness is.

The breathing tax observation

The per-move route entropy stays analytic through every step — all e transitions are geometric — and its peak sits strictly below log 2 whenever e ≥ 2, by 3.3·10−3 at √3 up to 9.8·10−3 at ζ8: a peak at log 2 needs a fair per-move coin, and with the e clocks distinct at most one stripe's coin can sit at 1/2 at any one temperature. Perfect per-move irreducibility is exclusive to breathless columns.

Scope. Observation over the tested breathing columns; the exclusion argument is the staircase's ordering.

Irreducibility's critical points are Ostrowski's places across global fields, read thermodynamically. What the critical temperature keeps of the field beyond (p, e, f) is its splitting law, not its local torsion; the torsion survives one layer down, in the mode's stripes; and the transparent family's finiteness decides whether the transition it marks is sharp or long-ranged.
