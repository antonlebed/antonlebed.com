The dual pole

Floating point as the mirror deletion: one reading criterion for both poles, two ends of one numeration, and the measure the pole brings with it.

A rung of the primorial tower keeps every finite place of the integers and deletes the archimedean one, where size lives (The Object). The mirror deletion keeps only the archimedean place: an integer read through its sign, its size and finitely many leading digits. Its rung element is already in every machine. A floating-point number is a sign, an exponent and t leading digits, and its precision ladder mirrors the tower's window ladder. Both deletions embed the integers whole, so both ask one question with the poles swapped, the finite places on one side and the archimedean place on the other: what does a window, a nested grid of cells refining toward a point, compute of arithmetic at finite precision? A fiber is the set of inputs one cell of the window cannot tell apart, deep when the inputs carry more digits than the window reads. The dual window at base b and precision t reads a nonzero integer n as its sign, its exponent e = ⌊logb |n|⌋ and its t leading digits. Reading a map f at lookahead c means the output window at precision t is a function of the input window at precision t + c; at the trailing end, the low digits, the same notion reads f(n) mod bt from n mod bt+c, and at the leading end it reads leading digits from leading digits. Throughout, rad m is the product of the distinct primes dividing m, and vp(m) is the exponent of p in m. The geometry these criteria share with the windows beyond the positional pair is on Reading.

The mirror object

The fiber lemma property

The fibers of the dual window are sign-definite contiguous integer intervals of length exactly bmax(0, e+1−t), deep when the integer outruns the read, e + 1 > t. One fiber geometry per pole: a finite window's fibers are arithmetic progressions, the dual window's are intervals. Progressions hide size, intervals hide residue. No deep fiber decides a residue: n mod p is non-constant on every fiber of length at least 2, and the predicate [p | n] on every fiber longer than p. Below the prime's own scale the predicate can leak, the fiber [4, 6) deciding [7 | n] false, a shallow boundary the finite pole's size wall does not have.

Scope. Exhaustive at bases 2, 3 and 10 at precisions up to 4.

The dual hiding law rule

The bias of [n ≡ r (mod p)] away from 1/p on a deep fiber is exactly zero on every deep fiber iff p | b, and otherwise nonzero on every deep fiber, with the ceiling (p − 1)/(pL) attained at fiber length L. For a composite modulus m the count is exact iff m | bj at fiber depth j: perfect hiding of m needs rad m | rad b and depth at least maxp ⌈vp(m)/vp(b)⌉. The mirror of the finite pole's hiding lemma (Walls).

Scope. Derived, the ceiling attained at the scanned depths at bases 2, 3 and 10; the composite clause a rule at m ≤ 25 over bases 2, 6 and 10.

verifiers: explore_dual_pole.py, explore_dual_locality.py

The exchange of crown and blind spot property

A product's exponent is read from the operand exponents up to one, e(ab) − e(a) − e(b) ∈ {0, 1}. A difference's exponent is unbounded below: one deep fiber realizes difference exponents 0 to 5, both signs, and zero. So equality, the comparison of nearby values and the zero-test of a difference are unreadable at every finite precision. The finite pole's crown capability, the exact zero-test, a residue being zero or not in every window, is exactly this pole's blind spot; and this pole's native reads, size, sign and order, are exactly what the tower's deletion removes. The two poles exchange crown and blind spot. The blind spot is the logarithm's pole at zero, one wall at both poles: a window reading sign, exponent and leading digits is a log coordinate whose chart excludes zero, and a difference is the operation that reaches it. The finite pole's index coordinate is the same log shape and affords the pole exactly, log 0 = −∞ adjoined as a grade with the zero-test kept, where here the vanishing is a limit and prints as unreadability at every precision.

Scope. Property with witnesses, exhaustive at bases 2, 3 and 10 at precisions up to 4; the difference witness is one deep fiber of length 64 at base 2. Multiplication's exceptions at output precision 2 are confined to two adjacent output windows at every lookahead scanned, while subtraction's ambiguity width grows linearly in fiber depth: catastrophic cancellation is a width phenomenon, not a density one.

verifier: explore_dual_pole.py

The two-ends law rule

Base b's trailing digits read exactly the finite places p | b; its leading digits perfectly hide exactly the same places. One numeration, two ends: the same primes exactly readable at one end and exactly invisible at the other. Leading windows hide the finite places as trailing windows hide the archimedean one, pole for pole. The same radical condition runs the arithmetic in exchange. At the trailing end multiplication is free and division by v is gated by rad v | rad b; at the leading end division is free and multiplication by u is gated by rad u | rad b. One condition on opposite parts of the fraction: each end freely reads the operation whose carry flow points away from it.

Scope. The hiding half is derived and attained at the scanned depths (bases 2, 3 and 10); the exchange is derived and swept at bases 2, 6 and 10.

verifiers: explore_dual_pole.py, explore_dual_locality.py

Each pole prices in its own metric property

Where a price survives the exchange, dividing by v costs the trailing end maxp ⌈vp(v)/vp(b)⌉ digits of lookahead, a maximum over places, while multiplying by u costs the redundant leading end ⌈Σp vp(u) logb p + margin⌉, a sum over places, the product formula's own sum. The ultrametric window prices by the maximum, the archimedean window by the sum: the max/sum split is Ostrowski's dichotomy between the two triangle inequalities, the pricing face of the two orders a rung carries (Walls). What one pole gates by a radical the other prices by a size. The conservation law between the windows is the product formula itself, whose archimedean term the tower's deletion removes; the deleted term's value group is the whole budget, so the finite price sheet carries no residual conservation law (The division algebras).

Scope. An identity, measured at the scanned systems.

The reading criterion

The two-pole reading criterion rule

A window reads f at bounded lookahead iff f is Lipschitz at cell scale in the window's own metric and the image of each deep input cell fits one output cell. At cell scale means the Lipschitz ratio is measured between cells, an image cell's diameter against its input cell's, never between points, and the coarseness is load-bearing: the readable class contains permutations of the digits below the top one whose pointwise ratios are unbounded. At ball windows, whose cells are metric balls as the trailing end's cosets are, cell scale and pointwise coincide. The fitting clause is where windows differ, and it follows from the cell family's Lebesgue number, the largest radius at which every ball of that radius sits inside some cell:

The lookahead unifies as logb(Lipschitz constant) plus a window margin: 0 at ultrametric windows, where cells nest and the bound is exact; logb(2a/(2a − b + 1)) at redundant covers with digits {−a..a}, where the committed stream departs from the bound by at most one digit in either direction, a redundant cell's children overlapping inside it but leaving no margin at its edges; and infinite, a wall, at misaligned non-redundant reads. The wall corpus is the criterion's negative space: residue reads at the leading end and the order reads are discontinuous in the log metric, and catastrophic cancellation is subtraction's log-metric blowup at its zero locus. Bounded lookahead is a metric notion and not a topological one: extracting the even-position digits is uniformly continuous and readable at no bounded lookahead.

Scope. The four regimes verified under the one statement: the trailing pair at bases 2, 6 and 10 with collision witnesses below each threshold, the leading pair by the alignment sweep at the same bases and the redundant-cover scans; the cell-scale sharpening and the stream correction by their own rigs. Component tiers are the blocks below; the slope delay is priced exactly on Redundant arithmetic.

Readability is Lipschitz at the trailing end criterion

Trailing readability at lookahead c is, verbatim, db(f n, f n′) ≤ bc db(n, n′), and the minimal lookahead is exactly max(0, ⌈logb Lipb(f)⌉). Multiplication by any m is nonexpanding, free at c = 0. ⌊n/v⌋ has Lipschitz constant b to the power maxp ⌈vp(v)/vp(b)⌉, finite iff rad v | rad b: the trailing division gate and its composite depth threshold are b-adic Lipschitz facts, and a rough denominator is non-Lipschitz with growing witnesses at every depth. Shifts are isometries, so the phases ⌊(n + s)/v⌋ ride free.

Scope. Elementary, verified at bases 2, 6 and 10 with collision witnesses below every threshold.

verifier: explore_dual_lipschitz.py

The coset rigidity criterion

Digits {−a..a} with 2a + 1 > b are redundant, a value having many spellings; with 2a + 1 = b they are balanced and non-redundant. A redundant prefix read least-significant-first has completions that realize the full coset V + btZ, and cosets of one subgroup are equal or disjoint, so no digit set buys the trailing end a positive overlap. The trailing criterion holds verbatim under redundancy, the division gate survives it, and redundancy is a leading-end-only door.

Scope. A two-line proof; the completeness lemma verified at both scanned redundant systems, every element and every residue class.

verifier: explore_dual_lipschitz.py

An arithmetic function is gated by its own order tower rule

2n is trailing-readable at base 10 with minimal lookahead 1 on the saturated range n ≥ t, because the order of 2 mod 5t, 4·5t−1, divides 10t+1; below saturation the 2-column still ramps, the fiber lemma's shallow leak in mirror. The same function is trailing-dead at base 3, whose radical lacks the factor 2 the order tower carries, and leading-dead at any redundancy, a depth-4 fiber spanning four output exponents. Conversely |x| is trailing-dead, its low digits needing the sign, and a two-slope monotone piecewise map is trailing-dead at every base while redundant-leading local at its tail slope's delay: piecewise structure is free at the scale-confined end and fatal at the all-scales end.

Scope. Rules at the scanned precisions and digit sets.

The leading end

Every scaling x ↦ (u/v)x is an isometry of the log metric, so at a non-redundant leading window Lipschitz is necessary and never sufficient: the whole gate is the fitting clause, which here is alignment, whether the image of every deep cell sits inside one cell of the output grid. In floating-point vocabulary, exactly window-local means correctly truncatable from bounded extra precision, the bounded case of the table maker's dilemma. The witnesses are constructed and never swept: a monotone map is exception-free at a scale iff every input point where it crosses an output-grid line is itself grid-aligned there, and a misaligned crossing exists inside every period-length run of boundaries.

The numerator criterion rule

⌊(u/v)n⌋ with gcd(u, v) = 1 is exactly window-local iff rad u | rad b, and the denominator is free: division by any v is exact at every base, long division emitting digits most-significant-first from a bounded remainder, while multiplication's carries flow against the read. The radical law for c·x is the v = 1 case, and the same condition governs the trailing division gate and perfect hiding: one condition, three laws, both ends of the numeration.

Scope. Rule at bases 2, 6 and 10, by the boundary-preimage method above.

verifiers: explore_dual_locality.py, explore_dual_pole.py

The phase-shape law rule

Each non-redundant window's one free rounding phase is its own cell shape. At standard digit sets floor is free and ceiling and round-to-nearest are walled, ceiling minus floor being a residue read the fiber lemma forbids. At balanced digit sets round-to-nearest is free at lookahead 0 for every scanned odd denominator and every other phase class is walled; even denominators are tie-walled there, the ties of n/2 sitting on cell boundaries. The numerator gate is phase-invariant.

Scope. Rule at the scanned bases, denominators and digit sets.

verifiers: explore_dual_lipschitz.py, explore_dual_locality.py

The shift and curvature walls rule

n + a is graded, exceptions at every depth, for every a ≠ 0: the crossing at M·bJ − a is misaligned at every depth past vb(a). n2 and n3 are graded at every lookahead. No additive or curved structure survives the non-redundant leading read. On the scale circle addition is log-nonexpanding, so its grading is alignment's; subtraction's ratio blows up at its cancellation locus, a Lipschitz failure.

Scope. Rules at bases 2, 6 and 10, with constructed witnesses.

verifiers: explore_dual_locality.py, explore_reading_geometry.py

The structural split property

The exactly local class strictly exceeds arithmetic: an XOR mask below the top bit at base 2, and a digit permutation below the top digit at base 10, are exactly local at lookahead 0 and non-monotone. Local means fiber-cellular; the arithmetic members across every swept family, scalings, phases, shifts and powers, are exactly the numerator family. Locality and algebraicity split at this pole, where the finite pole's coincide, channel-local being polynomial there (Walls).

Scope. Property with witnesses; that the arithmetic members are exactly the numerator family is conjectured beyond the swept families.

The redundant purchase

Reading through a redundant digit set is the redundant purchase: the window partition becomes an overlapping cover, the fitting clause is bought, and behind that one purchase the wall table inverts. Every rational slope reads at bounded lookahead, both radical gates dissolving; addition reads at lookahead 1 or 2; negation is digitwise; pair multiplication and squaring run at the literature's delay. The curvature wall and the shift wall dissolve together. Why the dissolution is wholesale here is a fact about rates: a positional window refines by the same factor at every level, so input and output scales always keep step and alignment was the only gate, where a window whose refinement rate is set by the orbit it reads, the continued-fraction window, is cured of walls and never of rates by the same purchase (Reading).

The prefix-interval law rule

Under redundant digits a t-digit prefix of value V followed by j more digits has completions forming the full interval V·bj ± a·Rj, Rj the j-digit repunit, with Lebesgue count 2a·Rj − bj + 2; the non-redundant reading is its degeneration at Lebesgue number 0. At radix 2 with digits {−1, 0, 1} the number of spellings of n at width t is Stern's diatomic sequence at 2t − |n|, so the window's own string measure weights values unequally and is not counting measure. At integer base with a ≤ b − 1 zero has only the all-zero spelling, so every near-zero wall below is built from near-zero prefixes and never from a nontrivial zero.

Scope. Rule at five systems, depths 1 to 3; the Stern law a rule at its one system.

The hedge rule

|x| is exactly local at lookahead 0 at both scanned redundant systems while sign stays open on the all-zero prefix at every depth: the window computes the sign-symmetric map without ever reading the sign, hedging with the same near-zero output prefixes that wall division. Continuity beats information.

Scope. Rule at the two scanned redundant systems.

What the purchase spends rule

The order reads, sign, equality, comparison and reading one's own exponent, are non-local at every lookahead behind the purchase. But sign's non-locality is a signed-digit window's and not redundancy's: a balanced non-redundant window, which folds the sign into the digits instead of storing it as its own coordinate, has it already, and what that wall is made of is The order wall. What redundancy itself costs is three things. The order-blur law: both orders are realizable for prefix values V, V′ iff |V − V′|·bj < 2a·Rj, an ambiguity of three cells where the non-redundant window's is one, the equality wall widened to an order wall. No in-window normalization: the prefix V = 1 straddles the exponent boundary at every depth. And the redundancy leak: exact hiding degrades to a bias that is zero iff p | 2a + 1, vanishing as b−j under counting measure and constant under the string measure. A prime keeps its hiding only where it divides the digit count, 5 at radix 10 with digits {−7..7} and 3 at radix 9 with {−4..4}; the digit count being odd, every even base leaks the parity the non-redundant window hides exactly. Bare stream division stays non-local, a near-zero divisor prefix admitting quotients of both signs and unbounded ratio, and the archimedean side-certificate |D| ≥ Dmin reopens it: the one archimedean bit the redundant window cannot manufacture. Redundancy is the archimedean deletion performed a second time, inside the archimedean window: every operation with bounded carry inflow walks through, and what the purchase spends is archimedean data.

Scope. Rules at both scanned redundant systems, the order-blur law an iff; sign's non-locality on either side of the purchase exhaustive at the widths run over seven redundant digit sets and two balanced ones.

The pole's own measure

The criterion above is a yes-or-no about a whole map. Short of it the pole's laws are graded: they report how often a read fails, an exception density over fibers, and a density is not a number until a measure is named. At the finite pole counting always served unnamed, and the Benford block says why it could. Fix a multiplier a, output precision t, lookahead l and operand depth j; call the t leading digits read as a scaled number the mantissa. A fiber is exceptional when its image under n ↦ an straddles a line of the output grid, so that the read is not determined. Write α = ⌊logb a⌋ and let θ ∈ {0, 1} record whether the mantissa product carries: the output grid is coarser than the fiber by exactly bΛ, Λ = l + α + θ. θ splits the mantissa range into two carry classes, and every count below is taken over fibers inside one class; the single fiber straddling the boundary is an edge term, not a law.

The period law rule

Over any bΛ consecutive class-interior fibers at depth j the number of exceptional ones is exactly d ⌊a(bj − 1)/(bjd)⌋ with d = gcd(a, bΛ), independent of where the period starts, and equal to a − d on deep fibers, bjd > a. The count vanishes iff d = a, which is rad a | rad b together with Λ ≥ maxp ⌈vp(a)/vp(b)⌉: the numerator criterion and the composite depth threshold, read on Λ, are the vanishing locus of one density formula. The classification and the grading are not two facts, the classification being where the grading is zero.

Scope. Rule, exhaustive over 219 scopes: bases 2, 6 and 10, multipliers coprime, mixed and radical, lookaheads 1 to 3, depths 1, 2 and 4, both carry classes, three period phases each; all 48 zero-locus cells coincide with the criterion.

verifier: explore_dual_measure.py

The counting collapse rule

Mixing the two carry classes with their counting weights cancels α. On deep fibers, for a coprime to b, the exception density under counting measure is exactly (1 − 1/a)/bl: the prefactor forgets the base and the multiplier's size, keeping only its reciprocal, and b survives solely as the cell count per unit of lookahead.

Scope. A deep-fiber statement: a shallow fiber, bj < a, sits at its own mixed law, which the period law still covers. Measured exact at bases 2, 6 and 10.

verifier: explore_dual_measure.py

Benford is the dual Haar property

The mantissa space of the dual rung is the scale circle R>0/bZ, a compact group, and log-uniform mass, Benford's law, is its Haar measure, the only scale-invariant one. That makes the mirror between the poles exact one level below the arithmetic. At the finite pole the ambient group is additive Z, the rung is Z/N, Haar is counting measure, and the reading cells are cosets, every cell of equal Haar, which is why no density at the finite pole ever needed a measure named. Here the reading cells are digit windows and they are not cosets: cell m carries Haar logb((m + 1)/m). Benford's law is that grid/Haar mismatch, and the finite pole's measure-blindness is the degenerate luxury of a coset reading partition. The same mismatch at the continued-fraction window is the Gauss–Kuzmin law (Reading).

Read under its own measure the pole reports what counting cannot. The density of the same exception sets becomes (1 − 1/a) R(x)/bl, where x = {logb a} and R(x) = bx−1(1 + (b − 1)(1 − x)), a hump with R(0) = R(1) = 1 and R > 1 strictly between, and a multiplier coprime to b never lands on those endpoints: Benford sees strictly more exceptions and carries the multiplier's mantissa exactly where the counting prefactor forgot it. The same x runs the carry layer: under the pole's own measure the scaling carry θ occurs with probability exactly x, where counting gives a rational function of a and b, and two operands' mantissa product carries with probability exactly 1/2 at every base, the class boundary being a straight line in log coordinates, where counting gives a base-entangled transcendental.

Scope. Property; the Haar statement is classical and the mirror is what is claimed. Scale invariance is checked in exact rational arithmetic with no logarithms, the preimage of a window cell under y ↦ qy telescoping to (m + 1)/m, and counting measure fails the same check exactly, which is the control. The Benford density and both carry laws are rules, converged at every scanned case.

verifier: explore_dual_measure.py

The pair limits rule

Two operands multiplied together at common lookahead c and depth j give a counting density converging to 2(1 − b−j)/bc in the unsaturated regime 2b1−c ≤ 1, which excludes c = 1 at every base, where the image interval covers a whole grid period and the density saturates instead. The limit is base-free, each operand contributing b−c and the two adding, so lookahead spends as an additive budget. The Benford limit is the same times (b − 1)2/(b ln2 b), which equals the mean of the hump R over x uniform: two derivations of one constant. A pair is exceptional iff the product's low digits sit within a near-miss of the grid, one test per pair, and it reproduces the measured densities as exact integer counts.

Scope. Rule; the limits measured to about 10−4 at the precisions swept, the pair test exact on every validated pair.

verifier: explore_dual_measure.py

The arithmetic the purchase buys

Behind the redundant cover ⌊(u/v)n + φ⌋ reads at a delay given by a closed form, proved for every radix, every symmetric redundant digit set, every rational slope and every phase, and the states that decide a read are counted by closed forms rather than walked, so what would be a search over a whole window becomes a single inequality. Addition goes local at lookahead 1 or 2 under a necessary and sufficient condition on how many digits the set carries beyond the radix and how far it reaches on either side of zero, and the window margin above overpays on a wedge that only asymmetric digit sets separate from a surplus of two digits. All of it is on Redundant arithmetic.

The order wall

What the exchange leaves non-local is not shapeless. Sign factors: a most-significant-first scan into two absorbing verdicts, whose minimal state count is known exactly for every finite digit set with a member of each sign, and whose transition monoid, above the ideal of maps that have decided, is a nilpotent algebra with a composition law and no machine in it. The wall sits entirely in the product, finite-state and not finite-window at fixed width. All of it is on The order wall.

Each deletion reads exactly what is continuous in the geometry it keeps: one numeration carries both verdicts, read exactly and hide exactly, at its two ends.
