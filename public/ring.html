The Ring

Z/510,510 is the product of the first seven primes. Everything on this site follows from its definition. This page is the definition.

The factorization

510,510 = 2 × 3 × 5 × 7 × 11 × 13 × 17

By the Chinese Remainder Theorem, this ring decomposes into 7 independent channels:

Z/510510 ≅ Z/2 × Z/3 × Z/5 × Z/7 × Z/11 × Z/13 × Z/17

Every number n in the ring maps to a unique 7-tuple of residues. This map is a bijection. Arithmetic in the full ring equals independent arithmetic in each channel. Every channel is a prime field — no zero divisors, every nonzero element invertible.

Four data channels carry information. Three parity channels provide error correction (rate 4/7). The code is maximum distance separable: any 4 of 7 channels reconstruct the original. No [7,4] code can do better.

Why these primes

The seven primes are the first seven consecutive primes: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17. They are uniquely determined by a single construction:

Start with seed 2. Apply the Cunningham map c(n) = 2n + 1 to generate two chains, each terminating when the map hits a composite:

From 2: 2 → 5 → 11 → 23 → 47 → 95 (stop: 95 = 5×19)
From 1: 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 (stop: 15 = 3×5)

The extension primes 13 = 2² + 3² and 17 = 2 + 3 + 5 + 7 close the set. Why 2 is the only possible seed: any prime > 2 is odd, so the next integer is even and ≥ 4 — composite. The only even prime is 2. Everything else follows.

The tower

The seven primes build a tower of rings. Each level adds one prime, one channel. Every channel at every level is a prime field.

Lambda (λ) is the Carmichael function — the universal period of the power map. At the full ring: λ = lcm(1, 2, 4, 6, 10, 12, 16) = 240. Adding prime 11 jumps λ from 12 to 60 (its φ(11) = 10 contributes a new factor of 5). Adding 17 jumps 60 to 240 (its φ(17) = 16 contributes a factor of 4). Prime 13 changes nothing: φ(13) = 12 already divides 60.

Fattening

Raising exponents beyond 1 (Z/8 instead of Z/2, Z/49 instead of Z/7) increases resolution per channel at a cost: channels become rings, not fields. Zero divisors appear. The ECC minimum distance drops from 4 (optimal) to 3.

Raised-exponent rings trade field structure for larger residue spaces. The all-exponent-1 ring is the mathematical foundation; raising exponents is a specialization for applications that need more resolution per channel.
