The Ring

Z/214,414,200 is an integer ring constructed from seven primes. Everything on this site follows from its definition. This page is the definition.

The factorization

214,414,200 = 23 × 32 × 52 × 72 × 11 × 13 × 17

By the Chinese Remainder Theorem, this ring decomposes into 7 independent channels:

Z/214414200 ≅ Z/8 × Z/9 × Z/25 × Z/49 × Z/11 × Z/13 × Z/17

Every number n in the ring maps to a unique 7-tuple of residues. This map is a bijection. Arithmetic in the full ring equals independent arithmetic in each channel.

Why these primes

The seven primes are the first seven consecutive primes: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17. They are uniquely determined by a single construction:

Start with the seed D = 2. Apply the Cunningham map c(n) = 2n + 1 to generate two chains. Both chains self-terminate when the map produces a composite:

CC1(2): 2 → 5 → 11 → 23 → 47 → 95 (stop: 95 = 5×19)
CC1(1): 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 (stop: 15 = 3×5)

The extension primes 13 = 2² + 3² and 17 = 2 + 3 + 5 + 7 close the set. The uniqueness proof: if D > 2 is prime, D is odd, so K = D+1 is even and ≥ 4 — composite. The only even prime is 2, forcing D = 2. Everything else follows.

Why these exponents

The exponents {3, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1} are Pareto-optimal and uniquely forced by two independent routes that agree:

  • 23 = 8 — the Klein four-group V&sub4; lives only at 2³. Going to 2&sup4; costs ring size with no structural gain.
  • 32 = 9 — lambda(3³) = 18 does not divide 420. Exponent 2 is the maximum compatible with the heartbeat.
  • 52 = 25 — lambda(5³) = 100 does not divide 420. Same constraint.
  • 72 = 49 — lambda(7³) = 294 does not divide 420. Same constraint.
  • 11, 13, 17 — extension primes enter at exponent 1.

The Carmichael lambda of the full ring: λ = lcm(2, 6, 20, 42, 10, 12, 16) = 1680. The heartbeat at the DATA level: λ(Z/210) = 12.
