Digit shifts

Moving every digit of a string up by a fixed stride, and which of those moves a bounded reader can follow.

A window is a nested grid of cells refining toward a point, and it reads a map at lookahead c when the output cell at depth t is a function of the input cell at depth t + c. The windows here are the Ostrowski ones: for irrational α = [0; a1, a2, …] the digits of n write n = Σ bk qk over the denominators of α's continued-fraction convergents (q0 = 1, q1 = a1, qk = ak qk−1 + qk−2, the ak being α's partial quotients), and the depth-t cell of n is the set of nonnegative integers agreeing with n on their t low-order digits — cells partition at each depth and nest across depths. Legality — what makes the writing unique — caps b0 at a1 − 1 and bk at ak+1, and forces a zero below any digit sitting at its cap. What this family is, and which arithmetic maps its windows read — the arithmetic gate, where multiplication and division are held to no bounded lookahead while the unit action is not — are on Quadratic windows.

Where α is a quadratic irrational its quotient sequence is eventually periodic, and a window fixes more than one repeating length: each block below says which one it reads against. And the agreement depth of a pair of inputs is the number of low-order digits they share, which is what a scan reports when it asks how deep two inputs can agree while their images differ.

The maps read here are the shifts: Lr sends n = Σ bk qk to Σ bk qk+r, moving every digit up by a stride r. Shifting by one period of the quotient sequence multiplies asymptotically by the fundamental unit ε of Z[α]; a stride that is not a multiple of the period is nobody's unit action, so the shifts are a family of questions and not one.

A shifted string need not be legal. Digit bk arrives at position k + r, whose cap is ak+r+1, where legality promised only bk ≤ ak+1. Where the quotient sequence is non-decreasing along the stride nothing can overflow, the shift is the bare coordinate map, and it reads at lookahead 0. Otherwise the illegal string is renormalized — the repair — and the converse fails: a shift whose repair fires on almost every input can still read at bounded lookahead. So there are three verdicts, and which (window, stride) pair gets which is the question: lookahead 0, bounded, and gated — no one lookahead serving every depth, the deepest witness growing with the range scanned.

The stride law rule

On the designed family [0; (1, …, 1, a)∞] — one large quotient a per period of length P, every other quotient 1 — the verdict is a function of r mod P alone, and carries no dependence on a whatever: r ≡ 0 is the period shift and reads at lookahead 0; r mod P even and nonzero reads at bounded lookahead; r mod P odd gates. Unanimous, no exceptions. Read off P = 2, 3, 4, 5, it then calls all 34 (window, stride) pairs at P = 6 and 7 — periods no fitted window carried, and where r mod P = 6 is the first even residue above 4 anywhere in the family. And it is not a fit: e − 2 = [0; 1, 2, 1, 1, 4, 1, 1, 6, …] — outside the family, and its continued fraction never eventually periodic — is the P = 3 pattern with a growing large quotient, and obeys the P = 3 row at every stride to 8. Two rivals die on the way: parity of r itself fails at e − 2's r = 4, even and gated, and the criterion "gated iff gcd(r, P) = 1" fits every pair through P = 4 and is parted at P = 5, whose residues 2 and 4 are coprime to 5 and bounded.

The mechanism is derived on the gated side and decided on the bounded. Put θk = qkα − pk: a string's value mod 1 is its sum Σ dkθk, two legal strings share a value exactly when their sums differ by an integer, and the values with two codings are the cuts −sα, s ≥ 1. With the quotients periodic the recurrence gives θk+P = ηθk, η a unit of the lattice ℤ + ℤα with |η| < 1 and its conjugate above 1. A shifted input's value is the shifted sum Σ bkθk+r, so a stride gates exactly when such sums, over inputs agreeing ever deeper, converge to a cut from both sides — and every odd residue carries an explicit comb, a periodic digit pattern written in closed form rather than searched, whose image limit lands on one. At even P every even position has cap 1, so the recurrence telescopes the comb of 1s at the odd positions, shifted by any odd r, to exactly −θr — the cut −qrα — and raising the comb's deepest tooth on the large-quotient class from 1 to 2 adds a term of opposite sign strictly larger than the remaining deficit, so the pair straddles: image values on both sides of the cut, forcing the images apart at a fixed position while input agreement climbs without bound. At odd P the comb of 1s at the even positions with 2 at the class has its shifted sum collapse onto a lattice multiple of 1 − η at every odd r, so the image limit sits in the lattice; its conjugate exceeds 2 while its absolute value stays below 1, which forces it onto a cut, with s = qr+1 at every cell read — and η < 0 alternates the truncation deficit's sign, so consecutive truncations of the comb itself straddle.

The bounded side is a decision procedure, not a scan. A string whose value lands on a cut owes, at each depth, a deficit — the lattice point its remaining tail must sum to — and legality boxes the walk of deficits: between exact tail extremes in the real embedding, positive and geometrically bounded in the conjugate. The walk therefore lives on a finite graph, and a stride gates exactly when that graph carries a cycle through a state strictly inside its tail interval — proved in both directions, quantifying over all strings and all periods at once, aperiodic strings included. At 105 cells — P = 2..8, a = 2..4, every stride — the verdict lands on the stride law with no exceptions, interior cycles at the odd residues only. And the cycles are classified: a cycle is a periodic legal pattern whose tail value lands in the lattice, and at all 57 even-residue cells the cycle set equals the comb orbits exactly. At even P that is one comb alone — 1s at the even positions — whose value is the cut −qr−1α approached from one side only, every cycle state sitting at its tail interval's extreme: a touch and never a straddle. At odd P and even nonzero residue there is no cycle at all, every lattice hit a finite string's exact value. And the r ≡ 0 column carries its own endpoint cycles at both parities — the window's classical telescopes, all caps on one parity of position, values 1 and −α — and its classification is a theorem at every purely periodic window, not a certificate: the period shift is the bare coordinate map, so no interior cycle exists, and an endpoint suffix pins a periodic pattern to the telescope, the extreme's only other coding terminating where a nonzero periodic pattern cannot. Every residue reduces to that base: consecutive convergents are a unimodular basis of the lattice, so the residue-r condition at any window is the r-rotated window's own r = 0 condition on digits that keep the original caps, with the same unit η — the class of positions carrying the large quotient misaligned by r. What decides a cell over all periodic patterns is lattice avoidance — that no periodic legal pattern beyond the comb and the zero pattern lands the lattice — and its even-P half is a theorem at every even P: every even nonzero residue, every a ≥ 2, all period multiples at once. The chain is four exact steps and consumes legality exactly once. A pattern of period mP lands the lattice only where one digit-weighted sum is a multiple of one explicit integer, the index of (1 − ηm) times the lattice; positive multiples die on a telescope; negative multiples die on the odd-support theorem — once the residue is even the large-quotient slots all sit at odd positions, so each even position charges its odd right neighbour, whose weight dominates and which legality leaves a unit of cap, making the legal cyclic maximum of the sum exactly its odd columns' total — and that total sits below the index at every cell and every period multiple, its step from one multiple to the next a single closed form in the block's convergents, positive by three elementary lemmas; and at zero, one identity collapses membership to a divisibility only the zero pattern and the comb satisfy. Legality is load-bearing exactly at the charging step: drop the forced zero after a capped digit and small patterns sum to exactly θr. The odd-P half closes deeper. The chain transports with one remainder — at odd P the slots carrying the large cap land on an even position once per two periods, and the charge keeps a positive part of each such slot's overflow — yet the total still sits below the index at every even period multiple, its two-period step telescoping in the block's convergents; the positive-multiple telescope turns strict, beating the maximum with room to spare; and at zero the divisibility admits nothing — where the even-P census leaves the comb, the strict bound leaves no nonzero pattern at all. An odd multiple rides the even ones, a pattern of period mP being one of period 2mP with the same tail value. So at odd P and even nonzero residue no nonzero legal periodic pattern of any period lands the lattice — both parities' halves are theorems, and with the cycle criterion the no-gate verdict at odd P's even nonzero residues is a theorem at every cell.

The step-of-two reading survives as the mechanism's gloss. At a position whose quotient is a the denominator recurrence reads a·qk = qk+1 − qk−1, a rewrite spanning two positions, so a repair walks the digit lattice in steps of two, and an even residue's walk can return to the class carrying the large quotients where an odd one's cannot — the same step-of-two recursion the greedy string of qK − 1 runs on (the down-borrow, the maximal string). The naive interpretation — the cap above the landing site where an up-carry would need room — stays refuted at 29 pairs, five at the cubic window ∛2 − 1 and twenty-four across the designed family. One thing the law settles behind it: the repair's reach, the lowest position any repair changes, does not decide the verdict but is exactly the lookahead-0 floor — every gated stride's excess-lookahead column, the per-depth record of how much further than its depth a reader must see (the object of The lookahead limit), has its first nonzero entry one position above it, at all 32 pairs.

The parting law rule

Where a comb family lands on the cut −sα, the position at which the straddling pair's images part is fixed by the cut alone: the two codings of −sα differ first at ps = min{p : qp+1 ≥ max(s, 2)}, read on the window's own denominators. The two derived families give two closed forms. At odd P the cut has s = qr+1, and on the designed family ps = r: the parting is the stride itself. At even P the cut has s = qr, parting pqr — 1, 2, 4 at r = 1, 3, 5 on the designed windows. And the two forms are the two recorded aperiodic parting signatures. The one-class windows — aperiodic windows whose quotients above 1 keep to one residue class — of the prefix closures part at exactly the stride — the odd-P form, their class period 3 being odd — while e − 2, one-class mod 3 like them, parts at 1, 3, 6 at strides 1, 4, 7, which is pqr read on its own denominators: the even-P form. Why a window of odd class period takes the even form is not settled; the law reduces the question to which cut an aperiodic ladder's family straddles.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: the parting sits at exactly ps on every certificate printed — twelve odd-P cells across eleven truncation depths, parting r from the first scanned depth, and twelve even-P cells across eight depths, parting pqr — and the aperiodic parting vectors are the certified ladders' own records. The formula is read off the cuts the derived families land on, not proved for an arbitrary cut.

The shifts sit beside the arithmetic gate rather than under it. Where the continued fraction is periodic the shift by one period and multiplication by ε are the same map, so no window in the family can say which of the two its reading was ever about — and off that stride the shift is nobody's unit action and, on the designed family, reads at bounded lookahead anyway at every nonzero even residue of the stride mod the period of its large quotients.

The limit of the lookahead

What decides a shift is the stride read against a length the window itself fixes, and the excess a reader needs past its depth is itself a column with an exact limit: at every purely periodic window the shift is a finite automaton and the column's limit is computed per cell, the graded window's half-period stride gating exactly where its two large quotients differ; a run of nonzero entries outlasting the period reads a verdict off one table, with one calibration constant doing inductive work at the windows with no period; and there, wherever the large quotients keep to one residue class, the window's own tiled prefixes answer — the class plays the period — while the cubic window ∛2 − 1 refuses every instrument read. The widened output

The finite automaton of the limit of the lookahead, run as a game with the digits the reader may WRITE widened past legality while the input stays legal, separates the gate's two refusals, ×m's and ⌊n/m⌋'s, which are not held for the same reason. ×m's is the writing's: raise any output cap and the map has a bounded reader at every irrational window. ⌊n/m⌋'s is the map's and survives however far the alphabet is widened. And an integer input, which must finish, costs that reader lookahead of its own — never one digit, exactly none where the widening writes m dk digit by digit and at least two everywhere else, a rule at every periodic window — with the whole account on The widened output.
