Digit shifts

Moving every digit of a string up by a fixed stride, and which of those moves a bounded reader can follow.

A window is a nested grid of cells refining toward a point, and it reads a map at lookahead c when the output cell at depth t is a function of the input cell at depth t + c; cmin(t) is the least lookahead that works at depth t, its values over t a map's column, and a map is gated when no bounded lookahead works at all (Reading). The windows here are the Ostrowski ones: for irrational α = [0; a1, a2, …] with convergents pk/qk, the digits of n write n = Σ bk qk greedily, legality capping bk at the partial quotient ak+1 (b0 at a1 − 1) and forcing a zero below any digit at its cap; the depth-t grid cell of n is the integers agreeing with n on their t low-order digits. What this family is, and which arithmetic maps its windows read — the arithmetic gate, where multiplication and division are held to no bounded lookahead while the unit action is not — are on Quadratic windows. A string is read through its star Σ bk θk, θk = qkα − pk the convergent remainder, |θ| falling geometrically with alternating signs; the completion is an odometer whose points code circle points, and its cut points — the circle points with two codings — are −sα for s ≥ 1.

A shift by stride r sends every digit bk to position k + r, and its image point is the shifted pseudo-star Σ bk θk+r. Where α is quadratic its quotients are eventually periodic, and the shift by one period is the action of the fundamental unit of α's order; a stride that is not a multiple of the period is nobody's unit action. The designed family is the one-class window [0; (1, …, 1, a)∞], one large quotient a per period of length P, the position carrying it the class slot, and a cell is a (window, stride) pair, written (P, a, r) on that family. A scan whose deepest witness tracks the range it imposes rather than settling is cap-shaped; the agreement depth of two inputs is the number of low-order digits they share.

Off periodicity

The odometer is not the unit shape rule

By Lagrange the quadratic irrationals are exactly the α with eventually periodic continued fraction, so every quadratic window carries a shift action for a fundamental unit to be. Off that case there is none. At three non-quadratic windows — ∛2 − 1 (cubic), e − 2, and the window [0; 2, 4, 8, 16, …] whose alphabet explodes — the completion is an odometer again: n + 1 reads at delay ≤ 1 with that delay constant as the range grows tenfold, while 2n, 3n and ⌊n/2⌋ are gated with the witness depth tracking the data cap, as the tear at a grid cell's endpoint says they must be at any irrational α (Completions). The cubic isolates the axis: Q(∛2) has unit rank 1, so units exist in abundance and none acts — ε·n is not an integer, and the window's maps are Z → Z — and on the arithmetic maps it behaves exactly like the transcendental window. The gate's mechanism never used the period: normalizing an overflowing digit rewrites through qk+1 − qk−1, an up carry with a down-borrow attached, from the recurrence alone.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope, exhaustive to 3·105 per window, the first two windows' quotients certified by interval arithmetic over exact rationals.

The stride decides, with the unit group nowhere in it rule

The quadratic rows shift by the period, which is why the question only ever looked like one question; readability is a property of the cell. The sufficient half is mechanism and measurement agreeing: where the quotient sequence is non-decreasing along stride r the shifted string is never illegal (digit bk arrives at a cap ak+r+1 ≥ ak+1), so the repair — renormalizing an illegal string — fires on no input, the shift is the bare coordinate map, and it reads at delay 0: every stride at [0; 2, 4, 8, …], and stride 3 at e − 2, whose pattern 1, 1, 2n is non-decreasing along that stride with no periodicity anywhere. The converse fails: at the cubic, strides 2 and 4 repair on 88% and 97% of inputs and still read at bounded delay, as does stride 2 at e − 2 — where strides 1 and 2 repair the identical number of inputs at every range while stride 1 is cap-shaped and stride 2 reads at delay 0 at every depth. The bounded repairing cells are Lipschitz with a local constant, attained at one depth and 0 elsewhere, byte-identical across three ranges. A repair decides nothing by its extent, only by whether its cascade imports information from above the digit it lands on.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: the repair counts at three ranges (29,390, 99,390 and 298,403 inputs at e − 2's strides 1 and 2), the local constants at the cubic's strides 2 and 4.

The parity law

The parity law rule

On the designed family, at every stride scanned, the verdict is a function of r mod P alone and carries no dependence on a: r ≡ 0 is the period shift and reads at delay 0; r mod P even and nonzero reads at bounded delay; r mod P odd gates. It is not a fit: e − 2 = [0; 1, 2, 1, 1, 4, 1, 1, 6, …], never eventually periodic, is the P = 3 pattern with a growing large quotient, and obeys it at every stride to 8. Two rivals die on the way: the parity of r itself fails at e − 2's stride 4, even and gated, and “gated iff gcd(r, P) = 1” fits every pair through P = 4 and is parted at P = 5, whose residues 2 and 4 are coprime to 5 and bounded. Two things the law settles behind it: the repair's reach is exactly the delay-0 floor — cmin vanishes at and below the lowest position a repair touches, and a gated column opens one above it — and the stride parity the cubic's strides suggested was the right kind at the wrong modulus, agreeing at every period-2 window and parting first at P = 3.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: read at P ≤ 5 and called out of sample at P = 6, 7 (34 pairs); the gated half derived and the bounded half decided cell by cell at P ≤ 9, a ≤ 4, the two blocks below. At the nine windows with P = 3, 4, 5 the reading is exact in the limit: the shift's carry automaton (The lookahead limit) prints the zero column at r ≡ 0, an infinite column at every odd residue, and at every even nonzero residue a finite column of period P whose longest nonzero run and largest entry are at most 2.

The gated half is a derivation theorem

A stride gates exactly when image points of ever-agreeing inputs straddle a cut −sα, and the odd residues carry explicit comb families — periodic digit patterns written down rather than searched — whose image limits land on cuts. At even P every even position has cap 1, so the comb of 1s at the odd positions telescopes under any odd stride, θ2j = θ2j+1 − θ2j−1, to exactly −θr, the cut −qrα; raising the comb's deepest class tooth — a tooth being one nonzero digit of the pattern — from 1 to 2 adds a θ of the opposite sign strictly above the deficit, what the remaining tail must still sum to for the image to land on the cut, so the pair straddles. At odd P — on the lattice L = Z + Zα with unit η = −θP−1, so that θk+P = ηθk — the comb of 1s at the even positions 2..P − 3 with 2 at the class slot has shifted star sum Ψ ≡ 0 mod (1 − η)L at every odd r, the Fibonacci coefficients collapsing onto the quotient's own relation; every conjugate θ′ is negative and increasing, so the limit λ = Ψ/(1 − η) has conjugate above 2 while |λ| < 1, forcing λ = −sα + m with s ≥ 1, a genuine cut, read at the scanned cells as s = qr+1 = Fr+2 independent of a; and η < 0 alternates the truncation deficit, so consecutive truncations of the comb straddle. At the aperiodic one-class windows the cut is extracted exactly — each certified pair brackets one point −sα + t in integer arithmetic on the window's convergents — and which cut it is, is the parting law's.

Scope. Theorem at every even-P one-class window and every odd stride; at odd P the lattice membership and the cut are theorems, and s = qr+1 a rule at 75 cells, P ≤ 11, a ≤ 6. The aperiodic extraction at r = 1, 4, 7 at every window read: the designed windows straddle −qr+1α, e − 2 straddles −qrα.

The parting law rule

A straddled cut's two codings part at ps = min{p : qp+1 ≥ max(s, 2)}, so the parting position is ps of whichever cut the family lands on: at odd P the cut has s = qr+1 and on the designed family ps = r, the parting the stride itself; at even P the cut has s = qr, parting 1, 2, 4 at r = 1, 3, 5. The two forms are the two aperiodic signatures — the designed one-class windows part at the stride, e − 2 parts at 1, 3, 6 at strides 1, 4, 7 — and which form a window takes is settled by an origin lemma. The class phase is the residue mod 3 the large caps' 0-based positions keep to. The straddling family is the double-class comb, digit 2 at every class position from the first legal one c0, whose teeth telescope by two cap-1 lines alone (2θj = θj+2 − θj−1, consecutive teeth cancelling), so the image limit is −θc0+r−1 and the origin sets the form: phase 2 starts at c0 = 2 and lands s = qr+1; phase 1 at c0 = 1 lands qr; phase 0, where the first-digit cap forbids c0 = 0, starts at 3 and repairs the head with one tooth, landing qr too. The large values never enter the telescope, which is why probes crossing prime-valued and 2n-valued quotients read nothing off them; the truncation deficit is one θ whose index steps by 3, so consecutive truncations straddle.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: the parting at exactly ps on every certificate printed, twelve odd-P and twelve even-P cells, the formula read off the cuts the derived families land on and not proved for an arbitrary cut. The origin lemma's telescope a theorem, its certified pairs verified digit-for-digit to be the family at six cells; that no family lands a shallower cut is certified per scanned cell by the least parting any certified pair there realizes, open as a universal statement.

The bounded half

The bounded half is decided, not enumerated theorem

A cut-landing string owes, at each depth, a deficit — here a lattice point, the one its remaining tail must sum to — and legality boxes the walk of deficits: between the exact tail extremes in the real embedding, positive with a geometric bound in the conjugate. The walk lives on a finite graph, and a stride gates exactly when that graph carries a cycle through a state strictly inside its tail interval — a test quantifying over all strings and all periods at once, aperiodic included. A cycle is a periodic legal pattern whose tail value lands in the lattice, and at every even residue the only such patterns are the telescope combs: at even P the alternating comb alone, touching the cut −qr−1α from one side and never straddling; at odd P and even nonzero residue no comb survives and the graph is acyclic. The r ≡ 0 column is classified at every purely periodic window: the period shift is the bare coordinate map, so no interior cycle exists, and an endpoint suffix pins a periodic pattern to the full-cap telescope. And every residue transports onto that base: consecutive convergents are a unimodular basis of L, so θk+r = −θr−1·θ̃k with θ̃ the r-rotated window's own sequence, and the residue-r condition is the rotated window's aligned condition on digits that keep the original caps, the class slot misaligned by r. The parity law is then a cap-fit census: at even P and even r the two cap vectors' maximal combs coincide and the alternating comb is the shared survivor; at odd P the class slot wanders both parities once per 2P and no comb fits; at odd r the rotated comb needs its class cap where the original caps at 1 — the gated cells. Legality is load-bearing: with the cap-after-nonzero rule dropped, small patterns sum to exactly θr.

Scope. The criterion a theorem in both directions; the verdicts certified at every cell with P ≤ 9 and a ≤ 4, every one landing on the parity law, interior cycles at the odd residues only; the transport and the r = 0 classification theorems at every purely periodic window.

Lattice avoidance at even P theorem

At every even P, even nonzero r, a ≥ 2 and period multiple m ≥ 1, no legal periodic pattern beyond the zero pattern and the m-fold comb lands the lattice, in four exact steps. (1) The aligned coefficients at period mP are the palindrome forms ĉj = (−1)j qmP−2−j − qj, so a member's weighted sum Φm = Σ dj ĉj is a multiple of Nm = pmP−1 + qmP − 2, the index of (1 − ηm)L. (2) Positive multiples die unconditionally, the mirrored comb's value sitting below Nm. (3) Negative multiples die on the odd-support theorem: the class slots all sit at odd aligned positions, so an even position's weight in the deficit is dominated by its right neighbour's, which legality leaves a unit of cap, and the legal cyclic maximum of the deficit is exactly the all-odd-at-cap total; that total sits below Nm at every m, its increment one closed form in the block's convergents that three elementary lemmas keep positive, and its base positive since Fr + FP−r < FP — no threshold, no strip. (4) At Φm = 0 one identity collapses membership to the divisibility qmP−1 | Σ dj qj, which admits only the zero pattern and the comb. The chain consumes legality exactly once, at step (3).

Scope. Theorem in P, a and m, machine-confirmed at 315 cells to ten period multiples.

Lattice avoidance at odd P theorem

At every odd P, even nonzero residue, a ≥ 2 and period multiple, no nonzero legal periodic pattern lands the lattice. The chain rides the same four steps at even multiples, and a doubling closes the odd ones — a period-mP pattern is a period-2mP pattern with the same tail value. The congruence layer and the collapsing identity hold verbatim at mP even; positive multiples die on a strict bound, beaten by one telescope identity with (a − 1)m/2 to spare; negative multiples die on the mass bound — the mass being the deficit's legal cyclic maximum — the odd-support theorem's charging argument transporting with a remainder — legality right-charges every supported even position except the even class slots — and the margin staying positive at every even m by a two-bracket Fibonacci closed form; and at the final census the strict bound forces t = 0, where even P's slack left the t = 1 comb. The margin is asymptotically tight at (r, a) = (3, 2): the legality-free relaxation crosses there at every a, so legality is load-bearing at every scale. At odd r the chain breaks at exactly the strict bound — the class cap lands where the mirrored telescope needs cap 1 — while the layer, the divisibility and the mass bound hold verbatim.

Scope. Theorem at every odd P, every a ≥ 2 and every period multiple; the margin machine-confirmed at 270 instances to twelve multiples, the doubling's odd-multiple census enumerated empty at 28 cell-instances.

The corner limit theorem

The avoidance lives on one comparison, the deficit's legal maximum against the index, and the ratio of the two says how close a cell comes to losing it. From P = 5 up the recorded worst ratio sits at one corner, (r, a, m) = (2, 2, 2). At a = 2 the denominators are shifted Fibonacci numbers, the charging remainder vanishes so the maximum is exact at both parities, and the comparison telescopes: the mass is q2P − 1 = 2FP+1 FP+2, Cassini gives the index 4FP+12, and the odd-P ratio is FP+2/(2FP+1) — half the Fibonacci convergent — falling to exactly φ/2 from above. Parity enters the masses only through the j = P + 1 slot at even P, subdominant, so the even ratio falls to the same limit at rate φ−P and the odd at its square. And the corner forgets the period multiple: one exact fraction per P across every multiple scanned.

Scope. The closed forms and both parities' limits theorems at every P, machine-confirmed as exact integers at P = 3..40, the odd-P exact maximum confirmed against a direct search to P = 13. Component tiers: the multiple-freeness a rule at m ≤ 8, P ≤ 21, its derivation open; the corner's location an observation of the recorded sweeps — at P = 3 the worst sits off the corner, at a = 8 and six periods.

The gated cells' members

The member monoid rule

At the odd residues, where the parity law gates, nonzero periodic legal patterns landing the lattice exist — each a member, their deficit orbits the cycles of the bounded half's graph. A member's value is linear in its digits, so a legal digit-wise sum of members is a member, and every scanned member is such a sum over its cell's indecomposables, one to five generators per cell, every one of three shapes. The comb: t(−θr) at even P, t(−θr+1) at odd, the gated derivation's cuts, scaled to the class cap only where every supported position is a class slot. The pure class tooth, all of its digits on one large-quotient slot, whose height-h word is a member exactly when h0 divides h — h0 the order of θP−1+r in L/(1 − η)L, a divisor of det(I − H) for the period matrix H — so the pure tooth generates exactly where h0 ≤ a. And tooth-plus-unit words — a class tooth of some height, the digit at the slot, with unit digits beside it — carrying deeper cuts as the cap rises, each such cut a rung and a column's rungs its ladder. The cycle set equals the scaled comb orbits except where a tooth-plus-unit word joins at a = 4: 42 of 48 cells at P ≤ 8, 10 of 12 at P = 9.

The ladder is the stride's. The r = 3 column carries −θr+2 at class-slot height 4, −θr+3 at height 6 with a unit at the slot's predecessor, 2(−θr+2) at height 8 and −θr+4 at height 10 — the same cuts at the same heights at P = 8 and P = 9, only the unit digits beside the tooth differing; the r = 5 column at P = 9 carries −θr+2 at height 4, −θr+3 at 6 and 2(−θr+3) at 13, where at P = 8 it stopped at its cap-4 word; the r = 1 and r = 7 columns are the comb alone at every cap read. A generator's value is not always a single cut: from cap 9 the (7, ·, 3) column carries 2(−θ5) − θ2, no multiple of any cut. What fixes the heights, and which columns carry a ladder, is open. The double-period members are decided by a concatenation criterion: member words u, v concatenate to a period-2P member iff λu + Tu ≡ λv + Tv mod (1 + η)L, T the cyclic wrap's r-truncation term, every period-2P member's blocks being members themselves — so the branching is concatenation freedom, not sum headroom, and exactly four cells fuse.

Scope. Rule at scanned scope: the chart at P ≤ 9, a ≤ 4, every odd residue, periods one and two; the monoid at P ≤ 8 to cap 8 and at P = 9 to cap 13; the columns at P ≤ 8 to cap 13. Component tiers: closure under legal sums a property; the tooth criterion a criterion, with h0 | det(I − H) a property checked at every cell; the ladder's cuts and heights a rule at that scope, the landed-cut reading of a generator's value a rule only to cap 8 and an observation past it; the concatenation criterion derived and machine-confirmed at all 95 legal pairs over 48 cells with zero mismatches. Missing for a theorem: the odd-r analogue of the lattice-avoidance chain — a bound on a member's support, from which the shapes fall out — and the rung law, what fixes the heights.

The verdict on the gate's two forms

Unit-ness is a periodicity artifact rule

At all three non-quadratic windows unit-ness has no subject — no period, no order, no fundamental unit, and at e − 2 no algebraic units of any kind — and at every one of them some stride reads at bounded delay regardless, at delay 0 in six of the twelve (window, stride) cells scanned: maps that are nobody's unit action and are not multiplication by anything, the ratio qk+r/qk varying with k instead of settling. Where the read is immediate its reason is the coordinate one, with no unit anywhere in it; and the window at which no scanned stride reaches delay 0 is the cubic, the one window here whose field has units — as clean a separation of “units exist” from “units act” as the family offers. So where the continued fraction is eventually periodic the digit shift and multiplication by the order's fundamental unit are the same map, and no quadratic row of the gate table on Reading could tell which of the two was doing the work; off periodicity they separate, and the action on the completion is the form left standing — the native one everywhere, the table's rows included, at Lipschitz strength and not at continuity.

Scope. Rule at the three windows scanned; whether ∛2's partial quotients are bounded is open, so the cubic's unbounded strides are not leaned on. The weakest cell is the exploding window, where depth t costs qt, a tenfold range moving the data cap only from 4 to 5. What a numeration window can hand its inverse limit in general stays open; the further shapes are realized off the Ostrowski family (Completions).

The limit of the lookahead

A column has an exact limit as the range grows, and at every purely periodic window the shift is a finite automaton that computes it per cell. At a window whose period carries two large quotients the half-period stride — the one carrying each large quotient onto the other — gates exactly where the two differ. A single table's runs give a first reading and the automaton the verdict; at a window with no period, one calibration constant does inductive work that nothing derives. And there, wherever the large quotients keep to one residue class, closing the window's own prefixes into periodic windows answers — the class plays the period — while the cubic refuses every instrument read. The lookahead limit.

The widened output

The same automaton, run as a game with the digits the reader may write widened past legality while the input stays legal, separates the arithmetic gate's two refusals: ×m's is the writing's — raise any output cap and the map has a bounded reader on the completion at every irrational window — while ⌊n/m⌋'s is the map's and survives every widening. An integer input, which must finish, costs that reader lookahead of its own, never one digit, and the flush is the whole price — The widened output.
