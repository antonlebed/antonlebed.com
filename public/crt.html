Independence

Add 1 to n. Some channels change, others stay. Each channel is modular arithmetic in its own world — what happens in mod-8 cannot affect mod-9.

Arithmetic

Pick two numbers. Their sum or product decomposes channel-by-channel: the CRT of (a + b) equals the channel-wise sum of CRT(a) and CRT(b).
