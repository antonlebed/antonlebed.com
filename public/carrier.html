The door a state charges

A place already seated sees the whole state through one integer: the p-adic valuation of the invariant. Other places' residue fields put powers of p into that integer without bound, so a ring can be SHOPPED for a place that raises another's door — a carrier — and the window it stays load-bearing in is a menu fact a head breaks in both directions.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient. A place is a prime of the ring, of norm pf for its residue degree f and with ramification index e, tame when e < p and wild otherwise; a unit sits at level i when it is 1 modulo the i-th power of the place. Seating a place is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move. A place's column is λ read across its depths, its ladder the depths where the column jumps, and its door the least climb that raises λ from where it stands: the lone door against its own column, the populated door against the state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated λ's. A ladder is headed when a leading stretch of gaps exceeds its eventually constant tail, the head's seat being e/(p − 1). The surplus at a seated place over p is the power of p standing in L above what the place's own λ carries; a place whose λ supplies a power of another prime ℓ is a carrier, the place over ℓ whose door it raises its consumer, and ablating a carrier deletes it from a state to read the consumer's door without it. A carrier is load-bearing at a state when ablating it lowers the consumer's door. The menu is the priced set of admissible moves; a walk locks when it stops opening for good and the move it repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle. The whole vocabulary is bound at The pricing schedule; what a lone place hands over is The clock.

One valuation

A seated place's door is one p-adic valuation, and the excess over its lone door is set by other places' residue fields, unbounded rule

The unit group of O/Pa is the residue field's units, cyclic of order N(P) − 1, times the principal units modulo level a, a p-group, so λ's prime-to-p part is N(P) − 1 at every depth. For a SEATED place that factor already divides L and the door loses its prime-to-p clause: it is the least r with the p-part of λ(Pe+r) above the p-part of L. A seated place sees the state through one integer and nothing else — 188 distinct (column, exponent, valuation) keys, 0 clashes, 60 hit by more than one ring — so the transfer from the ladder is CORRECTABLE rather than false. The closed form door = vp(L) − e + 2 holds exactly where the local unit group is cyclic, which an unramified place breaks wherever 2 splits. The valuation is a maximum over seated places: one over the same p contributes a pure p-power's valuation moving with its own depth, the EXPONENT route; one over another prime contributes the p-part of its norm less one, its residue cardinality with its depth absent, the RESIDUE route. Z[i]'s ramified place at exponent 3 has lone door 5 and populated door 7, a move costing 128 against 32, supplied by the inert place over 3 whose residue field F9 puts 8 into L; planting a split place over 257 drives the same door to 17, and the 2-part of p − 1 is unbounded over the primes p ≡ 1 mod 4, so the residue characteristics SET the excess and never bound it. Unseated places widen in every ring, 20.0%, 12.6% and 24.4% of readings; the seated widening a walk reaches is that one Z[i] state, excess 2. The exponent route fires at 0 of the 717 — no swept state seats two places of equal characteristic, a place and its conjugate tying at every menu — but a different seed belt builds 50 such states of 263, and planted directly a split place over 41 beside its conjugate widens a door from 1 to 6: rare rather than absent, and where absent the tie-break's doing. A clock move raises the valuation at its own characteristic by exactly one and touches no other, 51 of 51 in the ideal world; an element vehicle whose rider lands on a second place of the core's characteristic raises it by two.

Scope. The identity a property at every place with a finite residue field, the condition the unit-group splitting needs; the excess figures a rule in range, three quadratic rings swept from the void and every planted seed, 717 seated readings.

Shopping a ring for a cheap supply

Residue degree 3 buys a cheaper carrier, not a longer reach, and a walk pays it in a ring shopped for rule

Any valuation is reachable at degree 1, so what degree 3 changes is what a carrier COSTS, which is what a menu charges: N − 1 = (p − 1)(p² + p + 1) supplies the primes ℓ ≡ 1 mod 3 dividing p² + p + 1 from far below anything p − 1 or p² − 1 reaches — 7 from norm 8 against 29 at degree ≤ 2, 13 from norm 27 against 53 — and attains the floor ℓk + 1. Since e ≤ 3 in a cubic field the head's seat admits only (p, e) = (2, 1), (2, 2), (3, 2), so taking 2 INERT for the norm-8 carrier leaves the partially ramified place over 3 as the head's only seat: 88 of 331 fields have 2 inert, 9 carry a head, all 9 there. At the first field also holding a degree-1 consumer over 7, discriminant 321, the walk seats the norm-8 place and its N − 1 = 7 lifts a seated consumer's door from 1 to 2, the consumer's norm SQUARED; a walk locks ON the norm-8 place at one seed. The pairing is rare because the raised door prices the consumer out of the menu and holds it at depth 1 — 33 of 397 walked states, all 33 load-bearing, consumer at depth 1 at every one — and held there the carrier moves the LIMIT: the same pair locks at 11 per move with it and 7 without. 3 per move is the head's LOCKED price, not what it charges from a state leaving its door above 1 (27 seated at depth 2, 243 after a norm-31 seed).

Scope. A rule in range for the carrier table and the shop, 331 cubic fields to |d| ≤ 2000; an observation for the walks.

The window

The supply bites only while the consumer is shallow — the tame window, which a head breaks in both directions rule

A seated place over ℓ supplies itself ⌈(a − 1)/e⌉ from its own ladder at depth a, so a carrier giving valuation k is load-bearing exactly while that is under k — the window a ≤ e(k − 1) + 1, read off the standard column, which is the orbit of ψ(i) = min(p·i, i + e), the head's law on The clock, exactly when the multiplying regime is empty, e ≤ p − 1: exact at every tame headless consumer, 14 of 14, and wrong at a wild headless one by ψ alone (Z[21/3] over 2, e = 3, a k = 2 window of 2 against the form's 4). At a HEADED consumer it is wrong from k = 2 on and in both directions — wider by 1 at Z's place over 2, at a split place over 2 and at Q3(√−3); narrower by 1 at Q(i)'s ramified place at k = 2, then wider by 2 — because the head's transient climbs fast at the bottom before flattening, so a shallow window sits in the fast stretch. At k = 1 the window is the single depth 1 at every consumer, 8 of 8, since every column starts at 0 and reaches 1 by depth 2. The window is a MENU fact: the deepest depth at which seating a norm-5 carrier moves a consumer over 2's door is 3, 2, 2 at split, inert and ramified 2, exactly one value per shape over 41 fields, against the form's 2, 2, 3. The head's transient costs depth and buys size, one mechanism seen twice: Q(i)'s column climbs 0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3 in the 2-part, closing its window a depth early, and the flat stretch is what the door must cross — the carrier moves that door from 1 to 7, a move costing 2 against 128. A k = 1 carrier never reaches past the first rung of a flat stretch, so k ≥ 2 is what makes that half visible.

Scope. A rule in range: brute unit-exponent columns at eleven places over three rational primes, k ≤ 4; 41 quadratic fields at k = 2.

The cheapest supply, and the ramified door derived

A deep supply is cheapest at ℓ = 2, where the pairing needs no shopping; the depth is the shape's and the door is the discriminant's mod 8, derived rule

A supply of valuation k at ℓ costs a norm ≡ 1 mod ℓk, so its floor ℓk + 1 is governed by how small ℓ is: at ℓ = 2 the floor is attained at k = 1 to 4, norms 3, 5, 9, 17, all inside the walked belt, while ℓ ≥ 5 holds k = 1 alone. The degree-3 undercut is a k = 1 fact and the departure an ℓ = 2 one, and the two never compete, ℓ ≡ 1 mod 3 being the family that cannot contain 2. A carrier over 5 of residue degree 1 supplies 2-part 4 whether 5 splits or ramifies — the supply reads the residue cardinality and never the carrier's own depths — and sits beside a place over 2 in 141 fields to |m| ≤ 200. The k = 2 window takes one value per shape because split and inert fix the completion and at every ramified one (1 + π)² sits at exact level 2. The depth-1 door is 4 and 3 flat at the split and inert shapes and 5, 6 or 7 at the ramified one, DERIVED: a ramified place over 2 has residue field F2, so against a state of 2-part exactly 4 the door is the minimum over units of the valuation of u4 − 1, which the norm factors into two outer terms and a SUM OF TWO SQUARES; every unit at level 2 or deeper costs at least 7, and at level 1 the sum of squares decides — door 5 at m ≡ 2, 6 mod 8, and 6 plus the 2-part of s² + 1 with s = (m − 3)/4 at m ≡ 3 mod 4. The m ≡ 7 class runs longest because an odd square is 1 mod 8; the case analysis is finite and complete, so 5, 6, 7 are the only doors a ramified quadratic has. The residue is COARSER than the completion: the 28 ramified fields to |m| ≤ 60 realize all six ramified quadratic extensions of Q2 against four residues, and the door is constant across completions the residue merges because no step reads m beyond mod 8. Two of the three consumer shapes pay the supply with a WALK — 156 and 13 walk-seated load-bearing states at the split and inert consumers — and the ramified one, the one with the largest door, never does, its 66 load-bearing states all seeded. The limit moves at all three; at the split and inert consumers the carried walk locks on a third place (9 and 19 per move), so the carrier moved the limit without being it, while at the ramified one it locks ON the carrier at 5 per move against an ablated 9, the one row where supply and vehicle cannot be told apart.

Scope. A theorem for the ramified door, verified at 1,619 fields; a rule in range for the carrier floors and the 141-field census.
