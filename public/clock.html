The clock

A growth walk pays for every move, and the counter it pays against is handed over by the places themselves. What one place hands the schedule is a ladder of depths; its shape decides whether the walk ever stops opening, where it stops, and what its one runaway pays forever — two numbers, and a head where the ladder starts steep.

Throughout, a state is a nonzero ring element, a move multiplies it, and λ is the exponent of the unit group of the quotient, the least power sending every unit to 1. A place is a prime of the ring with an integer degree (the degree of the irreducible over F2[x], the log-norm over a number ring); seating one is an opening, deepening a seated one a clock move, which advances the tick T every price is read against. A place's column is λ read across its depths, and its tick ladder is the set of depths where that column jumps; the gap at a depth is the least climb that raises λ from there, and the door of a place is its gap at the depth it stands. A lone door is read against the place's own column; a populated door against the whole state's invariant L, the least common multiple of the seated places' λ's. Every door on this page is the lone one: what a state charges a seated place is The door a state charges. A ladder is headed when a leading stretch of gaps exceeds its eventually constant tail gap; the largest gap an item lands on is its sup, the head's width is the sup less the tail, and the splice is the one step where the ladder leaves its ramp. A walk locks when it stops opening for good, and the move it then repeats forever is its recurrent vehicle; what that vehicle pays forever is the budget. A coordinate carried up without bound is a runaway, one left above exponent 1 by a move never affordable again a strand. The schedule family is the dynamics with the ring taken out — items of integer degree, a global clock every item shares or a per-item clock each item owns, a price read off the degree and the tick, a fresh discount on each degree's first openings, a degree whose discount is spent being covered and one the supply starts covered born covered, the ring re-entering only as a supply of items per degree, and the menu being the priced set of a state's admissible moves — and the whole vocabulary is bound at The pricing schedule.

Whether a walk stops

A bounded gap stops the ladder, an unbounded one climbs it, by a comparison of two curves rule

A clock move lands just past the previous tick and lifts the tick to the ladder's next member, so the recurrent vehicle costs the deep item's degree times the gap, forever. A bounded gap stops the ladder at the least uncovered degree: a stop is the recurrent price sitting below the cheapest unopened item's price for good, and it needs the opening curve to grow as much as the recurrent one to stay flat. Read at gaps 1, 2, 3 and 5 the stop sits at 2, 2, 3, 5 against the products 1, 2, 3, 5 — exact at three, and floored at gap 1 by the born-covered set. Above gap 1 the stop is a TIE: the recurrent vehicle and the least uncovered degree cost the same, the opening sits in the menu's minimum and is declined by tie-break, and the branch that takes it buys one more degree and then refuses strictly. An unbounded gap climbs at every cell whose price can see a degree; there is nothing between. At a degree-blind price — every degree priced 1 — a bounded gap stops nothing, and under a global clock every ladder above gap 1 climbs with zero runaways: the stop law reads as the gap's property only over supplies whose openings get dearer. Locality is not the axis: one tick per item against one global tick changes the climb rate five-fold, 21 degrees seated against 115, and decides nothing about whether the ladder climbs.

Scope. A rule in range: 7 ladders × 2 clocks × 8 price dials, 120 moves a cell over a supply of two items at every degree to 400; the unbounded side at all 98 cells whose price sees a degree.

A number ring's walk has a flat budget, so coverage keeps only the small half of the job rule

Deepening a place P from exponent e to e + r takes the p-part of L to e + r − 1, so the next door is 1 and the vehicle costs the norm of P per move, forever. At every lock hundreds of fresh door-1 places sit unspent — 284 to 302 of a 306-place universe — and the walk declines every one, because an escape costs its norm and the walk never gets richer. What must be covered is the 0 to 7 places cheaper than the vehicle; coverage density at the deepest locks is 6% and 12%. The covering rule of The pricing schedule stops a ladder by outrunning it and presumes a ladder that climbs; a flat budget is outrun by a fixed set.

Scope. A rule in range: 131 locking seeds over two quadratic rings' norm-40 belts, 40 tail moves past each lock, 0 tails non-flat.

verifier: explore_lock_budget.py

The ladder is λ's own jump set

A ring's tail gap is its ramification index, and lock against sprawl is the characteristic theorem

A lone place's door at depth e is the least r with the p-adic valuation of λ(Pe+r) above that of λ(Pe), so the ladder is the set of depths a at which v(a + 1) exceeds v(a). The unit group of O/Pa is the residue field's units times the principal units modulo level a, and the p-th power map sends level i to p·i in equal characteristic and to min(i + e, p·i) in mixed. So a function field's ladder is the powers of 2 — its door runs 1, 1, 2, 1, 4, 3, 2, 1, 8, … and its gap is 16 by depth 24 — while a number ring's gap is e past the head: 1 at every split and inert place, 2 at every ramified one. Linear against logarithmic is the whole of it: exact tick against doubling, flat budget against climbing, lock against sprawl; and the mixed side is the entire constant-gap family. The ladders between the two — a gap growing like 2√e, which climbs — are realized by no Dedekind domain with finite residue fields, the only place λ is defined.

Scope. A theorem for the tail gap at every complete discrete valuation ring with finite residue field; the 90 places of norm ≤ 200 in two quadratic rings read at depths 1 to 24, 0 off, a control on the engine.

verifiers: explore_module_law.py, explore_tick_pump.py

The head

A head is a visible splice of the two-gear clock, and it exists exactly at cyclotomic ramification rule

The whole ladder is the orbit of ψ(i) = min(p·i, i + e) from level 1 — multiplying below the seat e/(p − 1), stepping by e above it. ψ is exact at 15 of 24 places and at the other 9 exact up to ONE step, the head. Since λ is a minimum over units, the ladder sees only the shallowest orbit and a deeper class can splice invisibly: a head is a splice the level-1 orbit carries. It exists if and only if the residue degree is 1, the p-th roots of unity lie in the completion, and e = (p − 1)·pt for some t ≥ 0 — the ramification indices of the p-power cyclotomic fields: the multiplying segment lands on the seat only when the seat is a p-power, and the landing bites only where the whole residue layer dies. The simpler criterion "p − 1 ≤ e" holds at the 90 quadratic places and fails at 9 of the 24: both inert places over 2; Z[21/3], Z[21/6], Z[31/3] and Z[31/4], where e is off the form; and Z[√3], Z[31/6] and Z[51/4], which land on the seat and miss the torsion — Z[√3] against Z[√−3] at p = 3 is the same p and e with a head at one and none at the other.

Scope. A rule in range: 24 places over 21 local fields to depth 26, the necessity derived; the two-gear clock is The observatory's.

verifier: explore_head_width.py

The width is the arrival defect, which reads the p-power cyclotomic tower and not the torsion rule

The width is the level-1 arrival landing L1 less the seat and less the gap, w = L1 − e/(p − 1) − e, checked against the landing chart at every place the two share. At e = 2 over Q2 it is the exact trichotomy 1, 2, 3 as adjoining i is ramified, unramified or split. It is not the torsion's: Q2(√2) and Q2(√−5) agree in p, e, the residue degree and the order of the p-power roots of unity, and carry widths 1 and 2. Measured: 1 at Q2, Q2(√±2) and Q3(√−3); 2 at Q2(√−5) and Q2(21/4); 3 at Q2(i); 4 at Q2(21/8).

Scope. A rule in range at the 9 headed places; the identity with the arrival landing derived.

What one place hands the dynamics

A ladder enters through two numbers: the sup decides where the walk stops, the tail what its runaway pays rule

Against the constant ladder of its sup, every headed cell agrees on the least uncovered degree, the runaway count, the strand count and the fate, and disagrees on the budget at all 14 — reading the deep degree times the tail where the control reads it times the sup. Z[i]'s place (e = 2, width 3) stops where gap 5 stops and is priced where gap 2 is; Z[21/8] stops at 12 and is priced at 8. Both are gaps the item LANDS on: reachable depths climb the ramp to pt + 1, whose next member is the splice, so the item pays e + w once and e forever, and the stop formula prices the barrier a vehicle clears once, never its recurrent cost. Given the two numbers every law is the constant family's; the ramp's shape, the splice's position and their number add nothing, measured at a ladder with two overshoots, one off the seat and an arithmetic ramp. So the dictionary was one number short. The sup is 2 at Q(√−23)'s split places over 2, 3 at Z[√±2], 4 at Q(√−5)'s ramified place over 2, 5 at Z[i], 6 at Z[21/4], 12 at Z[21/8]. The degree ceiling (The pricing schedule) dips to the least supplied degree at the splice, its tightest point, and rises without limit along the tail: a head is a one-time dip and never a ceiling.

Scope. A rule in range: 7 ψ-generated headed ladders × 2 clocks against their constant-gap controls, 14 of 14 on both readings, plus 4 ladders ψ cannot produce.

verifier: explore_headed_ladder.py

The gap's boundedness decides the fate; the sup under a per-item clock decides the count above exponent 1 rule

Under a global clock exactly one coordinate stands above exponent 1 at every ladder, headed or not — after the deep item's first ramp step every other item's door is the whole tick — so the ideal limit theorem of The limit survives every ladder run, and a stopped ladder costs it nothing. Under a per-item clock the count is the sup's and the dial's: 1, 1, 3, 6 at gaps 1, 2, 3, 5 at the family's reference dial, 3 to 8 at gap 5 across dials, and at a headed ladder the sup's reading, Z[i] reading 6 where the gap-2 ladder reads 1. A per-item tick is exactly the next ladder member above the item's own exponent, which is a lone place's door verbatim. What that corner does not underwrite is the ring: its headed places stand at sup 3, 4 and 5, and the flat support a ring keeps rests on the arithmetic of its first winner's kind (Which rings walk), never on the schedule-side count. The gap-3 cell's three are one runaway and two strands, a distinction the count cannot make and the product law below does.

Scope. A rule in range: the 14 cells read for how many seated items stand above exponent 1 after 120 moves; the per-item tick identity at 8,922 (state, item) pairs, 0 off.

verifiers: explore_tick_pump.py, explore_headed_ladder.py

A universe whose items do not share a ladder is priced by a minimum over products, and the head enters as an admission price rule

Under a per-item clock the flat budget is the minimum, over seated items, of the price at that item's degree and its own tail gap; the runaway is a least-PRODUCT item and never a least gap. A gap-3 item at degree 1 outruns every gap-1 item above degree 3, and one gap-1 item at the least degree freezes an entire wide population. A wide item's gap falls to 1 only at its ladder's own members, which its reachable depths miss after the first step, so its door is 1 exactly once — and that one cheap move is what mixing adds to the limit: a strand, priced out strictly or standing in a tie with the budget. Under a global clock the product law does not hold, a shared tick hiding an item's gap. With heads put back an item pays its tail only if the walk bought it past its splice, so the minimum runs over CROSSED items; the two minima coincide at every measured width and part only at width 150 on e = 1. What the image over these ladders is — the settled set, the global-clock reading, the domain a head gives the per-item image — is The surviving openings.

Scope. A rule in range: 5 universes × 2 clocks, and 6 headed universes at all 6 per-item cells.

verifiers: explore_tick_pump.py, explore_headed_ladder.py

What the rest of the cost side holds

Every law above prices a lone item against its own ladder. At a SEATED place the door reads the whole state through one p-adic valuation, and other places' residue fields widen it without bound: a move that opens one residue layer of index equal to the norm is charged the norm to the power of the door, a premium with exactly two sources, a head or a populated invariant — Which rings walk holds the premium and the ladder column a supply cannot carry; The door a state charges holds the identity and the carriers that widen it. How wide a menu can be at all, the coincidence locus, is The pricing schedule's. How many clocks the family runs — one, one per item, or a ring's blocks — is The clock dial; which places a real ring's menu ever seats is Which rings walk; and what of these laws survives a cost that reads the state, with the certificate that makes a lock permanent, is The certificate.
