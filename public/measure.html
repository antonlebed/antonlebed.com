Measurement without size: what the ring decides about its own elements, which textbook logic that is, and the exact price of each read.

  • Words and curves — the two worlds the logic grades — words and curves

A rung of the tower is Z/N with N the product of the first k primes, one channel per prime (The Object), and the construction deletes the archimedean place, so nothing below is measured by size (Walls). An idempotent is an element with e² = e: one 0-or-1 residue per channel, so 2k of them, one per set of channels, and eS is the one reading 1 exactly on the set S. Under the ring's own polynomials for AND, OR and NOT the idempotents are a Boolean algebra; every other element is a graded truth value, and the ring's instrument for reading one is the power map ◇a = aλ = esupp(a), where λ = lcm(p−1) is the exponent of the unit group and supp a the channels on which a is nonzero. Read at the coarsest grain — each channel true, false or unknown — the graded logic is nothing new: it is Kleene's strong three-valued logic, channel by channel, with one arithmetic departure. What the ring adds is finer than that reading: where a law fails is located to a channel and an ideal, the power map is a quantifier with an exact conjugate, and between the two sits a ladder of order gates, each level of read with an exact reach and an exact price.

The idempotent algebra

The prison rule

The ring endomorphisms of Z/N are exactly the idempotents: f(n) = an is a ring map iff a² = a, so there are 2ω(N) of them, ω counting the distinct primes — fattening a prime changes nothing. No idempotent except 1 preserves any unit: partial projection destroys all reversibility. The idempotents partition into the extremes {0, 1}, the k primitive ones of a single channel, and 2k − k − 2 composite ones, a count that is tower-smooth for k = 3..7 (3, 10, 25, 56, 119 = 7·17) and admits the boundary prime at k = 8 (246 = 2·3·41).

Scope. The endomorphism count and the unit statement are proved; the partition counts are computed for k = 3..8.

The power maps rule

The number of solutions of xk = x in Z/N is ∏p (1 + gcd(k−1, p−1)) over the channels; per channel the fixed points of x → xp are all p residues (Fermat), so xp = x for every x at every squarefree rung and breaks at a fat channel. At Z/510510 squaring has 128 fixed points — the idempotents — cubing 1,458 and x¹³ has 40,950. Under squaring, 0 has basin 1 (every channel a field, no nilpotents) and 1 has basin 2¹¹, the units of 2-power order, 11 = Σ v2(p−1); the odd part of p − 1 sets a channel's cycles — 7 carries 2-cycles, 11 carries 4-cycles and alone sets the maximum — and the maximal tail per channel is v2(p−1). The maximal squaring cycle is frozen at 4 through k = 7, the lcm of odd parts being 15 from k = 5 on, and unfreezes at p = 19.

Scope. The counting formula is proved; the squaring census is exhaustive over all 92,160 units of Z/510510; the freeze is an observation of k ≤ 7, not a law.

verifier: verify_algebra.py::test_power_map_fixed_points, test_squaring_dynamics, test_squaring_tails, test_odd_core_saturation.

The graded logic

The graded logic rule

The ring polynomials AND = ab, OR = a+b−ab, NOT = 1−a, IMP = 1−a+ab restrict to exact Boolean logic on the 2k idempotents — intersection, union, complement — and the residuation law (x AND a) ≤ b iff x ≤ (a IMP b) holds over all 128³ idempotent triples of Z/510510. On the rest of the ring the same polynomials give a graded logic whose every failing Boolean law fails inside the defect ideal generated by δ(x) = x² − x: the idempotents of any commutative ring form a Boolean algebra under these connectives (standard), so a law's LHS − RHS vanishes in Z[x]/(xi² − xi) — every failure is a combination Σ δ(xi)·gi. The eleven charted laws each deform in a single variable — each holds whenever that one variable is classical, the others arbitrary: excluded middle by +δ, contradiction by −δ, absorption by δ(1−b), the two distributivities by δ·bc and −δ(1−b)(1−c), modus ponens by −δ(1−b); De Morgan, double negation, and contraposition survive identically. Truth is per channel: δ vanishes on channel p iff the residue reads 0 or 1, so supp δ(a) is exactly the non-classical channels of a. The graded region has no internal order (a ≤ a already needs idempotence): degrees are not ordered as in fuzzy logic, they are located.

Scope. The defect-ideal law proved; the deformation identities verified exhaustively at Z/30 and on 20,000 triples at Z/510510.

The three-valued shadow is strong Kleene rule

Read each channel at the coarsest grain — a residue is true (1), false (0) or unknown (anything else) — and the four connectives are Kleene's strong three-valued logic K3 on every channel: 0 absorbs AND, 1 absorbs OR, an unknown passes through otherwise, NOT of unknown is unknown, and the absorptions of the defect calculus below are K3's own. Whenever at least one input is classical the ring output's class is K3's, without exception. The one departure is arithmetic: two unknowns that are mutual inverses conjoin to true (ab = 1), and under the conjugates disjoin or imply to false — on exactly p − 2 of the (p−2)² unknown-unknown pairs per connective, a share 1/(p−2) that is total at p = 3 and thins as 1/p. So the graded logic locates a conclusion's unreliability no finer than three-valued propagation does; what it adds is the ideal the failure lives in and the arithmetic of the departure.

Scope. Exhaustive over every input pair and connective at every odd p ≤ 23 and channel by channel at Z/30 (208 departures per connective, all unknown-unknown, as the pair census predicts); the formula-level statement follows by induction on the nodes and is sampled on 20,000 formulas to depth 4 at Z/510510.

The defect calculus rule

The defect composes exactly: δ(NOT a) = δ(a), and δ(a AND b) = δ(a)δ(b) + δ(a)b + aδ(b) — a twisted Leibniz rule (twist a → a²) whose NOT-conjugates are the OR and IMP laws. Additively δ(a+b) = δ(a) + δ(b) + 2ab, the polarization that dies in characteristic 2 — channel 2 is wholly classical (F2 = {0, 1}), so the graded logic lives on the odd channels. Nesting in the four connectives therefore never creates non-classicality: supp δ(F) lies inside the union of the inputs' defect supports for every AND/OR/NOT/IMP formula F. Bare ring + escapes the scope, its polarization 2ab creating defect from two classical truths (δ(1+1) = 2). A non-classical channel is classicalized in exactly two ways per binary connective — absorption (partner 0 for AND, 1 for OR) or its own inverse (partner x⁻¹ for AND, the NOT-mirror for OR) — and NOT never cancels.

Scope. Proved by substitution; verified exhaustively at Z/30 and on 20,000 pairs at Z/510510; the classicalization census over all p ≤ 23, all non-classical residues.

verifier: explore_idempotent_logic.py

The quantifier pair

The power map is the logic's measurement, and it comes as a conjugate pair quantifying the degree of truth within a channel. Quantifying over channels instead is walled — [a = 0] is not channel-local (the locality criterion) — so degree quantifiers come free and channel quantifiers cost the deleted place. What such a measurement is asked to decide is a shadow: an exact Boolean condition on the ring — [a = 0], [ord a = d], [ab = 1] — and every reach and every price in this section is the reach or the price of deciding one.

The quantifier pair rule

◇a = aλ reads “true to some degree” (channel 1 iff residue ≠ 0); its De Morgan conjugate □a = 1 − (1−a)λ reads “fully true” (1 iff residue = 1); and ◇NOT = NOT□ exactly — the measurement's NOT-failure is NOT exchanging the pair, not NOT breaking. ◇ commutes with AND exactly (fields), □ with OR exactly; each leaks on the dual connective at the same per-channel count: two true channels disjoin to false under ◇ iff (1−x)(1−y) = 1 — exactly p − 2 of the (p−1)² true-true pairs per channel, so under ◇ truth can be lost, never fabricated — exactly as □ gains truth over AND by inversion, xy = 1 with x ≠ 1, the same p − 2 pairs, NOT carrying one mismatch set onto the other. Over uniform random pairs the per-channel mismatch (p−2)/p² predicts a tuple-level rate of 0.4237 at Z/510510; measured 0.4234. The mixed-form laws T, 4, 5, K, necessitation and the Halmos closure law hold exactly: an S5-shaped pair. An orthomodular reading has no carrier, a lattice law needing the order the graded region lacks.

Scope. Leak laws proved by substitution, census p ≤ 23; the modal laws checked on samples at Z/510510, the K law exhaustively at Z/30.

verifier: explore_idempotent_logic.py

The pair's gap is the defect rule

◇a − □a = ◇δ(a) = esupp δ(a), equivalently ◇a AND ◇(NOT a) = ◇δ(a): “true to some degree and false to some degree” is exactly the non-classical region, and the two bits the pair returns about an element are its support and its defect support.

Scope. Exhaustive over all channels and at the ring level.

verifier: explore_quantifier_envelope.py

The quantifier envelope rule

The pair turns measurement into a two-sided certificate for every connective formula (AND/OR/NOT/IMP; bare + stays outside the scope): push NOT to the leaves — the input variables; De Morgan survives off the lattice — and keep per leaf only the two bits (◇a, □a). The Boolean evaluations roof U (◇ at positive leaves, NOT□ at negated — exact there, by the conjugacy) and floor L (□ / NOT◇) bracket the true shadow channel-wise, L ≤ □F ≤ ◇F ≤ U, by an induction whose two inequality slots are exactly the one-directional leak laws. U = 0 certifies the shadow false, L = 1 fully true, U = L the verdict outright — a two-sided decision computed in-ring; ◇ alone certifies only falsity, □ alone only truth, so deciding both ways needs the pair. Slack is located — the uncertainty region U AND NOT L lies inside the union of the inputs' defect supports, so classical-input channels are always certified — and counted: the same p − 2 mismatch sets, now as envelope slack at the dual-connective nodes. Under a classical-leaning mixture the pair-certified verdicts err zero times over 127,371 certified channel verdicts where the ◇-only mask errs 79 times on the same stream.

Scope. The bracket and the slack location proved; verified exhaustively per channel (trees to 4 leaves, p ≤ 23 at two leaves) and on ring-level samples; the error rates by full weighted enumeration per channel.

verifier: explore_quantifier_envelope.py

The envelope's sharpness rule

Read-once formulas — every leaf used once — achieve both ends of the bracket at p = 5, 7, 11: over inputs consistent with the measured bits, ◇F reaches U and □F reaches L. At p = 3 the envelope can overshoot — the graded region of F3 is the single self-inverse point −1, forcing (−1) OR (−1) = 0 and (−1) AND (−1) = 1, the p = 3 rigidity. Repeated leaves break achievability only at p = 3 in the swept one-variable family; at p ≡ 1 mod 3 the dependency shows instead as a dependent shadow sitting inside the bracket: a AND NOT a is fully true wherever δ(x) = −1 is solvable, while its floor is identically 0 — a shadow the pair can never certify true.

Scope. Rule for the swept families (read-once trees to 4 leaves at p = 3, 5, 7, 11; the one-variable family to 3 leaves at p ≤ 13), observation beyond; multi-variable repeated-leaf families beyond the sweep are open.

verifier: explore_quantifier_envelope.py

The quantifier ladder

The quantifier ladder rule

The pair is the two ends of one family: for m | λ the partial collapse ◇m a = am filters — per channel its image is the gcd(m, p−1)-th powers plus 0, and m | m′ makes am′ a function of am — the conjugacy ◇mNOT = NOT□m is definitional at every rung, and the bit-valued gate gatem(a) = □(am) reads the order: [ord a | m] per channel. gate1 = □, gateλ = ◇, monotone along the divisor lattice, meet-exact (gategcd = gatem AND gatem′), gcd-complete (any integer exponent's gate is a divisor rung's), outputs on the idempotent lattice — one quantifier per divisor of λ between “fully true” and “true to some degree” (at Z/510510: λ = 240 and 20 of them, all distinct). Repeated leaves are the ladder: □ of the m-fold AND of one leaf is gatem, ◇ of the m-fold OR its conjugate, and the two cross-reads absorb into the pair — what a repeated leaf computes beyond the pair is exactly order information.

Scope. Proved (the image law, the gate identity, the lattice laws); verified exhaustively at every channel p ≤ 23 over all 20 divisors, and at the ring level.

verifier: explore_quantifier_ladder.py

The order-6 shadow rule

The ladder earns its keep on the envelope's own dependent shadow: □(a AND NOT a) = [Φ6(a) = 0] identically, Φ6 = x² − x + 1 the sixth cyclotomic polynomial, the order-6 read for p ≠ 3, and 6 — the smallest non-prime-power order — is the ladder's first join defect: gate joins are exact below 6 and first leak at lcm(2, 3), so the shadow is gate6 AND NOT(gate2 OR gate3). Whether the pair alone can decide it is the splitting law of Q(ζ6): split channels p ≡ 1 mod 3 blind the pair (graded residues share its bits with different shadows), inert channels are vacuously false, and the ramified p = 3 is pinned by the rigidity — which is Φ6's ramification (discriminant −3; the double root −1 is F3's single graded point), the ladder folding there (gate6 = gate2). The ladder decides the shadow on every channel with zero errors on a formula where the pair-only floor is identically 0; at Z/510510 it earns exactly channels 7 and 13.

Scope. Proved per channel (the Φ6 algebra and the splitting of primes in Q(ζ6)); the join-defect identity and the decision exhaustive at p ≤ 23 plus 5,000 ring-level samples; the root law verified at every p ≤ 199.

verifier: explore_quantifier_ladder.py

The ladder's wall rule

The leaf ladder, both directions, measures exactly the joint order profile (ord a, ord(1−a)) — so a shadow whose root condition is not cyclotomic escapes it: □(a AND a AND NOT a) = [a²(1−a) = 1] first collides at p = 11, where the residues 9 and 3 share the profile (5, 5) and differ on the shadow, while the Φ6 shadow never collides. And a multi-variable dependent shadow escapes every single-leaf measurement: [ab = 1] admits same-profile b's with different shadows at p = 7 — relative order is not a leaf quantity, which is where the two-leaf world begins.

Scope. Rule for the swept range (the one-variable family p ≤ 100, 16 colliding profile classes; the multi-variable witness at p = 7), observation beyond.

The Hall projections rule

Beneath the gates, the power maps x → xe that are honest projections — idempotent as maps — are exactly the idempotent exponents e² ≡ e mod λ: 2ω(λ) of them, splitting truth-degree by λ's primes — the class-e projection keeps the q-part of an element's order for the primes q with e ≡ 1 mod the q-part of λ and kills the rest — with the identity (e = 1) and the collapse (e = 0) as top and bottom. At Z/510510 the eight are {0, 1, 16, 81, 96, 145, 160, 225} mod 240. The logic's projection lattice is the idempotent lattice of the exponent ring Z/λ, one level down (index coordinates).

Scope. Proved; the census of exponent classes and the order-splitting law exhaustive over every channel of Z/510510.

The measurement table

The measurement table rule

The whole hierarchy is one chart. A measurement program is a word — a formula over the leaves in MUL/INV/NOT, the meadow alphabet, INV being the meadow inverse that inverts each unit channel and fixes 0, so division is total; dropping NOT leaves the monomial alphabet, whose words are the aibj with exponents in Z. A word is read by gates gatem(w) = [wm = 1] under a Boolean readout, and its three budgets — alphabet level, word ops, gate count — bind one per level, the gate-budget grading. The measurement walls are the table's ∞ rows: monomial gates are constant on conjugacy orbits — the sets the power automorphisms x → xu sweep — so an orbit-splitting shadow has no finite monomial-gate price at any count — and every alphabet wall falls at a deeper row until the meadow closure breaks the last, the wall returning as a price that grows without bound (the height floor).

Words and curves

Two leaves fix each other's discrete logs up to one shared unit and nothing further: every multiplicative coset is decided, while the additive world stays blind except for a skeleton of orders 1, 2, 3, 6 — the crystallographic restriction, arriving as a fact about words. Past degree 1 the question inverts, and which loci hold torsion is settled by a coding bound: a shadow of M monomials sees an order only if that order's least prime is at most M, unless it swallows the whole orbit — Words and curves.

The price of a read

A stable torsion orbit costs exactly m(C) + ω(d) monomial gates — proved both directions, and in any finite abelian group, not just this one. The meadow level buys that count down to a single gate and pays for it in word operations, where the deleted place returns as a height tax that grows without bound. The same page carries what the logic's own valuation does to evidence: one rank-1 test valid in every semiring, and an e-calculus that can waste evidence but never fabricate it — The price of a read.
